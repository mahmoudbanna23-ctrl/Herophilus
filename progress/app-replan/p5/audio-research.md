### Recommendation ranking (my read of the table above -- the owner must verify against each vendor's live terms himself before shipping any file)
1. ElevenLabs Music -- (a) cleanest ownership statement found, user retains Output rights, but grants ElevenLabs a perpetual, irrevocable, sublicensable licence back; (b) commercial use unlocked cheaply at $6/mo; (c) names an OEM/bundling terms document, i.e. bundling is a contemplated use case, though its content could not be fetched.
2. Suno (Pro/Premier) -- (a) explicit IP assignment to the user on paid tiers; (b) grant survives subscription cancellation ("perpetual... not affected by expiry"); (c) but redistribution-in-a-bundle is not explicitly addressed and the indemnity obligation falls on the user.
3. Stable Audio / Stability AI -- (a) clean one-line ownership statement ("you own outputs"); (b) Enterprise-licence trigger only above $1M revenue, fine for a free student app now; (c) but the live pricing/terms page could not be fetched directly, so tier details are third-party-sourced and unverified.
- AIVA and Google Lyria are usable but thinner on the specific bundling question. Mubert has a live contradiction between "usable in apps" and "distribution prohibited" -- do not use until that is cleared with them directly. Udio is currently the weakest choice: its own terms pages returned no text at all to the fetch tool, and third-party reporting says it disabled downloads post-UMG deal, which would break "ship the file in the bundle" outright.

The owner must read each vendor's current live Terms of Service himself before any file is shipped -- several cells above are third-party-sourced or unverified because the vendor's own JS-rendered page would not return text to the fetch tool.
