## Runtime (from the pilot, `spike-report.md` / `spike-measure.md`)
- **three.js r147 classic, vendored in `app/vendor/`** — 711,095 B before assets. Picking,
  lights, shadows and hinge animation come free. The 14 KB hand renderer would need all of that
  written by hand; rejected.
- Works from `file://`: textures base64 (a file-loaded WebGL texture throws SecurityError there).
- **Never strands boot**: the painted plate paints first with hotspots; 3D loads after, swaps in
  when ready. No WebGL / weak device / `prefers-reduced-motion` -> stays on the painted plate,
  items still clickable. Device key `wardround.full3d` (forces 3D). New keys only; no renames.
- Real scene fps under CPU throttle is **still unmeasured** — measured in step 4, with a shed rule.

## Geometry and art
- Books, pile, tablet, letters, scroll, lamp: **procedural geometry in code** (a book = boxes +
  hinged cover), textures painted by Codex `image_gen` at **>= 2x** display size. Meshy (CC BY,
  approved) only for an item where procedural reads cheap.
- Desk top and room: from `painted-codex-3.png` (owner's plate); master-v2 candidates exist in
  `scene-pilot/plates/`.
- Rules: no opacity-only entrance (every move has a physical cause) · no upscaled raster · no
  text in painted art (labels are live HTML/3D text).
