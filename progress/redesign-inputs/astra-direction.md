Herophilus is a working medical folio kept by a student in Alexandria: a place to accumulate understanding, mark uncertainty, and return to unfinished pages.

That is the whole direction. Build the authority of a well-edited medical book, with the personal marks of its reader. The connection across 2,300 years belongs in the name, the painted clepsydra, and one sentence at the gate: “Medicine, studied in Alexandria.” It does not need columns, parchment, Greek borders, or an invented ancient seal. The student’s present work is the connection.

Use Georgia for question stems, clinical vignettes, theory prose, and page titles, with “Times New Roman”, serif as fallbacks. Use Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif for navigation, answer options, tables, and controls. These are local system stacks; load nothing remotely. Set reading text at 19px with 1.65 line height, options at 18px/1.5, interface text at 15px/1.4, and citations at 14px/1.5. Titles stop at 30px. Keep prose around 62 characters wide. Offer persistent text sizing from 18px to 24px; let the layout grow. Hierarchy comes from space, weight, and alignment, never from making essential text tiny. Reserve italics for actual editorial meaning.

The light theme uses warm paper #F3EFE6, a slightly lighter reading surface #FFFCF5, ink #252A28, and secondary text #595E58. The dark theme uses charcoal #181D1C, a reading surface #202725, ink #E5E6DC, and secondary text #B1BAB3. These are flat surfaces, without simulated paper grain. Interactive text is deep teal #155E59 in light mode and pale teal #8BCBC0 in dark mode. Correct answers use #286448 / #97D2AC; incorrect answers use #963E35 / #F0ACA0. Those states always include words and distinct marks. Verify text contrast against every actual surface, including selected options. Subjects have names and fixed positions, not four competing brand colours. Colour tells the student what happened and what can be acted upon.

On a laptop, use a 208px navigation rail and one generous content area. A narrow utility line carries profile, academic term, and appearance settings. Keep the gate sequential: sign in, choose a named profile, then choose year and semester. Show the chosen profile and term together before entry, and keep them visible afterward so shared-machine mistakes are easy to catch. On phones, replace the rail with a labelled navigation button; preserve the reading column rather than shrinking the desktop arrangement.

Home begins with the last unfinished activity and “Due today”, followed by four subject rows and the next seven scheduled days. Show progress as “312 of 840 answered”; label accuracy separately, with its attempt count. A progress bar cannot explain its own denominator. The schedule is a sober calendar anchored to the four actual exam dates, with editable study blocks and clear overdue items. An unfinished block remains movable work, never an accusation. On phones, days become a chronological agenda.

The subject index is a table of contents, not a field of cards. Group chapters beneath strong, sticky group headings; give each row a consistent title position and right-aligned question count and accuracy. Use 48px minimum row height, faint rules, and additional space between groups. Search, source-bank filters, and “Hide unwritten chapters” sit above the index. On narrow screens, put labelled statistics beneath each title. Preserve the three source-bank names in filters and question citations so provenance remains readable.

Deliberately keep unwritten chapters visible by default. Most designers would remove these apparent dead ends. Here they reveal the boundary of a hand-transcribed collection: absence of questions must not be mistaken for absence from the syllabus. Write “Not yet written” instead of a zero or dash, omit accuracy, and provide no quiz action. Keep the chapter title at normal contrast. The index should tell the truth about what this particular student can revise here.

The quiz gives up the navigation rail, subject overview, decorative progress charts, and persistent statistics. Retain a return link, chapter name, question position, and quiet flag control. The stem leads; a clinical image follows at useful resolution with a keyboard-accessible enlargement. Stack every option vertically, including when there are ten. Selection and answer submission are separate actions. Reveal the answer and explanation beneath the options without moving them or automatically advancing. Follow with the source and a labelled note field. Highlight and report-an-error remain secondary controls; highlighting has a keyboard route and never carries essential meaning alone.

Cases use the same reading page, followed by a generous response field. After submission, show expected concepts as present, missing, or uncertain, with the grading basis explained; do not pretend approximate concept matching is exact judgment. Mock exams retain this restraint but add a stable clock and question navigator. Time warnings are explicit text, never flashing. Review preserves the original response alongside the correct answer and offers re-attempt. Weak chapters rank evidence of difficulty with attempt counts; flagged questions retain the student’s deliberate selections. Keep these destinations distinct.

Long reading changes the interaction contract. Save notes and reading position, confirm saving quietly, and never erase a response when switching views. Provide visible keyboard focus, semantic headings, properly labelled controls, 44px minimum touch targets, and layouts that reflow at 200% zoom. Theme follows the system initially, then respects a persistent manual choice. Theory tables scroll within their own region on phones. A4 printing uses white paper, black text, 18mm margins, 11pt Georgia, repeated table headings, and controlled page breaks; navigation, controls, and the companion disappear.

House the owner’s painted figure in a reserved lower corner of the navigation rail, on an untextured neutral backing. Preserve her original pixels, proportions, and colours: no tint, crop, outline, filter, or drawing over her. Provide a quiet hide control. On focused reading screens and phones, her compartment starts collapsed. Existing poses may change at meaningful session boundaries, without animation. She witnesses the work; she does not celebrate a correct answer while the student is reading its explanation.

Allow only brief, 120ms colour transitions for control feedback, removed under reduced-motion preferences. There is no animated page entrance, answer movement, scrolling intervention, or ticking clock animation. Study sessions show the current work or break phase and elapsed time; live statistics sit behind an optional disclosure. Refuse streak pressure, confetti, leaderboards, emoji, decorative anatomy, and faux antiquity. Implement the folio with ordinary HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and bundled local assets, with no framework, build step, package installation, or CDN: opening it from a USB stick must be enough.
