## Verdict
Best: k1, a conditional pass. Camera, coast, lighthouse side, cloud-below all right. Weak points: palette too monochrome blue (not the navy-and-amber of the references), and clouds too cartoon-curly.
Prompt/post fixes if redone: ask for warmer amber on the lamps and a faint amber haze on the far coast; clouds as soft broad gouache masses, less curled-wave shapes; sky indigo with visible stroke direction like shot2; stars as plain dots only; lamps varied in spacing and size, clustered toward the lighthouse side.
Cheap post step on k1: warm-lift the lamps and grade the clouds toward shot2's lavender-grey. Do not edit the source image; make a copy.
