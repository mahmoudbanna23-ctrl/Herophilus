## Design

### The dive — three offline shots, scroll picks the frame

| Shot | Camera | Join to next |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Aerial approach | High over coast and sea, drifting down | Into cloud |
| 2 Lighthouse pass | Down past the tower | Into cloud / mist |
| 3 Landing | Settles over the city, four buildings in view | Ends on the resting still |

Same travel direction, apparent speed, horizon and light across joins. One join is tested before
all shots are made. Shot 3 is composed from the start for both uses: end of the scroll dive and
the two-second daily landing.

**Making a shot (Blender 5.2 headless, ffmpeg):**
1. Camera move and framing are decided first, for landscape **and** upright iPad, with a safe
   area that keeps the four buildings in both. Upright gets its own 1024x1536 plate.
2. Codex image seat paints the plate for the end framing.
3. A vision seat reports percent boxes of the main masses; a builder places simple solids
   (tower, quay, building blocks) and projects the painting on them; far scenery stays a card.
   Mist volumes between depths. No simulated water at first.
4. Each camera move stays modest — only as far as the projection holds. The kill test measures
   how far that is.
5. Render with Eevee; ffmpeg packs WebP. Start at 1280x800 landscape, 800x1152 upright; 48 frames
   for shot 3, about 120 for all three. Raise only after measuring visible stepping and bytes.
6. Dawn = the night plates repainted as dawn with the existing light-plate script
   (`paint-room-light.sh`), then the same scene and camera re-rendered. Not a lighting change.

**Playing it (`city.js`):** one canvas, one `apply(progress)`. Frames load through `Image` +
`decode()` (the pattern in `rooms.js`); the wanted frame has priority, stale requests are
dropped, images outside a small window are released. When the film stops, a separately rendered
sharp still replaces the canvas; a light low-size still paints first so the page is usable at
once. Buildings are percent hot-spots over the still, each with a real text label and progress,
Tab-reachable.

| Visitor | What plays |
|---|---|
| Has answered no question yet (`done==0`, `render.js:176-186` — this is "not started", not "first visit") | Starts in the sky; scroll plays all three shots; "Skip" is the first Tab stop |
| Any other page load | Shot 3 on a timer (~2 s, eased), once per load, **only if its frames are decoded within ~1 s**; otherwise skipped for that load. Input is held for that window only. Any key or click skips. |
| Back from another screen | Resting still |
| Reduced motion / phone | Resting still |

No speculative download of shots 1-2 for students who never scroll up.

**Alive at rest (city and rooms only):** two small transparent mist textures drifting by
transform and cross-fading by opacity, plus one static lighthouse glow whose opacity breathes
slowly. No sweeping beam, no water highlights unless a later test looks right. Unmounted on study
views and under reduced motion.

### Inside a building — a room with things to pick

Clip-path iris from the building onto the room plate. Four purpose-composed painted objects, each
a labelled hot-spot (label always visible):

| Object | Opens |
|---|---|
| Shelf of scrolls | Lessons: chapters with theory |
| Writing desk | Practise: chapters with counts and accuracy, "Practise all" |
| Sand-glass on a stand (never the mascot's shape) | Timed exam |
| Chest | Weak spots, review due, flagged |

Picking one opens a `sheet` growing from the object by clip-path, holding a plain scrolling
ledger. Keyboard: four buttons in order. The four approved room plates are kept only if a looker
finds these objects clear at all three sizes; otherwise repainted with the approved plate as
reference (`EXTRA_REF`). `rooms.js` today supplies backgrounds only — its loader is reused, the
object layer is new.

### Quiet screens, shared parts, navigation, mascot

Seven parts in `ui.css`: `stage` (plate + veil) · `folio` (text column) · `row` (hairline ledger
row) · `rule` (scaleX hairline) · `act` (lettered action) · `sheet` · `plaque` (results numeral).
Quiz options are lettered rows; selection draws a gold rule; right/wrong carry a mark, never
colour alone. Room plate behind is dimmed and still.

Slim top bar (wordmark = back to city, search, theme, backup, menu); `#rail` becomes an
off-canvas drawer at every width via `toggleRail`/`closeRail` (`boot.js:303-304`); ids `nav`,
`railTerm`, `railProf`, `streakBox`, `railSync`, `navSessClock` kept. Mascot stands in the city
and each room with one line from existing state (review due, weakest chapter, today's session).

## Risks, cheapest test first

1. Projected paint over blind-fitted solids reads as cardboard or cheap CGI → slice 1, twelve
   frames, before anything else.
2. The film passes but the app still feels like the old one → slice 3 is the whole route, not the
   film alone.
3. Upright iPad crop loses the buildings → framing and safe area fixed in slice 2.
4. Cloud joins show as cuts → one join proved before shots 1-2 are finished.
5. Slow connections and iPad decode → light still first, one attempt inside a deadline, capped
   loading; fallback is fewer or smaller frames.
6. Dawn and upright each double the film → last, after the night landscape route is approved.
7. Existing rooms lack clear objects → looker check before any repaint.
8. Markup rewrites break hidden hooks → hook list + check scripts each slice.
