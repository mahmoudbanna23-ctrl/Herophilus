The same stone room inside the lighthouse at night, the same arched window in the back wall
showing the night harbour, the same wall lamps, the same camera. THREE changes.

1. FOUR OBJECTS ARE ADDED. Each is large, complete, standing on its own with clear empty floor or
   wall around it, lit by its own small warm lamp, and recognisable in one glance. None overlaps
   another. They are the four things a visitor can pick, so they are the stars of the room:
   - BACK WALL, LEFT of the window: A TALL WOODEN SHELF OF SCROLLS. Open pigeon-hole shelving
     from floor to above head height, every hole holding rolled papyrus scrolls, their round ends
     facing us, a few with hanging ribbon ties.
   - FRONT LEFT, on the floor: A WRITING DESK. A sturdy low wooden table seen at three-quarter
     angle, with one open papyrus sheet, a reed pen, an ink pot and a small oil lamp on it, and a
     simple stool beside it.
   - BACK WALL, RIGHT of the window: A SAND-GLASS ON A STAND. A large hourglass, two clear glass
     bulbs joined at a narrow neck, pale sand running, held in a turned wooden frame with three
     posts, on a waist-high stone pedestal.
   - FRONT RIGHT, on the floor: A CHEST. A big wooden chest with bronze bands and a curved lid,
     closed, a small lamp on the floor beside it.
2. THE OLD ORNAMENTS STEP BACK. The round brass mirrors and glass spheres stay only as a few small
   quiet pieces high on the top shelf and at the far side walls. They must not compete with the
   four objects, and no glass sphere stands anywhere near the sand-glass.
3. THE WHOLE ROOM IS REPAINTED BY HAND IN A MATTE FINISH. See the style notes below. The floor is
   dry worn stone, not a polished mirror.

The middle of the floor, in front of the window, stays completely empty and calm. Nothing
important within the outer tenth of the picture on any side.



STYLE. A hand-painted film matte painting in gouache and oil: MATTE, dry, chalky surfaces,
visible confident brushstrokes, soft broken edges, slightly uneven hand-mixed colour. NOT glossy,
NOT shiny, NOT wet-looking, NOT plastic, no specular highlights, no bloom, no glow halos, no
computer-game or 3D-render look, not photographic, not flat vector. Rich in small believable
detail (worn stone edges, wood grain, frayed papyrus), painted loosely rather than modelled.

SETTING. A room inside the great lighthouse of ancient Alexandria, about 300 BC, at night. Seen
from standing height, straight on. Normal lens, no fisheye, no tilt: vertical lines stay vertical.

LIGHT. Moonlight through the window and small oil lamps only. Cool night-blue shadows (deep blue,
never brown, never pure black, every surface keeps visible detail) with small warm amber pools of
lamp light, painted as soft dabs of warm colour, not as glowing bulbs.

HARD RULES.
- No text of any kind: no letters, numerals, inscriptions, labels, or writing on the scrolls or
  the papyrus sheet.
- No people, no figures, no statues of people, no faces, no hands, no living animals.
- No frame, no border, no vignette, no watermark, no signature, no neon, no lens flare.
- No modern objects: nothing electric, printed, plastic or machine-made. No books with spines:
  scrolls only.
- Exactly ONE shelf of scrolls, ONE desk, ONE sand-glass, ONE chest.
