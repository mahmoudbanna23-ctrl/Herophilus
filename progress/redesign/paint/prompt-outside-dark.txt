
The Library of Alexandria seen from OUTSIDE, evening, seen straight-on from the foot of the steps,
perfectly symmetric about the centre line. The building facade fills the whole picture.

A wide flight of broad stone steps rises across the full width of the lower picture to a terrace.
In the exact centre of the facade stands ONE large central doorway: a plain opening with a simple
round-arched top, straight vertical sides and no door leaf. The doorway is about 24% of the picture's
width and about 48% of its height; its foot rests on the top step at about 80% of the way down the
picture. The straight vertical sides run up to about 62% of the doorway's height, then the round arch
begins.

Inside the opening there is ONLY the soft warm amber glow of the hall beyond: a smooth, calm,
glowing gradient of lamplight with no readable detail, no shelves, no objects, no shapes.

On each side of the doorway stand tall stone columns with simple capitals, evenly spaced and
symmetric, and lit bronze oil lamps on the columns and steps whose warm glow falls on the stone.
Above the doorway runs a calm, plain, unadorned stone pediment and, above that, a deep evening sky
(deep blue to warm violet and amber near the horizon). The top fifth of the picture is calm: only
sky and plain, smooth stone pediment, with no ornament, so a line of text can sit on it.

Light: evening sky light plus oil-lamp light. Deep warm brown shadows between the columns, but never
murky and never pure black: every surface keeps visible detail. Amber, bronze, honey, umber and a
deep blue sky. Clear and rich.

STYLE. The same painted world as the style reference image (the first image): the same stone, the
same warm palette and the same finish. Smooth, glossy, soft-lit, hand-painted 3D finish, calm and
cinematic, rich in small believable detail (worn stone edges, grain, dust on the steps). Painterly,
never photographic, never flat vector.

HARD RULES.
- No text of any kind: no letters, numerals, inscriptions, labels, signage or carved writing anywhere.
- No people, no figures, no statues of people, no faces, no hands, no animals that are alive.
- No clock faces or round dials.
- No neon, no lens flare, no frame, no border, no vignette frame, no watermark, no signature.
- Straight-on view: the camera faces the facade squarely, eye level, vertical lines stay vertical.
- The doorway is fully visible, nothing cropped, and nothing stands in front of it.
