
A reading hall inside the ancient Library of Alexandria, late morning, seen straight-on from the
middle of the floor. Upright picture. The far wall fills the whole picture and is a two-storey
gallery.

The far wall holds FOUR arched alcoves arranged as TWO rows of TWO (a 2 x 2 grid): identical size,
identical gaps, perfectly symmetric about the centre line, all four fully visible with a clear
margin of wall on every side. A plain stone string course separates the two storeys.

Each alcove, top to bottom:
1. a round arch;
2. a small unlit bronze oil lamp hanging in the arch;
3. the upper half: pigeonhole shelves packed with papyrus scrolls;
4. at mid height a stone ledge carrying ONE object, clearly lit;
5. the lower third: a plain, smooth, empty stone panel in mid-tone sand, evenly lit, with no
   carving, no object and no pattern. It must stay empty — a label is added later in code.

The four objects:
- top left: a bronze ear trumpet (a flared listening horn) lying beside a small bronze speculum;
- top right: a clear crystal reading sphere on a small bronze stand, catching the sun;
- bottom left: a carved white marble anatomical model of a human brain on a low plinth;
- bottom right: a child's painted wooden toy horse on four wheels beside a small terracotta
  feeding cup.

Above the upper arches runs a plain band of calm, unadorned stone (about the top 12% of the
picture). Below the lower alcoves is a polished pale stone floor (about the bottom 8%).
There is NOTHING in the near foreground: no columns, no furniture, no objects in front of the wall.

Light: golden daylight pouring diagonally from high windows that are out of frame at the upper
left; a few soft visible shafts of light crossing the wall. Warm cream limestone, honey and pale
gold. Bright and airy but never washed out: no blown white areas, full detail in the highlights.



STYLE. The same painted world as the style reference image: smooth, glossy, soft-lit, hand-painted
