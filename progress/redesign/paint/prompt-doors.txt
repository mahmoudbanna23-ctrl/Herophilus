ONE pair of tall bronze double doors from the entrance of an ancient Greek library in Alexandria,
SHUT, painted in the same rich, hand-painted, cinematic finish as the style reference: aged dark
bronze with warm brass highlights, verdigris in the recesses, lit softly from the front by warm
evening lamp light.

Layout, strict:
- Dead front-on, orthographic, no perspective, no tilt. The doors fill the picture from edge to
  edge: the outer left edge of the left leaf touches the left side of the picture, the outer
  right edge of the right leaf touches the right side, the bottom edge sits on the bottom.
- The top is a single round arch spanning both leaves: straight sides for the lower three
  quarters, then one smooth semicircular curve across the full width at the top.
- Exactly two leaves, mirror images of each other, meeting at one clear, dark, perfectly vertical
  seam in the exact centre of the picture, running from the bottom to the top of the arch.
- Each leaf: a few large recessed panels framed by a raised border, a band of Greek key (meander)
  pattern along the border, rows of round bronze studs, and one heavy ring handle near the seam at
  mid height. No lettering of any kind.
- Flat, even lighting across both leaves so they can later be brightened or darkened as a whole.

Background: everything outside the arched door shape fully transparent (alpha) if you can produce
it. If you cannot, a perfectly flat, uniform, single colour, cool light grey-blue (#B8C4D6), with
no gradient, no texture and NO cast shadow or glow on the background.

Do not include: a wall, a door frame, columns, steps, a floor, a threshold, people, hands, torches,
text, labels, numbers, a border, or any object other than the two door leaves.
