# Room paintings — prompts

`paint-room.sh <section>` builds `prompt-<section>.txt` from the section `## <section>` plus
`## common-room`. One room per subject, each grown from the object in that subject's hall alcove
(hall prompt, `prompts.md` section `hall-dark`, "The four objects"): ear trumpet and speculum, crystal
reading sphere, marble brain model, wooden toy horse and terracotta cup. Landscape 1536x1024.

## common-room

STYLE. The same painted world as the style reference image: smooth, glossy, soft-lit, hand-painted
3D finish, calm and cinematic, rich in small believable detail (worn stone edges, individual scroll
ends, grain, dust on ledges). Painterly, never photographic, never flat vector.

SETTING. Ancient Alexandria, about 300 BC, at night, seen straight-on from the middle of the floor
of one room. The room has real depth: the eye travels toward a far wall or a far opening, with
stone floor and side walls receding in clear perspective. Vertical lines stay vertical, eye level.

LIGHT. Oil-lamp light only. Cool night-blue shadows (deep blue, never brown, never pure black, every
surface keeps visible detail) with small warm amber pools of lamp light. The room reads as a blue
night with a few warm lamps, not a brown interior.

COMPOSITION, FIRM RULE. Page text will be laid over the centre and the lower half, so those must be
QUIET and darker: smooth plain floor and plain dark wall, no objects, no bright lamps, no strong
detail there. All detail, objects and light sources live at the TOP of the picture and along the
LEFT and RIGHT edges.

HARD RULES.
- No text of any kind: no letters, numerals, inscriptions, labels, signage or carved writing.
- No people, no figures, no statues of people, no faces, no hands, no animals that are alive.
- No frame, no border, no vignette, no watermark, no signature, no neon, no lens flare.
- No modern objects: nothing electric, glass-bottled, printed, plastic or machine-made.
- No medical scenes, no anatomy, nothing gory.

## room-ent-dark

A room of sound. Along the left and right walls, shelves and brackets hold large bronze listening
horns of different sizes, their flared mouths turned toward the middle, with rows of narrow clay
sounding jars set in wall niches above them. A few small bronze lamps hang from the upper wall, each
throwing a warm amber pool on the horns nearest it. A long bronze speculum lies on a ledge at one
side. The far wall is a plain dark stone arch opening onto a faint blue night passage. The centre of
the floor and the whole lower half are smooth, empty, dark blue-grey stone.

## room-ophtho-dark

A lantern room of light. Along the left and right walls, tall bronze stands hold polished round
bronze mirrors and clear crystal spheres, each catching a small warm reflection of the lamps. Small
bronze oil lamps hang high on the side walls and from the ceiling beams near the top of the picture,
and a row of glass globes on a high shelf glints along the upper far wall. The far wall is plain dark
stone with a single quiet arched window of deep blue night. The centre of the floor and the whole
lower half are smooth, empty, dark blue-grey polished stone.

## room-neuropsych-dark

A scroll reading room. Floor-to-ceiling pigeonhole shelves packed with papyrus scrolls line the left
and right walls, in blue shadow, individual scroll ends readable. Near the top, a bronze armillary
star sphere hangs from the ceiling beams on a chain, and small bronze lamps hang beside it, casting
warm amber pools on the upper shelves. A small plain white marble sphere rests on the highest shelf
in the top left corner. The far wall is plain dark stone with a small high arched opening onto a
starry night sky, set in the upper third. Nothing stands on the floor anywhere: no plinth, no table,
no jugs, no vases, no furniture. The centre of the floor and the whole lower half are smooth, empty,
dark blue-grey stone, and the lower shelves fade into deep blue shadow.

## room-pediatrics-dark

A small courtyard at night, open to the sky at the top, where stars show above a dark blue band of
sky. Low stone arcades with clay roof tiles line the left and right edges, hung with small bronze
lamps whose warm amber glow falls on a painted wooden toy horse on four wheels, a few clay toys
and small terracotta cups on the arcade ledges. A round stone fountain stands far back against the
distant wall, its water a soft pale blue ribbon. The centre of the paving and the whole lower half
are smooth, empty, dark blue-grey stone, with no toys or plants there.

## common-room-light

TASK. The attached reference image is THE SAME ROOM. Repaint exactly that room: same walls, same
objects in the same places, same camera and perspective. Change only the light and the palette.

STYLE. The same painted world as the reference: smooth, glossy, soft-lit, hand-painted 3D finish,
calm and cinematic, rich in small believable detail (worn stone edges, individual scroll ends,
grain, dust on ledges). Painterly, never photographic, never flat vector.

LIGHT. Calm morning daylight in Ancient Alexandria, about 300 BC. All lamps are unlit. Soft cool
daylight enters from the far opening or the sky. Pale warm-grey limestone and sand stone, gentle
soft shadows, a light airy palette of parchment, cream and pale stone, like a bright morning hall
with dark text to be set on pale parchment.

COMPOSITION, FIRM RULE. Page text will be laid over the centre and the lower half, so those must
be QUIET, PALE and evenly lit: smooth plain pale floor and pale wall, no objects, no hard shadows,
no bright hotspots, no strong detail there. All detail and objects stay where the reference has
them, at the top and along the left and right edges.

HARD RULES.
- No text of any kind: no letters, numerals, inscriptions, labels, signage or carved writing.
- No people, no figures, no statues of people, no faces, no hands, no animals that are alive.
- No frame, no border, no vignette, no watermark, no signature, no neon, no lens flare.
- No modern objects: nothing electric, glass-bottled, printed, plastic or machine-made.
- No medical scenes, no anatomy, nothing gory.
