# Hall paintings — prompts

`paint.sh <name> <WxH>` builds `prompt-<name>.txt` from the section `## <name>` plus `## common`.
Daily cap on the picture maker is 8 (factory `staff.json`, seat `image`); five paintings are planned.

## common

STYLE. The same painted world as the style reference image: smooth, glossy, soft-lit, hand-painted
3D finish, warm palette, calm and cinematic, rich in small believable detail (worn stone edges,
individual scroll ends, grain, dust on ledges). Painterly, never photographic, never flat vector.

HARD RULES.
- No text of any kind: no letters, numerals, inscriptions, labels, signage or carved writing.
- No people, no figures, no statues of people, no faces, no hands, no animals that are alive.
- No neon, no lens flare, no frame, no border, no vignette frame, no watermark, no signature.
- Straight-on view: the camera faces the far wall squarely, eye level, vertical lines stay vertical.
- Nothing cropped by the picture edge that is described as "fully visible".

## hall-dark

A reading hall inside the ancient Library of Alexandria, evening, seen straight-on from the middle
of the floor. The far wall fills the whole picture.

The far wall holds FOUR tall arched alcoves in ONE evenly spaced row: identical size, identical
gaps, perfectly symmetric about the centre line, all four fully visible with a clear margin of wall
at the far left and far right. Slim engaged stone pilasters stand between the alcoves.

Each alcove, top to bottom:
1. a round arch;
2. a small bronze oil lamp hanging in the arch, its glow a warm amber pool on what is below;
3. the upper half: pigeonhole shelves packed with papyrus scrolls, in shadow, still readable;
4. at mid height a stone ledge carrying ONE object, clearly lit by the lamp;
5. the lower third: a plain, smooth, empty stone panel, evenly lit, mid-dark, with no carving,
   no object and no pattern. It must stay empty — a label is added later in code.

The four objects, left to right:
1. a bronze ear trumpet (a flared listening horn) lying beside a small bronze speculum;
2. a clear crystal reading sphere on a small bronze stand, catching the lamp light;
3. a carved white marble anatomical model of a human brain on a low plinth;
4. a child's painted wooden toy horse on four wheels beside a small terracotta feeding cup.

Above the arches runs a plain band of calm, unadorned stone (about the top 14% of the picture).
Below the alcoves is a polished dark stone floor with soft warm reflections (about the bottom 14%).
There is NOTHING in the near foreground: no columns, no furniture, no objects in front of the wall.

Light: oil-lamp light only. Deep warm brown shadows between the alcoves, but never murky and never
pure black — every surface keeps visible detail. Amber, bronze, honey and umber.

## fore

A cut-out foreground layer for a parallax scene: TWO massive stone columns of an ancient Alexandrian
hall and nothing else.

One column hugs the far LEFT edge of the picture and one hugs the far RIGHT edge. Each column is
about 12% of the picture width and runs the full height: a plain square base at the very bottom, a
smooth slightly tapered shaft, and a papyrus-bundle capital at the very top. The two columns are
mirror images of each other. They are close to the viewer and in deep shadow: dark warm umber stone,
with a thin soft warm rim of light along the inner edge of each (the edge facing the middle of the
picture).

Everything that is not a column must be FULLY TRANSPARENT (a PNG with an alpha channel): the whole
middle of the picture, between the columns, is empty transparent space. No wall, no floor, no
ceiling, no beam joining the columns, no shadow on the ground, no background colour.
If you cannot produce real transparency, fill everything that is not a column with one flat pure
magenta, exactly #FF00FF, with a hard clean edge and no magenta spill on the stone.
