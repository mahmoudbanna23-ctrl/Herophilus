0 · Cinzel + EB Garamond

HEROPHILUS

The Library of Alexandria

OPHTHALMOLOGY

36 chapters

A traveller reaches the harbour of a coastal town carrying three baskets. The first holds twice as many figs as the second, and the third holds as many as the first two together. Once the market opens, which basket should the clerk weigh first?

  1. The first basket, because it is the heaviest of the three.
  2. The second basket, because it is the lightest.
  3. The third basket, because it holds the most figs.
  4. Any basket, because the order does not change the total.

The harbour ledger records every cargo in a fixed order. First entries list the goods by weight, then by origin, and the clerk adds a short note in the margin whenever a figure was estimated rather than measured. Corrections are never erased; a second line is written beneath the first, so that a later reader can see both. When two ledgers disagree, the older one is checked against the original receipts before anything is changed.

Dose 5 µg/kg ± 0.5 at 37 °C, 2 × 10⁶ cells, pH ≤ 7.35 or ≥ 7.45, H₂O, 1st 2nd α β γ Ηρόφιλος
