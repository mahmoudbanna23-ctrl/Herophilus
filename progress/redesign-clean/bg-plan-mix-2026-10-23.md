# App background — the premium way (trial copy only)

## Context
Fourteen moving wallpapers tried, none felt right: liquid, nerves, stars, star map, smoke, statue, sea and the rest.
The likely reason: each one moved behind the whole app, all the time.
The 2026 research says premium sites do the opposite.

What the research found:
- **No verified premium site runs a moving picture behind all its content.** Heavy motion, such as 3D, video or scroll scenes, lives in one place: the top of the landing page.
- **Behind long reading or work, they go quiet.** Linear's redesign speaks of cutting "visual noise". Riot's game client swaps big videos for still images in low-spec mode and only animates cheap things.
- **Life comes from response, not idling.** A soft light that follows the cursor and scenes that move as you scroll. They react to you and stay still when you stop.
- **Culture sites show their theme through objects, type and a tight palette**, not ornament behind text. British Museum: objects on black. Rijksmuseum: its own typeface. An award site: two colours, off-white on black.
- **Rich colour gets a fine grain** so it looks like paper or film rather than plastic.
- **Accessibility rule (WCAG 2.2.2):** anything that moves on its own for more than 5 seconds needs a way to pause it. That applies to every wallpaper we tried.

Most named-site claims beyond those were unverified; the research said so.

## Owner's pick (2026-10-23): the mix — all three layers below
- Home scene: **nerves**, the looker's top pick. The **liquid** gets a second film so the owner can compare.
- The star map is dropped. Slowing it would change its timing, which breaks the copy rule.
- 5-second rule: a small plain-text pause control in the corner of the scene. It must not be a pill. The scene also stops once it has scrolled off screen.

## Direction: "one stage, quiet ground"
1. **Quiet ground everywhere.** A still, deep base colour with fine grain, plus one soft, unmoving warm light, like lamplight on stone. Same on every page, including quiz and reading. No idle motion at all.
2. **One stage, home page top only.** One living scene in the top band of home, behind the title. Picks from what was already filmed: the nerves, the Greek star map (slowed), or the flowing liquid. It fades away as you scroll down, then stops. The 5-second rule is met either by a small pause control or by the scene settling still after its entrance. The owner chooses which.
3. **Life by response.** A faint pattern under the whole ground, such as a meander or grid, shown only where the cursor is, as a soft spotlight. It needs a published open-licence piece. If none is found that copies cleanly, it is dropped, not hand-made.
4. **Theme through the content, not the wallpaper.** Greek feel carried by the title type, the subject colours and the Clepsydra. The Clepsydra is untouched, as always.
