8. Which fibers are most resistant to glaucomatous damage?
a. Papillomacular bundle
b. Superior arcuate fibers
c. Inferior arcuate fibers
In glaucoma, the superior and inferior arcuate fibers are damaged first, leading to visual field loss in
the corresponding areas. The papillomacular bundle, which carries fibers from the macula to the
optic disc (central vision), is most resistant to glaucomatous damage and usually affected only in
late stages.
