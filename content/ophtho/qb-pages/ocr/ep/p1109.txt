Differential Diagnosis
For corneal clouding / opacity:
•Birth trauma (forceps injury Descemet tears: vertical/oblique)
•Congenital anomalies: Sclerocornea
•Intrauterine infection (TORCH keratitis)
•Inborn errors of metabolism (Mucopolysaccharidoses)
•Corneal dystrophies (Congenital hereditary endothelial dystrophy)
For epiphora:
•Nasolacrimal duct obstruction
•Conjunctivitis
•Corneal abrasion
•Keratitis
.uveitis
For corneal enlargement:
•High axial myopia
•Megalocornea (enlarged clear cornea with normal IOP)
