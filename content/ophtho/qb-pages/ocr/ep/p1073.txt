Treatment of Strabismus
Goal
Equalize vision & promote fusion
Correct refractive errors
Surgical correction
Paralytic strabismus
Method
Treat amblyopia (patching the good eye)
Glasses for hypermetropia/myopia
Indicated in constant deviations or
unresponsive cases
Observe for 6 months (spontaneous recovery
possible); surgery if persistent
