15. What is the grade of chemical eye Injury that is characterized by total loss
of corneal epithelium and stromal haze obscuring iris details?
A. Grade 1
B. Grade 2
C. Grade 3
D. Grade 4
Grading of chemical eye injuries -5
A. Grade 1: (excellent prognosis): Clear cornea (epithelial damage only) and no
limbal ischaemia.
B. Grade 2: (good prognosis): Hazy cornea but with visible iris detail and less
than 30% limbal ischaemia.
C. Grade 3: (guarded prognosis):
Total loss Of corneal epithelium.
Stromal haze obscuring iris detail.
1/3 to 1/2 limbal ischaemia.
D. Grade 4: (poor prognosis):
Opaque cornea.
More than 50% limbal ischaemia.
