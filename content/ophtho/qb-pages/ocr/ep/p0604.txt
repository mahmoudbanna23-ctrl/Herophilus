12. Which of the following is used to detect corneal abrasions?
A. Flourescine dye
B. Steroids
C. Pachymetry
D. None of the above
Corneal epithelial defects (e.g. abrasions) are demonstrated by
staining with 1% fluorescein sodium and viewing with cobalt
blue filtered light.
