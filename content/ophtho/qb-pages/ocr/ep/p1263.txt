(6) Multiple Sclerosis (MS)
Ocular Manifestations
•Optic neuritis blurred vision, color vision loss (most common)
•RAPD (Relative afferent pupillary defect)
•Diplopia, nystagmus, ptosis
•Optic atrophy
•Central scotoma (damage to macular fibers)
Diagnosis
•MRI: Demyelinating plaques in white matter of brain/brainstem
