appropriate management for that child?
A. Spectacles
B. Refractive surgery
C. Full cycloplegic correction with eye patching
D. Eye patching
Treatment of amblyopia
Treatment of the cause: spectacles for anisometropia, treat media opacity or
ptosis... etc.
Occlusion Therapy: Patching the good eye to force the brain to use the non-
dominant eye and redevelop ts vision.
Atropine cycloplegic drops to impair accommodation and blur vision of the
better-seeing eye.
