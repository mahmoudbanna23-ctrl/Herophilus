I l. A 12-year-old child presents with acute onset of red eye, watery discharge,
and photophobia. On examination, the cornea shows punctate epithelial lesions.
What is the most likely corneal involvement in this condition?
a. Shield ulcers
b. Punctate epithelial keratitis
c. Geographic ulcer
d. Corneal neovascularization
Adenoviral keratoconjunctivitis can involve the cornea, producing
punctate epithelial lesions. These small epithelial defects cause
photophobia and blurred vision.
