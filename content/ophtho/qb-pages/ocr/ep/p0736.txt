Sympathetic Ophthalmia (SO)
Definition:
A bilateral granulomatous panuveitis occurring after penetrating trauma or intraocular
surgery to one eye (exciting eye), followed by involvement of the fellow (sympathizing)
eye.
4 Pathophysiology:
•Autoimmune sensitization to melanin or melanin-associated proteins in uveal tissue after
trauma exposes them to the immune system.
Q Clinical Findings:
•Bilateral granulomatous uveitis
•Iris nodules
•Mutton-fat keratic precipitates
•Disc edema
• Choroiditis
•May lead to vision loss if untreated
