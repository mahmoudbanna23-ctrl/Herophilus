Zn 7. Intraocular Pressure (IOP) Measurement
Normal IOP: 10—21 mmHg
Depends on: Aqueous humor outflow resistance.
Methods:
I.Digital Palpation:
• For rough estimation (very high or very low pressures).
Patient looks down, eyelids closed.
'Alternate gentle finger pressure through upper eyelid.
'Compare both eyes.
Normal: moderately firm; Hard TIOP.
2.Schiotz (Indentation) Tonometry:
'Simple, inexpensive, no power source.
Measures indentation (not direct pressure).
• Heavier weights confirm readings using conversion chart.
Higher IOP less indentation lower scale reading.
