Amaurosis Fugax
•Transient monocular blindness (minutes).
•Embolic in nature (carotid or cardiac source).
•May indicate TIA or risk of stroke.
•Investigate: carotid Doppler, echocardiography, ESR/CRP, lipid profile.
•Treat cause antiplatelets, anticoagulants, ± carotid endarterectomy.
