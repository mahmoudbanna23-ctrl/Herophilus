Cellulitis
9. Pre-septal (Periorbital) Cellulitis
• Children: H. influenzae
'Adults: Staphylococcus aureus / Streptococcus
Treatment: Systemic antibiotics (e.g., amoxicillin-clavulanic acid)
10. Orbital Cellulitis
Requires hospital admission
• Investigations: Blood cultures, orbital CT
Treatment: IV antibiotics (ceftriaxone + vancomycin) ± oral metronidazole for 1 week
'Surgical drainage of abscess if necessary
11. Complications
• Vision loss (NII%)
'Optic nerve inflammation
'Cavernous sinus thrombosis
Meningitis / brain abscess
• Death if untreated
