4. The axial length of the eye can be measured by:
a. Keratometry
b. Exophthalmometry
c. Ophthalmoscopy
d. A scan ultrasound
A-scan ultrasonography measures the eye's axial length,
essential for calculating intraocular lens power before cataract
surgery.
