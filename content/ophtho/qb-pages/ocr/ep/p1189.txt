Main Ocular Findings
Orbit
•Retrobulbar hemorrhage emergency
• Pain, proptosis, Uvision, RAPD
• Treatment: Lateral canthotomy + cantholysis within 2 h
• Medical: acetazolamide
• Aim decompress orbit, prevent optic nerve ischemia
Lids
•Lacerations: repair respecting margin, canaliculi if involved
Conjunctiva
•Laceration or subconjunctival hemorrhage
•Large hemorrhage + low IOP suspect hidden scleral rupture
Cornea
•Abrasion (fluorescein + cobalt blue)
•Laceration shallow AC, iris prolapse
•Endothelial injury edema
