4. Paediatric Cataract
Definition: Lens opacity in childhood.
A. Congenital Cataract
•Present at birth; % associated with systemic (genetic/metabolic) disease.
•Types:
• Not visually significant: Blue dot, sutural cataract no treatment.
• Visually significant: Lamellar, dense total surgery needed.
Clinical features: Leukocoria, poor fixation, or squint.
Complications if untreated:
•Unilateral sensory squint
•Bilateral nystagmus & poor vision
Treatment:
•Early surgical removal of cataractous lens
•± IOL implantation (primary or secondary)
•Post-op spectacles/contact lenses + amblyopia therapy
•Evaluate for systemic/metabolic disorders
B. Developmental Cataract
•Appears later in childhood (not present at birth).
