Blepharitis Inflammation of lid margin + itching + crusts + burning sensation.
Types:
•Seborrheic greasy scales.
•Staphylococcal collarettes, phlyctenules, poliosis.
•Posterior (Meibomianitis) foamy discharge, unstable tear film.
Rx = Lid hygiene (warm compresses + diluted baby shampoo scrubs).
Ptosis = Drooping of upper eyelid covering >2 mm of superior limbus.
Types:
•Congenital smooth lid + absent skin fold.
•Myogenic (Myasthenia gravis) asymmetric, at end of day.
•Aponeurotic (senile) good levator function.
•Pseudoptosis due to dermatochalasis (redundant skin).
MC cause of upper lid retraction = Thyroid eye disease.
Lid retraction scleral show.
