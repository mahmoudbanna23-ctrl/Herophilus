2. Hypermetropia (Far-sightedness)
Definition: Rays focus behind retina.
Far point: virtual, behind the eye.
Causes:
•Short axial length (most common)
•Flat cornea (U curvature)
•U RI of lens
•Aphakia
Symptoms:
Age
Young
Middle
Manifestation
Eye strain (asthenopia), headache, blurred near vision
Early presbyopia
Blurred near & far vision
