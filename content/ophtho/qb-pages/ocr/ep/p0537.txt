2. To differentiate between scleritis and episcleritis, which test is used?
a. Phenylephrine 100/0
b. Fluorescein staining
c. Schirmer test
d. Tonometry
In episcleritis, the superficial vessels blanch with phenylephrine drops, while in
scleritis, deeper vessels do not. This test helps distinguish between the two
inflammatory conditions.
