Anterior Segment Anatomy
1.Anterior chamber: Lies between the back surface of the cornea anteriorly and the iris posteriorly.
2.Posterior chamber: Lies between the back surface of the iris anteriorly and the crystalline lens
posteriorly.
3.1ris: The coloured portion of the eye.
4.Pupil: Jet-black in the newborn; gradually acquires a greyish tinge with age due to senile sclerosis.
Without the lens, it regains its jet-black color.
5.Ciliary body: Functions (3 AS):
Accommodation via contraction/relaxation of ciliary muscle transmitted to lens via zonules.
Aqueous humor secretion.
Attachment to zonules, keeping the lens in place.
6. Ciliary muscle & lens shape:
'Relaxed muscle zonules tense lens flattens far vision.
•contracted muscle zonules relaxed lens more convex -+ near vision.
