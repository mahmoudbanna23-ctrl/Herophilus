4. Retrobulbar Hemorrhage
•Can cause:
• Optic neuropathy
• CRAO/CRVO
•Diagnosis: Clinical (no orbital imaging required initially)
•Management: Within 2 hours to prevent permanent visual loss
5. Traumatic Optic Neuropathy
•Urgent treatment: high-dose IV methylprednisolone
