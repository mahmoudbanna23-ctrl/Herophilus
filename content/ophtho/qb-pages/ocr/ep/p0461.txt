9. A 35-year-old man complains of chronic bilateral eye redness, intense
itching, and watery discharge. On examination, you find small papillae on the
tarsal conjunctiva and red, scaly, dry eyelids. What is the most likely diagnosis?
a. Atopic keratoconjunctivitis
b. Vemal keratoconjunctivitis
c. Bacterial conjunctivitis
d. Allergic conjunctivitis
Atopic keratoconjunctivitis affects Older children or adults with
atopy and shows small papillae with red, scaly eyelids. It is
chronic and not seasonal, unlike vernal conjunctivitis.
