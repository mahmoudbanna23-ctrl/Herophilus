Primary Congenital Glaucoma (PCG)
•Cause: Abnormalities in trabecular meshwork & anterior chamber angle
•Complications:
• Buphthalmos (increased IOP)
• Vision loss due to corneal scarring, optic nerve damage, amblyopia
•Management: Surgical intervention required
