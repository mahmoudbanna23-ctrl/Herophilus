Clinical Features
2. Generalized Muscle Weakness
•Weakness and fatigue of other skeletal muscles — worsens with effort, improves with
rest
•Commonly involves:
• Facial muscles "myopathic facies" (expressionless face + ptosis)
• Bulbar muscles dysphagia, dysarthria, difficulty chewing
• Respiratory muscles (rare but serious) myasthenic crisis
• Limb muscles (especially proximal) weakness in arms and legs
3. Course of the Disease
•Fluctuating symptoms with remissions and exacerbations
•Symptoms progress over a few years from ocular to generalized weakness
