6. Accommodation of the eye can be achieved by:
a. Changing of the anterior-posterior diameter of the eye
b. Ciliary muscle contraction
c. Changing the curvature of the cornea
d. Changing the depth of the anterior chamber
Accommodation occurs when the ciliary muscle contracts,
releasing tension on the zonules, allowing the lens to become
more convex for near vision.
