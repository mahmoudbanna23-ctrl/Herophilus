6. A 55-year-old patient presents with gradual, painless diminution ofvision.
Examination reveals an intraocular pressure of 31 mmHg. Fundoscopy shows
optic disc cupping. What is the most appropriate management for this
condition?
a. Trabeculotomy
b. Trabeculectomy
c. Goniotomy
d. Laser peripheral iridotomy
Open-angle glaucoma with high intraocular pressure and optic
disc cupping is treated surgically by trabeculectomy, which
creates a new drainage pathway. It lowers IOP to prevent
further optic nerve damage.
