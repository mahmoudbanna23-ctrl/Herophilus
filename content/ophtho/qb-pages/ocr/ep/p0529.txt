Scleritis
Treatment
•Urgent referral (vision-threatening).
•Oral NSAIDs steroids immunosuppressants (methotrexate, cyclophosphamide, etc.).
•Surgery if scleral thinning or perforation.
Prognosis
•Mild/moderate good vision.
•Necrotizing high risk of vision loss & mortality.
