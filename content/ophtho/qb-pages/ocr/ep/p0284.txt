Fast Important Notes
2. Dry Eye Disease
•Causes:
• Aqueous tear deficiency
• Evaporative dry eye disease
• Ora combination of both
•Special cause: Thyroid eye disease can cause both types.
• Clinical picture:
• Symptoms: FB sensation, burning, itching, redness, reflex tearing, discharge, blurred
vlslon.
• Signs: Decreased TWT seconds, punctate epithelial erosions on fluorescein
staining.
•Treatment:
• Medical: Artificial tears, cyclosporine A 0.05% eye drops, topical steroids.
• Interventional: Punctal plugs, lid taping overnight, lateral tarsorrhaphy.
• Cause-specific: e.g., treat blepharitis.
