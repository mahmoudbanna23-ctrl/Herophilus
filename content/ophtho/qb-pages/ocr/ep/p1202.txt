Quick Notes
6. Globe Rupture / Penetrating Injuries
•Globe rupture:
Occurs at weakest point, not necessarily site of impact
Poor prognosis if initial VA S light perception
•Penetrating vs perforating:
Penetrating: 1 wound
Perforating: 2 wounds
•Demographics:
Males 3x more common
• Age: 21—40 years
•Intraocular foreign body (IOFB):
Most common: metallic & magnetic
• Posterior segment most affected
• Iron siderosis
Copper chalcosis
• Lens opacity: usually in periphery
•Imaging:
• MRI contraindicated if suspected metallic FB
