8. Which of the following is the area mostly affected by dryness in case of
inability to completely close the eyelids?
A. Middle 1/3 of the cornea
B. Upper 1/3 of the cornea
C. Lower 1/3 of the cornea
D. The whole cornea
Early EK is characterized by corneal dryness and superficial punctate
keratopathy (show punctate epithelial erosions in EK staining upon
administration of fluorescein dye) most commonly localized to the
inferior one-third of the cornea in cases of lagophthalmos.
