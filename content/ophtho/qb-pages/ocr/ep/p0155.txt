Astigmatism
No single focus point; instead two focal lines are formed.
Regular astigmatism (most common): uniform curvature, usually corneal.
Irregular astigmatism: due to keratoconus or corneal scars.
Types:
Simple: one meridian normal, one myopic/hypermetropic.
Compound: both myopic or both hypermetropic.
Mixed: one myopic, one hypermetropic.
Management:
Regular soft toric (spherocylindrical) lenses or refractive surgery.
Irregular rigid gas-permeable (RGP) lenses, intracorneal ring (ICR), or keratoplasty.
