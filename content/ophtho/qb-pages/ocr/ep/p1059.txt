23. 45-year-old woman comes to the emergency center because of severe left
sided headache and double vision that began the night before, immediately
following vigorous exercise. As you examine her, she continues to complain of
severe left-sided headache. Her neuro-ophthalmologic examination is normal,
except that the left upper eyelid is ptotic; and the left pupil is dilated 3 mm
more than the right and responds very poorly to light. Her motility looks
grossly normal. What is your diagnosis?
A. Myasthenia gravis
B. Optic nerve disease
C. Third nerve palsy
D. Diabetic neuropathy
Ptosis with a dilated, poorly reactive pupil indicates
parasympathetic involvement of the oculomotor (Ill) nerve.
The normal motility suggests partial or early third nerve palsy,
often due to aneurysm or vascular compression.
