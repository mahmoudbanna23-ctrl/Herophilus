5. The normal range for IOP as measured by applanation tonometry is:
a. 10-21 mmHg
b. 16 mmHg
c. 12-17 mmHg
d. 8-17 mmHg
The normal intraocular pressure (IOP) measured by
applanation tonometry ranges between 10 and 21 mmHg.
Values outside this range may indicate glaucoma or ocular
hypotony.
