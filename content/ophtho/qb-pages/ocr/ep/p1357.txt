7. Which of the following is an adverse effect of local cycloplegics?
A. Yellowish discoloration of skin
B. Comeal ulceration
C. Delirium
D. None of the above
Systemic toxicity of cycloplegics: (especially in children) causes the following:
1. Increased body temperature
2. Flushing (reddening) of skin
3. Dryness of skin and mouth
4. Irregular rapid pulse
5. Hallucinations, confusion, and delirium
