Astigmatism
13.Definition: Parallel rays focus as two focal lines instead of a single point due to non-spherical
cornea/lens.
14. Types:
•Corneal astigmatism (more common)
• Regular: Two principal meridians at right angles, gradual change of power
• Irregular: Irregular cornea (e.g., scars, keratoconus)
15.Subtypes of regular astigmatism:
•Simple: One meridian emmetropic, other myopic/hyperopic
•Compound: Both meridians myopic or hyperopic
•Mixed: One meridian myopic, other hyperopic
16. Treatment:
•Glasses: Cylindrical lenses (regular/mild irregular)
•Contact lenses:
Soft toric for regular astigmatism
• Rigid gas permeable (RGP) for irregular astigmatism
•Refractive surgery: LASIK/PRK (regular astigmatism)
•Intracorneal ring segments / keratoplasty (irregular astigmatism)
