7. Which of the following is a cause of dilated fixed pupil?
A. Morphine
B. Horner syndrome
C. Stage 3 anaesthesia
D. Pontine hemorrhage
Causes of dilated fixed pupil Trauma to the iris sphincter or the
orbital parasympathetic nerves (traumatic mydriasis), Pharmacologic
dilatation, Ciliary ganglion disease / damage, Oculomotor (Ill) Paralysis,
Brain stem affection (dorsal midbrain syndrome), Stage Ill anaesthesia &
Death (bilateral).
