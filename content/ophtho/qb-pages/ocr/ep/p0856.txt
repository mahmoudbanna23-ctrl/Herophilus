Vitreous Disorders & Intraocular Infections
Endophthalmitis / Vitritis
Investigations:
•CT orbit: If trauma rule out intraocular foreign body.
•Consider cause:
• Metal striking metal -5 Bacillus.
• Plant material fungal infection.
Management:
•Emergency!
•If PL or worse: Admit Immediate vitrectomy + intravitreal antibiotics.
•If HM or better: Vitreous tap for culture + intravitreal antibiotics.
•Topical fortified antibiotics in all cases.
•Systemic antibiotics sometimes added.
•Avoid IOP measurement if globe rupture suspected.
•Eye shield, NPO, elevate head 30—450, check tetanus.
Prognosis:
•Poor if presenting vision is PL or worse.
