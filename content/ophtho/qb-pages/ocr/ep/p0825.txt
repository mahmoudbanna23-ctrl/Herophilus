14. A patient with iridocyclitis was found to have WBC clumps on the back
surface of the cornea. Which of the following might occur as a complication of
that condition?
A. Glaucoma
B. Anterior synechiae
C. Hypotony
D. Corneal erosions
Anterior uveitis can be complicated by 2ry glaucoma, Posterior
synechiae, Complicated cataract, Calcific band keratopathy (if
the iritis becomes chronic) especially in juvenile idiopathic
arthritis & Macular oedema (if the iritis becomes chronic).
