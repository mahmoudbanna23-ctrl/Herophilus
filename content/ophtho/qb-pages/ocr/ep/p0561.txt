CORNEA
Functions
1. Light transmission.
2. Refraction (2/3 of eye's refractive power 42-44D).
3. Clarity via endothelial pump (relative dehydration).
4. Protection.
Transparency Maintained By
•A vascularity
•Uniform collagen arrangement
•Relative dehydration (78% water content)
Layers (Outer Inner)
1. Epithelium
2. Bowman's membrane
3.Stroma (90%)
4. Descemet's membrane
5. Endothelium (non-regenerating)
