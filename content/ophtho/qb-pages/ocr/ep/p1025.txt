6. Marcus Gunn pupil is found in which condition?
a. Compressive optic neuropathy
b. Glaucoma
c. Cataract
d. Retinal detachment
A Marcus Gunn pupil (relative afferent pupillary defect) occurs
in optic nerve lesions, such as compressive optic neuropathy.
The affected eye shows reduced constriction to direct light.
