Anterior Uveitis (Iritis / Iridocyclitis)
Definition: Inflammation of iris + ciliary body (usually unilateral)
Causes:
Idiopathic (75%)
Autoimmune (HLA-B27): Ankylosing spondylitis, Psoriatic arthritis, IBD
Infectious: TB, Syphilis, HSV, HZV, Toxoplasmosis
[S Others: Sarcoidosis, Trauma, Post-surgery
Symptoms:
•Photophobia
•Ocular pain + brow ache
•Red eye (ciliary injection)
•Lacrimation
•U Vision
