II. Topical Ocular Drugs
2. Local Anaesthetics
Examples:
•Proparacaine 0.5%, Tetracaine 0.5%, Benoxinate 0.4%.
Uses: Tonometry, foreign body removal, gonioscopy, surgery prep.
Onset: 10—20 sec. Duration: 20 min.
Adverse Effects:
•Stinging, corneal epithelial damage, allergy (rare).
•Do NOT prescribe for repeated use causes corneal ulceration.
