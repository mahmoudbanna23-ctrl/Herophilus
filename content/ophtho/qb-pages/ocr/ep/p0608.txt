14. What is the initial management in case of chemical eye burn?
A. Neutralization
B. Copious irrigation
C. Antibiotics
D. Eye patching
The initial management of a case presenting with an intra-ocular foreign body is
copious irrigation. However, the definitive management of that case is surgical
removal of the foreign body.
