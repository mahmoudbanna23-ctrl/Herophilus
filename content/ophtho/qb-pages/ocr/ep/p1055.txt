21. 53 years old patient complaining of near vision problems. The patient is
asthmatic and has some degree of heart block that is controlled medically. His
visual acuity is 6/6 in both eyes. While examination you notice nuclear sclerosis
in both eyes. IOP is 28 mmHg in his right eye and 30 mmHg in his left eye, the
fundus examination revealed large cup/disc ratio in both eyes. You suspect that
patient has POAG.
What further tests will help you to confirm the diagnosis?
A- Visual field test
B. Fluoresceine angiography
C. Amsler grid test
D. OCT for macula
Visual field testing detects the characteristic arcuate scotomas
Of glaucoma. It confirms functional loss from optic nerve
damage.
