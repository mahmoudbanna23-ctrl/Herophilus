(9) Sickle Cell Anaemia
Pathophysiology
•Sickling vascular occlusion ischemia retinal neovascularization
•Can progress to vitreous hemorrhage or tractional retinal detachment
Ocular Features
•Capillary non-perfusion and arteriolar occlusion
• "Sea-fan" neovascularization in peripheral retina
Management
•Early ophthalmologic evaluation
•Treatment (e.g., laser photocoagulation) can prevent vision-threatening complications
•Especially indicated if new floaters or visual loss
