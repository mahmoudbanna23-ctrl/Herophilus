8. Patients with photokeratitis are advised to avoid which of the following
medications?
A. Oral analgesics
B. Artificial tears
C. Topical anaesthetics
D. None of the above
During treatment of a patient with photokeratitis, Instruct the
patients NOT TO USE topical anaesthetics (tempting) as they
may delay healing.
