Quick Notes
Hordeolum externum (Stye) Acute painful swelling at lid margin + hair follicle / Zeiss
gland infection + Staph aureus + Rx: topical antibiotics ± epilation or I&D (never squeeze).
Hordeolum internum Acute painful swelling away from lid margin + Meibomian
gland infection + Staph aureus + Rx: systemic ± topical antibiotics ± I&D if abscess.
Chalazion Chronic painless swelling + sterile granulomatous inflammation of
Meibomian gland + firm nodule in tarsal plate + Rx: incision & curettage through
conjunctival side.
Recurrent chalazion in elderly = suspect Meibomian gland carcinoma biopsy
needed.
MC benign eyelid lesion = Chalazion.
MC eyelid infection Hordeolum externum.
