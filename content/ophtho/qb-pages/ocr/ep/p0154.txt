Hypermetropia (Far-sightedness)
Near vision decreased, far vision relatively normal in young patients.
Image focused behind the retina.
Corrected by convex (+) lenses.
Most common cause: Axial hypermetropia (short eyeball).
In young age: good accommodation asymptomatic or only asthenopia,
accommodative esotropia.
In middle age (40—50 years): near blurring.
After 50 years: near and far blurring.
Predisposes to narrow-angle glaucoma.
Refraction performed under cycloplegia.
Refractive surgery: total correction after cycloplegic refraction.
