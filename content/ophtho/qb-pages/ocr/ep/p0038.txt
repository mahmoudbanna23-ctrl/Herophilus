6. Extraocular Muscle Testing (Ocular Alignment & Movements)
Cover Tests:
•Cover Test: Detect manifest squint (tropia).
• If covered eye moves was fixating.
• If uncovered eye moves was deviated.
• Inward Exotropia
• Outward Esotropia
• Up/Down Hyper/Hypotropia
•Cover—Uncover Test: Detect latent squint (phoria).
• Covered eye drifts deviation appears when fusion broken.
•Alternate Cover Test: Detect total deviation (tropia + phoria).
•Pseudostrabismus (Epicanthus):
• Apparent deviation due to broad nasal bridge.
• Confirm true vs false squint via light reflex or cover test.
