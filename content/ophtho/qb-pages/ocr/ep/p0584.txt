2. Regarding keratoconus:
a. It presents by painless progressive diminution of vision but rapid drop
of vision may also occur
b. Munson's sign occurs early in the disease
c. Pachymetry shows increased corneal thickness
d. Soft contact lenses may be used in moderate cases
Keratoconus causes progressive, painless blurring due to corneal thinning
and irregular astigmatism; acute hydrops can cause sudden vision drop.
Corneal thinning (not thickening) is seen on pachymetry.
