Investigations:
•CT orbit & sinuses
•Blood cultures
Treatment:
1. Hospital admission
2.1V antibiotics: ceftriaxone + vancomycin ± metronidazole
3.Surgical drainage if abscess
4. Monitor vision
Complications:
•Optic nerve inflammation vision loss
•Cavernous sinus thrombosis
•Meningitis, brain abscess
•Death if untreated (17—20% mortality pre-antibiotic era).
