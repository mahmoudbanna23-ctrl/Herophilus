4. Which of the following is useful to differentiate between episcleritis and
scleritis?
a. Phenylephrine 2.5% drops
b. Phenylephrine 5% drops
c. Phenylephrine 100/0 drops
d. Phenylephrine 20% drops
Phenylephrine 10% causes blanching Of superficial vessels in
episcleritis but not in scleritis. This helps differentiate the two
conditions clinically.
