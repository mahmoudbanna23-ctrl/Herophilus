I O. What is the definitive management of a case presenting with an intra-ocular
foreign body?
A. Copious irrigation
B. Neutralization
C. FB removal
D. Antibiotics
The initial management of a case presenting with an intra-
ocular foreign body is copious irrigation. However, the
definitive management of that case is surgical removal of the
foreign body.
