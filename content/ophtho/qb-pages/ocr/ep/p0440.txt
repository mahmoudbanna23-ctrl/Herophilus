Adult Chlamydial Conjunctivitis
•Causative agent: Chlamydia trachomatis serovars D—K
•Associated with: STD screen for other STDs, urethritis
•Signs:
• Follicles in both upper & lower tarsal plates
• Papillae in chronic cases
•Management:
• Systemic antibiotics: azithromycin or doxycycline
