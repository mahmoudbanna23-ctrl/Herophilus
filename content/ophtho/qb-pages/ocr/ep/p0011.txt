History of Present Illness
Analyze each complaint by:
•Laterality one or both eyes
•Onset sudden, gradual, accidental, or insidious
•Course progressive, stationary, regressive, intermittent
•Duration & daily variation
•Associated symptoms headache, tearing, diplopia, etc.
•Previous care medications, compliance, interventions, and responses
Don't rush to diagnosis; form a differential list and confirm after full examination.
