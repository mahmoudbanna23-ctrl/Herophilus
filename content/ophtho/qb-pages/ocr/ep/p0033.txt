Steps of Ocular Examination
1. Measure Visual Acuity (VA)
2.Assess Visual Fields
3.1nspect eyelids, conjunctiva, cornea, sclera, iris
4.Assess Anterior Chamber depth & contents
5. Check Pupillary reactions
6. Test Extraocular muscle movements
7. Measure Intraocular pressure
8. Evaluate Lens clarity with ophthalmoscope
9. Perform Fundus examination
10.Do Additional tests (if required)
