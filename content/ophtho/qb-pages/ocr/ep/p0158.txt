Anisometropia: unequal refractive power between the two eyes; most common cause
of amblyopia in children.
If difference 4 D use glasses.
If difference > 4 D use contact lenses or refractive surgery.
Anisokonia: unequal image size on the retina; occurs in anisometropia > 4 D (fusion
failure).
Myopia is associated with open-angle glaucoma (but not a predisposing factor).
