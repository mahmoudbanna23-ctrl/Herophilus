Fast Notes
Epidemiology & Etiology:
•Most common type overall: Allergic (15—40%)
•Most common infectious causes:
• Viral (most common)
• Bacterial
•Most common external ocular infection: Viral
•Most common viral agent: Adenovirus (90%)
• Most common form: Non-specific acute follicular conjunctivitis
•Leading cause of irreversible blindness worldwide: Trachoma (due to reinfection cycles)
•Most severe form of conjunctivitis: Epidemic keratoconjunctivitis
