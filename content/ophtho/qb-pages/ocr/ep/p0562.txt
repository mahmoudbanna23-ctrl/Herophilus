Corneal Foreign Body
•Common cause of ocular trauma (young males yrs).
•Symptoms: Pain, FB sensation, tearing, red eye, photophobia.
•Complications: Rust ring, scarring, infection, secondary iritis.
• Treatment:
• Remove FB under local anesthesia.
• Topical antibiotics.
• Never patch if infection suspected.
