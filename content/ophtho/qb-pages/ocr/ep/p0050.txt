Ocular Emergencies
These conditions require urgent ophthalmology consultation because they can cause
permanent vision loss or be life-threatening.

1. Chemical burns
2. Central retinal artery occlusion (CRAO)
3.Acute angle-closure glaucoma
4.Acute iritis (anterior uveitis)
5.Endophthalmitis (intraocular infection)
6. Gonococcal conjunctivitis
7. Lid or globe lacerations
