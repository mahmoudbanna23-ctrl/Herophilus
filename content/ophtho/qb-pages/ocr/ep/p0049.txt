When to Refer an Apparently Normal Patient
1. Routine exam:
Every 2—4 yrs (age 40—65); every 1—2 yrs (>65).
'Children: newborn, 6 mo, 3 yrs, 5 yrs.
2. Decreased Visual Acuity:
'<6/6 with complaint refer.
*6/12 in both eyes refer even if asymptomatic.
'22-line asymmetry between eyes refer.
Presbyopia: refer for corrective lenses.
3.Abnormal Fundus, Shallow AC, or High IOP refer immediately.
