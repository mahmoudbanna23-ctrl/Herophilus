2. Eyelids
General Features
•The eyelids (palpebrae) protect the eyeball and help lubricate the ocular surface.
•Upper and lower lids meet at medial and lateral canthi.
•The space between open lids is the palpebral fissure.
•Eyelashes (cilia) are directed:
• Upward and outward in the upper lid.
• Downward and outward in the lower lid.
•Posterior lid margin fits snugly against the globe.
