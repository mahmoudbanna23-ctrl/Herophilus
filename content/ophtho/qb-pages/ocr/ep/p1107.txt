Primary Congenital Glaucoma (PCG)
Definition
•A rare developmental anomaly of the trabecular meshwork and anterior chamber angle
leads to elevated intraocular pressure (IOP) in infants.
•Isolated trabeculodysgenesis (no other ocular or systemic malformations).
•Causes optic nerve damage, corneal scarring, and amblyopia if untreated
Epidemiology
•Accounts for about 5% of childhood blindness worldwide.
•Vision loss results from:
• Corneal opacity/scarring
• Optic nerve damage
• Secondary amblyopia (especially if unilateral/asymmetric)
•With early diagnosis and surgical control of IOP, vision can often be saved.
