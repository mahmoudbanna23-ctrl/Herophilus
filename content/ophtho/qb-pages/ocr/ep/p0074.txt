5. Which of the following is located between the iris and lens?
a. Posterior chamber
b. Anterior chamber
c. Vitreous cavity
d. Canal of Schlemm
The posterior chamber lies between the iris and the lens, filled
with aqueous humor. The anterior chamber, by contrast, is
located between the cornea and iris.
