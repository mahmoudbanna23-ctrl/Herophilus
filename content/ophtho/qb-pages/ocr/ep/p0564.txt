Corneal ulcer / Infectious Keratitis
Definition
Loss of corneal epithelium + stromal inflammation ± necrosis.
Symptoms
Pain, redness, photophobia, vision, discharge.
Causes
I.lnfectious: Bacterial, viral, fungal, Acanthamoeba
2. Vitamin A deficiency (keratomalacia)
3. Immunologic (e.g. RA, vernal keratoconjunctivitis)
Complications
Scarring, perforation (Seidel's test +ve), iritis, endophthalmitis.
Treatment
•Urgent referral
•Smear & culture
•Intensive topical antimicrobials
•Cycloplegics
