4. Afferent pupillary defect is seen in:
a. Third nerve paralysis
b. Sympathetic paralysis
c. Optic atrophy
d. Lesions of the occipital cortex
An afferent pupillary defect (Marcus Gunn pupil) occurs when
the optic nerve is damaged, as in optic atrophy. The pupil
constricts less when light is shone in the affected eye.
