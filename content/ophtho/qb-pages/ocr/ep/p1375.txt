darkness + vomiting + nausea + colored halos around lights— Primary Angle Closure
Glaucoma
Old age + history of HTN or DM + blurred vision + generalized constriction of visual field
+ patient uses eye drops to IOP= Primary Open Angle Glaucoma
Baby with bilateral large globes + lacrimation + opaque cornea + IOP > 21 + parent
reports complication— Congenital Glaucoma (Buphthalmos)
History of DM + vision + new blood vessels at optic disc— Proliferative Diabetic
Retinopathy (PDR)
night vision + positive family history + affected retinal and optic function + bony
spicule-like pigmentation— Retinitis Pigmentosa
Flashes of light + moving black spots + veil-like defect in vision
•Flashes of light photopsia due to retinal tear
•Moving black spots = muscae volitantes
•Veil-like defect shadow or curtain in visual field
= Rhegmatogenous Retinal Detachment
