16. What is the risk of diabetic retinopathy at the initial time of diagnosis of
diabetes mellitus type 2?
A. 20%
In type II (NIDDM) patients:
20% have DR at the time of diagnosis.
(Since many patients with type II DM may not be diagnosed until years after the
onset of the disease, many of them will have already developed significant DR
changes at the time of diagnosis). 60% have DR 20 years after diagnosis.
