6. Retinopathy of Prematurity (ROP)
Screening:
•Essential due to narrow treatment window.
•Timing:
• GA 2 28 wk screen at 4 wk postnatal
• GA 27 wk screen at 31 wk GA
• Maybe done in NICU
Treatment:
1. Laser photocoagulation
2.Anti-VEGF intravitreal injections
3. Vitrectomy (late cases)
Prognosis:
•Good if treated early
•Late retinal detachment poor prognosis
•T risk of myopia even if successfully treated
