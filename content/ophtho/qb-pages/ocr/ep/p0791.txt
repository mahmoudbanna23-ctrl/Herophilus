Normal-Tension Glaucoma (NTG)
•Optic neuropathy & field loss with normal IOP
•Often vascular cause (migraine, vasospasm, hypotension, sleep apnea)
•Treatment: Same as POAG (lower IOP target further)
Secondary Open-Angle Glaucoma
Due to obstruction of trabecular meshwork.
Examples:
•Steroid-induced
•Phacolytic
•Traumatic
•Inflammatory (uveitis)
•Early neovascular glaucoma
