Subtypes (Regular):
•Simple: one meridian emmetropic
•Compound: both myopic or both hyperopic
•Mixed: one myopic, one hyperopic
Symptoms:
•Blurred/distorted vision
•Eye strain, headache
Treatment:
•Cylindrical lenses (regular)
•Toric soft CL / RGP CL
•LASIK/PRK (regular)
•ICR or keratoplasty (irregular)
