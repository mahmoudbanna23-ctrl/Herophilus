Corneal Degenerations
Arcus Senilis (Gerontoxon)
Definition:
Bilateral lipid deposition in peripheral corneal stroma (most common peripheral opacity)
Associations:
•Elderly: physiologic
• Young: check lipid profile may indicate dyslipidemia (arcus juvenilis)
Features:
•Asymptomatic
•Clear zone between opacity & limbus (Lucid interval of Vogt)
Treatment:
•None; only check lipid profile if in young patients
