3. Ophthalmic manifestations of thyroid eye disease include EXCEPT:
a. Upper lid retraction
b. Proptosis
d. Visual loss due to optic nerve compression
In thyroid eye disease, diplopia is binocular due to extraocular
muscle restriction, not monocular. Monocular diplopia is
usually optical in Origin.
