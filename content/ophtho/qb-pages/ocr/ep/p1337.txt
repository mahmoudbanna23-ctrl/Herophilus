II. Topical Ocular Drugs
B. Adrenergic-stimulating (Sympathomimetics):
•Phenylephrine 2.5% mydriasis (no cycloplegia).
•Used with tropicamide for max dilation.
•Avoid 10% phenylephrine in cardiac patients can cause hypertension, MI.
Precautions:
•Avoid driving post-dilation.
•Use sunglasses.
•Caution in Down's syndrome and angle-closure glaucoma.
