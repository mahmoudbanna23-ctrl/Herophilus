15. A 65-year-old patient presents with sudden, painless, and persistent loss of
vision in one eye. Fundus examination shows a pale retina with a swollen optic
disc. What is the most likely diagnosis?
a. Anterior ischemic optic neuropathy
b. Central retinal vein occlusion
c. Optic neuritis
d. Retinal detachment
This condition results from sudden interruption of blood
supply to the optic nerve head, leading to a pale swollen disc
and permanent vision loss. It is common in elderly patients
with vascular risk factors.
