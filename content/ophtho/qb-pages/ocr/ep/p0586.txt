3. The keratometer is used to measure:
a. Radius of curvature of the cornea
b. Thickness of the cornea
c. Diameter of the cornea
d. Clarity of the cornea
The keratometer (ophthalmometer) measures the anterior corneal
curvature, which helps determine corneal power for contact lens fitting or
intraocular lens calculation. Pachymetry measures thickness, not
curvature.
