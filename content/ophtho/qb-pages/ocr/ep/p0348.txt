Blepharitis (Inflammation of lid margin)
Types:
Posterior (Meibomian gland dysfunction)
•Foamy discharge, plugged orifices, lid telangiectasia
•Symptoms: dry eye, irritation, recurrent chalazion
Treatment:
•Warm compress + lid scrubs
•Oral doxycycline
•Tear substitutes
•Topical steroids if inflammation
•Newer: cyclosporine, light therapy, gland expression
