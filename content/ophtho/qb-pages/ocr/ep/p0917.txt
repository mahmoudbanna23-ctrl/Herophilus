9. A 62-year-old diabetic woman with pre-existing proliferative diabetic
retinopathy (PDR) is at risk of developing which of the following?
A. Rhegmatogenous retinal detachment
B. Tractional retinal detachment
C. Exudative retinal detachment
D. All of the above
Tractional retinal detachment (TrRD) -5 Caused by vitreous,
epiretinal, or subretinal membrane pulling the neurosensory
retina away from the underlying RPE. It is found in conditions
such as DR, CRVO, sickle cell disease, ROP, and ocular trauma.
