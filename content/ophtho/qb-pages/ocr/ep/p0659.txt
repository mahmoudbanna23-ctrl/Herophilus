Ectopia Lentis (Dislocated/Subluxated Lens)
Definition:
Partial (subluxation) or total (dislocation) displacement of the lens from its normal position.
Causes:
1. Hereditary/Congenital:
Madan's syndrome
Ehlers—Danlos syndrome
Homocystinuria
2. Trauma
Clinical Picture:
•U Vision, monocular diplopia
•Visible lens edge through pupil
•Iridodonesis & phacodonesis (iris and lens tremulousness)
•Unequal anterior chamber depth
•Posterior dislocation vitreous cavity (like aphakia)
•Anterior dislocation emergency (t lop)
