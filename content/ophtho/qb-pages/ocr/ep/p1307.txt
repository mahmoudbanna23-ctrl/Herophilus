13. A 30-year-old pregnant woman presents with seizures and blurred vision.
Ophthalmoscopic examination reveals retinal vasospasm and exudates. What is
the first step in management?
a) Measure the blood pressure
b) Measure the blood glucose level
c) Perform visual field testing
d) Administer magnesium sulfate immediately
In a pregnant woman with seizures and visual symptoms,
eclampsia must be ruled Out first by checking blood pressure
before other tests or treatments.
