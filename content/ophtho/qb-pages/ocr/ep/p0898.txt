2. Wet (Neovascular / Exudative) AMD
•10% of cases, but 80% of severe vision loss
•Mechanism: Choroidal neovascular membrane (CNV) leakage haemorrhage
disciform scar
•Symptoms: Rapid central vision loss, metamorphopsia (wavy lines), scotoma
•Diagnosis:
• OCT: Detects CNV, subretinal fluid
• Fluorescein angiography (FA): Maps leakage
•Treatment:
• Intravitreal anti-VEGF (Ranibizumab, Bevacizumab, Aflibercept)
• Old: Laser photocoagulation / Photodynamic therapy (Verteporfin)
Amsler Grid Test
•Monitors macular function (central 100 visual field).
•Patient looks at central dot; reports wavy, missing, or dark areas.
