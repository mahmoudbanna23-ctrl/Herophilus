Panuveitis
Definition: Inflammation Of all parts Of the uveal tract.
Causes:
• Infectious:
• Exogenous (penetrating trauma)
• Secondary from nearby tissues (cornea, sclera, retina)
• Endogenous spread (TB, syphilis, toxoplasmosis, viral infections)
•Non-infectious: Autoimmune (Behcet's)
•Neoplastic / traumatic
Clinical Picture:
Combination Of anterior + intermediate + posterior uveitis findings.
Treatment:
Corticosteroids (mainstay for noninfectious)
Cycloplegics
Systemic immunosuppressants
Treat underlying cause
Always rule Out infection before giving steroids.
