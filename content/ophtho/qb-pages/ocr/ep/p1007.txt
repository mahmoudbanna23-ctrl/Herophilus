B. Arteritic (Giant Cell Arteritis—related) AION
Signs:
•Pale "chalky-white" swollen disc
•RAPD
•Tender, thickened, non-pulsatile temporal artery
•Fellow eye may be involved within days
Investigations:
•ESR > 60 mm/hr, CRP
•Temporal artery biopsy confirms diagnosis
Treatment:
•Urgent high-dose systemic corticosteroids (to prevent bilateral blindness)
