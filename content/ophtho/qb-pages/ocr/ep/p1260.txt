(4) Thyroid Eye Disease (TED)
Etiology
•Autoimmune inflammation enlargement of extraocular muscles + orbital fat
•Independent of thyroid hormone control
•Smoking worsens prognosis
Phases
1. Inflammatory (active)
2. Fibrotic (inactive)
Clinical Features
•Eyelid retraction (most common, 90%)
•Proptosis (exophthalmos) "Thyroid stare"
•Restricted eye movements (esp. inferior rectus upward gaze limitation)
•Diplopia
•Corneal exposure keratopathy
•Compressive optic neuropathy (sight-threatening)
