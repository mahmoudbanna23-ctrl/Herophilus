I. Consecutive optic atrophy occurs in:
a. Optic neuritis
b. Papilledema
c. Glaucoma
d. Retinitis pigmentosa
Consecutive optic atrophy follows degenerative retinal diseases like retinitis
pigmentosa, where retinal degeneration leads to secondary optic nerve atrophy. It
differs from primary optic atrophy, which Originates from optic nerve pathology.
