7. Nasolacrimal Duct Obstruction
Definition: Congenital blockage of nasolacrimal duct epiphora from birth.
Clinical Picture:
•Watering, discharge, crusting
•Reflux of mucopus on pressure (positive regurgitation test)
•Mucocele or abscess formation possible
Treatment:
•<1 year: 85% resolve spontaneously massage over lacrimal sac
•>1 year / persistent: Probing of nasolacrimal duct
•Mucocele: Immediate probing
•Lacrimal abscess: Incision & drainage
