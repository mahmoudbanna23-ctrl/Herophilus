5. Venous nicking in hypertensive retinopathy is commonly associated with
which of the following?
a) Central retinal vein occlusion (CRVO)
c) Central retinal artery occlusion (CRAO)
d) Diabetic macular edema
Venous nicking occurs where arteries cross veins, compressing
them in hypertension, which predisposes to branch retinal vein
occlusion.
