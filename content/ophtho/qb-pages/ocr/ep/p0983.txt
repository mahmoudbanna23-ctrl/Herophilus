Neuro-Ophthalmology
. Pupillary Disorders
Pupillary Light Reflex
•Direct reflex: constriction of the stimulated pupil.
•Consensual reflex: constriction of the opposite pupil.
•Pathway: Retina Pretectal Nucleus Bilateral Edinger-Westphal Nuclei Oculomotor
Nerve Ciliary Ganglion Sphincter Pupillae.
• Abnormal Pupillary Size
Condition
Dilated fixed pupil
pin-point pupil
Small pupil
Causes / Notes
Oculomotor nerve injury, ciliary ganglion damage, trauma, pharmacologic
dilatation.
Morphine use, organophosphate poisoning, pontine hemorrhage.
Senile miosis, Horner syndrome, Argyll Robertson pupil, iritis.
