4. A 22-year-old male patient presented to the ophthalmology clinic with a
complaint of seeing bugs or cobwebs together with light flashes.
Ophthalmoscopy revealed an evident Weiss ring. Which of the following might
occur as a complication to that patient's condition?
A. Endophthalmitis
B. Cataract
C. Retinal tears
D. Glaucoma
This case is a case of PVD. Complications of PVD may be:
A. Traction at areas of abnormal vitreoretinal adhesions or
areas of weak retina may cause retinal tears/detachment
B. Retinal tears/detachment may cause vitreous haemorrhage
if a bridging retinal blood vessel is torn.
