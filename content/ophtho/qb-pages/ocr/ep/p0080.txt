8. How do you test the visual field in a patient who can only detect hand
motion?
a. Light perception
b. Light projection
c. Confrontation test
d. Snellen chan
If a patient can only detect hand movement, visual field testing is done by
light projection. It helps assess whether the patient can identify the
direction from which light enters.
