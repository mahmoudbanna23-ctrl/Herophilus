I I. A 22-year-old female patient complains of hard dry scales around the bases
of eyelashes. Examination revealed ulceration of lid margin and scarring of
lashes. Which of the following is the causative organism of that patient's
condition?
A. Staphylococcus aureus
B. Streptococcus pneumoniae
C. Pseudomonas aeruginosa
D. None of the above
Ulcerative anterior blepharitis is characterized by ulceration Of
the lid margin and hard dry scales around the bases Of the
lashes. This type Of blepharitis is caused by staphylococcus
aureus.
