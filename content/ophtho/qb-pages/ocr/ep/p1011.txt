VISUAL PATHWAYS & FIELD DEFECTS
Lesions anywhere from retina occipital cortex cause characteristic field loss.
Term
Scotoma
Hemianopia
Altitudinal hemianopia
Homonymous hemianopia
Bitemporal (heteronymous) hemianopia
Meaning
Localized area of reduced/lost vision
surrounded by normal field
Loss of half of the visual field
Loss of upper or lower half
Same side of both eyes (right or left)
Temporal halves of both eyes
