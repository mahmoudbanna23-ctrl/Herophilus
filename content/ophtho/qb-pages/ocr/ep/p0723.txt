Uveitis
Definition: Inflammation of one or more parts of the uveal tract.
Common causes: Idiopathic, autoimmune, infectious, or malignant.
Managed by: Ophthalmologist (urgent condition).
Anatomical Classification
Type
Anterior uveitis
Intermediate uveitis
Posterior uveitis
