I. The type of cataract that is specific for diabetes:
a. Rosette shaped
b. Sunflower
c. Snow flakes
d. Blue dotted
Snowflake cataract is specific to diabetes, resulting from
sorbitol accumulation in the lens due to high glucose. It
presents as small, white, snowflake-like opacities in the cortex
of the lens.
