Cataract
Definition
Opacity of the crystalline lens (partial or total) from any cause reversible blindness
(world's leading cause).
Types
Paediatric Cataract
•Congenital or developmental; covered in paediatric ophthalmology.
Senile (Age-Related) Cataract
50 yrs; 90 % of cases.
