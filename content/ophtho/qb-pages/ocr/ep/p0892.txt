Clinical Features (4 F' s)
•Flashes (photopsia)
•Floaters (mobile black spots)
•Field defect ("curtain " or shadow)
•Failed central vision (if macula detached)
Other signs:
Vision, IOP, RAPD (+), grey reflex on fundoscopy, corrugated retina.
Diagnosis
•Ophthalmoscopy: Greyish detached retina with visible folds.
•B-scan ultrasound: If fundus not visible (shows highly reflective membrane).
