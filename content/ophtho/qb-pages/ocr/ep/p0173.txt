7. Which lens is used to correct astigmatism?
a. Cylindrical
b. Spherical
c. Convex
d. Concave
Astigmatism is caused by unequal curvature of the cornea or
lens. It is corrected using a cylindrical lens that focuses light
differently in specific meridians.
