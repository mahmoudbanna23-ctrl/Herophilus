II. Topical Ocular Drugs
4. Decongestants
Examples: Naphazoline 0.012%, Phenylephrine 0.12%, Tetrahydrozoline 0.05%.
Action: Vasoconstriction temporary whitening.
Overuse: Rebound redness, rare angle-closure glaucoma.
Note: Not for chronic use or true "eye hygiene."
5. Antiallergic Agents
•OTC: Naphazoline + Antazoline or Pheniramine (decongestant + antihistamine).
•Rx:
• Cromoglicate, Olopatadine mast cell stabilizers.
• Ketotifen antihistamine + mast cell stabilizer.
• Ketorolac anti-inflammatory relief.
