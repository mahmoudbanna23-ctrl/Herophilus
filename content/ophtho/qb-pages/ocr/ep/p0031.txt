3. Vascular and Neurologic Changes
•Arteriosclerosis may predispose to:
• Cranial nerve (Ill, IV, VI) palsies
• Retinal artery or vein occlusion
• Anterior ischemic optic neuropathy
•Retina and Optic Pathways:
• Delayed visual pigment regeneration impaired dark adaptation night vision
difficulty.
• Retinal vascular and neuronal loss decreased visual acuity, contrast sensitivity,
and visual fields.
