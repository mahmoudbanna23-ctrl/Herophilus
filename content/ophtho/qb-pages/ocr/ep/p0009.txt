Types of Complaints
A. Visual Complaints
1. Diminution or loss of vision
2. Night blindness
3. Color blindness
4. Photophobia — light intolerance
5. Glare — difficulty seeing in bright light
6. Diplopia — double vision
7. Polyopia — seeing more than two images
8. Photopsia — flashes of light without source
9. Floaters — black shadows in bright light
10.Duskiness — sensation of dim light
11.Central scotoma — dark spot affecting central vision
12.Macropsia — objects appear larger
13.Micropsia — objects appear smaller
14.Metamorphopsia — distorted image perception
