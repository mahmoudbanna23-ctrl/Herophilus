I. Which of the following is responsible for the secretion of the outer oily layer
of the tear film?
a. Meibomian gland
b. Main lacrimal gland
c. Goblet cells
d. Krause glands
The Meibomian glands secrete the outer Oily layer Of the tear
film, which prevents tear evaporation. Goblet cells produce
mucin, and lacrimal glands produce the aqueous layer.
