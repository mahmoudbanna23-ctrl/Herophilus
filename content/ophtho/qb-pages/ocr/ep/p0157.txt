Accommodation and Presbyopia
Near point: the nearest distance at which an object is seen clearly.
In myopia near the eye.
In hypermetropia far from the eye.
Accommodation power: 14 D at 10 years 3 D by 40 years lost by 60 years.
Presbyopia: loss of accommodation with age requires convex (+) lenses for near
work.
Special Combinations
Low myopia + presbyopia: may balance each other (can read without glasses).
High myopia + presbyopia: needs bifocals for distance, + for near).
Hypermetropia + presbyopia: both near and distant vision impaired; requires stronger
+ lenses.
