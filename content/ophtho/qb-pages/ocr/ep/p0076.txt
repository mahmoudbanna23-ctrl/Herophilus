6. Which muscle is supplied by the trochlear nerve?
a. Superior oblique
b. Superior rectus
c. Lateral rectus
d. Inferior oblique
The trochlear nerve (cranial nerve IV) supplies only the
superior oblique muscle, which helps in depression and
intorsion of the eyeball. It passes through a pulley-like structure
called the trochlea.
