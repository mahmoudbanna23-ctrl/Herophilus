Quick Notes
2. Chemical Injuries
•Initial management:
• Immediate irrigation by first person seeing the patient (even non-ophthalmologist)
• Duration: 15—30 minutes
• Most important prognostic factor: speed & efficacy of irrigation
•Incidence & causes:
• Mostly accidental
• Alkali injuries 2x more common than acids
2/3 occur at work
•Pathophysiology:
• Alkalis: liquefactive necrosis penetrate deep
• Acids: coagulative necrosis form protective barrier
