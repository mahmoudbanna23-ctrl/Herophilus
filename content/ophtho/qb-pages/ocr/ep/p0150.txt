Hypermetropia (Hyperopia / Farsightedness)
9.Definition: Parallel rays focus behind the retina; accommodation at rest. Causes blurred
near vision, asthenopia, accommodative esotropia.
10.Far point of hypermetropic eye: Virtual point behind the eye
11. Complications:
•Predisposition to narrow-angle glaucoma (later in life)
•Squint (accommodative esotropia)
12. Treatment:
•Glasses: Spherical convex (plus) lenses
•Contact lenses
•Refractive surgery (LASIK, PRK)
