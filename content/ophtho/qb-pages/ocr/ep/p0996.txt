Acquired Nystagmus
•Symptoms: Oscillopsia (perceived motion of environment), vertigo.
•Causes: Vestibular disease, trauma, multiple sclerosis, brain tumours, CNS degeneration.
•Investigations:
• Full neurological evaluation
• MRIfor all acquired cases
• Pathologic types seen in primary position of gaze
