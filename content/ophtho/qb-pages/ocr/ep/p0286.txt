Dacryoadenitis
5.
•Definition: Acute or chronic inflammation of the lacrimal gland (outer one-third of upper lid).
•Most common infectious cause: Viral (Epstein-Barr virus).
•Clinical picture:
A. Acute dacryoadenitis:
• Symptoms: Pain in superolateral orbit, worsened by eye movement, drooping upper lid,
difficulty opening eye.
• Signs: Tenderness, edema, erythema, induration, ptosis with S-shaped upper lid
deformity.
•B. Chronic dacryoadenitis:
• Painless enlargement of lacrimal gland.
•Investigation: CT orbit or MRI (especially for chronic cases).
•Treatment (cause-dependent):
• Viral: Symptomatic treatment.
• Bacterial: Systemic broad-spectrum antibiotics.
• Idiopathic: Oral corticosteroids may be considered.
• Chronic: Treat underlying disorder.
