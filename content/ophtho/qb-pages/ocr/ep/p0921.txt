I l. Which of the following is a well-known cause of tractional retinal
detachment?
A. Malignant hypertension
B. Posterior uveitis
C. High myopia
D. Sickle cell disease
Tractional retinal detachment (TrRD) Caused by vitreous,
epiretinal, or subretinal membrane pulling the neurosensory
retina away from the underlying RPE. It is found in conditions
such as DR, CRVO, sickle cell disease, ROP, and ocular trauma.
