5. Which glaucoma medication may cause systemic hypotension?
a. Timolol
b. Pilocarpine
c. Latanoprost
d. Brimonidine
Timolol may cause systemic hypotension. While timolol is a
topical eye drop, it can be absorbed into the bloodstream and
cause systemic side effects, including low blood pressure
(hypotension), particularly in susceptible individuals.
