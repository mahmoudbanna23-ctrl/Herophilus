Myopia (Nearsightedness)
5.Definition: Parallel rays focus in front of the retina; accommodation at rest. Causes
blurred distance vision.
6.Far point of myopic eye: Lies in front of the eye (closer than infinity)
7. Complications of high myopia:
Chorioretinal degenerations:
Central: Subfoveal CNV, macular hemorrhage, macular scarring
Peripheral: Night blindness, retinal tears, rhegmatogenous retinal detachment
Posterior staphyloma
'Complicated cataract
'High association with primary open-angle glaucoma
8. Treatment of myopia:
Glasses: Spherical concave (minus) lenses
Contact lenses
Refractive surgery (LASIK, PRK)
