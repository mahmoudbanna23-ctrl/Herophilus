Malignancies of the Eye and Its Adnexa
General Overview
•The eye is an uncommon site for primary malignancy.
•Most intraocular tumors are metastatic from other sites.
•Most common primary tumors:
• Children: Retinoblastoma
• Adults: Choroidal melanoma
