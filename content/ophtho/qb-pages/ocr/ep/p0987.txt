II. @ Motility Disorders
Clinical Features of Cranial Nerve Palsy
•Manifest strabismus (limitation in direction of paralyzed muscle).
•Binocular diplopia (T in direction of affected muscle).
•Abnormal head posture (to minimize diplopia).
Treatment:
•Manage cause
•Temporary monocular occlusion
. prisms
•Surgery for chronic stable deviation
