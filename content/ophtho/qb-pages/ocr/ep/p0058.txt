Squint / Ocular Alignment
21.Cover test: Detects tropia (eye misalignment)
•Uncovered eye moves inward exotropic
•Uncovered eye moves outward esotropic
•Up/down hypotropic/hypertropic
22.Cover-uncover test: Detects phoria
•Eye moves outward after cover esophoria
•Eye moves inward exophoria
Intraocular Pressure
23.Normal IOP: 10—21 mmHg (applanation tonometry)
24.Gold standard IOP measurement: Goldmann applanation tonometry
