Specific Etiologies
•Bilateral orbital tumors: Common in lymphoma
•Cavernous sinus thrombosis: Bilateral orbital symptoms
•Carotid-cavernous fistula: Pulsating exophthalmos
Blow-out Fractures
•Occur when impacting object > orbital aperture
•Most common site: Orbital floor (including infraorbital canal)
•Medial wall may also fracture
•Clinical course:
• Early: Proptosis
• Late: Enophthalmos
• Large fractures globe displacement
• Small fractures muscle entrapment limited motility diplopia
Diplopia Causes in Blow-out Fracture:
1.Hemorrhage & edema
2.Mechanica/ entrapment of inferior rectus or inferior oblique diplopia in upgaze & downga
3. Direct injury to extraocular muscles
Important: Avoid blowing the nose after fracture
