13. Which of the following is the best line of management of a diabetic patient
presenting with acute angle closure glaucoma together with lower limb edema
and dyspnea?
A. Alpha blockers
B. Topical mydriatics
C. Mannitol
D. Glycerine
Medical treatment of acute angle closure glaucoma (to lower IOR reduce
pain, and clear corneal oedema in preparation for iridotomy).
Topical medications: IOP lowering drugs (previously mentioned), miotics &
Prednisolone.
Systemic medications: Carbonic anhydrase inhibitors (for IOP lowering) &
Osmotic agents: Mannitol (contraindicated in cardiac patients) and glycerine
(contraindicated in diabetics).
