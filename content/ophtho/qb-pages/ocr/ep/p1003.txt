Retrobulbar Neuritis
Aetiology:
•Multiple sclerosis (most common; 50% progress to MS)
•Idiopathic
•Post-viral (parainfectious)
•Autoimmune (SLE, PAN, sarcoidosis)
•Infectious (syphilis, Lyme, herpes zoster, sinusitis, cryptococcus)
Investigation: MRI brain & orbits ± contrast demyelinating plaques
Treatment:
•IV methylprednisolone 1 g/day x 3 days oral prednisolone (1 mg/kg) x 11 days, then
taper
