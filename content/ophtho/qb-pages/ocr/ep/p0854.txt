Vitreous Disorders & Intraocular Infections
Vitreous Hemorrhage (VH)
Investigations:
•B-scan ultrasound: To rule out RD.
•Examine fellow eye for etiology (e.g., PDR).
Management:
•Observation: Small hemorrhages reabsorb in 3—6 months.
•Treat cause:
• PDR Panretinal photocoagulation (PRP) ± anti-VEGF.
•Vitrectomy: Long-standing or non-resolving VH or associated RD.
