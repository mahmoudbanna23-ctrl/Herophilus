Retinitis Pigmentosa (RP)
• Fundus Triad
1. Bone-spicule pigment deposits
2.Attenuated (narrow) retinal vessels
3. Waxy pale optic disc
Investigations
•ERG: rod/cone responses
•EOG: function of RPE
Management
•No cure
•Vit A / E supplementation may slow progression
•Manage complications (e.g., cataract extraction, low-vision aids)
