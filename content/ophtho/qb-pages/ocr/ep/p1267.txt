(10) Acquired Immunodeficiency Syndrome (AIDS)
Major Ocular Manifestations
Condition
AIDS Retinopathy (most common)
Cytomegalovirus (CMV) Retinitis
Kaposi's Sarcoma (Eyelid/Conjunctiva)
Herpes Zoster Ophthalmicus (HZO)
Herpes Simplex Keratitis (HW)
Description / Features
Cotton-wool spots ± retinal hemorrhages
Sharply demarcated sectors of hemorrhage
and necrosis along retina/ vessels; indicates
poor prognosis even with HAART
Reddish-purple vascular lesions; may mimic
subconjunctival hemorrhage
