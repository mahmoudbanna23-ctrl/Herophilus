Primary Angle-Closure Glaucoma (PACG)
Mechanism: Pupillary block peripheral iris bows forward closes angle
Predisposing:
• Elderly females
•Hypermetropia
•Short axial length
•Shallow AC
•Southeast Asians
•Dim light or mydriasis (stress, drugs)
Types:
•Acute
•Subacute (intermittent)
•Chronic
