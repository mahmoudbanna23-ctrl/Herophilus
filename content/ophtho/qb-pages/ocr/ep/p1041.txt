14. A 60-year-old man reports sudden, transient, painless loss of vision in one
eye lasting a few minutes, which resolved spontaneously.
What is the most likely diagnosis?
a. Amaurosis fugax
b. Migraine without aura
c. Optic neuritis
d. Retinal detachment
Amaurosis fugax is transient monocular vision loss caused by
retinal ischemia, Often from carotid artery emboli. Vision
typically returns within minutes.
