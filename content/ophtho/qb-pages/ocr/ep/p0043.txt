9. Additional Tests
b. Colour Vision Testing
•Normal retina has three pigments — red, green, blue.
•Defects:
• Congenital: X-Iinked (mostly red-green), affects 8% males, 0.5% females.
• Acquired: Seen in retinal or optic nerve diseases.
• Test:
• Use pseudoisochromatic plates (Ishihara test).
• Conduct under good natural light, each eye separately, with glasses (if used for near
vision).
Type of defect identified from specific plate errors using test instructions.
43
