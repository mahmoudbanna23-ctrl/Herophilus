Type
Comitant Strabismus
Incomitant Strabismus
Description
Degree of deviation is equal in all gaze
directions
Degree of deviation varies with gaze
direction
Key Features
- EOMs are normal - Common in
children - May lead to amblyopia
- Usually benign (rarely
neurologic)
- Due to muscle paralysis or
mechanical restriction - Indicates
neurologic or orbital pathology
(e.g. 3rd, 4th, 6th nerve palsy,
thyroid ophthalmopathy,
trauma) * •
