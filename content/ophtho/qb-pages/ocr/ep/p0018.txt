Anatomy of the Ocular Adnexa
Definition:
Ocular adnexa are the structures that support and protect the eye and assist in its
function.
They include:
Orbit, Eyelids, Lacrimal system, and Extraocular muscles.
(The conjunctiva is also considered part of the ocular adnexa.)
1. Orbit
•The orbit is the bony cavity (socket) in the skull that houses the eye and its appendages.
•Volume: 30 mL in adults; the eyeball itself occupies about 6.5 mL.
•Functions:
• Protects the eye.
• Provides smooth movement and support for the eyeball.
• Contains important neurovascular and muscular structures (optic nerve, ophth
artery, ophthalmic nerve, extraocular muscles).
