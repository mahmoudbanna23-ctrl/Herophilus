14. A 66-year-old male patient complaining of recent progressive difficulty in
seeing far objects (myopic shift). He reports that he used to see perfectly before.
Which of the following might be the cause of that patient's condition?
A. Presbyopia
B. Glaucoma
C. Nuclear cataract
D. Retinitis pigmentosa
Second-sight phenomenon -5 Regaining the ability to read and
do near work without near correction in a previously
presbyopic patient. It is due to increased refractive index Of the
lens nucleus (inducing myopia). It is seen in early cases Of
nuclear cataract.
