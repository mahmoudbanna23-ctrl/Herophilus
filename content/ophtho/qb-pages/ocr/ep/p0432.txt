Fast Notes
Anatomy:
•Palpebral conjunctiva: firmly adherent to tarsal plate
•Bulbar conjunctiva: loosely attached to ocular surface except at limbus
•Overlying CT of sclera: Tenon's capsule
•Limbal stem cells: in Palisades of Vogt
• Deficiency conjunctivalization of corneal epithelium
•Conjunctival cul-de-sac: dead space between bulbar & palpebral conjunctiva
