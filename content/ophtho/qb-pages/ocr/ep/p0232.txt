I. Proptosis is not present in:
a. Orbital cellulitis
b. Endophthalmitis
c. Thyroid ophthalmopathy
d. Orbital tumors
Endophthalmitis causes severe intraocular inflammation but
usually not proptosis. Proptosis occurs in orbital pathologies
like cellulitis, thyroid ophthalmopathy, or orbital tumors.
