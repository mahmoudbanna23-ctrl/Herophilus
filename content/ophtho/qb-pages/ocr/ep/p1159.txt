I. A two-year-old child came with esotropia, leukocoria, pain and proptosis.
The probable diagnosis is:
a. Orbital cellulitis
b. Cavernous sinus thrombosis
c. Retinoblastoma
d. Congenital cataract
Retinoblastoma is a malignant intraocular tumor in children
that presents with leukocoria, strabismus (esotropia), pain,
and proptosis. The other conditions usually cause inflammation
or infection without leukocoria.
