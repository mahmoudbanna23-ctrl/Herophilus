Infectious keratitis
- Bacterial: Pneumococcus, Pseudomonas Hypopyon ulcer
- Fungal: Trauma with plant, feather-like margins, satellite lesions
Treat with antifungal (No antibiotics)
- Acanthamoeba: Contact lens users perineural infiltrates severe pain
Arcus Senilis (Gerontoxon)
- Elderly, lipid deposition, asymptomatic
- Lucid interval of Vogt between opacity & limbus
- In youth check lipid profile (Arcus juvenilis)
Calcific Band Keratopathy (CBK)
- Band in interpalpebral zone (middle %)
- Fine dust-like Ca2+ deposits (subepithelial—Bowman—ant. stroma)
- Grey chalky white; affects vision if over pupil
- Seen with silicone-filled eyes
- Rx: Chelation (EDTA/PTK)
