Vitreous Disorders & Intraocular Infections
Endophthalmitis / Vitritis
Definition:
Intraocular infection (acute, subacute, or chronic).
Etiology:
•Postoperative (esp. after cataract surgery 1%).
•Post-traumatic (3—7%).
•Intravitreal injections.
•Endogenous spread (e.g., sepsis).
•Organisms: usually bacterial > fungal.
Symptoms:
•Painful red eye, photophobia, vision.
