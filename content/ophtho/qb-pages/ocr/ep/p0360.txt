I. Ectropion can be due to:
a. Scarring of the conjunctiva
b. Atrophic eye
c. Injury to the third nerve
d. Injury to the seventh nerve
Ectropion, or outward turning of the eyelid, can result from
facial nerve palsy (7th nerve), which weakens orbicularis oculi
muscle tone.
