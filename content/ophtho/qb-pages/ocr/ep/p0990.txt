Myasthenia Gravis (MG)
Definition
A chronic autoimmune disorder that interferes with neuromuscular transmission at
skeletal muscle junctions, leading to fluctuating muscle weakness and fatigability.
Epidemiology
•Can affect any age or gender
•Women: more common under 40 years
•Men: more common over 60 years
•Cardiac and smooth muscles are not affected
Pathophysiology
•Autoantibodies against acetylcholine receptors (AChR) or associated proteins (MuSK,
LRP4) at the neuromuscular junction.
•Leads to impaired synaptic transmission and decreased muscle contraction
