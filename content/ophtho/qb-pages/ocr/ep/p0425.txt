Bacterial Conjunctivitis
1. Acute Bacterial
•Organisms: Staph aureus, S. pneumoniae, H. influenzae, Moraxella
•Symptoms: red eye, burning, mucopurulent discharge, lid sticking
•Signs: papillae, chemosis, ± corneal ulcer (gonococcus, meningococcus)
• Treatment:
• Stop contact lenses
• Topical antibiotic drops + ointment
• Systemic antibiotics for gonococcal/ H. influenzae infection
2. Adult Chlamydial (Inclusion) Conjunctivitis
•C. trachomatis D—K (sexually transmitted)
•Findings: follicles (upper/lower tarsal), preauricular LN, mucopurulent discharge
• Treatment:
• Oral azithromycin / doxycycline / erythromycin
• Topical tetracycline / erythromycin
• Treat sexual partner; screen for STIs
