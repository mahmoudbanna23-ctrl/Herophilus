3. Unilateral dilated pupil is not caused by:
a. Horner syndrome
b. Ocular trauma
c. Atropine toxicity
d. Third cranial nerve palsy
Horner syndrome causes miosis (small pupil), not dilation. Dilated
pupils result from third nerve palsy, ocular trauma, or exposure to
anticholinergic agents like atropine.
