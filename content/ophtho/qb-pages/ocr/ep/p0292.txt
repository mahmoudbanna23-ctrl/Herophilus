Dacryocystitis
Causative organisms: Staph, Strep
Acute:
•Painful, tender swelling at medial canthus
•May form abscess preseptal cellulitis
Rx: Warm compress + nasal decongestant + systemic antibiotics
After inflammation subsides DCR
Chronic:
•Painless epiphora
• Mucocele formation
•Recurrent conjunctivitis
•+ve Regurgitation test
Rx: DCR (definitive)
