4. Cause ofvision loss in diabetic retinopathy?
a. Vitreous hemorrhage
b. Cataract
c. Glaucoma
In diabetic retinopathy, vision loss most commonly occurs due to
vitreous hemorrhage, which results from rupture of fragile new vessels
(neovascularization) in proliferative diabetic retinopathy. Cataract and
glaucoma may also coexist but are less frequent direct causes Of sudden
vision loss in diabetic patients.
