Humor
•Aqueous humor: Watery, drained via episcleral venous plexus
•Vitreous humor: Gel-like, avascular, 2/3 of eye volume, 98% water +
collagen, mucopolysaccharides, hyaluronic acid
Eye Segments
•Anterior segment: Cornea, anterior chamber, iris, lens, posterior chamber
• Ciliary sulcus: Peripheral posterior chamber; alternative site for PC IOL
fixation
•Posterior segment: Vitreous humor, retina, choroid, optic disc, optic nerve
Sclera & Choroid
•Sclera: Protects retina from overexposure to light
•Choroid: Provides blood supply to outer retinal layers
