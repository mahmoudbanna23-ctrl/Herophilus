Lagophthalmos (Incomplete lid closure)
Causes:
•Facial nerve palsy (Bell's palsy, trauma, tumor, CVA)
•Cicatricial (after surgery or scarring)
•Physiologic nocturnal (during sleep)
•Medication-related (sedatives, ICIJ patients)
•Functional (GA, unconsciousness)
Complication: exposure keratitis ulceration
Treatment:
•Artificial tears (day) + ointment (night)
•Lid taping at bedtime
•Tarsorrhaphy or gold weight implant if chronic
•Treat underlying cause
