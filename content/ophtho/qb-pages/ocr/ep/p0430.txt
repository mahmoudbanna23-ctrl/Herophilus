Conjunctival Degenerations
Pterygium
•Definition: Fibrovascular, triangular, wing-shaped growth of conjunctiva onto the cornea, usually from
the nasal side.
•Effects:
Can induce astigmatism
May cover the pupil, decreasing vision
•Indications for Surgical Excision:
• Chronic inflammation
• Threat to visual axis
• Induced astigmatism
Cosmetic concern
•Management:
• Lubricating drops for mild irritation
• Simple excision with bare sclera
• Recurrence:
after simple excision
to 5% with conjunctival autograft or mitomycin-C adjunct
