2. Ciliary Body
•Ring-shaped thickened tissue between iris and choroid.
•Contains blood vessels, connective tissue, and ciliary muscle.
•Divided into:
• Pars plicata (anterior) aqueous humour secretion
• Pars plana (posterior)
Functions:
Accommodation
•Ciliary muscle contraction zonules relax lens becomes convex near focus
•Relaxation lens flattens distant focus
Aqueous humour production
Attachment for zonular fibers (lens support)
