Anterior Chamber
•Shallow: corneal wound or lens swelling
•Deep: scleral rupture
•Hyphaema: blood in AC (graded I—IV)
• Complications: TIOP, corneal blood staining, CRAO
• Tx: rest, head elevation 450, cycloplegic, steroid, IOP control, surgery if persistent
•Hypopyon: pus from infection (endophthalmitis)
Iris & Pupil
•Iridodialysis D-shaped pupil
•Traumatic mydriasis / miosis
•Iris prolapse
•Late iris atrophy
Ciliary Body & IOP
•Hypotony suspect open globe
•Angle recession late secondary glaucoma risk
