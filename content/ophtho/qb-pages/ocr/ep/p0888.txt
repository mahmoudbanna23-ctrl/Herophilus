BRVO
•At A/V crossing due to compression by thickened arteriole.
•Fundus: sectoral hemorrhages, cotton-wool spots in affected quadrant.
•May cause sectoral field loss ± macular edema.
Management (CRVO / BRVO)
No therapy restores perfusion; treatment aims to prevent complications:
1. Treat underlying cause (HTN, DM, hyperlipidemia).
2.Laser photocoagulation (PRP) for ischemic areas / neovascularization.
3.1ntravitreal anti-VEGF or steroids —Y for macular edema.
4.Manage glaucoma if present.
