23. 50 years old female patient came to her family doctor complaining of
redness of her left eye. Examination showed 28 mmHg IOP, 6/24 VA, hazy
cornea and pupillary constriction.
The most probable diagnosis is?
A. Bacterial conjunctivitis
B. Acute closed angle glaucoma
C. Acute iridocyclitis
D. Bacterial keratitis
Inflammation of the iris and ciliary body causes photophobia,
small pupil, and moderate rise in IOR Cornea appears hazy due
to keratic precipitates.
