Central Retinal Vein Occlusion (CRVO) & Branch Retinal Vein Occlusion (BRVO)
Pathophysiology
•Thrombus in central/branch retinal vein venous congestion hemorrhages + ischemia.
•Second most common retinal vascular disorder (after diabetic retinopathy).
Risk Factors:
•HTN, DM, arteriosclerosis
•Glaucoma
•Smoking, hyperlipidemia
•Hyperviscosity (polycythemia, leukemia, sickle cell)
•Oral contraceptives, diuretics
