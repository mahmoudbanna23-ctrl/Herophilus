II. Topical Ocular Drugs
9. Antivirals
•Acyclovir (Zovirax) for herpes simplex keratitis.
•Use only under ophthalmologist supervision (short-term).
10. Anti-VEGF Agents
•Used for wet AMD, diabetic macular edema, PDR.
•Examples: Bevacizumab, Ranibizumab, Aflibercept (intravitreal).
•Systemic risks: Thrombosis, stroke, MI, hypertension.
