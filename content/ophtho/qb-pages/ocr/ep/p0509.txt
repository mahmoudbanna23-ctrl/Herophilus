32. 65-year-old man with history of a recent bronchitis awoke this morning
with a red eye and has no other symptoms. He has no significant medical
problems. On examination, the patient has a sector of the eye that is solid red
without injection of the conjunctival vessels.
What is the most likely diagnosis?
A. Scleritis
B. Subconjunctival hemorrhage
C. Early viral conjunctivitis
D. Pterygium
A subconjunctival hemorrhage appears as a sharply
demarcated red patch on the sclera without pain or
inflammation. It is benign and resolves spontaneously.
