I. All of the following tests are useful in evaluating a patient with a retained
iron intraocular foreign body EXCEPT:
a. Ultrasonography
b. CT scan
c. MRI
d. Indirect ophthalmoscopy
MRI is contraindicated in patients with possible iron
intraocular foreign because magnetic forces may move
the metal, causing further eye injury. CT scan and ultrasound
are safe alternatives.
