2. D-shaped pupil is present in:
a. Iridodonesis
b. Iridodialysis
c. Posterior sychiae
d. Total sector iridectomy
Iridodialysis is a separation of the iris root from the ciliary body,
often due to trauma, giving a D-shaped pupil.
