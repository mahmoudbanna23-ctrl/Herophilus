Globe Displacements
Exophthalmos (Proptosis)
Definition:
Forward displacement of the globe (>22 mm).
Measured with Hertel exophthalmometer.
Causes:
1.Graves' disease (most common in adults).
2.0rbital cellulitis (most common in children).
3.0rbital tumors (e.g., lymphoma).
4. Orbital/retrobulbar hemorrhage (trauma/surgery).
5.Cavernous sinus thrombosis (life-threatening).
6.Carotid-cavernous fistula pulsating exophthalmos.
