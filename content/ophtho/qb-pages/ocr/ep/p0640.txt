The Crystalline Lens
Anatomy & Function
•Transparent, biconvex structure located behind the iris and in front of the vitreous.
•Works with the cornea to focus light on the retina — total refractive power z 18—20 D (z%
of eye's total).
•Suspended by zonules (Zinn's ligaments) from the ciliary body.
•No nerves, blood vessels, or connective tissue.
•Anterior surface: flatter; posterior: more convex.
•Diameter: NIO mm, thickness: N4 mm (increases with age).
•Posterior to lens: vitreous; anterior: iris and aqueous humor.
•Posterior chamber: space between iris and anterior capsule.
