(7) Sarcoidosis
Nature
•Chronic autoimmune, multisystem disease (lungs, lymph nodes, eye)
Investigations
•T Serum calcium
ACE level
•CXR: Hilar lymphadenopathy
•Biopsy: Non-caseating granulomas (easier from conjunctiva/lacrimal gland)
Ocular Manifestations
•Dry eyes (Keratoconjunctivitis sicca)
•Uveitis (anterior, posterior, or panuveitis)
•Retinal vasculitis, hemorrhages, neovascularization
•Optic neuropathy, cranial nerve palsies, hemianopia
Complications
•Cataract, glaucoma, posterior synechiae
Treatment
•Topical steroids anterior uveitis
•Systemic steroids ± immunosuppressants posterior uveitis
