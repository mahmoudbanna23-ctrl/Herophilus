Ocular Examination Overview
•Integral part of every general physical examination.
•Minimum required tests:
• Visual acuity
• Pupillary reactions
• Extraocular muscle function
• Fundus examination
•Additional tests (if indicated): visual fields, eyelid/conjunctiva/cornea inspection, anterior
chamber and intraocular pressure (IOP) assessment, fluorescein staining, and color vision
testing.
Basic Tools
Penlight — Mydriatic & anesthetic eye drops — Direct ophthalmoscope — Near vision card
— Fluorescein strips.
'Slit-lamp is standard for magnified anterior segment exam; with lenses, can asse
posterior segment too.
