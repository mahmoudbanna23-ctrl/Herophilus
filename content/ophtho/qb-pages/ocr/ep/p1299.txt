9. Glycemic control in diabetic retinopathy is best indicated by which of the
following?
a. Fasting blood glucose
b. Random blood glucose
c. HbA1c<70/0
d. Postprandial blood glucose
Good glycemic control in diabetic retinopathy is best assessed
by HbA1c, which reflects average blood glucose over 2—3
months. Maintaining slows disease progression.
