Corneal Epithelial Defects
Corneal Abrasion
•Loss of epithelium due to trauma.
•Fluorescein stain positive, pain relieved by topical anesthetic.
• Treatment:
• Topical antibiotics, lubricants, ± patch (if no infection).
• Cycloplegic for comfort.
• Refer if not healed in 48h.
Recurrent Epithelial Erosions (RCE)
•Recurrent spontaneous epithelial defects (often on awakening).
•Causes: Previous abrasion or epithelial adhesion defect.
•Treatment: As for abrasion + ophthalmology referral.
