Juvenile Idiopathic Arthritis (JIA)
•Causes chronic asymptomatic iritis
•May lead to calcific band keratopathy
Sickle Cell Disease
•Baseline ophthalmic evaluation essential if new floaters or visual loss
AIDS Ophthalmopathy
•Most common ocular involvement: retina
•Can cause:
• AIDS retinopathy
• CMV retinitis (sharply demarcated sectors along retinal vessels; poor prognosis even
on HAART)
• Kaposi sarcoma (conjunctiva/skin)
• Herpes Zoster Ophthalmicus (HZO)
• HSV keratitis
