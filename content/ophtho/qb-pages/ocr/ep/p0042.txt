9. Additional Tests
Fluorescein Staining of the Cornea
a.
•Purpose: Detects defects in the corneal epithelium.
•Method:
• Apply fluorescein strip (moistened with saline/water/topical anesthetic) to palpebral
conjunctiva.
• After a few blinks, dye spreads over cornea.
• Bright green areas under cobalt-blue light = epithelial loss/damage.
•Precautions:
• Use fluorescein strips, not stored solutions (risk of Pseudomonas contamination).
• Remove soft contact lenses before use to avoid staining.
