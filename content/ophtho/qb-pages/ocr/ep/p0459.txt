8. A I O-year-old boy presents in spring with severe itching, tearing, and
photophobia in both eyes. On examination, you notice large cobblestone
papillae on the upper tarsal conjunctiva. What finding is characteristic of this
condition?
a. Tranta spots
b. Pseudomembranes
c. Corneal neovascularization
d. Kayser-Fleischer rings
Vernal keratoconjunctivitis presents with cobblestone papillae and Tranta
spots—white, chalky dots at the limbus representing eosinophil
accumulation. It is a seasonal allergic condition.
