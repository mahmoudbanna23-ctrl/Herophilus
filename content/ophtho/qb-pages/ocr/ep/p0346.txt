Infections & Inflammations
2. Hordeolum Internum
•Acute infection Of Meibomian glands (Staph. aureus)
•Painful, tender lid swelling away from margin
•Treatment:
• Warm compresses + gentle massage
• Topical ± oral antibiotics (if severe)
• Incise only if abscess forms
3. Chalazion (Meibomian cyst)
•Chronic granulomatous inflammation (sterile)
•Due to blocked Meibomian duct
•Painless firm nodule in tarsal plate
• Treatment:
Warm compress & massage
• Incision & curettage via conjunctival Side
• Steroid injection optional
• Recurrent biopsy (exclude sebaceous carcinoma)
