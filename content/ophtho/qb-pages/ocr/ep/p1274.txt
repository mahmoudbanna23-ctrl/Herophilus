•Macular Edema:
• Clinically significant if fovea involved
• Dx: Hard exudates/ retinal thickening
• Rx: Anti-VEGF (bevacizumab, ranibizumab, aflibercept)
•Proliferative DR (PDR):
• Hallmark: Neovascularization
• Rx: Pan Retinal Photocoagulation (PRP) with argon laser
• PRP reduces vision loss by 50—90% if timed correctly
•Glycemic & BP Control:
• Optimal HbA1c < 7% (tight control prevents DR)
Tight BP control also essential
• Retinal circulation = end-artery system
•Other Notes:
• Diabetic cataract sorbitol accumulation
• Pupil spared in diabetic 3rd nerve palsy
• Refractive shifts: Hyperglycemia myopic; Hypoglycemia hyperopi
