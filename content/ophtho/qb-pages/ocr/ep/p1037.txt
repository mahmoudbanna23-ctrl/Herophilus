12. Most common cause of bilateral abducent nerve palsy?
a. Elevated intracranial pressure
b. Intracranial tumor
c. Trauma
d. Infection
Intracranial pressure causes downward traction on both abducens
nerves as they run along the clivus, leading to bilateral abducent
nerve palsy. It's the most common cause due to the long intracranial
course of CN VI.
