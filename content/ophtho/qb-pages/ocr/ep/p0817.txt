I O. A 65-year-old patient presents with sudden onset severe eye pain, nausea,
and seeing colored halos around lights. On examination, there is ciliary
injection, a cloudy cornea, and a mid-dilated vertically oval non-reactive pupil.
What is the most likely diagnosis?
a. Acute angle-closure glaucoma
b. Open-angle glaucoma
c. Anterior uveitis
d. Optic neuritis
Sudden painful red eye, halos around lights, and mid-dilated
pupil are hallmarks Of acute angle-closure glaucoma. It is an
Ophthalmic emergency requiring immediate treatment.
