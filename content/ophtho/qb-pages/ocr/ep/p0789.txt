Primary Open-Angle Glaucoma (POAG)
Definition:
Chronic, bilateral, progressive optic neuropathy with open angles.
Risk factors:
•T IOP (>21 mmHg)
•Age
•Family history
•African descent
•Myopia, DM, HTN, thyroid disease
Clinical:
•Usually asymptomatic until late
•Peripheral field loss "tunnel vision"
•Optic disc cupping
•Elevated IOP
