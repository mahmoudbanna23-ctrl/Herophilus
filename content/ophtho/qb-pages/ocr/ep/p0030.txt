2. Intraocular Changes
•Lens:
• The crystalline lens continues to grow throughout life crowding of the anterior chamber
angle predisposes to angle-closure glaucoma, especially in hyperopic patients.
• Yellowing and opacification of the lens nucleus -5 nuclear sclerosis (cataract).
•Aqueous Humor:
• Decreased trabecular outflow gradual increase in intraocular pressure (lop) open-
angle glaucoma.
•Vitreous Humor:
• Vitreous syneresis (degeneration and liquefaction) posterior vitreous detachment (PVD)
may cause retinal traction, retinal tears, or retinal detachment.
•Cornea:
• Reduced endothelial cell density impaired corneal dehydration mechanism possible
chronic corneal edema.
•Pupil:
• Becomes smaller and less reactive to light and accommodation.
