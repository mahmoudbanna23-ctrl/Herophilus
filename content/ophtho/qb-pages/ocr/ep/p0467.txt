12. Which of the following causes red eye without vision loss?
a. Conjunctivitis
b. Acute glaucoma
c. Endophthalmitis
d. Corneal ulcer
Conjunctivitis causes redness without vision loss because it
only affects the conjunctiva. In contrast, glaucoma, corneal
ulcer, and endophthalmitis involve deeper ocular structures,
often impairing vision.
