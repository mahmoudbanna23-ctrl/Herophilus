6. Extraocular Muscle Testing (Ocular Alignment & Movements)
Purpose: Test each extraocular muscle in its primary field of action using the 6 cardinal
positions of gaze.
For Suspected Strabismus:
1. General Inspection:
• Detect deviation, abnormal head posture, or nystagmus.
• Deviation types:
Concomitant: angle same in all gaze directions.
Incomitant: angle changes (suggests muscle palsy).
'Nystagmus always refer to ophthalmologist.
2. Corneal Light Reflex (Hirschberg Test):
'Shine penlight from 2/3 m in primary gaze.
• Normally: symmetric reflection on both corneas.
Deviating eye: reflex displaced opposite the deviation.
Useful for infants/young children.
