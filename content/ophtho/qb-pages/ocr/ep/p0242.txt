6. Which of the following is found in preseptal cellulitis?
a. Chemosis
b. Proptosis
c. Pain on eye movement
d. Normal ocular mobility
In preseptal cellulitis, infection is confined anterior to the orbital septum, so
eye movements remain normal. Proptosis, pain on movement, and
chemosis occur only in orbital cellulitis.
