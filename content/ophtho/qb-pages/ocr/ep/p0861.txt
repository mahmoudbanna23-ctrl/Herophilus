2. Which of the following is a high risk factor for vitreous hemorrhage?
a. High myopia
b. Hyperopia
c. Astigmatism
d. Presbyopia
High myopia increases the risk of vitreous hemorrhage due to
retinal thinning and predisposition to retinal tears. It also raises
the risk of retinal detachment.
