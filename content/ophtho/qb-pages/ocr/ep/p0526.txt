Episcleritis
Definition: Inflammation of episcleral tissue (superficial to sclera).
Features
•Common, benign, self-limited.
•Often unilateral (2/3), females > males (3:1).
•May recur (check for systemic inflammatory disease).
Types
1.Simple (more common) : diffuse or sectoral redness.
2.Nodular —:discrete elevated inflamed nodule (slower course).
Causes
•Idiopathic (most common)
•Collagen vascular (RA, SLE)
•Vasculitides (PAN, Behcet, GCA)
•IBD, Rosacea, gout, atopy, herpes, syphilis.
