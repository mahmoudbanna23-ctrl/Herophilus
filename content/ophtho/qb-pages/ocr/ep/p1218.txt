7. A patient presents after ocular trauma, and ophthalmoscopic examination
reveals a white crescent-shaped streak at the posterior pole. What is the most
likely diagnosis?
a) Choroidal rupture
b) Retinal detachment
c) Commotio retinae
d) Vitreous hemorrhage
A choroidal rupture appears as a crescent-shaped white streak
at the posterior pole after blunt trauma due to tearing Of
Bruch's membrane and the choroid.
