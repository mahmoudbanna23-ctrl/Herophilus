Posterior Uveitis (Choroiditis / Chorioretinitis)
Definition: Inflammation of choroid ± retina
Causes:
•Infectious:
• Bacterial: TB, Syphilis
• Viral: HZV, CMV(esp. AIDS)
• Fungal: Candida, Histoplasma
• Parasitic: Toxoplasmosis (most common)
•Non-infectious:
• Autoimmune (Behcet's disease)
• Malignancy (Masquerade syndrome: lymphoma, melanoma)
Symptoms:
•Painless visual loss
•Floaters
