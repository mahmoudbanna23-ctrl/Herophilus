Viral Conjunctivitis
•Adenovirus (90%) : highly contagious
• Types:
• Pharyngoconjunctival fever (Adeno 3, 4, 7)
• Epidemic keratoconjunctivitis (Adeno 8, 19, 37) most severe
•Features: watery discharge, follicles, pseudomembrane, preauricular LN, subepithelial
infiltrates
•Treatment: hygiene + symptomatic care
• Topical steroids (short course if severe keratitis)
• Antibiotics if secondary infection
