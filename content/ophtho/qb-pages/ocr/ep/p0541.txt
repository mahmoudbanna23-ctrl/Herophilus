4. Bluish discoloration of the eye sclera occurs in which of the following
conditions?
A. Scleritis
B. Episcleritis
C. Osteogenesis imperfecta
D. Scleral tumor
The sclera is whitish in colour but can attain a bluish discolouration due to
thinning and subsequent transparency of scleral collagen, which allows
increased visibility of underlying choroidal pigment. This can be seen in
some conditions such as:
Marfan syndrome.
Osteogenesis imperfecta.
Buphthalmos.
