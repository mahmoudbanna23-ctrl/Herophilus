Clinical Features
•History of projectile injury, pain, Uvision, floaters
•Entry site on cornea or sclera
•Positive Seidel test (leakage)
•Iris defect or peripheral lens opacity along entry track
Complications
•Endophthalmitis
•Traumatic cataract
•Retinal detachment
• Vitreous hemorrhage
Management
•Protective eye shield
•IV broad-spectrum antibiotics
•Surgical removal of IOFB ± repair retinal tears or detachments
•Intravitreal antibiotics for contaminated FBs
