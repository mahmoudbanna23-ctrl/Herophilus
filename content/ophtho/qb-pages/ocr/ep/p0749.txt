5. A 28-year-old patient presents with sudden onset of unilateral eye redness,
pain, and photophobia. Work-up reveals uveitis. Which systemic condition is
most likely associated with this presentation?
a. Ankylosing spondylitis
b. Diabetes mellitus
c. Hypertension
d. Hyperthyroidism
Anterior uveitis is commonly associated with HLA-B27 diseases
such as ankylosing spondylitis. It presents with unilateral
redness, pain, and photophobia.
