7. patient with viral conjunctivitis is asking about treatment. What is
recommended?
a. Topical vasoconstrictors
b. Topical antibiotics
c. Topical steroids
d. Topical antivirals
Viral conjunctivitis is self-limiting, and treatment is mainly
with lubricants or mild vasoconstrictors. Antibiotics
and steroids are not indicated unless secondary infection
occurs.
