5. Retinoblastoma
Most common primary intraocular malignancy in children.
Types:
•Hereditary: Bilateral, multifocal
•Sporadic: Unilateral, unifocal
Spread: Direct, extraocular, or hematogenous.
Clinical features:
•Leukocoria or strabismus
•US/CT: Calcified intraocular mass
Treatment:
•Radiotherapy, chemotherapy, laser, cryotherapy, or enucleation
•Screen siblings / offspring
