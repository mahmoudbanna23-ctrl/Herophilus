• STRABISMUS (SQUINT)
Definition
Misalignment of the eyes so that both eyes cannot focus on the same object
simultaneously.
Pseudostrabismus
•False appearance of eye deviation.
•Visual axes are properly aligned.
•Causes:
• Prominent epicanthal folds
• Hypertelorism (wide-set eyes)
Sequelae of Strabismus
Age group
Children yrs)
Older individuals
Sequelae
Amblyopia (lazy eye)
Diplopia and visual confusion
