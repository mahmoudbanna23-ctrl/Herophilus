14. A 40-year-old woman complains of recent changes in her facial appearance,
insomnia, and palpitations. What is the most appropriate initial investigation?
a) Electrocardiogram (ECG)
b) Thyroid function tests
c) Serum cortisol level
d) Blood glucose level
Symptoms like facial changes, insomnia, and palpitations
Suggest hyperthyroidism. The initial diagnostic Step is to check
thyroid function (T3, T4, TSH).
