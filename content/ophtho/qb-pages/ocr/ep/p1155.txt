Quick Notes
Eyelid Malignancies
•Most common eyelid malignancy: Basal cell carcinoma (BCC)
• Typical site: Lower lid near medial canthus
•Squamous cell carcinoma (SCC):
• Typical site: Upper lid, lateral canthus
• Behavior: Local invasion by perineural infiltration
• Appearance: Keratotic, flaky
• In immunocompromised: More aggressive
