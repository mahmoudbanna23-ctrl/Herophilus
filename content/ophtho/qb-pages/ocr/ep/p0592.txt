6. Deficiency of which vitamin can cause comeal ulcers?
a. Vitamin A
b. Vitamin C
c. Vitamin D
d. Vitamin E
Vitamin A deficiency causes xerophthalmia and
keratomalacia, leading to corneal ulceration. It is common in
malnutrition and can cause permanent blindness if untreated.
