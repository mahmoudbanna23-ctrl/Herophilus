Intermediate Uveitis (Pars Planitis / Cyclitis)
Definition: Inflammation of vitreous & peripheral retina.
Age: 15—40 years (often bilateral, asymmetric).
Causes:
•Infectious: TB, Syphilis, Toxoplasmosis, Toxocariasis
•Non-infectious: Sarcoidosis, Multiple sclerosis
Symptoms:
•Floaters
•Blurred vision (insidious onset)
Signs:
•Vitreous cells & haziness
•Snowballs: white aggregates in inferior vitreous
•Snowbanking: grey-white plaques on pars plana
•May have anterior uveitis in 50%
