Management (Acute Attack)
Immediate Medical:
•Topical 6-blocker, a-agonist, pilocarpine (after IOP drops)
•IV/Oral CAI (acetazolamide)
•IV mannitol (osmotic)
•Topical steroid
•Analgesic / antiemetic
•Supine position & hospitalize
Definitive Treatment:
•Laser iridotomy (both eyes!)
•± Lensectomy / Trabeculectomy / Valve / Cyclophotocoagulation (if refractory)
