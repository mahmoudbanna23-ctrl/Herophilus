Eyelid Malpositions
Ptosis (Drooping upper lid)
1.
Definition: Upper lid covers >2 mm of cornea
Causes (Types):
•Congenital: defective levator development poor function
•Paralytic: Ill nerve palsy
•Myogenic: myasthenia gravis, muscular dystrophy
•Sympathetic: Horner's syndrome (Müller's muscle)
•Aponeurotic (senile): dehiscence of levator aponeurosis good function
•Mechanical: tumor, edema, scarring
• Traumatic
Treatment:
•Surgical (levator resection / frontalis sling)
•Treat cause (e.g. MG)
