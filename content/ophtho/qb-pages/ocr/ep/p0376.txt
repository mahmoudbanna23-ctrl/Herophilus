9. Which of the following is among the complications of herpes zoster
ophthalmicus?
A. Dacryocystitis
B. Entropion
C. Trigeminal nerve palsy
D. None of the above
The most important complication of HZO is post-herpetic
neuralgia. Other complications: Cicatrisation of the skin of the
eyelids which can result in ptosis, lid scarring, ectropion, and
entropion.
