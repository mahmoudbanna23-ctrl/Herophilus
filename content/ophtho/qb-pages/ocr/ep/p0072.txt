4. The following cranial nerve is tested by corneal sensation testing:
a. Optic nerve
b. Trigeminal nerve
c. Abducent nerve
d. Trochlear nerve
Corneal sensation is mediated by the ophthalmic branch of the
trigeminal nerve (cranial nerve VI). Loss indicates trigeminal
dysfunction.
