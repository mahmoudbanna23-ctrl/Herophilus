Hypertension & Hypertensive Retinopathy
•Chronic HTN arteriolar sclerosis
• Diastolic BP >IOO increased light reflex copper wire silver wire arterioles
•Arteries & veins share common CT sheath in retina
•Severe venous nicking predisposes to BRVO
•Malignant HTN macular star, optic disc edema, exudative retinal detachment
•Avoid rapid BP lowering risk of optic nerve infarction
Pregnancy
• Visual changes may indicate impending seizure in preeclampsia
•Severe retinal changes = indication for termination in eclampsia
•Exudative retinal detachment occurs in NIO% of eclamptic patients
•Diabetic women:
• Baseline eye exam 1st trimester
• NPDR may worsen 2nd trimester, improve later
• Follow-up: at least once per trimester
•Gestational DM risk factor for DR
