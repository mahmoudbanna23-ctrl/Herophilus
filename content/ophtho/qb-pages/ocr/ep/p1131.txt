7. What should pediatricians do for all neonates and infants?
a. Red reflex testing
b. Fundus photography
c. OCT
d. Visual acuity testing
All neonates should undergo red reflex testing to detect early
ocular abnormalities such as congenital cataract or
retinoblastoma, which can threaten vision or life.
