Retina & Optic Nerve
•Optic nerve axons: From ganglion cell layer of retina
•Entry/exit of central retinal vessels: Optic cup
•Orientation:
• Optic nerve & disc Nasal
• Macula & fovea Temporal
Pupillary Reactions
•Important for evaluation of CNS & ocular diseases
•Pupil appearance: Jet black in newborns
Ocular Adnexa
•Orbit: Volume N30 mL, eye occupies 6.5 mL
•Extraocular muscles (EOMs): 6 muscles per eye + levator palpebrae superioris
•Eye movements:
• Monocular: Ductions
• Binocular, same direction: Versions
• Binocular, opposite direction: Vergences
