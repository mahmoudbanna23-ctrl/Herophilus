B. Excretory (Drainage) Part.
Structure
Lacrimal puncta
Lacrimal canaliculi
Lacrimal sac
Nasolacrimal duct
Function
Tiny openings on medial lid margins collecting tears.
Convey tears to lacrimal sac.
Located in the lacrimal sac fossa at the anteroinferior medial orbital wall.
Drains tears from the sac into the nasal cavity under the inferior
turbinate.
Functions of the Lacrimal System
•Maintains a moist ocular surface.
•Provides antibacterial protection (contains lysozyme).
•Washes away debris.
•Drains tears into the nasal cavity.
