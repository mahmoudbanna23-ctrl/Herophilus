Trachoma
•Cause: C. trachomatis A, B, Ba, C
•Common in: poor, crowded areas (Middle East, Africa)
•Stages (WHO):
• TF: follicles (25, >0.5 mm)
• Tl: intense inflammation, obscures tarsal vessels
• TS: scarring of conjunctiva
• TT: trichiasis (lashes touch globe)
• CO: corneal opacity
•Complications: trichiasis, entropion, corneal scarring blindness
•Treatment: SAFE strategy:
• Surgery for trichiasis
• Antibiotics (azithromycin single dose)
• Facial hygiene
• Environmental improvement
