9. A 7-year-old boy with Down syndrome shows an angulation of the lower
eyelid when looking downward. What is the name of this clinical sign?
a. Munson'S Sign
b. Arlt's line
c. Kayser-Fleischer ring
d. Hutchinson sign
Munson's sign is the V-shaped indentation of the lower eyelid
in downgaze seen in keratoconus. It occurs due to forward
protrusion of the conical cornea.
