2. The most common cause ofunilateral proptosis in an adult is:
a. Cavernous sinus thrombosis
b. Orbital cellulitis
c. Thyroid eye disease
d. Carotid cavernous fistula
In adults, the most common cause of unilateral (and bilateral)
proptosis is thyroid eye disease due to orbital tissue
inflammation and expansion.
