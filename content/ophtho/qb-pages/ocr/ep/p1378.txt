Female + history of gout or rheumatoid arthritis + localized redness + localized
tenderness + visual acuity normal + sclera uninvolved = Episcleritis
Female + history of gout or rheumatoid arthritis + localized redness + localized
tenderness + visual acuity normal + sclera involved = Scleritis
Redness improves with topical vasoconstrictor = Episcleritis (superficial)
Redness does not improve with topical vasoconstrictor = Scleritis (deep)
Eye pain + lacrimation + blepharospasm + photophobia + vision = Acute uveitis/
uveal case
Acute inflammation signs (redness + tenderness) + miotic pupil Acute iridocyclitis
Erythematous nodules + painful joints + oral & genital ulcers = Behcet disease
Yellow reflex in vitreous + no proptosis + no limitation of eye movement =
Endophthalmitis
Yellow reflex in vitreous + proptosis + limitation of eye movement = Panophthalmitis
