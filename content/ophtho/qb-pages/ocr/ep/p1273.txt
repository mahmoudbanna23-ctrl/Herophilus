Quick Notes
Diabetic Retinopathy (DR)
•Screening & Diagnosis:
• Any retinal vascular problem Fluorescein Angiography, OCT
• Complications: Neovascular Glaucoma (NVG), vitreous hemorrhage, tractional
retinal detachment
•Epidemiology & Risk:
• Leading causes of irreversible blindness worldwide: AMD, glaucoma, DR
• MCC of blindness (reversible): Cataract
• MCC of vision loss in DR: Diabetic Macular Edema (DME)
• Type 1 DM:
• After 5 yrs 20% affected
• After 10—15 yrs affected
• Type 2 DM:
• At diagnosis 20% affected
• After 20 yrs 50% develop PDR
