Anterior Uveitis (Iritis / Iridocyclitis)
Signs:
•Miosis (sluggish pupil)
•Perilimbal/ciliary injection (violaceous hue)
•AC cells & flare (WBCs & protein "plasmoid aqueous")
•Hypopyon (pus in AC)
•Keratic precipitates (KPs) on corneal endothelium
•U IOP (ciliary body shutdown) or IOP (trabeculitis / blockage)
Complications:
•Secondary glaucoma
•Posterior synechiae irregular pupil
•Complicated cataract
•Band keratopathy (esp. JIA)
•Macular edema
