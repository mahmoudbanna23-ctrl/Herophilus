4. A 39-year-old gentleman came to the clinic complaining of recurrent
bilateral severe itching and mucous ropy discharge. On examination, there is
bilateral small papillae with diffuse conjunctival injection and chemosis. The
skin of the eye lids of both eyes was dry, red and scaly. Which of the following
is the most probable diagnosis?
a. Bacterial conjunctivitis
b. Allergic conjunctivitis
c. Adenoviral conjunctivitis
d. Dacryocystitis
Allergic conjunctivitis presents with bilateral ropy mucous
discharge, and conjunctival papillae. It is Often associated with
atopic dermatitis, as seen with dry, red, scaly eyelids.
