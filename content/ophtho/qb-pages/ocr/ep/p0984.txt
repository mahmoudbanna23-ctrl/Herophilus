Dorsal Midbrain (Parinaud) Syndrome
•Lesion: Upper midbrain (tectum).
•Triad (3 Ls):
• Light-near dissociation (absent light reflex, intact accommodation)
• Loss of upgaze
• Lid retraction (Collier sign)
•Causes: Hydrocephalus, MS, stroke, tumor, midbrain hemorrhage.
•Investigation: MRI mandatory.
