Keratoconus
- Bilateral, painless, progressive vision (irregular astigmatism/scarring)
- Non-inflammatory, asymmetric, familial
- Begins 2nd decade, slows later
- Associated: Down's, atopy (vernal catarrh), post-LASIK, rubbing
- Signs:
- Munson's sign (lower lid angulation)
- Acute hydrops (Descemet's breaks edema)
Topography (curvature), Pachymetry (thickness), Pentacam
- lx:
- Early Spectacles
- Moderate RGP lenses / ICR
- Never refractive surgery
- Cross-linking halts progression
- Keratoplasty for scarred stage
