Dacryoadenitis (Inflammation of lacrimal gland)
Aetiology
•Infectious: EBV (most common viral), others (adenovirus, HSV, mumps, bacteria)
•Autoimmune: Sjögren's, thyroid eye disease
•Inflammatory/Neoplastic: NSOI, sarcoidosis
Clinical Features
Acute:
•Pain in superolateral orbit
•Tender swelling outer % of upper lid S-shaped ptosis
•Red eye, fever, proptosis, diplopia (especially up/lateral gaze)
Chronic:
•Painless enlargement of lacrimal gland
