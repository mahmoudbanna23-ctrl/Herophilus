8. A 30-year-old woman presents with sudden ocular pain after her 6-month-
old child accidentally scratched her eye with a fingernail. What is the most
appropriate immediate treatment?
a. Topical antibiotics
b. Topical steroids
c. Observation
d. Cycloplegics
A comeal abrasion from a fingernail increases infection risk;
thus, topical antibiotics are given to prevent bacterial keratitis.
Steroids are contraindicated as they delay healing.
