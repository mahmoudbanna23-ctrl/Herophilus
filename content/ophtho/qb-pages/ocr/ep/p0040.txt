8. Direct Ophthalmoscopy (Fundoscopy)
Purpose: Examine retina, optic disc, vessels, and macula.
Steps:
1. Dim lights, remove glasses, patient looks straight ahead.
2. Use right hand/right eye for patient's right eye.
3. Identify red reflex move closer at 150 angle.
4. Follow vessels to find optic disc (nasal side).
5. Examine:
Optic Disc: pink, sharp margin, central cup (C/D ratio 0.4).
I.C/D >0.5 or difference >0.2 suspect glaucoma.
Vessels: A:V ratio 2:3; observe crossings (HTN signs).
Fundus: uniform red-orange background.
Macula: darker, temporal to disc, foveal reflex visible.
