Diagnostic Criteria (All required for diagnosis):
I.NO history of penetrating ocular trauma or surgery.
2.No other ocular disease that explains the inflammation.
3. Bilateral granulomatous panuveitis.
4. Neurological and/or auditory manifestations:
• Headache (aseptic meningitis)
Tinnitus
Vertigo
Hearing loss
5.Skin and hair involvement:
Vitiligo (depigmented patches)
Alopecia
Poliosis (whitening of eyelashes or eyebrows)
