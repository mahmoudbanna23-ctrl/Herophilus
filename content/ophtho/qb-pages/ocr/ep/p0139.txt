1. Myopia (Near-sightedness)
Definition: Rays focus in front of retina.
Far point: in front of eye.
Types:
•Axial long eyeball (most common)
•Refractive curvature or RI (keratoconus, nuclear cataract)
Symptoms:
•Blurred distance vision
•Squinting to see clearly
•Normal near vision (except high degrees)
