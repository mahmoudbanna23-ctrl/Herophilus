(3) Pregnancy
Physiological Ocular Changes
•U Intraocular pressure
•U Corneal sensitivity
•Weak accommodation
•Dry eyes and refractive instability (avoid refractive surgery postpartum)
Pathological Ocular Conditions
A. Pregnancy-Induced Hypertension (PIH)
•Visual disturbances (blurred vision, field defects, diplopia)
•Retinal changes: arteriolar narrowing, hemorrhages, exudates, optic disc edema
•Exudative retinal detachment in 10% Of eclampsia
•Severe cases indication for termination of pregnancy
B. Diabetes Mellitus in Pregnancy
•Baseline eye exam in 1st trimester
•NPDR: may worsen during 2nd trimester improve later
•PDR: often progresses -5 close follow-up each trimester
•Gestational diabetes: no risk for DR
