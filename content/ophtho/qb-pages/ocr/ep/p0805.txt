4. A 55-year-old patient came to the clinic with frequent changes of the reading
glasses. On examination, the visual acuity was 6/6 and the intraocular pressure
was 28 mmHg in both eyes. Fundus examination showed a hemorrhage on the
optic disc of the right eye and the cup to disc ratio was 0.8 in both eyes. Which
of the following investigations is useful to confirm the diagnosis?
a. Automated perimetry
b. Fundus fluorescein angiography
c. Pentacam
d. Biometry
Automated perimetry assesses visual field loss typical Of
glaucoma. It confirms the diagnosis suggested by elevated IOP
and optic disc cupping.
