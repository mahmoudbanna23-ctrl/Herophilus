12. A patient complains of foreign body sensation, burning and redness of eye.
Dry eye disease was suspected so tear film breakup time test was done and it
was 13 seconds.
Which of the following applies to that patient?
A. Severe dry eye disease
B. Moderate dry eye disease
C. Mild dry eye disease
D. No dry eye disease
Normal tear film breakup time (TBUT) is >10 seconds. Since the
patient in the previous case has TBUT of 13 seconds, so he
does NOT have dry eye.
