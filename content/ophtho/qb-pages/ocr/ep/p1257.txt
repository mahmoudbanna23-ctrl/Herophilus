(2) Hypertension (HTN)
Pathogenesis
Changes due to:
1. Arteriolar sclerosis
2. Elevated blood pressure
Features
1. Arteriolar Sclerosis
•Thickened arteriole wall light reflex
•Copper-wire Silver-wire appearance
•A—V nicking (compression at crossing site) may cause Branch Retinal Vein Occlusion (BRVO)
2. Acute Hypertensive Changes
•Flame-shaped hemorrhages
•Cotton-wool spots
•Malignant HTN:
• Optic disc edema
• Macular star (exudation in macula)
• Exudative retinal detachment
