Pterygium
•Can induce astigmatism
•Recurrence rates:
• Simple excision: 1/3
• Conjunctival autograft: 5%
• Mitomycin-C adjunct reduces recurrence
Subconjunctival Hemorrhage (SCH)
•Usually asymptomatic reassurance if no suspicious findings
•Resolution: spontaneously in 2—3 weeks
•Red flag: Large SCH with history of trauma suspect globe rupture
