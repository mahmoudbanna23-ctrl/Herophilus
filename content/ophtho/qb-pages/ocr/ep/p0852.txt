Vitreous Disorders & Intraocular Infections
Posterior Vitreous Detachment (PVD)
Definition:
Separation of the vitreous body from the internal limiting membrane of the retina posterior to the
vitreous base.
Etiology:
•Age-related syneresis (liquefaction and shrinkage).
•May follow trauma or occur in high myopia.
Symptoms:
•Floaters: "Spots" or "cobwebs" moving with eye movement.
•Flashes (Photopsia): Due to vitreoretinal traction.
Signs:
•Weiss ring: Glial tissue ring around optic disc (seen in ophthalmoscopy).
Complications:
•Retina/ tear or detachment may cause vitreous hemorrhage.
Management:
•Dilated fundus exam to rule out retinal tears.
•No specific treatment for floaters/f/ashes unless complications.
