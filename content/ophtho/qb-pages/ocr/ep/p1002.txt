OPTIC NEURITIS
Definition: Inflammation and demyelination of the optic nerve.
Common in: Women 15—45 yrs.
Types
1.Papillitis — disc visible & swollen
2.Retrobulbar neuritis — disc appears normal
3.Neuroretinitis — with macular star (rare)
Retrobulbar Neuritis
Symptoms:
•Periocular pain 'T' with eye movement
•Acute/subacute visual loss
•Colour vision loss
•Central or centrocaecal scotoma
•Headache/tenderness
