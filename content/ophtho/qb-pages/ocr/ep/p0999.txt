OPTIC DISC SWELLING
Papilledema
Course:
•Early: Vision normal, mild hyperemia, loss of venous pulsation.
•Later: Transient visual obscurations.
•Late: Optic atrophy permanent blindness.
Symptoms: Headache, nausea, vomiting, transient blurred vision, diplopia.
Investigations:
•Urgent MRI / CT
•Lumbar puncture CSF pressure and analysis
