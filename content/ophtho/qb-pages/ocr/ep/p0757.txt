9. What is the percentage of aqueous humor that is drained by the uveo-scleral
pathway?
The trabecular pathway is responsible for 90% of the aqueous
outflow from the eye. On the other hand, the uveoscleral
pathway is responsible for 10% of the aqueous outflow from
the eye.
