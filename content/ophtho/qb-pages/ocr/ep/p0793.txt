Acute Angle-Closure Glaucoma (Ophthalmic Emergency!)
Symptoms:
•Sudden severe ocular pain, ...l, vision
•Colored halos around lights
•Headache, nausea, vomiting
Signs:
•Red eye, ciliary congestion
•Corneal edema (hazy cornea)
•Shallow AC
•Mid-dilated fixed pupil
•IOP (>40 mmHg, "stony hard")
Complications:
Permanent vision loss within hours—days.
