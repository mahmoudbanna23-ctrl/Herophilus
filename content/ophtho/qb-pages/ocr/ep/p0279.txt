Epiphora (Excessive tearing)
Treatment:
•Treat lid or surface causes
•Infants:
a) Massage + topical antibiotics (<1 yr 85% resolve)
b) Probing after 1 yr (repeat once if needed)
c) If fails Silastic intubation
d) If fails Dacryocystorhinostomy (DCR)
•Adults: DCR (probing ineffective)
