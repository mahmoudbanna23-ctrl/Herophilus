10. Which of the following eye conditions is best described as (inverted eyelid
margin)?
A. Trichiasis
B. Ectroion
C. Entropion
D. None of the above
Enroption -5 The eyelid margin is rotated inwards toward the eye. It most
commonly affects the lower lid.
Trichiasis The eyelid margin is in its normal anatomic condition, but the
eyelashes are misdirected toward the eye, causing irritation.
Ectropion The eyelid margin is rotated outward away from the eye. It
mainly affects the lower lid.
