Infections & Inflammations
1. Hordeolum Externum (Stye)
•Acute suppurative inflammation of lash follicle or Zeiss gland
•Organism: Staph. aureus
•Painful, red, tender swelling at lid margin with lash at apex
•Treatment:
• Topical antibiotics
• Epilate lash or small incision
• Never squeeze !
• Treat associated blepharitis
