Pediatric Blindness
•2nd MCC Retinopathy of Prematurity (ROP)
•5% of childhood blindness Primary congenital glaucoma
Glaucoma Associations
•Glaucoma of anterior uveitis = same mechanism as neovascular glaucoma
• Early: Secondary open-angle glaucoma
• Late: Secondary angle-closure glaucoma
•Hemorrhage on/around optic disc sign of active glaucomatous damage
•First retinal layer affected: Ganglion cell layer
Transient Visual Loss
•Minutes: Amaurosis fugax
•Seconds: Papilledema
