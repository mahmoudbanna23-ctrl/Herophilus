13. Cause of pinpoint pupil?
a. Organophosphorus poisoning
b. Mydriatics
c. Glaucoma
d. Horner syndrome
Organophosphorus compounds inhibit acetylcholinesterase, leading
to excess acetylcholine and constricted (pinpoint) pupils. Mydriatics
and Horner syndrome cause dilated pupils instead.
