Multiple Sclerosis (MS)
•MCC of optic neuritis
•Retrobulbar optic neuritis RAPD positive, central scotoma
•MRI demyelinating lesions in white matter & brainstem
•Can lead to optic atrophy
Sarcoidosis
•Chronic autoimmune disease non-caseating granulomas
•Ocular biopsy easier than mediastinal/bronchial biopsy
•CNS involvement more likely if ocular fundus affected
•Can cause bitemporal or homonymous hemianopia (chiasmal/retrochiasmal)
