Main Components
Lens Fibers
•Long, transparent, tightly packed cells (onion-like layers).
•Regions (from center outward):
• Embryonic nucleus
• Fetal nucleus
• Adult nucleus
. Cortex
•New fibers added to periphery increase lens thickness and hardness with age (nuclear
sclerosis).
•Mature fibers: no nuclei or organelles transparency.
