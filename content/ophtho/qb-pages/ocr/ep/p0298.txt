2. A 59-year-old lady presented with chronic painless tearing and recurrent
conjunctivitis in her right eye over the last year. On examination, there was a
positive regurge test on the affected side. Which of the following is the
treatment of choice?
a. Digital massage of the lacrimal sac
b. Nasolacrimal duct probing
C. Dacryocystorhinostomy
d. Systemic antibiotics
Chronic dacryocystitis due to nasolacrimal duct obstruction is
treated surgically by DCR, which creates a new drainage
pathway between the lacrimal sac and nasal cavity.
