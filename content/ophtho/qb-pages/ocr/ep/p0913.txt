7. A 20-year-old patient has gradual night vision loss, pale optic disc, and
attenuated vessels. Which tests help diagnose?
a. Electrophysiological tests
b. OCT
c. Fundus fluorescein angiography
d. Visual field test
Retinitis pigmentosa causes night blindness, attenuated
vessels, and pale disc. Electroretinography confirms diagnosis
by showing reduced photoreceptor activity.
