3. Lacrimal System
The lacrimal system is responsible for tear production and drainage.
Secretory Part
•Main lacrimal gland:
• Lies in the lacrimal fossa (superolateral part of orbit).
• Secretes tears via ducts onto the ocular surface.
•Accessory lacrimal glands: Present in the conjunctiva.
•Meibomian glands & goblet cells: Contribute lipid and mucous layers of the tear film.
