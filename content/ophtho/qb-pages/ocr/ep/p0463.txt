I O. What is the most common cause ofviral conjunctivitis?
a. Adenovirus
b. Herpes simplex virus
c. Varicella zoster virus
d. Enterovirus
Adenovirus is the most common cause of viral conjunctivitis,
often associated with respiratory infections. It spreads easily
and may occur in epidemics.
