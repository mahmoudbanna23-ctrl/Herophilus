HZO = Herpes Zoster Ophthalmicus
- Always unilateral (bilateral in immunocompromised)
- Nerves: Nasociliary, Frontal, Lacrimal
- Hutchinson sign tip of nose eye involved (75%)
- May 20 open-angle glaucoma (trabeculitis)
- CN palsy (111, IV, VII)
- Most imp complication: Postherpetic neuralgia (T with age)
- Early Rx (before rash) complications
HZO Treatment
- Topical steroids interstitial keratitis/uveitis
- Oral/lV steroids scleritis/retinitis/choroiditis/optic neuritis
- Neuralgia Gabapentin 200—600 mg/day
