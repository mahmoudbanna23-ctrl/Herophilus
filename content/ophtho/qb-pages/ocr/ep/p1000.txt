Pseudotumor Cerebri (Idiopathic Intracranial Hypertension)
•Definition: ICP without mass, thrombosis, or CSF abnormality.
•Epidemiology: Women 20—40 yrs, often after weight gain.
•Symptoms: Headache, transient visual blurring, pulsatile tinnitus, diplopia, neck stiffness,
pain with eye movement.
•Signs: Papilledema ± 6th nerve palsy.
•Treatment:
• Carbonic anhydrase inhibitors
• Weight reduction
• CSF shunt (lumboperitoneal/ventriculoperitoneal)
• Optic nerve sheath fenestration (to preserve vision)
