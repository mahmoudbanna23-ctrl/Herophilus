9. A 60-year-old patient presents for routine eye examination. Visual field
testing shows peripheral field defects. Intraocular pressure is elevated at 30
mmHg, and fundoscopy reveals optic disc cupping.
What is the most likely diagnosis?
a. Glaucoma
b. Optic neuritis
c. Papilledema
d. Retinitis pigmentosa
Elevated lop, optic disc cupping, and peripheral field loss are
classic features of chronic open-angle glaucoma. It is usually
asymptomatic until late stages.
