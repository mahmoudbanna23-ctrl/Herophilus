Red Reflex:
•Normal: uniform red glow.
•Abnormal:
• Black shadow: opacity (corneal scar, cataract).
• White reflex: retinoblastoma.
• Grey reflex: retinal detachment.
• Yellow reflex: pus in vitreous (endophthalmitis).
Pupil Dilation:
•Tropicamide 1% (parasympatholytic) mydriasis + cycloplegia.
•Phenylephrine 2.5% (sympathomimetic) mydriasis + lid elevation.
•Contraindicated: shallow AC (risk of angle closure), neuro monitoring cases.
