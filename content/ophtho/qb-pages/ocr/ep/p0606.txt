13. Which of the following is among the side effects that accompany the use of
local anaesthetics?
A. Corneal epithelial defect
B. Keratoconus
C. Corneal opacification
D. Retinal detachment
Local anesthetics cause corneal epithelial defects; this is why
they are not advised during the treatment Of photokeratitis (as
they delay the healing process by causing corneal epithelial
defects)
