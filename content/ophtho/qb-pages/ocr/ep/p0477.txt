17. What is the drug that is used to treat resistant cases ofvemal
keratoconjunctivitis?
A. Local decongestants
B. Anti-histamines
C. Mast cell stabilizers
D. Topical tacrolimus
Treatment of allergic conjunctivitis:
Cold compresses to relieve itching.
Artificial tears (to dilute allergens).
Topical mast cell stabilizers and antihistamines.
Topical steroids (although effective but are rarely needed).
Topical cyclosporine or topical tacrolimus may be
considered for severe cases and as steroidsparing agents (in
VKC).
