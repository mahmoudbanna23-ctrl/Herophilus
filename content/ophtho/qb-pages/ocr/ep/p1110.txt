Treatment
Definitive management is surgical — to prevent irreversible vision loss.
Performed as soon as diagnosis is confirmed.
Main surgical options:
1.Goniotomy (if cornea is clear)
2. Trabeculotomy (if cornea is hazy)
3. Trabeculectomy (or combined procedures in resistant cases)
Medical therapy: Only temporary — used to control IOP before surgery.
