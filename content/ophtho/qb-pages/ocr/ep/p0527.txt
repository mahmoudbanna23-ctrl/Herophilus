Episcleritis
Clinical Picture
•Symptoms: Red eye, mild pain/tenderness, photophobia.
•Signs: Sectoral/diffuse redness, movable tender nodule, no discharge.
•DDx test:
Phenylephrine 2.5% causes blanching in conjunctivitis, not episcleritis.
Treatment
•Usually self-limited.
•Supportive: cold compress, artificial tears.
•Oral NSAIDs (Ibuprofen 800 mg TDS).
•If recurrent systemic evaluation.
•Ophthalmologist may add topical steroids.
