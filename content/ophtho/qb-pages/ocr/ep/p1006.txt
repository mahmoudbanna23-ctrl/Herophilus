B. Arteritic (Giant Cell Arteritis—related) AION
•Occurs in: Elderly (> 60 yrs)
•Cause: Giant Cell (Temporal) Arteritis
Symptoms:
•Sudden, profound unilateral visual loss
•Temporal/scalp pain & tenderness
•Jaw claudication
•Headache, malaise, fever, myalgia, weight loss
•Amaurosis fugax may precede infarction
