Traumatic Cataract
•Any age; appears soon or years after trauma.
•Causes: blunt/penetrating trauma, chemical burns, radiation.
•Usually unilateral.
Complicated Cataract
•Secondary to ocular/systemic disease or drugs.
Ocular causes:
•Chronic uveitis
•High myopia
•Long-standing retinal detachment
•Retinitis pigmentosa
Systemic causes:
•Diabetes mellitus
•Hypocalcemia (hypoparathyroidism)
•Metabolic disorders: galactosemia, homocystinuria, Wilson's disease
Drugs:
•Corticosteroids (topical/systemic, prolonged use)
•Phenothiazines
