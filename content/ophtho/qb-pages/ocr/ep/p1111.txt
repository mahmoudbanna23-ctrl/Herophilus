Quick Notes
Leukocoria
•Most common cause: Cataract
•Most dangerous cause: Retinoblastoma
•Other less common causes:
• Persistent Hyperplastic Primary Vitreous (Persistent fetal vasculature)
• Toxocariasis
• Retinal detachment
• Coat's disease
•Retinoblastoma:
• Most common primary intraocular malignancy in children
• Imaging (US/CT): Calcified mass
• If hereditary: screen all siblings
•Screening test: Red reflex test (for all children)
