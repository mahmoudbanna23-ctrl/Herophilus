5. A 20-year-old female patient presented with one day of history of redness
and pain in the left upper eyelid. On examination, you noticed acute
suppurative inflammation of one of the lash follicles. Which of the following
terms describes the condition?
b. Chalazion
c. Hordeolum internum
d. Trichiasis
A Stye is an acute bacterial infection (usually Staphylococcus
aureus) Of an eyelash follicle or its sebaceous gland. It presents
with localized painful swelling, redness, and tenderness at the
eyelid margin.
