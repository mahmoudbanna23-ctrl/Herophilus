Nasolacrimal Duct Obstruction (NLD)
•Onset: Symptoms appear in first month of life
•Management:
• Most resolve spontaneously by 1 year
• Exceptions:
• Mucocele probing upon diagnosis
• Lacrimal abscess incision & drainage
Ophthalmia Neonatorum
•Mostly acquired from birth canal (STDs)
•Treatment:
• Gonococcal: Ceftriaxone
• Chlamydial: Erythromycin
