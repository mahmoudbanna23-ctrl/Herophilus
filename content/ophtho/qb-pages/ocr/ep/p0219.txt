Orbital Infections
Preseptal Cellulitis
•Anterior to orbital septum.
•Cause: trauma or skin infection.
•Common pathogens:
• H. influenzae (children)
• S. aureus, Streptococcus (adults)
•Treatment: Oral/systemic antibiotics (e.g., amoxicillin-clavulanate)
