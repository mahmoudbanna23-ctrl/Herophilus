8. Which is a complication of high myopia?
a. Rhegmatogenous retinal detachment
b. Glaucoma
c. Cataract
d. Optic neuritis
High myopia stretches and thins the retina, predisposing to
retinal tears and rhegmatogenous detachment. Other
complications may include glaucoma or cataract, but
detachment is most characteristic.
