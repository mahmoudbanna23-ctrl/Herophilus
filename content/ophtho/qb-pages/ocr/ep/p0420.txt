Conjunctivitis = inflammation of conjunctiva
Features:
•Redness (conjunctival injection)
•Discharge (watery / mucoid / mucopurulent / purulent)
•FB sensation, itching, tearing
•±follicles or papillae, ± chemosis, ± membranes
Types
Non-infectious
•Allergic (most common, 15—40%)
•Toxic (e.g., Brimonidine, chemicals, smoke)
•Secondary (dacryocystitis, cellulitis, immune disorders)
Infectious
•Viral (most common overall)
•Bacterial
•Chlamydial
•Fungal / Parasitic (rare)
