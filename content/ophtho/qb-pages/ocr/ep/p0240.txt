5. What is the distance between the tip of the cornea and the plane of the lateral
orbital margin?
a. 1—11 mm
b. 12—22 mm
c. 23—30 mm
d. 31—40 mm
The normal exophthalmometric reading (corneal apex to
lateral orbital rim) ranges between 12—22 mm. Values above
this suggest proptosis.
