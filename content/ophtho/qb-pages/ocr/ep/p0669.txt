3. A 92-year-old female patient came to the clinic complaining of gradual
progressive diminution of the vision in her left eye. On exammation, the visual
acuity was hand motion, and the red reflex was absent. There was no fundus
view in the left eye. The pupillary reflexes were normal in both eyes. What is
the most probable diagnosis?
a. Nuclear sclerosis
b. Posterior subcapsular cataract
c. Mature cataract
d. Immature cataract
In a mature cataract, the lens becomes completely opaque,
causing absent red reflex and no fundus view. Pupillary reflexes
remain normal since the optic pathway is intact.
