25. 45 years old woman presented to her family doctor complaining of diplopia.
On examination there was retraction of upper eyelid of right eye, proptosis and
limited ocular movement especially in up-gaze, with mild pain. Pupil of the
right eye was slightly dilated and showed sluggish reaction to light compared to
her left eye.
What is your diagnosis?
A. Myasthenia gravis
B. Thyroid eye disease
Proptosis, lid retraction, and restricted upgaze with mild pain
are typical of thyroid eye disease due to autoimmune
inflammation of extraocular muscles. The sluggish pupil helps
rule out isolated nerve palsy.
C. 3rd nerve palsy due to microvascular ischemic lesion
D. 3rd nerve palsy due to aneurysm of the posterior communicating artery
