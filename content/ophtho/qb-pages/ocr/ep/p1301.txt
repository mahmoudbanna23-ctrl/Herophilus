I O. Which of the following is a major risk factor for progression of thyroid eye
disease?
a. Smoking
b. Hypercholesterolemia
c. Obesity
d. Alcohol consumption
Smoking worsens thyroid eye disease by increasing orbital
inflammation and fibroblast activation. It is the most important
modifiable risk factor for progression.
