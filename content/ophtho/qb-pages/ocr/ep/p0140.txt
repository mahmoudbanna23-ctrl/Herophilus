1. Myopia (Near-sightedness)
Complications:
•Retinal detachment
•Chorioretinal degeneration
•Fuchs's spot (macular hemorrhage)
•Posterior staphyloma
•Glaucoma, cataract, squint
Treatment:
•Concave (—) lenses
•Contact lenses
•LASIK/PRK (after stabilization)
•Phakic IOL (<40 yr), Clear lens extraction (>40 yr)
