6. A 60-year-old patient complains of sudden painless vision loss. He has a
history of transient ischemic attacks. Ophthalmoscopy shows a cherry-red spot.
What is the diagnosis?
a. Central retinal artery occlusion (CRAO)
b. Central retinal vein occlusion (CRVO)
c. Retinal detachment
d. Diabetic retinopathy
CRAO presents with sudden painless vision loss and a cherry-
red spot on fundoscopy due to retinal ischemia. It is an
Ophthalmic emergency related to embolic or vascular causes.
