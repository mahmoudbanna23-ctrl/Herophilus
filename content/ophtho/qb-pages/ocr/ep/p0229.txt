Orbital / Preseptal Cellulitis
•Preseptal cellulitis: Usually secondary to dermal infection
• Common organisms:
• Children Haemophilus influenzae
• Adults Staphylococcus aureus / streptococci
•Orbital cellulitis: Usually secondary to sinus, facial, or dental infections
•Severe preseptal cellulitis or children <1 year treat as orbital cellulitis
Orbital cellulitis statistics:
•Visual loss: 7—11%
•Mortality (without antibiotics): 17—20%
Poor prognostic sign: Relative Afferent Pupillary Defect (RAPD)
