2. Which of the following is the most common malignant tumor of the eyelid?
a) Basal cell carcinoma
b) Squamous cell carcinoma
c) Sebaceous carcinoma
d) Malignant melanoma
Basal cell carcinoma is the most common malignant eyelid
tumor, usually affecting the lower lid. It grows slowly and rarely
metastasizes but can cause local tissue destruction.
