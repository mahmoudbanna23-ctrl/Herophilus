Dry Eye Disease (DED)
Definition: Instability or deficiency of tear film ocular surface irritation
Prevalence: Common yrs (8—40%)
Aetiology
1. Aqueous tear deficiency
•Sjögren's syndrome (autoimmune; dry mucous membranes)
•Non-Sjögren's causes: aging, hormones, thyroid eye disease, meds (antihistamines, 6-
blockers, etc.), LASIK, sarcoidosis
2. Evaporative dry eye
•U blinking (screens), Meibomian gland dysfunction, eyelid inflammation
•Thyroid eye disease, exposure keratitis, vitamin A deficiency
3. Mixed causes — common
