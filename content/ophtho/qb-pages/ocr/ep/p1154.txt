Quick Notes
•Most common intraocular malignancies: Secondaries (metastases)
•Primary intraocular malignancies: uncommon
• Children: Retinoblastoma
• Adults: Choroidal melanoma
Uveal Malignancies
•Most common primary uveal malignancy Choroidal melanoma
•Risk factors: More common in fair-skinned individuals
•Location: Usually within 3 mm of optic disc
•Metastasis: Most commonly to the liver
