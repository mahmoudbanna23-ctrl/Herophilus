Importance of Eye Examination
•A standardized eye exam allows proper evaluation of ocular complaints for treatment or referral.
•Many serious eye diseases are asymptomatic ("silent assassins").
•Early detection prevents irreversible blindness from:
Glaucoma
Diabetic retinopathy
Macular degeneration
Retina/ detachment
• Amblyopia (in children)
•The eye can reveal systemic diseases, such as:
• Diabetes Mellitus
Hypertension
Temporal arteritis
• Embolic events
• Stroke
Multiple sclerosis
• Brain tumors (meningioma)
• Intraocular malignancies (melanoma, retinoblastoma)
