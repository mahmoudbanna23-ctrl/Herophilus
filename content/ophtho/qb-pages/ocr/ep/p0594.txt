7. A 45-year-old patient presents with severe facial pain and vesicular rash on
the face. Involvement of which area indicates that the eye might also be
affected?
a. Tip of the nose
b. Cheek
c. Forehead
d. Earlobe
Involvement Of the tip of the nose indicates Hutchinson's sign,
showing nasociliary nerve involvement in herpes zoster
ophthalmicus. This increases the risk Of ocular complications.
