7. What does a visual acuity of 0.5 mean?
a. 6/12
b. 6/6
c. 6/18
d. 6/24
A visual acuity of 0.5 means the patient can see at 6 meters
what a normal person sees at 12 meters. It indicates mild
visual impairment.
