Paediatric Ophthalmology
Leukocoria
3.
Definition: White pupillary reflex replacing normal red reflex.
Differential Diagnosis (common to serious):
1.Cataract (most common)
2.Retinoblastoma (most serious)
3. Retinopathy of prematurity (ROP)
4. Persistent hyperplastic primary vitreous (PHPV)
5. Toxocariasis
6. Retinal detachment
7. Coats disease (exudative telangiectasia)
