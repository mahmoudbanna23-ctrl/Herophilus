a. 3 layers
b. 4 layers
c. 5 layers

Structure of the Cornea
The cornea consists of five layers: epithelium, Bowman's
membrane, stroma, Descemet's membrane, and endothelium.
Each plays a role in maintaining transparency and refractive
power.
