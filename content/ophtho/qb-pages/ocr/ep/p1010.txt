OPTIC A TROPHY
Definition:
Degeneration of the optic nerve from the retina to the lateral geniculate body.
Fundus appearance: Pale optic disc due to loss of vascularity.
Treatment: None (irreversible).
Types of Optic Atrophy
Type
Primary
Mechanism / Cause
Damage Outside eyeball (no prior swelling)
Causes: Congenital, MS (demyelination),
Syphilis, Compression by intracranial/orbital
tumour
Follows long-standing papilledema or papillitis
Secondary to retinal diseases (e.g., Retinitis
pigmentosa, degenerative myopia)
End-stage chronic glaucoma
Key Fundus & Notes
Pale, sharply demarcated disc
Disc edges blurred, preceded by
swelling
Retinal pathology visible
Marked cupping of disc
