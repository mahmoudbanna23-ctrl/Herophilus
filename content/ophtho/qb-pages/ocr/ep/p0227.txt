Quick Notes
Most Common Etiologies
Patient Group
Adults, unilateral proptosis
Adults, bilateral proptosis
Children, proptosis
Most Likely Cause
Thyroid Eye Disease (Grave's)
Thyroid Eye Disease (Grave's)
Orbital cellulitis (usually unilateral)
Causes of Globe Displacement
•Change in orbital soft tissue volume
•Alteration of orbital wall integrity
Measurement of Proptosis
•Distance from tip of cornea to lateral orbital margin
• Tools: Ruler or Hertel exophthalmometer
•Normal: 12—22 mm
Terminology:
•Exophthalmos: Endocrine etiology (e.g., Thyroid Eye Disease)
•Proptosis: Other etiologies
•Pseudo-exophthalmus: Lid retraction, contralateral ptosis, high myopia, or large globe
