Retinitis Pigmentosa (RP)
Definition:
Hereditary degeneration of photoreceptors, mainly rods night blindness & tunnel vision.
Inheritance Patterns
•Autosomal dominant best prognosis
•Autosomal recessive most common (z60%)
•X-Iinked worst prognosis
Symptoms
•Night blindness (early)
•Peripheral field loss "tunnel vision"
•Later: central vision, glare (posterior subcapsular cataract)
