1. Visual Acuity
•Definition: Sharpness of vision compared to a standard eye.
•Distance VA: Measured at 6 m using Snellen or E-chart.
• Example: 6/12-5 patient sees at 6 m what a normal person sees at 12 m.
• Decimal or feet notation may also be used.
•Pinhole Test: If VA S 6/12, improvement with pinhole refractive error.
• Worsening macular or retinal lesion.
•If unable to read chart: test for counting fingers (CF), hand motion (HM), or light perception
(PL/NPL).
•Legal blindness: VA S 3/60 in better eye (WHO).
•Near Vision: Test at 30—40 cm using near card or reading material.
•Children: Use picture, E, or Landolt C charts; infants assessed via fixation, following objects, and
light reflexes.
•uncooperative patients: Observe facial/light responses or perform electrophysiologic tests (ERG,
•Common global causes of vision impairment: refractive errors, cataract, AMD, glaucoma, •abetic
retinopathy, corneal opacity, trachoma.
