3. The physiological blind spot is:
a. Fovea centralis
b. Macula lutea
c. Optic disc
d. Peripheral retina
The physiological blind spot corresponds to the optic disc,
where no photoreceptors are present. The fovea and macula
are areas of maximum visual acuity, not blind spots.
