Dacryoadenitis (Inflammation of lacrimal gland)
•May indicate serious systemic disease
Causes:
•Viral (MCC): EBV
•Systemic diseases: Thyroid eye disease, Sjogren's, Sarcoidosis do Chest X-ray
•Idiopathic give oral corticosteroids
Clinical Presentation:
Acute:
•S-shaped ptosis of upper lid
•Painful swelling
•Diplopia on upward & lateral gaze
Chronic:
•Painless enlargement (more common than acute)
• Viral / bacterial / idiopathic
