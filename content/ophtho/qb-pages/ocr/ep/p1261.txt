(4) Thyroid Eye Disease (TED)
Investigations
•Thyroid hormone levels, TSH, anti-thyroid antibodies
•Orbital CT/MRI enlarged extraocular muscles (spares tendons)
Treatment
•Control thyroid disease (not always effective for eye)
•Lubricants / antioxidants for mild cases
•Systemic corticosteroids (active phase)
•Orbital decompression (for optic neuropathy/proptosis)
•Surgery for strabismus/eyelid retraction (after quiescent phase)
