Quick Notes
2. Chemical Injuries
•Complications:
• Sterile corneal ulcer
• Severe injuries globe atrophy
•Severity grading:
• Limbal ischemia (most important)
• Corneal clarity
• Hospitalization: Grade 3 & 4
•Adjunctive treatment:
• Vitamin C -+ ascorbic acid
• Collagenase inhibitors:
• Topical tetracycline
• Systemic doxycycline
. Grafting:
• Amniotic membrane, conjunctiva, or other mucous membrane
•Eye protection: Patch eye after chemical injury
