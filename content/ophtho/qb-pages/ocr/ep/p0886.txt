Q Clinical Features
CRVO
Symptoms:
•Painless, unilateral vision loss (sudden or subacute).
Signs (Fundus):
• "Blood and thunder" appearance
Dilated tortuous veins, flame & blot hemorrhages, cotton-wool spots, swollen disc,
macular edema.
•RAPD may be present.
