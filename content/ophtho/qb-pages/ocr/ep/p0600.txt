I O. A 25-year-old woman presents with eye pain, tearing, and photophobia. She
has a history of contact lens use. Fluorescein staining shows a corneal ulcer.
What is she at risk for?
a. Corneal perforation
b. Cataract
c. Glaucoma
d. Retinal detachment
A corneal ulcer, especially in contact lens users, can progress to
perforation if untreated. This may lead to endophthalmitis and
vision loss.
