II. Topical Ocular Drugs
1. Fluorescein Dye
Forms: Eye drops, impregnated strips, IV injection.
Uses (Topical):
•Detect corneal defects (abrasions, ulcers green).
•Seidel test detects corneal wound leakage.
•Contact lens fitting.
•Applanation tonometry.
•Tear film stability (TBUT <10s dry eye).
•Lacrimal drainage patency (Jones I & II tests).
Uses (IV):
•Retinal angiography (visualize vasculature).
Adverse Effects (IV):
• Yellow skin/urine (resolves <36h)
•Nausea, vomiting, headache
•Rare: Hypersensitivity or anaphylaxis
