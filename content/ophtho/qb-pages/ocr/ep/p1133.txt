8. When to screen neonates born at 26 weeks gestation for ROP?
a. 6 weeks after birth
b. At birth
c. 2 weeks after birth
d. 1 month after birth
Screening for retinopathy of prematurity (ROPI is done at 6
weeks postnatal age Or 31—33 weeks post-conceptional age,
whichever is later. This allows early detection and treatment
before retinal detachment develops.
