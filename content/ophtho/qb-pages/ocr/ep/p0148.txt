• Power in diopters 1 / focal length (meters)
2. Determinants of refraction:
'Axial length of the eyeball
• Power of the cornea and crystalline lens (depends on curvature and refractive index)
Normal Refraction
3.Emmetropia: Parallel rays from infinity focus on the retina when accommodation is at
rest. No refractive error.
