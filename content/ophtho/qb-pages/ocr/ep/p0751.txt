6. A 35-year-old patient presents with decreased vision, ocular pain, and a
constricted pupil that reacts sluggishly to light. Slit-lamp examination confirms
anterior uveitis. What is the first-line treatment for this condition?
a. Steroids
b. Antibiotics
c. Observation
d. Cycloplegics only
Topical corticosteroids are the mainstay treatment for anterior
uveitis to reduce inflammation. Cycloplegics may also be
added to relieve pain and prevent synechiae.
