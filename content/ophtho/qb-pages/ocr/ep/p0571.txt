Corneal Degenerations
Exposure Keratopathy (EK)
Definition:
Corneal damage from prolonged exposure due to incomplete eyelid closure
Causes:
•Lagophthalmos, proptosis, lid malposition
•V Blink reflex (Parkinsonism, neuromuscular, neurotrophic conditions)
Symptoms:
•FB sensation, burning, tearing, photophobia, intermittent blurring
•Worse in morning (nocturnal lagophthalmos)
Signs:
•Punctate epithelial erosions (inferior cornea)
•ulceration/infection in severe cases
Treatment:
•Lubrication (prophylactic in high-risk)
• Treat underlying cause
•Topical antibiotics if infection suspected
•Ophthalmology referral
