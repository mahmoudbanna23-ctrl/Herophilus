(5) Giant Cell Arteritis (Temporal Arteritis)
Epidemiology
•Elderly women (>60 years)
Symptoms
•Sudden unilateral visual loss
•Temporal headache
•Jaw claudication
•Scalp tenderness, malaise, fever
Signs
•Tender, cord-like temporal artery
•Anterior ischemic optic neuropathy (AION)
•50% risk of vision loss in fellow eye if untreated
Investigations
ESR & CRP
• Temporal artery biopsy (within 2 weeks)
Treatment
•Start high-dose systemic corticosteroids immediately (don't delay for biopsy)
