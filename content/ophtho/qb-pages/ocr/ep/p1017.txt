2. A pituitary adenoma produces:
a. Binasal hemianopia
b. Bitemporal hemianopia
c. A central scotoma
d. An arcuate scotoma
A pituitary adenoma compresses the optic chiasm, where
nasal fibers from both eyes cross, leading to bitemporal
hemianopia (loss of temporal visual fields in both eyes).
