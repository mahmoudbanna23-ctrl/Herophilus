9. A 45-year-old man presents with unilateral ptosis, anhidrosis, enophthalmos,
and anisocoria that is more apparent in dim lighting.
What is the most likely diagnosis?
a. Oculomotor nerve palsy
b. Horner syndrome
c. Myasthenia gravis
d. Adie's pupil
Horner syndrome is characterized by ptosis, miosis, anhidrosis,
and enophthalmos. The anisocoria is more evident in dim light
because the affected pupil fails to dilate.
