Conjunctival Changes & Signs:
•Conjunctival injection: spares limbal area
•True membrane: Corynebacterium diphtheriae (membranous conjunctivitis)
•Pseudomembrane: epithelium intact viral
Follicles (lymphocytes):
• Viral: lower tarsal conjunctiva
•Trachoma: upper tarsal conjunctiva
•Adult chlamydia: both upper & lower conjunctiva
Papillae (bacterial):
•Chronic
•Giant papillae (>1 mm, flat-topped):
• Cobblestone papillae
• Seen in: Vernal catarrh, prolonged contact lens use, artificial eye
