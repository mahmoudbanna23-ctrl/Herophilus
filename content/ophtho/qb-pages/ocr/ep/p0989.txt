6th Nerve (Abducent) Palsy
•Muscle affected: Lateral rectus loss of abduction.
•Features:
• Esotropia
• Horizontal diplopia (T in ipsilateral gaze)
• Face turn to same side
•Causes:
• ICP (bilateral possible)
• Diabetes, HTN
• Tumor, trauma, meningitis, otitis
•Investigations: MRI brain + systemic workup (BS, BP, lipids).
