I. Presbyopia is a deficiency in:
a. Far vision
b. Near vision
c. Day vision
d. Night vision
Presbyopia is an age-related loss of lens elasticity, reducing the
eye's ability to accommodate for near objects. Distant vision
remains unaffected.
