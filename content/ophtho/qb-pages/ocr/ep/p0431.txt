Subconjunctival Hemorrhage
•Definition: Blood beneath the conjunctiva; bright-red patch, otherwise asymptomatic.
•Symptoms: No pain, no discharge, no vision loss.
•Causes:
• Idiopathic
. Trauma
• Valsalva maneuver (coughing, sneezing, straining)
• Hypertension
• Bleeding disorders
• Anticoagulant therapy
•Management:
• Reassurance : resolves spontaneously in 2—3 weeks
• Measure BP & systemic work-up if recurrent
• Artificial tears for mild irritation
• Check INR if on anticoagulants
• If large / post-traumatic, suspect globe rupture urgent ophthalmologyfeferral
