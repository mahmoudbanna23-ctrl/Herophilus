Eyelids
Anatomy of Eyelids
•Protect the eye + help lubricate ocular surface.
•Palpebral fissure: space between open eyelids.
•Lashes:
upper outward & upward
Lower outward & downward
Layers (anterior posterior):
O] Skin — thinnest in body
Subcutaneous tissue — loose, allows swelling
Muscular layer: Orbicularis oculi (blinking, VII nerve)
[S] Tarsal plate: contains Meibomian glands oily secretion
orifices form white line
CS] Palpebral conjunctiva
Lines of lid margin:
•White line: Meibomian gland openings
•Grey line: avascular line surgical division into:
- Anterior lamella: skin + orbicularis
- Posterior lamella: tarsus + conjunctiva
