I I. Which of the following is the organism that most commonly contaminates
fluorescein dye in case of prolonged storage?
A. Staphylococcus aureus
B. Streptococcus pneumoniae
C. Pseudomonas aeruginosa
D. E-Coli
Sodium fluorescein solution has a high susceptibility to
bacterial contamination, especially by Pseudomonas
aeruginosa (as it grows easily in fluorescein).
