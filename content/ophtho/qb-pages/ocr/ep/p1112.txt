Amblyopia
•Definition: Deprivation/occlusion amblyopia caused by media opacity or lid ptosis
•Risk factors:
• More common in high hypermetropia than myopia
• Squint may be a cause or sign of amblyopia
•Diagnosis:
• On exam, pathology may not always be found in amblyopic eye
• Eyes with good acuity are central and fixed
• Treatment:
• Window: Up to 8—10 years (before visual maturation)
• Occlusion therapy: Patch the good eye
