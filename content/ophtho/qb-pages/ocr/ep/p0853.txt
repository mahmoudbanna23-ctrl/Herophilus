Vitreous Disorders & Intraocular Infections
Vitreous Hemorrhage (VH)
Definition:
Bleeding into the vitreous cavity.
Causes:
•Proliferative diabetic retinopathy (most common).
•Retinal tear or detachment.
•Retinal vein occlusion.
•PVD, trauma.
•In children rule out shaken baby syndrome.
Symptoms:
•Sudden vision, "black shadows" or "floaters."
Signs:
•Black shadows on red reflex or absent red reflex if extensive.
•Retina may not be visible.
