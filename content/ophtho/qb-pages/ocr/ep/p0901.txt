I. A retinal break without detachment can be treated by:
a. Vitrectomy
b. Buckle
c. Argon laser
d. All of the above
Argon laser photocoagulation seals small retinal breaks to
prevent detachment. Vitrectomy or scleral buckling is reserved
for cases with established retinal detachment.
