2. The fundus picture of central retinal artery occlusion does not include:
a. Marked narrowing of retinal arterioles
b. Numerous flame shaped hemorrhages
c. Opaque white retina
d. Cherry red spot
Central retinal artery occlusion (CRAO) causes sudden vision loss with a
pale opaque retina and a cherry red spot, along with narrow arterioles.
Flame-shaped hemorrhages are characteristic Of venous occlusion, not
arterial occlusion.
