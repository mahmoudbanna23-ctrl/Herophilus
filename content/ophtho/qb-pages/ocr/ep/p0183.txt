12. A middle-aged man complains of blurred near vision. His right eye was
found to be +3 while his left eye was +2. What is the position of the far point in
that patient?
A. Closer to the eye
B. Faraway from the eye
C. Behind the eye
D. None of the above
The far point Of hypermetropic eye is a virtual point that lies
behind the eye.
