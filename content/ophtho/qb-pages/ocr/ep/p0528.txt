Scleritis
Definition: Inflammation of the scleral stroma (deep, painful, can threaten vision).
Types
1.Anterior (common) : diffuse, nodular, or necrotizing.
'Pain, bluish hue, tender globe.
• Necrotizing = severe pain ± choroid exposure.
Scleromalacia perforans painless necrotizing form (RA).
2.Posterior (rare) : pain on eye movement, vision, serous retinal detachment.
Causes
•Rheumatoid arthritis (most common), SLE, gout, infections (TB, syphilis, herpes zoster),
trauma, idiopathic.
Diagnosis
•Phenylephrine 10%: No blanching (unlike episcleritis).
•Ultrasound or MRI: Detect posterior scleritis.
