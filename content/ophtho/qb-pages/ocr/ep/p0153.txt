Quick Notes
Total refractive power of eye = 60 D
Power of a lens 1 / focal length (in meters)
Myopia (Short-sightedness)
Distant vision decreased, near vision normal.
Image focused in front of the retina.
Corrected by concave (—) lenses.
Most common cause: Axial myopia (increased axial length).
Appears during 1st—2nd decade; stops early 3rd decade.
If it develops after age 25 consider diabetes mellitus or nuclear cataract.
Patients often squint or partially close eyes to see clearly.
Simple myopia < 6 D, progressive myopia > 6 D (can reach 20 D).
Complications of high myopia (retinal detachment, maculopathy) cannot be prevented
by refraction correction.
Association: Open-angle glaucoma (co-existence, not predisposition).
