ISCHEMIC OPTIC NEUROPATHY (ION)
A. Non-Arteritic ION (NAION)
•Mechanism: Transient hypoperfusion of posterior ciliary arteries
•Epidemiology: > 40 yrs with vascular risk factors (HTN, DM, dyslipidemia, smoking, OSA)
•Often noted on waking (nocturnal hypotension)
•Features:
• Unilateral vision loss
• Altitudinal visual field defect
• Visual acuity
• RAPD
• Mild disc oedema ± splinter haemorrhages
• No systemic symptoms
•Treatment: No proven therapy — control risk factors, avoid BP over-lowering
•Prognosis: Usually poor visual recovery
•Tests: ESR/CRP in > 50 yrs to rule out arteritic form
