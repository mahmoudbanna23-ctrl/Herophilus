Surgical Techniques
Extracapsular Cataract Extraction (ECCE)
•Large limbal incision (8—10 mm) —Y sutured with 10/0 Nylon
•Lens nucleus removed, cortex aspirated
•Posterior capsule remains intact
•Rigid PMMA IOL implanted (usually in ciliary sulcus)
More astigmatism due to suturing
Intracapsular Cataract Extraction (ICCE)
•Removal of entire lens with capsule (after zonulysis)
•No IOL implantation results in aphakia
•Obsolete (used rarely in special cases)
