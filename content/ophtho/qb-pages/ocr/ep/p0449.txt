3. Which ofthe following is the most frequent causative agent of viral
conjunctivitis?
A. Cytomegalovirus
b. Herpes zoster virus
c. Herpes simplex virus
d. Adenovirus
Adenovirus is the most common cause of viral conjunctivitis,
often following an upper respiratory infection. It causes watery
discharge, follicles, and preauricular lymphadenopathy.
