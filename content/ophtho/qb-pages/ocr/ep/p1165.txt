4. A 6-year-old child developed an intraocular malignant mass.
Ultrasonography revealed a calcified mass. What is the most likely diagnosis?
A. Choroidal melanoma
B. Squamous cell carcinoma
C. Basal cell carcinoma
D. Retinoblastoma
The most common primary intraocular malignancy in children
is retinoblastoma.
