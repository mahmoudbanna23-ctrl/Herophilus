RETINAL VASCULAR OCCLUSIVE DISEASES
Central Retinal Artery Occlusion (CRAO)
Cause:
Emboli (carotid, cardiac), thrombosis, Giant Cell Arteritis (GCA), atherosclerosis.
Symptoms:
•Sudden, painless, severe unilateral vision loss PL or worse).
•May be preceded by amaurosis fugax (transient blindness).
Signs:
•RAPD (Marcus Gunn pupil)
•Retinal pallor (opaque white retina)
•Cherry-red spot at fovea
•Arteriolar attenuation ("cattle trucking" / boxcarring)
•Possible visible embolus (Hollenhorst plaque)
Mechanism:
—Y Ischemia ganglion cell necrosis -+ optic atrophy.
—Y Choroidal circulation preserved at fovea cherry-red spot.
If cilioretinal artery present: some central vision may be preserved.
