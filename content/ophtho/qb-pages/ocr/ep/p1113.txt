Congenital Cataracts
•Visually significant (requires Rx): e.g., Lamellar cataract
•Not visually significant (no Rx): e.g., Blue dot cataract, Sutural cataract
•Complications if neglected:
• Unilateral: Sensory squint
• Bilateral: Searching nystagmus
•IOL implantation:
• Not always done in primary surgery
• May be secondary implantation later
•Association: 1/3 of cases have systemic disease refer to pediatrician
