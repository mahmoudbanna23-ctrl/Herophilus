Horner Syndrome
•Pathophysiology: Interruption of sympathetic pathway.
•Features:
• Miosis (anisocoria 'T' in dark)
. ptosis
• Anhidrosis
• Enophthalmos
• Heterochromia (if congenital)
•Causes:
• Apical lung tumor (Pancoast)
• Carotid dissection / aneurysm
• Brainstem infarction
• Trauma / neck surgery
• Cervical rib
•Investigations: MRI (brain to mid-thorax), carotid angiography (if painful).
