• RETINA
Anatomy & Structure
•Retina: Innermost coat of the eye, from ora serrata optic disc.
•Parts:
• Neurosensory retina (9 layers): Contains photoreceptors ganglion cells.
• Retinal pigment epithelium (RPE): Outer single layer over Bruch's membrane.
Blood Supply:
•Outer 5 layers from choroid.
•Inner layers central retinal artery (CRA) (end artery).
•20% have cilioretinal artery (extra macular supply).
