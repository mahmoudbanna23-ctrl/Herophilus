Differential Diagnosis (for ocular form)
•Cranial nerve palsies (Ill, IV, VI)
•Chronic progressive external ophthalmoplegia (CPEO)
•Thyroid eye disease
•Brainstem lesions
Treatment
No definitive cure, but symptomatic and immunosuppressive therapy improves function.
Drug / Approach
Anticholinesterase (Pyridostigmine)
Corticosteroids
Immunosuppressants (Azathioprine,
Mycophe nolate)
Plasmapheresis / IVIG
Thymectomy
Mechanism / Use
Improves neuromuscular transmission
Reduces autoimmune activity
For long-term control
For severe or myasthenic crisis
