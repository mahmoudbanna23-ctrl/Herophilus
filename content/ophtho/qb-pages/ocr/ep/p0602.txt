conical protrusion and thinning of the cornea. Which of the following is a
useful tool for decision making and management of that patient?
A. Amsler's grid
B. CT orbit
C. Pentacam
D. None of the above
Investigations Of keratoconus Corneal topography (Map Of corneal
curvature is diagnostic), Pachymetry (measurement Of the corneal

corneal thickness data, other data that help decision making and
management Of KC).
