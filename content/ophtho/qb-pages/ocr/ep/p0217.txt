Enophthalmos
Definition:
Backward displacement of the globe.
Causes:
1. Orbital fat atrophy (trauma, age).
2. Cicatrizing tumors/metastasis.
3.Blow-out fracture (most common traumatic cause).
Blow-out fracture:
•Usually affects orbital floor infraorbital canal involvement.
•Symptoms:
• Diplopia (esp. on up/down gaze)
• Infraorbital nerve anesthesia (cheek, upper lip, teeth)
• Enophthalmos (delayed)
•CT scan diagnostic "tear-drop sign."
