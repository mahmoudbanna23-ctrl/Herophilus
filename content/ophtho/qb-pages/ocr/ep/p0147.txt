Presbyopia
Definition:
Age-related loss of near vision due to decreased accommodative power (lens hardening
and reduced elasticity).
Normal accommodation:
•14D at age 10 to 3D by 40 lost by 60.
Clinical picture:
•Begins at N40 years.
•Emmetrope: holds reading material farther away.
•Myope: sees better near without glasses.
•Hyperope: develops symptoms earlier (uses accommodation for far and near).
Treatment:
•Reading glasses, bifocal, or progressive lenses (plus lenses for near).
