2. The classical treatment of congenital glaucoma:
a. Long term miotics
b. Long term beta blockers
c. Laser treatment
d. Surgery
The definitive treatment for congenital glaucoma is surgical
(e.g., goniotomy or trabeculotomy). Medical therapy offers only
temporary pressure reduction before surgery.
