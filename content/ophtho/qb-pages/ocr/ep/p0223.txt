Proptosis / Exophthalmos
1. Exophthalmos (Proptosis)
Forward displacement of the globe.
• Measured from the tip of the cornea to the lateral orbital margin using a ruler or
Hertel exophthalmometer.
• Normal values: 12—22 mm.
2. Pseudo-exophthalmos
'Apparent forward protrusion without actual displacement.
'Causes: Lid retraction, contralateral ptosis, large globe in high myopia.
3.Most common cause
'Adults: Graves' disease
Children: Orbital cellulitis
4. Enophthalmos
Posterior displacement of the globe.
