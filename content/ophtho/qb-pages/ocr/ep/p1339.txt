II. Topical Ocular Drugs
6. Anti-inflammatory Agents
•Corticosteroids: Potent but risky risk of infection, cataract, glaucoma.
•NSAIDs (safer): Diclofenac, Ketorolac, Nepafenac, Bromfenac, Flurbiprofen.
• Used for ocular itching, macular edema, miosis prevention during surgery.
7. Dry Eye Treatment
•Lubricating drops/ointments (with or without preservatives).
•Cyclosporine A (Restasis): Immunomodulator for chronic moderate-severe dry eye.
8. Antibiotics
• Used for bacterial conjunctivitis/keratitis.
•Common agents: Erythromycin, Sulfacetamide, Aminoglycosides, Fluoroquinolones.
•Avoid neomycin (allergy).
•Avoid steroid combinations unless ophthalmologist prescribes.
