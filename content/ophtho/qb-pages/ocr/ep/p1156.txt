Quick Notes
Sebaceous Carcinoma
•Occurs mostly in elderly (7th—8th decades)
•Common site: Upper lid
•Arises from sebaceous glands & glands of Zeis at lash roots
•Red flags:
• Older patient with recurrent chalazion
• Chronic unilateral blepharitis or conjunctivitis
•Management: Biopsy to confirm diagnosis
