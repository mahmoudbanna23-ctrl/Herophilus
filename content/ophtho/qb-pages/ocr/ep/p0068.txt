2. Testing the visual field in a patient with hand motion vision is done by:
a. Confrontation method
b. Automated perimetry
c. Light perception
d. Light projection
In patients with hand motion vision, visual fields are tested by
light projection—determining the direction from which light is
perceived. Automated perimetry requires better visual acuity.
