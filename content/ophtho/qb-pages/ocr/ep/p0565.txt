Specific Infectious Keratitis
Bacterial
•After trauma / contact lens / surface defect.
•Hypopyon may be present.
•Tx: Fortified antibiotics (Fortum + Vancomycin), fluoroquinolone, cycloplegic.
x
No steroids.
Fungal
•Trauma with plant material.
•Slow, dull gray ulcer with feathery edges + satellite lesions.
•Tx: Topical ± systemic antifungal (e.g., natamycin, amphotericin).
Acanthamoeba
•Contact lens wearer, severe pain disproportionate to signs.
•Ring infiltrate, perineural infiltration.
•Tx: Anti-acanthamoeba drugs + cycloplegics.
