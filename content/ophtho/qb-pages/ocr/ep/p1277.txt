Thyroid Eye Disease (TED)
•Autoimmune attack on: orbit, thyroid, skin
•Mechanisms:
• Sympathetic overstimulation
• Inflammatory infiltration of orbital fat & muscles
•Can occur in euthyroid patients
•Risk factor for progression: Smoking
•Clinical features:
• Thyroid stare: Proptosis + lid retraction
• MCC of unilateral & bilateral proptosis in adults = TED
• Restrictive strabismus
• Compressive optic neuropathy
•Imaging: CT orbit fusiform EOM enlargement
•Surgery:
• Stepwise: Orbital decompression strabismus correction eyelid surgery
