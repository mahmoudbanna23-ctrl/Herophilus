1. The Iris
•Anterior-most part, gives eye color.
•Contains the pupil regulates light entry.
•Layers:
• Stroma: contains sphincter & dilator pupillae muscles —Y control pupil size
• Posterior pigment epithelium
Iris root: attached to sclera continuous with ciliary body
•In front of iris root = trabecular meshwork (main aqueous drainage route).
•Also allows uveoscleral outflow (secondary drainage pathway).
Regions:
•Pupillary zone -+ around pupil
•Ciliary zone peripheral part
•Collarette —Y thickest region, separates both zones
•Crypts depressions between muscle bundles
Iris color: depends on pigment.
•Albinism: pinkish-white iris
•Heterochromia: different iris colors (can be normal or due to disease)
