DRUGS AND THE EYE
l. General Instructions for Ocular Medications
Patient guidance:
•Always check expiration date.
•Use one drop only; more doesn't increase effect.
•Wait 25 minutes between different drops.
•Ointments/gels should be applied last.
•Wash hands before application.
•Avoid touching the applicator tip to eye/lids.
•Close eyes gently after instillation; wipe excess.
•Always ask about drug allergies before use.
