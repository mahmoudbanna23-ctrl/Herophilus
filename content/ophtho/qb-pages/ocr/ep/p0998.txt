OPTIC DISC SWELLING
Papilledema
Definition: Bilateral optic disc swelling secondary to intracranial pressure (ICP).
Fully Established Features:
1. Disc hyperaemia
2. Venous tortuosity/congestion
3. Blurred, elevated disc margins
4. Vessel obscuration near disc
5. Haemorrhages on/around disc
6. Enlarged blind spot
7. Pupils normal
8.± 6th nerve palsy
Aetiology:
•Brain tumours / space-occupying lesions
•Cerebral trauma or haemorrhage
•Meningitis / encephalitis
•Dural sinus thrombosis
•Idiopathic intracranial hypertension (pseudotumor cerebri)
