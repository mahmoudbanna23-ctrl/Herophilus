3rd Nerve (Oculomotor) Palsy
•Features: Ptosis, eye "down & out, " ± dilated pupil.
•Pupil involvement:
• Compressive lesions (aneurysm) dilated pupil
• Ischemic lesions (DM, HTN) pupil spared
•Causes: Posterior communicating artery aneurysm, trauma, herniation, tumors,
microvascular infarction.
•Investigations: MRI + MR angiography.
4th Nerve (Trochlear) Palsy
•Muscle affected: Superior oblique depression in adduction.
•Symptoms: Vertical diplopia (T in downgaze).
•Compensation: Head tilt & face turn to opposite side.
•Causes: Trauma, microvascular, congenital, tumor.
