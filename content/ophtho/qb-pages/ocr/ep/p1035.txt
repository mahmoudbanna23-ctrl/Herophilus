I l. A 50-year-old patient presents with severe headache, nausea, and projectile
vomiting. On ophthalmologic examination, bilateral blurred disc margins are
noted.
What is the most likely diagnosis?
a. Optic neuritis
b. Papilledema
c. Retinal detachment
d. Central retinal vein occlusion
Bilateral blurred disc margins with symptoms Of raised
intracranial pressure indicate papilledema. It results from
increased intracranial pressure causing optic disc swelling.
