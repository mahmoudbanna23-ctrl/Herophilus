15. Damage to which of the following structures causes corneal superficial
vascularization and conjunctivalization of corneal epithelium?
A. Limbus
B. Sclera
C. Cornea
D. Conjunctiva
The conjunctiva at the limbus hosts the palisades of Vogt which contain the
stem cells that provide regenerative cells to the corneal epithelium. Damage
to these structures results in corneal stem cell deficiency with corneal
superficial vascularization and conjunctivalization of the corneal epithelium.
