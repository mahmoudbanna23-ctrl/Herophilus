2. Which is the most dangerous and has bad prognosis?
a. Acid burn
b. Alkali burn
c. Blunt trauma
d. Central retinal vein occlusion
Alkali burns penetrate ocular tissues rapidly, causing severe
and deep damage to the cornea and anterior segment. Acid
burns tend to coagulate proteins and thus are less penetrating.
