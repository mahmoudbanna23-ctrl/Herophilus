22. 53 years old patient complaining of near vision problems. The patient is
asthmatic and has some degree of heart block that is controlled medically. His
visual acuity is 6/6 in both eyes. While examination you notice nuclear sclerosis
in both eyes. IOP is 28 mmHg in his right eye and 30 mmHg in his left eye, the
fundus examination revealed large cup/disc ratio in both eyes. Your diagnosis is
POAG. You are going to prescribe medical treatment to him.
What are group of drugs that should be avoided in that patient?
A. PG analogues
B. B blockers
C. Alpha adrenergic agonist
Topical beta-blockers like timolol can cause bronchospasm and
worsen heart block, so they should be avoided. Other agents
like prostaglandin analogues are preferred.
D. Carbonic anhydrase inhibitor
