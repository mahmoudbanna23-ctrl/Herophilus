7. A 55-year-old patient presents with ptosis, diplopia, and the eye deviated
"down and out." The pupil is dilated and non-reactive to light.
What is the most common cause of this presentation?
a. Posterior communicating artery aneurysm
b. Diabetic neuropathy
c. Cavernous sinus thrombosis
d. Multiple sclerosis
Aneurysm compressing the oculomotor nerve causes ptosis,
"down and out" eye, and pupil dilation. This is an emergency
due to risk of rupture and subarachnoid hemorrhage.
