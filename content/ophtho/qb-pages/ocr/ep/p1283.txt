I. Ageing can be associated with all of the following EXCEPT:
a. Epiphora
b. Macular degeneration
c. Optic disc cupping
d. Decrease in accommodative power of the lens
Aging is associated with macular degeneration, increased optic disc cupping (due
to glaucoma), and a decrease in the lens's ability to focus (presbyopia). Epiphora,
or watery eyes, is not a direct consequence of aging, though other age-related
conditions like dry eye or eyelid issues can contribute to it.
