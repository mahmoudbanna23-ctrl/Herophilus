Vogt-Koyanagi-Harada (VKH) Syndrome
Definition:
An idiopathic multisystem autoimmune disease causing inflammation of melanocyte-
containing tissues such as:
•Uvea
•Skin
•Inner ear
•Meninges
Pathophysiology:
•Autoimmune reaction against melanocytes (melanin-associated antigens).
•Leads to bilateral granulomatous panuveitis and systemic manifestations
