3. Sign of primary open angle glaucoma:
a. Ciliary congestion
b. Oval dilated
c. Absent red reflex
d. Large cup/disc ratio
In primary open-angle glaucoma, the optic disc shows a large
cup-to-disc ratio due to progressive loss of nerve fibers. Other
findings include raised IOP and visual field defects.
