3. What is the most common primary intraocular malignancy in adults?
A. Retinoblastoma
B. Basal cell carcinoma
C. Squamous cell carcinoma
D. Choroidal melanoma
The most common primary intraocular malignancy in
children is retinoblastoma.
The most common primary intraocular malignancy in adults
