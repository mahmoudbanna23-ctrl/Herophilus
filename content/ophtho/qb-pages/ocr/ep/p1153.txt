Infancy & early childhood
Most common primary intraocular malignancy in children
Retina (neuroectodermal tumor'
Often bilateral in heritable type
- Leukocoria (White pupil) - Strabismus - May present With
inflammation or pain
White/yellowish mass With calcification
Calcified tumor (seen on US/CT)
- Local invasion, optic nerve, brain - Hematogenous spread

mutation

enucleation (depending on stage) - Screen siblings/offspring
