5. Signs of activity of trachoma include:
a. Herbert's pits of the upper limbus
b. Scarring of the palpebral conjunctiva
c. Follicles in the upper palpebral conjunctiva
d. Cicatricial entropion
Active trachoma shows follicles in the upper tarsal conjunctiva,
indicating ongoing Chlamydia trachomatis infection. Scarring
and Herbert's pits indicate inactive or healed disease.
