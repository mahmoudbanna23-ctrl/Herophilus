2. Visual Field Testing
•Confrontation method: Compare patient's visual field with examiner's.
•Light projection: For very low vision (<1/60).
•Other methods: Automated perimetry (quantitative), Amsler grid (central 100).
3. Inspection
•Examine eyelids, conjunctiva, cornea, sclera, and iris with penlight.
•Upper eyelid eversion: To detect/ remove foreign bodies.
4. Anterior Chamber Depth
•Shine light from temporal side; shadow on nasal iris shallow chamber.
•Shallow AC seen in: hypermetropia, angle-closure glaucoma, cataract, perforated ulcer.
•Deep AC myopia, aphakia, buphthalmos.
•Do not dilate if angle-closure suspected—refer urgently.
