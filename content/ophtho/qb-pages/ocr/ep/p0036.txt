e 5. Pupillary Examination
Assess:
A. Size and shape — normal = equal, round; anisocoria >1 mm is abnormal.
B. Light Reflex (direct & consensual)
C. Near Reflex (accommodation + convergence + miosis)
D. Swinging Flashlight Test (RAPD test)
•RAPD / Marcus Gunn pupil: affected eye dilates when light swung to it optic nerve lesion.
•Cataract or vitreous hemorrhage do not cause RAPD.
