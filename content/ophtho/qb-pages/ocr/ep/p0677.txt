7. An elderly man reports that he no longer needs reading glasses for near
vision, but his distance vision has deteriorated (-2D). What is this phenomenon
called?
a. Second sight
b. Presbyopia
c. Myopia progression
d. Hyperopia
In nuclear cataract, the increasing lens density increases
refractive power, temporarily improving near vision ("second
sight"). However, distance vision worsens due to myopic shift.
