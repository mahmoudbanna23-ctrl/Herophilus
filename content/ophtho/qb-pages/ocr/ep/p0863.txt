3. A 70-year-old patient presents with sudden onset floaters and flashes in one
eye. On examination, the red reflex is absent.
What is the most appropriate next step in management?
a. Dilated fundus examination
b. Immediate cataract surgery
c. Observation only
d. Optical coherence tomography (OCT) of the macula
Flashes, floaters, and absent red reflex suggest retinal
detachment or vitreous hemorrhage. A dilated fundus exam is
essential for diagnosis and urgent management.
