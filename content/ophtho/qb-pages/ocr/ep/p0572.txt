Corneal Degenerations
Photokeratitis (Ultraviolet Keratitis)
Cause:
UV exposure epithelial damage (e.g. welding arcs, snow reflection, UV lamps, tanning
beds)
Symptoms:
•Bilateral intense pain, photophobia, FB sensation (after 6—12 hrs)
Signs:
•Bilateral injection, chemosis
•U Vision
•Fluorescein: diffuse punctate epithelial staining
Treatment:
•Supportive: artificial tears, ointment, oral analgesics, cold compresses
•Avoid topical anesthetics (delay healing)
•Heals in 24—72 hrs
