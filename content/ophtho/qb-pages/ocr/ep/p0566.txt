Herpes Zoster Ophthalmicus (VZV)
•Reactivation in ophthalmic branch of CN V.
•Unilateral dermatomal vesicles + pain.
•Hutchinson's sign = nasal tip lesion eye involved.
•Complications: keratitis, scleritis, uveitis, glaucoma, post-herpetic neuralgia.
