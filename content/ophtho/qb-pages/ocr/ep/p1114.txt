Retinopathy of Prematurity (ROP)
•Cause: Incomplete retinal vascularization
•Mediator: VEGF
•Risk factors:
• Birth weight <1500g
• Gestational age <33 weeks
•Screening:
• GA 228 weeks: screen at 4 weeks of age
• GA '27 weeks: screen at 31 weeks GA
•Long-term risk: Higher risk of myopia, even if treated
