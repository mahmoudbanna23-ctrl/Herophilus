3. Which of the following is a feature of preseptal cellulitis?
a. Conjunctival chemosis
b. Normal ocular motility
c. Binocular diplopia
d. Proptosis
Preseptal cellulitis affects tissues anterior to the orbital septum, so eye
movements and vision remain normal. In contrast, orbital cellulitis involves the
orbit and causes proptosis and restricted motility.
