Preoperative Planning & Preparation for Cataract Surgery
Purpose
•No role for medical therapy Surgery is the only treatment.
•Preoperative evaluation ensures patient safety and optimal IOL calculation.
A. General Considerations
•Cataract surgery is elective, except in emergencies (e.g. phacomorphic or phacolytic
glaucoma).
•Systemic diseases (e.g., uncontrolled diabetes, CHF, hypertension) must be controlled first.
