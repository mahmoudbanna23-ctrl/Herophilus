Notes
Scleritis:
• Types:
• Anterior scleritis:
• Painful
• Nodules adherent to sclera
• IOP may be elevated (due to trabeculitis)
• Posterior scleritis:
• May cause serous retinal detachment
• Diagnosis: ocular B-scan US, MRI
•Special Forms:
• Scleromalacia perforans rare, necrotizing anterior scleritis, painless
•Associations:
• Most common: Rheumatoid arthritis
•Important clinical indicators:
. Pain most important for disease progression
