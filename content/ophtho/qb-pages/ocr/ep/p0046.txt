•Lids/Lacrimal/Orbit: Hordeolum, blepharitis, entropion/ectropion, dacryocystitis, cellulitis.
•Cornea: Foreign body, abrasion, ulcer, laceration.
•Conjunctiva/Sclera: Conjunctivitis, subconjunctival hemorrhage, dry eye, pterygium,
episcleritis/scleri tis.
•Anterior Chamber: Hyphema, hypopyon.
•Others: Trauma, post-surgery, drugs, endophthalmitis.
