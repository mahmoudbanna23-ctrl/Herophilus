II. Clinical Divisions of the Eyeball
The eye is divided into two main segments:
A. The Anterior Segment
Includes all structures anterior to the vitreous humour, in order:
1.Conjunctiva : Thin, vascular mucous membrane covering the sclera (bulbar conjunctiva)
and lining eyelids (palpebral conjunctiva).
• Protects and lubricates the ocular surface.
2.Cornea : Transparent anterior coat responsible for 2/3 of the eye's total refractive
power.
'Avascular and highly sensitive to pain.
3.Sclera : Tough, opaque fibrous coat providing protection, light shielding, and attachment
for extraocular muscles.
4.Anterior Chamber : Contains aqueous humour (see above).
5.1ris:Colored diaphragm between cornea and lens.
Regulates light entry through sphincter and dilator pupillae muscles.
'Separates anterior and posterior chambers.
