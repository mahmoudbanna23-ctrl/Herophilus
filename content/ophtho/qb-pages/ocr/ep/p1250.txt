2. Severe NPDR (Pre-proliferative Stage)
4-2-1 Rule
(any one):
1. Hemorrhages in 4 quadrants
2. Venous beading in 2 quadrants
3. IRMA (intraretinal microvascular anomalies) in 1 quadrant
•IRMA shunt vessels bypassing capillary bed non-perfusing
•40% progress to PDR within 1 year
•Two criteria very severe NPDR
