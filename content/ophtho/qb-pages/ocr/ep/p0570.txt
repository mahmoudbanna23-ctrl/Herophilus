Corneal Degenerations
Calcific Band Keratopathy
Definition:
Calcium deposition in subepithelium, Bowman's layer, and anterior stroma horizontal
band-like opacity in interpalpebral zone
Causes:
•Chronic ocular inflammation (uveitis)
•Silicone oil (post-retinal surgery)
•Systemic hypercalcemia
Clinical Picture:
•Grey chalky white horizontal opacity
•U Vision if crosses pupil
Treatment:
•Treat underlying cause
•EDTA chelation or phototherapeutic keratectomy
•Ophthalmology referral required
