2. In immature cortical senile cataract:
a. Visual acuity is hand motion
b. The red reflex is absent
c. Posterior segment evaluation is impossible
d. Decision of the operation is related to the patient's needs
In immature cortical cataract, some clear lens areas remain,
allowing vision and fundus examination. Surgery timing
depends on how much the cataract affects the patient's daily
visual function.
