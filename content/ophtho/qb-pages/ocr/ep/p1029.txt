8. A 60-year-old man presents with ptosis, miosis, and enophthalmos.
Which investigation should be done to identify the cause?
a. Echocardiography
b. MRI thorax
c. MRI abdomen
This presentation suggests Horner syndrome, which may result
from a lesion along the sympathetic pathway commonly due
to apical lung (Pancoast) tumor. Therefore, MRI of the thorax
is the best investigation to identify the underlying cause.
