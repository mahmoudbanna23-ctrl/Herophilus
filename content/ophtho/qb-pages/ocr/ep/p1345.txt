I. Topical steroids are used to treat all of the following EXCEPT:
a. Giant papillary conjunctivitis
b. Dendritic corneal ulcer
c. Acute iridocyclitis
d. Adenoviral conjunctivitis
Topical steroids worsen herpetic dendritic ulcers by promoting
viral replication and delaying healing. They are, however, useful
in inflammatory conditions like iridocyclitis or giant papillary
conjunctivitis.
