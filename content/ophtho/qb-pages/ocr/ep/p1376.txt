Yellow point not related to lashes + severe tenderness = Hordeolum internum
Painless swelling in eyelid + chronic + skin movable over it = Chalazion
Dripping of water during drinking + lagophthalmos + DDT > 5 min = Facial nerve palsy
causing epiphora
Painless swelling in eyelid + chronic + below medial palpebral ligament = Chronic
dacryocystitis
Swelling below medial palpebral ligament + redness + pain + hotness in eyelid
= Acute
dacryocystitis
