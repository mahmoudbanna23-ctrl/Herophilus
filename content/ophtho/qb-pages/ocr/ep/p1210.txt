3. Corneal blood staining is due to:
a. Partial hyphema
b. Vitreous hemorrhage
c. Total hyphema
d. Total hyphema with prolonged rise in IOP
Corneal blood staining occurs when total hyphema is
accompanied by persistently high intraocular pressure, forcing
red cells and hemoglobin into the cornea.
