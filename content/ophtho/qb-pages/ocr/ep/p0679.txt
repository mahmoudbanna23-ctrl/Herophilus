8. A 70-year-old man complains of gradual, painless loss of vision.
Ophthalmoscopy shows an absent red reflex. What is the most likely diagnosis?
a. Nuclear cataract
b. Cortical cataract
c. Mature cataract
d. Posterior subcapsular cataract
A mature cataract causes complete lens opacity and absent
red reflex, leading to painless vision loss. The pupil appears
white or grayish.
