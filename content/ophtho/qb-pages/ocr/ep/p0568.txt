Corneal Degenerations
Keratoconus (KC)
Investigations:
•Corneal topography diagnostic (map of curvature)
•Pachymetry corneal thickness
•Pentacam combines both + more data for staging
Treatment:
1. Visual correction:
'Spectacles (early) RGP lenses (advanced)
2.Corneal collagen cross-linking (CXL): halts progression
3.1ntrastromal corneal ring (ICR): flattens cornea, improves vision
4.Keratoplasty (penetrating/lamellar): for advanced scarring may need specs/contact
