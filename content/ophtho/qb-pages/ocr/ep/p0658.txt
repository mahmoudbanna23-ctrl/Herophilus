Aphakia
•Definition: Absence of the crystalline lens.
Causes:
1.Post-surgical (Iatrogenic) — after cataract removal w/o IOL
2.Posterior lens dislocation into vitreous cavity
Signs:
•Deep anterior chamber
•Iridodonesis: tremulous iris
•Jet-black pupil (no lens glow)
•Marked hypermetropia (+10 to +12 D)
