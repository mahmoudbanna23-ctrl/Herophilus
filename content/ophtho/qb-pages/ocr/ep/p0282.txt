Dacryoadenitis (Inflammation of lacrimal gland)
Investigations
•CT/MRI orbit (with contrast)
•Chest X-ray (for sarcoidosis)
•Biopsy (if atypical or chronic)
Treatment
•Viral supportive (warm compress, analgesics)
•Bacterial systemic antibiotics
•Idiopathic oral corticosteroids
•Chronic treat underlying disease
