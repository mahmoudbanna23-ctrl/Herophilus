Definitive Treatment
Medical
• Cycloplegic drops
•Topical antibiotics
•Topical corticosteroids (ophthalmologist)
•Systemic vitamin C (ascorbic acid)
•Tetracyclines / doxycycline (collagenase inhibitor)
•Manage IOP (acetazolamide or topical)
•Prevent symblepharon by breaking adhesions
Surgical
•Limbal stem cell transplantation
•Amniotic membrane graft
•Mucous membrane / conjunctival graft
•Keratoplasty (delayed)
•Keratoprosthesis in severe bilateral cases
