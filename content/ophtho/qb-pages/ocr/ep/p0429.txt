Conjunctival Degenerations
Pinguecula (Pingueculum)
•Definition: Yellow-white, subepithelial deposit of hyaline and elastic tissue near the nasal
or temporal limbus, sparing the cornea.
•Associations:
Sun & wind exposure
Aging (degenerative change)
•Features:
• Benign, may slowly enlarge
• May cause irritation due to abnormal tear film distribution
• Treatment:
• Lubricating drops for irritation
• Surgical excision only for cosmetic reasons
