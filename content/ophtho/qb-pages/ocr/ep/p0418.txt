Conjunctiva
Anatomy
•Thin vascular mucous membrane covering:
• Bulbar conjunctiva: over sclera (loosely attached except at limbus)
• Palpebral conjunctiva: lines inside of lids (firmly attached to tarsus)
•Fornices: junctions between bulbar & palpebral conjunctiva
•Conjunctival sac: potential space when eyelids are closed
•Palisades of Vogt (at limbus): contain stem cells for corneal epithelium
•Glands: Krause & Wolfring (accessory lacrimal), Manz, Henle (mucus)
