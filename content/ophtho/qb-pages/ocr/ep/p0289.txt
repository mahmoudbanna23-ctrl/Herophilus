Dry Eye Disease (DED)
Nerve control:
•Trigeminal nerve (V) tear production
•Facial nerve (VII) controls blinking prevents evaporation
Clinical Presentation:
•Heaviness in eyelid
•Eye fatigue
•Photophobia (light sensitivity)
•Grittiness / burning / fluctuating vision
Signs:
•TWT sec (Tear Break-Up Time)
•Schirmer test (may be U)
