Dacryocystitis (Infection Of lacrimal sac)
NLD obstruction (9 Staph/Strep)
Common cause:
A. Acute
•Pain, redness, swelling below medial canthus
•Tender tense mass, may form abscess
•May cause preseptal cellulitis
Treatment:
•Warm compresses, nasal decongestants
•Systemic + topical antibiotics
•I&D if abscess
•DCR after infection resolves
Chronic
B.
•Painless swelling (mucocele)
•Reflux of mucopurulent discharge on pressure
•Chronic unilateral conjunctivitis
Treatment:
•DCR (definitive)
