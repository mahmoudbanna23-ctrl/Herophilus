8. Ophthalmia Neonatorum
Definition: Conjunctivitis within first month of life, may cause corneal ulcer/perforation.
Etiology:
•Sexually transmitted (from birth canal):
• Neisseria gonorrhoeae purulent discharge
• Chlamydia trachomatis (D—K) inclusion blennorrhoea
• HSV type 2 vesicular lesions
•Non-STD: Staph, Strep, H. influenzae
Diagnosis:
Clinical + culture of discharge
Treatment / Prevention:
•Gonococcal: Systemic ceftriaxone + topical antibiotics
•Chlamydial: Oral erythromycin ± topical erythromycin
•Prevention: Treat maternal STDs before delivery
Complications:
•Conjunctival scarring
•Corneal ulceration / perforation blindness
