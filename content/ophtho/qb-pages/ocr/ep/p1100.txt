Paediatric Ophthalmology
Red Reflex Test
2.
Purpose: Essential neonatal & child screening test.
Detects:
•Cataract
•Glaucoma
•Retinoblastoma
•Retinal abnormalities
•Systemic diseases with ocular signs
•High refractive errors
Normal: Bright, symmetric red reflection from both eyes.
Abnormal: Dim, absent, or asymmetric reflex refer urgently.
