30. 23-year-old teacher complains that her right eye is red and irritated. You
note moderate injection of the larger conjunctival vessels, watery discharge,
and a palpable preauricular lymph node.
Based on the above information, which of the following symptoms or facts
might be elicited by careful history taking?
A. Blurred vision
B. Sore throat
D. Itching
Viral conjunctivitis is highly contagious and Often follows upper
respiratory infections or contact with infected individuals,
especially children. It presents with watery discharge and
preauricular lymphadenopathy.
