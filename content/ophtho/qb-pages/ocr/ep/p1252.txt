G. Diabetic Macular Edema (DME)
•Present in 5—15% of diabetics
•Most common cause of visual impairment in DR
•May occur at any stage (NPDR or PDR)
Diagnosis:
•Hard exudates in macula
•OCT: measures retinal thickness
•Fluorescein angiography (FA): localizes leakage
Clinically Significant Macular Edema (CSME):
•Hard exudates and thickening involving the fovea
