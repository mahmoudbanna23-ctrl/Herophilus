D. Systemic Associations
•Tight glycemic control (HbA1c <7%) reduces DR risk and severity
•Tight BP control equally important
•Advanced DR cardiovascular mortality risk (stroke, MI, amputation)
•Very tight control (HbA1c <6%) not advised in Type II / CVD due to cardiac mortality
E. Pathogenesis
•Retina end-artery circulation no collateral supply
•mechanisms:
• Pericyte loss weak capillary walls microaneurysms
• vascular permeability leakage hard exudates, retinal edema
• Capillary occlusion ischemia cotton-wool spots (soft exudates)
