8. A 55-year-old diabetic patient presents for a routine eye check-up.
Fundoscopy reveals new blood vessels on the optic disc, but his visual acuity is
still good. What is the most likely diagnosis?
a) Non-proliferative diabetic retinopathy
b) Hypertensive retinopathy
c) Proliferative diabetic retinopathy
d) Macular edema
Neovascularization on the optic disc indicates proliferative
diabetic retinopathy, even if visual acuity remains good. It
results from retinal ischemia stimulating VEGF release.
