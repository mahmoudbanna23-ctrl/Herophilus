Clinical Features
•Gradual, painless, progressive vision
•Glare, halos, poor night vision
•Monocular diplopia
•Second sight: regained near vision in early nuclear cataract (due to myopic shift)
•VA even with old glasses
•Brisk pupillary reflex and normal color sense good prognosis
•Slit lamp: confirms type and stage.
