Assessment & Management
•ABC of trauma
•Protect eye with rigid shield (never patch / press)
•Never measure IOP
•Record visual acuity
•Nil per os (NPO) for general anesthesia
•IV antibiotics
•Immediate ophthalmology referral
Investigations
•CT scan (best for IOFB localization)
•MRI contraindicated if metallic FB suspected
•Plain X-ray for radiopaque FBs
