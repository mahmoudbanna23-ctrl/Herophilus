I O. Which of the following represents imprinting of the iris lens on the anterior
capsule of the lens from the pupillary margin?
A. Hyphema
B. Hypopyon
C. Vossious ring
D. None of the above
Vossious ring refers to imprinting of iris pigment on the anterior
capsule of the lens from the pupillary margin (in case of blunt eye
trauma).
Hypopyon Pus in the AC (usually in neglected open wounds of the
globe with secondary infection) [post-traumatic infectious
endophthalmitis].
Hyphema Haemorrhage in the anterior chamber. The source of
bleeding is the iris root.
