Gonococcal & Meningococcal Conjunctivitis
•Complications:
• Corneal ulcer
• Preauricular & submandibular lymphadenopathy
•Treatment:
• Systemic antibiotics are mandatory
• Example: Ceftriaxone
•Note: H. influenzae can also cause similar findings in children
