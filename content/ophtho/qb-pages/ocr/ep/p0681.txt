9. What is the treatment for diminished vision 2 years after cataract surgery?
a. YAG laser capsulotomy
b. Repeat cataract surgery
c. Topical steroids
d. Observation
After cataract surgery, posterior capsule opacification may
cause vision loss. It is treated with Nd:YAG laser capsulotomy,
which creates an opening in the cloudy capsule.
