## Book p.9 — Q72–Q79

**72.** Recession of the near point with ageing: making near uncomfortable is termed:
A. Presbyopia. · B. Asthenopia · C. Hyperopia. · D. None of the above. — **A**

**73.** On cycloplegic refraction, one meridian is hyperopic, and the other is myopic:
A. Simple astigmatism. · B. Compound astigmatism. · C. Mixed astigmatism. · D. None of the
above. — **C**

**74.** With complete relaxation of accommodation, a myopic eye focuses
A. In front of the lens · B. Behind the retina · C. In front of the retina · D. Behind the
cornea — **C**
> **Explanation (printed):** In myopia, the eye's optical power is too strong or the axial length is
> too long, so even when accommodation is fully relaxed, parallel rays focus **in front of the
> retina**, causing blurred distance vision.

**75.** Hypermetropia can be corrected by:
A. A convex lens · B. Spherocylindrical lens · C. A concave lens · D. A -2-diopter lens for
reading — **A**

**76.** If the refraction of the right eye of a patient is minus two while that of the left eye is
minus nine, the following statements are correct **EXCEPT**:
A. The patient may complain of diplopia if he wears full-correction glasses · B. This diplopia is
uncrossed · C. His left eye might give the impression of a left convergent deviation · D. The visual
acuity of his left eye might not exceed 6/24 even with full correction — **B**

**77.** If keratometry revealed a vertical corneal power of 46 diopters and a horizontal corneal
power of 45 diopters while retinoscopy showed that the patient is emmetropic. The most probable
diagnosis is:
A. Irregular astigmatism · B. Diabetes mellitus · C. Lenticular astigmatism · D. Pseudophakia — **C**

**78.** You can have an idea about visual acuity by:
A. Ishihara charts · B. Preferential looking · C. Retinoscopy · D. Maddox rod — **B**

**79.** In regular astigmatism, light rays focus on:
A. Multiple points · B. A focal line in front of or behind the retina · C. Two focal lines · D. A
focal line perpendicular to the retina — **C**

## Book p.10 — Q80–Q88

**80.** Accommodation of the eye can be achieved by:
A. Changing the antero-posterior diameter of the eye. · B. Ciliary muscle contraction. · C. Changing
the curvature of the cornea. · D. Increasing the depth of the anterior chamber — **B**

**81.** Irregular Astigmatism is **NOT** corrected by:
A. Glasses. · B. Contact lenses. · C. Radial keratotomy. · D. LASIK. — **A**
> **Explanation (printed):** Treatment of Astigmatism:
> **1. Glasses**: Cylindrical lenses in case of **regular** astigmatism or mild irregular astigmatism
> **2. Contact lenses:** • Soft toric (sphero-cylindrical) contact lenses in cases of **regular
> astigmatism**. • **Rigid gas permeable (RGP) contact** lenses in cases of **irregular
> astigmatism.**
> **3. Refractive surgery: (For regular astigmatism):** Corneal refractive surgery in the form of
> LASIK and PRK or lenticular surgery.
> **4. Intracorneal ring (ICR) segments or Keratoplasty: (For irregular astigmatism)**

**82.** A myopic person of minus 2 has the advantage of:
A. Improvement of his distance visual acuity by the age of 40. · B. Being protected from complicated
cataract. · C. Not needing reading glasses by the age of 50. · D. Less incidence of chronic open
angle glaucoma — **C**

**83.** Ultrasonography is valuable in diagnosis of all of the following conditions **EXCEPT**:
A. Posterior scleritis. · B. Determination of IOL power in a cataractous patient. · C. Age related
macular degeneration. · D. Diabetic vitreous hemorrhage. — **C**

**84.** A patient gets poor vision when he looks through a stenopic aperture, the possible diagnosis
is:
A. Macular edema. · B. Early cataract. · C. Myopic astigmatism. · D. Open angle glaucoma. — **A**

**85.** Retinoscopy is
A. One method to visualize the retina. · B. A measure for retinal electric response. · C. An
objective method to determine the state of refraction of the eye. · D. It is used to test retinal
sensitivity — **C**

**86.** The optical power in myopia:
A. It is more than needed. · B. It is less than needed. · C. It is not affected by accommodation. ·
D. Physiologically increases with age. — **A**

**87.** Regarding the components of hypermetropia:
A. The amount of latent hypermetropia increases with age. · B. Absolute hypermetropia decreases with
age. · C. Facultative hypermetropia decreases with age. · D. Total hypermetropia increases with
age. — **C**
> **Explanation (printed):** **Facultative hyperopia:** is the element of the refractive error that
> can be corrected by the patient's own accommodation (decreases with age)
> • **Absolute hyperopia:** is the portion of the refractive error that is not compensated for by
> accommodation
> • **Manifest hyperopia**: is the sum of absolute and facultative hyperopia.
> • **Latent hyperopia:** is due to the inherent ciliary muscle tone
> • **Total hyperopia:** is the total error (LH+MH); after using cycloplegic drug (e.g., atropine)

**88.** The uncorrected visual acuity of a 20-year-old male is 6/6 in the right eye & 6/6 in the left
eye; he is wearing glasses of +2 in both eyes & his BCVA is 6/6 in both eyes. He complains of
headache; the most likely explanation is:
A. He is overcorrected. · B. He is undercorrecetd. *(sic)* · C. He does not really need glasses. ·
D. He has sinusitis. — **A**

   *"A irregular astigmatism"* (Q99 box).
5. **Q84 and Q92 are near-duplicates within the bank** — both ask what a pinhole worsening implies,
   and both key macular oedema. **Two of the four options differ** (Q84 offers myopic astigmatism and
   open-angle glaucoma; Q92 offers hyperopic astigmatism and early keratoconus), so by the folding
   test — *a replaced distractor makes a new question* — **both are held.** Recorded because the pair
   is the clearest within-bank near-repeat so far.
6. **Q91's key is a genuine defect of the OPTION, not of the key.** Option C reads *"If vertical
   meridian is more curved than horizontal, astigmatism against the rule is present"* and is keyed as
   the false statement (the stem is EXCEPT). That is correct — a more curved vertical meridian is
   astigmatism **WITH** the rule — so the key is sound. Noted only because the double negative makes
   it easy to mis-mark.
