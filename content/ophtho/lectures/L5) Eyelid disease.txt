⚠️ CONVENTIONS ADOPTED HERE.
  • Every slide gets a block, in order, all 47. Nothing is skipped or summarised.
  • Every picture is described — what kind of picture it is (clinical
    photograph, operative photo, line diagram, before-and-after pair,
    histology), what it shows, which lid, which side, and every label or caption
    printed on it is transcribed. At ~2.5 images a slide, a block that recorded
    only the heading would have recorded almost nothing.
  • TWO DISTINCT DAMAGE TYPES ARE KEPT SEPARATE, because a later question about
    a gap turns on which one it was:
        [text cut off in the original]  = text running past the slide edge, so
                                          the words themselves are lost.
        [obscured by the image]         = text still present in the slide but
                                          hidden underneath an overlaid picture
                                          or shape.
    Neither mark is used speculatively; each appears only where it was seen.
  • A HEADING WITH NOTHING UNDER IT is a third kind of gap and is said so
    explicitly in the block where it happens.
  • Spelling, capitalisation and spacing are the lecturer's own and are NOT
    tidied ("TensilonTest" set solid, "Hordeolum Externum", "Seborrheic",
    "Echymosis", "Lid Swelling (Edema))" with its doubled bracket, etc.).
  • READING SURFACE. The four-up contact sheets are an INDEX, not a reading
    surface: at ~760 px a cell cannot resolve which margin a lesion sits on,
    whether the lashes are gone over it, or the texture of a crust. Every slide
    was navigated on its sheet and then READ from the full-size single-slide
    render in `raw\s-NN.png`. Four slides needed more than that and were
    re-rendered as 300 dpi crops before being described: SLIDE 37 (the eye),
    SLIDE 43 (left panel), SLIDE 45 (left panel) and SLIDE 46. Where a crop
    still could not settle a question — which lid, which canthus — the block
    SAYS SO instead of guessing; that happens on slides 38, 43 and 45–46.
  • A HEADING IS NEVER A DIAGNOSIS THE SLIDE DOES NOT PRINT. Slides 37–46 have
    no heading at all, so their blocks are headed "[no heading printed]" and
    describe what is visible. None of those ten pictures is given a name here.
