⚠️ CONVENTIONS ADOPTED HERE, because the usual ones do not fit a deck
with no text:
  • `[picture only]` heads every slide with no typed word at all.
  • `PICTURE:` lines are MY DESCRIPTION of what the photograph shows.
    They are observation, not the lecturer's words, and must never be
    quoted as if the slide said them.
  • `PRINTED CAPTION:` lines are text physically visible in the image —
    almost always a figure caption from Kanski/Bowling or from Nerad
    that was captured when the lecturer screenshotted the book page.
    These ARE verbatim. Where such a caption is present but too small or
    too clipped to read even at 200 dpi it is marked
    `[caption present but illegible]`.
  • Where a pasted book figure carries the circled letters (A), (B), (C)…
    those letters are reproduced, because several of the captions key to
    them.
  • Diagnoses are NAMED only where the printed caption names them, or
    where the photograph is unambiguous and the naming is then marked
    `[my reading, not printed]`. Do not treat a `[my reading]` as
    sourced.
  • No slide overflows its text box, because no slide has a text box.
    The `[text cut off in the original]` marker therefore appears
    nowhere in this file. Clipped BOOK captions inside pasted
    screenshots are marked `[caption clipped in the pasted image]`.
