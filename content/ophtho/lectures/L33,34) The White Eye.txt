                              Take Home Message
• The white eye involves lesion of the cornea, pupillary membrane, the lens or the posterior segment

• Symptoms include variable degrees of diminution of vision, disfigurement and cause-related

• Treatment depends on the cause

• Corneal scars may follow healed inflammations or hydrops

• Pupillary membranes may be congenital or acquired (inflammatory)

• Cataracts may be congenital or acquired

• White pupil in a child may be a sign of retinoblastoma
