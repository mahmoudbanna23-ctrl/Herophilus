 • **NEAR ADD POWER** at any age. p81 says "spherical plus lens addition" and stops.
 • **SNELLEN NOTATION / VISUAL ACUITY RECORDING.** 6/6, 20/20 and LogMAR never appear. The only
   acuity content is an unexplained Landolt-C chart photograph on p38 and the "20' or 6 M"
   testing distance inside the figures on p40 and p51.
 • **REFRACTIVE INDEX VALUES.** p16 gives n = c/v and the deck never prints a single value of n —
   not for cornea, aqueous, lens, vitreous, air or water.

**CLINICAL TOPICS a refraction lecture would be expected to teach and this deck does not:**
 • **AMBLYOPIA** — named once on p83 as a consequence of anisometropia, then never defined,
   classified, or treated. No occlusion therapy, no critical period, no age limit.
 • **MYOPIA CONTROL** — atropine, orthokeratology, defocus spectacles, outdoor time. Nothing.
   The deck says myopia "often stops progression in the late 2nd decade" and offers no
   intervention to slow it.
 • **PATHOLOGICAL/DEGENERATIVE MYOPIA** beyond the p46 complication list — no Fuchs spot, no
   lacquer cracks, no tilted disc, no myopic crescent/conus.
 • **LATENT / MANIFEST / TOTAL HYPERMETROPIA**, and facultative vs absolute hypermetropia. This
   standard classification is absent; the deck classifies hyperopia only by cause (axial,
   curvature, index).
 • **ACCOMMODATIVE ESOTROPIA** beyond the bare name on p55 and p58 — no AC/A ratio, no
   refractive vs non-refractive type, no full-correction rule.
 • **CONTACT LENSES** as a subject — p35 gives one line ("Cosmetic & a wider field of vision …
   risk of infection") and p71 names soft toric and RGP. No material, no wear schedule, no
   fitting, no care, and **no named complication** (no microbial keratitis, no giant papillary
   conjunctivitis, no hypoxia).
 • **REFRACTIVE SURGERY** as a subject — PRK, LASIK, phakic IOL, clear lens extraction, ICR and
   keratoplasty are named as options in the treatment trees and **never described**. No mechanism,
   no flap, no ablation, no patient-selection criterion beyond the <40 / >40 age split on p47, no
   contraindication (thin cornea, keratoconus, unstable refraction), and **no complication of any
   refractive procedure** — no ectasia, no dry eye, no haze, no halos, no regression.
 • **KERATOCONUS** — named twice, as a cause of curvature myopia (p42) and of irregular
   astigmatism (p64). Never described, staged or worked up.
 • **APHAKIA** — named once on p53 as a cause of index hypermetropia; its correction is never
   discussed.
 • **PSEUDOPHAKIA and IOL POWER CALCULATION** — not mentioned at all.
 • **ANISEIKONIA MANAGEMENT** beyond "contact lens or refractive surgery" on p85. No iseikonic
   lens, no percentage limit of image-size disparity.
 • **PREVALENCE, INCIDENCE AND EPIDEMIOLOGY.** The deck contains **no percentage and no
   prevalence figure of any kind**, for any refractive error, anywhere.
 • **GENETICS / RISK FACTORS** for myopia (family history, near work, education, ethnicity) —
   not covered.
 • **SCREENING** — no age, no interval, no school-vision-screening protocol.
 • **PAEDIATRIC REFRACTION** — no emmetropisation, no normal refraction at birth, no
   age-appropriate norms, no retinoscopy in children.
 • **ANY EPONYM.** There is no named test, no named sign, no named law (Snell's law is used
   implicitly on p13/p17 and never named) and no named person anywhere in the deck.
 • **ANY CLINICAL CASE, MCQ OR SELF-TEST QUESTION.**
