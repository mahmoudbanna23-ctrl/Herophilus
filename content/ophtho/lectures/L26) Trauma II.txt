(Coagulate tissue proteins  less
penetration)

Stages of severe chemical injuries:
Immediate acute phase: (3 days)

Conjunctiva: epithelial necrosis, chemosis,
limbal blanching.

Cornea: epithelial necrosis,

edema and opacification.

IOP: increased (collagen shrinkage)
Stages of severe chemical injuries:


Subacute phase: (3-7 days)

Active inflammatory destruction of tissues.

Cornea: ulceration and perforation.
Stages of severe chemical injuries:
Late or chronic phase: (> 7 days)
     Cicatrization
Conjunctiva: scarring 
Dry eye, symblepharon, entropion.
Cornea: Scarring, vascularization,
opacification.
IOP: increases (scarring of episcleral venous
plexus).
Management
First aid treatment
Copious irrigation ( 15-30 min ) - to restore normal pH
Remove particulate matter
Emergency room:
   Surface anesthetic
   Sedation or analgesics
   Separate lids.. Evert lids… Remove particulate matter
   Copious irrigation with saline 2L over 1 hour
   Monitor with litmus paper
   Antidote: Alkalis  boric acid 4%    Lime  EDTA
            Acids  Na bicarbonate 3%
            Iodine  starch
            Aniline dyes  glycerine

Topical: Cycloplegic drops
           Antibiotic drops
           Steroid eye drops: to decrease inflammation
           Citric acid: to inhibit neutrophil activity
          Lubricants

          Ascorbic acid: to enhance collagen production
           Tetracyclines: to inhibit collagenase and neutrophil activity

Systemic
Ca Chelators: EDTA, Penicillamine: to inhibit collagenase

Bandage soft contact lenses
IOP reduction.
