⚠️ CONVENTIONS ADOPTED HERE.
  • Every slide gets a block, in order, even when it carries no typed text at all.
  • Where a slide has NO typed title, that is recorded as "(no typed title on this
    slide)" — it is a real property of the deck, not a reading failure.
  • Picture descriptions state the kind of image (slit-lamp photograph,
    everted-lid photograph, diagram, clinical photograph, before/after pair), what
    it shows, and every label or caption printed on it.
  • Where a picture is repeated later in the deck the repeat is named, because the
    lecturer re-shows images when he moves from one disease to the next and the
    repeat is where the second diagnosis is being taught.
  • Two distinct damage types would be marked if present, and they are NOT the
    same thing: `[text cut off in the original]` = text running past the slide
    edge, so the words exist but are unreadable; `[obscured by the image]` = text
    lying underneath an overlaid picture or shape, so the words are present in the
    file but hidden on the rendered slide. NEITHER occurs in this deck — no slide
    overflows and no picture covers typed text.
  • A heading with nothing under it is a third, different kind of gap. In this
    deck almost every slide IS a heading with a picture under it, which is not
    the same as an empty heading; genuinely empty slides are called out where they
    occur.
  • Spelling and capitalisation are the lecturer's own, kept verbatim
    ("Vernal KeratoConjunctivitis", "Nebula ex Pannus", "PTCs").

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PICTURE: a four-panel composite clinical photograph (2×2) of the same young woman
