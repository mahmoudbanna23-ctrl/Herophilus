• The vestibule: The most anterior part of the nose.

• The olfactory area : The upper part of the nose related to
 the superior turbinate, cribriform plate.
                   lined by columnar non ciliated cells

Choanal atresia
History
■ In 1755, Roederer first described congenital
  choanal atresia.

■ In1854, Emmert reported the first successful
 surgical procedure for congenital choanal atresia.
Choanal atresia
■ Choanal atresia is the most common congenital nasal
  anomaly (1 in 7,000 to 1 in 10,000 live births).
■ unilateral to bilateral cases is 2:1.
■ Unilat. atresia occurs more frequently on RT
■ Maternal age or parity does not increase the frequency
  of occurrence.
■ Chromosomal anomalies are found in 6% of infants with
  choanal atresia
■ Sex: F> M
■ No racial differance
Choanal atresia
■ Etiology
theories proposed to explain the occurrence of
   choanal atresia
1) Persistence of the buccopharyngeal, Failure of
   the bucconasal membrane to rupture
2) Medial outgrowth of vertical and horizontal
   processes of the palatine bone
3) Abnormal mesodermal adhesions forming in the
   choanal area
4) Misdirection of mesodermal flow due to local
   factors
Choanal atresia
■ Symptoms:
■ Bilateral choanal atresia:
   • Symptomes of severe airway obstruction and
     cyclical cyanosis alleviated by crying are the
     classic signs of neonatal bilateral atresia.
■ Unilateral choanal atresia:
   • present later in life with unilateral rhinorrhea.
