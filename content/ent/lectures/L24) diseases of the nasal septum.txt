                        ANATOMY

   NASAL SEPTUM HAS 3 PARTS :
 Membranous septum.


 Cartilagenous portion composed of quadrilateral
  cartilage. Quadrilatral cartilage(Septal cartilage) not
 only forms a partition between the right and left nasal
 cavities but also provides support to the tip and
 dorsum of cartilaginous part of nose.
 is 3-4 mm thick

 Bony septum : mainly by perpendicular plate of
  ethmoid and vomer, which is continuous above with
  the cribriform plate and crista galli.

Fructure of the nasal bones and septum

  Aetiopathogenisis:

  -Trauma ( RTA , Sports)inflicted from front, side
    or below.
  - The septum and nasal bones may buckle on
    itself, fracture vertically, horizontally or get
    crushed.
   -Fracture of septal cartilage or its dislocation
    can occur without nasal bones fracture in cases
    of trauma to lower nose.
 Classification
 Nature of injury
 Extent of deformity
 Pattern of fracture
Signs :

External deformity difficult to examine in acute condition,
facial deformity and contusions.
 Better seen after the edema has reduced
 Look for movements of eye
 Palpate the nose to look for : deformity, deviation,
  crepitus, mobility, any tenderness
 Look for septal hematoma, abscess
 Investigation : xray nasal bone
 In severe facial injury : CT Scan nose and PNS to be done
 Treatment
 Most patients don’t need any active treatment in mild
  condition just observation.
 Closed Reduction of fracture : under GA or LA
 Principle for reduction : mobilize the fragments first by
  increasing and then decreasing the degree of deformity

     Ashe
    walsham forceps
 Splints may be necessary
 Open reduction  ( Septorhinoplasty): fractures with
 dislocation of nasal dorsum and significant septal
 deformity
            DEVIATED NASAL SEPTUM
 Extremly common 80% of population , more in males.
 Etiology :
1-Trauma with or without nasal bone fractures
2 – Developmental;
a-Birth mouldin theory
b-Abnormal intrauterine postures with compression
forces acting on the nose and upper jaws
c-Post natal trauma
3-Racial (Hereditary).
4- Mass effect.
Deviations
 Cartilagenous, C or S
  shaped deviations either
  in vertical or horizontal
  plane

 Cartilagenous or bony
  septum and Spurs.

 Anterior dislocation
Symptoms :
Nasal obstruction
 Can be on the same side of the deviation or opposite
  side because of the hypertrophic changes in
  turbinate.
 Snoring
 Mucosal changes : dryness, crusting and epistaxis
 Headache: pressure exerted by septal deviations on
  adjacent sensory nerves (Anterior ethmoidal nerve
  syndrome).
 Sinusitis
 Deviations in region of nasal valve cause greatest
  obstruction : cottle test
