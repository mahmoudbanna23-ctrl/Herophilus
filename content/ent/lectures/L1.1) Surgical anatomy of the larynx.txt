SLIDE 1 — Title.

SLIDE 2 — Two plates.
  (A) Block diagram of the upper aerodigestive tract: nasal cavities, oral cavity, pharynx,
      larynx, trachea, oesophagus, shown as interlocking colour-coded volumes.
  (B) Lateral view of the laryngeal skeleton: Epiglottis, Laryngeal inlet, Hyoid bone,
      Thyroid cartilage, Cricoid cartilage, Trachea, with the extent of "Larynx" bracketed.

SLIDE 3 — Laryngeal skeleton, two views.
  Left (posterior): Greater cornu of hyoid, Epiglottis, Cartilago triticea, Thyrohyoid
   membrane, Lateral thyrohyoid ligament, Superior cornu of thyroid cartilage, Thyroid lamina,
   Thyroepiglottic ligament, Corniculate cartilage, Arytenoid cartilage, Inferior cornu of
   thyroid cartilage, Posterior cricoarytenoid ligament, First tracheal cartilage.
  Right (sagittal): Epiglottis, Hyoid bone, Thyrohyoid membrane, Thyroid cartilage lamina,
   Corniculate cartilage, Arytenoid cartilage, MUSCULAR PROCESS, VOCAL PROCESS, Vocal ligament,
   Cricothyroid ligament, Cricoid cartilage, Trachea.
  ⚠️ THE ARYTENOID'S TWO PROCESSES ARE LABELLED HERE — muscular and vocal.

SLIDE 4 — Thyroid and cricoid cartilages in isolation.
  (A) Thyroid cartilage, three aspects: Thyroid notch, Laryngeal prominence, Superior cornu,
      Inferior cornu, Superior thyroid tubercle, Inferior tubercle, Oblique line.
  (B) Cricoid cartilage: LAMINA and ARCH labelled separately, Airway, Trachea, and two facets —
      "Facet for articulation with arytenoid cartilage" and "Facet for articulation with
      inferior horn of thyroid cartilage"; arytenoid shown with its Ridge and Depressions.
  ⚠️ THE CRICOID IS DRAWN AS A COMPLETE RING (lamina behind, arch in front) — the deck shows
  this but nowhere states in words that it is the only complete ring of the airway.

SLIDE 5 — Epiglottis and the small cartilages.
  Anterior and posterior surfaces of the epiglottis, Epiglottic tubercle, Right thyroid lamina,
  Thyro-epiglottic ligament, Cricoid, Trachea; second figure labels the CUNEIFORM CARTILAGES
  and CORNICULATE CARTILAGES sitting on the arytenoids.

SLIDE 6 — Extrinsic membranes and ligaments.
  Hyo-epiglottic ligament, Hyoid bone, Lateral thyrohyoid ligaments, Triticeal cartilage,
  "Aperture for internal branch of superior laryngeal nerve and associated artery",
  Thyrohyoid membrane, Median thyrohyoid ligament, Cricotracheal ligament.
  Second (line) figure: Hyoid, Middle thyro-hyoid ligament, Thyroid cartilage,
  Middle crico-thyroid ligament, Cricoid cartilage, Trachea.
  ⚠️ THE INTERNAL BRANCH OF THE SLN PIERCES THE THYROHYOID MEMBRANE — labelled here.

SLIDE 7 — Internal membranes.
  Hyo-epiglottic ligament, Airway, QUADRANGULAR MEMBRANE (left), Cuneiform cartilage,
  Corniculate cartilage, Arytenoid cartilage, Vestibular ligament (cut away);
  second figure: Vocal ligament, Cricothyroid ligament, Median cricothyroid ligament.

SLIDE 8 — The glottis from above, plus an endoscopic photograph.
  Diagram labelled: Quadrangular membrane, Epiglottis, Vestibular ligament, Vocal ligament,
  Cricothyroid ligament, Vocal process of arytenoid, Muscular process of arytenoid,
  Corniculate cartilage. Photograph: normal cords in adduction.

SLIDE 9 — Intrinsic muscles, three plates.
  Posterior view: TRANSVERSE ARYTENOID, OBLIQUE ARYTENOID, POSTERIOR CRICO-ARYTENOID,
   Rima glottidis, Vocal ligaments, VOCALIS MUSCLE, LATERAL CRICO-ARYTENOID,
   Lateral cricothyroid ligament.
  Lateral view: Aryepiglottic muscle, Superior thyroid notch, Saccule, THYRO-ARYTENOID MUSCLE.
  Inset: CRICOTHYROID MUSCLE, oblique part and straight part.
  ⚠️ THE ONLY MUSCLE NAMED "POSTERIOR CRICO-ARYTENOID" IS THE ABDUCTOR; the adductor pair is
  the LATERAL crico-arytenoid. There is no "anterior cricoarytenoid" muscle anywhere in the
  deck or in anatomy. (Relevant to Grade Gain Throat Q19, whose option a invents one.)
  ⚠️ VOCALIS IS DRAWN AS THE MEDIAL PART OF THE THYRO-ARYTENOID.

SLIDE 10 — The laryngeal cavity.
  (A) Laryngeal inlet, Epiglottis, Aryepiglottic fold, Cut edge of mucosa, Cuneiform tubercle,
      Corniculate tubercle, Interarytenoid depression, Cut edge of right thyroid lamina,
      Vestibule, LARYNGEAL SACCULE, LARYNGEAL VENTRICLE, Infraglottic space, Cricoid arch.
  (B) Coronal: Laryngeal saccule, Middle part of cavity, VESTIBULAR FOLD (mucosa overlying
      vestibular ligament), VOCAL FOLD (mucosa overlying vocal ligament), Trachea.
  (C) From above: Vocal fold, Vestibular fold, Aryepiglottic fold, RIMA VESTIBULI,
      RIMA GLOTTIDIS, Cuneiform tubercle, Corniculate tubercle, Interarytenoid fold.
  ⚠️ THE RIMA GLOTTIDIS IS THE SPACE BETWEEN THE TRUE VOCAL FOLDS — labelled, not defined in prose.

SLIDE 11 — Sagittal line drawing of the laryngeal compartments.
  Base of tongue, VALLECULA, Suprahyoid epiglottis (tip), Hyoid, PRE-EPIGLOTTIC SPACE,
  Aryepiglottic fold, Infrahyoid epiglottis, FALSE CORD, VENTRICLE, TRUE VOCAL CORD,
  Thyroid cartilage, Thyro-cricoid membrane, SUBGLOTTIC SPACE, Cricoid cartilage.
  ⚠️ THE SUPRAGLOTTIS / GLOTTIS / SUBGLOTTIS DIVISION IS SHOWN HERE GRAPHICALLY.

SLIDE 12 — Cadaveric dissection (posterior) and a schematic.
  Dissection labelled: Superior horn of thyroid cartilage, Epiglottis, Aryepiglottic fold,
  Thyroid cartilage, Aryepiglottic muscle, Arytenoid cartilage, Posterior cricoarytenoid
  muscle, Cricoid cartilage, Inferior horn of thyroid cartilage.
  Schematic (coronal): Hyoid bone, PIRIFORM SINUS, Thyroid cartilage, Ventricle,
  Cricoid cartilage — showing the piriform sinus lateral to the larynx.

SLIDE 13 — The layered structure of the vocal fold.
  Left: EPITHELIUM, then SUPERFICIAL / INTERMEDIATE / DEEP layers, then MUSCULARIS.
  Right: SQUAMOUS EPITHELIUM over the fold, COLUMNAR EPITHELIUM above and below it,
  Vestibular ligament, VOCALIS, and the LAMINA PROPRIA bracketed as
  Superficial / Intermediate / Deep.
  ⚠️ THE TWO EXAMINABLE FACTS OF THIS SLIDE: the vocal cord is covered by SQUAMOUS
  epithelium (the rest of the larynx is columnar/respiratory), and the SUPERFICIAL LAYER OF
  THE LAMINA PROPRIA is the subepithelial layer — REINKE'S SPACE. The deck draws it but does
  not print the eponym "Reinke".

SLIDE 14 — Nerve supply, two plates (right lateral view).
  Superior laryngeal nerve with its INTERNAL BRANCH and EXTERNAL BRANCH; the external branch
  running to the CRICOTHYROID MUSCLE; inferior pharyngeal constrictor; CRICOPHARYNGEUS MUSCLE
  (part of inferior pharyngeal constrictor); RECURRENT LARYNGEAL NERVE.
  Second plate (thyroid lamina removed): Internal branch of superior laryngeal nerve,
  SENSORY BRANCHES TO LARYNX, Anastomosis (of Galen), Aryepiglottic muscle, Thyroepiglottic
  muscle, Transverse and oblique arytenoid muscles, Thyroarytenoid muscle, Vocalis muscle,
  Lateral cricoarytenoid muscle, Posterior cricoarytenoid muscle, Cricothyroid articular facet,
  Anterior and posterior branches of inferior laryngeal nerve, Recurrent laryngeal nerve.
  ⚠️ THIS IS THE SLIDE BEHIND FOUR QUESTIONS: the EXTERNAL branch of the SLN supplies the
  CRICOTHYROID — the one intrinsic muscle the RLN does NOT supply; the INTERNAL branch carries
  the sensory supply of the larynx above the cords; the RLN supplies every other intrinsic
  muscle and the sensation below the cords.

SLIDE 15 — Recurrent laryngeal nerve injury, schematic.
  "Transection of recurrent laryngeal nerve" → cords drawn ABDUCTED (apart).
  "Trauma but no transection" → cords drawn ADDUCTED, with arrows pushing them together.
  ⚠️ The deck gives NO named cord positions (median / paramedian / cadaveric) and NO table of
  voice outcomes. Those must come from elsewhere.

SLIDE 16 — Blood supply.
  Right lateral: Internal carotid artery, Superior laryngeal artery, External carotid artery,
  Thyrohyoid membrane, Superior thyroid artery, Common carotid artery, Thyroid cartilage,
  Inferior constrictor muscle of pharynx, Cricoid cartilage, INFERIOR LARYNGEAL ARTERY,
  Inferior thyroid artery, Trachea, Thyrocervical trunk, Subclavian artery, Oesophagus,
  Scalene tubercle on rib I, Rib I.
  Anterior: Right internal jugular vein, Hyoid bone, Superior thyroid vein, Superior laryngeal
  vein, Thyrohyoid membrane, Cricothyroid ligament, Thyroid gland, Inferior laryngeal vein,
  Inferior thyroid vein, Right subclavian vein, Manubrium of sternum.
  ⚠️ THE CAROTID BIFURCATION IS DRAWN but NOT LABELLED WITH A VERTEBRAL LEVEL, and no slide in
  either L1.1 or L1.2 states a C-level for it. (Relevant to Grade Gain Throat Q2.)
