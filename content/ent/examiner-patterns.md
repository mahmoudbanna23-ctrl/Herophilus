## 5. Defects and judgement calls in this run

- **⚠️ p.3046 prints a prompt with no answer.** Line 4 reads *"Initial presentation of glottic
  carcinoma"* and stops — no arrow, no answer. **Nothing is lost:** the very next line is *"Most
  common presentation of glottic carcinoma → HOV"*, and p.3056 prints *"The first symptom of glottic
  carcinoma → hoarseness of voice"*. Recorded because a reader counting lines would otherwise think
  a fact went missing.
- **The "preserved sulcus" line is right, and reads wrong.** p.3064 gives *postauricular redness,
  oedema, **preserved** sulcus → acute mastoiditis*, whereas the sign usually taught is the
  **obliterated** sulcus. Both are true at different stages, and the slide says so: `L16) Chronic
  otitis media.txt` separates *"the stage of acute mastoiditis"* (tenderness and redness over the
  mastoid) from *"when post-auricular abscess develops"* (the auricle **pushed outwards and
  downwards**). The sulcus goes when the **subperiosteal abscess** forms, not when the mastoiditis
  starts. **The theory must draw that line explicitly**, because the slide's own listed differential
  is *furunculosis with post-auricular lymphadenitis* — the exact look-alike the sulcus decides.
- **The adenoid-imaging line settles an earlier open question.** pp.3048 and 3054 both key **plain
  X-ray**, and p.3054's stem says *"best **imaging** modality"*. That corroborates the resolution
  already recorded for `entep-enr-11` vs `entep-nose-56`: where the stem says *imaging*, the answer
  is X-ray; where it does not, the bank keys **nasopharyngoscopy**. Not a contradiction.
- **The pyriform-fossa nerve is confirmed twice** — pp.3050 and 3065 both key **vagus**, matching
  `entep-enr-21`.
- **"Ear wash + antifungal" for otomycosis in a diabetic** (p.3069) sits oddly beside p.3064's
  *"suction + local antifungal"*, since irrigation is normally avoided in a diabetic ear. Both are
  recorded as printed. *(The caution about irrigating a diabetic ear is not taken from the course
  material.)*

## 6. How to use this file

1. **Set `w` from §2.** Anything in that table is `must`. A fact printed once and covered by a
   transcribed question is `high`. A fact printed once with no question behind it is `know`.
2. **Write each chapter's `intro` from the shape, not the topic.** The examiner's habit here is
   *EXCEPT* stems and *timing* stems; say so in the intro rather than describing the disease.
3. **§4 is a checklist.** Those six facts have no question to teach them, so if the notes do not
