1. ⚠️ **THE E/F RANKINGS WERE TRUNCATED AT TOP-20, AND THAT IS A SILENT COVERAGE LIMIT.** Q363's
   stem Jaccard is **0.57** and Q347's is **0.50**; the first pass's twentieth-place entry sat at
   **0.57**. **They were ranked correctly and fell off the printed list.** A ranking you truncate is
   a ranking that quietly stops being an instrument.
2. ⚠️ **STAGE C's OPTION-OVERLAP FLOOR OF 0.34 IS TOO HIGH FOR SYNONYM MENUS.** Q363's option-token
   overlap with its twin is ≈0.2 — because *congestion* and *obstruction* share no token at all.

### What the re-sweep did differently

- **Stripped filler before comparing options** — `it`, `its`, `the`, `in nature`, `type`, `form`,
  `more/less than`, `per year/week`. This is what caught Q347.
- **Dropped the stage-C floor to 0.20** and **ranked stems to 0.45** instead of a fixed top-N.
- Added a **combined key-plus-stem** score that neither original stage computed.
