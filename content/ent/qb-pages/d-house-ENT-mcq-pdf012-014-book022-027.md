## p.23

**1.** Which of the following middle ear walls harbors the tensor tympani muscle?
A. Anterior wall · B. Posterior wall · C. Lateral wall · D. Medial wall — **Answer: A**

**2.** Which of the following structures of the inner ear is responsible for producing endolymph?
A. Reissner's membrane · B. Basilar membrane · C. Stria vascularis · D. Organ of Corti — **Answer: C**

**3.** Trigeminal nerve is responsible for referred otalgia that is caused by pathology in which of the following sites?
A. Larynx · B. Nasopharynx · C. Tonsils · D. Cervical spine — **Answer: B**
*(options C–D and the answer continue on p.24)*

## p.24

**4.** Which of the following is the first part in drum to be retracted in acute otitis media?
A. Pars tensa · B. Pars flaccida · C. Umbo · D. Handle of malleus — **Answer: B**

**5.** Which of the following statements best describes the healthy tympanic membrane?
A. It's a semi-transparent membrane with an elastic upper part · B. It's a semi-transparent membrane with an elastic lower part · C. It's an opaque membrane with an elastic upper part · D. It's an opaque membrane with an elastic lower part — **Answer: B**

**6.** The communication between mastoid antrum and middle ear attic is called….?
A. Hypotympanum · B. Mesotympanum · C. Hypertympanum · D. Aditus ad antrum — **Answer: D**

**7.** The tegmen tympani is related to which of the following anatomical areas?
A. Middle cranial fossa · B. Posterior cranial fossa · C. Anterior cranial fossa · D. All of the above · E. None of the above — **Answer: A**

**8.** The Eustachian tube in adults at rest is ….?
A. Open · B. Closed · C. Partially open · D. Corrugated — **Answer: B**
*(options C–D and the answer continue on p.25)*

## p.25

**9.** What is the number of constrictions found in the external auditory canal?
A. 1 · B. 2 · C. 3 · D. 4 — **Answer: B**

**10.** During otoscopic examination of the ear of an infant, the examiner has to pull the ear pinna in which direction?
A. Upwards, backwards and laterally · B. Upwards, forwards and laterally · C. Upwards, backwards and medially · D. Downwards — **Answer: D**

**11.** During otoscopic examination of the ear of an adult, the examiner has to pull the ear pinna in which direction?
A. Upwards, backwards and laterally · B. Upwards, forwards and laterally · C. Upwards, backwards and medially · D. Downwards — **Answer: A**

**12.** A 5-year-old child complains of recurrent attack of acute otitis media, food regurgitation through nose and hypernasality of sound. What's your provisional diagnosis?
A. Adenoid hypertrophy · B. Cleft palate · C. Nasopharyngeal carcinoma · D. Cancer in the posterior 1/3 of tongue — **Answer: B**

**13.** Which of the following nerves give supply to the tympanic membrane?
A. Glossopharyngeal nerve · B. Vagus nerve · C. Trigeminal nerve · D. All of the above · E. None of the above — **Answer: D**
*(options B–E and the answer continue on p.26)*

## p.26

**14.** Which of the following is true regarding vestibular system of the ear?
A. Sacule is responsible for balance during linear movement in forward and backward directions · B. Utricle is responsible for balance during linear movement in upward and downward directions · C. Sacule is responsible for balance during linear movement in upward and downward directions · D. Vestibular organ for linear movement is called crista ampullaris — **Answer: C**

**15.** An old-aged male patient complained of ear pain which made him see a doctor. After examination, the doctor told him that he has a pyriform fossa mass which caused his ear pain. Which of the following is the nerve along which the pain is referred to the patient's ear?
A. Trigeminal nerve · B. Glossopharyngeal nerve · C. Vagus nerve · D. Hypoglossal nerve — **Answer: C**

**16.** The middle ear cleft is lined by which type of epithelium?
A. Ciliated columnar epithelium · B. Keratinized stratified squamous epithelium · C. Non keratinized stratified squamous epithelium · D. Non ciliated columnar epithelium — **Answer: A**

**17.** Ear skeleton is composed of cartilage except which of the following areas?
A. Lobule · B. Incisura · C. Tragus · D. A and B — **Answer: D**

## p.27

**18.** An 8-year-old boy was brought by his parents to the otology clinic complaining of ear discharge and pain. The otologist found that the child screams in pain whenever he touches his tragus during the examination. Which of the following is the most probable diagnosis of this condition?
A. Acute otitis media · B. Chronic otitis media · C. Acute otitis externa · D. All of the above — **Answer: C**

**19.** A 22-year-old female patient visits the ENT clinic because she has been suffering from reduced hearing in her left ear together with tinnitus and pain. On examination, the doctor found that a foreign body was impacted in her external auditory canal. Which of the following is the most common site where that foreign body is impacted?
A. External auditory meatus · B. Junction between lateral 1/3 and medial 2/3 of external auditory canal · C. Junction between lateral 2/3 and medial 1/3 of external auditory canal · D. Inner portion of external auditory canal just lateral to ear drum — **Answer: B**

**20.** Which of the following anatomical areas is the most suitable area for incision in surgical interventions?
A. Ear lobule as it's devoid of cartilage · B. Helix as it's curved so the scar will be invisible · C. Ear concha as it's depressed so incision will be easy · D. Trago-helicine incisura as it's devoid of cartilage — **Answer: D**

**21.** A 32-year-old male patient complained that his ear was extremely painful; so he sought medical advice and was told by his physician that he has an infection in his external ear called "furunculosis". Which of the following parts of the external ear is affected by this infection?
A. The whole external auditory canal · B. The outer 1/3 of external auditory canal only · C. The inner 2/3 of external auditory canal only · D. External auditory meatus only — **Answer: B**
