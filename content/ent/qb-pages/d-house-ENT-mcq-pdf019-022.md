**58.** A 7-year-old male child with bilateral secretory otitis media and snoring with nasal obstruction for 2 days. What is the treatment of choice?
A. Wait and see for 3 months · B. Adenoidectomy · C. Bilateral grommet's tube insertion after myringotomy · D. Medical treatment — **Answer: A**

**59.** A 10-year-old child developed acute otitis media. He was treated with antibiotics for10 days. His pain and fever subsided completely but still had conductive hearing loss, next line of treatment is….?
A. Wait and watch for 3 months for fluid to drain spontaneously · B. Do a myringotomy and insert a grommet · C. Give another course of a different antibiotic · D. Do a myringotomy and culture the middle ear fluid — **Answer: A**

**60.** Which of the following is the earliest diagnostic otoscopic finding in pediatric acute otitis media?
A. Bulging tympanic membrane · B. Throbbing pain · C. Retracted tympanic membrane · D. Loss of luster of the tympanic membrane — **Answer: D**

**61.** Which of the following is the sequel of otitis media with effusion?
A. Chronic suppurative otitis media · B. Acute mastoiditis · C. Adhesive otitis media · D. Labyrinthitis — **Answer: C**

**62.** Which of the following is the first line of treatment of a child with acute otitis media and LMN 7th nerve paralysis?
