### 7. Nasal fractures

**442.** An adolescent football player sustained a blunt trauma 30 min ago while playing and was brought to you because of nosebleed and nasal deformity. On examination, there is no active bleeding, minimal edema but the nose is grossly deviated when compared to his ID photo. What is the best next step?
A. CT nose and paranasal sinuses · B. X-ray nose · C. Anterior nasal packing and referral · D. Referral for reduction immediately · E. Assurance and counselling for reduction after 10 days — **Answer: D**

**443.** A football player presented with deviated and fractured nose with a history of nose trauma two hours ago. He had severe edema and tenderness, what is the best management?
A. Immediate digital reduction without anesthesia · B. Immediate digital reduction under anesthesia · C. Immediate reduction intraoperatively under anesthesia · D. Conservative treatment for 1 week then reassessment — **Answer: D**

**444.** A football player presented with deviated and fractured nose with a history of nose trauma two hours ago. He had no edema or hematoma, what is the best management?
A. Immediate digital reduction without anesthesia · B. Immediate digital reduction under anesthesia · C. Immediate reduction intraoperatively under anesthesia · D. Conservative treatment for 1 week then reassessment — **Answer: A**
