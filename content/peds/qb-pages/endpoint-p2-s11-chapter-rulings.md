## The rulings

**1. Leukaemia in all its forms is `malignant`.** ALL, AML, the film, the marrow, the
immunophenotype, the risk groups, the treatment. Measured on nineteen pages — it is the section's
dominant topic.

**2. Brain tumours and medulloblastoma are `malignant-solid`,** `mal-19` and `mal-20`. Measured on
pp.1219, 1221, 1231-1232, 1263-1264 and inside several multi-topic option ladders.

**3. Wilms and neuroblastoma are `malignant-solid`,** `mal-22`, which is written as the comparison
between them. ⚠️ **Wilms has a second shelf and section 7 already ruled on it:** `renal`/`ren-8`
takes a Wilms question only when it is a haematuria differential — *what else could cause this
child's red urine*. When the question asks **which tumour this abdominal mass is**, it is
`malignant-solid`. In a section the book calls Malignant diseases the second reading is the usual
one; the abdominal-mass pages are 1227-1228, 1241-1244 and 1251-1252.

**4. ⚠️ Pancytopenia splits, and the split is measurable.** `haematology`/`haem-21` is written for
bone marrow failure and pancytopenia and mentions it four times; `malignant`/`mal-5` mentions it
three times as what marrow invasion by leukaemia does. File to `malignant` when the vignette is a
leukaemia and the pancytopenia is its consequence. File to `haematology` when the question is about
marrow failure as its own diagnosis — aplastic anaemia, Fanconi, red cell aplasia. Measured on
pp.1247-1248.

**5. Down syndrome here is `malignant`, not `genetics`.** `mal-11` is a dedicated section on Down
syndrome and AML, including that it is treated *less* hard. A question pairing Down syndrome with a
leukaemia risk is `malignant`; only a question about the karyotype or the syndrome itself goes to
`genetics`. Measured on pp.1261-1262.

**6. The oncological emergencies are `malignant`,** `mal-13` — tumour lysis, SVC obstruction, febrile
neutropenia. They do not go to `emergencies`, which is a resuscitation and shock chapter and teaches
none of them. This is the same shape as section 8's ruling that status epilepticus is `neurological`
rather than `emergencies`.

**7. ⚠️ TWO GAPS, MEASURED — answered and tagged, never declared.**

- **Retinoblastoma is absent from every theory chapter in the module.** Zero hits across all of
  `theory.peds.js`. It appears on **eight** section-11 pages: 1235-1238, 1254, 1265-1266 and in the
  section's contents. File it `malignant-solid`, answer it in full from general paediatric oncology,
  and tag the outside content `not taken from the course material` as a short tag on the claim — the
  shape to copy is `entep-throat-103`. Do not write a dead-end sentence saying the material does not

- **Hodgkin is absent from every theory chapter,** and lymphoma appears on pp.1212, 1224, 1258, 1262

Both were found by measurement rather than by an agent hitting them mid-draft, which is the point of
writing this file first.
