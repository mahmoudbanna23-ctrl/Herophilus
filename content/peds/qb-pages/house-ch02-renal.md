**1.** *(book p.15)* Emily is a 2-year-old girl. She presents to the Emergency Department with a

**8.** *(book p.17)* Finlay, a 5-year-old boy, recently had a birthday party at his local farm. Since
then he has had 3 days of diarrhoea. The loose stool had some blood in it. His mother is concerned
as he is still not himself. He appears to be very pale and has not passed urine for 12 hours. He has
no other medical problems and is not normally on any medication. On examination you note that he has
pale conjunctivae. His blood pressure is 120/70 mmHg. You decide to take some blood tests and get
the following results:
- haemoglobin: 76 g/L; white blood cells: 14.2 × 10⁹/L; platelet count: 50 × 10⁹/L
- creatinine: 200 µmol/L (normal: 20–80 µmol/L)
- prothrombin time: 13 seconds (control: 12–15 s)
- activated partial thromboplastin time: 34 seconds (control: 25–35 s)

What is the most likely diagnosis? *Select one answer only.*

D. Immune thrombocytopenic purpura · E. Post-streptococcal glomerulonephritis — **Answer: B.**
