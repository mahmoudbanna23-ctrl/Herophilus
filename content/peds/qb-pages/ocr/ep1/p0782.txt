Necrotizing Enterocolitis (NEC)
•Incidence inversely related to gestation.
•Etiology: ischemia + bacterial invasion + altered microbiome.
•Risk factors: IUGR, perinatal asphyxia, formula feeding.
•Signs: feed intolerance, bilious vomiting, distension, bloody stool.
•X-ray: intramural/portal venous gas, perforation.
•Tx: stop feeds, broad-spectrum antibiotics, parenteral nutrition, surgery if perforation.
•Complications: strictures, malabsorption, poor neurodevelopment.
