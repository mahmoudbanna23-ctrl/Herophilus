75. Best investigation for anest of catheter at I O cm from nostril in a newborn:
A. Plain X-ray
B. Barium swallow
C. Lipidol swallow
D. Gastrografin meal
If a catheter cannot pass beyond a certain point, a plain X-ray can show
the level of obstruction or coiling and help diagnose esophageal atresia.
Contrast studies are generally not first-line in this situation.
