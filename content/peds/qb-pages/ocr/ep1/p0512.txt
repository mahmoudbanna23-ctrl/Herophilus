49. Which of the following is not consistent with Hirschsprung's disease?
a. No passage of meconium after 24 hours of birth
b. Abdominal distension
c. Bile-stained vomiting
d. Refractory constipation
e. Resolves spontaneously
Hirschsprung disease is a congenital absence of ganglion cells and does not
resolve spontaneously; it requires surgical intervention.
