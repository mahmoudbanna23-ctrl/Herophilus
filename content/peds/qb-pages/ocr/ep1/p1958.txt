Type 111b intestinal atresia: Proximal jejunal atresia with distal small bowel
spiraling around marginal artery ("apple peel" deformity).
Undescended testes surgery: Optimal timing for orchidopexy is
6-12
months to reduce infertility and malignancy risk.
Preterm respiratory distress: RDS in preterm infants presents with
tachypnea, intercostal recession, and ground-glass CXR due to surfactant
deficiency.
Necrotizing enterocolitis: Presents with abdominal distension, bile-stained
aspirates, bloody stools, and pneumatosis intestinalis on X-ray; neonatal
emergency.
Stunting assessment: Use height-for-age charts; low height-for-age re ects
chronic malnutrition.
