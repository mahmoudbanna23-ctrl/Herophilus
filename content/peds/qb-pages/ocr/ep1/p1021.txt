28. Which of the following organisms is NOT a known cause of bacterial
meningitis in children?
a. Group A Streptococcus
b. Streptococcus pneumoniae
c. Haemophilus influenzae type b
d. Neisseria meningitidis
e. Listeria monocytogenes
Group A Streptococcus rarely causes meningitis in children,
while the others are common causative pathogens.
