Resuscitation
•Pediatric arrest usually secondary to hypoxia (not primary cardiac).
•Basic Life Support (BLS):
• Ensure safety, stimulate, shoutfor help.
• Open airway: head tilt—chin lift (neutral in infants), jaw thrust if trauma.
• 5 rescue breaths check for signs of life.
• If no signs compressions + breaths (15:2 ratio; rate 100—120/min).
• Infant: 2 thumbs or 2 fingers; Child: heel ofl or 2 hands; depth = % chest.
6.
Airway Emergencies
•Unconscious child: risk of airway obstruction airway maneuvers, oral/nasal airway.
•Conscious with obstruction (e.g. croup/epiglottitis): allow position of comfort, avoid
agitation.
•Foreign body: back blows, chest thrusts (infant); abdominal thrusts (child).
