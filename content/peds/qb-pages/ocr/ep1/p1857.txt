A firm, poorly mobile, nontender abdominal mass in a 4-year-old is most likely
neuroblastoma, the most common extracranial solid tumor of childhood. It
typically arises from the adrenal gland or sympathetic chain and can cross the
midline.
•Wilms' tumor usually presents as a smooth, unilateral renal mass that does
not cross the midline.
•Constipation is unlikely since bowel movements are normal.
•Lymphoma is more common in older children.
•Hepatoblastoma is rare and usually associated with elevated AFP.
