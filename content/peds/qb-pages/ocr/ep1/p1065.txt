50. A 50-month-old child presents with a I -day history of intermittent fever and
runny nose. She is eating less but continues to drink fluids. On examination, her
temperature is 37.90C and she has a fine macular rash mainly over the trunk. What
is the most likely causative agent?
a. Measles
b. Parvovirus B 19
c. Roseola infantum
d. Rhinovirus
This child has a short history Of mild fever, coryza, and a fine trunk
rash, consistent with a viral exanthem from a common cold virus.
Measles causes high fever and facial rash, parvovirus B19 causes a
"slapped cheek" rash, and roseola usually affects children under 3
years with several days Of high fever before rash.
