20. Which of the following is NOT a feature of marasmus?
a. Looking apathetic
b. No edema
c. Gross muscle wasting
d. Loss of subcutaneous fat
e. Weight for height is >3SD below the median in growth chart
This question is Of lesser importance. Apathy occurs in both marasmus and
kwashiorkor, but it is more common with kwashiorkor. Therefore, we go
with option A, as it is the agreed-upon answer. However, the wording Of the
question is not very good, but it has appeared before in the Old system, so
we included it as a backup.
