34. Which of the following findings is not consistent with Hirschsprung's disease?
a. No passage of meconium after 24 hours of birth
b. Abdominal distension
c. Bile-stained vomiting
d. Refractory constipation
e. Spontaneous resolution
Hirschsprung's disease is a congenital condition caused by absence of ganglion
cells in the distal colon, leading to functional intestinal obstruction. Common
features include failure to pass meconium within 24 hours, abdominal
distension, bile-stained vomiting, and chronic constipation. However,
spontaneous resolution not occur, and surgical intervention is typically
required.
