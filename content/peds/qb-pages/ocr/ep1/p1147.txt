91. A 4-month-old girl presents with two weeks of cough that includes severe
coughing episodes with associated cyanosis, respiratory distress, and post-
tussive emesis. Her mother and father both have upper respiratory infections.
Which of the following is the treatment of choice?
A. Amoxicillin
B. Azithromycin
C. Nebulized albuterol
This is pertussis (whooping cough). Macrolides
D. Reassurance
(azithromycin/clarithromycin) are the treatment of choice and
also used for prophylaxis.
