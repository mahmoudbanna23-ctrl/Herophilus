34. Which tool allows identification of moderate and severe malnutrition?
a. BMI
b. Weight for age
c. Mid-arm circumference
d. Skin fold thickness
Mid-arm circumference is a quick, age-independent screening tool for moderate and severe
malnutrition in children 6 months—5 years.
