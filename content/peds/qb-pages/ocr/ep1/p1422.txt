50. Which of the following vaccines would be contraindicated in a 4-year-old boy
receiving immunosuppressive therapy for autoimmune hepatitis?
A. Tetanus toxoid
B. Hepatitis B vaccine
C. Acellular pertussis vaccine
D. Inactivated polio vaccine
E. BCG
Live vaccines (e.g., BCG, MMR, OPV) are contraindicated in
immunosuppressed patients. Inactivated vaccines remain safe
