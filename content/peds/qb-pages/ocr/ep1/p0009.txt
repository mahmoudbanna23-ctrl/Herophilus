Mid-Parental Centile (MPC)
•Purpose: Assesses a child's growth in relation to family height.
•Methods:
• Parent height comparator: Plot parents' heights, draw a line, and compare with
MPC line.
• Calculation:
• Mid-parental height (Father's height + Mother's height) + 2
• Add +7 cm for boys, subtract —7 cm for girls.
• Locate the centile at age 18 years.
•Interpretation:
• Normal: Within 2 centile spaces of MPC.
• Abnormal: >2 centile spaces away (suggests pathology).
