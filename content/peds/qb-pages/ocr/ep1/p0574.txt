Sudden Infant Death Syndrome (SIDS)
•Definition: unexplained sudden death in a previously well infant.
•Epidemiology: peak 2—4 months, more in boys, incidence now reduced (0.3/1000 UK).
•Risk factors: LBW, young maternal age, unsafe sleep (bed sharing, prone sleeping,
smoking, overheating).
•Prevention (Safe Sleep):
• Back to sleep (supine position).
• Cot in parents' room for 6 months.
• Firm mattress, no pillows/duvets.
• Avoid overheating/smoking.
• Breastfeeding encouraged.
•Post-event management:
• Pediatric assessment, postmortem samples, agency communication.
• Support for parents, bereavement care.
