7. A newborn has hepatosplenomegaly, petechial rash, and fails the hearing
screen. Red reflex and cardiac exam are normal. The mother's antenatal bloods
were normal. What is the most likely congenital infection?
A. Cytomegalovirus (CMV)
B. Rubella
C. Syphilis
D. Toxoplasmosis
E. Varicella zoster
CMV is the most common congenital infection and the leading
cause Of non-hereditary sensorineural hearing loss. Antenatal
screening does not typically include CMV.
