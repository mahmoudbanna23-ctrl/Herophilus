74. A ten-month-old presents with an abdominal mass and anemia. Imaging
reveals an adrenal mass. Which of the following metastatic sites is inconsistent
with Stage I V-S neuroblastoma?
A. Bone marrow
B. Brain
C. Skin
D. Liver
Stage IV-S only involves liver, skin, and limited bone marrow, not the brain.
Brain metastasis indicates more advanced disease beyond stage IV-S.
