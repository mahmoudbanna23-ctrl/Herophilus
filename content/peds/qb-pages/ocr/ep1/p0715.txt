Neonatal Resuscitation
•Most infants: cry, breathe, pink skin-to-skin, delayed cord clamping (1—3 min).
•At-risk infants (fetal distress, preterm <35w, breech, multiple pregnancy, maternal
infection, emergency C-section) prepare resuscitation team.
Initial steps
1.Start clock, keep warm (36.5—37.5 oc).
2. Dry baby (first step), remove wet towel, stimulate.
3.Assess HR, breathing, tone, colour.
4. If not breathing/gasping open airway (neutral position, suction if obstructed).
Airway & Breathing
•Give 5 inflation breaths (2—3 sec, 30 cmH20 term / 25 crnH20 preterm).
•Use air if >32w; 21-30%02 if 28-31w; 02 if <28w.
•Monitor HR (ECG best), Sp02 (right hand).
