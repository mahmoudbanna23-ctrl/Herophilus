68. Which of the following is NOT a typical cause of neonatal intestinal
obstruction?
A. Intussusception
B. Meconium ileus
C. Hirschsprung's disease
D. Incarcerated hernia
Intussusception is rare in neonates; common causes include meconium ileus,
Hirschsprung's disease, and incarcerated hernia.
