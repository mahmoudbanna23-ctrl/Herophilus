Risk Stratification
By Age
•<3 months:
• Non-specific signs.
• High risk of bacterial infection.
• Needs urgent septic screen + IV antibiotics unless clear benign cause.
•Maternal IgG protects early, but susceptibility once levels fall.
Risk Factors
•Family illness, community outbreaks.
•Lack of immunization.
•Recent travel (malaria, typhoid, hepatitis).
•Animal exposure (brucellosis, Q fever, E. coli 0157).
•Immunodeficiency (secondary: prematurity, nephrotic syndrome, splenectomy, HIV;
rarely primary: SCID).
