57. A neonate in the NICU was noticed to have frothy secretions around his
mouth. The neonatologist tried to insert a nasogastric tube but he failed. Which
of the following investigations is essential before taking this neonate to the
operatmg room?
A. Renal ultrasound (US)
B. ECG
C. Echocardiogram (ECHO)
D. spinal US
Cardiac anomalies are commonly associated with tracheoesophageal fistula, so
ECHO is essential before surgery.
