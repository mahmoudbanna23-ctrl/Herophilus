65. During a screening examination of a 3200 g boy, an absence of anal
opening was noted. How can you reliably determine whether this imperforate
anus case is high or low?
A. Administration of water-soluble contrast by nasogastric tube with delayed
images
B. Ultrasound of the perineum
C. Magnetic resonance imaging scan of the pelvis
D. Wait 24 hours and observe the perineum for meconium
E. Plain abdominal films with a radiopaque marker on the perineum
immediatel after birth
After 24 hours, a plain abdominal X-ray with perineal marker helps define the
level of gas. This determines if the malformation is high or low.
