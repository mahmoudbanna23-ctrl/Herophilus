Immunization
•Impact: prevents millions of deaths; eradicated smallpox; near eradication of polio &
neonatal tetanus.
•Herd immunity: required coverage depends on Ro (measles needs 95%, rubella 80%).
Types of vaccines:
•Live attenuated:
MMR, BCG, rotavirus, opv.
•Inactivated: IPV, influenza.
•Subunit: diphtheria, pneumococcal, HPV, HBV.
Egyptian child immunization schedule (main points):
•At birth: Hep B, BCG, opu
•2, 4, 6 months: OPV, Pentavalent (DPT, Hep B, Hib), IPV
•9 months: OPV+ Vit A.
•12 months: OPV + MMR.
•18 months: opv, DTP, MMR + VitA.
•Boosters as indicated.
