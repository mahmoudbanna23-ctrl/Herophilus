5. A 3-month-old boy has a maculopapular rash that appeared on the face then
spread all over the body. It has been preceded by very high fever for 3 days.
Today he looks well active, afebrile with this rash. The most likely causative
agent is:
a. Rubella virus
b. Human herpes virus type I
c. Human herpes virus type 6
d. Epstein—Barr virus
e. Measles
High fever for 3 days then maculopapular rash after
defervescence = roseola infantum.
