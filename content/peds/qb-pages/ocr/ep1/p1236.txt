39. A I O-month-old infant has fallen from the 50th to the 5th percentile for
weight over the past 4 months. The family recently moved, and the mother has
been stressed. The infant is breastfeeding and eating small amounts of solids.
What is the most likely cause of the growth faltering?
A. Inadequate caloric intake
B. Thyroid dysfunction
C. Chronic infection
D. Gastroesophageal reflux
E. Metabolic disorder
Growth faltering with recent stress, breastfeeding with little
solids, and normal development point to inadequate nutrition
rather than organic disease.
