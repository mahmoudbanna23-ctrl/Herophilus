Recurrent abdominal pain in children: Most cases are functional rather than
due to organic disease.
Macrocephaly:
Chronic subdural hematoma can cause increased head size;
other listed conditions usually don't.
Constitutional growth delay: Delayed bone age with eventual height
matching mid-parental height indicates normal variant of short stature.
Kwashiorkor: Edema with low weight in a child on a maize-based diet
indicates protein deficiency.
Hypoglycemia risk in neonate: Low birth weight infant of preeclamptic
mother is at risk due to limited glycogen stores.
Respiratory distress in preterm infant: Surfactant deficiency causes
respiratory distress syndrome in infants <34 weeks.
