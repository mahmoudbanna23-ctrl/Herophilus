61. The appropriate repair of duodenal obstruction due to annular pancreas is:
A. Division of the pancreatic ring
B. Resection of the pancreatic ring
C. Duodenoduodenostomy
D. Subtotal pancreatectomy
Annular pancreas causes duodenal obstruction due to a pancreatic ring
encircling the duodenum. Resection or division risks pancreatic damage, so
bypass with is the safest and most effective option.
