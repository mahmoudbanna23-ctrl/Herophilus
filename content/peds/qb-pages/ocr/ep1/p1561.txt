39. You perform a routine newborn examination on a baby who is 72 hours old.
Which one of the following features requires further immediate assessment?
A. Acrocyanosis (cyanosis of the hands and feet)
B. A heart murmur
C. White vaginal discharge
D. Breast enlargement
E. Subconjunctival hemorrhages
(acrocyanosis, breast enlargement, vaginal discharge, subconjunctival hemorrhage)
are benign. A murmur needs urgent evaluation.
