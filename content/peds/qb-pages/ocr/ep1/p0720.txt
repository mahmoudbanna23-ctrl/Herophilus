Size at Birth
Small-for-Gestational-Age (SGA)
•Birthweight <10th centile (sometimes <2nd).
•Causes: constitutional (normal small) or IUGR.
•Risks: hypothermia, hypoglycemia, polycythemia, perinatal asphyxia, IUFD.
Types of IUGR
•Asymmetrical (late pregnancy, uteroplacental dysfunction, maternal
smoking/preeclampsia):
• Brain spared, abdomen U.
• Catch-up growth possible later metabolic syndrome risk (DOHaD hypothesis).
•Symmetrical (early pregnancy insult):
• Head & body equally U.
• Causes: congenital infection, genetic syndromes, drugs, malnutrition.
Often remain small.
