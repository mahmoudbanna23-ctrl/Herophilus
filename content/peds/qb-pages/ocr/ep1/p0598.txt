I l. Jenny, a 3-year-old girl, suddenly develops facial swelling, urticaria, and noisy
breathing at a village fete. She is distressed and has widespread wheeze. What is
the first-line medication?
A. Intramuscular adrenaline
B. Oral corticosteroid
C. Intravenous hydrocortisone
D. Oral antihistamine
E. Intramuscular antihistamine
Facial swelling, urticaria, respiratory compromise (wheeze)
suggest anaphylaxis. Adrenaline 1M is the first-line treatment.
