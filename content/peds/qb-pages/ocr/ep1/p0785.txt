Respiratory distress in term infants:
Signs
• Tachypnoea (>50/min)
•T Work of breathing (recession, nasal flaring)
•Expiratory grunting
•Cyanosis (if severe)
Causes
Common
•Transient tachypnoea of the newborn (TTN)
•Meconium aspiration
•Persistent pulmonary hypertension of the newborn (PPHN)
Less common
•Pneumonia
•Pneumothorax
•RDS (rare in term infants)
Non-pulmonary
•Diaphragmatic hernia, tracheo-oesophageal fistula, pulmonary hypoplasia, milk aspiration, choanal
atresia, pulmonary haemorrhage
•Congenital heart disease, H/E, severe anaemia, metabolic acidosis, sepsis
