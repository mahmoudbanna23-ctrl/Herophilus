33. A male infant was delivered at 40 weeks of gestation. The baby was apneic,
limp, pale, and covered with meconium-stained amniotic fluid.
What is the most common complication?
A. Pneumothorax
B. Sepsis
C. Bacterial pneumonia
D. Hyperglycemia
E. Hypernatremia
Meconium aspiration can lead to air trapping and
pneumothorax as the most common complication.
