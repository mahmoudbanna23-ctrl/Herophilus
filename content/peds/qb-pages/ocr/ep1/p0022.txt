Precocious Puberty (pp)
Definition: Onset of secondary sexual characteristics before age 8 in girls, 9 in boys.
Types:
1. Gonadotrophin-dependent (central, true ppl
Mechanism: Premature activation of hypothalamic—pituitary—gonadal axis.
Hormones: LH, FSH.
Development: Consonant (normal sequence).
Causes: Idiopathic/familial, CNS abnormalities (tumors, hydrocephalus, trauma, infection,
post-irradiation), hypothyroidism.
2. Gonadotrophin-independent (peripheral, pseudo-PP)
Mechanism: Excess sex steroids outside pituitary control.
Hormones: Low/normal LH & FSH.
Development: Dissonant (abnormal sequence).
Causes:
Adrenal: CAH, adrenal tumors.
Gonadal: ovarian tumor (granulosa), testicular tumor (Leydig).
Exogenous sex steroids
