72. What is the most important factor affecting growth during the "infantile phase"
in humans?
A. Maternal nutrition
B. Infant nutrition
C. Size at birth
D. Vitamins
E. Parental size
Growth in the infantile phase (first 2 years) is primarily dependent on
nutrition. Later phases are influenced by hormones and genetics.
