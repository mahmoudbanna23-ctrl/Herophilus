Hypoglycemia in Newborn
•Risk groups: LGA (maternal DM), SGA, preterm, polycythemia, hypothermia, sick
infants.
•Symptoms: jitteriness, irritability, lethargy, seizures, apnea.
•Prevention: early/frequent feeds.
•Management (BAPM 2017):
• 2—2.6 mmol/L feed, monitor, ± dextrose gel.
• <2 mmol/L or symptomatic IV dextrose.
• Persistent investigate (hypoglycemia screen).
