2. At the 20-week routine antenatal scan, the sonographer notes normal
abdominal and head circumferences but an abnormally small amount of amniotic
fluid (oligohydramnios). What is the most likely cause?
A. Duodenal atresia
B. Gastroschisis
C. Maternal diabetes
D. Poorly functioning fetal kidneys
E. Severe intrauterine growth restriction
Amniotic fluid is primarily produced by fetal kidneys from the
second trimester onward. Renal dysfunction leads to reduced
urine output and hence low amniotic fluid.
