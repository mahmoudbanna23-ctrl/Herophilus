20. A 7-year-old boy presents with fever, severe headache, and multiple episodes
of vomiting. He is drowsy, febrile (380C), with HR 135/min and BP 90/60 mmHg.
On examination, extension of the knee is limited with hip flexion (positive
Kernig's sign). What is the next most important step?
a. Complete blood picture
b. Blood culture for aerobic and anaerobic organisms
c. Brain CT
d. CSF examination and culture
e. ESR and C-reactive protein
The child presents with signs of meningeal irritation and possible
meningitis. Lumbar puncture for CSF analysis is the most urgent diagnostic
step, unless there are signs of raised intracranial pressure or focal
neurological deficits.
