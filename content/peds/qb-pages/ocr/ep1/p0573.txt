BRUE (Brief Resolved unexplained Event)
Definition
•A sudden, brief min), now resolved episode in an infant with any of:
Color change: cyanosis or pallor.
Breathing change: apnea or irregular breathing.
Tone change: hypertonia or hypotonia.
Responsiveness change: altered interaction.
Low-Risk Criteria
•Age > 60 days.
•Born 2 32 weeks gestation.
•First event.
•Duration < 1 min.
•No CPR needed by healthcare professional.
•No concerning features on history/exam.
Management
•LOW risk
Short observation.
Parent reassurance + basic life support (BLS) training.
•High risk
Hospital admission.
• Investigations: ECG (for arrhythmia), pertussis swab, monitoring, ± other tests guided by findin
