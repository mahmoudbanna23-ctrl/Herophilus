HIV in Children
Diagnosis
•>18 months: HIV antibody test (ELISA, Western blot).
•<18 months: HIV DNA PCR / viral load (maternal antibodies cross placenta, so antibody
tests not reliable).
Management
•Antiretroviral therapy (ART): start in all confirmed cases regardless of CD4 count.
•PCP prophylaxis: cotrimoxazole.
•Vaccination: follow routine schedule but avoid live vaccines (e.g., BCG, OPV, MMR,
varicella) in immunocompromised.
•Supportive care: nutrition, psychosocial and family-centered multidisciplinary care.
