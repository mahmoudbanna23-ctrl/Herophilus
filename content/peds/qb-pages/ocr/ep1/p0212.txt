Nutrition in Childhood
•Children are vulnerable high requirements, low reserves, rapid growth & brain development.
•Infancy:
Energy needs high (100—115 kcal/kg/day).
Growth: weight x2 by 5 months, x3 by 1 year.
• Brain accounts for % of basal metabolism at birth.
Causes of vulnerability:
1.High nutritional requirements.
2.Low nutritional stores (esp. preterm, IUGR).
3.Rapid brain growth (last trimester 2 yrs).
4.111ness/surgery poor intake + demands.
Long-term impact of early malnutrition:
•Stunted growth in populations.
•Adult disease risk (CHD, T2DM, HTN).
Theories (Developmental Origins of Health & Disease):
•Fuel-mediated in utero hypothesis: fetal overnutrition later obesity, diabetes.
•Accelerated postnatal growth hypothesis: rapid infant weight gain later metabolic risk.
•Mismatch hypothesis: fetal undernutrition + postnatal overnutrition visceral adiposity, insulin
resistance.
