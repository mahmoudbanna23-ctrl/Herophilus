'Determinants: genetics, maternal nutrition, fetal/placental health.
'Accelerated by maternal/fetal glucose, IGF-I.
• Restricted by maternal disease, smoking, uteroplacental insufficiency, infection.

• Rapid but decelerating growth.

• Failure = faltering growth.

'Steady growth.
'Driven by GH IGF-I at growth plates, plus thyroid hormone, vitamin D,
steroids.
