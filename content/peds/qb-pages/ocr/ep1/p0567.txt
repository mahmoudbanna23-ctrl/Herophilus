Respiratory Failure
•Definition: inadequate gas exchange (hypoxemia ± hypercarbia).
•Signs of distress (moderate): tachycardia, RR >50, nasal flaring, grunting, retractions, poor feeding.
•Severe: cyanosis, exhaustion, consciousness, Sp02 <92% despite 02, rising pC02.
•Management:
• 02 ifSp02
• Non-invasive support: high-flow nasal cannula, CPAP, BiPAP.
• Intubation & ventilation if severe or airway protection lost.
Circulatory Failure — Shock
8.
•Types: hypovolemic, distributive (sepsis, anaphylaxis), cardiogenic, neurogenic.
•Early (compensated): tachycardia, tachypnea, prolonged capillary refill, mottled/cold skin, urine.
•Late (decompensated): hypotension, bradycardia, acidosis, confusion, absent urine, blue peripheries.
•Fluid resuscitation:
• 10 ml/kg isotonic crystalloid bolus (Hartmann's, 0.9% NaCl, Plasma-Lyte).
• Reassess after each bolus.
• Trauma: 5—10 ml/kg blood products.
• 40 ml/kgfluid likely need ventilation & ICIJ.
