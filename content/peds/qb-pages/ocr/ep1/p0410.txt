Chronic Non-specific Diarrhea (Toddler's Diarrhea)
•Common in preschoolers, stools vary in consistency, contain undigested food.
•Child otherwise thriving.
•Often due to gut dysmotility, excessive fruit juice, or mild food intolerance.
•Improves with age.
Constipation
•Common between ages 2—4 years.
•Defined by 22 of: <3 stools/week, hard/large stools, "rabbit droppings," overflow
soiling.
•Causes: dietary change, stool withholding, fissures, psychosocial factors.
•Red flags: delayed meconium, abdominal distension with vomiting, abnormal perianal
anatomy, growth failure.
•Management:
• First-line: Macrogol (PEG), stool softeners ± stimulant laxatives.
• Maintenance: long-term laxatives, toilet training, behavioral support.
• Severe cases: NG macrogol or manual evacuation (specialist only).
