67. In retro-sternal diaphragmatic hernia of Morgagni, the defect is situated:
a. Postero-laterally in the diaphragm at the area of the embryonic
pleuroperitoneal canal
b. Anteriorly through the opening for the superior epigastric artery
c. Through esophageal hiatus at gastroesophageal junction
d. In the central tendon
This is a rare anterior diaphragmatic defect.
