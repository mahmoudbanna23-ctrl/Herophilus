Hygiene Hypothesis
•Fewer infections/microbial exposure in modern living risk of allergy/autoimmunity.
•Lower risk in large families and farm children.
Clinical Evaluation
•History: cornerstone (timing, triggers, reproducibility).
•Exam: allergic facies, nasal obstruction, eczema, conjunctivitis, wheeze.
•Growth: check for faltering (food allergy, steroid use).
Investigations
1.Skin prick test (SPT): 24 mm wheal positive; negative allergy unlikely.
2.Specific lgE: raised levels suggest atopy; correlate with history.
3.Spirometry: for asthma.
4.Allergen challenge: gold standard (esp. food); must be in hospital with resuscitation.
