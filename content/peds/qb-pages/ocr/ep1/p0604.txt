14. Kelsey, a 2-year-old girl, is found unconscious in the garden. In the
resuscitation room, she is gasping and moaning. What is the next most
appropriate step?
A. Check blood glucose
B. Check pupils
C. Airway opening manoeuvres
D. High-flow oxygen therapy
E. Stan chest compressions using hands-encircling method
An unconscious child requires immediate airway management.
Gasping suggests inadequate breathing; airway positioning
should be done first.
