58. What is appropriate regarding the role of vitamins in children's nutrition?
A. Vitamin C deficiency can lead to petechiae and mucosal bleeding
B. Vitamin K's main function is in the synthesis of RBCs
C. Vitamin E deficiency leads to decreased clot generation
D. Vitamin B12 deficiency is common in infants
E. Folic acid has no role in blood production
Vitamin C deficiency (scur") leads to defective collagen synthesis,
bruising, bleeding gums, and petechiae. Vitamin K is for coagulation, not
RBC synthesis.
