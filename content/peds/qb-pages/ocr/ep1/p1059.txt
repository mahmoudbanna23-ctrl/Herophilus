47. Which of the following are possible complications of impetigo?
a. Cellulitis
b. Guttate psoriasis
c. Sepsis
d. Staphylococcal scalded skin syndrome
e. All of the above
Impetigo, a superficial skin infection, can lead to complications
like cellulitis, sepsis, and post-infectious issues like guttate
psoriasis or staphylococcal scalded skin syndrome.
