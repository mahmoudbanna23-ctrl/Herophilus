55. A 3-month-old baby is seen in clinic with a severe nappy rash. The rash covers
the perineal area including the skin folds and features satellite lesions. There is also
a white coating on the baby's tongue. The baby is well and thriving. Which of the
following is the most appropriate treatment?
A. Topical flucloxacillin preparation
B. I % hydrocortisone ointment
C.Barrier cream, e.g Zinc and castor oil cream
D. Antifungal preparation, e.g. miconazole
E. Emollient, e.g. white soft paraffin
Candida infection presents with involvement of skin folds, satellite lesions,
and oral thrush. Antifungal cream is the treatment.
