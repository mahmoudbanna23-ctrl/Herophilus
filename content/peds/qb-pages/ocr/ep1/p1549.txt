33. A preterm infant, intubated at birth, develops worsening of existing
respiratory distress and shifting of mediastinum to one side. What is the most
likely diagnosis?
A. Congenital heart disease
B. Apnea of prematurity
C. Herpes simplex infection
D. Pneumothorax
Mediastinal shift in a ventilated preterm infant indicates
pneumothorax.
