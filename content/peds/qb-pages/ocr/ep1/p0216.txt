Formula Feeding
•Needed if not breastfeeding. Usually cow's milk—based, modified to resemble human milk.
•May include prebiotics, probiotics, PUFA, nucleotides (benefit uncertain).
•Never fully replicates breast milk (dynamic composition).
•WHO prohibits advertising breast milk substitutes.
•Sterilization & accurate preparation essential.
•Cow's milk: not suitable as main drink <12 months (low iron risk of anemia).
• From 1 yr: whole milk acceptable.
• From 2 yrs: semi-skimmed if growth adequate.
• From 5 yrs: skimmed can be used.
Specialized Formulas
•Preterm formula: higher energy, Na, Ca, phosphate.
•Extensively hydrolyzed/amino acid formula: cow's milk protein allergy.
•MCTformula: fat malabsorption.
•Soya-based formula: largely avoided (<6 months).
•Anti-reflux (thickened) formula: for uncomplicated GERD.
