Sepsis
•Pathophysiology: bacteria in blood inflammatory cytokines endothelial activation dysregulated
response septic shock, MODS.
•Common organisms (UK):
Gram +: S. aureus, non-pyogenic streptococci, S. pneumoniae.
Gram —: N. meningitidis, E. coli.
•Neonatal sepsis:
Early-onset: GBS, E. coli.
Late-onset: CoNS.
•Clinical features: fever/hypothermia, tachycardia, tachypnea, poor feeding, irritability, lethargy, purpura,
shock, MODS.
•High risk: immunodeficient children, catheters, chronic disease.
•Management:
Early recognition + antibiotics (ceftriaxone beyond neonatal period).
Fluid resuscitation (beware capillary leak).
Circulatory' support (inotropes).
PICU if shock/MODS.
•Complications: DIC bleeding, non-b/anching rash FFP, cryoprecipitate, platelets.
