69. The following can be done for a massive gastroschisis EXCEPT:
a. Abdominal silo
b. Delayed primary closure
c. Topical eschar-forming agents
d. Gastric decompression and parenteral nutrition should be continued
These are not used. Silo or delayed closure are standard
approaches.
