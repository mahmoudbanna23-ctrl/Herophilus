15. Which of the following not a finding in Kwashiorkor?
a. hyperglycemia
b. muscle wasting
c. distributed muscle: fat ratio
d. dermatosis
e. edema
Kwashiorkor is characterized by edema, dermatosis, and muscle wasting
with disproportionate fat retention, but hyperglycemia is not typical.
