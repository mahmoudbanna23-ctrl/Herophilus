5. A 4-year-old girl with a family history of atopy visits her GP. Her brother has a
nut allergy and hay fever. Her mother wants skin-prick testing before introducing
nuts. Which of the following statements about skin-prick testing is true?
A. The severity of a reaction can be predicted by the size of the skin-prick
reaction
B. It detects both IgE- and non-lgE-mediated allergies
C. It is more accurate than blinded food challenge
D. Positive (histamine) and negative (saline) controls should be used to
ensure test validity
E. It tests for specific serum IgE levels
Skin-prick testing requires control samples to confirm that the test was performed
and interpreted correctly. It only detects lgE-mediated allergy and cannot predict
severity.
