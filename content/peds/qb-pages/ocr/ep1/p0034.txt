3. Which is a characteristic of constitutional growth delay?
A. Adequate bone age
B. More common in girls
C. Anal height is affected
D. A delayed onset of puberty
E. Presentation is in infancy
Constitutional growth delay presents with normal health, slowed growth,
delayed bone age, and late puberty. It is more common in boys and improves
with age.
