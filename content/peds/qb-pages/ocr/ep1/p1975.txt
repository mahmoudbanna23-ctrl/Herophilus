Signs
First sign of normal puberty in girls: Palpable breast bud.
• Last sign of puberty in girls: Menarche.
Pathognomonic sign of measles: White spots on the buccal mucosa (Koplik spots).
•silk glove sign: Suggestive of patent processus vaginalis (inguinal hernia).
Blue-dot sign: Torsion of testicular appendage.
Prehn sign: Relief of pain on elevation of testis (testicular torsion vs epididymitis); cremasteric
reflex present.
Barium meal:
•string sign narrow, elongated canal (pyloric stenosis).
• Beak sign pointed lumen.
Shoulder sign
impression on antrum.
Double-track sign indicates partial obstruction.
•corkscrew sign: Indicates volvulus.
Dance sign: Emptiness in the right iliac fossa (intussusception).
Ultrasound (preferred for intussusception): Target/doughnut sign.
Contrast enema:
•cobra head sign intussusception.
• Keyhole sign dilated bladder + prostatic urethra (posterior urethral valves).
1975
