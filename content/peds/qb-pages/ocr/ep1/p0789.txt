Prolonged Neonatal Jaundice (>2 weeks, >3 weeks if preterm)
•Important to evaluate for biliary atresia (delayed diagnosis worsens outcome).
•Step 1: Determine type of hyperbilirubinemia (unconjugated vs conjugated).
Unconjugated Hyperbilirubinemia
Causes:
•Breast milk jaundice most common (up to 15% of healthy breastfed infants), fades by
12 weeks.
•Infection (esp. urinary tract).
•Congenital hypothyroidism prolonged jaundice before other signs (coarse facies, dry
skin, constipation, hypotonia). Identified on Guthrie screen.
