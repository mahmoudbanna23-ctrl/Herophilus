Anaphylaxis
•Definition: severe, life-threatening systemic allergic reaction.
•Triggers in children: mostly food (milk, peanuts, tree nuts). Others: insect stings, meds.
•Risk factors: asthma, adolescence.
•Clinical features (ABCDE):
• Airway: swelling, hoarseness, stridor.
• Breathing: wheeze, tachypnea, Sp02 <92%.
• Circulation: weak pulses, hypotension, cold extremities.
• Disability: faint, drowsy, coma.
• Skin: urticaria, angioedema.
•Management:
• Call for help, lie flat, raise legs.
• 1M adrenaline every 5 min until improvement.
• Oxygen, 'V fluids, antihistamines (chlorphenamine), hydrocortisone.
• If refractory IV adrenaline infusion (ICU only).
•Prevention: allergy workup, avoidance, action plan, training.
