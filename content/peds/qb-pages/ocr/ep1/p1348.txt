13. The parents of a I -week-old breastfed infant report that the physiologic
jaundice has never resolved completely. The stool is grayish-white while the
baby's urine stains the diaper brown.
What is the MOST likely diagnosis?
A. Neonatal sepsis
B. Biliary atresia
C. G6PD deficiency
D. Congenital hypothyroidism
E. Breast milk jaundice
Persistent jaundice with acholic stools and dark urine indicates cholestasis,
most commonly due to biliary atresia.
