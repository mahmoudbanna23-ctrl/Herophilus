I O. A 15-year-old boy develops repetitive attacks of myoclonic jerks not
associated with loss of consciousness. He has been experienced poor academic
experience, irritability, reduced attention span. He has a history of measles
infection at the age of 5 years.
What is the MOST likely diagnosis?
a. Brain tumor
b. Encephalitis
c. Myoclonic seizure
Progressive myoclonic jerks, cognitive decline years after
measles -5 subacute sclerosing panencephalitis.
d. Progressive rubella panencephalitis
e. Sub-acute sclerosing panencephalitis (SSPE)
