32. At what age can semi-skimmed cow's milk be introduced as the main drink
for a child?
a. 2 years
b. 6 months
c. 12 months
d. 18 months
Semi-skimmed cow's milk is not recommended before 2 years because toddlers
require higher fat content for brain development. Whole milk is preferred until
then.
