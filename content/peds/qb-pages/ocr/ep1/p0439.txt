13. Max, aged 9 years, has been brought to the Emergency Department because
he is crying and saying his tummy hurts. He has had a 2-day history of fever and
coryza. He has been drinking orange juice but has only eaten some jelly and
yogurt. He has not opened his bowels. His temperature is 38.20C. His throat is red
and he has tender cervical lymph nodes. He is not dehydrated. He has mild
generalized tenderness of the abdomen, with no guarding. Which of the following
is the most likely diagnosis in this patient?
A. Constipation
B. Pneumonia
C. Appendicitis
Mesenteric adenitis commonly follows a viral illness and
mimics appendicitis. The presence of fever, coryza, red throat,
and cervical lymphadenopathy suggests a viral etiology causing
inflamed mesenteric lymph nodes.
D. Mesenteric adenitis
E. Functional abdominal pain
