16. Which is most often fractured bone in difficult deliveries?
a. clavicle
b. humerus
c. skull
d. femur
e. tibia
The clavicle is the most commonly fractured bone during difficult deliveries,
especially in shoulder dystocia or large infants. It usually heals well without
intervention.
