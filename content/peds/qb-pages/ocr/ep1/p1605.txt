62. Which of the following is the best intervention that effectively prevents the
occurence of sudden infant death syndrome (SIDS)?
A. Feet to foot of cot
B. Keeping baby in parent's room until 6 months of age
C. Keeping room cool to prevent overheating
D. Parents not smoking in the same room as infant
E. Supine sleeping
The best intervention that effectively prevents the occurence
of sudden infant death syndrome (SIDS) Supine sleeping.
