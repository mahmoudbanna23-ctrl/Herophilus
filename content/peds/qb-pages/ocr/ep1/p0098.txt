35. Which of the following is the most important cause of failure to establish
breathing and the requirement of resuscitation at birth?
A. Congenital lung anomalies
B. Birth trauma
C. Birth asphyxia
D. Retained lung fluid
Birth asphyxia is the leading cause Of neonatal respiratory failure and
need for resuscitation. Congenital anomalies or retained fluid are less
common causes.
