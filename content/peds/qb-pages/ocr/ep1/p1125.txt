80. A mother with no prior chickenpox exposure is breastfeeding a neonate and
her toddler has chickenpox. Best step?
A. Check maternal antibodies; if negative, give V ZIG
B. Observe neonate
C. Discharge and isolate
D. No precautions
E. Treat neonate with aciclovir
Neonates are at risk if the mother lacks immunity. If maternal antibodies are
absent, VZIG is indicated.
