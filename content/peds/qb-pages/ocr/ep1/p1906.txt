lack of sexual development. He had a normal growth pattern, although he was
always shorter than children of his age. Examination revealed height at the 5th
percentile, upper segment to lower segment ratio is 0.98. A small number of fine
axillary and pubic hairs are present.
Which of the following is the most appropriate action?
a. Measure pituitary gonadotropin
b. Measure serum testosterone
c. Obtain a CT scan of the pituitary gland
d. Reassure the parents
This is constitutional delay of growth and puberty; reassurance
