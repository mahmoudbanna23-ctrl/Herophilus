7. A 6-year-old boy presents with a fever of 390C, sore throat, and a red rash
with a sandpaper texture. He also has red cheeks and a "strawberry" tongue.
What is the most appropriate treatment?
A. Amoxicillin
B. Acetaminophen
C. Antihistamines
D. Acyclovir
E. Penicillin
The child's presentation is scarlet fever (sandpaper rash, strawberry
tongue), which requires penicillin to eradicate Group A streptococcus.
Supportive therapy alone is insufficient.
