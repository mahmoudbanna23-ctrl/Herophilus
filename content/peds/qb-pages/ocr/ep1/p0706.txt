Pre-pregnancy Care
•Habits: stop smoking, alcohol, and drugs reduce miscarriage, growth restriction,
stillbirth, SIDS.
•Folic acid:
• Routine: 400 mcg/day.
• High risk: 5 mg/day (obesity, diabetes, anticonvulsants, family history of NTD).
•Medical history: review chronic conditions (diabetes, epilepsy, hypertension).
•Avoid teratogenic drugs (valproate, warfarin, retinoids, isotretinoin, thalidomide).
•Obesity: miscarriage, GDM, pre-eclampsia, macrosomia, stillbirth.
•Food precautions: avoid unpasteurized cheese, liver (vitamin A excess),
raw/undercooked meat (toxoplasmosis), swordfish/tuna (mercury).
•Genetic counseling for couples at risk (family history, consanguinity, carriers of
recessive disorders).
