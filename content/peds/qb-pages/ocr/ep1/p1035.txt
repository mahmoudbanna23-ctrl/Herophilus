35. An 8-month-old child presents with episodes of vomiting and screaming for 12
hours. On examination, a sausage-shaped mass is palpable in the right upper
quadrant. Which of the following would be most useful for diagnosis and
treatment?
a. Passage of a nasogastric tube
b. Stool examination for ova and parasites
c. Blood culture
d. Complete blood count
e. Rectal air insufflation or barium enema
These findings suggest intussusception. A contrast enema (air
or barium) confirms the diagnosis and can be therapeutic by
reducing the intussusception
