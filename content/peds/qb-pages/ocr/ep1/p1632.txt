75. Which of the following is NOT TRUE regarding intussusception?
A. More common in boys
B. Can occur secondary to Meckel's diverticulum
C. Bilious vomiting is not always present
D. Bleeding per rectum is the most common presenting symptom
The hallmark is intermittent pain and a mass, not
bleeding, which appears later. It is more common in boys and can occur
secondary to Meckel's diverticulum.

75. Which ofthe following is NOT TRUE regarding intussusception?
A. More common in boys
B. Can occur secondary to Meckel's diverticulum
C. Bilious vomiting is not always present
D. Bleeding per rectum is the most common presenting symptom
The hallmark is intermittent abdominal pain and a sausage-shaped mass, not
bleeding, which appears later. It is more common in boys and can occur
secondary to Meckel's diverticulum.
