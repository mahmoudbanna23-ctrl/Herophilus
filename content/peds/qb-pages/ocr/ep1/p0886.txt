45. Which of the following is a common complication in infants of diabetic
mothers?
a. Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy
b. Microsomia
c. Hyperglycemia
d. Hypocalcemia
Infants of diabetic mothers may develop hypertrophic cardiomyopathy
due to fetal hyperinsulinism causing septal hypertrophy.
