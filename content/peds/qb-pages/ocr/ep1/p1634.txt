76. Which of the following is TRUE regarding duodenal atresia?
A. Most common form of bowel atresia is thrush
B. Presents after birth with non-bilious vomiting
C. Prenatal diagnosis is common
D. Gastrojejunostomy is the procedure of choice
Duodenal atresia can Often be seen on prenatal ultrasound, typically as a
"double-bubble" sign. Vomiting is usually bilious, not non-bilious, and
gastrojejunostomy is not the procedure Of choice.
