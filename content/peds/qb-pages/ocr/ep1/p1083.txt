59. Harry, 21 months old, has a 3-day history of cough, fever, conjunctivitis,
and a widespread rash.
What is the most likely causative organism?
A. Influenza virus
B. Rhinovirus
C. Measles virus
D. Roseola infantum
E. Cytomegalovirus
The classic triad Of cough, coryza, and conjunctivitis followed
by a maculopapular rash points to measles, caused by the
measles virus.
