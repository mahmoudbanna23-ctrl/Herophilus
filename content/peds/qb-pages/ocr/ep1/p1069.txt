52. What typically happens when a patient with infectious mononucleosis is
incorrectly treated with ampicillin?
a. Severe anaphylactic reaction
b. Rapid defervescence
c. Diffuse erythematous rash
d. Excessive nausea and vomiting
e. Severe diarrhea
Ampicillin or amoxicillin given during EBV infection commonly
leads to a widespread erythematous rash, which is not due to
allergy but a drug-virus interaction.
