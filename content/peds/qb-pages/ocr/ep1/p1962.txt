Infectious mononucleosis: EBV causes fever, pharyngitis, lymphadenopathy,
hepatosplenomegaly, and atypical lymphocytes.
Bacterial meningitis: CSF is turbid with neutrophils, high protein, and low
glucose.
German measles (Rubella): Low-grade fever with transient maculopapular
rash and mild cervical/occipital lymphadenopathy.
Annular pancreas repair: Duodenoduodenostomy is preferred for duodenal
obstruction; division or resection risks pancreatic injury.
Commonest choledochal cyst: Type I (fusiform dilation of the common bile
duct) accounts for 80—90% of cases.
Imperforate anus with meconium per urethra: Indicates high anorect
malformation with fistula; initial management is colostomy, then definitive
1962
