57. John, a 2-year-old boy, has a rash on both feet and tender lesions on his
hands. He is otherwise well.
What is the most likely cause?
A. Coxsackie A
B. Flea bites
C. Herpes zoster
D. Scabies
E. Varicella zoster
Coxsackie A virus causes hand, foot, and mouth disease,
characterized by vesicular lesions on the hands, feet, and
sometimes the mouth, often in otherwise well children.
