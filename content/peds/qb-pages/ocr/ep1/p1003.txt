19. An 8-year-old girl develops a ring-like rash after a camping trip in the New
Forest. What is the next best step?
a. Prescribe amoxicillin (if not allergic)
b. Prescribe an antihistamine
c. Prescribe doxycycline
d. Take acute and convalescent Borrelia serology and arrange outpatient follow-
up
e. Take blood for Borrelia serology
Erythema migrans is diagnostic Of Lyme disease. Empiric amoxicillin is indicated
without waiting for serology.
