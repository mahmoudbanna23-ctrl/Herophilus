66. Which of the following is not a feature of hypospadias?
A. Dorsal chordee
B. Hooded foreskin
C. Ventrally placed meatus
D. Proximal meatus
Hypospadias involves ventrally placed meatus, hooded foreskin, and sometimes
proximal meatus. Chordee, if present, is ventral, not dorsal
