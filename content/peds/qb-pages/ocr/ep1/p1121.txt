78. A toddler with severe egg allergy needs MMR vaccine. What is the best
advice?
A. He can safely receive all vaccines
B. Do not give MMR
C. Avoid all live vaccines
D. MMR can be given in hospital; avoid influenza/yellow fever
E. Give MMR with antihistamine
MMR is safe even in children with allergy, though hospital administration is
preferred in severe cases. Influenza and yellow fever vaccines pose more risk.
