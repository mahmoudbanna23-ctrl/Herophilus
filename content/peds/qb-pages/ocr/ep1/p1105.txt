70. A 3-year-old girl has fever, conjunctivitis, rash, lymphadenopathy, and
extremity redness. IVIG and aspirin are started. What test is essential?
A. Full blood count
B. Throat swab
C. ASO titer
D. Echocardiogram
E. CXR
This is Kawasaki disease, which may cause coronary artery
aneurysms. An echocardiogram is crucial to monitor cardiac
involvement.
