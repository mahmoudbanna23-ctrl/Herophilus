23. A I -day-old male newborn is admitted to the pediatric unit due to abdominal
distension and failure to pass meconium since birth. The infant was born at term
via cesarean section, and the pregnancy was unremarkable. The parents report
that the baby has been feeding poorly and seems irritable. Ground glass or soap
bubble appearance in right lower quadrant showed in abdominal X-ray. The most
likely diagnosis is:
A. Hirschsprung's disease
B. Imperforate anus
C. Meconium ileus
D. Intestinal atresia
E. Malrotation
Failure to pass meconium, abdominal distension, and "ground
glass/soap bubble" appearance in the right lower abdomen are
classic for meconium ileus. Strongly associated with cystic
fibrosis.
