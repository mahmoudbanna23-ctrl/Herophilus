very keen to have her tested before introducing her to nuts and requests skin-prick
testing.Which of the following statements describe the role of skin-prick testing in
this case?
A. A positive test confirms the presence of clinical allergy.
B. It is suitable for identifying both IgE and non-lgE-mediated allergy.
C. It tests only for IgE-mediated allergy.
D. It tests for levels of IgE in serum
Skin-prick testing detects lgE-mediated sensitization. A positive test shows
sensitization, but clinical correlation is required for allergy.
