9. A 2-year-old boy presents with a head circumference that has increased from
the 50th percentile to above the 97th percentile over the last six months. His
height and weight remain stable. What is the most appropriate next step?
A. Order a brain MRI
B. Reassure and follow up in 6 months
C. Measure parents' head circumference
D. Measure serum IGF-I levels
Rapidly increasing head circumference crossing percentiles suggests
hydrocephalus or intracranial pathology, requiring imaging. Familial
macrocephaly is less likely here.
