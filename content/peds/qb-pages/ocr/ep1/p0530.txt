58. Maternal polyhydramnios is frequently observed in all the following
conditions except:
A. Esophageal atresia
B. Duodenal atresia
C. Hirschsprung disease
D. Pyloric atresia
polyhydramnios is seen in conditions where swallowing is impaired (esophageal
or duodenal atresia). Hirschsprung usually causes postnatal obstruction, not
polyhydramnios.
