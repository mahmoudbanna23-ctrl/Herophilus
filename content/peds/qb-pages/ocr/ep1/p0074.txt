23. Chelsea, a 7-year-old girl, is brought by her mother to the paediatric clinic.
Her mother is concerned about early puberty. Chelsea has developed breasts and
recently some pubic hair. On examination, she has breast development stage 3
and pubic hair stage 2. She has not started her periods but has had a growth spurt.
A pelvic ultrasound shows multicystic ovaries and an enlargmg uterus. What is
the most likely cause of her early pubertal development?
A. Brain tumor
B. Congenital adrenal hyperplasia
C. Idiopathic precocious puberty
D. Ovarian tumor
E. Turner syndrome
True central precocious puberty is suggested by breast
development, growth spurt, and estrogen-sensitive organ
changes like multicystic ovaries and enlarged uterus. In girls,
this is most commonly idiopathic.
