33. Which of the following is hue regarding capillary refill time (CRT)?
a. It is affected by cold environment
b. It is assessed by pressing on the forehead
c. It is not prolonged in compensated (early) shock
d. It is considered prolonged if > 4 seconds
CRT is influenced by temperature and perfusion status. It is normally
assessed on the sternum or fingertip and considered prolonged if >2—3
seconds, not only >4 seconds.
