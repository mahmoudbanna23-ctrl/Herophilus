Birth Asphyxia
•Normal labour: transient hypoxia during contractions usually well tolerated.
•Severe/prolonged interruption (placental abruption, uterine rupture, cord prolapse)
fetal adaptive mechanisms fail.
• Primary apnoea: gasping, then apnoea; HR maintained.
• Secondary apnoea: irregular gasping apnoea, HR & BPfall.
• Recovery possible only with positive pressure ventilation (PPV).
•Common setting: intermittent asphyxia during prolonged/obstructed labour, IUGR, or
prematurity.
