Retinopathy of Prematurity (ROP)
•In infants <32w or <1500g.
•Vascular proliferation detachment blindness.
•Risk: oxygen therapy (hyperoxia).
•Tx: laser therapy, anti-VEGF.
Bronchopulmonary Dysplasia (BPD)
•02 requirement at 36 weeks PMA.
•Etiology: delayed lung maturation, ventilation trauma, oxygen toxicity, infection.
•Tx: CPAP, oxygen, steroids (short low-dose in severe cases).
•Prognosis: some go home on 02, risk of pulmonary HTN, infection-triggered failure.
