49. The following are contraindications to further pertussis immunization
EXCEPT:
a. Encephalopathy within 7 days
b. Persistent screaming
c. Convulsions within 48—72 hours
d. Shock
e. Fever of 39.50C
Mild to moderate fever is not a contraindication to vaccination.
Serious neurologic events such as encephalopathy or
prolonged convulsions may be contraindications.
