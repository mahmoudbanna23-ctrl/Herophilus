Perinatal Medicine
Definition
•Care of the fetus and infant before, during, and after birth, especially those with
complex problems.
•Emphasizes continuity between fetal and neonatal life.
Key Features
•Maternal health & care optimize obstetric and neonatal outcomes.
•Antenatal screening detects many congenital anomalies.
•All staff caring for newborns must be trained in newborn resuscitation.
•Newborn exam within 72 hrs of birth is mandatory.
•Requires multidisciplinary collaboration (obstetric, neonatal, surgical, genetic teams).
