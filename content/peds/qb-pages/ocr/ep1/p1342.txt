I O. Which complication is most likely in an infant of a diabetic mother?
A. Microsomia
B. Hyperglycemia
C. Anemia
D. Hypercalcemia
E. Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy
Infants of diabetic mothers are prone to macrosomia, hypoglycemia,
hypocalcemia, and transient hypertrophic cardiomyopathy due to fetal
hyperinsulinism.
