31. During a normal term newborn resuscitation, which of the following should
be performed first?
a. The infant should be dried and kept warm under a radiant heater
b. The heart rate should be auscultated.
c. Breath sounds should be auscultated.
d. The nose and oropharynx should be suctioned
The initial step is to prevent hypothermia by drying and warming. Airway,
breathing, and circulation steps follow as needed.
