31. A 5-year-old girl is receiving induction chemotherapy for acute
lymphoblastic leukemia. Which of the following vaccines is contraindicated to
be given to her 4-month-old sibling?
A. Oral polio vaccine
B. H. Influenza vaccine
C. Hepatitis B vaccine
D. Pneumococcal vaccine
E. DPT vaccine
Note: OPV is a live attenuated viral vaccine that is contraindicated in
children with immunodeficiency or to children who are close contacts of
immunodeficient patients due to risk of transmission.
