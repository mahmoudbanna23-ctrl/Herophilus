27. In hyaline membrane disease which of the following is WRONG?
a. Manifestations appear within 4 hours after birth
b. More common in infants of diabetic mothers
c. Surfactant is an effective replacement therapy in severe cases
d. Steroids given antenatally to the mother is useless
e. Cesarean section delivery is a risk factor
Antenatal steroids significantly reduce the risk Of RDS by accelerating
surfactant production. Saying they are useless is incorrect.
