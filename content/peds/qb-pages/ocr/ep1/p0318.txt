41. Weight faltering is usually identified when:
a. Weight fluctuates within one centile space
b. Weight crosses down two or more centile spaces
c. Height falters
d. BMI increases
Crossing downward by 22 centile spaces on a growth chart is considered clinically
significant weight faltering.
