13. A 13-year-old girl is brought to the clinic for concerns about delayed
puberty. She is in the 10th percentile for height and 25th percentile for weight.
Physical examination reveals no signs of puberty, and her bone age is mildly
delayed. Her mother also had a past history of delayed puberty. What is the
most likely diagnosis?
A. Turner syndrome
B. Constitutional growth delay
C. Growth hormone deficiency
D. Hypothyroidism
E. Familial short stature
Delayed puberty + mildly delayed bone age + family history
constitutional growth delay, a benign variant.
