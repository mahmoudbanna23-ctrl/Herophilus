crying and redcurrant jelly-like stools. What investigation would confirm the
most likely diagnosis?
a. Abdominal computerized tomography (CT)
b. Abdominal magnetic resonance imagmg (MRI)
c. Abdominal ultrasound
d. Abdominal X-ray

the characteristic "target" or "donut" sign.
