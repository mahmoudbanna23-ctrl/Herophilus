22. The following is NOT a cause of anemia m severe protein-energy
malnutrition:
a. Iron deficiency
b. Carbohydrate deficiency
c. Protein deficiency
d. Folic acid deficiency
e. Infections
Anemia in PEM is due to deficiencies in iron, folic acid, protein, or
secondary infections; carbohydrate deficiency does not directly cause
anemia.
