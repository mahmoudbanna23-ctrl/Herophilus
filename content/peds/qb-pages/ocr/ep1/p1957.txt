Polyhydramnios: Seen in conditions with impaired fetal swallowing
(esophageal or duodenal atresia), not Hirschsprung disease.
Esophageal atresia type: Most common type is proximal esophageal
atresia with distal tracheo-esophageal fistula (85% of cases).
CDH repair timing: Surgery is delayed until cardiorespiratory stabilization
with optimal ventilation and oxygenation.
Dance sign: Empty right iliac fossa indicates intussusception.
Intussusception US sign: "Target" or "donut" sign on ultrasound is
diagnostic.
Jejunal atresia X-ray: Multiple air—fluid levels indicate proximal bowel
obstruction.
