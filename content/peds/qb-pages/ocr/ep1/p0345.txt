Fortified breast milk combines the nutritional adequacy needed for preterm
growth with the immunologic and protective benefits of breast milk. Preterm
formula supports growth but does not provide the same protective immune
factors, so infants fed formula are at higher risk for serious complications like
NEC and late-onset sepsis.
