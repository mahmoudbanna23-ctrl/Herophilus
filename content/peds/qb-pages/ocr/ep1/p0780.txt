Vulnerability to Hypothermia
•Large surface area vs. mass high heat loss.
•Thin, permeable skin transepidermal water loss.
•Little subcutaneous fat.
•Cannot conserve heat (no shivering/curling).
Control of temperature:
•Neutral thermal environment (incubators, radiant heaters).
•Prevent heat loss:
• Convection: warm air, clothing, cover head, avoid draughts.
• Radiation: cover baby, incubator double walls.
• Evaporation: dry/wrap at birth, plastic bag if very preterm, humidify incubator.
• Conduction: heated mattress.
