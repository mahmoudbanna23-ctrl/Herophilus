12. Which of the following is a character of familial short stature?
A. Height falls within 2 centile spaces of mid-parental height
B. More common in girls
C. Delayed onset of puberty
D. Presentation is in adolescence
Children with familial short stature track a low centile consistent with genetic
potential. Puberty is normal and not delayed.
