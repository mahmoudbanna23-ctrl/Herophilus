24. A 4.3 kg infant is delivered to a woman whose diabetes mellitus is poorly
controlled. Of the following, the MOST likely neonatal manifestation of maternal
diabetes is:
a. Anemia
b. Hirschsprung disease
c. Hypercalcemia
d. Polycythemia
e. Blood glucose 75 mg/dl
Infants of diabetic mothers are prone to polycythemia due to chronic
intrauterine hypoxia stimulating erythropoiesis. They may also have
hypoglycemia, hypocalcemia, and cardiomyopathy.
