31. A farmer's child presents with weight below the 2nd centile and no edema.
What is the most likely diagnosis?
a. Marasmus
b. Normal
c. Kwashiorkor
d. Obesity
Marasmus presents with severe wasting without edema, Often in
children with inadequate caloric intake. Kwashiorkor, in contrast, has
edema despite some fat Stores.
