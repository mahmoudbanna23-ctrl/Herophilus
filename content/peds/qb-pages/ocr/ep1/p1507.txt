12. Which of the following conditions may cause macrocephaly?
A. Chronic subdural hematoma
B. Cerebral palsy
C. Congenital infection
D. Hypoglycemia
E. Perinatal asphyxia
Chronic subdural hematoma can lead to increased head size
due to fluid accumulation. Other listed conditions usually don't
cause macrocephaly.
