56. What is the treatment of choice for a child presenting with white plaques on
oral mucous membranes (oral thrush)?
A. Antifungal
B. Antiviral
C. Antibacterial
D. Steroids
Thrush is treated with topical nystatin or an azole antifungal
agent such as fluconazole.
