23. Which is a feature of chickenpox infection?
A. It has an incubation period of 5—7 days.
B. The rash is confluent, centrifugal, and pustular.
C. It is associated with Koplik spots.
D. There is a generalized lymphadenopathy
E. It can cause visceral dissemination in the immunocompromised host
Varicella can cause visceral dissemination in
immunocompromised patients. The incubation is 14—16 days,
and rash is vesicular, not pustular.
