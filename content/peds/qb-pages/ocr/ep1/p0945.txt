•Shingles (zoster): reactivation in dermatomal distribution, more common if early
varicella or immunocompromised.
•EBV (infectious mononucleosis):
• Fever, sore throat, lymphadenopathy, hepatosplenomegaly, maculopapular rash
(esp. if given amoxicillin).
• Complications: airway obstruction, prolonged fatigue.
• Diagnosis: atypical lymphocytes, Monospot test.
• Management: supportive; steroids if severe airway obstruction.
•CMV:
• Transmission: saliva, breastmilk, blood, organ transplant, transplacental.
• Usually asymptomatic; mononucleosis-like in some.
• Immunocompromised: retinitis, pneumonitis, hepatitis, marrow failure.
• Congenital CMV: neurodevelopmental problems.
• Treatment: ganciclovir/valganciclovir.
•HHV-6/7 (Roseola): high fever rash as fever settles; may cause febrile convulsio
