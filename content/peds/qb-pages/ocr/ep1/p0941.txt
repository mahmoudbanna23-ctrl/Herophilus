Impetigo
•Localized staph/strep infection honey-colored crusts.
•Rx: topical mupirocin (mild); oral flucloxacillin / co-amoxiclav (severe).
Boils
•Folliculitis/abscess by S. aureus.
•Rx: systemic antibiotics, drainage.
•Recurrent —Y check for nasal carriage or immunodeficiency.
Periorbital Cellulitis
•Fever + unilateral eyelid erythema/edema.
•Risk of spread orbital cellulitis, meningitis, cavernous sinus thrombosis.
•Rx: urgent IV antibiotics + ophthalmology.
Staphylococcal Scalded Skin Syndrome (SSSS)
•Exfoliative toxin epidermal separation (Nikolsky sign).
•Infants/young children.
•Rx: IVflucloxacillin, analgesia, fluid balance.
