22. A 12-hour newborn is noted to be jaundiced. Indirect bilirubin 6.4 mg/dl.
What is the appropriate management?
a. Admit the baby to the incubator for phototherapy and lab rx.
b. Repeat the bilirubin measurement after another 12 hours
c. Ensure adequate breast feeding and repeat bilirubin measurement after 24 hours
d. Reassure the mother that this is normal
e. Give bottle feeds for 24 hours then resume breast feeding
Jaundice appearing within the first 24 hours is always pathologic, often due to
hemolysis. Such cases require immediate phototherapy and investigations to
determine the cause.
