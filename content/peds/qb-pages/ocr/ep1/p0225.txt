Causes of Rickets
Malabsorption / Defective Vit D production
•Coeliac disease, CF, cholestatic liver disease, pancreatic insufficiency.
•Anticonvulsants ( 't Vit D breakdown).
•Chronic liver disease (025-hydroxylation).
•CKD, Fanconi syndrome (01, 25 production, phosphate loss).
Inherited / Genetic
•Vit D—dependent rickets (Type 1 & 2).
•X-Iinked hypophosphataemic rickets.
•Hypophosphatasia.
