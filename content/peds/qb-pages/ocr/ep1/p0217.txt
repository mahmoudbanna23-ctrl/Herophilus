Barriers & Complications of Breastfeeding
•Mother-related: embarrassment, return to work, sore nipples, mastitis, inadequate
milk.
•Medical issues: breast milk jaundice, tongue-tie, rare metabolic disorders (e.g.,
galactosemia).
•Infections: HIV (contraindicated in high-income settings, allowed with ART in low-
income).
•Drugs: some contraindicated (e.g., chemo, lithium, radioactive therapy).
