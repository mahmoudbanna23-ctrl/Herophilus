15. A 38-year-old Gl woman had an abnormal quad screen at 16 weeks' gestation
with an elevated human chorionic gonadotropin (hCG) level and a low alpha-
fetoprotein (AFP). Amniocentesis confirms the diagnosis of trisomy 21. Which of
the following defects are found in this patient type?
A. Diaphragmatic hernia
B. Duodenal atresia
C. Inguinal hernia
D. Meckel diverticulum
E. Wilms tumor
Down syndrome (trisomy 21) is associated with duodenal
atresia. It presents with bilious vomiting and the "double
bubble" sign on x-ray.
