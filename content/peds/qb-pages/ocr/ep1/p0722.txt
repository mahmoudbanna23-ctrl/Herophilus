Red Reflex & Eye Abnormalities
•Red reflex check: with direct ophthalmoscope (15—20 cm away).
•Abnormal findings:
• Absent reflex congenital cataract (3—4/10,000 births, 10% childhood blindness
worldwide).
• Others: congenital glaucoma, iris coloboma.
•Management: urgent ophthalmology; early surgery (first weeks) improves outcome.
