4. Alysha is a 2-month-old baby who is seen in the paediatric outpatient
department. She was born at term, weighing 3.5 kg, and is breastfed. Her mother
is concerned as she has vomited some of the milk after most feeds since birth. She
cries when she vomits. She is continuing to grow along the 50th centile. What is
the most likely diagnosis?
A. Gastro-oesophageal reflux
B. Helicobacter pylori infection
C. Infant colic
D. Overfeeding
E. Pyloric stenosis
Physiological reflux is common in infants due to immature
lower esophageal sphincter tone. Vomiting without weight loss
or systemic signs supports this diagnosis.
