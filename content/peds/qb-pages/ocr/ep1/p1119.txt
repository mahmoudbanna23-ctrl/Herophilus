77. Which pathogen is not typically associated with vesicular skin lesions?
A. Herpes zoster
B. Varicella zoster
C. Staph. aureus
D. Herpes simplex
E. Human herpesvirus (e.g. III-IV-6)
HHV-6 causes roseola, which results in a maculopapular rash
not vesicular. Others are known to cause vesicles.
