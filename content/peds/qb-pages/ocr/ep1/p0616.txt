20. A known epileptic patient in status epilepticus received rectal diazepam 10
minutes ago but continues to seize. IV access is now available and blood sugar is
5.5 mmol/L. What is the next most appropriate treatment?
A. Intravenous lorazepam
B. Intravenous phenytoin
C. Intravenous thiopental
D. Rectal paraldehyde
E. Buccal midazolam
Lorazepam is the next line of treatment in Status epilepticus if
benzodiazepines (like rectal diazepam) have failed and IV
access is available. It is effective and has a longer duration Of
action than diazepam.
