Childhood growth (after infancy, before puberty) is mainly driven by
growth hormone rather than nutrition or sex hormones.

Chronic constipation in children can cause firm stools, pain, and palpable
fecal mass, sometimes with overflow diarrhea.
Rotavirus is the most common cause ofacute watery diarrhea in young
children.
Cephalhaematoma is a subperiosteal hemorrhage that does not cross
suture lines, appears hours—days after birth, and is soft and fluctuant.
Progressive drop in growth centiles suggests pathological short stature
