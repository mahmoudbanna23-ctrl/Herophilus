30. A 3-week-old infant has been referred to you because of persistent jaundice.
Her current weight is 4050 g. Her neonatal screening test was normal, which one
of the following is the most appropriate management?
a. STOP breastfeeding
b. Commence phototherapy
c. Reassure the mother and continue breastfeeding
d. Perform liver function tests
e. Arrange an urgent liver ultrasound
In a healthy infant with normal weight gain and normal neonatal screen,
breastfeeding jaundice is usually benign. No intervention is needed other than
reassurance and continued feeding.
