3. Measles
•Still causes outbreaks if immunization coverage is insufficient.
•Transmission: Droplet, highly infectious.
•Clinical features:
• Prodrome: fever, cough, coryza, conjunctivitis, Koplik spots.
• Rash: starts behind ears spreads downward, maculopapular, may desquamate.
•Complications:
• Encephalitis (1 in 5000, 15% mortality, 40% sequelae).
• SSPE (1 in 100,000, 7 yrs after infection, progressive neurodegeneration).
• Pneumonia, otitis media, corneal ulcers, myocarditis, diarrhea.
• Worse in malnourished/HlV+ children.
Supportive; ribavirin + vitamin A in severe/immunocompromised cases.
• Treatment:
•Prevention: Immunization (MMR).
