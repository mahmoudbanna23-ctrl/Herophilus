Cow's Milk Protein Allergy
(1)
If the newborn is exclusively formula-fed and cow's milk allergy is suspected
(+) The reaction is Acute
(Nausea, Vomiting, Colicky abdominal pain, Pruritus, Erythema soon after a feed)
lgE-mediated reactions
Assess for cow milk protein allergy using a skin prick test or a blood test
(2)
If the newborn is exclusively formula-fed and cow's milk allergy is suspected
(+) The reaction is Delayed
(Reflux, Vomiting, Loose stool, Not gaining weight, Perianal redness, Skin
manifestations like erythema or atopic eczema)
Non-lgE-mediated reactions
Change to hypoallergenic formula, e.g. extensively hydrolysed formula
1984
