3. A febrile child presents with a purpuric rash. What is the most likely diagnosis?
a. Meningococcal septicemia
b. Viral exanthem
c. Immune thrombocytopenic purpura
d. Scarlet fever
A purpuric rash in a febrile child is a red flag for meningococcal septicemia, a medical
emergency.
