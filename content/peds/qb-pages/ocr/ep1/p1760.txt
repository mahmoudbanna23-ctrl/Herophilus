58. Katie is 6 years old. She missed a few days of school as she had a fever and
was generally lethargic. Now she has a temperature of 38.30C and has a marked
erythematous rash on her cheeks. Which of the following is the organism that is
most likely to be the causative agent in this clinical scenario?
A. Parvovirus infection (fifth disease)
B. Roseola infantum (sixth disease)
C. Measles virus
D. Mumps virus
E. Cytomegalovirus infection
Parvovirus 819 causes erythema infectiosum or fifth
disease, which is also known as 'slapped cheek syndrome'
because Of its characteristic facial rash. This infection will
temporarily reduce red cell production, which can result
in serious anaemia in children with more rapid red cell
turnover (hereditary spherocytosis or sickle cell disease)
or the fetus.
