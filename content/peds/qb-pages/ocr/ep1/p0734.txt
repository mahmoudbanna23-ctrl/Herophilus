4. A mother is pregnant with dichorionic, diamniotic twins. Which condition
carries the greatest increased risk in this pregnancy?
A. Congenital abnormalities
B. Gestational diabetes
C. Macrosomia
D. Post-term gestation
E. Twin-to-twin transfusion
Twin pregnancies are associated with a higher rate Of
congenital malformations, especially in dichorionic
pregnancies. Twin-to-twin transfusion is more common in
monochorionic twins.
