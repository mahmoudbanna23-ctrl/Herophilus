Activated charcoal can reduce absorption if given early; vomiting and
gastric lavage are generally unsafe, and not all toxins are detectable in blood.
In anaphylaxis, airway compromise and hypoxia are immediate threats;
assess airway and give high-flow oxygen before other interventions.
Oral ulcers with vesicles on hands and feet indicate hand-foot-mouth
disease, most commonly caused by Coxsackie A virus.
The fastest growth occurs in utero ( fetal) , especially during the second
trimester; postnatal growth is slower.
Suspected meningococcal disease requires immediate parenteral
antibiotics before hospital transfer to reduce mortality.
