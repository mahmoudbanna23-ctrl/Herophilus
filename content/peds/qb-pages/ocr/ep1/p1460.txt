70. A diagnosis of Hirschsprung disease is made on a newbom baby. What is
the definitive diagnostic test for Hirschsprung disease?
A. Barium enema
B. Rectal biopsy
C. Diagnostic laparoscopy
D. Anorectal manometry
The gold standard is biopsy showing absence Of ganglion cells in the submucosa.
Barium enema or manometry are supportive, not definitive.
