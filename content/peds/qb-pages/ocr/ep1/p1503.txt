I O. Which is a clear indication for tonsillectomy in children?
A. Recurrent febrile viral respiratory tract infections with febrile convulsions
B. Exudative tonsillitis with positive monospot test
C. Two attacks of follicular tonsillitis in a two-month time
D. Seven episodes of exudative tonsillitis in the preceding year
E. A high ASOT after an episode of tonsillitis
Frequent severe tonsillitis is a clear indication. Seven episodes
Of exudative tonsillitis in a year justify surgical removal.
