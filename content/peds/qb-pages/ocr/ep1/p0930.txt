The Febrile Child
General Points
•Most febrile children brief, self-limiting viral illness.
•Challenge: detect the small minority with serious infection.
•Fever definition: >38 oc. Axillary temp underestimates by 0.5 oc.
•Height of fever not important presence matters more.
