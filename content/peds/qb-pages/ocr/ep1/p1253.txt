47. What is the main driver for growth in the childhood phase?
A. Nutrition
B. Growth hormone
C. Testosterone
D. Thyroid hormones
E. Testosterone and estrogen
During childhood (after infancy, before puberty), growth is largely
regulated by growth hormone rather than nutrition or sex hormones.
