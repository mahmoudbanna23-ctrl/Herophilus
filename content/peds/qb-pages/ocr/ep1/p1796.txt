76. Ideal position for newborn with esophageal atresia:
A. Supine
B. Prone
C. Semi-sitting
D. Trendelenburg
Newborns with EA are placed semi-sitting to prevent aspiration of saliva
or gastric contents. Supine or prone can increase risk of aspiration, and
Trendelenburg is not appropriate.
