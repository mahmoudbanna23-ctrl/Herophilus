16. Ted, aged 2 years, has had a 2-day history of low-grade fever and coryza. His
mother has brought him to the Emergency Department because he is crying
inconsolably. She thinks his tummy is hurting him. He has not opened his bowels
for 2 days. He appears reasonably well, has minimal abdominal tenderness, but
has an indentable mass on the left side of the abdomen. Which of the following is
the most likely diagnosis?
A. Constipation
B. Pneumonia
C. Appendicitis
D. Mesenteric adenitis
Chronic constipation can present with a palpable fecal mass
and abdominal discomfort. The minimal tenderness and history
of not opening bowels support this diagnosis over acute
surgical causes
E. Functional abdominal pain
