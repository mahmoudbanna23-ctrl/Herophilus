41. How would you manage a choking child with mild airway obstruction?
A. Start early CPR
B. Give 5 abdominal thrusts
C. Do 5 back blows
D. Give 5 chest thrusts
E. Encourage cough
If the child has mild Obstruction and can cough effectively, the best
management is to encourage coughing. Back blows or thrusts are for
severe Obstruction.
