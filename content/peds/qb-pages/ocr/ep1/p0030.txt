I. When evaluating a child for stunting, which growth chan you will use?
a. height for age charts
b. weight for age charts
c. weight for length charts
d. body mass index charts
e. mid arm circumference measurements
Stunting reflects chronic malnutrition, which primarily impairs linear growth.
Therefore, height-for-age is the correct indicator, not weight-based charts.
