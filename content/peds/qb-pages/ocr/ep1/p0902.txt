53. Which clinical condition is left without further intervention on neonatal
examination?
A. Central cyanosis
B. Heart murmur
C. Inguinal hernia
D. Umbilical hernia
E. Jaundice at 24 hours of age
Common in neonates, usually closes spontaneously by 2—3
years of age. No intervention needed unless persistent after 3—
5 years or very large.
