minute, 4 at 5 minutes, and 7 at 10 minutes. She was ventilated for 3 days. She
now has global developmental delay. What is the most likely explanation?
A. Congenital hypothyroidism
B. Meningitis
C. Inborn error of metabolism
D. Intracranial hemorrhage
E. Hypoxic-ischaemic encephalopathy (HIE)
A history of low Apgar scores, ventilation, and developmental delay strongly
suggests HIE, resulting from perinatal asphyxia. This is a major cause of
cerebral palsy and developmental disorders.
