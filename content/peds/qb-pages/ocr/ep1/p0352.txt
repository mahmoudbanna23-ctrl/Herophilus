57. Which of the following types of food is contraindicated and has to be avoided
in a child under one year of age?
A. Eggs
B. Honey
C. Peanut
D. All of the above
Foods high in salt and sugar should be avoided and honey must not be given
until 1 year of age because of risk of infantile botulism.
Early introduction Of common allergens such as egg and peanut at, or around
4 months of age in infants at high risk of developing lgE-mediated allergy may
reduce the risk of developing allergy to these foods.
