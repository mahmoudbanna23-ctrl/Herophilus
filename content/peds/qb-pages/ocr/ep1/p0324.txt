44. Nutritional status of a child can be reliably assessed by:
a. A single weight measurement
b. A single height measurement
c. Multiple anthropometric indices
d. Only head circumference
A single measurement is insufficient; weight, height, BMI, and mid-arm
circumference together provide a comprehensive assessment.
