Strangulation
•Clothes/bedding caught on furniture (accidental) or suicidal in adolescents.
•Signs: dysphonia, dysphagia, facial petechiae, neck edema; risk of hypoxic brain injury.
Drowning
•Babies/toddlers: bathtubs, paddling pools, garden ponds.
•Older children: canals, lakes, sea.
•Asphyxia ± aspiration death.
•Prevention: on-scene resuscitation (saves up to 30%).
•Cold water hypothermia may be protective continue resuscitation until rewarmed,
even with fixed pupils.
