21. A 3-year-old boy presents with 24 hours of irritability, vomiting, and fever. He
has a purpuric rash on his upper limbs and abdomen. Vitals: Temp 38.50C, HR
140/min, BP 90/60 mmHg. What is the most likely diagnosis?
a. Henoch-Schönlein purpura
b. Immune thrombocytopenic purpura
c. Measles
d. Meningococcal sepsis
e. Leukemia
This presentation is classic for meningococcal sepsis, especially
with the purpuric rash, fever, and hypotension. It's a medical
emergency requiring immediate antibiotic therapy.
