79. Most common complication after repair of EA with distal TEF:
A. Anastomotic leak
B. Esophageal stricture
C. Recurrent TEF
D. Gastroesophageal reflux
E. Tracheomalacia
The most common long-term complication after repair Of EA with
distal TEF is GER, which can cause dysphagia and respiratory
problems. Anastomotic strictures are common early, but GER
predominates over time.
