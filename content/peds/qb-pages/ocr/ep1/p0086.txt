29. A 12-year-old girl is referred to the paediatric endocrinology clinic for
evaluation of delayed puberty. She is diagnosed with constitutional delay of
growth. What investigation is most likely to confirm this diagnosis?
A. MRI brain
B. Thyroid function tests
C. Growth hormone level
D. Bone age
E. Karyotype
Constitutional delay is confirmed by a bone age that is delayed
relative to chronological age. It helps differentiate from
pathological causes Of delayed puberty.
