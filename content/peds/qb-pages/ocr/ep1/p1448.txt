64. A full-tenn boy was presented with imperforate anus shortly after birth.
After a few hours of resuscitation, he was noted to pass meconium per urethra.
What is the next step?
A. Colostomy
B. Perineal anoplasty
C. Posterior sagittal anorectoplasty
D. Urethral dilatation
E. Watchful expectancy
Meconium passing via urethra indicates a high anorectal malformation
with fistula. Initial management is colostomy, followed later by definitive
repair.
