Patent Ductus Arteriosus (PDA)
•Common in extremely preterm/ill infants.
•Left right shunt, often with RDS.
•Signs: apnoea, bradycardia, bounding pulses, systolic murmur, heart failure.
•Dx: echocardiography.
•Tx: ibuprofen/paracetamol, surgical ligation if persistent.
Infection
risk due to:
• Lack of IgG (transferred late in pregnancy).
• Perinatal infection triggering preterm labour.
• Nosocomial infections (lines, ventilation).
•Leading cause of prematurity, mortality, BPD, brain injury.
