30. A 15-year-old girl is referred to the paediatric endocrinology clinic for
delayed puberty. Her height is on the 0.4th centile. She has an ejection systolic
murmur and widely spaced nipples. Gonadotrophin levels are raised. What is the
most likely diagnosis?
A. Turner syndrome
B. Klinefelter syndrome
C. McCune-Albright syndrome
D. Hypothyroidism
Short Stature, delayed puberty, heart murmur, and raised
gonadotropins point to Turner syndrome. It is caused by
E. Silver-Russell syndrome
gonadal dysgenesis and confirmed with karyotyping.
