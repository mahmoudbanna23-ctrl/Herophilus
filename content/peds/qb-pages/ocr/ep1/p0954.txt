Causes of Prolonged Fever
Infective
•Localized: Osteomyelitis, deep abscesses (intra-abdominal, retroperitoneal, pelvic).
•Systemic bacterial: Typhoid, Bartonella (cat scratch), Brucella, infective endocarditis,
TB, non-TB mycobacteria.
•Viral: EBV, CMV, Hlu
•Parasitic: Malaria, toxocariasis, amoebiasis.
Non-infective
•Systemic onset juvenile idiopathic arthritis (JIA).
•Autoimmune/lnflammatory: SLE, vasculitis (esp. Kawasaki), inflammatory bowel
disease, sarcoidosis.
•Malignancy: Leukemia, lymphoma, neuroblastoma, Ewing sarcoma.
•Other: Drug fever, macrophage activation syndrome (HLH), autoinflammatory
syndromes (FMF), fabricated illness (Munchausen by proxy).
