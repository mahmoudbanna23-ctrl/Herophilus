25. Which of the following is the most common causative organism of acute otitis
media in children?
a. Streptococcus pneumoniae
b. Staphylococcus aureus
c. Adenovirus
d. Herpes simplex virus
e. Influenza virus
Streptococcus pneumoniae is the most frequent bacterial
cause of otitis media in young children, followed by
Haemophilus influenzae and Moraxella catarrhalis.
