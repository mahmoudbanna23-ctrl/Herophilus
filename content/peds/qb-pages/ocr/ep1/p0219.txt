Faltering Growth
Definition: Suboptimal weight gain for age/sex.
•Previously called failure to thrive (now avoided in UK).
•Effects: stunting, reduced head growth, delayed development.
Identification:
•Fall in weight 22 centile lines, or weight below 2nd centile.
•Requires serial measurements (weight, height, head circumference).
•One-off measurements unreliable.
Normal Patterns:
•Birthweight may drop 10% then regained by 2 weeks.
•Catch-down growth (large babies), catch-up growth (small babies).
•Acute illness temporary weight loss, usually regained in 2—3 weeks.
Differentiation:
•Distinguish faltering from constitutionally small/normal thin babies.
•Consider prematurity or IUGR when interpreting growth charts.
