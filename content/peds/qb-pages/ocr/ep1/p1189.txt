16. Which of the following statements concerning shock is TRUE?
A. Dehydration due to gastroenteritis can cause shock due to maldistribution of
fluid
B. Bradycardia occurs in compensated shock
C. Mottled, pale and cold skin is a sign of compensated shock
D. Anaphylaxis can cause shock due to hypovolemia
E. Decreased urine output is a sign of decompensated shock
Compensated presents with tachycardia and poor perfusion (cold
extremities, delayed cap refill). Decreased urine output is a later
decompensated sign.
