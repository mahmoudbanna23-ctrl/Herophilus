58. Imran, a 3-year-old boy who recently moved from Bangladesh, has not
gained weight and presents with a cough. A chest X-ray is requested.
What is the most likely diagnosis?
A. Asthma
B. Neuroblastoma
C. Pertussis infection
D. Pneumonia
E. Tuberculosis
A history of recent immigration, failure to thrive, and chronic
cough are red flags for tuberculosis, especially in high-
prevalence countries like Bangladesh.
