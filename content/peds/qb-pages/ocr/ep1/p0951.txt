4. Mumps
•Incidence reduced by MMR, but resurged with reduced uptake.
• Droplet; winter/spring; incubation 15—24 days.
• Transmission.
•Clinical features:
• Fever, malaise, parotitis (often bilateral).
• Earache, pain on eating/drinking.
Plasma amylase (parotid/pancreatic involvement).
•Complications:
• Viral meningitis (50% CSF lymphocytes; encephalitis 1 in 5000).
• Orchitis (esp. post-pubertal males, may sperm count but infertility rare).
• Rare oophoritis, mastitis, arthritis, hearing loss.
