Adaptation to Extrauterine Life
•Fetal circulation:
• 02 via placenta ductus venosus -5 RA foramen ovale LA -5 heart/brain.
• Deoxygenated blood via SVC RI.' * PA ductus arteriosus aorta placenta.
• Fetal 02 sats: 65% (upper body), 35% (lower body). High Hb & fetal Hb (left-shift
curve) improve 02 delivery.
•Birth transition:
• Labour & catecholamine surge lung fluid, stimulate breathing, fluid
reabsorption.
• First breath Pa02 -5 PVR -5 'TN pulmonary blood flow.
• Closure of foramen ovale & ductus arteriosus.
•Delivery practices:
• Skin-to-skin + delayed cord clamping (1—3 min): circulating volume, smoother
transition, anemia, bonding.
•C-section (no labour): risk of TTN (delayed fluid absorption).
•Failure to breathe causes: birth asphyxia (most common), trauma, drugs, prematurit , lung
fluid retention, airway anomaly.
