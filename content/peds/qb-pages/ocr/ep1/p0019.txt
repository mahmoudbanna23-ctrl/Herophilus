Abnormal Head Growth
Normal head growth
•80% of adult size by age 5.
•Sutures open at birth; posterior fontanelle closes by 8 weeks, anterior by 12—18 months.
Microcephaly
•Definition: OFC < 2nd centile (Egypt: >3 SD below mean).
•Causes:
• Familial (often normal development).
• Genetic syndromes.
• Craniosynostosis, cerebral malformations.
• Congenital infections, fetal alcohol syndrome.
• Acquired (asphyxia, meningitis, hypoglycemia).
•Often associated with cerebral palsy, seizures, cognitive delay.
