Tuberculosis (TB)
•Still a major cause of prolonged fever in children.
•Forms: pulmonary (75%) + extrapulmonary (lymphadenitis, bone/joint, GU, meningitis).
•Features: fever, weight loss, anorexia, lymphadenopathy, cough.
•Diagnosis:
TST/Mantoux, IGRA (less reliable in children).
• Gastric aspirates / induced sputum for AFB culture.
• Imaging (CXR, CT).
•Treatment: Standard quadruple therapy (2 months), then dual therapy (4+ months).
Longer for CNS or bone TB.
•Latent TB: Isoniazid or Rifampicin + Isoniazid prophylaxis.
•BCG vaccine: protective but not absolute.
