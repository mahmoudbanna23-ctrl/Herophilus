Fetal Medicine Interventions
•Maternal therapy for fetus:
• Antenatal steroids lung maturity, RDS, IVH, neonatal mortality.
• Antiarrhythmics (digoxin, flecainide) for fetal SVT.
•Direct fetal therapy:
• Intrauterine transfusions for Rh isoimmunization.
• IVIG for neonatal alloimmune thrombocytopenia.
Maternal Conditions Affecting Fetus
•Diabetes mellitus:
• Risks: miscarriage, polyhydramnios, pre-eclampsia, congenital anomalies, IUGR,
macrosomia, stillbirth.
• Fetal problems: cardiac defects, sacral agenesis, hypoplastic colon.
• Neonatal problems: hypoglycemia, RDS, HCM, polycythemia.
•Obesity: risk of macrosomia, stillbirth, neonatal mortality.
