47. A 6-day-old baby who is breastfeeding well and thriving is referred by the
midwife because she appears jaundiced. She has pigmented stools and is passing
urine normally. Examination is unremarkable. Her bilirubin is 5 mg/dl. What
would be the appropriate management of this infant?
A. Complete a prolonged jaundice screen
B. Commence phototherapy
C. Top up feeds with formula milk
D. Repeat the bilirubin level in 8 hours
E. No treatment required
This is physiological jaundice; bilirubin is low, baby is well, stools/urine
normal. No intervention is needed.
