34. An infant was born at 35 weeks of gestation to a 30-year-old G3P2 mother
via spontaneous vaginal delivery. Rupture of membranes occurred 15 hours
prior to delivery. Birth weight is 3400 g, and Apgar scores were 6 and 9 at I
and 5 minutes, respectively.
Which of the following factors places this infant at greatest risk for sepsis?
A. Male gender
B. Gestational age
C. Apgar score
I)- Gestational age
Prematurity (35 weeks) is the strongest risk factor for neonatal
sepsis in this case. Prolonged rupture of membranes adds
further risk.
