76. Which of the following is a character of familial short stature?
A. Height falls within 2 centile spaces of mid-parental height
B. More common in girls
C. Delayed onset of puberty
D. Presentation is in adolescence
Familial short stature is characterized by normal growth velocity and bone age, with height
consistent with genetic potential. Unlike constitutional delay, puberty occurs at a normal age.
