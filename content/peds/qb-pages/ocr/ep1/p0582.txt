3. A 3-year-old boy who is unconscious arrives at the Emergency Department.
Airway, breathing, and circulation have been managed. glucose is normal.
On examination, his pupils are bilaterally pinpoint. Temperature and other vitals
are normal. What is the most likely cause?
A. Third nerve lesion
B. Severe hypoxia
C. Hypothermia
D. Tentorial hemiation
E. Opiate poisoning
Pinpoint pupils in an unconscious child with stable vital signs
and normal glucose strongly suggest opioid ingestion. Opiates
cause central nervous System depression with characteristic
miosis.
