56. Which of the following would be the most likely radiographic finding in a

B. Shortening of the radius and ulna

Calcium-deficient rickets causes expansion of the growth plates and metaphyseal changes,
often seen as prominent costochondral junctions ("rachitic rosary"). Other classic
radiographic features may include cupping and fraying of metaphyses.
