26. A 15-year-old girl attends a clinic concerned about episodic abdominal pain
and loose stool over the past year. She dates the symptoms back to when she
changed schools and says they occur more often on school days. She describes
crampy abdominal pain followed by the need to pass a stool, occurring about
twice a month. Her bowel habit is normal on other days. There has never been
any blood, and she has not lost any weight. What is the most likely underlying
cause of her symptoms?
A. Cow's milk protein intolerance
B. Coeliac disease
C. Ulcerative colitis
Crampy abdominal pain associated with bowel habit changes,
occurring during stress (school days), and without red flags
suggests irritable bowel syndrome. This is a functional Gl
disorder common in adolescents.
D
Irri I
