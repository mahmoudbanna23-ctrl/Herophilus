47. Immunodeficiency in malnutrition is mainly indicated by:
a. Low serum immunoglobulins
b. Impaired cell-mediated immunity
c. Reduced neutrophil count
d. Low complement levels
Protein-energy malnutrition primarily causes T-cell defects, while
immunoglobulin levels are often preserved.
