Respiratory Distress Syndrome (RDS)
•Cause: Surfactant deficiency alveolar collapse, poor gas exchange.
•Risk: Preterm (<28 wks), infants of diabetic mothers, rare genetic mutations.
Antenatal corticosteroids ('fr surfactant).
•Prevention:
•Clinical signs (within 4h): Tachypnoea >60/min, recession, grunting, cyanosis.
•X-ray: "Ground glass" lungs + air bronchogram.
•Management:
• Oxygen (target sats 91—95% preterm, avoid hyperoxia).
• CPAP/ high-flow nasal cannula.
• Surfactant via tracheal tube/catheter.
• Mechanical ventilation if severe.
