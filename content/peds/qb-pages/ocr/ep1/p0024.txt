Delayed Puberty
Definition:
•Girls: No breast development by 13 yrs, no menarche by 15 yrs, >3 yrs between thelarche & menarche.
•Boys: No testicular enlargement by 14 yrs.
Causes:
1.Constitutional delay of growth & puberty (CDGP):
Most common in boys.
Family history common.
Delayed bone age; final height normal.
2.Hypogonadotropic hypogonadism (low gonadotrophins):
Systemic: CF, Crohn, anorexia, chronic illness.
Pituitary/hypotha/amic: tumors, Kallmann syndrome, GH deficiency.
Acquired hypothyroidism.
3.Hypergonadotropic hypogonadism (high gonadotrophins):
Gonadal failure: Turner (45,XO), Klinefelter (47,XXY).
Acquired: surgery, chemo/radiation, torsion, autoimmune.
Management:
•Treat underlying cause.
•Sex steroids if necessary (estradiol for girls, low-dose testosterone for boys).
•Support psychosocial adjustment.
