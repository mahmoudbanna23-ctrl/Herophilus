Antimicrobial Resistance
: inappropriate/overuse of antibiotics.
•Cause
•Antibiotic stewardship: pathogen-specific, narrow-spectrum, correct dose/duration,
monitoring.
•Neonatal challenges:
• Empirical antibiotics often started for nonspecific signs.
• Major issue Gram-negative pathogens (E. coli, Klebsiella).
• ESBL-producers resistant to penicillins/cephalosporins.
• Carbapenem-resistant Enterobacterales (CRE): resistant to meropenem,
gentamicin, others.
•Policies: use restrictive, standardized protocols. Avoid 3rd-gen cephalosporins unless
needed.
•Stop antibiotics if blood cultures negative + no infection signs.
•Infection prevention: hygiene, surveillance, MDT approach.
