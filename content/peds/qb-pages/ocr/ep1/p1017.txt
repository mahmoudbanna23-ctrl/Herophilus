26. Which vaccine is contraindicated in a 4-year-old boy on immunosuppressive
therapy for autoimmune hepatitis?
a. Tetanus toxoid
b. Hepatitis B vaccine
c. Acellular pertussis vaccine
d. Inactivated polio vaccine
e._BCG
Live vaccines like BCG are contraindicated in
immunosuppressed children due to the risk of disseminated
infection.
