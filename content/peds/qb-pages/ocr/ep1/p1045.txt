40. A 4-year-old girl presented with high-grade fever and sore throat one day ago.
A pinpoint red papular rash has now developed all over her body, with circumoral
pallor and a whitish-coated tongue. What is the most likely diagnosis?
a. Measles
b. Scarlet fever
c. Rubella
d. Chickenpox
e. Mumps
Scarlet fever presents with fever, sore throat, "sandpaper"
rash, circumoral pallor, and "white strawberry tongue." It is
caused by toxin-producing Streptococcus pyogenes.
