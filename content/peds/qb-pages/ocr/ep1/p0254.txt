9. Jonas is an 18-month-old Black African boy in KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa.
He was born weighing 3.2 kg. He was breastfed until 9 months of age when his
sibling was born. He now mainly eats traditional maize-based porridge, which is
grown on the family farm. His weight is just below the 0.4th centile. He looks
thin but has a distended abdomen. There is oedema around his eyes and feet. His
hair has a red tinge. Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis?
A. Vitamin D deficiency
B. Vitamin K deficiency
C. Kwashiorkor
D. Marasmus
Kwashiorkor is a form Of protein-energy malnutrition
associated with a protein-deficient diet (like maize). Classic
features include edema, hepatomegaly, growth failure, and
hypopigmented hair.
E. Cow's milk protein allergy
