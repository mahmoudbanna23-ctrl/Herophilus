Pediatric Emergencies
Primary Principles
1.
•ABCDE approach (Airway, Breathing, Circulation, Disability, Exposure).
•Aim: Early recognition + intervention to prevent respiratory/circulatory failure.
•Pediatric resuscitation same principles as adults, but adapted for age.
•Teamwork, simulation-based training, and regular updates are essential.
Triage & Early Warning
2.
•Triage: urgency based on severity, not arrival order.
•Tools: Pediatric Early Warning Scores (PEWS).
•Parameters: HR, RR, BP, temperature, 02 saturation, consciousness.
•Capillary refill >2s = abnormal.
•Vital signs vary by age (HR highest in newborns, lowest in adolescents).
