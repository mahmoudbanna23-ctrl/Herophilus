Red Flags (serious illness)
•Fever >38 oc in infant <3 months.
•Color: pale, mottled, cyanosed.
•Altered consciousness, seizures, bulging fontanelle, focal neuro signs.
•Severe respiratory distress.
•Shock.
•Purpuric rash (think meningococcal sepsis).
