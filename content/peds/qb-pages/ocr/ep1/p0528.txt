57. In acute appendicitis, the initial pain is typically:
a. Right iliac fossa, constant
b. Central, colicky
c. Left hypochondrium
d. Epigastric burning
Appendicitis often starts as periumbilical, colicky pain before localizing to
the right lower quadrant as inflammation progresses.
