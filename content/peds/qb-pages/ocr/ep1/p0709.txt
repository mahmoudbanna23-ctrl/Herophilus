Gestational Diabetes
•Definition: Carbohydrate intolerance occurring only in pregnancy.
•Risk factors: Obesity, Black/Asian ethnicity, +ve family history.
•Effects:
• Macrosomia risk of birth trauma, obstructed labour, asphyxia.
• Congenital malformations: only slightly (unlike pre-existing DM).
• Neonatal risks: hypoglycemia, polycythemia.
•Maternal diabetes (general):
• Good preconception & antenatal control reduces morbidity/mortality.
• Fetus may be macrosomic (maternal hyperglycemia fetal hyperinsulinism) or
growth-restricted (maternal microvascular disease).
