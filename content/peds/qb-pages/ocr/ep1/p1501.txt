9. Which is characteristic of "lactose intolerance" in children?
A. It is often secondary to viral gastroenteritis
B. Always present with nausea and vomiting
C. Mucus in stools is a usual finding
D. Lactase supplements are essential in treatment
E. A congenital form is common
It often occurs secondary to viral gastroenteritis due to
temporary lactase deficiency. Symptoms like diarrhea and
bloating occur, but congenital forms are rare.
