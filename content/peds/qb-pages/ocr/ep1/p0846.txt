25. A baby is bom with cord bilirubin of 5 mg/dl. Two hours later the serum
bilirubin increased to 8 mg/dl. There is no history of premature rupture of
membrane (PROM). The most probable cause is:
a. Congenital spherocytosis
b. Sickle cell anemia
c. RH incompatibility
d. Breast milk jaundice
e. Thalassemia
Rapid rise in bilirubin within hours of life strongly suggests
hemolysis, most commonly Rh incompatibility in a sensitized
mother. This requires urgent evaluation and treatment.
