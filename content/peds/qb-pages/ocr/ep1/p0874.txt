39. Which one of the following is the most important cause of jaundice presenting
in the first 24 h of life?
a. Prematurity
c. Breastfeeding
d. Physiological jaundice
e. Early-onset sepsis
Jaundice appearing within 24 hrs is always pathologic, most often due to
hemolysis (Rh or ABO incompatibility). Physiological jaundice never appears
this early.
