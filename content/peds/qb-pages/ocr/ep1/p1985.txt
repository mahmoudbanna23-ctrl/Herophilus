(3)
(Nausea, Vomiting, Colicky abdominal pain, Pruritus, Erythema soon after a feed)
Continue breastfeeding but remove dairy from the mother's diet
(4) If the newborn is exclusively breastfed and has no symptoms of cow's milk protein
allergy
(only minimal weight loss, which is normal in the first days of life and will return to
