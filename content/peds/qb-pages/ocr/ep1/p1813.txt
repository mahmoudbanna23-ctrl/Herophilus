3. A 3-day-old baby is vomiting and has a right iliac fossa mass. The ward nurses
report that the baby has yet to pass meconium. A barium enema demonstrates
soap bubble appearance. Which is the single most likely diagnosis?
A. Anorectal malformation
B. Cystic fibrosis
C. Hirschsprung's disease
D. Intussusception
E. Jejuno-ileal atresia
Failure to pass meconium, right iliac fossa mass, and "soap
bubble" appearance on imaging indicate meconium ileus,
which is strongly associated with cystic fibrosis. Thick sticky
meconium Obstructs the distal ileum.
