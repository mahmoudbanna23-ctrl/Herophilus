30. When you are called for a child CPR, how do you open an unresponsive
victim's airway?
a. Use a one-way mouth guard
b. Tilt their head back.
c. Turn the victim on their side
d. Tilt their head back and lift the chin.
The correct method is the head tilt-chin lift (unless cervical spine injury
suspected), to Open the airway during resuscitation.
