55. A I-month-old boy has a fever of 102.7 OF (39.3 oc), is irritable, has diarrhea,
and has not been eating well. On examination, he has an immobile, dull, and red
TM that has pus behind it. Which of the following is the most appropriate course
of action?
A. Admission to the hospital with complete sepsis evaluation
B. Intramuscular ceftriaxone and close outpatient follow-up
C. Oral amoxicillin-clavulanate
D. Oral cefuroxime
E. High-dose oral amoxicillin
Infants months with fever and systemic illness require full sepsis workup and
admission. Outpatient antibiotics are not safe in this age group.
