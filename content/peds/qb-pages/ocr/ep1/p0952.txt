5. Rubella (German Measles)
•Usually mild in children, but dangerous in pregnancy (congenital rubella syndrome).
•Incubation: 15—20 days, spread via respiratory route.
•Clinical features:
• Rash: face spreads centrifugally, fades in 3—5 days.
• Prominent suboccipital/postauricular lymphadenopathy.
• Often mild or no prodrome.
•Complications: Rare — arthritis, encephalitis, thrombocytopenia, myocarditis.
•Diagnosis: Serology if risk of maternal exposure.
•Treatment: Supportive only.
•Prevention: Immunization (MMR).
