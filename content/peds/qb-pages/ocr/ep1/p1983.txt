Kawasaki Disease
Fever 2 5 days that does not respond to antipyretics
.4 of the following:
• Conjunctivitis (non-purulent)
Painless cervical lymphadenopathy
• Strawberry tongue / red cracked lips
• Red palms and soles with later desquamation (peeling)
Diagnosis: Clinical
Management: IV Immunoglobulin (WIG) + High-dose Aspirin
Scarlet Fever
Fever + Sore throat
± Sandpaper-like rash
± Painful cervical lymphadenopathy
± Tonsils covered with pale exudates
Note: NO redness of palms or soles
Cause: Group A 6-hemolytic Streptococcus
Management: Penicillin (or Erythromycin if allergic)
1983
