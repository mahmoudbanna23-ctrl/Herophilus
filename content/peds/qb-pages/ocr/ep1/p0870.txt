weighs 2.2 kg. He is admitted to the Special Care Baby Unit because of his size.

a. Anemia
b. Congenital cardiac abnormality
c. Group B streptococcus infection
d. Hypoglycemia
e. Hyperglycemia
Small-for-gestational-age infants have reduced glycogen and fat
stores, putting them at high risk Of hypoglycemia. Close
monitoring is required.
