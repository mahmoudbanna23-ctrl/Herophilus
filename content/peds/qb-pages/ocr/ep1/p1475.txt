77. A 2-month-old baby is seen in the pediatric outpatient department. She was
born at term, weighing 3.5 kg, and is breastfed. Her mother is concerned as she
has vomited some of the milk after most feeds since birth. She cries when she
vomits. She is continuing to grow along the 50th centile. What is the most
likely diagnosis?
A. Gastroesophageal reflux
B. Gastroenteritis
C. Infant colic
D. Overfeeding
E. Pyloric stenosis
Reflux is common, especially if baby is thriving and growing normally. Red
flags (projectile vomiting, poor growth) are absent.
