Fever & Rash
Maculopapular
•Viral: Roseola (HHV-6/7), enterovirus, parvovirus B19 (slapped cheek), measles, rubella.
•Bacterial: Scarlet fever, rheumatic fever, typhoid (rose spots), Lyme disease.
•Other: Kawasaki, systemic JIA.
Vesicular/Bullous/Pustular
• Viral: Varicella (chickenpox), HSV, Coxsackie (HFMD).
•Bacterial: Impetigo, staphylococcal bullous impetigo, staph. scalded skin syndrome.
•Other: Erythema multiforme, Stevens—Johnson syndrome.
Petechial/Purpuric
•Bacterial: Meningococcal sepsis, bacterial sepsis, endocarditis.
•Viral: Enteroviruses, adenovirus.
•Other: HSP, thrombocytopenia, vasculitis, malaria.
