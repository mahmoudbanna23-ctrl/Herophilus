68. A 2-year-old boy has barking cough, stridor, and intercostal recession.
What's the most likely causative organism?
A. Adenovirus
B. RSV
C. Parainfluenza virus
D. Rhinovirus
E. Influenza virus
Croup (laryngotracheobronchitis) is most commonly caused by
parainfluenza virus and presents with a barking cough and
stridor.
