2. Which ofthe following would be the single most important factor responsible
for a possible decline in the incidence of sudden infant death syndrome (SIDS)?
A. Feet to foot of cot
B. Keeping baby in parent's room until 6 months of age
C. Keeping room cool to prevent overheating
D. Parents not smoking in the same room as the infant
E. Supine sleeping
The most important factor in reducing SIDS has been the "Back to Sleep"
campaign, encouraging babies to sleep on their backs. Other measures help
but are less impactful.
