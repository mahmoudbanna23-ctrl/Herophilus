20. A 6-month-old infant has an upper-to-lower segment ratio of I. 7: l. What does
this indicate?
A. Normal
B. Long trunk
C. Short trunk
D. Disproportionate limbs
At birth, the ratio is 1.7:1, gradually decreasing with age as limbs grow faster
than the trunk. Thus, 1.7:1 at 6 months is expected and normal.
