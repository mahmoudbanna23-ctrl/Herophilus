32. A toddler presents with a 3-day history ofhigh fever. He develops a truncal
rash that appears after the fever subsides. He also experienced a simple febrile
convulsion. What is the most likely diagnosis?
a. Adenovirus
b. Enterovirus
c. Human herpesvirus 6 (HHV-6)
d. Meningococcal septicemia
e. Parvovirus Bl 9
This is consistent with roseola infantum (HHV-6), which can
also cause febrile seizures in infants and toddlers.
