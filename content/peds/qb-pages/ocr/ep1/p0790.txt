Conjugated Hyperbilirubinemia (>25 gmol/L)
Causes:
•Biliary atresia (key diagnosis to exclude; early surgery improves prognosis).
•Neonatal hepatitis syndrome.
Clues: dark urine, pale stools, hepatomegaly, poor weight gain.
