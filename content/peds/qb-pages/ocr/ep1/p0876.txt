40. A black mother is found to have glycosuria at her midwife appointment at 32
weeks' gestation. Her glucose tolerance test and fasting glucose are abnormal. She
is given dietary advice to control her blood glucose. What problem is her
newborn baby at most increased risk of?
a. Anemia
b. Hyperglycemia
c. Respiratory distress syndrome
d. Hypocalcemia
Hyperinsulinemia delays surfactant synthesis, so IDM infants have
increased risk Of RDS even at term.
