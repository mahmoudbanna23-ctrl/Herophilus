31. Which of the following is true regarding pneumococcal infection in children?
a. It is commonly found in the nasopharynx of healthy children
b. It is a viral infection with seasonal outbreaks
c. It is primarily transmitted via the fecal-oral route
d. It does not cause invasive disease in immunocompromised hosts
Streptococcus pneumoniae is often carried asymptomatically in
the nasopharynx, especially in young children, but can cause
invasive disease when immunity is low.
