Prolonged Fever & Childhood Infections
General Principles
•Most childhood infections are acute and self-limiting.
•If fever persists:
• Reassess for complications (e.g., secondary bacterial infection).
• Consider an unidentified source (e.g., UTI).
• Rule out new, unrelated febrile illness.
•Always consider Kawasaki disease in prolonged fever to prevent complications.
