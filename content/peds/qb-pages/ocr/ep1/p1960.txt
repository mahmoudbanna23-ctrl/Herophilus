Allergy prevention in infants: Early introduction of allergenic foods reduces
allergy risk; avoidance increases risk.
Physiological neonatal jaundice: Low bilirubin in well, thriving babies with
normal stools/urine requires no intervention.
Normal bowel habit in neonates: Frequent yellow semi-solid stools in
breastfed infants are normal if growth and general health are normal.
Skin-prick testing: Detects lgE-mediated sensitization; positive test shows
sensitization but requires clinical correlation.
Vaccines in immunosuppressed children: Live vaccines (e.g., BCG, MMR,
OPV) are contraindicated; inactivated vaccines are safe.
