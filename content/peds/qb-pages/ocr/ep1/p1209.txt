26. A 31 -week female infant is born via elective cesarean section for
intrauterine growth retardation and reversed end-diastolic flow; birthweight is
1010 g.
Which one of the following management options will be most effective in
decreasing the risk of developing necrotizing enterocolitis?
A. Oral antibiotics
B. Formula feeding
C. Probiotics
D. Oral immunoglobulin
Probiotics reduce the risk Of necrotizing enterocolitis in
preterm, low-birth-weight infants. Formula feeding increases
the risk.
