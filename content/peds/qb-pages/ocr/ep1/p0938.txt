Encephalitis
•Definition: Brain parenchyma inflammation (vs meningitis = meningeal).
•Causes:
• Infectious: direct viral (HSV, VZV, enteroviruses, influenza, HHV-6), post-infectious
(e.g. post-varicella), slow virus (HIV, SSPE).
• Non-infectious: metabolic, autoimmune.
•Presentation: Fever + altered consciousness ± seizures, behavioral change.
•Management:
• Always cover HSV start IV high-dose acyclovir until excluded.
• Confirm with CSF PCRfor HSV, EEG, CT/MRI (temporal lobe changes).
• Untreated HSV encephalitis >70% mortality; survivors = severe sequelae
