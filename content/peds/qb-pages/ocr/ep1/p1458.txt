69. "Corkscrew sign" on upper GI study indicates the presence of:
A. Intussusception
B. Infantile hypertrophic pyloric stenosis
C. Meconium ileus
D. Midgut volvulus
On upper Gl contrast study, a "corkscrew appearance" indicates twisted bowel
loops due to malrotation with volvulus.
