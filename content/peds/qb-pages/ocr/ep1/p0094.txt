33. A 5-year-old boy is referred by the school nurse because his height and weight
are both on the 99th centile at school entry. He appears healthy, with tall parents,
no birthmarks, and normal blood pressure. What is the most appropriate next
step?
A. Thyroid function tests
B. 9 a.m. cortisol level
D. Dietary assessment
E. Genetic studies for Prader-Willi syndrome
A child growing along the 99th centile with tall parents and no
red flags likely has familial tall stature. Calculating mid-parental
height is appropriate to assess genetic potential.
94
