Acute Gastroenteritis
•Epidemiology:
• Major cause of child mortality in LMIC.
• High morbidity in HIC; common hospital admissions <3 years.
• Rotavirus after vaccine; Norovirus now most common.
•Causes:
• Viruses: Norovirus, adenovirus, astrovirus, sapovirus, rotavirus.
• Bacteria: Campylobacter, Shigella, Salmonella, E. coli, Cholera, C. difficile.
• Parasites: Giardia, Cryptosporidium.
•Clinical: sudden watery diarrhea, 'TN stool frequency, vomiting, ± fever, ± travel/contact history.
• Severe risk: dehydration shock.
•Risk factors for dehydration: infants <6m, LBW, frequent stools (>5/24h), vomiting (>2/24h),
inability to drink, malnutrition, immunodeficiency.
•Assessment:
• Classify: No dehydration (<5% wt loss), Clinical dehydration (5—10%), Shock (>10%).
• Types: Isonatremic, hyponatremic, hypernatremic (dangerous, subtle signs, risk seizu sif
corrected too fast).
