28. Jake, a 5-year-old boy with Down syndrome, presents for a routine check-up.
He has dropped one centile in height and two centile lines in weight over the past
6 months. His mother reports increased irritability and poor appetite. Which
investigation is most likely to lead to a correct diagnosis?
A. Coeliac screen
B. Thyroid stimulating hormone (TSH)
C. Full blood count
D. MRI scan of the brain
E. Karyotype
Children with Down syndrome are at increased risk for coeliac
disease, which can present with growth faltering, irritability,
and poor appetite. A coeliac screen is warranted.
84
