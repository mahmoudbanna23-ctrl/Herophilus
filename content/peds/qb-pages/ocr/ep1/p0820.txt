12. Rebecca was born 48 hours ago at term weighing 3.2 kg. She is breathing
very quickly and not feeding. On examination, respiratory rate is 68 breaths/min,
with mild chest recession. She appears unwell. Femoral pulses are weak, and
oxygen saturation is 85% in air. What is the most likely diagnosis?
A. Pneumonia
B. Transient tachypnoea of the newborn
C. Coarctation of the aorta
D. Respiratory distress syndrome
E. Anaemia
Weak femoral pulses, hypoxia, and poor feeding in a newborn
Suggest duct-dependent congenital heart disease like
coarctation. This typically presents after ductal closure in the
first few days Of life.
