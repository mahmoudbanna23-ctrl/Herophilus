Management of Growth Faltering
Primary Care
•Most cases managed by health visitors: observe mealtimes, keep food diaries.
•Pediatric dietitian Assess intake, increase energy density (add cheese, butter, cream).
•Speech & language therapist Feeding disorders.
•Clinical psychologist / social services Support for behavioral or psychosocial issues.
Feeding & Behavioral Strategies
•3 meals + 2 snacks daily.
•Encourage variety, allow messy self-feeding.
•Limit milk, avoid excessive juice.
•Regular mealtimes, praise eating, ignore refusal, avoid conflict.
•Never force feed.
Outcome
•Weight gain with upward centile movement expected within 4—8 weeks.
•Severe cases hospital admission for active refeeding + MDT involvement.
