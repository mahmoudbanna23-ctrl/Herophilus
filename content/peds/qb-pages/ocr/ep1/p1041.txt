38. Severe viral and fungal infections are most commonly associated with
deficiency of which immune component?
a. T cells
b. B cells
c. Complement
d. Leukocytes
T-cell deficiencies impair cell-mediated immunity, making
individuals particularly vulnerable to severe viral and fungal
infections such as CMV or candidiasis.
