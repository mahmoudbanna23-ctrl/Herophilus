78. Diagnosis in a neonate with excessive salivation, respiratory distress, and
coiling NG tube:
A. Proximal esophageal atresia without fistula
B. Proximal EA with distal TEF
C. H-type TEF
D. EA with both proximal and distal TEF
E. Congenital esophageal stricture
Coiling of the NG tube in the upper pouch plus drooling and respiratory distress
strongly suggests proximal EA without distal connection.
