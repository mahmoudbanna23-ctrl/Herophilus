2. Poisoning
Assessment
•Identify agent (history, parents, symptoms).
•Consider toxicity, dose, time since ingestion.
•Investigations:
• FBC, renal/liver function as indicated.
• ECG if cardiotoxic drug suspected.
• Blood levels for paracetamol, iron, salicylates, alcohol.
• Urine tox screen not useful acutely.
Management
•Activated charcoal: within 1 h of ingestion; not effective for iron, hydrocarbons,
pesticides.
•Gastric lavage & induced vomiting: no longer recommended.
•Specific antidotes per poison information service.
•Assess circumstances to prevent recurrence.
•Psychiatric/mental health referral if deliberate self-harm.
