Kawasaki Disease
•Acute systemic vasculitis, peak age: 6 months—4 years.
•More common in Japanese and Black-Caribbean children.
•Diagnostic criteria: Fever 2 5 days + 24 of:
Bilateral non-purulent conjunctivitis.
Oral changes (cracked lips, strawberry tongue, mucosal erythema).
Cervical lymphadenopathy.
Rash (polymorphous).
Extremity changes (erythema, edema, peeling).
•Incomplete Kawasaki: common in infants months); high suspicion required.
•Complications:
Coronary artery aneurysms (T risk if diagnosis delayed).
Myocarditis, pericarditis, arrhythmias, sudden death.
• Treatment:
• IVIG within first 10 days.
Aspirin (low-dose for anti-platelet effect).
Corticosteroids / infliximab if resistant.
Echo at diagnosis + 4—6 weeks follow-up.
•Mortality: 0.5%.
