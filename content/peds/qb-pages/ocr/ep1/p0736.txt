5. During a routine newborn examination at 20 hours old, which of the following
findings requires further immediate assessment?
A. Acrocyanosis (cyanosis of hands and feet)
B. A heart murmur
C. An undescended testis
D. Breast enlargement
E. Subconjunctival haemorrhages
A heart murmur may indicate congenital heart disease. Early
evaluation, especially if symptomatic, is critical.
