15. Which of the following is the suitable time of performing Guthrie test in
Egypt?
A. Immediately after birth
B. 3-6 days after birth
C. 1-2 weeks after birth
D. 3-6 weeks after birth
Guthrie test (Neonatal blood spot screening) is done after
breastfeeding has been established (on day 5; average: 3-6
days).
