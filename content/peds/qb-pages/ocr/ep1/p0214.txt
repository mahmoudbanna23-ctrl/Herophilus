•Prolactin (from anterior pituitary) milk secretion.
•Oxytocin (posterior pituitary) milk ejection reflex ("let-down").
•Feedback inhibitor of lactation (FIL): regulates local milk production; regular emptying required.
Advantages
For Infant:
•U Gastroenteritis, respiratory' infections, otitis media.

•Long-term: obesity, diabetes, HTN; IQ (+3 points).
For Mother:
•Promotes bonding, postpartum bleeding, delays menses (birth spacing).
•U Risk of breast & ovarian cancer, postpartum depression.
•Aids weight loss, economical & convenient.
