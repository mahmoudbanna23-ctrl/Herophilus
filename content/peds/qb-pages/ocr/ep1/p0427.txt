7. Matthew is a 3-day-old term infant who has not passed meconium since birth.
On examination, his abdomen is distended, but the remainder of the examination
is normal. An x-ray of the abdomen shows distended loops throughout the bowel,
including the rectum. What is the most likely diagnosis?
A. Congenital hypothyroidism
B. Cystic fibrosis
C. Duodenal atresia
D. Hirschsprung's disease
E. Rectal atresia
Failure to pass meconium in the first 48 hours with abdominal
distension suggests Hirschsprung's disease. The rectum may
still appear full on imaging due to functional Obstruction.
