28. A term infant born by elective C-section had developed acute respiratory
distress few hours later. What is the most likely diagnosis?
A. Respiratory distress syndrome
B. Transient tachypnea of the newborn
C. Pneumothorax
D. Meconium aspiration syndrome
Transient tachypnea of the newborn occurs due to delayed
clearance of fetal lung fluid, especially after elective C-section.
