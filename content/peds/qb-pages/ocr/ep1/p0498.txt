42. In gastroesophageal reflux, which statement is TRUE?
a. it is a rare phenomenon in infants
b. The parents can be reassured, it is a process the child will outgrow as they
get older
c. Thickening the feedings does not work
d. Most infants have slow weight gain
e. Always deserves further evaluation
GER is common and physiological in infants, usually resolving spontaneously by
12—18 months. Most infants grow normally and do not require intervention unless
complications arise.
