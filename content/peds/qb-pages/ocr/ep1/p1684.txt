20. Child growth in childhood phase depends on which of the following
parameters?
B. Nutrition
C. Sex hormones
D. Thyroid hormones
The main determinants of growth in different growth phases:
•Fetal phase The fetus's genetic growth potential, maternal nutrition and the
health of the fetus, placenta and mother.
•Infantile phase Adequate nutrition, good health and normal hormone levels,
especially thyroid hormones.
•Childhood phase -5 Pituitary growth hormone (GH) secretion acting to produce
IGF-I at the epiphyses of the bones is the main determinant of a child's rate of
growth, provided there is adequate nutrition and good health.
