Post-gastroenteritis Syndrome
•After gastroenteritis, reintroduction of normal diet may cause watery diarrhea due to
temporary lactose intolerance.
•Cause: Inflammation damages intestinal microvilli reduced lactase enzyme.
•Management:
• ORS for 24 hrs, then gradual reintroduction of diet.
• Rarely, multiple dietary intolerances may require exclusion of milk, disaccharides,
and gluten.
Gastroenteritis
•Major cause of child mortality worldwide (mostly viral, sometimes bacterial:
Campylobacter, Shigella, Salmonella).
•Infants prone to dehydration.
•ORS is first-line treatment, IV fluids only for shock/vomiting/deterioration.
