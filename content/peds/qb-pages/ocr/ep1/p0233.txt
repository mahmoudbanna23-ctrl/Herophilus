Malnutrition
•Causes: Inadequate intake, malabsorption, 'TN requirements (infection, chronic disease),
restrictive diets.
•Assessment (Anthropometry):
• Weight-for-height wasting (acute malnutrition).
• MIJAC (<115 mm) severe malnutrition.
• Height-for-age stunting (chronic malnutrition).
• Skinfold thickness fat stores.
•Consequences: immunity, infection risk, delayed healing, impaired cognition.
Protein-calorie Malnutrition
•Marasmus: Severe wasting, no edema, "monkey face," thin skin, sunken eyes.
•Kwashiorkor: Edema, moon face, flaky-paint dermatitis, sparse depigmented hair,
hepatomegaly, apathy.
•Marasmic Kwashiorkor: Combination.
•WHO Management (10 steps): Treat hypoglycemia, hypothermia, dehydration (ReSoMal),
electrolytes, infection, micronutrients, cautious feeding (F-75 F-IOO or RUTF), then reha
(catch-up growth, psychosocial support).
