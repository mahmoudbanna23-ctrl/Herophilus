13. When examlning an infant, you note bilateral genu varum, widening of her
wrists, and frontal bossing. The mother states she noticed her child's bow legs
when the child started walking and it has been getting worse with time.
What additional possible clinical features would you expect in this patient?
a. Clinodactyly
b. Delayed posterior fontanelle closure
c. Harrison sulcus
d. Microcephaly
Bow legs, wrist widening, frontal bossing = rickets. Harrison
sulcus (rib cage deformity) is another expected feature.
