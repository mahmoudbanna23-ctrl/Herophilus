34. Which of the following describes type 111b intestinal atresia?
A. Two blind-ending atretic ends connected by a fibrous cord
B. Intraluminal diaphragm
C. Apple peel appearance
D. Multiple atresia
Type 111b jejunal atresia is characterized by a proximal jejunal atresia with the distal small bowel
spiraling around a marginal artery called "apple peel" deformity.
