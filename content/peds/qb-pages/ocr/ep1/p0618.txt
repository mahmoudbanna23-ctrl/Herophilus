21. A previously well 5-year-old boy is brought in by ambulance to the
emergency department with an acute onset of breathing difficulty. On arrival, he
appears unwell, pale with audible stridor, and is sitting upright unable to speak.
His temperature is 400C. What is the first priority in this child's management?
A. Lie the child down
B. Take a throat swab
C. Obtain intravenous access
D. Give oral dexamethasone
E. Summon immediate anesthetic help
This presentation suggests epiglottitis, a life-threatening airway
emergency. The first priority is to call for senior anesthetic help
urgently to secure the airway in a controlled environment.
