Viral Meningitis
•Causes: Enteroviruses, parechoviruses, EBV, adenoviruses, mumps (rare post-MMR).
•Severity: Milder than bacterial; most recover fully.
•Infants: Parechovirus may mimic sepsis.
•Diagnosis: CSF PCR (confirmatory); stool, urine, NPA, throat swab supportive
Other Causes
•Uncommon: Mycoplasma, Borrelia burgdorferi (Lyme), TB, fungi.
•Immunocompromised: atypical / opportunistic pathogens.
•Recurrent meningitis: immunodeficiency, or structural skull/meningeal defects.
•Aseptic meningitis: malignancy, autoimmune disease.
