Papular urticaria: Chronic hypersensitivity reaction to insect bites,
commonly seen in children.
Purpura in febrile child: Fever with purpura strongly suggests
meningococcal septicemia, a medical emergency.
Primary TB in children: Pulmonary TB is the most common presentation;
lymphadenitis and CNS involvement also occur.
Septic shock management: IVfluid bolus (10 mL/kg) is the first step in
children with poor perfusion and septic shock.
Developmental delay: Perinatal hypoxia with prolonged resuscitation (HIE)
is a common cause of global developmental delay.
Hemorrhagic disease of newborn: Vitamin K deficiency in breastfed in nts
without prophylaxis causes bleeding and pallor.
1956
