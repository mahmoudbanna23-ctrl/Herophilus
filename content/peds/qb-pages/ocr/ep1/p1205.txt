24. A 4-month-old infant is brought in for concerns about small size. The infant
was born at 32 weeks gestation and has been primarily formula-fed.
Which of the following is the first most important factor to consider in
managing this infant's growth?
A. Adjusting for corrected gestational age
B. Introducing solid foods early
C. Switching to a higher-calorie formula
D. Recommending breastfeeding
Premature infants' growth should be assessed using corrected
age, not chronological age, to avoid mislabeling them as small.
