33. Of the following, the MOST likely complications to expect for infants of
diabetic mothers are:
a. hyperacusis, hypercalcemia, hydronephrosis
b. hyperbilirubinemia, hypercalcemia, polydactyly
c. hyperglycemia, hypocalcemia, polysplenia
d. hypoglycemia, hypocalcemia, polycythemia
e. hypogonadism, hypocalcemia, polyuria
Hyperinsulinism causes hypoglycemia; calcium regulation is impaired, and hypoxia-
induced erythropoiesis causes polycythemia. These are the classic complications.
