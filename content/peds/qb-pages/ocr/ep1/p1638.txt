78. Infants with anorectal anomalies tend to have other congenital anomalies.
Which of the following are commonly associated?
A. Abnormalities of the cervical spine
B. Hydrocephalus
C. Duodenal atresia
D. Heart disease
Anorectal malformations are often associated with cardiac defects,
genitourinary anomalies, and vertebral abnormalities, while cervical
spine and hydrocephalus are less frequent.
