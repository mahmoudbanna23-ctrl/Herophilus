62. Philip is 4 years old and presents with a widespread, intensely itchy
vesicular rash after starting school. What is the most likely causative organism?
A. Chickenpox (varicella zoster virus)
B. Measles virus
C. Mumps virus
D. Lyme disease (Borrelia burgdorferi)
E. Chickenpox virus (varicella zoster virus)
Chickenpox, caused by varicella zoster virus, typically presents
with an itchy vesicular rash in children. It is highly contagious
and common after starting school.
