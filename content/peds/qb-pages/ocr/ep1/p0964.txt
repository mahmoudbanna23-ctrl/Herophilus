Immunodeficiency
Investigations:
•Basic: CBC with differential, immunoglobulin levels, lymphocyte subsets.
•HIV test if faltering growth/opportunistic infections.
•Functional tests: NBT (CGD), complement assays.
•Genetic testing if suspected.
Management:
•Prophylaxis: cotrimoxazole (PCP), antifungals.
•Prompt & longer antibiotic courses.
•Screening: e.g., CT chest for bronchiectasis.
•Immunoglobulin replacement (for B-cell defects).
•Bone marrow transplant (SCID, CGD).
