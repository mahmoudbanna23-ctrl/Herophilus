33. Patients with congenital diaphragmatic hemia should have:
A. Immediate postnatal surgical repair
B. Immediate face mask ventilation
C. Immediate intubation
D. Immediate ECMO
Face mask ventilation should be avoided because it worsens gastric distension
and lung compression. Intubation and gentle ventilation are the correct initial
steps before surgical repair.
