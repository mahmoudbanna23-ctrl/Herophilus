1. Influenza
•Cause: Influenza A or B, droplet spread.
•Incubation: 1—4 days.
•Risks: Severe in infants, immunodeficient, asthma, neurodevelopmental/metabolic
disease; but many deaths occur in healthy children.
•Complications: Pneumonia, myocarditis, pericarditis, encephalopathy, bacterial/fungal
superinfection.
•Treatment: Neuraminidase inhibitors (oseltamivir, zanamivir) — most effective early.
Severe cases may need intensive care.
•Prevention: Annual influenza vaccine (esp. school-aged children).
