54. A common clinical condition that mimics appendicitis in children is:
a. Mesenteric lymphadenitis
b. Pyloric stenosis
c. Intussusception
d. Peptic ulcer disease
Mesenteric lymphadenitis can cause right lower quadrant pain and
tenderness, mimicking appendicitis, especially after viral infections.
