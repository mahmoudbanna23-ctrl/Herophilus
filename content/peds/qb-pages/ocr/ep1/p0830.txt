17. A female infant born at 28 weeks is now 10 days old. She recently started
feeds via NG tube. Over the past 12 hours, she has become unwell with
abdominal distension, temperature instability, bile-stained aspirates, and blood in
stool. AXR shows air within bowel walls. What is the most likely diagnosis?
A. Sepsis
B. Intestinal obstruction
C. Necrotizing enterocolitis
D. Malrotation
E. Duodenal atresia
NEC is a serious gastrointestinal emergency in preterm infants,
presenting with feeding intolerance, distension, and bloody
stools. The pathognomonic X-ray finding is pneumatosis
intestinalis (air in the bowel wall).
