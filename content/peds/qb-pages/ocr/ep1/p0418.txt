3. Ellie is a 4-year-old girl who has been complaining of tummy pain for a month.
It is worse when she goes to the toilet; her stools are firm, and she opens her
bowels only every 2—3 days. She has not had any vomiting. For the last 2 weeks,
her stools have become loose. On examination, she has a mass in the left iliac
fossa. What is the most likely diagnosis?
A. Appendix mass
B. Constipation
C. Gastroenteritis
D. Intussusception
E. Hirschsprung's disease
Chronic constipation can lead to overflow diarrhea and a
palpable fecal mass in the left iliac fossa. Pain with defecation
and hard stools suggest functional constipation.
