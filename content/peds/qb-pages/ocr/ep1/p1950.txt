Benign toddler's diarrhea presents with loose stools but normal growth
and no systemic illness.
Growth stops later in males; puberty starts later and lasts longer, despite
males being taller at adulthood.
Breastfeeding is generally beneficial but harmful in rare genetic disorders
like galactosemia.
Lumbar puncture is contraindicated in patients with signs of raised
intracranial pressure.
Honey must be avoided in infants under 12 months to prevent botulism.
Intussusception is the most common cause of intestinal obstruction in
infants after the neonatal period.
1950
