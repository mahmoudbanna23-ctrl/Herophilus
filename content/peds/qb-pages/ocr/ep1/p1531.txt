24. How would you manage a choking child with mild airway obstruction?
A. Start early CPR
B. Give 5 abdominal thrusts
C. Do 5 back blows
D. Give 5 chest thrusts
E. Encourage cough
If a child is choking but can still cough, encourage coughing. No
back blows or abdominal thrusts are needed yet.
