The most frequent anorectal malformation in females is a
rectovestibular
fistula.
Uretero-pelvic junction obstruction can result from intrinsic narrowing or
extrinsic vessels crossing the junction.
Breast milk jaundice is unconjugated, can persist for weeks to months, but
the infant remains healthy.
To reduce SIDS risk, infants should sleep on their back on a firm mattress
with no loose bedding or objects.
Marasmus requires gradual, cautious refeeding to avoid refeeding
syndrome.
Menarche is usually the last major sign of puberty in girls, after breas and
pubic hair development.
