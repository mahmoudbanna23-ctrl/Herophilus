Status Epilepticus
•Definition: seizure min OR repeated seizures min without full recovery.
•Prevention: treat seizures >5 min early to avoid progression.
•Causes: hypoglycemia, electrolyte disturbances.
•Management (Stepwise, after ABCDE):
• O min: airway, 02, check glucose.
• 5 min: Ist-line Lorazepam IV/IO or Midazolam buccal/ Diazepam rectal.
• 15 min: repeat benzodiazepine.
• 25 min: Phenytoin IV/IO (or phenobarbital if already on phenytoin).
• 45 min: RSI with thiopental + ICU transfer.
