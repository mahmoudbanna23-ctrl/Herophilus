Rubella (German Measles)
Low-grade fever
(+) Maculopapular rash, starts on face, spreads to body, fades by day 3—5
(+) Lymphadenopathy: cervical, suboccipital, postauricular
(±) red petechiae on the soft palate
Rubella = German Measles
Note: Similar to measles but with
Enlarged lymph nodes
red petechiae on soft palate (not buccal mucosa)
1981
