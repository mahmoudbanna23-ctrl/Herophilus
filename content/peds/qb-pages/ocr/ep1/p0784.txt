Nutrition
•High requirements due to rapid growth.
•Early breast milk (even colostrum); donor milk if needed.
•Often NG/OG tube feeds.
•May need fortified breast milk or preterm formula.
•Parenteral nutrition via central lines if <lkg or very sick.
•Risks: sepsis, NEC with formula.
•Supplements: phosphate, Ca, Vit D, iron.
Fluids
•Start: 60—90 ml/kg/day to 150—180 ml/kg/day by day 5.
•Adjusted per electrolytes, urine output, weight change.
