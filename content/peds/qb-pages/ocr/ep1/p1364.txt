21. In papular urticaria, which clinical feature is most appropriate?
A. It is an immediate hypersensitivity reaction.
B. There is a rapid clinical response to antihistamines.
C. Lesions are more common on the trunk.
D. It resolves within a few hours.
E. It follows an insect bite in most cases.
Papular urticaria is a hypersensitivity reaction to insect bites, Often recurrent and
chronic in children.
