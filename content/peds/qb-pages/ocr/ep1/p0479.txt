33. A 3-week-old breastfed baby has been referred by Emergency. His parents are
very concerned because he has not opened his bowels for 3d. He has episodic
screaming episodes which occur mostly in the evenings. He is the first baby of
non-consanguineous parents. He was born at full term by emergency C-section dt
prolonged 2nd stage of labor. There was meconium stained liquor. His APGARS
were 9,10 and 10, and he was discharged home the next day. He is otherwise well
and feeding well. What is the MOST likely diagnosis?
a. Hirschsprung's disease
b- Infantile colic
c. Intussusception
d. Meckel's diverticulum
e. Pyloric stenosis
Episodic evening crying in a well-fed, healthy infant with
normal growth and no systemic symptoms is typical of colic;
Hirschsprung's would show persistent constipation and
abdominal distension.
