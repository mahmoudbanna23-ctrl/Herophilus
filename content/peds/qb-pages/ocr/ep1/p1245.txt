43. A 2-year-old girl has been ill for 2 days with fever, decreased appetite, and
a rash. On physical examination, you note ulcers on the mouth and tongue. You
also see vesicles with surrounding erythema on the palms and the dorsum of the
hands and feet. What is the most likely causative microorganism?
A. Candida albicans
B. Herpes simplex type I
C. Parvo B 19 virus
D. Varicella
E. Enterovirus
The classic presentation is oral ulcers plus vesicles on hands
and feet, most often caused by Coxsackie A virus.
