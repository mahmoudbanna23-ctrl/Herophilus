27. A 5-year-old girl is receiving induction chemotherapy for acute
lymphoblastic leukemia. Which of the following vaccines is contraindicated to
be given to her 4-month-old sibling?
A. Oral Polio Vaccine
B. Hemophilus influenzae Vaccine
C. Hepatitis B Vaccine
D. Pneumococcal Vaccine
E. Diphtheria, Pertussis, Tetanus (DPT) Vaccine
Live oral polio vaccine is contraindicated in siblings of
immunocompromised children due to risk of transmission.
