Inguinal hernia repair in infants is usually safe as a day-case procedure.
Meconium ileus in cystic fibrosis shows disparate-sized bowel loops with
small-bowel air-fluid levels and absent rectal gas.
The most common intussusception in infants is ileo-colic.
Repair of congenital diaphragmatic hernia is delayed until the infant is
stabilized to reduce mortality.
Tracheoesophageal fistula is often associated with cardiac anomalies;
echocardiography is essential before surgery
Duodenal atresia presents with double-bubble sign and is treated surgically
with duodenoduodenostomy.
1948
