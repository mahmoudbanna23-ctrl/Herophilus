24. Sophia, an 18-month-old girl, is brought to outpatients by her mother, who is
concerned as Sophia has developed breasts. She is otherwise well and growing
normally. On examination, she has breast development stage 3 (BIII), but no
pubic or axillary hair. Her bone age is 20 months. What is the most likely cause of
her early pubertal development?
A. Brain tumor
B. Congenital adrenal hyperplasia
C. Idiopathic precocious puberty
D. Premature thelarche
E. Premature puberty
Sophia has isolated breast development without pubic or
axillary hair and with normal growth and bone age. This is
characteristic Of premature thelarche, a benign and self-
limiting condition in infants and toddlers. There are no signs to
suggest true precocious puberty or hormonal pathology.
