Short Stature
•Definition:
• Height <2nd centile (or SD).
• In Egypt: <3rd centile or >2 SD below mean.
•Prevalence:
• 1 in 50 children short but often normal.
• 1 in 250 below 04th centile more likely pathological.
•Assessment:
• Compare height centile vs weight centile.
• Compare to genetic target height (MPC).
• Review growth chart, history, and exam.
•Psychosocial impact: bullying, poor self-esteem, sports disadvantage, mistaken for
younger.
