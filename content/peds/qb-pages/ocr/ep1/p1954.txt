Very low birth weight: Defined as birth weight <1500 g; extremely low
birth weight is <1000 g.
Large-for-gestational-age complications: LGA infants, often of diabetic
mothers, are prone to birth asphyxia, trauma, and shoulder dystocia.
Infant of diabetic mother: At risk for macrosomia, hypoglycemia,
hypocalcemia, and transient hypertrophic cardiomyopathy.
CMV in pregnancy: Congenital CMV infection can cause fetal microcephaly,
periventricular calcifications, and growth restriction.
Vitamin roles: Vitamin C deficiency causes bleeding and petechiae; vitamin
K is essential for coagulation, not RBC synthesis.
Biliary atresia: Persistent jaundice with
acholic stools and dark urine
suggests cholestasis from biliary atresia.
