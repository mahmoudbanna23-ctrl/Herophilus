Roseola presents with high fever that ceases before a maculopapular rash
appears.
TB meningitis shows lymphocytic pleocytosis, very high protein, and low
glucose in CSF.
SCID presents with chronic diarrhea, failure to thrive, recurrent infections,
and diffuse infiltrates on chest X-ray.
Proportionate short stature with normal bone age and growth velocity
suggests a benign variant; monitor growth every 6 months.
Growth faltering with recent stress and inadequate caloric intake is usually
nutritional rather than organic.
Severe food allergies require the child to carry an epinephrine auto-in • tor
for life-saving treatment; avoidance alone is insufficient.
