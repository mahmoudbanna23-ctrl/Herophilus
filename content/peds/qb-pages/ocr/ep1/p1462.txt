71. A 7-month-old child presents with fever of 390C for 3 days, mildly injected
pharynx, and mild diarrhea. On the fourth day of the illness, the fever ceases
and a maculopapular rash appears. What is the most likely diagnosis?
A. Measles
B. Rubella
C. Drug reaction to antipyretics
D. Roseola infantum
E. Enteroviral infection
Caused by HHV-6, it features high fever for 3—4 days, then a maculopapular
rash appears as fever resolves.
