80. Which of the following is the most common abdominal solid tumor in
infancy and childhood?
A. Neuroblastoma
B. Wilms tumor
C. Sarcoma
D. Teratoma
Neuroblastoma is the most common extracranial solid tumor in children and
the most common abdominal solid tumor in infancy and childhood. It arises
from neural crest cells of the adrenal medulla or sympathetic chain.
