Antenatal Diagnosis & Fetal Medicine
Screening tests:
•Maternal blood: group & antibodies, infections (HIV, Hep B, syphilis), NTD (MSAFP),
aneuploidy risk.
•Ultrasound: gestational age, multiples, structural malformations (CNS, cardiac, Gl,
renal, skeletal), growth, amniotic fluid.
•Advanced: CVS, amniocentesis, NIPT, fetal blood sampling, fetoscopy, MRI.
Uses:
•Early reassurance, optimal delivery planning, intervention (e.g. pleural effusion
drainage), option of termination for severe anomalies.
