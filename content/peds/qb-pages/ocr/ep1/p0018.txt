Tall Stature
•Less common than short stature.
•Usually not pathological; many cases are familial.
•Concerns: psychosocial issues (being treated as older, adolescent self-consciousness).
•Importance of diagnosis:
Screen for complications (e.g. Marfan aortic root dilatation).
Treat endocrine causes if present.
Causes
1. Genetic
•Familial tall stature (most common).
Marfan syndrome (connective tissue, long-limbed, CV risk).
Klinefelter (47,XXY) — extra SHOX gene long legs.
•Other: Homocystinuria, Beckwith-Wiedemann, Sotos syndrome.
2. Nutritional
•Obesity early rapid growth, early puberty final height not excessive.
3.Endocrine
• Precocious puberty tall in childhood, but early epiphyseal fusion final height normal/sh
• Hormonal excess: GH, thyroid, adrenal androgens, sex steroids.
'Examples: hyperthyroidism, CAH, precocious puberty, GH excess.
