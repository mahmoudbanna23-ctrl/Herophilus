49. What is the most important and effective action in neonatal resuscitation if
the baby does not start to breathe after delivery?
A. Provide oxygen.
B. Perform chest compressions.
C. Ventilate the lungs.
D. Give epinephrine.
E. Tracheal intubation.
Failure to breathe requires positive pressure ventilation, the single most
important intervention.
