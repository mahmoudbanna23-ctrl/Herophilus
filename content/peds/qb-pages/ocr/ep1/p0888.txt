46. A 12-hour newborn is noted to be jaundiced. Indirect bilirubin is 6.4 mg/dl.
What is the appropriate management?
a. Admit the baby to the incubator for phototherapy and lab investigations
b. Repeat the bilirubin measurement after another 12 hours
c. Ensure adequate breast feeding and repeat bilirubin measurement after 24 hours
d. Reassure the mother that this is normal
e. Give bottle feeds for 24 hours then resume breast feeding
As with earlier case, jaundice before 24 hours is pathologic. The
appropriate action is phototherapy with evaluation of hemolysis or
infection.
