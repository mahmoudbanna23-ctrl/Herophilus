49. A 4-year-old girl has been complaining of pain in her tummy for a month. It
is worse when she goes to the toilet; her stools are firm, and she passes stools
only once every 2—3 days. She has not had any vomiting. For the last 2 days her
stools have become loose. On examination, she has a mass in the left iliac fossa.
What is the most likely diagnosis?
A. Appendix mass
B. Constipation
C. Gastroenteritis
D. Inguinal hernia
E. Wilms tumor
Chronic constipation causes firm stools, pain, and palpable
fecal mass in the left iliac fossa. Loose stools may occur due to
overflow diarrhea.
