66. What is true about the management of gastroesophageal reflux in children?
A. Parents can be reassured; it is a process the child will outgrow as they get
older
B. Thickening the feedings does not work
C. Most infants have slow weight gain
D. It always deserves further evaluation
E. Surgery is indicated in preterm infants
Most infants with GER are "happy spitters," and reassurance is sufficient. It
usually resolves by 12—18 months.
