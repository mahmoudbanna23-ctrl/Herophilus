15. A newborn baby is delivered by caesarean following obstructed labor. He
requires full resuscitation including adrenaline. His initial capillary blood gas
shows pH 6.9. Which of the following is least likely related to perinatal asphyxia?
A. Hypotension
B. Renal failure
C. Necrotizing enterocolitis
D. Seizures
E. Heart murmur
While hypotension, renal failure, NEC, and seizures are well-
known complications Of perinatal asphyxia, a heart murmur is
not a direct consequence. It may occur coincidentally but is not
a specific outcome.
