33. Parents are concerned that their daughter may have been exposed to hepatitis A
after spending time with a grandparent recently diagnosed with the infection. What
is the typical incubation period of hepatitis A?
a. 1—7 days
b. 7-14 days
c. 7—21 days
d. 14—21 days
e. 14-28 days
Hepatitis A has an incubation period ranging from 14 to 28
days. It is transmitted via the fecal-oral route and often
presents with nonspecific symptoms before jaundice appears.
