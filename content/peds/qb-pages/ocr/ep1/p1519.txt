18. What is the most likely cause of respiratory distress in a preterm infant born
at 30 weeks of gestation?
A. Pneumothorax
B. Meconium aspiration syndrome
C. Congenital heart disease
D. Respiratory distress syndrome
E. Transient tachypnea of the newborn
Premature infants (<34 weeks) often develop respiratory
distress syndrome due to surfactant deficiency.
