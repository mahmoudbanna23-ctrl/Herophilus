26. A three-day term infant born at home, exclusively breastfed, presents with
lethargy and hematemesis. On examination, he is markedly pale but not jaundiced
and has bulging anterior fontanel. His hemoglobin is 7.2 g/dL in venous blood gas
(VBG) report. What is the most likely etiology of his illness?
A. Vitamin D deficiency
B. Vitamin K deficiency
C. Folate deficiency
D. Iron deficiency
E. Hypocalcemia
Newborns who are exclusively breastfed and did not receive
vitamin K prophylaxis can develop hemorrhagic disease of the
newborn.
