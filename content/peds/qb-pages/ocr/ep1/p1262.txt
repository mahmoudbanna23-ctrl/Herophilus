•Cephalhaematoma = subperiosteal blood collection does not cross
suture lines, localized to one cranial bone (often parietal).
•Appears hours—days after birth, soft and fluctuant, as in this case.
•Caput succedaneum crosses sutures, is present at birth, and resolves
quickly.
•Subaponeurotic hemorrhage is serious, extensive, and needs urgent care.
•CT is not routinely indicated unless neurological signs suggest underlying
trauma.
