Investigations
•Dietary history prolonged breastfeeding, poor diet.
•Bloods (typical in Vit D deficiency/calciopenic rickets):
• U/NCa, ...1, phosphate, PTH, ALP, 25-0B Vit D.
•X-ray (wrist/knee): cupping, fraying, widening of metaphysis.
Management
•Nutritional Rickets:
• Vit D supplementation + adequate dietary calcium.
• Treatment doses:
• oral Vit D 4000-6000 II-Uday.
• Or single high dose 600,000 IU (oral/lM) if poor compliance.
• Maintenance: 400 IU/day.
•Healing:
• Starts within 2—4 weeks.
• X-ray: resolution of cupping & broadening.
• Full correction of deformities may take years.
