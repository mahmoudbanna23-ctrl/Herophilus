17. An 18-month-old exclusively breastfed infant has not started standing or
walking yet. On examination, she has swelling of her wrists, an open fontanelle,
and has not yet erupted any teeth.
Which of the following is the most likely cause?
A. Vitamin A deficiency
B. Vitamin B deficiency
C. Vitamin C deficiency
D. Vitamin D deficiency
E. Vitamin E deficiency
These are classic features Of rickets due to vitamin D deficiency, common in
exclusively breastfed infants without supplementation.
