32. Which of the following statements is TRUE about Transient tachypnea of the
newborn?
a. Commonly associated with caesarian section delivery
b. Commonly associated with preterm delivery
c. Usually treated with mechanical ventilation
d. Caused by surfactant deficiency
e. Mild prenatal infection is a common association
TTN results from delayed clearance of lung fluid, more common after C-section
where labor-related catecholamine surge is absent. It is usually mild and self-
limited.
