68. In babies bom with umbilical hernia:
A. The majority need surgical correction
B. Spontaneous closure is the rule rather than the exception
C. A mesh is usually needed in surgical repair
D. None of the above
Most infantile umbilical hernias close spontaneously by 2—3 years of age.
