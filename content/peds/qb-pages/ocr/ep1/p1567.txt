42. A 5-year-old boy, at a birthday party, presented with sudden onset of edema
that started in his lips and eyelids, then spread to the dorsum of the hands and
the genitalia. What is the first possibility?
A. Nutritional anemia
B. Nephrotic syndrome
C. Hepatic insufficiency
D. Angioedema
E. Heart failure

angioedema.
