27. A mother brings in her 3-month-old girl because of repeated diarrhea and
vomiting that started the day before and is currently unable to retain anything in
her stomach. You find that the infant is severely dehydrated and feverish with
cold extremities. What is first thing you should do?
a. cold foments and antipyretic
b. anti-emetic and trial of breast feeding
c. IV line and administer normal saline
d. continue breast feeding with and give ORS
e. 1M antibiotics and oral rehydration solution
The infant is severely dehydrated and hemodynamically unstable; the priority
is rapid fluid resuscitation with IV isotonic saline to restore perfusion.
Symptomatic treatments or oral rehydration are insufficient at this Stage.
