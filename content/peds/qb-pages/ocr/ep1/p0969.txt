2. A four-year-old girl presented with high-grade fever and sore throat one day
ago, followed by a pinpoint red rash, circumoral pallor, and a white-coated
tongue. What is the most likely diagnosis?
a. Measles
b. Scarlet fever
c. Rubella
d. Chickenpox
e. Mumps
Scarlet fever presents with fever, sore throat, circumoral pallor, strawberry
tongue, and a fine, sandpaper-like rash.
