I. A healthy, full-term, breastfed infant is jaundiced at 22 hours of age. The
indirect serum bilirubin level is 13 mg/dl. The mother is blood type O, Rh
positive. Which of the following is the most appropriate next step?
A. Culture the blood
B. Determine glucose-6-phosphate dehydrogenase activity
C. Interrupt breast feeding
D. Obtain a reticulocyte count
E. Order baby blood group and Coombs test
Early jaundice within 24 hours suggests hemolytic disease (ABO incompatibility).
The most appropriate next step is to confirm with blood group and direct Coombs
test to check for maternal antibodies causing hemolysis.
