Other Respiratory Complications
•Pneumothorax: Air leak from overdistension; 02 need, breath sounds.
• Dx: Transillumination, US, or CXR.
• Tx: Needle decompression + chest drain.
•Apnoea/Bradycardia: Immature resp. control ± secondary causes (infection,
hypoglycemia, seizures, GERD).
• Tx: Stimulation, caffeine, CPAP/ventilation if severe.
Temperature Control
•Hypothermia risks: Hypoxia, hypoglycemia, poor weight gain.
•Preterm vulnerable due to: Large surface area, thin skin, little fat, poor
thermoregulation.
•Prevention: Plastic wraps, incubators, warmed humidified environment.
