45. A 12-hour-old infant who has been feeding poorly becomes tachypneic with
grunting. Which of the following initial tests has the lowest diagnostic yield?
A. Chest radiograph
B. Complete blood count
C. Urine culture
D. Blood culture
E. Glucose level
In the first 24 hours, sepsis workup includes blood culture, CBC, glucose,
and CXR, but urine culture is not useful since IJTIs rarely present this
early. It has the lowest diagnostic yield.
