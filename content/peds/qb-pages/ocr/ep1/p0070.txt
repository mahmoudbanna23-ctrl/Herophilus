21. Janine, a 9-month-old female infant, is seen by her family doctor because of
concern that she is not growing fast enough. She is on the 5th centile for height
and 2nd centile for weight. What is the greatest influence on her growth rate at
this age?
A. Genes
B. Growth hormone
C. Nutrition
D. Oestrogen
E. Testosterone
At 9 months of age, nutrition is the most important factor influencing
growth. Poor intake or malabsorption can lead to weight and height
centile drop despite normal hormones and genes.
70
