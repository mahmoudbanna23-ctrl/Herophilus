46. Which of the following is a poor tool for nutritional assessment?
a. Skin fold thickness
b. Weight for height
c. Mid-arm circumference
d. BMI
Skin fold measurements are operator-dependent and less reliable than
weight-for-height or mid-arm circumference for clinical assessment.
