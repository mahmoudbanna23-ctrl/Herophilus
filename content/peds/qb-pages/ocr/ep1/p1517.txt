17. What is the most common cause of gastroenteritis in children?
A. Shigella
B. Giardia
C. Cryptosporidium
D. Viruses
E. Campylobacter jejuni
Viruses (like rotavirus) are the most frequent cause Of
gastroenteritis in children. Bacterial causes are less common in
otherwise healthy children.
