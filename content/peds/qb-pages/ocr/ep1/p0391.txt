75. Which specialized milk formula best suits infants with cow's milk protein
allergy?
A. Lactose free formula
B. Amino acid-based formula
C. Soy protein formula
D. Formula high in omega 3 fatty acids
E. Medium-chain fatty acids formula
In cow's milk protein allergy (CMPA), the immune system reacts to the
proteins in cow's milk (casein and whey). Therefore, formulas that still
contain these proteins (even in modified form) can trigger allergic reactions.
