Specific Bacterial Infections
Haemophilus influenzae type b (Hib)
3.
•Previously common (meningitis, epiglottitis, osteomyelitis, septic arthritis).
•Now rare due to Hib vaccine.
Staphylococcal & Group A Streptococcal Infections
4.
•Mechanisms: direct invasion + toxin-mediated (superantigens cytokine storm).
•Post-strep immune sequelae: PSGN, rheumatic fever.
Scarlet Fever
•Group A strep pharyngitis + erythrogenic toxin.
•Features: pharyngitis, sandpaper rash, circumoral pallor, Pastia lines, strawberry
tongue.
•Rx: Penicillin V (10 days) prevents rheumatic fever & PSGN.
340
