Definitions
•Stunting: >2 SD (z-scores) below median height-for-age.
•Short stature: Height or length >2 SD below mean OR below the 3rd percentile for age and sex.
•Faltering growth:
• Fall in weight of 22 major centile lines, OR
• Weight centile 22 centiles below length/height, OR
• Head circumference or weight centile <2nd centile for age.
•Severe malnutrition: >3 SD (z-score -3) below median.
•Moderate malnutrition: 2—3 SD below median.
•Microcephaly: Occipitofrontal circumference >3 SD below mean.
•Small head: >2 SD below mean.
•Macrocephaly: Head circumference >98th centile (>2 SD).
•Small for gestational age (SGA): Birthweight <10th centile.
•Large for gestational age (LGA): Birthweight >90th centile.
•Overweight: BMI >85th percentile.
•Obese: BMI >95th percentile.
