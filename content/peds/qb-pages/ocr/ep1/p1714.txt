35. A 6-month-old female infant is admitted with a 4-day history of high fever
with no focus. A full septic screen is performed which yields negative cultures
and normal inflammatory markers. On day 7 of her illness she develops a
blanching morbilliform rash all over her body and her fever subsides. What is
the most likely diagnosis?
A. Measles
B. Rubella
C. Roseola infantum
D. Scarlet fever
E. Chicken pox
Note: Roseola infantum. Also known as 'sixth disease'. This is
commonly caused by HHV6 or HHV7 and is a well recognized
cause Of febrile convulsions.
