18. A 2-month-old infant was brought by his parents who were concerned about
his fever. Examination revealed fever accompanied by purpuric rash. Which of
the following would be a must to do before referral of that child?
A. Antipyretics
B. Oral antibiotics
C. Intramuscular antibiotics
D. None of the above
Note: Fever in an infant< 3 months Old + purpuric rash suspect
meningococcal septicemia and give 1M antibiotics immediately.
