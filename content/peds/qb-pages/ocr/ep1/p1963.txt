High vs. low imperforate anus: Plain abdominal X-ray with perineal marker
after 24 hours defines gas level and malformation type.
Hypospadias features: Ventral meatus, hooded foreskin, possible proximal
meatus; dorsal chordee is not a feature.
Morgagni hernia: Retrosternal anterior diaphragmatic defect through
foramina of Morgagni.
Umbilical hernia in infants: Most close spontaneously by 2—3 years; surgery
rarely needed.
"Corkscrew sign" on upper Gl: Indicates midgut volvulus due to malrotation.
Definitive diagnosis of Hirschsprung disease: Rectal biopsy showing abse
of ganglion cells; imaging/manometry are supportive.
1963
