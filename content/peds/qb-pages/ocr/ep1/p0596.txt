I O. Seb, a 2-year-old boy, suddenly develops widespread urticaria and facial
flushing at a party. Vitals are normal, and there is no respiratory compromise.
What is the most appropriate medication?
A. Intramuscular adrenaline
B. Intramuscular antihistamine
C. Intravenous hydrocortisone
D. Oral antihistamine
E. Oral corticosteroid
For urticaria without systemic involvement, oral antihistamines
are sufficient. There is no need for adrenaline or steroids if no
airway or circulatory compromise is present.
