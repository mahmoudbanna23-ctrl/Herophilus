71. Complications of cryptorchidism if untreated include all of the following
EXCEPT:
a. Future neoplasm
b. Hormonal imbalance
c. Testicular torsion
d. Risk of trauma
Untreated undescended testes increase the risk of testicular cancer,
torsion, and trauma, but hormonal imbalance does not occur because
Leydig cells produce testosterone normally.
