14. A 6-week-old baby is referred by the health visitor as she has only just
regained her birth weight. She is being fed with formula milk, and her stools are
normal. On examination, she appears thin but otherwise normal. She is admitted
to the ward and fed by the nursing staff for a week as her mother is readmitted
with an infection. She demonstrates excellent weight gain during this period.
What is the most likely cause for her initial poor weight gain?
A. Cow's milk protein intolerance
B. Gastro-oesophageal reflux
C. Cystic fibrosis
The excellent weight gain when fed by staff indicates that poor
weight gain was due to insufficient intake rather than medical
conditions like reflux, cow's milk intolerance, or CF.
D. Inadequate intake/neglect
E. Urinary tract infection
