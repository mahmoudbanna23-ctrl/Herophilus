Pediatric cardiopulmonary arrest: Respiratory failure is the most common
cause in children.
Common birth fracture: Clavicle is most frequently fractured during difficult
deliveries.
Laboratory finding in infantile rickets: Low phosphate with normal/low
calcium and high alkaline phosphatase is diagnostic.
Transient tachypnea of newborn: TTN results from delayed lung fluid
clearance, especially after C-section, resolving in 48—72 hours.
True immunization fact: MMR vaccine is live attenuated; OPV is unsafe in
immunocompromised infants.
Neonatal hypoglycemia in infants of diabetic mothers: Hyperinsulinem
causes early postnatal hypoglycemia.
