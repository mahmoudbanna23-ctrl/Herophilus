Birth Injuries
Soft tissue: caput succedaneum, cephalhaematoma, subaponeurotic haemorrhage (can
cause shock).
Nerve palsies:
•Erb's palsy (C5—6): "waiter's tip", usually recovers.
•Facial nerve palsy: pressure from forceps, usually transient.
Fractures: clavicle (most common, shoulder dystocia), humerus/femur (breech).
