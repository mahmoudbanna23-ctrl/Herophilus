Parvovirus B19
•Erythema infectiosum (slapped cheek disease).
•Complications: aplastic crisis (SCD, thalassemia), fetal hydrops/death (maternal
infection), arthritis in adults.
Enteroviruses
•Transmission: fecal-oral, respiratory.
•Usually asymptomatic or mild febrile rash illness.
•Syndromes:
• Viral meningitis (most common in high-income countries).
• Herpangina (vesicles on soft palate/uvula).
• HFMD (vesicles on hands, feet, mouth, buttocks).
• Eczema coxsackium (vesicular eruption in eczema, mimics eczema herpeticum).
• Pleurodynia (Bornholm disease): chest pain, fever.
• Myocarditis/pericarditis.
• Neonatal sepsis-like syndrome.
•Treatment: supportive (no effective antivirals).
