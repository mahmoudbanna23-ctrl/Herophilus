I. An infant missed his four-month vaccination and is now six months old. What
should be the advice to his mother?
a. Give the delayed dose now and resume the schedule in order
b. Start the schedule from the beginning
c. Skip the four-month dose and resume the rest of the schedule
d. Give the four-month dose followed a week later by the next dose
Vaccination schedules should never be restarted. Missed doses are given
as soon as possible, and the child continues with the rest of the schedule
normally.
