3. Cordelia, a 5-month-old infant, was exclusively breastfed until yesterday when
her mother introduced formula feeding. Soon after the feed, the baby developed a
rash covering her body. She has no past medical history and is not on
medications.What term best describes the rash?
A. Annular
B. Maculopapular
C. Purpuric
D. Urticarial
E. Vesicular
Urticarial rashes are raised, itchy, and often appear suddenly,
especially in allergic reactions such as cow's milk protein allergy
following formula introduction.
