28. A newborn baby boy is found to have a visible intestine emerging from his
abdomen. There is no covering to the contents. Which of the following is the
most likely diagnosis?
A. Epigastric hernia
B. Gastroschisis
C. Exomphalos
D. Paraumbilical hernia
E. Umbilical hernia
An abdominal wall defect with exposed bowel and no covering sac is
gastroschisis. Omphalocele is midline and covered by peritoneum and
amnion.
