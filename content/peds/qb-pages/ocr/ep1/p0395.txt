Acute Abdominal Pain
•Wide differential: surgical (appendicitis, intussusception, malrotation/volvulus, hernia,
trauma, Meckel diverticulum, peritonitis) and medical (gastroenteritis, UTI, DKA, IBD,
hepatitis, pancreatitis, HSP, sickle cell, pneumonia, gynecological causes).
•Appendicitis •most common surgical cause; may perforate quickly in young children.
•Intussusception 3 months—2 years, colicky pain, pallor, mass, red-currant jelly stool,
risk of shock; treat with rectal air insufflation or surgery.
•Meckel diverticulum 2% prevalence, may cause painless rectal bleeding,
intussusception, diverticulitis.
•Malrotation/volvulus -Y neonatal/infant bilious vomiting, surgical emergency due to risk
of ischemia.
