Investigations for Short Stature
Indications:
•Height <O.4th centile.
•Height << mid-parental height.
•Slow growth velocity (crossing centiles).
•Associated systemic symptoms.
Standard tests:
•FBC (anemia, chronic illness).
•Renal/liverfunction, electrolytes.
•TSH (hypothyroidism).
•Anti-TTG IgA (coeliac).
•Karyotype (e.g. Turner in girls).
•IGF-I (screen GH deficiency).
•Bone age X-ray (left hand/wrist).
