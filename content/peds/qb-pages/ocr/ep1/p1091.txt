63. Katie, age 6, has a fever and a bright red "slapped cheek" rash on both
cheeks. What is the most likely causative organism?
A. Parvovirus B19 (fifth disease)
B. Roseola infantum (sixth disease)
C. Measles virus
D. Mumps virus
E. Cytomegalovirus
Parvovirus 819 causes erythema infectiosum (fifth disease), which presents
with a "slapped cheek" rash and mild systemic symptoms. It's common in
school-aged children.
