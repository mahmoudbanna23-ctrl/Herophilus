93. A 10-year-old girl has had a rash for 4 days without other symptoms. She is
taking no medications. Physical examination reveals erythematous cheeks and a
lacy, reticulated erythema involving the extremities. Of the following, the MOST
likely causative organism is:
a. Epstein-Barr virus
b. Human Herpes viruses 6,7
c. Parvovirus B! 9
d. Rubella virus
The classic "slapped cheek" appearance and lacy rash on the extremities is
typical Of fifth disease (erythema infectiosum) caused by Parvovirus B19.
Other viruses may cause rash but not this characteristic pattern.
