95. A 4-month-old male infant has a history of failure to thrive, chronic diarrhea and
has been admitted twice to hospital with viral pneumonia since birth. There is
evidence of oral thrush. What is the most likely diagnosis?
A. Neutrophil defect (Chronic granulomatous disease)
B. B-cell defect (X-Iinked agammaglobulinemia)
C. T-cell defect (SCID)
D. Leukocyte function defect
E. Complement defects
This 4-month-old infant has failure to thrive, chronic diarrhea,
recurrent viral pneumonia, and oral thrush, which are classic signs of
severe combined immunodeficiency (SCID), a T-cell defect.
