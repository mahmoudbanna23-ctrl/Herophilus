51. Which condition presents with delayed puberty and short stature in teenage
years?
a. Constitutional growth delay
b. Familial short stature
c. Turner syndrome
d. Hypothyroidism
Teens with delayed puberty and proportionate short stature usually have
constitutional delay, often with family history.
