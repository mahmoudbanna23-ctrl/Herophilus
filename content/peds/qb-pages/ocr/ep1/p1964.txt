Roseola infantum: HHV-6 infection with high fever for 3—4 days followed by
maculopapular rash as fever resolves.
Measles: Fever for several days followed by maculopapular rash starting on
forehead, spreading downward, coinciding with fever peak.
Hand-foot-mouth disease: Vesicles on hands, feet, and mouth; caused by
enterovirus (Coxsackie A).
Persistent fever in a 2-month-old: Suggests serious bacterial infection;
requires full septic work-up and IV antibiotics.
Moderate dehydration in toddlers: ORS is first-line if extremities warm and
CRT normal; IVfluids reserved for severe dehydration or shock.
Fever of unknown origin (FUO): First step is careful history and verification of
severity/duration before investigations.
