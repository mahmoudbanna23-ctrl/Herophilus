76. A two-year-old male child is admitted with a history of fever for the last ten
days. History and clinical examination are unremarkable. What is the next most
appropriate step in managing this child?
A. Verification of the severity and duration of fever.
B. To do investigations to rule out tuberculosis.
C. Immediate hospital admission.
D. To do preliminary blood investigations.
E. Stan broad-spectrum antibiotics after blood culture.
This is fever of unknown origin (FUO). First step is careful history and
confirmation before investigations.
