64. Mohammed, age 2, has fever, cough, and crackles at the left lung base. He is
stable but not eating. What is the next best step in management?
A. Oral rehydration solution
B. Oral antibiotic
C. Intravenous immunoglobulin
D. Combined anti-tuberculosis medication
E. Antipyretic/analgesia
Crackles suggest pneumonia. Since he is stable with normal oxygen saturation
and hydration, oral antibiotics are appropriate.
