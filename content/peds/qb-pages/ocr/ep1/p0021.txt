Craniosynostosis
•Definition: premature fusion of sutures.
Types :
• Sagittal long narrow skull (most common).
• Coronal asymmetry.
• Lambdoid flattening.
•Syndromic associations: Apert, Crouzon.
•Dx: palpable ridge, skull X-ray/CT.
•Tx: surgery if ICP or cosmetic need.
