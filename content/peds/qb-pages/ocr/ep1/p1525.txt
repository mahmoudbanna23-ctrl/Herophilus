21. In neonatal resuscitation, what is the next step to do ifthe baby doesn't start
to breathe after delivery?
A. Perform chest compressions
B. Ventilate the lungs
C. Give epinephrine
D. Tracheal intubation
If a newborn isn't breathing, the first Step is to ventilate the
lungs. Oxygenation takes priority before chest compressions or
medications.
