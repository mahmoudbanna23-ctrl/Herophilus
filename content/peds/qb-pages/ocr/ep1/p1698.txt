27. Which of the following statements is hue about familial short stature?
A. It is usually associated with obesity
B. Child's height fluctuates 2 centiles between mid-parental height
C. It is a form of delay in attaining normal height
D. It leads to achieving normal height eventually
Note: In familial short stature, child's height fluctuates 2 centiles between
mid-parental height. Familial short stature is associated with being
underweight (not obesity).
