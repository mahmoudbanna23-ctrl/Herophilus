21. A 9-year-old presents with a 6-month history of non-bloody diarrhea
associated with 4 kg of weight loss. His mother says he is less energetic than
before and looks paler than normal. There is no history of foreign travel. Blood
tests show microcytic anemia (Hb 8.9 g/dL, MCV 72) but normal inflammatory
markers. What is the most likely diagnosis?
A. Crohn's disease
B. Salmonella infection
C. Coeliac disease
D. Cow's milk protein allergy
E. Ulcerative colitis
Chronic diarrhea, weight loss, pallor, and microcytic anemia
with normal inflammatory markers are classic for coeliac
disease. This autoimmune condition is triggered by gluten
intake and affects nutrient absorption.
