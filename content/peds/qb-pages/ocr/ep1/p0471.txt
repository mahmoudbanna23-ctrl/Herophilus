29. Which is not a consequence of watery diarrhea?
a. dehydration
b. Hyponatremia
c. Hypokalemia
d. Hypernatremia
e. Metabolic alkalosis
Watery diarrhea usually causes dehydration, hyponatremia, and
hypokalemia; it typically leads to metabolic acidosis due to bicarbonate
oss, not alkalosis.
