9. Bridgitta, an 8-year-old girl, presents to her family doctor with vomiting and
abdominal pain. Her vomiting only started today and she has no diarrhoea or
fever. She looks unwell and has clinical dehydration on examination and has
deep, rapid breathing. She is thirsty and pale. She has lost weight over the last few
weeks. Which of the following is the most likely cause of the vomiting in this
patient?
A. Intussusception
B. Diabetic ketoacidosis
C. Appendicitis
D. Coeliac disease
E. Pyloric stenosis
Vomiting with Kussmaul respiration, dehydration, and weight
loss in a child points to new-onset type 1 diabetes with DKA.
The absence Of diarrhea supports a metabolic cause.
