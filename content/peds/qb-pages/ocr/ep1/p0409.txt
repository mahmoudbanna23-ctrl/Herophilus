Lactose Intolerance
•Due to absent/deficient lactase enzyme.
•Symptoms: bloating, cramps, nausea, foul stools.
•Often post-infectious and temporary; rarely congenital.
•Management: dairy avoidance; lactase supplements (limited efficacy).
Other Causes of Malabsorption
•Cholestatic liver disease (U bile salts fat malabsorption).
•Lymphatic obstruction (intestinal lymphangiectasia).
•Short bowel syndrome.
•Terminal ileal resection (U bile acid, B12 absorption).
•Pancreatic insufficiency (e.g., CF).
•Small intestine mucosal disease (e.g., coeliac).
•Enzyme/transport defects (e.g., lactase deficiency, glucose-galactose malabsorption).
