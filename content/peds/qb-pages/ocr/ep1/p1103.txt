69. A 6-month-old develops a blanching rash after fever resolves. Cultures are
negative. What is the likely diagnosis?
A. Measles
B. Rubella
C. Roseola infantum
D. Scarlet fever
E. Chickenpox
Roseola (HHV-6) causes high fever for a few days followed by a
sudden rash after the fever subsides—classic in infants.
