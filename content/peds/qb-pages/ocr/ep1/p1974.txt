Management of shock in infants: IV normal saline 20 ml/kg is the first step in
resuscitating a shocked child.
Management of moderate dehydration: Oral rehydration solution is
appropriate if perfusion is maintained.
SIDS prevention: Supine sleeping is the most effective preventive measure.
