38. Which of the following is a characteristic of lactose intolerance in children?
A. It is often secondary to viral gastroenteritis
B. It always presents with nausea and vomiting
C. Mucus in stools is a usual finding
D. Lactase supplements are essential in treatment
E. A congenital form is common
Note: Lactose intolerance in children is Often secondary to viral gastroenteritis
(Temporary secondary acquired lactase deficiency).
