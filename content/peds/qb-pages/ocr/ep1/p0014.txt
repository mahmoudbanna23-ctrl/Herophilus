Nutrition
•Major influence antenatally & in the first year.
•10% of children born IUGR or very premature remain short.
•Later inadequate nutrition mainly affects weight, less effect on height.
General Health
5.
•Chronic/recurrent illness short stature + low weight (weight < height centile).
•Causes:
• Poor appetite (chronic illness).
• Increased metabolic demands (e.g. recurrent infections, CHD).
• Malabsorption (e.g. CF, coeliac).
• Electrolyte imbalance.
•Conditions: coeliac disease, Crohn's, CKD, CF, congenital heart disease, juvenile
idiopathic arthritis.
