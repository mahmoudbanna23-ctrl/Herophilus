23. Which of the following is a pathognomonic sign ofmeasles?
a. Vesicular rash at the hairline
b. Cervical lymphadenopathy
c. White spots on the buccal mucosa
d. Red strawberry tongue
e. Desquamation of the rash
Koplik spots white lesions on the buccal mucosa are
pathognomonic for measles and usually precede the
characteristic rash.
