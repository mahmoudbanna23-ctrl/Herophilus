Introduction of whole cow's milk: Formula should not be replaced with whole
cow's milk until 12 months to prevent iron deficiency.
SIDS prevention: Supine sleeping (on the back) is the most important measure
to reduce sudden infant death syndrome.
Neonatal resuscitation: Ifthe baby does not breathe after delivery, the next
step is to ventilate the lungs.
Chickenpox in immunocompromised: Varicella can cause visceral
dissemination in immunocompromised hosts; incubation is 14—16 days and the
rash is vesicular.
Mild choking in a child: If the child can still cough, simply encourage
coughing; abdominal or back thrusts are not yet needed.
