4. Which of the following is a drawback (a disadvantage) of breastfeeding?
A. It is contraindicated in certain metabolic / genetic disorders
B. It carries a high risk of developing gastroenteritis
C. It increases maternal weight gain
D. It increases the risk ofhypertension later in life
In certain rare metabolic disorders (such as galactosaemia, glucose
galactose malabsorption, certain long chain fatty acid oxidation
disorders), breastfeeding is contraindicated.
