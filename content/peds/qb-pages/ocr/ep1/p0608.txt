16. Daniel, aged 10 years, has diabetes and collapsed after playing football. What
is the most appropriate next step?
A. Check blood glucose
B. Check pupils
C. Airway opening manoeuvres
D. High-flow oxygen therapy
E. Stan chest compressions using hands-encircling method
A diabetic child who collapses may be hypoglycemic. Blood
glucose must be checked immediately as hypoglycemia is
rapidly correctable.
