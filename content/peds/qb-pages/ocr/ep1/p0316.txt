40. Developmental delay occurs in weight faltering if:
a. Any degree of faltering is present
b. Only prolonged and severe faltering is present
c. Only mild faltering is present
d. Never occurs
Mild or short-term weight faltering usually does not affect development,
whereas prolonged severe malnutrition can impair growth and cognition.
