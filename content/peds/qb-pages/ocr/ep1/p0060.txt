16. A 3-year-old boy's height has fallen from the 50th percentile to below the 3rd
percentile over the past year. What is the most likely cause?
A. Familial short stature
B. Constitutional growth delay
C. Growth hormone deficiency
D. Turner syndrome
Crossing down centiles is always abnormal. A significant drop in height (not
weight) is most suggestive of GH deficiency.
