Key rationales:
•Diphtheria, pertussis, Hib, polio, meningococcal, measles, pneumococcal, rotavirus,
HPV, influenza, hepatitis B, BCG, varicella.
•Vaccination programmes dramatically reduce disease burden.
Contraindications:
•Postpone if acutely ill (minor infections not contraindication).
•Avoid live vaccines in immunocompromised (except HIV on ART can get MMR).
•Anaphylaxis to prior dose = contraindication.
Vaccine hesitancy issues:
•Parents not seeing diseases anymore.
•Safety fears (e.g., MMR-autism false claim).
•Distrust of health system, social media misinformation.
