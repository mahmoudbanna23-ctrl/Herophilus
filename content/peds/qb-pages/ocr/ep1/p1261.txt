51. You are performing a routine discharge examination on a term baby on day
4 of life and notice a swelling on the head. The baby had been delivered by
vaginal delivery and has been well since birth. The swelling is confined to the
right parietal bone and measures approximately 4 x 4 cm. It does not cross the
suture lines; it is soft and fluctuant. Which one of the following statements is
true?
A. This is most likely a subaponeurotic haemorrhage, and the baby should
immediately be admitted to the nursery for observation.
B. The baby has caput succedaneum.
C. This swelling may be associated with an underlying skull fracture, and the
baby should have a CT scan of the head performed before discharge.
D. This is a cephalhaematoma.
