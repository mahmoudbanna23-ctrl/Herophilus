34. A 14-month-old baby girl is brought to the clinic due to concerns about her
size. She is on the 2nd centile for weight, height, and head circumference. She
was born on the 2nd centile. She is taking cow's milk, fully weaned, with normal
stools and unremarkable examination. Her parents are short and are concerned
she may also be small as she grows. What is the most likely cause ofher size?
A. Constitutionally small
B. Cow's milk protein enteropathy
C. Hypothyroidism
D. Growth hormone deficiency
E. Skeletal dysplasia
Consistent growth along low centiles since birth in a healthy
child with short parents suggests constitutional smallness
rather than pathological causes.
