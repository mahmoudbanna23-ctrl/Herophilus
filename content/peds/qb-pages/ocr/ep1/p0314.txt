39. Delaying the introduction of allergenic foods (e.g., peanuts, eggs) will:
a. Prevent food allergy
b. Delay food allergy
c. Increase risk of allergy
d. Have no effect
Early introduction of allergenic foods like peanuts and eggs reduces the risk of
developing allergies, delaying them can increase risk.
