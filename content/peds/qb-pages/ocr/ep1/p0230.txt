Hypervitaminosis D
•Definition: Vitamin D toxicity hypercalcemia.
•Symptoms: Appear months after excess dosing anorexia, irritability, vomiting,
constipation, polyuria, dehydration, hypotonia, nephrocalcinosis.
•Treatment: Low-calcium diet + corticosteroids recovery in 1 month.
