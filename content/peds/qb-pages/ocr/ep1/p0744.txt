9. A 37-week infant born to a pre-eclamptic mother with IUGR weighs 2.2 kg.
What is he most at risk ot?
A. Anaemia
B. Congenital cardiac abnormality
C. Group B streptococcus infection
D. Hypoglycaemia
E. Hypercalcaemia
IIJGR babies have reduced glycogen and fat stores,
predisposing them to hypoglycemia. Early feeding and glucose
monitoring are essential.
