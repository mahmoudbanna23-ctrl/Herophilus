Stabilization of the Preterm Infant
Ethical Issues (20—22+6 weeks)
•Parental counselling essential.
•Decide between active resuscitation vs comfort care.
Post-resuscitation Care
•Stabilize before transfer: respiratory support, prevent hypothermia & hypoglycemia.
When to Stop Resuscitation
•No breathing or cardiac output after 10 min of effective resuscitation (senior-led
decision).
