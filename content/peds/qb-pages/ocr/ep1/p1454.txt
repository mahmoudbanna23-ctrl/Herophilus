67. In retrosternal diaphragmatic hernia of Morgagni, the defect is situated:
A. Posteriorly in the diaphragm at the area of the embryonic pleuroperitoneal
canal.
B. Anteriorly through the opening for the superior epigastric artery
C. Through the esophageal hiatus at the gastroesophageal junction.
D. In the central tendon
Morgagni hernia is a retrosternal diaphragmatic defect located anteriorly,
usually through the foramina of Morgagni.
