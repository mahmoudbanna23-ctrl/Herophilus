HIV in Children
Transmission
•Mainly vertical: pregnancy, delivery, breastfeeding.
•Less common: blood products, unsafe injections, sexual abuse.
Clinical Spectrum
•Failure to thrive, chronic diarrhea.
•Recurrent/severe infections (bacterial, viral, fungal).
•Generalized lymphadenopathy, hepatosplenomegaly.
•Interstitial pneumonitis.
•Opportunistic infections: e.g., Pneumocystis jirovecii pneumonia (PCP).
•Neurological: HIV encephalopathy, developmental delay.
