45. Which of the following is an example of passive immunity?
A. Palivizumab (monoclonal antibody to RSV)
B. Diphtheria-Tetanus toxoid
C. MMR
D. Influenza vaccine
E. Hepatitis B vaccine given at birth
Passive immunity is provided by pre-formed antibodies, such as
palivizumab. Vaccines like MMR or DTaP induce active immunity.
