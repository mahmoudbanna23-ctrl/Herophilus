16. A 2-day-old female newborn presents with excessive drooling, cyanosis
during feeding attempts, and the inability to pass an orogastric tube beyond a
certain point. The diagnosis of pure esophageal atresia (Type A) is suspected.
Which of the following is NOT true in type A (pure EA)?
A. There is no TEF
B. Usually the gap between the proximal and distal esophagus is long
C. Plain abdominal x-ray shows normal gas distribution in abdomen
D. The initial treatment is gastrostomy
E. Gastric pull up or colonic interposition is the treatment of choice
In pure esophageal atresia (type A), there is no TEF, so no gas enters the
stomach. Thus, the abdomen shows absence Of gas, not normal
distribution.
