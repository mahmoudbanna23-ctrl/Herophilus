Pediatric Multisystem Inflammatory Syndrome (PIMS post—COVlD-19)
•Severe systemic inflammation following SARS-CoV-2 (3—4 weeks later).
•Features:
• Persistent fever, neutrophilia, lymphopenia, CRP.
• Multi-organ involvement, Gl symptoms, rash, conjunctivitis.
• Often mimics Kawasaki disease but in older children/adolescents.
• May progress to shock, myocarditis, coronary artery changes.
•Management: IV antibiotics (until infection excluded), IVIG, aspirin, corticosteroids.
•Often PCR negative, serology positive.
