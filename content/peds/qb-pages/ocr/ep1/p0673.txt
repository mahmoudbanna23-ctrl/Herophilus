Unfortunately, no specific product has been proven to reduce the risk of SIDS.
The child's age, gender, and exposure to tobacco smoke are the main features
that make SIDS a possible cause of death. Physical signs of any trauma make
intentional injury and infanticide the most likely causes of death, whereas
anatomic defects make cardiac or metabolic causes likely. The occurrence of a
genetic susceptibility to SIDS within a family is exceedingly rare and SIDS
cannot be the diagnosis if the patient is not an infant, that is, older than 12
months.
