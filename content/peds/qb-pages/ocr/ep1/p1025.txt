30. A 7-month-old child presents with 3 days of fever, mildly injected pharynx,
and mild diarrhea. On the fourth day, the fever subsides and a maculopapular rash
appears. What is the most likely diagnosis?
a. Measles
b. Rubella
c. Drug reaction to antipyretics
d. HHV-6 infection
e. Enteroviral infection
This is classic for roseola infantum (exanthem subitum), caused
by HHV-6, where rash appears as fever resolves.
