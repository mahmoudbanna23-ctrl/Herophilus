Disability — Disordered Consciousness
•Assessment during primary survey:
• Level of consciousness: AVPU (Alert, Voice, Pain, Unresponsive) or Glasgow Coma Scale
(GCS, child-adapted).
• Posture: hypotonia, decorticate, decerebrate.
• Pupils: size/reactivity (diagnostic clues).
•Key points:
• Reduced consciousness —Y airway instability.
• Reversible causes: hypoglycemia, opiate toxicity.
• Serious causes: seizures, sepsis, meningitis, encephalitis, raised [CP.
•Neuroprotective strategies (t 'CP):
• Head midline, 20—300 up.
• Avoid hypoxia/hypercapnia; maintain normocapnia (PaC02 4.5—5.3).
• Restrict fluids to isotonic.
• Intubation/ventilation if GCS <9.
• Osmotic diuretics (mannitol).
• Maintain BP for cerebral perfusion.
