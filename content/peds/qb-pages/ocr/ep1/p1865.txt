26. A 3-day-old male newborn is brought to the pediatric clinic by concerned
parents because his big brother has Hirschsprung disease. The infant was born
full-term via a normal vaginal delivery. In Hirschsprung's disease the most
common clinical presentation is:
A. Flat abdomen
B. Non bilious vomiting
C. Delay passage Of meconium
D. Bleeding per rectum
E. Frequent bowel motion
The most common presentation of Hirschsprung disease is
delayed passage of meconium (>48 hours after birth). Vomiting
and distension often follow.
