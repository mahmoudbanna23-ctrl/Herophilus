14. Which of the following lesions in the newborn resolve spontaneously?
A. Meningocele
B. Umbilical hernia
C. Inguinal hernia
D. Cataract
Umbilical hernias in infants often close spontaneously within a few
years. Inguinal hernias and cataracts do not resolve without surgery.
