36. What is a pathognomonic sign of measles?
a. A vesicular rash at the hairline
b. Cervical lymphadenopathy
c. White spots on the buccal mucosa
d. Red strawberry tongue
e. Desquamation of the rash
Koplik spots (tiny white lesions on the buccal mucosa)are
pathognomonic for measles and typically appear before the
skin rash.
