Stabilization of the Preterm Infant
Cord Clamping
•Delay cord clamping if no immediate resuscitation needed.
•Benefits: Hb, transfusion need, NEC, IVH.
Prevention of Hypothermia
•Preterm infants highly prone to hypothermia.
•<32 weeks: place in plastic bag/wrap (except face) without drying radiant heater ±
thermal mattress.
•Use warmed humidified gases if possible.
Oxygenation
•Avoid hyperoxia (risk: brain, lung, eye injury).
•Use 21—30% 02 initially with blender.
•Target preductal Sp02 '95%.
•<32 weeks: consider CPAP to avoid intubation.
•Very premature: may need early surfactant.
