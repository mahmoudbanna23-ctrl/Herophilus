42. Which requires further immediate investigation in a newborn?
a. heart murmur
b. undescended testes
c. subconjunctival hemorrhage
d. breast enlargement
Unlike findings such as subconjunctival hemorrhage or breast enlargement, a
murmur may indicate serious congenital heart disease.
