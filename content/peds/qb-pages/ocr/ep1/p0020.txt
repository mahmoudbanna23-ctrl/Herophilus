Macrocephaly
•Definition: OFC > 98th centile.
•Causes:
• Familial (normal child).
• Raised ICP (hydrocephalus, subdural hematoma, brain tumor).
• Neurofibromatosis, mucopolysaccharidoses (Hurler).
• Sotos syndrome (cerebral gigantism).
Asymmetric Head
•Plagiocephaly: positional, parallelogram-shaped, improves with mobility.
•Brachycephaly: flat occiput, common with supine sleeping (SIDS prevention).
•Preterm infants: long, flat heads from incubator positioning.
