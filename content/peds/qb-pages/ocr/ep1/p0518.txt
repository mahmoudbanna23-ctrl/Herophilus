52. A 6-year-old boy presents with joint pain, rash and severe abdominal pain.
From mother's description, the rash has appeared in crops on his legs over the
past week. What is the MOST likely diagnosis?
a. Gastric bleed
b. Gastric ulcer
c. Henoch Schonlein purpura
d. Systemic lupus erythematosus
e. Varicella zoster (chicken pox)
HSP is a small-vessel vasculitis presenting with palpable purpura,
pain, and arthralgia, often following upper respiratory infections.
