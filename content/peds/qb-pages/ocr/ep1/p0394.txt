Gastrointestinal Disorders in Children
•Pyloric stenosis -Y projectile vomiting in early infancy.
•Gastroenteritis (acute) major global cause of under-5 mortality.
•Chronic nonspecific diarrhea benign, common in toddlers.
•Coeliac disease malabsorption, growth faltering.
•Lactose intolerance * diarrhea, bloating after dairy.
•Constipation (acute/chronic) very common; may require long-term management.
•Hirschsprung disease neonatal obstruction, constipation.
•Inflammatory bowel disease * increasing in children, but bowel cancer is rare.
