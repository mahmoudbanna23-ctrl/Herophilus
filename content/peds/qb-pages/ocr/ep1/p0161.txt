65. Which is a characteristic of constitutional growth delay?
A. Adequate bone age
B. More common in girls
C. Anal height is affected
D. A delayed onset of puberty
E. Presentation is in infancy
Children with constitutional growth delay have delayed bone age and puberty,
but eventually reach normal adult height. It is more common in boys.
