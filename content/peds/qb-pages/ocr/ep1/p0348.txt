Nutritional rickets from vitamin D deficiency commonly presents with hypocalcemia
and hypophosphatemia, which leads to secondary hyperparathyroidism and
elevated alkaline phosphatase. Clinical signs include bowing of legs, wrist
enlargement, frontal bossing, and hypotonia.
