• Treatment:
• Immediate IV antibiotics (don't delay).
• Add dexamethasone to reduce deafness risk.
•Complications: hearing loss, cranial nerve palsies, seizures/epilepsy, hydrocephalus,
subdural effusion, cerebral abscess.
•Prophylaxis:
• Household contacts of meningococcal cases ciprofloxacin.
• Vaccinate close contacts (MenACWY if appropriate).
