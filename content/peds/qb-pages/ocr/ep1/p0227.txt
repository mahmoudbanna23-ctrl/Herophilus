Limbs
•Wrist & ankle widening (metaphyseal expansion).
•Bowed legs (genu varum) after weight-bearing.
•Pathological fractures.
•Hypotonia, poor growth.
Symptoms
•Early: sweating, irritability, insomnia.
•Late: delayed motor milestones, delayed teeth eruption.
•Severe: seizures (hypocalcemia).
