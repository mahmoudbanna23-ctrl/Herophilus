59. A 3-year-old girl was at a birthday party. She suddenly developed swollen
cheeks and lips and a widespread urticarial rash. She is rushed to the nearby
general practice surgery, where it is noted that her breathing is very noisy. She is
distressed and frightened. On auscultation, she has widespread wheeze. Which
medication would you give first?
A. Intramuscular antihistamine
B. Intravenous hydrocortisone
C. Oral antihistamine
D. Oral corticosteroid
First-line treatment for anaphylaxis is 1M adrenaline.
Antihistamines and steroids are supportive but not lifesaving.
E. Intramuscular adrenaline
