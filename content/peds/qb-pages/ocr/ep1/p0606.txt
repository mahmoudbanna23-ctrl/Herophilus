15. Ahmed, a 2-month-old baby, is pale and floppy in his cot. Paramedics are
providing bag-valve-mask ventilation; chest is moving, but the heart rate is 40
bpm. What is the next step?
A. Check blood glucose
B. Check pupils
C. Airway opening manoeuvres
D. High-flow oxygen therapy
E. Start chest compressions using hands-encircling method
A heart rate below 60 bpm with signs of poor perfusion in an
infant indicates the need for chest compressions. The encircling
hands technique is preferred in infants.
