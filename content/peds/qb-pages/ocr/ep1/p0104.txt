38. You are examining the growth chart of an individual who presented as a
teenager complaining of short stature and delayed puberty. He has had X-rays for
bone age assessment done twice at the age of 5 and at the age of 11 years and
both were somewhat delayed. Finally, he ended with a height that is compatible
with his mid-parental height. Which type of short stature did he suffer from?
a. Genetic short stature
b. Growth hormone deficiency
c. Chromosomal disorder
Delayed bone age and puberty with eventual normal adult
height is characteristic of constitutional growth delay.
d. Constitutional short stature
e. Nutritional short stature
