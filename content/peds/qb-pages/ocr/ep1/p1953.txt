SIDS prevention: Supine sleeping ("Back to Sleep") is the single most
important factor in reducing SIDS incidence.
Constitutional growth delay: Characterized by delayed bone age and
puberty but normal adult height; more common in boys.
Uncompensated shock: Hypotension is a sign of decompensated shock;
earlier signs include tachycardia and prolonged capillary refill.
Puberty in girls: The first sign of normal puberty is breast budding
(thelarche), occurring around age 9—11.
Faltering growth: Most commonly occurs in infancy due to
nutritional and
feeding problems.
Lambdoid craniosynostosis: Fusion causes posterior plagiocephaly wi a
flat occiput and asymmetric skull. ( Flattening of the skull )
