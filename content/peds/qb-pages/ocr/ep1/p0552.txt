1. Accidents
Choking
•Toddlers prone due to small airway + mouthing behavior.
•Food = most common cause, then toys.
•Management:
• Mild obstruction encourage cough.
• Severe obstruction:
• Child: 5 abdominal thrusts (Heimlich).
• Infant: 5 back blows + 5 chest thrusts (no abdominal thrusts).
• Unconscious CPR.
Suffocation
•Plastic bags/packaging.
