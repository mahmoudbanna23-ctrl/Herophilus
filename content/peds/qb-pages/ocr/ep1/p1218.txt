30. At which of these ages is the biochemical screening test (Guthrie test) done
in Egypt?
A. At birth
B. 3-6 days
C. 12 days
D. 3 weeks
E. 3 months
The Guthrie test (metabolic screening) in Egypt is performed between 3—
6 days after birth to allow metabolites to accumulate.
