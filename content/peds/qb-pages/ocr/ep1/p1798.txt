77. Most common type of congenital esophageal anomaly:
A. Atresia with fistula into upper pouch
B. Atresia with fistula into lower pouch
C. Fistula into both pouches
D. Simple atresia without fistula
E. Fistula without atresia
About 85% of EA cases have a distal tracheoesophageal fistula, making it
the most common type. Other forms like isolated EA or H-type are less
frequent.
