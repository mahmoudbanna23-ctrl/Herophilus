Stabilization of Sick/Preterm Infant (<34 wks or ill newborns)
•Assess & support: Airway, breathing, circulation, temperature, nutrition.
•Monitoring: HR, RR, BP, 02 sat (91—95% preterm; >95% term), blood gases, glucose,
electrolytes, weight.
•Interventions: IV lines, umbilical catheters, antibiotics, X-rays, parenteral nutrition,
incubator/warmer.
•Handling: Minimal, gentle, provide analgesia.
•Parents: Encourage early bonding (kangaroo care, expressed breast milk).
Preterm Infant — Clinical Course
•Appearance & prognosis depend on gestational age.
•Extremely preterm (23—27 wks): thin skin, fused eyelids, absent suck/swallow, needs
resp. & nutritional support.
•Term infant (37—42 wks): mature skin, coordinated feeding, alert interaction.
•Problems with increasing gestation.
