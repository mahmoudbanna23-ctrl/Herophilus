73. A two-month-old male infant presents with history of fever for 6 days. No
respiratory or GI symptoms and no improvement with symptomatic treatment.
On examination, the child is sick looking and lethargic. What is the best next
step in his management?
A. Admit the child for more close examination and investigations in a
controlled setting
B. Send the preliminary investigations and continue symptomatic treatment
C. Full septic work up and start broad spectrum IV antibiotics
D. Hydrate the infant and observe for fever
Any febrile infant under 3 months requires a full septic screen
and empirical IV antibiotics regardless Of appearance.
