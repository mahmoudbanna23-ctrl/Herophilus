51. Which of the following vaccines does not help prevent pneumonia?
a. Pneumococcal vaccine
b. Varicella vaccine
c. Haemophilus influenzae type B vaccine
d. Measles vaccine
e. Rotavirus vaccine
Rotavirus vaccine prevents viral gastroenteritis, not
pneumonia. The other listed vaccines (e.g., pneumococcal, Hib,
measles, varicella) can prevent pneumonia.
