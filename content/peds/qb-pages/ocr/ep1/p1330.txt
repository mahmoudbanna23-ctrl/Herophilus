4. A I -year-old girl is brought to the emergency room with a history of persistent
vomiting and loose, watery stools.
Which of the following would be a sign of uncompensated shock?
A. Capillary refill time greater than 3 seconds
B. Heart rate greater than 120 beats/min
C. Cold extremities
D. Respiratory rate greater than 40 breaths/min
E. Low blood pressure
In compensated shock, blood pressure is maintained by tachycardia and
vasoconstriction. Hypotension indicates progression to uncompensated
(decompensated) shock.
