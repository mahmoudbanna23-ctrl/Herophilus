vitamin D deficiency rickets.
Functional abdominal pain: Chronic abdominal pain without red flags and
normal growth is managed with reassurance.
Candida/ nappy rash: Rash in skin folds with satellite lesions and oral
thrush is treated with topical antifungals.
Referred abdominal pain in children: Right lower lobe pneumonia can
present as abdominal pain mimicking acute abdomen.
