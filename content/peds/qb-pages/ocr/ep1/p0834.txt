19. A term infant born at home is brought on day 4 with bleeding from the
umbilical stump. The mother mentions her other children received vitamin K, but
she's unsure about this baby. Which blood result is likely to be abnormal?
A. Platelets
B. Prothrombin time (P T)
C. Activated partial thromboplastin time (APTT)
D. Hemoglobin
E. Antiplatelet antibodies
Vitamin K deficiency bleeding (VKDB) commonly presents
around day 4 with bleeding (e.g. from the umbilical stump).
The PT is typically prolonged due to deficient clotting factor
production.
