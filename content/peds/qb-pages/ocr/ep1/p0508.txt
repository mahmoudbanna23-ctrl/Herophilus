47. Features of gastro-esophageal reflux disease in infants do NOT include the
following:
a. Excessive crymg
b. Poor weight gain
c. Vomiting
d. Anemia, malnutrition and apnea may occur
e. Hemoptysis
GERD in infants can cause vomiting, poor weight gain, apnea, or anemia,
but coughing up blood is not a typical feature.
