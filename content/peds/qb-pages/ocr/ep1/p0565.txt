Pediatric Emergencies
Primary Survey (Initial Assessment)
3.
•Airway: look/listen/feel, obstruction, positioning, suction, foreign body removal.
•Breathing: RR, effort, stridor/wheeze, air entry, Sp02, cyanosis.
•Circulation: HR, pulses, BP, capillary refill, skin color/temp.
•Disability: consciousness, pupils, posture, seizures.
•Exposure: temperature, rashes, trauma, scars, MedicAlert bracelet.
4.
Common Presentations
•Respiratory distress: croup, asthma, bronchiolitis, pneumonia, pneumothorax.
•Shock: hypovolemia, sepsis, anaphylaxis, arrhythmias, trauma.
•Neurological: seizures (status epilepticus), meningitis, head injury, metabolic issues.
•Surgical: appendicitis, peritonitis, intussusception, malrotation, obstruction.
•Metabolic: DKA, hypoglycemia, electrolyte disturbances.
