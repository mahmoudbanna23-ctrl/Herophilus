24. A 9-year-old boy presents with a 24-hour history of abdominal pain, initially
around the umbilicus and now localized to the right iliac fossa. His mother says
he has not eaten today and has been listless. He finds it difficult to stand up
straight. On examination, he is tender and guarding on palpation. What is the next
step in management?
A. Referral to surgical team
B. Abdominal radiograph
C. Intravenous antibiotics
D. Full septic screen
The clinical picture is highly suggestive of appendicitis, with
localized tenderness, guarding, and inability to stand upright.
Urgent surgical evaluation is the appropriate next step.
E. Admit to the ward for review on the ward round
