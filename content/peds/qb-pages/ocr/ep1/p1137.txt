86. A 7-year-old girl has had 5 days of fever, conjunctivitis, erythema of the
oropharynx, and a generalized maculopapular rash. Her lips, lymph nodes, and
extremities appear normal. Which of the following laboratory tests would NOT
be used to establish a diagnosis?
A. Complete blood cell count (CBC)
B. Urinalysis (UA)
C. C-reactive protein (CRP)
D. Erythrocyte sedimentation rate (ESR)
E. Antinuclear antibody (ANA)
Kawasaki disease is diagnosed with CBC, ESR, CRP, UA (sterile pyuria) but
ANA is not useful. ANA is for autoimmune disease, not Kawasaki.
