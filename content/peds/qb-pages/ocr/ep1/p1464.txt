72. A 9-year-old girl presented with fever that increased gradually to 400C over
4 days. Then a maculopapular rash appeared on the forehead and spread to the
trunk and extremities over 3 days. When the rash had appeared on the feet, the
temperature dropped abruptly. What is the most probable diagnosis?
A. Measles
B. Rubella
C. Varicella
D. Scarlet fever
E. Roseola infantum
Measles presents with fever followed by a maculopapular rash starting at the
forehead, spreading downward. Rash coincides with fever peak.
