71. A healthy newborn starts spitting after the first feed. NG tube cannot be
passed. X-ray shows a dilated stomach. What is the likely diagnosis?
A. Hypertrophic pyloric stenosis
B. Esophageal atresia with distal fistula
C. Proximal fistula with distal fistula
D. Meconium ileus
Failure to pass an NG tube and proximal gastric dilation
indicate esophageal atresia, not pyloric stenosis or meconium
I euS.
