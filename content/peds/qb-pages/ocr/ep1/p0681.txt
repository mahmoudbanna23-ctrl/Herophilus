53. The investigation of an unexpected infant death includes a history, a postmortem
examination, and which of the following?
A. DNA studies
B. Maternal drug screen
C. Analysis of parental electrocardiograms
D. A death scene investigation
E. Stool studies
Standard SIDS investigation includes history, autopsy, and examination of the
death scene. This helps rule out neglect, trauma, or environmental hazards.
