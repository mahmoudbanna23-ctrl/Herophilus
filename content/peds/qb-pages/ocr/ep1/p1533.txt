25. Which of the following is true about "capillary refill time"?
A. Apply blanching pressure for 2 seconds
B. Prolonged capillary refill if > 3 seconds
C. It is affected by body exposure to a cold environment
D. It is reproducible only in children over the age of 2 years
CRT is prolonged (>3 sec) in poor perfusion and can be affected
by cold environments. It is measured by blanching pressure for
