77. Regarding congenital diaphragmatic hernia (CDH), which of the following
is TRUE?
A. Chest tube should be placed
B. Baby should be ventilated with bag-mask ventilation
C. Nasogastric tube should be placed
D. Ventilation with high-frequency oscillation is contraindicated
Preoperative management includes decompressing the stomach with an
NG tube. Bag-mask ventilation and routine chest tube placement are
avoided, and high-frequency ventilation may sometimes be used.
