15. A 4-year-old child presents with vomiting and diarrhea after eating shellfish
for the first time. Which of the following findings would most likely indicate a
food allergy rather than food intolerance?
A. Elevated blood glucose
B. Presence of eosinophils in stool
C. Positive skin prick test
D. Elevated serum amylase
E. Negative lgE levels
Food allergy is lgE-mediated and confirmed with positive allergy testing,
unlike intolerance which does not involve the immune system.
