Food Allergy
•Common: infants egg, milk, nuts; older children peanut, tree nuts, fish, shellfish,
sesame.
•Primary: reaction on first exposure.
•Secondary (cross-reactivity): e.g. pollen—food syndrome (birch pollen e apple).
•Clinical types:
• lgE-mediated: immediate (urticaria, swelling, anaphylaxis).
• Non-lgE: delayed Gl symptoms, proctitis, FPIES.
•Intolerance (non-immune): e.g. lactose intolerance after gastroenteritis.
Management:
•Avoid allergen (dietitian input).
•Some proteins (milk, egg) tolerated if baked/processed
•Mild reactions antihistamines.
•Severe 1M adrenaline (autoinjector).
•Immunotherapy: experimental, peanut product FDA-approved (US), not routine in
•Prognosis: milk/egg may resolve; nut/seafood lifelong.
