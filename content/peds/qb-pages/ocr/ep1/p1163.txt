3. Which vitamin is often deficient in breastfed infants?
A. Vitamin A
B. Vitamin B12
C. Vitamin C
D. Vitamin D
E. Vitamin E
Exclusively breastfed infants Often develop vitamin D deficiency, since
breast milk is low in vitamin D. This can lead to rickets if not supplemented.
