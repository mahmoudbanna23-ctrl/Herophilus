35. Which of following is most common cause of pediatric cardiopulmonary
arrest?
a. cardiac arrhythmias
b. metabolic anomalies
c. overwhelming infections
d. trauma
e. respiratory problem
In children, cardiac arrest usually follows prolonged hypoxia
and respiratory failure, not primary cardiac disease. This makes
early recognition and correction of respiratory issues critical.
