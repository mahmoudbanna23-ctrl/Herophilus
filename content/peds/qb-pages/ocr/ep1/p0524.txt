55. Surgical management of gastroesophageal reflux in infants is indicated:
a. In all cases after 6 months of age
b. Only in severe cases not responding to medical therapy
c. As soon as reflux is diagnosed
d. In every child with vomiting
Surgery is reserved for complications or failure of conservative
management, not all infants with reflux.
