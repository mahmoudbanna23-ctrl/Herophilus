31. A I -year-old presenting to the ER with abdominal colic and vomiting. X ray
of the abdomen shows distended small bowel and absence of air in the distal
colon and rectum, what is the diagnosis?
a. intussusception
b. Hirschsprung disease
c. Incarcerated hernia
d. Peptic ulcer disease
e. Fecal impaction
The X-ray findings Of distended small bowel with absence Of air in the distal
colon and rectum in a I-year-old with acute abdominal colic and vomiting
strongly Suggest a mechanical small bowel Obstruction, which is classic for
intussusception in this age group
