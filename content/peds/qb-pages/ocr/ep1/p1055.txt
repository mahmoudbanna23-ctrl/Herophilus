45. A 14-year-old boy develops a morbilliform rash after being prescribed
amoxicillin for an upper respiratory tract infection. What is the most likely cause
of the initial infection?
a. Glandular fever
b. Measles
c. Parvovirus
d. Rhlnovlrus
e. Streptococcus A
Amoxicillin in the setting of infectious mononucleosis (EBV)
often leads to a characteristic non-allergic, morbilliform rash.
This is a diagnostic clue for glandular fever.
