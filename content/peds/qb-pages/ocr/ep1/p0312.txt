38. Whole pasteurized cow's milk can be given as the main drink:
a. With iron supplements before 1 year
b. Safely after 1 year
c. At 6 months
d. From birth
Before 1 year, cow's milk can lead to iron deficiency and renal solute
overload. Whole milk is safe after 12 months.
