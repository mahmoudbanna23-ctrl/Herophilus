Features of Neonatal Medicine
•Prematurity: Survival improved (even at 23—28 wks) but risk of short/long-term
complications.
•Jaundice: Common; risk is kernicterus aim prevent high bilirubin.
•Respiratory distress: Needs urgent assessment & possible support.
•Infection: Newborns highly susceptible.
•Hypoxic-Ischaemic Encephalopathy: Major cause of death & disability.
•Global trends: Neonatal mortality reduced with improved obstetric, maternal, public
health & intensive care.
