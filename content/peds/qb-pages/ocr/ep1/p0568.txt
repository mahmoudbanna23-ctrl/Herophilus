Dehydration
9.
•Causes: gastroenteritis, burns, sepsis, DKA, Dl, nephrotic syndrome.
•Risk higher in infants (T body water, metabolic rate, surface area).
• Classification:
• None (<5% loss): well, alert, normal exam.
• Clinical dehydration (5—9%): lethargy, sunken eyes, dry mucosa, tachycardia,
delayed refill.
• Shock (210%): weak pulses, hypotension, very sunken eyes, mottled cold skin,
consciousness.
•Management:
• Oral/NG fluids if mild/moderate.
• IV fluids if severe/shock.
