32. Plain x-ray abdomen standing in case of jejunal atresia may show:
A. Cobra head sign
B. String sign
C. Air fluid levels
D. Shoulder sign
Jejunal atresia causes proximal bowel obstruction, so a plain X-ray in standing
position shows multiple air—fluid levels. Signs like cobra head or string sign are
associated with urinary tract/Gl conditions (not jejunal atresia).
