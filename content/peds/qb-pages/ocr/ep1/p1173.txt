8. A 36-week gestation infant is delivered via cesarean section because of
macrosomia and fetal distress. The mother has class D pregestational diabetes
(insulin dependent, with vascular disease); her hemoglobin Alc is 15% (normal
7%). This infant is at risk for which of the following?
A. Congenital dislocated hip
B. Polycythemia
C. Hyperglycemia
D. Pneumothorax
Infants of diabetic mothers often develop polycythemia due to intrauterine
hypoxia. They are prone to hypoglycemia, not hyperglycemia, after birth.
