27. Eleanor, a 7-year-old girl, is referred for short stature. She is on the 5th centile
for height and 25th centile for weight. Nine months ago, her height and weight
were on the 15th centile. Her mother notes that she has become lethargic, her
school performance has worsened, and she refuses to participate in sports. Which
investigation is most likely to lead to a correct diagnosis?
A. Coeliac screen
B. Thyroid stimulating hormone (TSH)
C. Full blood count
D. MRI scan of the brain
E. Karyotype
Falling height with preserved or increased weight, lethargy, and
cognitive decline are classic for hypothyroidism. TSH testing will
help confirm the diagnosis.
