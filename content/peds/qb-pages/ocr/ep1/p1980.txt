Measles (Rubeola)
Fever, irritability
Rash (often itchy) -Y begins on face/neck, then spreads to body
(+) Koplik spots red with white center "grains of salt" on buccal mucosa
(+) URTI symptoms -Y runny nose, sore throat (coryza)
No cervical lymphadenopathy
-5 Measles = Rubeola
Mnemonic (Measles "K"): Koplik spots, Cough, Coryza, Conjunctivitis
Management
Mainly supportive (Paracetamol/lbuprofen for pain and fever)
Notifiable disease
If not listed Reassurance (no antibiotics or steroids; resolves spontaneously within a
week)
