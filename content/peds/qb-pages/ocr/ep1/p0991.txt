13. An 18-month-old child presents with 4 days of fever and coryza. The fever has
now subsided, and a maculopapular rash has appeared. What is the most likely
cause?
a. HHV-6
b. Measles
c. Rubella
d. Parvovirus B 19
The most likely cause is HHV-6, which causes roseola infantum (also known as
exanthema subitum or sixth disease). This condition is characterized by high fever
for several days, followed by a rash that appears suddenly as the fever breaks. The
rash is typically maculopapular and appears on the trunk, face, and limbs
