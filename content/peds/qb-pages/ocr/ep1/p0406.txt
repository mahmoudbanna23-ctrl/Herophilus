Acute Gastroenteritis
•Investigations: usually none. Stool culture if septic, blood/mucus,
immunocompromised, prolonged diarrhea, recent travel.
•Management:
• No dehydration: prevent continue breastfeeding, oral fluids, ORS if risk.
• Dehydration: oral rehydration solution (ORS cornerstone, small frequent sips or
via NG).
• Shock: IVfluids (0.9% saline ± 5% glucose), careful with hypernatremia (correct
slowly <0.5 mmol/L/hr).
• Drugs: No antidiarrheals/antiemetics. Antibiotics only if specific indication (sepsis,
extraintestinal infection, infant <6m with Salmonella, immunocompromised, C. diff,
cholera, shigella).
