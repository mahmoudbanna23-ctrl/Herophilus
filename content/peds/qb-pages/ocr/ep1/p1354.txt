16. A 2-year-old girl is brought to the clinic for a routine check-up. Her mother
states that she does not like to eat but prefers eating only a few spoons of pasta
each day.What is the best method to check her nutritional status?
A. Head circumference
B. Height
C. Weight
D. Weight-for-height
E. Skinfold thickness
Weight-for-height assesses acute malnutrition and is the best parameter for
nutritional status in toddlers.
