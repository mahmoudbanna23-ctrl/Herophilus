73. True about Esophageal Atresia (EA) EXCEPT:
A. Primary repair isn't always possible
B. Associated with anomalies in 5%
C. Semi-sitting position is a good nursing position
D. Failure of passage ofNG tube
Esophageal atresia is associated with other anomalies in about 50% of cases, not
5%. Primary repair may not always be possible, NG tube fails to pass, and semi-
sitting position is recommended for feeding.
