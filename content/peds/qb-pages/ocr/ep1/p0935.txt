Bacterial Meningitis
•Mortality: 5—10%; survivors neurological impairment.
•Pathophysiology: inflammation cerebral edema, raised ICP, cerebral blood flow,
hydrocephalus, vasculopathy, infarcts.
•Organisms by age:
• <3 months: GBS, E. coli, Listeria.
• 3 months: Neisseria meningitidis, S. pneumoniae.
Investigations
•Lumbar puncture (LP) — unless contraindicated.
•Blood cultures, PCR for bacteria/viruses.
•Imaging if signs of TICP.
CSF findings:
•Bacterial: turbid, T WBC (polymorphs), •frprotein, Uglucose.
•Viral: clear, lymphocytosis, normal glucose.
