Accidents & Poisoning in Children and Young People
General Features
•Accidental injury most common reason for emergency care in children.
•Poisoning:
• Young children accidental.
• Adolescents/adults often deliberate self-harm.
•Classified as external causes of morbidity & mortality (require environmental factor).
•Leading cause of death worldwide (1—15 yrs), but in UK now 2nd after malignancy.
•Still most common cause of death in 15—19 yrs
