Recurrent Abdominal Pain
•Defined as 24 episodes/month for 22 months.
•Mostly functional (90%):
• Irritable bowel syndrome (IBS) pain linked to stooling, altered
frequency/consistency.
• Abdominal migraine *episodic severe midline pain ± nausea, vomiting, headache,
photophobia.
• Functional dyspepsia -Y postprandial fullness, epigastric pain.
•Organic (<IO%): constipation, coeliac disease, IBD, peptic ulcer, pancreatitis, urinary
tract disease, gallstones.
•Red flags: persistent localized pain, nocturnal pain, vomiting, Gl bleeding, weight loss,
growth faltering, perianal disease, delayed puberty.
