Developmental Dysplasia of the Hip (DDH)
•Examination: infant relaxed.
• Barlow manoeuvre: adduct & push down femoral head dislocates.
• Ortolani manoeuvre: abduct + upward lift 'clunk' = hip relocates.
•Risk factors:
• Female (6x risk)
• Family history (20%)
• Breech (30%)
• Neuromuscular disorders
•Investigations:
• Clinical exam may miss mild cases.
• Hip ultrasound if abnormal exam, risk factors, or foot deformities.
•Management: early splinting in abduction long-term morbidity.
