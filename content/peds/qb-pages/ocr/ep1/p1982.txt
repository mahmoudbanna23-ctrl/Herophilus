Roseola (Roseola Infantum)
Sudden high fever followed by non-itchy rash on chest or legs, then spreads to body
Causative agent: Human Herpes Virus 6 (HHV-6)
Erythema Infectiosum (Fifth Disease — Parvovirus B19)
"Slapped cheek" appearance bright red rash on both cheeks
May spread to body, may be itchy (especially on soles of feet)
Hand, Foot, and Mouth Disease (Coxsackie Virus)
Painful ulcers on tongue
Grey blisters on hands and feet
Management (for all viral rash illnesses):
Supportive care + Reassurance
