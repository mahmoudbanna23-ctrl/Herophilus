Definition of preterm newborn: Preterm is defined as birth before 37
completed weeks of gestation.
Cause of edema in kwashiorkor: Hypoalbuminemia from protein deficiency
reduces oncotic pressure, leading to edema.
Feature of marasmus: Marasmus presents with severe wasting without
edema, unlike kwashiorkor.
Congenital infection signs: TORCH infections (CMV, rubella)
cause
hepatosplenomegaly, "blueberry muffin" rash, cataracts, and hearing loss.
Neonatal resuscitation: Positive pressure ventilation is the most important
intervention if a newborn fails to breathe.
