48. Unmodified cow's milk in infancy is:
a. Suitable from birth
b. Unsuitable
c. Safe at 3 months
d. Recommended after 6 months
Cow's milk is not recommended in the first year due to risk Of iron
deficiency, high renal solute load, and protein excess.
