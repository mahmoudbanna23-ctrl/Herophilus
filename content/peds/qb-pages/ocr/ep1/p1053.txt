44. Which of the following infections is typically associated with orchitis?
a. Chickenpox
b. Measles
c. Mumps
d. Rubella
e. Scarlet fever
Mumps virus can lead to orchitis, particularly in post-puberta
males, and may cause testicular pain and swelling. It can rarely
lead to infertility if bilateral.
