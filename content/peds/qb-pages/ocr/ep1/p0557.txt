I. A 14-year-old girl self-presents to the emergency department with a history of
deliberate self-poisoning. She agrees to blood tests which reveal high paracetamol
levels necessitating treatment.
Which is the appropriate treatment for this teenager?
A. Intravenous desferrioxamine
B. Intravenous N-acetylcysteine
C. Active cooling
D. Alkalinization of urine with bicarbonate
E. Activated charcoal
N-acetylcysteine (NAC) is the antidote for paracetamol
overdose. It acts to replenish glutathione and prevent hepatic
damage. Activated charcoal is only useful within 1 hour Of
ingestion and is not a substitute for NAC.
