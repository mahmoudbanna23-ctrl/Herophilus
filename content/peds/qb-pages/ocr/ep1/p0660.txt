43. A term female infant is born vaginally after an uncomplicated pregnancy. She
appears normal but has respiratory distress when she stops crying. When crying
she is pink; when not she makes vigorous respiratory efforts but becomes dusky.
Which of the following is the likely explanation for her symptoms?
A. Choanal atresia
B. Diaphragmatic hernia
C. Meconium aspiration
D. Neonatal narcosis
Newborns are obligate nasal breathers. In bilateral choanal atresia, they
turn pink while crying (mouth breathing) but become cyanotic and
distressed when quiet (trying to breathe through blocked nares).
