Rickets
Definition
•Rickets = failure of mineralization at growth plates in growing bone.
•Osteomalacia failure of mineralization in mature bone.
Causes of Rickets
Nutritional (most common, esp. LMlCs)
•U Sunlight exposure (northern latitudes, covered clothing).
•Dark skin.
•Exclusive prolonged breastfeeding (low Vit D, Ca, phosphate).
•Vegan / macrobiotic diets, unsupervised dairy-free diets.
•Prematurity phosphate deficiency.
•Prolonged parenteral nutrition.
