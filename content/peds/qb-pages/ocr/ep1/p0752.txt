13. A 2-month-old ex-preterm has a growing soft red mass on her forehead, first
noted at 3 weeks. What is the diagnosis?
A. Bruising
B. Milia
C. Erythema toxicum
D. Port-wine stain
E. Strawberry naevus (cavernous haemangioma)
Strawberry haemangiomas appear in the first few weeks of life
and grow rapidly. Most resolve spontaneously by age 5—7.
