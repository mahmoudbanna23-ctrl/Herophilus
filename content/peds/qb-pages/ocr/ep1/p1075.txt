Emergency Department with a headache. A lumbar puncture is performed with
the following CSF result:
250 RBCs/mm3 neutrophils/mm3 250 lymphocytes/mm3
Protein: 0.6 g/L CSF glucose: 2.1 mmol/L Blood glucose: 7.2 mmol/L
What is the most likely diagnosis?
A. Bacterial meningitis
B.
Blood-stained tap
The high neutrophil count and low CSF glucose relative to
blood glucose indicate bacterial meningitis, which typically
presents with a neutrophilic CSF response and
hypoglycorrhachia.
