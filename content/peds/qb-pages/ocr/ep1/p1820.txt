Congenital diaphragmatic hernia (CDH) causes severe respiratory distress at
birth due to pulmonary hypoplasia and pulmonary hypertension, not just
compression. The first step is cardiopulmonary stabilization with gentle
ventilation using low tidal volumes to avoid barotrauma. Definitive surgical
repair of the diaphragm is done after stabilization, not immediately.
