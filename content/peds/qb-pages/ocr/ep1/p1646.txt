I. Which of the following types of food is contraindicated and has to be
avoided in a child under one year of age?
A. Eggs
B. Honey
C. Peanut
D. All of the above
Foods high in salt and sugar should be avoided and honey must not be given
until 1 year of age because of risk of infantile botulism.
