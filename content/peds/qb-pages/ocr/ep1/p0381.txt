This 9-month-old with convulsions, large head, open fontanelles, and broad
wrists has vitamin D deficiency rickets.
•Vitamin D deficiency calcium absorption hypocalcemia.
•Secondary hyperparathyroidism phosphate excretion
hypophosphatemia.
•Alkaline phosphatase due to active osteoblasts trying to mineralize
bone.
Classic labs in nutritional rickets = low calcium + low phosphate + high ALP.
