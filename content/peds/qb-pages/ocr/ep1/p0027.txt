Indications for Investigation of Short Stature
•Growth velocity >1 SD below mean.
•Height >3 SD below mean.
•Height >2 SD below mean with delayed puberty or other abnormalities.
Causes
•Most common cause of faltering growth: Inadequate calorie intake.
•Most common endocrinal cause of short stature: Hypothyroidism.
•Pattern:
• All endocrinopathies proportionate short stature.
• Exception: Hypothyroidism disproportionate short stature.
