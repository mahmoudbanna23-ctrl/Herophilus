13. Breastfeeding is best for babies, and there are very few contraindications.
Which of the following is an absolute contraindication to breastfeeding in the
A. Galactosaemia
B. Phenylketonuria
C. Neonatal jaundice
D. Cleft palate
E. ABO incompatibility
Classic galactosaemia is the only absolute contraindication to
breastfeeding because affected infants cannot metabolize galactose,
which can lead to severe complications.
