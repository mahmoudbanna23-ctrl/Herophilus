38. When evaluating a child for stunting, which growth chart will you use?
A. Height-for-age charts
B. Weight-for-age charts
C. Weight-for-length charts
D. Body mass index charts
E. Mid-arm circumference measurements
Stunting is defined as low height-for-age -2 SD), reflecting chronic
malnutrition. Weight-for-height is for wasting (acute malnutrition).
