Major Medical Problems of Preterm Infants
•Respiratory: RDS, pneumothorax, apnoea/bradycardia.
•Cardiac: PDA, hypotension.
•Metabolic: Hypoglycemia, hypocalcemia, electrolyte imbalance, osteopenia.
•Nutrition: Feeding difficulty, growth failure.
•Infection & jaundice.
•Neurological: IVH, periventricular leukomalacia.
NEC
•Eye: Retinopathy of prematurity.
•Other: Anaemia, BPD, hernias.
