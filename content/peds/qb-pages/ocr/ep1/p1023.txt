29. Which of the following is NOT a live attenuated vaccine?
a. Varicella
b. Rubella
c. Inactivated influenza
d. MMR
Inactivated influenza vaccine is a killed-virus vaccine, unlike
varicella, rubella, and MMR which are live attenuated.
