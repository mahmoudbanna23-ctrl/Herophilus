75. Concerning the rectal biopsy for the diagnosis of Hirschsprung's disease,
which of the following is true?
A. A full-thickness biopsy is preferred
B. The absence of ganglion cells in the dilated bowel confirms the diagnosis
C. The biopsy should be done at the level of the dentate line
D. Rectal suction biopsy has a specificity of 100%
Diagnosis requires histology showing absence of ganglion cells, best
confirmed with a full-thickness rectal biopsy.
