30. Which of the following is not consistent with Hirschsprung's disease?
a. No passage of meconium after 24 hours of birth
b. Abdominal distension
c. Bile-stained vomiting
d. Refractory constipation
e. Treatment is by repeated finger dilatation
Hirschsprung's disease is managed surgically (pull-through procedure), not
by repeated digital dilatation. Other options reflect common clinical
features.
