Breastfeeding
•Natural & recommended food for all infants.
•Exclusive breastfeeding for the first 6 months (WHO).
•Protects against infections, reduces child mortality, improves survival in low-income
countries.
•Higher uptake in educated/professional mothers, but gap is narrowing.
Stages of Breast Milk
1.Colostrum (first 2—3 days) yellow, rich in protein, zinc, vitamin A, immunoglobulins.
2. Transitional milk (day 2—2 weeks).
3.Mature milk (after 2 weeks) balanced composition; fat concentration rises during
feeds.
Immunological Protection
•IgA coats intestinal mucosa.
•Human milk oligosaccharides act as prebiotics.
•WBCs, lysozyme, lactoferrin kill pathogens
