73. In patients with neuroblastoma, which of the following prognostic factors is
associated with decreased survival?
A. Patient age less than one year
B. N-myc amplification
C. Thorax as the primary site
D. Nodular, well-differentiated histology pattern
Amplification of N-myc is associated with aggressive tumor behavior and
lower survival, whereas age <1 year, thoracic site, or well-differentiated
histology generally indicate better prognosis.
