Large-for-Gestational-Age (LGA, >90th centile)
•Causes: maternal diabetes, genetic syndromes (e.g. Beckwith-Wiedemann).
•Risks:
• Birth trauma (shoulder dystocia, brachial plexus injury).
• Birth asphyxia.
• Hypoglycemia (hyperinsulinism).
• Hypocalcemia, polycythemia, respiratory problems (e.g. large tongue).
