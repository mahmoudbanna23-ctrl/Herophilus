9. A 6-year-old child is brought to the resuscitation room after a 15-minute
generalized seizure. Buccal midazolam was given 5 minutes ago. Airway and
oxygen are managed; capillary refill is <2 sec and heart rate is 120 bpm. What is
the next most appropriate step?
A. Administer another anticonvulsant
B. Check blood glucose
C. Gain IV access
D. Request senior review
E. Start bag-valve-mask ventilation
Hypoglycemia must be ruled out in any child with ongoing
seizure or reduced consciousness. It is a rapidly reversible
cause of altered mental status.
