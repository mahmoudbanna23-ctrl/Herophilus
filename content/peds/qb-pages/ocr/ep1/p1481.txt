80. Which one of the following is the most important cause of jaundice
presenting in the first 24 hours of life?
A. Prematurity
B. Hemolysis
C. Breastfeeding
D. Physiological jaundice
E. Early-onset sepsis
Early jaundice (<24h) is always pathological, most often due to
hemolysis (e.g., Rh or ABO incompatibility).
