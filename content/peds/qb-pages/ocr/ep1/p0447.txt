17. A 5-week-old baby boy is brought to the Emergency Department with a 48-
hour history of projectile vomiting. The infant is hungry after vomiting and has
not opened his bowels in 3 days. Clinical examination reveals a mass in the left
upper quadrant. A blood gas is performed: pH 7.50, pC02 5.5 kPa, and
bicarbonate 30 mmol/L. What does this blood gas represent?
A. Normal findings
B. Metabolic acidosis
C. Metabolic alkalosis
D. Respiratory acidosis
E. Respiratory alkalosis
Projectile vomiting leads to loss of gastric hydrochloric acid,
resulting in metabolic alkalosis. The high PH, elevated
bicarbonate, and clinical signs support this acid-base
imbalance.
