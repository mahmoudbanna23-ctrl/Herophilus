16. Which of the following does not cause acute wheezing?
a. Pertussis
b. Asthma
c. RSV bronchiolitis
d. Foreign body aspiration
e. Viral URTI
Pertussis causes paroxysmal coughing with inspiratory whoop, but not
wheezing. Asthma, bronchiolitis, viral URTI, and foreign body aspiration
all cause wheezing.
