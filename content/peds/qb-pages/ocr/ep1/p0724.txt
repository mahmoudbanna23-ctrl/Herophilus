Vitamin K Deficiency Bleeding (VKDB)
0.6/100,000.
•Incidence:
. Types:
• Early: first week.
• Late: 1—8 weeks.
•Clinical: bruising, Gl bleed, prolonged umbilical bleeding; some intracranial
hemorrhage.
•Causes:
• Exclusive breastfeeding (breast milk low in Vit K).
• Maternal anticonvulsants.
• Neonatal liver disease.
•Prevention:
• Universal Vit K prophylaxis:
• 1M injection at birth (preferred).
• Oral: 3 doses over 4 weeks (less reliable).
• Mothers on anticonvulsants antenatal Vit K + neonatal 1M dose.
