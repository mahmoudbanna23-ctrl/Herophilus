80. Which statements are true regarding EA?
A. Most diagnosed after first week
B. Excessive salivation
C. Feeding causes choking
D. B and C are true
Excessive salivation and choking with feeds are classic signs of EA.
Diagnosis is often made in the first days of life, not usually after a week.
