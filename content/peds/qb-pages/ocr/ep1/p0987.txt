I l. A 9-year-old girl had 4 days of fever, then a rash appeared starting from the
forehead and spread downward. Fever subsided as rash reached feet. Most
probable diagnosis?
a. Measles
b. Rubella
c. Varicella
d. Scarlet fever
e. Roseola infantum
Measles has the described pattern: high fever followed by descending rash and
fever resolution.
