Causes of Short Stature
1. Familial Short Stature
• Parents short, child within expected MPC range.
• Normal growth velocity, normal puberty.
2. Constitutional Delay of Growth & Puberty (CDGP)
• More common in boys.
'Delayed puberty & bone age.
• Family history of delayed puberty.
'Final height normal.
Treatment: Sex hormones may help.
GH useless.
