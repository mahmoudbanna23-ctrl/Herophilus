23. What is the most common clinical presentation of primary TB in children?
A. TB lymphadenitis
B. Osteoarticular TB
C. Genitourinary TB
D. TB meningitis
E. Pulmonary TB
Pulmonary disease is the most common presentation in children, though lymphadenitis and CNS
involvement also occur.
