Congenital Infections (TORCH)
•Definition: Infections acquired in utero via placenta, usually maternal primary infection.
•Major pathogens: Rubella, CMV, Toxoplasma, Parvovirus, Varicella, Syphilis.
•General features: IUGR, eye defects (cataracts, retinitis), hepatosplenomegaly,
jaundice/hepatitis, pneumonitis, intracranial calcifications, hydrocephalus/microcephaly,
anemia, rash ("blueberry muffin").
Rubella
•Risk depends on gestation at infection:
• wks deafness, congenital heart disease, cataracts (80%).
• 13—16 wks hearing loss.
• 18 wks minimal risk.
• Diagnosis: maternal serology, PCR (fetal/placenta/infant).
• Prevention: MMR vaccine (rare in UK with high uptake).
