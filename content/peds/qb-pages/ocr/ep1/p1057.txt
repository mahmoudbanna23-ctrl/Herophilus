46. You are asked to see a I-week-old baby with irritability, high-pitched cry, and
a bulging fontanelle. Meningitis is suspected. Which microorganism is the most
likely cause in this age group?
a. Group B Streptococcus
b. Haemophilus influenzae type B
c. Mycobacterium tuberculosis
d. Neisseria meningitidis
e. Staphylococcus aureus
Group B Streptococcus is the leading cause Of early neonatal
meningitis. It's acquired during birth and presents within the
first week Of life.
