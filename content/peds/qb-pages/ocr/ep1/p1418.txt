48. A 3-week-old baby is seen in the outpatient clinic for evaluation of frequent
bowel motions. He passes yellowish, semi-solid stools 8—10 times per day with no
blood or mucus. The baby is exclusively breastfed. On examination, the baby
appears well, active, and gaining weight. What is the most likely diagnosis?
A. Acute gastroenteritis
B. Lactose intolerance
C. Cow milk protein allergy
D. Normal bowel habit
E. Inflammatory bowel disease
Exclusively breastfed infants often pass many yellow, semi-solid stools
daily. Since the baby is gaining weight and well, this is normal.
