Weaning
•WHO: Exclusive breastfeeding for 6 months prevents infections.
•After 6 months: Breast milk alone inadequate (energy, vitamins, iron).
•In high-income countries: solids introduced at 4—6 months.
•Start gradually with pureed foods (fruit, veg, rice).
•Avoid foods high in salt/sugar; honey not before 1 year (botulism risk).
•Early introduction of allergens (egg, peanut) 4 months in high-risk infants may reduce
allergy risk.
