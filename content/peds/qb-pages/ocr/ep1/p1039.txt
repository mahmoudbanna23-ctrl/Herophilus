37. Which of the following is a known complication of the pertussis vaccine?
a. Convulsions
b. Metabolic acidosis
c. Hemolytic anemia
One known complication of the whole-cell pertussis vaccine is
febrile convulsions. Though rare, these are transient and not a
contraindication to future vaccination.
