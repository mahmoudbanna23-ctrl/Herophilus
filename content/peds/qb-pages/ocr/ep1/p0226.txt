Clinical Features
Head
•Delayed fontanelle closure (>18 months).
•Frontal/parietal bossing.
•Craniotabes ("ping-pong ball" skull).
•Delayed teeth eruption, enamel hypoplasia.
Chest
•Pigeon chest (pectus carinatum).
•Rachitic rosary (enlarged costochondral junctions).
•Harrison sulcus (diaphragm pull on softened ribs).
•Recurrent chest infections, cardiomyopathy.
Abdomen
•Abdominal distension (visceroptosis).
