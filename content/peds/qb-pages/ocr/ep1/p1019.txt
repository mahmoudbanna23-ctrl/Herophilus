27. A 14-year-old boy presents with fever, sore throat, and fatigue. Examination
reveals exudative pharyngitis. Which test is most helpful for confirming the
diagnosis?
a. Throat swab for Influenza A
b. HIV antibody test
c. Throat swab for Neisseria gonorrhoeae
d. Serology for Epstein-Barr virus (EBV)
These symptoms suggest infectious mononucleosis. EBV
serology or a Monospot test confirms the diagnosis.
