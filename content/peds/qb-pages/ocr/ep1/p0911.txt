I. Jonathan, aged 6 years, is brought to the Emergency Department after
becoming unwell at a family party. He is only able to say one word at a time and
indicates that he is struggling to breathe. He appears very anxious and has a raised
itchy rash spreading from his face down to his chest. This has never happened
before. His mother says he has asthma and uses a salbutamol inhaler for wheezy
episodes. What is the most likely diagnosis?
A. Acute asthma
B. Allergic reaction
C. Anaphylaxis
D. Idiopathic urticaria
E. Inhaled foreign body
Jonathan has multi-system involvement—respiratory distress,
anxiety, and an urticarial rash—following likely allergen
exposure, consistent with anaphylaxis. His underlying asthma
also increases the risk of severe respiratory compromise in
anaphylactic reactions.
911
