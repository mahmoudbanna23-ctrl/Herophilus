19. Meckel's diverticulum most commonly presents as:
A. gastrointestinal bleeding
B. intestinal obstruction
C. diverticulitis
D. intermittent abdominal pain
E. acute peritonitis
Meckel's diverticulum most commonly presents with painless
lower Gl bleeding due to ectopic gastric mucosa secreting acid.
Obstruction and pain are less common.
