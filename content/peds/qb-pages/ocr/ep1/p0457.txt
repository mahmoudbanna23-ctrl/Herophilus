22. An 8-year-old girl presents with a 6-month history of pain most days around
the umbilicus, lasting for an hour and responding to paracetamol syrup. It does
not usually occur at weekends. She opens her bowels daily and passes a soft stool.
She is thriving, with her weight and height on the 75th centile. Which of the
following is the appropriate next step?
A. Abdominal radiograph
B. Referral to a paediatric gastroenterologist
C. Inflammatory markers and liver function tests
D. Reassurance and no further investigation
E. Prescription for laxatives
Chronic periumbilical pain in a thriving
child with normal bowel habits and no
red flags suggests functional abdominal
pain. It is common and does not require
further investigation if there are no
alarm features.
