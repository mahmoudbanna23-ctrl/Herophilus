Immunodeficiency
• Types:
• Primary (genetic, rare) — >250 disorders.
• Secondary (common) — malnutrition, HIV, burns, splenectomy, immunosuppressive
drugs.
Clues to Primary Immunodeficiency (SPUR):
•Severe infections.
•Persistent infections (don't resolve).
•Unusual pathogens (e.g., PCP, Burkholderia).
•Recurrent infections.
•Family history, faltering growth, chronic diarrhea, severe eczema.
Types & presentations:
•T-cell defects: viral/fungal infections, failure to thrive.
•B-cell defects: recurrent bacterial (ENT, chest, skin).
•Neutrophil defects: abscesses, poor wound healing, invasive fungi.
•Complement defects: meningococcal, pneumococcal infections.
