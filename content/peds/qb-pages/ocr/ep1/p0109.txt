40. What is the most likely cause of faltering growth in a 9-month-old infant?
a. Small atrial septal defect (ASD)
b. Persistent diarrhea
c. Physiological gastroesophageal reflux
d. Frequent minor upper respiratory infections
Chronic or frequent diarrhea leads to poor nutrient absorption and
weight/height faltering; minor infections or reflux are less impactful.
