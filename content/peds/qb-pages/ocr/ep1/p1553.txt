35. Which of the following is a diagnostic sign of uncompensated shock?
A. Capillary refilling time equals 2 seconds
B. Heart rate greater than 120 beats/min
C. Cold extremities
D. Respiratory rate greater than 40 breaths/min
E. Low blood pressure
Low blood pressure is a late and definitive sign Of uncompensated
(decompensated) shock, showing that the body's compensatory mechanisms
have failed and organs are under-perfused. Other signs like rapid heart rate, fast
breathing, or cold extremities indicate early or compensated shock, but only
hypotension confirms the uncompensated Stage.
