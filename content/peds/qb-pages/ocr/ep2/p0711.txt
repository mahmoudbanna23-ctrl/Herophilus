41. Which of the following is NOT true about innocent (physiologic) heart
murmurs?
a. May be diastolic
b. Usually soft
c. Occur in an otherwise healthy child
d. Vary with position or respiration
Innocent murmurs are always systolic, soft, vary with position,
and occur in otherwise healthy children.
