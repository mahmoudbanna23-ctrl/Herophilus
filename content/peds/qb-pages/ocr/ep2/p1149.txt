Prolonged Neonatal Jaundice
•Physiological jaundice resolves by 2 weeks (term) / 3 weeks (preterm).
•Persistent jaundice investigate (esp. conjugated >25 gmol/L).
Unconjugated causes:
•Breast milk jaundice, infection (UTI), hemolysis (e.g. G6PD), hypothyroidism, high Gl
obstruction, Crigler—Najjar.
Conjugated causes:
•Biliary atresia, choledochal cyst, neonatal hepatitis, congenital infection.
•Inborn errors: galactosemia, tyrosinemia, al-antitrypsin deficiency, bile acid synthesis
errors, CF, PFIC, Alagille syndrome, intestinal failure—associated cholestasis.
•Always check for pale stools biliary obstruction.
