Management:
1. Bronchodilator Therapy:
•SABA (salbutamol, terbutaline): Rescue inhaler, rapid onset
•LABA (salmeterol, formoterol): Only with inhaled corticosteroid, not for acute attacks
•Ipratropium bromide: Adjunct in severe cases
2. Preventer Therapy:
•Inhaled corticosteroids ('CS): Reduce inflammation, minimal side effects at low dose
•Add-ons: Leukotriene receptor antagonists, theophylline, anti-lgE (omalizumab), anti-lL-5
(mepolizumab)
3.
Stepwise Management:
•Step up if symptoms uncontrolled; step down when well-controlled
•Goals: No symptoms, no activity limitation, normal lung function, no exacerbations
4. Non-pharmacological Measures:
•Allergen avoidance (dust-mite impermeable bedding)
•Allergen immunotherapy (limited, risk of systemic reactions)
•Avoid second-hand smoke and e-cigarette vapor
Special Considerations:
5.
•MART therapy: Combined ICS + LABA for maintenance and reliever
•Severe asthma may require oral steroids or biologics
