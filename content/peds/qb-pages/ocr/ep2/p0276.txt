5. Which of the following RBC indices is MOST helpful in the diagnosis of
hereditary spherocytosis?
A. RBC count
B. MCV
C. MCH
D. MCHC
E. RDW
In hereditary spherocytosis, MCHC (mean corpuscular
hemoglobin concentration) is elevated due to reduced
membrane surface area and cell dehydration.
