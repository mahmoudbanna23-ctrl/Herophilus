25. A 6-year-old boy is diagnosed with nephrotic syndrome and admitted to start
steroid therapy and be monitored for complications.
Which of the following complications is he unlikely to be at risk ot?
A. Bacterial infection
B. Hypovolaemia
C. Reduced glucose tolerance
D. Pleural effusion
E. Pulmonary embolus
Nephrotic syndrome is associated with complications such as
hypovolemia, infections, thromboembolism, and effusions due
to massive protein loss. Reduced glucose tolerance is not
typically a complication unless high-dose corticosteroids are
used for prolonged periods.
