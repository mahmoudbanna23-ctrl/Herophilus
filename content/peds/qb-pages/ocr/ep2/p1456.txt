12. What is the preferred (most common) method of treatment for
hydrocephalus?
A. Oral diuretics to relieve raised intracranial pressure
B. Oral carbonic anhydrase inhibitors
C. Insertion of ventriculoperitoneal shunt
D. Ventriculo-cardiac (right atrium) shunt
Ventriculoperitoneal shunt insertion is the mainstay treatment
for hydrocephalus. Occasionally, endoscopic third
ventriculostomy is performed.
