examination, there is mucosal pallor, yellow sclerae, and hepatosplenomegaly.
Blood tests show: Hb 6.1 g/dL, MCV 78 fL (normal), WCC 6,000/m1, platelets
300,000/m1.
What is the most likely cause of her anemia?
A. Thalassemia
B. Iron deficiency anemia
C. Sickle cell disease
Normal MCV with hemolytic features (jaundice, pain crises,
splenomegaly) points to sickle cell disease. Thalassemia
typically presents with microcytosis.
