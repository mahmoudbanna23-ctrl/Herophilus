34. Which of the following statements about innocent (physiologic) heart
murmurs is NOT true?
a. They are harsh murmurs
b. They are usually soft
c. They vary with position or respiration
d. They occur in an otherwise healthy child
Innocent murmurs are soft, vary with posture/respiration, and
occur in healthy children, not harsh in quality.
