27. A newbom infant with features of Down syndrome develops vomiting on day
1 of life. The pregnancy was complicated by polyhydramnios. On examination,
he is well-perfused, his abdomen is soft, and his anus is patent. He has not opened
his bowels yet. Which of the following is the most useful initial investigation to
make a diagnosis?
A. Plain abdominal radiograph
B. Abdominal ultrasound scan
C. CT abdomen
D. MRI abdomen
E. Barium follow-through
A plain abdominal X-ray is the first-line investigation to assess
for duodenal atresia, which commonly presents in infants with
Down syndrome and causes the "double bubble" sign.
