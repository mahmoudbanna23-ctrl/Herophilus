41. Which of the following is considered an asthma preventer?
a. Inhaled corticosteroids
b. Shon-acting beta agonists (SABA)
c. Oral antihistamines
d. Intravenous magnesium sulfate
Inhaled corticosteroids reduce airway inflammation and are the
mainstay of long-term asthma prevention. They help decrease
the frequency and severity of symptoms when used regularly.
