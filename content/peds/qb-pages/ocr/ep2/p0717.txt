44. A 4-week-old baby presents to the GP with poor feeding for 2 days. He is pale
with poor peripheral perfusion on arrival. Which of the following is single most
useful thing to do in order to diagnose coarctation of the aorta?
a. Auscultate the heart
b. Measure four limb blood pressures
c. Measure upper and lower limb saturations
d. Palpate the femoral pulses
e. Perform a chest X-ray
Weak or absent femoral pulses compared to radial pulses is the
most useful finding for diagnosing coarctation.
