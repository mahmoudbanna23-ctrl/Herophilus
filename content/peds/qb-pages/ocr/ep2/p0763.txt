66. A 3-year-old child presents with fever and runny nose. On examination, a left
parasternal harsh pansystolic murmur is heard. What is the next step?
a. Reassurance and dental hygiene
b. Surgical correction
d. Start antibiotics
A new pansystolic murmur after infection could mean
endocarditis or congenital lesion. Echo is needed to confirm the
diagnosis before treatment.
