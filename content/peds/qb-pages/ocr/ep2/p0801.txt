Management
•<3 months / very ill hospital + IV antibiotics (e.g. co-amoxiclav 5—7 days), then
prophylaxis.
•>3 months with pyelonephritis/upper UTI oral/lV antibiotics (7—10 days).
•Cystitis oral antibiotics (e.g. trimethoprim, nitrofurantoin 5 days).
•Adjust antibiotic choice per culture.
Prevention
•High fluid intake, double voiding, treat constipation, good hygiene.
•Probiotics (Lactobacillus) may reduce pathogenic flora.
•Antibiotic prophylaxis: controversial, considered in young children with congenital
abnormalities, severe reflux, or recurrent UTIs.
•Circumcision may reduce recurrence in boys.
