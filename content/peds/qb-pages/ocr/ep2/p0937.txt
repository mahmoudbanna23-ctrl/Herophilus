Epilepsy Syndromes (by age of onset)
• West syndrome (infantile spasms) 3—12 months.
•Lennox-Gastaut 1—3 yrs, multiple seizure types, poor prognosis.
•Childhood absence epilepsy 4—12 yrs, provoked by hyperventilation, usually remits.
•Rolandic epilepsy (centro-temporal spikes) 4—10 yrs, nocturnal, focal facial seizures,
benign.
•Panayiotopoulos syndrome* 1—5 yrs, occipital seizures with autonomic features.
•Juvenile absence epilepsy* 10—20 yrs, often persists.
•Juvenile myoclonic epilepsy 10—20 yrs, myoclonus on waking, lifelong, good response
to valproate.
