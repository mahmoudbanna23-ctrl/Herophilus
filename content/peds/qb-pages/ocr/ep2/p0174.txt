4. Which of the following is NOT a routine screening test required in children
with Down syndrome?
a. Thyroid function test
b. Echocardiography
c. Hearing assessment
d. Screening for hyperthyroidism
Hypothyroidism is the common thyroid problem in Down
syndrome, not hyperthyroidism. Routine screening includes
TSH, hearing assessment, and echo.
