64. Which is not a cause of acute abdomen?
a. Henoch—Schönlein purpura (HSP)
c. Thalassemia
d. Sickle cell disease
Thalassemia causes chronic anemia but not acute abdomen.
HSP, IJTI, and sickle cell disease can all present with acute
abdominal pain.
