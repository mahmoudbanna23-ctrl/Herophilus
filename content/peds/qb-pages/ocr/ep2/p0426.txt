78. You are asked to review a 2d old full term baby boy who started bleeding
excessively following circumcision. You request a full CBC & clotting. The CBC
is normal & the APTT is prolonged at 95 seconds. PT is normal. What is the
most likely diagnosis?
a. Hemophilia
b. ITP
c. Severe von Willebrand's disease
d. Vitamin K deficiency
Hemophilia (A or B) presents with prolonged APTT and normal
PT, Often first detected after procedures like circumcision.
