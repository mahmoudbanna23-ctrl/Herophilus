33. Which of the following is the best reliever therapy in asthma?
A. Antihistamines
B. Inhaled corticosteroids
C. Inhaled short-acting beta agonists
D. Short-acting theophylline
E. Inhaled anticholinergic bronchodilators.
Short-acting beta agonists quickly relieve bronchospasm in
asthma attacks, making them the first-choice reliever therapy.
