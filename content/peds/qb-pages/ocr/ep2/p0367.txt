49. Which of the following is the confirmatory test for diagnosing leukemia in
children?
a. Blast cells present in CBC
b. Hypercellularity of RBC precursors in bone marrow
c. Leukocytosis
d. Bone marrow aspiration with cytology
The confirmatory test for leukemia is bone marrow aspiration,
which shows blast cells. CBC alone is not sufficient for a definite
diagnosis.
