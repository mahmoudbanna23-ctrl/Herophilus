3. Anemia
•Definition: Hb below normal range (varies with age)
• Neonate: g/dL
• 1—12 months: <10g/dL
• 1—12 years: <11g/dL
•Mechanisms:
• Reduced red cell production (e.g., iron deficiency)
• Increased red cell destruction (hemolysis)
• Blood loss (less common in children)
•Diagnostic approach:
• Reticulocyte count, bilirubin, Coombs test, bone marrow, blood film, Hb
electrophoresis/HPLC
•Causes in children:
• Red cell aplasia (Diamond-Blackfan, Parvovirus 819, transient erythroblastopenia)
• Qualitative erythropoiesis disorders (iron, folate/B12 deficiency, chronic disease)
• Hemolysis (G6PD deficiency, hemoglobinopathies, autoimmune hemolysis)
• Blood loss (feto-maternal bleeding, Gl bleed, bleeding disorders)
