65. A 3-month-old female infant is seen during a routine check-up. She is well
and thriving, and her peripheral pulses are easily palpable. On examination, a
continuous murmur is heard throughout the precordium. What is the most likely
diagnosis?
a. Patent ductus arteriosus (PDA)
b. Ventricular septal defect (VSD)
c. Atrial septal defect (ASD)
d. Coarctation of the aorta
PDA produces a continuous "machinery" murmur best heard in
infancy. Good pulses and thriving suggest a moderate, not
severe lesion.
