Hearing Development and Screening
•Fetal hearing responds to sound at 25—26 weeks gestation.
•At birth: startles to loud noise, prefers voices.
•Importance: early detection improves speech, language, behavior, learning, and social
outcomes.
Neonatal Hearing Screening Methods:
1.Automated Otoacoustic Emission (AOAE)
Measures cochlear function via hair cell vibrations.
Quick, simple, suitable for all babies.
Limitations: misses auditory neuropathy, high false positives in first 24h.
2.Automated Auditory Brainstem Response (AABR
Measures auditory pathway from ear to brainstem.
Low false positives; used if AOAE abnormal or NICU baby.
Limitations: requires quiet/asleep infant, electrodes, complex equipment.
High-risk infants (NICU, congenital infections, family history, craniofacial anomalie
should have both AOAE and AABR.
