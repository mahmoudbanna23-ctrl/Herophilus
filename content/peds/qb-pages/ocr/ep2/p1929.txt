42. A baby has bilaterally undescended testes. Genitalia appear to be male.
Which is the most important initial investigation?
A. Karyotype with FISH for sex-determining region of the Y chromosome
B. Abdominal ultrasound scan
C. Abdominal CT scan
D. 17-Hydroxyprogesterone levels
E, Urea and electrolytes ((J&Es)
In a newborn with bilateral undescended testes, the most urgent concern is
salt-wasting congenital adrenal hyperplasia (CAH), which can cause life-
threatening electrolyte imbalances. Therefore, urea and electrolytes (U&Es)
should be checked immediately. Further tests like karyotype, ultrasound, and
17-hydroxyprogesterone are done after stabilization to confirm the diagnosis
and assess anatomy.
