Hepatitis B (HBV)
• Treatment:
• No treatment for acute infection.
• Chronic: interferon/peg-lFN (success 30—50%), oral antivirals (entecavir, tenofovir).
•Prevention:
• Universal vaccination (part of routine schedule).
• Pregnant women screened for HBsAg.
• Infants of positive mothers: vaccine at birth ± HBIG.
• Family members also vaccinated.
•Chronic Hepatitis B:
• 30—50% of carrier children develop chronic liver disease.
• 10% progress to cirrhosis; 'T' risk of hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC).
