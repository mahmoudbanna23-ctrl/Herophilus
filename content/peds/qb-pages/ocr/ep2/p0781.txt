75. The following can increase the risk congenital heart disease in children EXCEPT:
a. Down syndrome
b. Maternal diabetes
c. Hypothyroidism
d. Congenital rubella
e. Turner's syndrome
CHD is linked with Down syndrome, Turner's, maternal diabetes, and rubella.
Hypothyroidism does not increase CHD risk.
