I. At what age (median age) would one expect a boy to be able to follow his
mother's two-step commands, such as "Go to the cupboard and fetch your red
shoes"?
A. 18 months
B. 2 years
C. 3 years
D. 4 years
E. 5 years
By around 3 years, most children can understand and follow simple
two-step instructions. This reflects typical language comprehension
and short-term memory development. Younger children usually can
only follow one-step commands.
