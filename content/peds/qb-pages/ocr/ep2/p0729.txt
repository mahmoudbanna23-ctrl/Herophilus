49. A 16-year-old boy with Marfan syndrome is reviewed in the Cardiology
Clinic. Which cardiac abnormality is most likely to be found?
a. Aortic regurgitation
b. Atrial septal defect (ASD)
c. Dilated cardiomyopathy
d. Mitral valve stenosis
e. Persistent ductus arteriosus
Marfan syndrome predisposes to aortic root dilation, leading to
aortic regurgitation
