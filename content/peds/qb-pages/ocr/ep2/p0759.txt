64. A I O-year-old girl is noted to have a rumbling diastolic murmur at the apex,
which becomes more pronounced during exercise. What is the most likely
diagnosis?
a. Mitral stenosis
b. Aortic stenosis
c. Ventricular septal defect (VSD)
d. Tricuspid regurgitation
A diastolic rumbling murmur at the apex is classic for mitral
stenosis. It becomes more pronounced with exercise due to
increased flow across the narrowed valve.
