64. A 3-year-old boy developed a preference for using his left hand at 7 months of
age. His upper limbs are stiff, with increased reflexes. What is the type of
movement disorder that fits with this type of cerebral palsy?
A. Dyskinetic
B. Ataxic
C. Spastic diplegia
D. Spastic hemiplegia
E. Spastic quadriplegia
Spastic hemiplegia affects one side of the body, causing
increased tone and reflexes, often leading to early hand
preference on the unaffected side.
