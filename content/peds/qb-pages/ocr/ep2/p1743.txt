74. Anti-Streptolysin O titer (ASOT) is a specific diagnostic test for which ofthe
following?
A. Rheumatic fever
B. Rheumatic heart disease
C. Acute glomerulonephritis
D. Recent group A streptococcal infection
ASOT measures antibodies against streptolysin O, indicating
recent infection with group A streptococcus.
