58. A 6-year-old boy is admitted with first episode of seizures. He had sore throat
2 weeks ago. Mother noticed altered color of his urine since 2 days. On
examination, he is afebrile and has puffiness around the eyes. His ASO titer is
raised. What is the MOST likely cause of seizures in this child?
a. Hypovolemia
b. Hypertension
c. Low serum calcium
d. Thrombosis
e. Low serum albumin
Acute post-streptococcal glomerulonephritis causes
hypertension, which can lead to hypertensive encephalopathy
and seizures.
