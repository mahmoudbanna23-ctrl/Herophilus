B. Transposition of the Great Arteries (TGA)
•Anatomy: Aorta from RV, PA from LV parallel circulations.
•Clinical features:
• Profound cyanosis (esp. day 2 duct closure).
• Loud single 2nd heart sound; usually no murmur.
•Investigations:
• CXR: "egg on side" heart, pulmonary vascularity.
• Echo: diagnostic.
•Management:
• Maintain mixing: PGEI infusion, ± balloon atrial septostomy.
• Definitive surgery: arterial switch (neonatal period).
