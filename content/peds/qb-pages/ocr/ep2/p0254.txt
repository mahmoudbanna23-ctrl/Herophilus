3. Glucose-6-Phosphate Dehydrogenase (G6PD) Deficiency
•Incidence: 10—20% in central Africa, Mediterranean, Middle East, Far East
•Inheritance: X-Iinked; males mainly affected
•Pathogenesis: Oxidative stress causes RBC hemolysis
•Triggers: Infection, drugs (sulfonamides, antimalarials, nitrofurantoin,
fluoroquinolones), fava beans, naphthalene
•Clinical Features:
• Neonatal jaundice (first 3 days)
• Acute hemolytic episodes: fever, pallor, dark urine
•Diagnosis: G6PD enzyme assay (repeat after crisis)
•Management: Avoid triggers, supportive care, transfusions if severe
