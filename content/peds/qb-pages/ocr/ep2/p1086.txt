Management Steps:
1.Callfor help + give 02.
2.Shock: bolus 20 ml/kg 0.9% NaCl.
3. IV fluids:
• 5—10% dehydration depending on PH.
•use 0.9% Naci ± KCI over 48 h.
'Switch to glucose-containing fluids once BG <14 mmol/L.
4. Insulin infusion: 0.05 units/kg/h after 1 h of fluids.
5. Monitor: GCS, glucose, ketones, electrolytes.
6. Cerebral oedema risk:
Treat with mannitol (1 g/kg) or 2.7% NaCl if GCS falls.
Resolution:
•Defined by fall in ketones + correction of acidosis, not just glucose normalization.
•Switch to SC insulin + oral diet stop IV insulin 1 h later.
