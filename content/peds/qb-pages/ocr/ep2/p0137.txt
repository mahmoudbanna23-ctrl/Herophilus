21. Fatima is an 8-month-old girl who was born at term. She is seen in the
pediatric outpatient department. She can roll over. She does not crawl. She can
say 'dada' but says it to everyone not just her father. She reaches out and grasps
objects with her left hand but not with her right and puts objects in her mouth.
She smiles but is not able to wave bye-bye. Which aspect of her development is
of most concern?
a. Does not wave bye-bye
b. Inability to crawl
c. Inability to use sounds discriminately to parents
d. Left-hand preference
Hand preference before 18 months is concerning because it
may suggest weakness Of one side due to cerebral palsy.
Crawling and waving bye-bye can be delayed normally at
this age.
