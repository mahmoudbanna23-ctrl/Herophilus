79. What is the most common cause of cerebral palsy?
A. Prenatal cause
B. Perinatal brain injury
C. Neonatal infections
D. Accidents
E. Meningitis/encephalitis
The majority of cerebral palsy cases are due to prenatal brain
injury, not perinatal events or infections.
