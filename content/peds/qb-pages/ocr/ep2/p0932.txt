Types:
•Tension-type: bilateral, tightness/pressure, gradual onset, no other symptoms.
•Migraine: pulsatile, frontal/temporal, moderate—severe, aggravated by activity, ±
nausea, vomiting, photophobia, phonophobia.
• Without aura (90%): bilateral/unilateral, lasts 1—72h.
• Uncommon forms: hemiplegic migraine, basilar migraine, periodic syndromes
(cyclical vomiting, abdominal migraine, benign paroxysmal vertigo).
•Cluster headache: periorbital, nocturnal, rhinorrhea/eye redness (rare in children).
Medication overuse headache: chronic daily headache from frequent analgesic use.
Improves with withdrawal.
