29. Ali, aged 6, is brought to the Emergency Department after becoming unwell at
a family party. He is unable to say more than a single word and indicates that he is
finding it hard to breathe. He appears very anxious. He has a raised, itchy rash
that is spreading from his face to his chest. He has never had an episode like this
before. His mother reports that he has asthma and uses a salbutamol inhaler for
wheezy episodes.What is the most likely diagnosis?
a. Acute asthma
b. Allergic reaction
c. Anaphylaxis
d. Idiopathic urticaria
e. Inhaled foreign body
Sudden breathing difficulty with rash and swelling after
exposure to allergens strongly suggests anaphylaxis. Asthma is
a risk factor for severe reactions.
