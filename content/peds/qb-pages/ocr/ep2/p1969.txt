'Acute lymphocytic leukemia presents with pallor, bruising, lymphadenopathy,
and hepatosplenomegaly due to bone marrow infiltration. Pancytopenia
develops as malignant blasts replace normal hematopoietic cells.
Bone marrow aspiration is essential to confirm leukemia and determine its
subtype. Peripheral blasts may not always be present, so marrow examination
provides definitive diagnosis and classification.
