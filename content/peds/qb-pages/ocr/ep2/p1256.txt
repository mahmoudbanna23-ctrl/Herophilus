17. Carla, a 4-year-old girl, presents with persistent vomiting, headaches, and
abnormal eye movements. What investigation would confirm the diagnosis?
A. Full blood count
B. MRI scan
C. PET scan
D. Abdominal ultrasound
E. Urine catecholamines
These are signs of raised intracranial pressure from a brain
tumour. MRI is the best diagnostic tool.
