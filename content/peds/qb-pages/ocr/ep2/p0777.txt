73. Recognized complications of tetralogy of Fallot EXCEPT
a. Cerebral thrombosis
b. Heart failure
c. Cerebral abscess
d. Hypoglycemia
TOF is associated with cerebral abscess, thrombosis, and heart
failure, but hypoglycemia is unrelated.
