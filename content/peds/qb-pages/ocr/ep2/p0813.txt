IgA Nephropathy
•Presentation: Episodic macroscopic hematuria with URTIs.
•Histology/management: Similar to HSP.
•Prognosis: Better in children than adults.
Alport Syndrome / Thin Basement Membrane Disease
•Cause: Collagen defects in GBM.
•Inheritance: Most commonly X-Iinked (progressive CKD in males, with deafness & eye
defects); also AD or AR.
•Management: Long-term follow-up; ACE inhibitors slow CKD progression.
Vasculitis & SLE
•Vasculitis: Renal involvement may be severe (HSP, PAN, GPA). Diagnosis with ANCA;
treatment with immunosuppression.
Common in adolescent females (esp. Asian/Afro-Caribbean). Renal biopsy needed if
•SLE:
hematuria/proteinuria. Treatment: corticosteroids + immunosuppressants (e.g.
cyclophosphamide, biologics).
