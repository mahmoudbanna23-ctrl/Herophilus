44. A 13-year-old girl with epilepsy attends the outpatient clinic for review. Her
seizure frequency has increased and the type of seizures she is experiencing has
varied. You suspect psychogenic non-epileptic seizures (PNES). Which of the
following would be more typical of a PNES?
a. Events are shorter than her usual seizures
b. Her eyes are closed tightly during events
c. Prolactin is raised on blood taken after a seizure
d. There is a rapid response to anticonvulsants
e. There is unnary or faecal incontinence
In true epileptic seizures, eyes are usually open. Tight eye
closure suggests psychogenic seizures.
