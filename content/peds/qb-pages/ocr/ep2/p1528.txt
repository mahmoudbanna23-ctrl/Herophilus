48. All of the following are among the major manifestations of Jones criteria for
diagnosis of rheumatic fever except?
A. Chorea
B. Migratory arthritis
C. Carditis
D. Maculopapular rash
E. Subcutaneous nodules
Jones criteria for diagnosis Of rheumatic fever include:
Major manifestations: Carditis, migratory arthritis,
Sydenham chorea, erythema marginatum, and subcutaneous
nodules.
Minor manifestations: Fever, polyarthralgia, raised acute-
phase reactants (e.g., ESR).
