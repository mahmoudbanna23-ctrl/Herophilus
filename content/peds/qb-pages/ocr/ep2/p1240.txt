9. Natalia, a 4-year-old girl, has recurrent tonsillitis, pallor, bruising, and
hepatosplenomegaly. What is the most likely diagnosis?
A. Neuroblastoma
B. Brain tumour
C. Wilms tumour
D. Acute lymphoblastic leukaemia
E. Hodgkin disease
These features suggest bone marrow failure with organ
infiltration, typical of ALL. Recurrent infections are due to
neutropenia.
