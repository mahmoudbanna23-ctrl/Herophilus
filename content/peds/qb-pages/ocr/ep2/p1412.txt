71. A six-year-old boy presents with cyanosis. He was diagnosed to have a large
VSD earlier in infancy, but his parents have refused surgical repair. On physical
examination, his weight and height are below the 5th centile. He has central
cyanosis, with an 02 saturation of 81 % in room air. What is the pathophysiologic
mechanism of his cyanosis?
A. Reduced cardiac output
B. Shunt reversal
C. Heart block
D. Tachyarrhythmia
Long-standing large VSD can cause Eisenmenger syndrome,
where left-to-right shunt reverses, leading to cyanosis and
hypoxemia.
E. Methemoglobinemia
