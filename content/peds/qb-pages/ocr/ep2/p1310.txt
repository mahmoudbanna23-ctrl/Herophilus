20. A 3-month-old baby girl presents with a urinary tract infection. Which of the
following organisms is most likely to be isolated from her urine?
A. Enterococcus spp.
B. Pseudomonas
C. Proteus
D. Escherichia coli
E. Klebsiella
E. coli is the most frequent cause of UTI in infants due to its
prevalence in the gut flora and proximity to the urinary tract.
