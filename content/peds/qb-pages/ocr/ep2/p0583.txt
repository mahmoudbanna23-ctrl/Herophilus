53. A 3-year-old boy is seen by his GP due to recurrent wheezing associated with
upper respiratory tract infections. Which of the following features most supports a
diagnosis of asthma?
a. Daytime cough
b. Finger clubbing
c. Spasmodic cough
d. Persistent moist cough
e. The presence of symptoms between colds
Asthma is more likely when symptoms like wheeze or cough
persist Outside of viral illnesses. Isolated symptoms only during
colds may reflect viral-induced wheeze rather than true
asthma.
