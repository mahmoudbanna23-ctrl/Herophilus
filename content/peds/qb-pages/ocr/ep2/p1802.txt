22. Bronchiolitis is one of the most common respiratory conditions affecting
infants. Which of the following is not a clinical feature of bronchiolitis?
A. Tachypnoea
B. Apnoea
C. Wheeze
D. Fine crackles
E. Paroxysmal cough
Bronchiolitis typically presents with tachypnea, wheeze, fine
crackles, and sometimes apnoea; paroxysmal cough is not
classic.
