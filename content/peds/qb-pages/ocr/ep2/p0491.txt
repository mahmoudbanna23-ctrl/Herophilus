respiratory rate is normal, and there is no chest recession. What is the most likely
diagnosis?
A. Bronchiolitis
B. Frontal sinusitis
C. Pneumonia (lower respiratory tract infection)
D. Tonsillitis
E. Upper respiratory tract infection
Coryza, mild fever, and no chest recession or tachypnea suggest
a simple URTI, the most common cause Of such Symptoms in
