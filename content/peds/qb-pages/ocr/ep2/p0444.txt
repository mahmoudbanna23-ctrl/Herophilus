87. A 2y old boy is admitted to hospital for an elective repair of an inguinal
hernia. he has no other medical problems. His preoperative assessment reveals
the following results: Hb: 8.6 g/dl, MCV: 58 fl (normal: 75-87 fl), MCHC: 22
g/dl (normal: 32-35 g/dl), WBC 111200 /dl, platelets: 262.000/dl, HB
electrophoresis: hemoglobin A (HbA) 98%; hemoglobinA2 (HbA2) 2%. What is
the most likely diagnosis?
a. Thalassemia trait
b. Glucose-6-phosphate dehydrogenase deficiency
c. Iron-deficiency anemia
d. Normal variation for age
Microcytic hypochromic anemia with normal Hb
e. Sickle cell trait
electrophoresis indicates iron deficiency, not
thalassemia trait.
