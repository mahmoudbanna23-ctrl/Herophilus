25. A 6-month-old male infant has a cardiac murmur and oxygen saturation of
78% unresponsive to oxygen therapy. Chest X-ray shows a boot-shaped heart.
What is the other possible clinical presentation for this case?
A. Recurrent chest infection
B. Heart failure
C. Cyanotic spells
D. Choking while feeding
Boot-shaped heart is characteristic of tetralogy of Fallot, which
also presents with cyanotic spells (tet spells).
