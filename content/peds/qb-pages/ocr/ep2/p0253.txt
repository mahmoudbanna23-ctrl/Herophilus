2. Hereditary Spherocytosis (HS)
•Incidence: 1:5000 in Caucasians, usually autosomal dominant; 25% sporadic
•Pathogenesis: Mutations in RBC membrane proteins spherocytes premature splenic
destruction
•Clinical Features:
• Jaundice (may be neonatal)
• Mild anemia (Hb 90—110g/L)
• Splenomegaly
• Aplastic crisis (parvovirus B19)
• Gallstones
•Diagnosis: Blood film; dye binding or genetic testing; rule out autoimmune causes with
Coombs test
•Management:
• Oral folic acid
• Splenectomy (for severe symptoms or growth delay, after age 7, with vaccinatio
penicillin prophylaxis)
Transfusion for aplastic crisis
