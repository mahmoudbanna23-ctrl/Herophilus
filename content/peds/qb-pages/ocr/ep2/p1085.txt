2. Diabetic Ketoacidosis (DKA)
Diagnostic Criteria:
•Glucose >11 mmol/L.
•Ketones >3 mmol/L.
•Metabolic acidosis: pH < 7.3 or HCOs <15 mmol/L.
Severity:
•Mild: pH >7.2, HCOs 10-15.
•Moderate: pH 7.1-7.2, HC03 5-10.
•Severe: pH < 7.1, HCOs <5.
Essential Investigations:
•U&E, creatinine, cultures (infection), blood gases, ECG (K+).
•Weight (to assess dehydration).
