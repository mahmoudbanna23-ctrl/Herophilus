69. Which is a common cause of chronic wet (productive) cough in children?
A. Bronchial asthma
B. Habit cough
C. Persistent bacterial bronchitis
D. Tracheomalacia
E. Bacterial tracheitis
Chronic wet cough that persists beyond acute infection often
indicates persistent bacterial bronchitis in children.
