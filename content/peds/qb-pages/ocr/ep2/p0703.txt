37. The following condition is not associated with a systolic murmur:
a. Anemia
b. Hyperthyroidism with hyperdynamic circulation
c. Mitral stenosis
d. Aortic stenosis
e. Fever.
Mitral stenosis produces a diastolic murmur, not systolic, unlike
the other listed conditions.
