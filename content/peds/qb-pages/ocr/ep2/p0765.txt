67. A 7-week-old infant presents with breathlessness on feeding and failure to
thrive. On examination, his femoral pulses are difficult to feel but present. Chest
X-ray shows cardiomegaly and increased pulmonary vascular markings. What is
the most likely diagnosis?
a. Coarctation of the aorta
b. Patent ductus arteriosus (PDA)
c. Ventricular septal defect (VSD)
d. Tetralogy ofFa110t
Weak femoral pulses and heart failure signs point to
coarctation. Increased pulmonary markings reflect left heart
failure due to Obstruction.
