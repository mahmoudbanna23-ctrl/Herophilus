In childhood acute lymphoblastic leukemia (ALL), good prognostic factors
include: age between 1—10 years, normal or slightly elevated WCC (<50
x109/L),
and favorable immunophenotype (common B-cell ALL). Poor
prognostic factors include age <1 or >10, high WCC, T-cell or certain B-cell
subtypes, and presence of adverse genetic translocations like the Philadelphia
chromosome.
