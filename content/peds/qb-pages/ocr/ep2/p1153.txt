Galactosemia
•Rare, AR
•Infants: poor feeding, vomiting, jaundice, hepatomegaly on milk.
•Complications: liver failure, cataracts, developmental delay, Gram-negative sepsis.
•Diagnosis: galactose-I-phosphate uridyl transferase.
•Treatment: galactose-free diet (prevents liver disease but risk of ovarian failure,
learning issues).
