Nephrotic Syndrome
•Definition: Heavy proteinuria hypoalbuminemia, edema, hyperlipidemia.
•Causes: Mostly idiopathic (minimal change disease); secondary to systemic diseases
(HSP, SLE), infections (malaria, hepatitis B/C), allergens.
•Clinical signs:
• Periorbital edema (early, worse in morning).
• Generalized edema (scrotal/vulval, legs, ascites, pleural effusion).
• Infection risk (loss of lg in urine).
•Complications: Hypovolemia, severe edema, thrombosis (loss of ATIII, hypercoagulable
state), infections, hypercholesterolemia.
•Investigations: urine protein (23+), blood count, U&E, albumin, complement (C3/C4),
ASO/anti-DNase B, hepatitis/malaria screen.
