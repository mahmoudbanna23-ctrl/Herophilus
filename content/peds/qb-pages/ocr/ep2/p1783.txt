13. A 3-year-old girl with complex congenital heart disease is admitted with fever.
On examination, her temperature is 39.50C, and there is a loud ejection systolic
murmur. Her CRP is 250 mg/L, and a transthoracic echocardiogram confirms
vegetations. What is the most likely causative pathogen?
A. Streptococcus pneumoniae
B. Streptococcus pyogenes
C. Streptococcus viridans
D. Group A haemolytic streptococcus
E. Group B haemolytic streptococcus
Viridans streptococci are the most common cause of infective
endocarditis in children with underlying heart defects.
