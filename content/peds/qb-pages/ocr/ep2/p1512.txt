40. A 3-year-old child presents with a barking cough and inspiratory stridor. What
is the most likely diagnosis?
A. Bronchiolitis
B. Epiglottitis
C. Foreign body aspiration
D. Laryngotracheobronchitis (croup)
E. Asthma
Croup is a common cause of barking cough and stridor in
children aged 6 months to 6 years.
