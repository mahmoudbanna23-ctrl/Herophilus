The Hypotonic ("Floppy") Infant
•Clinical signs: slips through hands when lifted, rag doll on ventral suspension, head lag
on pull-to-sit.
•Central hypotonia (floppy + strong):
• Causes: cerebral malformations, hypoxic-ischemic injury, metabolic/endocrine
(hypothyroidism), chromosomal (Down, Prader-Willi).
• Features: preserved antigravity movements, brisk reflexes, seizures, dysmorphism.
•Peripheral hypotonia (floppy + weak):
• Causes: SMA type 1, congenital myopathies, myotonic dystrophy, neonatal MG.
• Features: weak/absent antigravity movements, U/absent reflexes, frog-leg
posture.
