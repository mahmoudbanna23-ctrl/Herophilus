25. Down syndrome is the most common form of trisomy encountered in children
and occurs on chromosome 21. Which of the following statements is true?
A. Most babies with Down syndrome are born to older mothers
B. Males are frequently infertile
C. The incidence is 1 in 1,400
D. Epicanthic folds are pathognomomc
E. The majority have severe intellectual impairment
Most males with Down syndrome are infertile due to abnormal
spermatogenesis. While incidence increases with maternal age,
most cases still occur in younger mothers due to higher birth
rates in that group.
