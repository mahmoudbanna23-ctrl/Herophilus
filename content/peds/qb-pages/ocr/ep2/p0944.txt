Non-Epileptic Seizures (Paroxysmal Disorders / "Funny Turns")
•Mimics epilepsy but not epileptic in origin.
•Commonest cause: syncope transient cerebral hypoperfusion.
•Types:
• Convulsive syncope (breath-holding spells).
• Vasovagal syncope (vasodepressor, cardioinhibitory, or mixed).
• Hypovolaemic (bleeding, dehydration, anaphylaxis).
• Cardiac (e.g., long QT syndrome).
Diagnosis: detailed history, videos, clinical exam.
•Interictal EEG: useful for confirmed epilepsy classification.
•Ictal EEG: helpful if seizures frequent enough to capture.
