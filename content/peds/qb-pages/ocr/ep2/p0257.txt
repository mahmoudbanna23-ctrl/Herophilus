C. Sickle Cell Disease (SCD)
•Cause: Point mutation in 6-globin (HbS) sickled RBCs, hemolysis, vaso-occlusion
•Inheritance: Autosomal recessive
.Types: HbSS (most severe), HbSC, Sickle-thalassemia, Sickle trait (asymptomatic)
•Pathogenesis: HbS polymerization rigid sickled RBCs microvascular occlusion,
ischemia, organ damage
•Exacerbating Factors: Hypoxia, dehydration, cold, infection, acidosis
Clinical Features:
•Chronic hemolytic anemia (Hb 60—100 g/L)
•Increased infection risk (splenic dysfunction)
•Vaso-occlusive crises: hand-foot syndrome, bone pain, acute chest syndrome, stroke
•Long-term: growth delay, leg ulcers, renal/cardiac complications, gallstones
