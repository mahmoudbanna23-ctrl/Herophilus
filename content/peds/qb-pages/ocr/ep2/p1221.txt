Management
•Surgery first-line if feasible.
•± Radiotherapy, ± chemotherapy (depends on type).
Prognosis
•Astrocytoma: cystic, slow growing good prognosis if resected.
•Medulloblastoma: surgery + chemo + RT 5-yr survival z 50%.
•Craniopharyngioma: good survival but long-term visual/pituitary deficits.
•Brainstem glioma: palliative RT only, <IO% survival.
•Ependymoma: often aggressive, variable prognosis.
