52. All ofthe following are true about thalassemia EXCEPT?
a. Decreased serum ferritin
b. Microcytic hypochromic anemia
c. Increased HbA2 in beta-thalassemia minor
d. Genetic inheritance
Thalassemia shows normal or increased ferritin (not decreased),
microcytic hypochromic anemia, and raised HbA2 in ß-thalassemia
minor.
