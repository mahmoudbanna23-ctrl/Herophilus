Prognosis
•Depends on CP type, comorbidities, and developmental delay
•Walking ability by type:
• Hemiplegic CP: most walk by 2 years
• Spastic diplegia: 50% walk by 3 years
• Spastic quadriplegia: only N33% eventually walk; 25% need full assistance
•Motor ability at 2 years is predictive of ambulation at age 6:
• Can sit likely walk unaided
• Can roll but not sit may walk
• Cannot roll or sit unlikely to walk
