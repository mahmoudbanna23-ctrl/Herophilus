21. A 6-year-old boy is seen by his general practitioner because of recurrent
wheezing associated with upper respiratory tract infections. Which of the
following features most supports the diagnosis of asthma?
A. Daytime cough
B. Finger clubbing
C. Spasmodic cough
D. Persistent moist cough
E. The presence of symptoms between coughs and colds
Persistent symptoms Outside acute infections suggest
underlying asthma. Finger clubbing or constant cough is
uncommon in mild/moderate asthma.
