B. Autosomal Recessive (AR)
•Gene location: Autosomes.
•Inheritance: Two abnormal alleles required; carriers usually unaffected.
•Recurrence risk:
• Both parents carriers 25% affected, 50% carriers.
• One affected + one carrier 50% affected, 50% carriers.
• Both affected 100% affected.
•Consanguinity: Increases risk.
•Examples: Cystic fibrosis, Sickle cell disease, Thalassemia, Tay-Sachs disease.
