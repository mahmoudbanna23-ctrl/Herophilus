67. Which nutritional problem do children with chronic liver disease suffer from?
A. Defective absorption of medium-chain fatty acids
B. Defective absorption of long-chain fatty acids
C. Increased absorption of cholesterol
D. Improper digestion of proteins
E. Defective absorption of folic acid and vitamin B
Chronic liver disease affects bile production and fat absorption,
leading to deficiency of fat-soluble vitamins and long-chain fatty
acids.
