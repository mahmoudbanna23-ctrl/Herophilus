34. A 14-month-old is seen in the clinic because his mother is concerned that he
may be having seizures. The episodes occur when he is angry or upset. He has
color changes followed by collapse and occasional jerking movements. What is
the most likely diagnosis?
A. Breath-holding spells
B. Myoclonic epilepsy
C. Non-epileptic seizure
D. Vasovagal syncope.
These spells are triggered by emotional upset and may include brief
cyanosis or pallor with collapse; they are benign and self-limiting.
