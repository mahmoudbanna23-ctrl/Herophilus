Primary Ciliary Dyskinesia (PCD)
•Definition:
Autosomal recessive disorder with abnormal ciliary structure/function impaired
mucociliary clearance recurrent respiratory infections.
•Pathophysiology:
• Dynein arm defect immotile cilia.
• Impaired clearance recurrent sinusitis, otitis media, bronchiectasis.
• Defective embryonic ciliary function abnormal organ lateralization 50% have
Kartagener syndrome (situs inversus + dextrocardia + bronchiectasis).
