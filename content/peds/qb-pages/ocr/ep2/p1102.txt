5. Zeinab, aged 15 years, has had 3 months of diarrhea, weight loss, and
palpitations. You suspect hyperthyroidism. Which combination of thyroid
function test results would confirm the diagnosis?
A. High TSH and high T4 levels
B. High TSH and low T4 levels
C. Low TSH and high T4 levels
D. Low TSH and low T4 levels
E. Normal TSH and high T4 levels
Primary hyperthyroidism is characterized by low TSH due to
negative feedback from elevated circulating T4. Zeinab's
Symptoms and this hormonal profile confirm the diagnosis.
