A. Tetralogy of Fallot (TOF)
•4 classic features:
Large VSD
Overriding aorta
Subpulmonary stenosis (RV outflow obstruction)
RV hypertrophy
•Symptoms/signs :
Antenatal diagnosis or murmur in infancy.
Cyanosis, hypercyanotic ("tet") spells, squatting, clubbing.
Harsh ejection systolic murmur (V with worsening obstruction).
• Investigations:
CXR: small "boot-shaped" heart, pulmonary markings.
ECG: RV hypertrophy (later).
Echo: definitive.
•Management:
Surgery at 6 months: VSD closure + RVOT relief.
Early palliation: BT shunt, balloon dilation/stent if severely cyanosed.
Tet spells: morphine, propranolol, fluids, bicarbonate, ventilation.
