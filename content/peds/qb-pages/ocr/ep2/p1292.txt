I l. Which type of genetically determined diseases is responsible for the
production of Turner syndrome?
A. Single gene mutation
B. Imprinting
D. Multifactorial disorder
Turner syndrome is caused by monosomy X or structural abnormalities
Of the X chromosome. It is not caused by single-gene mutations or
multifactorial disorders.
