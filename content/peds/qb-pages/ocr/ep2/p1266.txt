22. A 2-month-old baby has a discrepancy in the red reflex in a family photo. On
exam, only one eye has a red reflex. What is the most likely diagnosis?
A. Retinoblastoma
B. Retinopathy of prematurity
C. Congenital cataracts
D. CMV retinitis
E. Glaucoma
A discrepancy in the red reflex (leukocoria) is a classic presentation Of
retinoblastoma, the most important cause to rule Out because it is life-threatening.
Congenital cataract can also cause an absent reflex, but in exam questions,
retinoblastoma is the top concern when leukocoria is described. Urgent
ophthalmology referral is required.
