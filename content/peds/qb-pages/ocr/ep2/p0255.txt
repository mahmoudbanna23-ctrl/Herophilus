4. Hemoglobinopathies
A. Beta-Thalassemia
•Cause: Reduced/absent 6-globin low HbA, excess HbF
. Types:
• Major (Cooley's anemia): Transfusion-dependent
• Intermedia: Non-transfusion dependent
•Clinical Features: Severe anemia 4—6 months, jaundice, growth failure, extramedullary
hematopoiesis (hepatosplenomegaly, skull bossing, maxillary overgrowth)
•Complications: Iron overload, organ damage, alloimmunization, infection, venous
access issues
•Diagnosis: Hypochromic microcytic anemia, target cells, reticulocytosis, HPLC
•Management: Lifelong transfusions, iron chelation (deferasirox), bone marrow
transplantation (curative)
