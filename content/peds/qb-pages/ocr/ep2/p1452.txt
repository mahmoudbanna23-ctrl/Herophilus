I O. Which of the following tests is diagnostic for hereditary spherocytosis?
A. Hemoglobin electrophoresis
B. CBC
C. Osmotic fragility test
D. Hormonal assay
The osmotic fragility test measures red blood cell resistance to hemolysis in
hypotonic saline solutions. It is diagnostic for hereditary spherocytosis.
