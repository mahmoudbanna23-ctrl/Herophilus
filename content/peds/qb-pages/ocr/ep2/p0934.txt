Seizures & Epilepsy in Childhood
Definition
•Seizure: transient brain dysfunction from excessive, hypersynchronous cortical neuronal
activity -5 paroxysmal motor, sensory, autonomic, or behavioral disturbance.
•Convulsion: seizure with motor components (tonic, clonic, myoclonic, hypermotor).
•Epilepsy: 22 unprovoked epileptic seizures due to chronic brain disorder.
•Acute symptomatic seizures: occur with acute insult (e.g., stroke, trauma, meningitis, metabolic
derangement). Not epilepsy.
•Febrile seizures: seizure triggered by fever, common in young children.
Causes of Seizures
I.Epilepsies (genetic/idiopathic, 70—80%) — complex inheritance.
2.Structural/metabolic: dysgenesis, malformation, congenital infection, HIE, hemorrhage, tumor,
neurodegeneration, tuberous sclerosis.
3.Acute symptomatic: stroke, TBI, infection, hypoglycemia, hypocalcemia, electrolyte imbalance,
toxins.
4.Non-epileptic paroxysmal disorders: breath-holding spells, syncope, migraine, psychoge
seizures, benign paroxysmal vertigo.
