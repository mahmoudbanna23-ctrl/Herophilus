Nephrotic Syndrome
Management:
•Corticosteroids first-line (prednisolone).
•Supportive: salt restriction, diuretics, ACE inhibitors, albumin ± furosemide.
•Vaccinations, penicillin prophylaxis, infection management.
•Steroid-sparing agents for frequent relapses: levamisole, mycophenolate, tacrolimus,
rituximab.
