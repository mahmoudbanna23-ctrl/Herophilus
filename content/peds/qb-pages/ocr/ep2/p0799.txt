Clinical Features
•Infants: non-specific —fever, vomiting, poor feeding, lethargy, jaundice, sepsis, febrile
seizures.
•Children: dysuria, frequency, urgency, abdominal/loin pain, fever, haematuria, cloudy
urine, enuresis.
•Errors: missed diagnosis (9 renal damage) or over-diagnosis (contaminated samples).
Urine Collection
•Infants: clean-catch (preferred), Quick-wee, bag (contamination risk), catheter,
suprapubic aspiration.
•Older children: midstream sample after cleaning.
