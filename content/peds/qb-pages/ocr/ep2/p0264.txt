Disseminated Intravascular Coagulation (DIC)
Definition:
•Disorder of widespread coagulation activation microvascular fibrin deposition
consumption of platelets and clotting factors.
Causes:
•Severe sepsis or shock (e.g., meningococcal septicemia)
•Extensive tissue damage (trauma, burns)
Types:
•Acute or chronic, usually via the tissue factor pathway.
Clinical Features:
•Bruising, purpura, hemorrhage
•Microvascular thrombosis, possibly purpura fulminans
