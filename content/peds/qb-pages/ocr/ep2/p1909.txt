74. Which is a clear indication for tonsillectomy in children?
A. Recurrent febrile viral respiratory tract infections with febrile convulsions
B. Exudative tonsillitis with positive monospot test
C. Two attacks of follicular tonsillitis in a two-month time
E. A high ASOT after an episode of tonsillitis
Recurrent, severe bacterial tonsillitis meeting this frequency is
a clear surgical indication for tonsillectomy.
