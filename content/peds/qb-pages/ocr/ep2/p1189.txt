I l. Which of the following is true regarding hepatitis A?
A. It is a DNA virus which is spread by faecal—oral transmission.
B. It requires urgent treatment
C. Cure is usually achieved after 4 weeks of infection
D. Chronic liver disease usually occurs
Hepatitis A Virus Infection:
•Hepatitis A virus (HAV) is an RNA virus transmitted via the faecal—oral route.
•There is no specific treatment, and there is no evidence that bed rest or
dietary modifications improve outcomes.
•Most children experience a mild illness and recover clinically and
biochemically within 2 to 4 weeks.
•Chronic liver disease does not occur following hepatitis A infection.
