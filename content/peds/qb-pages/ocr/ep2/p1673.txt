39. What is the most probable type of abnormality found on karyotyping in cases
of Down syndrome?
A. Mitotic nondisjunction
B. Translocation
C. Meiotic nondisjunction
D. Mosaicism
E. Robertsonian translocation
Most cases of Down syndrome are caused by meiotic
nondisjunction during gametogenesis, leading to trisomy 21.
