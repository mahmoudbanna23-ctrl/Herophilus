4. Josh is a 2-year-old boy with weight loss, lethargy, and splenomegaly. You
suspect leukaemia and perform a full blood count. What are the most likely
haematological findings?
A. Low haemoglobin and low platelets
B. High haemoglobin and low platelets
C. Normal haemoglobin and normal platelets
D. High haemoglobin and raised platelets
E. Low haemoglobin and raised platelets
In leukaemia, bone marrow is infiltrated by blasts, reducing red
cell and platelet production. This causes anaemia and
thrombocytopenia.
