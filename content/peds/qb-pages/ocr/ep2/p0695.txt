33. During regular checkup of an 8y old patient you heard soft ejection systolic
murmur of grade 2 with a fixed and widely split second heart sound at upper left
sternal border that does not change during respiration, the patient is otherwise
active and healthy. The most likely heart lesion is:
a. VSD
b. pulmonary stenosis
c. ASD
d. fallot tetralogy
e. aortic stenosis
A fixed wide split of S2 with a soft ejection systolic murmur is
the classic finding for atrial septal defect.
