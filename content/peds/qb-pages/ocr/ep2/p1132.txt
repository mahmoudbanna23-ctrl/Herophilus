20. Which of the following is not a manifestation of untreated congenital
hypothyroidism?
a. Puffy eyes
b. Constipation
c. Hoarse voice
d. Delayed motor and mental milestones
e. Overweight due to voracious appetite
Untreated congenital hypothyroidism typically causes poor feeding and
lethargy rather than a voracious appetite. Puffy eyes, constipation,
hoarse voice, and developmental delays are classic features.
