Investigations
•CBC: low Hb, thrombocytopenia, circulating blasts.
•Bone marrow aspiration: confirms diagnosis, subtype, cytogenetics.
•Coagulation screen (10% have DIC).
•Lumbar puncture: check CSF disease.
•Chest X-ray: mediastinal mass.
Prognostic Factors in ALL
•Age: <1 or years = poor.
•WCC: x 109/1 poor.
•Cytogenetics: high-risk (MLL rearrangement, t(4;11), hypodiploidy).
•Treatment response: persistent blasts, MRD positivity.
Management
•Chemotherapy (risk-adapted, multi-agent).
•Supportive care: infection control (neutropenic sepsis emergency), pain managem
psychosocial & fertility support.
•Some may require radiotherapy, surgery, or stem cell transplant.
