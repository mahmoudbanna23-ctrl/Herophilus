Primary Ciliary Dyskinesia (PCD)
•Clinical Features:
• Recurrent productive cough
• Chronic purulent nasal discharge
• Recurrent ear infections conductive hearing loss
• Chronic sinusitis
• Bronchiectasis (if untreated)
• Subfertility (male & female, due to dysfunctional fallopian tube/sperm cilia)
•Diagnosis:
• Nasal epithelial cell brush biopsy assess ciliary structure & function
• Low nasal nitric oxide (screening)
• Genetic testing
•Treatment:
• Similar to bronchiectasis management: airway clearance physiotherapy, pro
antibiotics for infections, ENT management for ear/sinus disease.
