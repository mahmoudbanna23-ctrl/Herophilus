47. What does acanthosis nigricans (dark skin in the armpits or the neck) signify?
A. The presence of type I diabetes
B. The presence of type 2 diabetes
C. Obesity
D. Polycystic ovary
E. Insulin resistance
Acanthosis nigricans indicates hyperinsulinemia and insulin
resistance, Often linked to type 2 diabetes and Obesity.
