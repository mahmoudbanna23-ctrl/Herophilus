58. A 5-year-old boy presents with a low-grade fever during the last two weeks.
On examination, large lymph nodes are palpable on both sides of the neck, under
the axilla and in inguinal regions and there is hepatosplenomegaly. He is pale and
has some purpuric spots are over the back. What is the most likely diagnosis?
a. Acute lymphocytic leukemia
b. Military tuberculosis
c. Idiopathic thrombocytopenic purpura
d. Henoch Schonlein purpura
e. Aplastic anemia
Generalized lymphadenopathy, hepatosplenomegaly, pallor,
and purpura in a child strongly suggest acute lymphoblastic
leukemia (ALL).
