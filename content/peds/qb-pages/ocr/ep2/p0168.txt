I. In a newbom suspected of having Down syndrome, which of the following
investigations are routinely indicated in all patients with Down syndrome
regardless of the symptoms?
a. Echocardiography
b. Renal ultrasound
c. Upper gastrointestinal (GI) series
d. Head ultrasound
e. Chest X ray
Nearly 50% of infants with Down syndrome have congenital heart disease,
so echocardiography is always required. Other tests (renal US, head US, etc.)
are not routinely indicated unless symptoms suggest them.
