54. What is the best method to reduce the risk of developing food allergies in
children?
a. Early exposure to common food allergens
b. Adding prebiotics and probiotics to the infant's diet
c. Adding omega-3 fatty acids to the diet
d. Delaying introduction of allergenic foods until after I year of age
Research supports early introduction of allergenic foods (like
peanuts and eggs) around 4—6 months of age as a strategy to
reduce the risk of developing food allergies. Delaying
introduction may increase the risk, especially in high-risk
infants.
