Leukemia in Children
. Types:
• Acute lymphoblastic leukemia (ALL) 80% (peak 2—5 years).
• Acute myeloid leukemia (AML)/ANLL most of remainder.
• CML and others rare.
Presentation (ALL/AML)
•General: malaise, anorexia, pallor, lethargy.
•Bone marrow infiltration: anemia, bleeding (thrombocytopenia), infection
(neutropenia), bone pain.
•Reticuloendothelial infiltration: hepatosplenomegaly, lymphadenopathy.
•Other organ infiltration: CNS (headache, vomiting, nerve palsies), testes (enlargement),
mediastinal mass (esp. T-cell).
