Jones Criteria (Diagnosis)
•Evidence of preceding strep infection (ASO, culture, antigen).
•2 major OR 1 major + 2 minor.
•Major: carditis, migratory polyarthritis, Sydenham chorea, nodules, erythema
marginatum.
•Minor: fever, polyarthralgia, TESR/CRP, prolonged PR interval.
