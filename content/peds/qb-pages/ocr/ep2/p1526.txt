47. Which of the following is the most common solid tumor in children?
A. Wilm's tumor
B. Retinoblastoma
C. Brain tumors
D. Kaposi sarcoma
E. Lymphomas
Brain tumors are the most common solid tumour in children
and are the leading cause Of childhood cancer deaths in the IJK.
In contrast to adults, brain tumours in children are almost
always primary rather than metastatic, and 60% are
infratentorial (located below the tentorium cerebelli).
