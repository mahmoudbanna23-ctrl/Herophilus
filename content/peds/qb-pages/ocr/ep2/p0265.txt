Investigations:
•No single test; suspect when:
• Thrombocytopenia
• prolonged APTT
• Low fibrinogen, high D-dimers
• Microangiopathic hemolytic anemia
• Low protein C/S and antithrombin
Management:
•Treat underlying cause (e.g., sepsis)
•Supportive care: FFP, cryoprecipitate, platelets
•Heparin use controversial
Differential Considerations in Children with Purpura:
•Henoch-Schonlein purpura: non-thrombocytopenic, joint pain, abdominal pain, hematuria
•Sepsis (meningococcal/viral): fever, septicemia, positive glass test (rash doesn't blanch)
•Immune thrombocytopenia (ITP): usually self-limiting; treatment if major bleeding
•Leukemia: malaise, pallor, hepatosplenomegaly, lymphadenopathy, blood film with sts
