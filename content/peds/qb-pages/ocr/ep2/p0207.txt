20. Mr. and Mrs. Walsh attend the clinic with their new baby, Ophelia, who has
Down syndrome. They are keen to have further children and want to know more
about their future risk of having children with Down syndrome. What
chromosomal abnormality is most likely to have caused Ophelia's condition?
A. Mosaicism
B. Nondisjunction
C. Point mutation
D. Translocation
E. Triplet repeat expansion
Most cases Of Down syndrome are due to nondisjunction, a
random error during meiosis. Risk increases with maternal age.
