69. What is the most probable type of abnormality found on karyotyping in cases
of Down syndrome?
A. Mitotic nondisjunction
B. Translocation
C. Meiotic nondisjunction
D. Mosaicism
Most cases of Down syndrome result from trisomy 21 due to
meiotic nondisjunction during gamete formation.
