51. In which neurological disorder does "muscle fasciculations" occur?
A. Spinal muscular atrophy
B. Peripheral neuropathies
C. Myopathies
D. Neuromuscular junction disorders
E. Cerebral palsy
Muscle fasciculations occur due to denervation, commonly
seen in motor neuron disorders like spinal muscular atrophy.
