38. What is the optimal emergency management of a child presenting to the ER
with severe croup?
A. Suction of the pharynx
B. IV epinephrine
C. Oral ampicillin
D. Nebulized Epinephrine
E. Immediate intubation
Nebulized epinephrine reduces airway swelling rapidly and is
first-line emergency treatment for severe croup.
