Causes of Abnormal Development
Prenatal: genetic, structural brain defects, cerebrovascular, metabolic, teratogenic,
congenital infections, neurocutaneous syndromes
Perinatal: extreme prematurity, asphyxia, hypoglycemia, hyperbilirubinemia, IVH, HIE
Postnatal: infection, trauma, metabolic disorders, malnutrition, neglect
Developmental Assessment
•Observation: first moment, during play, interaction with parent/carer.
•Play-based assessment: cubes, balls, dolls, pencils, paper, pegboard, picture books.
•Physical exam: weight, height, head circumference, dysmorphic features, skin lesions,
neurological exam, vision/hearing, cardiovascular, abdominal assessment.
•Goal: describe abilities in gross motor, fine motor/vision, hearing, speech,
social/emotional behavior, and determine normality for age.
