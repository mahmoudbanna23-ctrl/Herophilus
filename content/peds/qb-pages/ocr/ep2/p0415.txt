73. An 18-month-old boy whose main diet is cow milk presents with pallor. Stool
is negative for blood. Which lab test will most probably reveal the diagnosis?
a. Chest Xray
b. Examination of stool for ova and parasites
c. Blood reticulocyte count
d. Serum ferritin
e. Bone marrow aspirate
Cow's milk diet predisposes to iron deficiency anemia. Serum
ferritin is the best test for confirming low iron stores.
