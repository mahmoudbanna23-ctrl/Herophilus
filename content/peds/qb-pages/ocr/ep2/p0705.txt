38. A 14-year-old boy with a small ventricular septal presents to the
emergency department with a 2-week history of lethargy and night sweats. On
examination, he is pale, has a temperature of 390C, and looks unwell.
Auscultation reveals a loud systolic murmur audible throughout his precordium.
There is a palpable splenic tip, and urinalysis shows microscopic hematuria. What
is the next step?
a. Perform at least 3 sets of blood cultures
b. Immediate surgical repair ofVSD
c. Start empiric antibiotics without cultures
d. Order an ECG
This child has infective endocarditis; blood cultures are
essential before starting antibiotics to guide therapy.
