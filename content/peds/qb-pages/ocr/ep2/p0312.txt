This child presents with anemia, jaundice following a viral illness,
splenomegaly, and a microcytic picture on blood film, which can be seen in
hereditary spherocytosis, especially during aplastic crises. Though the MCV is
low (which is more typical of thalassemia), spherocytes may have a falsely
low MCV. Hereditary spherocytosis is also more common in Caucasian
children and can worsen during infections, consistent with her history.
