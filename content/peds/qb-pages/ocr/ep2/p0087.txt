Types of CP
B. Dyskinetic CP
•Cause: basal ganglia damage (historically hyperbilirubinemia, now HIE)
•Movements: involuntary, uncontrolled, dystonia, chorea, athetosis
•Presentation: floppy, poor trunk control, delayed motor development
•GMFCS: often IV-v
C. Ataxic CP
•Cause: cerebellar or genetic
•Features: hypotonia, poor balance, intention tremor, ataxic gait
D. Mixed cp
•Combination (usually spastic + dyskinetic)
