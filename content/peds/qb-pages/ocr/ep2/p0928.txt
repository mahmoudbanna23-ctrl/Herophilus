54. A 4-month-old girl presents with a urinary tract infection and a positive
dipstick test. What is the next step?
a. Clean catch for urine culture
b. Suprapubic aspiration
c. Start antibiotics immediately without culture
d. Renal ultrasound
In infants with a positive dipstick suggesting UTI, a urine culture must be
obtained to confirm the diagnosis and guide antibiotics. A clean catch
specimen is the preferred method in a 4-month-old, while suprapubic
aspiration is reserved for cases where clean catch is not possible. Renal
ultrasound is done later after diagnosis, not as the first step
