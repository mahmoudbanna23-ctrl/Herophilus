23. Which of the following is the most common malignant tumor in children?
A. Leukemia
B. Lymphoma
C. Kaposi sarcoma
D. Wilm's tumor
For children in the Western world, leukaemia is the most common malignancy
followed by brain tumours. Acute lymphoblastic leukaemia (ALL) accounts for 80%
Of leukaemia in children. Most Of the remainder is acute myeloid leukaemia/acute
non-lymphocytic leukaemia (AML/ANLL). Chronic myeloid leukaemia and other
myeloproliferative disorders are rare.
