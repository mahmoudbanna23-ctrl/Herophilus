upper respiratory tract infection. Yesterday, he developed bruises on his trunk and
generalized petechiae, more prominent on the legs. On physical examination, he
appears well, with no lymphadenopathy or hepatosplenomegaly. Laboratory

platelet count is 20,000/mm3. Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis?
a) Thrombotic thrombocytopenic purpura
b) Von Willebrand disease
c) Acute lymphoblastic leukemia
