Blood cultures and echocardiography are essential to diagnose infective
endocarditis. Cultures identify the pathogen, while echo confirms vegetations.
A moderate to large PDA causes tachypnea, failure to thrive, and bounding
pulses due to left-to-right shunting. The murmur is typically heard below the left
clavicle.
• In infants under 2 months, UTIs require IV antibiotics to prevent sepsis.
Prompt treatment prevents renal damage and systemic infection.
Post-streptococcal glomerulonephritis occurs 1—2 weeks after a streptococcal
infection. It presents with hematuria, edema, and hypertension due to immune
complex deposition.
