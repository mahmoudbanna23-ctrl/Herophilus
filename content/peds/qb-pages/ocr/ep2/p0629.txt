I. Alan, a 4-month-old boy, sees his general practitioner for an ear infection. On
listening to his chest, a heart murmur is heard. Which one of the following
features most suggests that it requires further investigation?
A. A thrill
B. Disappearance of murmur when lying flat
C. Murmur maximal at the left sternal edge
D. Sinus arrhythmia
E. Systolic murmur
A thrill indicates a palpable vibration caused by turbulent blood
flow, typically associated with significant structural heart
disease. Its presence warrants further investigation to rule Out
a pathological murmur.
