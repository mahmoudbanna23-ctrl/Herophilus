Alagille Syndrome
•Autosomal dominant, variable severity.
•Features: triangular facies, butterfly vertebrae, congenital heart disease (pulmonary
stenosis), renal & eye anomalies, severe pruritus, cholestasis.
•Treatment: supportive; some require liver transplant.
•Mortality often due to cardiac disease.
al-Antitrypsin Deficiency
AR, incidence (UK).
•PiZZ phenotype misfolded protein accumulates in hepatocytes.
•Infants: prolonged jaundice, vitamin K—related bleeding, hepatomegaly.
•Prognosis: 50% good outcome; others cirrhosis, portal HTN, transplant.
•Adults: emphysema (esp. smokers).
