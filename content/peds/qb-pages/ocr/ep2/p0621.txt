Clinical Features
•Onset 2—6 weeks post-GAS pharyngitis/pyoderma.
•Polyarthritis (migratory, large joints, earliest sign).
•Carditis (60%):
• Endocarditis (valvulitis murmurs, dysfunction).
• Myocarditis (HF, arrhythmia).
• Pericarditis (rub, effusion, tamponade).
•Sydenham chorea (20%): involuntary movements, emotional lability.
•Subcutaneous nodules (<IO%).
•Erythema marginatum (<5%).
•Labs: ESR/CRP, ASO T, ECG (PR prolongation).
