5. Red Cell Aplasia
•Causes in children:
• Diamond-Blackfan anemia (DBA) — congenital, genetic (ribosomal protein genes)
• Transient erythroblastopenia of childhood (TEC) — viral trigger, self-limiting
• Parvovirus B19 infection — transient, affects children with hemolytic disorders
•Diagnostic clues: Low reticulocytes, normal bilirubin, negative Coombs, absent marrow
precursors.
•DBA treatment: Steroids, transfusions, stem cell transplantation for severe cases.
