Investigations
•Genetic: microarray, Fragile X, whole genome sequencing
•Biochemical/Metabolic: U&E, CK, TFTs, vitamins, metabolic panels
•Imaging: cranial US, CT, MRI (abnormal neurology only)
•Neurophysiology: EEG, nerve conduction, EMG
•Specialist-guided metabolic tests
Motor Development: Normal vs Abnormal
•Normal milestones: lifts head, sits, pulls to stand, walks independently.
•Abnormal signs: poor head control, stiff/floppy limbs, asymmetry, inability to
crawl/stand, abnormal gait, involuntary movements.
•Cerebral palsy (hemiplegia/quadriplegia) is most common.
•Early detection allows interventions and monitoring of secondary complications
(epilepsy, scoliosis, sensory impairments, sleep, growth disorders).
