70. Which clinical condition is associated with a diastolic cardiac murmur?
A. Anemia
B. Hyperthyroidism with hyperdynamic circulation
C. Mitral stenosis
D. Aortic stenosis
E. Fever
Diastolic murmurs arise from turbulent blood flow during
ventricular filling, commonly due to mitral stenosis.
