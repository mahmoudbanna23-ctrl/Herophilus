I l. Darren is a 3-year-old boy who has difficulty climbing stairs. He always needs
support. He walked unsupported at 14 months. His development is otherwise
normal. On examination, leg power is reduced, he is hypotonic, but reflexes are
normal. Which investigation would you initially undertake to confirm the
diagnosis?
A. Chromosome karyotype
B. Creatine kinase
C. CT or MRI scan of the brain
D. Congenital infection screen
E. Thyroid function tests
Proximal weakness with hypotonia and delayed gross motor
milestones Suggest a muscular dystrophy. Raised CK levels
support the diagnosis.
