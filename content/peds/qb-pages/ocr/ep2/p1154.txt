General Clinical Features of Acute Viral Hepatitis
•Nausea, vomiting, abdominal pain, lethargy, jaundice (30—50% of children do not
develop jaundice).
•Large tender liver common, 30% also have splenomegaly.
•Markedly raised transaminases.
•Coagulation usually normal.
