50. Most cases of community-acquired pneumonia in children under 5 years are
caused by:
a. Viral pneumonia
b. Streptococcus pneumoniae
c. Mycoplasma pneumoniae
d. Staphylococcus aureus
e. Haemophilus influenzae
Viral infections, especially RSV and parainfluenza, are the most
frequent cause of pneumonia in young children. Bacterial
causes become more prominent with age.
