Etiology
•Often early in viral infections with rapidly rising temp.
•30—40% may recur (more likely in younger children, lower fever, short illness, +family
history).
Exclusions
Not FS if: CNS infection, metabolic abnormalities, prior afebrile or neonatal seizures.
Management
•Reassure & educate parents.
•Teach seizure first aid.
•Antipyretics: may help comfort, but do not prevent FS.
•Rescue therapy: buccal midazolam for prolonged seizures (>5 min).
•No long-term prophylactic AEDs (risks > benefits).
