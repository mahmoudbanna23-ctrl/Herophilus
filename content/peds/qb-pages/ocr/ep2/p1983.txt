In a child with G6PD deficiency, oxidative drugs such as sulfonamides trigger
hemolysis. This results in dark urine, jaundice, and anemia following exposure.
Both urinary tract infection and post-streptococcal glomerulonephritis can
cause hematuria in children. UTIs produce dysuria and frequency
, while PSGN
follows streptococcal infections.
Hemoglobin electrophoresis is the diagnostic test for sickle cell disease and
thalassemia. It identifies abnormal hemoglobin variants such as HbS or
increased HbA2.
'A newborn boy with prolonged bleeding and markedly prolonged APTT likely
has haemophilia A. The deficiency of factor VIII causes impaired intrinsic
pathway clotting.
