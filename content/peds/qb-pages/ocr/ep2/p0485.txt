5. Amir, a 4-year-old boy, presents to his family doctor with a history of eczema,
rhinitis, chronic nocturnal cough, and intermittent wheeze. Asthma is suspected,
and a bronchodilator is prescribed. How should his bronchodilator be delivered?
A. Dry powder inhaler
B. Metered-dose inhaler (MDI)
D. Nebulizer
E. Syrup
In preschool children, bronchodilators should be delivered via
an MDI with a spacer for optimal lung deposition. Dry powder
inhalers are not suitable for young children due to insufficient
inspiratory flow.
