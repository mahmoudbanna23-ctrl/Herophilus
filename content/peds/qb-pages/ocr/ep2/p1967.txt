Empyema is pus accumulation in the pleural cavity following pneumonia. It
presents with fever, stony dull percussion, diminished breath sounds, and
reduced chest expansion on the affected side.
Streptococcus pneumoniae is the most frequent cause of lobar pneumonia in
children. It presents with fever, cough, localized bronchial breathing, and
consolidation signs on chest examination.
• Most cases of Down syndrome result from trisomy 21 caused by meiotic
nondisjunction during gametogenesis. This error leads to an extra chromosome
21 in all body cells.
Mitral stenosis produces
a diastolic murmur due to turbulent blood flow
across a narrowed mitral valve during ventricular filling. It is best heard at e
apex with the patient in the left lateral position.
