Leukemia Treatment Phases
Remission Induction
•Supportive care before treatment:
• Correct anemia (blood transfusion).
• Platelets to reduce bleeding risk.
• Treat infection.
• Hydration + allopurinol/urate oxidase (to prevent tumor lysis renal damage).
•Definition of remission:
• Eradication of leukemic blasts.
• Normal marrow function restored.
•Treatment: Combination chemotherapy incl. steroids.
•Outcome: --95% remission rate.
Intensification (Consolidation)
•Intensive chemotherapy block.
•Improves cure rates but increases toxicity.
