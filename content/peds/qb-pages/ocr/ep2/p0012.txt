Hearing, Speech & Language
•4 months: Coos, vocalizes "aa".
•6 months: Laughs, turns to soft sounds.
•7—10 months: Responds to sounds, babbling.
•12 months: Says 2—3 words (besides mama/dada).
•18 months: 6—10 words, points to 2 body parts.
•2 years: Joins 2 words into simple sentences.
•2.5—3 years: Talks constantly in 3—4 word sentences, hand dominance begins.
