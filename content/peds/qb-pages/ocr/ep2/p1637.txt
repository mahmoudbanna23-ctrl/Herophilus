21. A 6-year-old boy with a history of large VSD presents with central cyanosis
and oxygen saturation of 81 %. His parents previously refused surgical repair. His
height and weight are below the 5th percentile.
What is the pathophysiologic mechanism ofhis cyanosis?
A. Reduced cardiac output
B. Shunt reversal
C. Heart block
D. Tachyarrhythmia
E. Methemoglobinemia
Long-standing large VSD can cause Eisenmenger syndrome,
where left-to-right shunt reverses, leading to cyanosis.
