Complications of Chronic Liver Failure
5 Hepatic Encephalopathy
•Precipitated by Gl bleed, sepsis, sedatives, renal failure, electrolyte imbalance.
Symptoms: irritability/sleepiness (infants), mood/behavioral disturbance (children).
'Diagnosis: ammonia, abnormal EEG.
• Treatment: lactulose, non-absorbable antibiotics (rifaximin).
6 Esophageal Varices
• Due to portal hypertension, rapid onset in children.
• Diagnosis: endoscopy.
•Treatment: blood transfusion, H2 blockers/PPls, octreotide, vasopressin analogues, endoscopic
•banding/sclerotherapy, TIPS if refractory.
7.Ascites
• Mechanisms: hypoalbuminemia, sodium retention, renal impairment, fluid redistribution.
Treatment: sodium/fluid restriction, diuretics, albumin infusion, paracentesis.

•Features: jaundice, spider naevi, palmar erythema, malnutrition, splenomegaly, varices, ascit
