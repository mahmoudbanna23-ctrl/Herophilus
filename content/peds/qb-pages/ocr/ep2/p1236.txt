7. Essa, a 2-year-old boy, presents with a white reflex in one eye (leukocoria).
What is the likely cause?
A. Congenital cataract
B. Glaucoma
C. Retinoblastoma
D. Allergic conjunctivitis
E. VI nerve palsy
A white reflex in a child's eye is a classic sign of retinoblastoma.
Early diagnosis is vital to preserve vision and life.
