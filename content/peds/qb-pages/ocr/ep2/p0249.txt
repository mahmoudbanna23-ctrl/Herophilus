•Clinical features: Often asymptomatic until Hb g/L; may see pallor, pica,
anorexia, angular stomatitis.
•Diagnosis: Microcytic hypochromic anemia, low serum ferritin, blood film changes.

• Oral iron supplementation until Hb normal + 3 months for stores.
• Dietary advice.
• Investigate malabsorption or chronic blood loss if no response.
