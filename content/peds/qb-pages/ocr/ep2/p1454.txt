I I. Which of the following is a feature of pediatric nephrotic syndrome?
A. Low C3 level
B. It is usually associated with hematuria
C. It rarely causes chronic renal failure
D. It is usually associated with hypertension
Pediatric nephrotic syndrome (steroid-sensitive) typically
presents with normal blood pressure, normal complement
levels, and no hematuria. It rarely leads to chronic renal failure.
