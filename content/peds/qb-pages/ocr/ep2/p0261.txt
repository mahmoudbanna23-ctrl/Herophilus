Common Genetic Hemolytic Disorders
•Hereditary Spherocytosis: autosomal dominant; may cause jaundice, splenomegaly,
gallstones; treat with folic acid, splenectomy if severe
•G6PD Deficiency•
. X-Iinked; triggers: infection, drugs, fava beans; management:

•Hemoglobinopathies:
• 6-Thalassemia Major: severe anemia, hepatosplenomegaly; requires lifelong
transfusions + iron chelation; bone marrow transplant is curative
• a-Thalassemia Major (Hb Barts): fatal in utero or at birth
• Sickle Cell Disease: autosomal recessive; sickled RBCs vaso-occlusion, hemolysis,
