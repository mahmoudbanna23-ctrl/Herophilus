Pediatric Bleeding Disorders
3. Platelet Disorders
•Thrombocytopenia: <150 x 109/L
• Causes: immune (ITP), congenital, marrow disorders, drugs, infections
• Clinical: bruising, petechiae, purpura, mucosal bleeding
•Immune Thrombocytopenia (ITP): autoimmune platelet destruction; usually self-
limiting; treat severe or persistent bleeding with steroids, IVIG, or thrombopoietin
agonists
4. Diagnostic Workup
•CBC & blood film
•Coagulation studies: PT, APTT, thrombin time, fibrinogen, D-dimers
•Factor assays & genetic tests for suspected inherited disorders
•Platelet function studies if platelet count normal but bleeding present
