coryzal symptoms. He is afebrile but has marked intercostal recession, and a
'barking cough' is heard. What is the most likely causative organism?
A. Adenovirus
B. Respiratory syncytial virus
C. Parainfluenza virus
D. Rhinovirus
E. Influenza virus
