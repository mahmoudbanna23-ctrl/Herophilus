55. Which of the following is the diagnostic test for hereditary spherocytosis?
a. Bone marrow examination
b. Coombs test
c. Osmotic fragility test
d. Hemoglobin electrophoresis
Hereditary spherocytosis is confirmed by osmotic fragility
testing. It shows increased red cell lysis in hypotonic solutions.
