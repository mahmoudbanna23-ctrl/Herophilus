66. Which of the following can be used as a treatment modality in cerebral palsy?
A. Resection of the affected part of the brain
B. Treatment of the cause of cerebral palsy
C. Restriction of certain proteins in the diet
D. Botulinum toxin injections to the spastic muscles
Botulinum toxin reduces spasticity and improves mobility in
children with cerebral palsy, but it does not cure the underlying
brain injury.
