2. Which disease can commonly be discovered during follow-up of a child with
Down syndrome?
a. Celiac disease
b. Hyperthyroidism
c. Type I diabetes mellitus
d. Asthma
Children with Down syndrome have a higher risk Of
autoimmune disorders, especially celiac disease and
hypothyroidism. Regular screening is recommended during
follow-up.
