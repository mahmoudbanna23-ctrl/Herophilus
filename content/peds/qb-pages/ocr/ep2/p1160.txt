Hepatitis C (HCV)
•RNA virus, main cause of post-transfusion hepatitis (before 1991).
•High prevalence in IV drug users; vertical transmission most common in children (6%,
with HIV).
•Clinical: rarely acute infection, but most chronic carriers.
• Lifetime risk of cirrhosis/HCC: 20—25%.
• Treatment:
• Direct-acting antivirals (sofosbuvir, ledipasvir, glecaprevir, pibrentasvir): 98—100%
curative.
• Licensed in children >3 years (US), >12 years (EU).
• Not treated before age 3 (possible spontaneous clearance).
screening of high-risk mothers/children.
