51. Sudden onset of edema starting in eyelids, then lower limbs, then ascites is
most likely due to:
a. Renal disease
b. Hepatic disease
c. Allergy
d. Cardiac disease
Nephrotic syndrome typically begins with periorbital edema
that progresses to generalized edema and ascites.
