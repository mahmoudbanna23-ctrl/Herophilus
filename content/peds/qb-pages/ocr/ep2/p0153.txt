29. What is the most likely cause of faltering growth in a 9-month-old infant?
a. Small atrial septal defect (ASD)
b. Persistent diarrhea
c. Acute otitis media
d. Minor umbilical hernia
Chronic diarrhea leads to malabsorption and poor weight gain, making it
the most likely cause Of faltering growth. Minor ASD, Otitis media, or
umbilical hernia are less likely to cause significant growth failure.
