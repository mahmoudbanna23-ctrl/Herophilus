RR 60 bpm. Examination reveals absent femoral pulses and a palpable liver edge 4 cm
below the costal margin. Chest X-ray shows a normal cardiac silhouette with clear lung
fields. Initial blood tests reveal pH 6.9, pC02 16 mmHg, and elevated lactate. What is
the next most appropriate management?
a. Diuretics
b. High flow oxygen via a non- rebreathable mask
c. Intubation and ventilation
d. Prostaglandin infusion
e. Transfer to a cardiac center
Prostaglandin keeps the ductus arteriosus open in duct-
dependent lesions like coarctation or critical aortic stenosis,
stabilizing circulation.
