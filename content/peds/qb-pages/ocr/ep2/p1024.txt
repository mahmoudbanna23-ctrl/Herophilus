34. Which of the following is not true about cerebral palsy?
a. Meningitis/encephalitis is the most common cause.
b. Preterm infants are more vulnerable
c. Spastic form is the most common.
d. Clinical manifestations can evolve over time.
e. Often accompanied by disturbances of cognition.
Cerebral palsy is usually caused by antenatal or perinatal brain injury (e.g.,
prematurity, hypoxic-ischemic insult), not meningitis/encephalitis. Spastic
type is most common, and associated problems like cognitive issues may
occur.
