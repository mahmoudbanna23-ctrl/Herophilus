feed. He was born at 27 weeks' gestation, weighing 979 g, and was discharged
home at 3 months of age. On examination, he has a temperature of 37.40C and a
respiratory rate of 60 breaths/min. He is coughing. His chest is hyperinflated with
marked intercostal recession. On auscultation, there are generalized fine crackles
and wheezes. Select the most likely diagnosis:
a. Acute asthma
b. Bronchiolitis

preterm babies, and presents with wheeze, crackles, and
respiratory distress. The hyperinflated chest and feeding
difficulty support this diagnosis.
