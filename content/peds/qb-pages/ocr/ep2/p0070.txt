26. Which of the following is not typically seen in a 6-week-old baby?
a. Smiling
b. Fixing and following with eyes
c. Vocalizing
d. Grasping objects
At 6 weeks, babies begin smiling, vocalizing, and visually tracking. Grasping
objects intentionally develops later, typically around 3—4 months.
