Hemolytic Uremic Syndrome (HW)
Definition & Triad
H US Acute renal failure + Microangiopathic hemolytic anemia + Thrombocytopenia
Causes
•Typical (post-diarrheal, most common):
• Verocytotoxin-producing E. coli 0157:H7 (from farm animals, undercooked beef).
• Shigella (less common).
• Follows bloody diarrhea prodrome.
•Atypical:
• No diarrheal prodrome.
• Often genetic (complement pathway dysregulation).
• Relapsing, more severe.
