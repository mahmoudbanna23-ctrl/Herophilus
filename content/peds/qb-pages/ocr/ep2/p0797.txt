Urinary Tract Obstruction
•Levels: pelviuretericjunction, vesicoureteric junction, bladder neck, posterior urethral
valves (boys).
•Consequences: hydronephrosis, dysplasia, CKD, Potter sequence.
•Postnatal care: delayed ultrasound, prophylactic antibiotics, MCUG in bilateral
hydronephrosis (boys).
Vesicoureteric Reflux (VIJR)
•Defective ureter entry into bladder reflux.
•Familial (30—50%), may resolve with age.
•Complications: recurrent UTI, pyelonephritis, reflux nephropathy (scarring CKD, HTN).
•Severe cases surgery.
