23. Development in children refers to changes occurring over which age range?
a. Birth to I year
b. Birth to 3 years
c. Birth to 5 years
d. Birth to 10 years
Development spans multiple domains (motor, language, social, etc.) and occurs
rapidly from birth to around 5 years. This is the most critical period for observing
developmental milestones.
