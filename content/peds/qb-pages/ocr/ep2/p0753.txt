61. One week old present with shock. What is most congenital heart disease that
can cause?
a. Coarctation of aorta
b. VSD
c. Fallot
d. ASD
Coarctation Often presents when the ductus arteriosus closes,
leading to shock and poor perfusion. VSD, ASD, and Fallot
usually don't present this early with shock.
