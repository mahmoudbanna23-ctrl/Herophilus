24. A 3-day-old male infant is noted to have a cardiac murmur. Four-limb blood
pressures are normal, but oxygen saturation is 78% and does not improve with
oxygen therapy. Which of the following congenital heart defects is the most likely
diagnosis?
A. Patent ductus arteriosus
B. Tetra10kY of Fallot
C. Ventricular septal defect
D. Atrial septal defect
E. Coarctation of the aorta
Impaired oxygenation suggests right-to-left shunt, which is
caused by tetralogy of Fallot or TGA. Tetralogy of Fallot
comprises pulmonary stenosis, overriding aorta,
ventricular septal defect and right ventricular hypertrophy.
