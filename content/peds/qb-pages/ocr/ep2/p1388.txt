59. What is the most common solid childhood cancer?
A. Lymphomas
B. Brain tumors
C. Wilms tumor
D. Soft tissue sarcomas
E. Neuroblastoma
Brain tumors are the most common solid tumors in children,
more frequent than Wilms tumor or neuroblastoma.
