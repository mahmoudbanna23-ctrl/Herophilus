56. From the following medications, pick the drug that is not used as a
"preventer" in asthma therapy:
a. Antihistamines
b. Inhaled corticosteroids
c. Leukotriene receptor antagonists
d. Slow-release oral theophylline
e. Long-acting beta agonists (LABA)
Antihistamines are not part of standard asthma preventer
therapy. Preventers like inhaled corticosteroids and leukotriene
antagonists reduce inflammation and prevent attacks, whereas
antihistamines are used in allergic rhinitis.
