I. Liam, a 7-year-old boy, complains to his family doctor of a sore throat and has
a mild fever. The appearance of his throat is as shown in the opposite figure.
What is the most likely diagnosis?
A. Diphtheria
B. Glandular fever (Epstein—Barr virus)
C. Group A Streptococcal tonsillitis
D. Measles
E. Herpes simplex stomatitis
Streptococcal tonsillitis presents with sore throat, fever, and
tonsillar exudates, especially in school-aged children. The
absence of systemic features like rash or gray
pseudomembrane helps exclude other options like measles or
diphtheria.
