27. The following is NOT characteristic of typical (simple) febrile convulsion:
a. May recur in 24 hours
b. Last less than 15 minutes
c. Always generalized
d. No post-ictal manifestations
e. Not preceded by previous non-febrile seizures
Simple febrile seizures are generalized, last <15 minutes, and
occur once per febrile illness. Recurrence within 24 hours or
being focal suggests a complex febrile seizure, not simple.
