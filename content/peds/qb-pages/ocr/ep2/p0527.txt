26. Bronchiolitis is one of the most common respiratory conditions affecting
infants. Which of the following is not a clinical feature of bronchiolitis?
A. Tachypnoea
B. Apnoea
C. Wheeze
D. Fine crackles
E. Paroxysmal cough
Bronchiolitis causes tachypnea, wheeze, and fine crackles but
does not present with paroxysmal cough, which is more
characteristic of pertussis.
