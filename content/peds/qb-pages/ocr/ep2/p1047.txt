45. Which of the following is not typical for a febrile seizure?
a. Recurrence or may be focal and generalized
b. Occurs in children 6 months to 5 years
c. Usually lasts less than 15 minutes
d. No underlying CNS infection or metabolic cause
Simple febrile seizures are generalized, single, short, and occur
without CNS infection. Recurrence or focality indicates complex
febrile seizures.
