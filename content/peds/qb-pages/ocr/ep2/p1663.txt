34. A 5-year-old girl with type I diabetes mellitus diagnosed two months ago is
running around the garden when her mother notices she suddenly becomes
aggressive toward her brother and appears pale. Her blood glucose is 50 mg/dL.
What is the best action to take immediately?
A. Intramuscular (1M) glucagon
B. Intravenous infusion of 5% glucose
C. Intravenous infusion of normal saline (0.9% sodium chloride)
D. Oral glucose drink
E. Subcutaneous insulin
The child is conscious and hypoglycemic. Immediate oral glucose is
safe and effective; IV or 1M routes are needed only if the child cannot
take orally.
