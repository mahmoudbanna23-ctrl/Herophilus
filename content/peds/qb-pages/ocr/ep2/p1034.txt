39. A toddler cries, holds his breath, and turns blue, with a brief period of loss of
consciousness. What is the most likely diagnosis?
a. Blue breath-holding spells
b. Seizure disorder
c. Cardiac arrhythmia
d. Syncope due to dehydration
These are benign episodes triggered by crying, leading to
breath-holding, cyanosis, and brief unconsciousness. They are
not epileptic seizures.
