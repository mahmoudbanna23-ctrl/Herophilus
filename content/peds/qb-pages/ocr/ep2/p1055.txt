49. Dora is an 8-year-old girl who used to excel at school but is now
underperforming. In clinic, she has brief episodes where she suddenly stops,
stares ahead, flickers her eyelids for a few seconds, and then resumes her activity.
Asking her to perform an action like blowing out an imaginary candle triggers the
same episode. She is otherwise healthy. What is the most likely diagnosis?
a. Absence seizures
b. Focal seizures
c. Myoclonic seizures
d. Tonic-clonic seizures
Absence seizures are brief staring episodes, sometimes with
eyelid flutter, often provoked by hyperventilation
