46. A 9-year-old girl is brought to her GP with a 2-month history of headaches.
Which of the following features of her headaches is not a red-flag symptom?
A. Associated with vomiting
B. Morning headache
C. Worse on lying down
D. Presence of focal neurology
E. Symmetrical, band-like in nature
Red-flag headache features include vomiting, morning
headaches, worsening when lying down, or focal neuro signs. A
band-like headache is typically tension-type and not a red flag.
