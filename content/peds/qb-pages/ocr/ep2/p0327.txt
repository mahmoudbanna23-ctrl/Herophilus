Hb (hemoglobin): 86 g/L' WBC (white blood cell count): 10.2 x 109/L
Platelet count: 350 x 109/1* Prothrombin time: 16 seconds (control: 12—15 seconds)
Activated partial thromboplastin time: 46 seconds (control: 25—35 seconds)
Fibrinogen: 2.5 g/L (normal: 2—4 g/L) Factor VIII: just below the normal range
What is the most likely diagnosis?
A. Vitamin K deficiency
B. Liver disease
