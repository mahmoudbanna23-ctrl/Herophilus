43. Red urine is NOT a feature in:
a. Nephritis
b. Henoch Schönlein purpura
c. Favism
d. Congestive heart failure
e. Administration of rifampicin
Red urine occurs in nephritis, HSP, favism, or drugs (rifampicin),
but not in CHF.
