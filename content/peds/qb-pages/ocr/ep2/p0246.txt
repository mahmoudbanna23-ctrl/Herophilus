1. Hemopoiesis
•Definition: Lifelong production of blood cells.
•Primary site: Bone marrow.
•Stem cells: Hemopoietic stem cells are crucial for normal blood production; deficiencies
bone marrow failure (e.g., aplastic anemia).
•Clinical use: Stem cells can be transplanted for therapy (e.g., children with marrow
failure).
