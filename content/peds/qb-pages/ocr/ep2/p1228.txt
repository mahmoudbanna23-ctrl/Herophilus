3. Mohammed is a 3-year-old boy with weight loss, lethargy, and abdominal pain.
On examination, he has an extensive abdominal mass. Which of the following
investigations would be most useful in making the diagnosis?
A. Plasma ammonia
B. Plasma LDH (lactate dehydrogenase)
C. Serum a-fetoprotein
D. Serum ß-hCG
E rin
h I min
Neuroblastoma produces catecholamines, so raised urinary
catecholamine levels confirm the diagnosis. Other markers are
used for different tumours.
