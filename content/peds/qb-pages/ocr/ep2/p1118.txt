13. A newbom is noted to have coarse facies, a large fontanelle, hypotonia, and
jaundice. Blood sugar levels are normal. Which investigation is most likely to
reveal the diagnosis?
A. Growth hormone
B. Karyotype
C. Thyroid function tests
D. Blood group
E. Creatine kinase (CK)
Coarse facies, hypotonia, prolonged jaundice, and large
fontanelle are classical signs Of congenital hypothyroidism. A
thyroid function test will confirm the diagnosis by showing low
T4 and high TSH levels.
