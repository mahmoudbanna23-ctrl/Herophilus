26. Which of the following is NOT characteristic of autism spectrum disorder?
a. Repetitive actions
b. Sensory blocking and echolalia
c. Language and learning deficiencies
d. Encopresis and overeating
e. Highly restricted and fixated interests.
Autism is characterized by repetitive behaviors, communication
problems, sensory issues, and restricted interests. Encopresis
and overeating are not typical features.
