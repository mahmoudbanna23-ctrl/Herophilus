59. Which laboratory finding supports the diagnosis of disseminated intravascular
coagulation (DIC)?
A. Thrombocytosis
B. Elevated D-dimer
C. Elevated antithrombin, protein C and protein S
D. Normal PT
E. Normal APTT
DIC causes widespread clot formation and breakdown, which raises
D-dimer levels. Thrombocytopenia, prolonged PT/APTT, and low
clotting factors are also typical, unlike thrombocytosis or normal
coagulation tests.
