63. A 2-year-old presents with fever and a cough for 2 days. She has a fever of
380C, a respiratory rate of 25 breaths/min, and oxygen saturation of 98%. She is
having coryzal symptoms and an inflamed pharynx. Select the next step in
management from the list below:
a. Antipyretic/analgesia
b. Intravenous aciclovir
c. Intravenous antibiotics
d. Oral antibiotic
e. Oxygen
This child has no respiratory distress or hypoxia and likely has a
viral upper respiratory infection. Supportive care with
antipyretics is appropriate.
