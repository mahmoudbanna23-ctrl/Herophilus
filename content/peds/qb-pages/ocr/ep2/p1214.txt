Oncological Emergencies in Children
•Hematological:
• Bone marrow failure (anemia, neutropenia, thrombocytopenia).
• Hyperleukocytosis (>IOO x 109/L).
• Coagulopathy, DIC.
•Infectious: Bacterial, viral, fungal sepsis.
•Neurological: Raised ICP, spinal cord compression.
•Metabolic/Endocrine: Hypercalcemia, SIADH, diabetes insipidus.
•Other: Tumor lysis syndrome, airway obstruction, cardiac tamponade, SVCO.
