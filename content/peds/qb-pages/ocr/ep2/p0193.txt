13. Which of the following is NOT a commonly associated problem of Down
syndrome?
a. Congenital heart disease
b. Leukemia
c. Hypothyroidism
d. Shon stature
e. Gastro-esophageal reflux disease
Common associations include congenital heart disease,
hypothyroidism, leukemia, and short stature. GERD is not a
classic feature.
