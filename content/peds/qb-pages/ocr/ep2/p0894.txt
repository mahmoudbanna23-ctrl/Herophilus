38. The classical clinical presentation of minimal change nephrotic syndrome
DOES NOT include:
a. A child aged between 2-6 years
b. Normal blood pressure
c. Abnormal kidney function tests
d. Serum cholesterol and triglycerides
e. Response to corticosteroid therapy
Minimal change nephrotic syndrome usually has normal renal
function, normal BP, and responds well to steroids.
