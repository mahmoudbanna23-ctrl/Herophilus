2. Hematological Values at Birth & Early Life
•Hemoglobin (Hb):
• Birth: 140-215 g/L
• Nadir at 2—3 months: 100 g/L
• Preterm infants: 65—90 g/L at 4—8 weeks
•Hemoglobin types:
• HbF (60%) — high 02 affinity advantageous in fetus
• HbA (40%)
•Blood volume:
• Preterm: 100 ml/kg
• Term: 80 ml/kg
Platelets: 150—400x109/L, WBC: 10-25x109/L
•Other counts:
•Nutrient stores: Iron, folic acid, B12 adequate at birth; lower in preterm infants
