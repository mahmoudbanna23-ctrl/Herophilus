Hepatitis B (HBV)
•DNA virus, major cause of acute & chronic liver disease.
• Transmission :
• Perinatal (carrier mothers), horizontal spread in families.
• Blood transfusion, needlestick, dialysis.
• Sexual (adults).
•Clinical:
• Infants: usually asymptomatic, 90% chronic carriers.
• Older children: acute hepatitis; most resolve, but:
• 1—2% fulminant hepatic failure.
• 5—10% chronic carriers.
•Diagnosis:
• HBsAg: infectivity.
• Anti-HBs: immunity (past infection/vaccine).
• HBeAg: high transmissibility.
• Anti-HBc IgM: acute infection.
