5. Gloria is a 19-month-old girl who presents to you in primary care. Her health
visitor is concerned because she is still only babbling and says no distinct words.
She is able to walk, scribble with crayons, and feed herself with a spoon. What is
the most appropriate first action?
A. Hearing test
B. Assessment by a team specializing in autism spectrum disorders
C. Reassure the health visitor
D. Refer to an ear, nose, and throat surgeon
E. Refer to a paediatrician for a full developmental assessment
A child who babbles but has no distinct words should first be
evaluated for hearing loss, as it is a common and reversible
cause of speech delay.
