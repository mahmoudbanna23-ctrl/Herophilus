38. What is the diagnostic clue for anemia due to acute blood loss?
A. Normal retic count and high bilirubin
B. High retic count and high bilirubin
C. High retic count and normal bilirubin
D. High retic count and marked red cell hypochromia
E. Marked red cell hypochromia and microcytosis
Acute blood loss stimulates the bone marrow to produce
reticulocytes, while bilirubin remains normal because
hemolysis is not the cause.
