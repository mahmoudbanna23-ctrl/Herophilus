26. A 14-month-old boy presents with knee swelling and large bruises. CBC
shows normal platelets, prolonged APTT, and normal PT.
What is the most likely diagnosis?
A. Vitamin K deficiency
B. Hemophilia
C. Von Willebrand disease
D. Idiopathic thrombocytopenic purpura
E. Non-accidental injury
Prolonged APTT with normal PT and platelets indicates intrinsic
pathway defect, classic for hemophilia A Or B.
