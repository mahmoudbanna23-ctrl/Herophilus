Peripheral Motor Disorders (Neuromuscular)
Neuromuscular Junction Disorders
3.
•Fatigability worse with activity, improves with rest.
•Features: ptosis, ophthalmoplegia, proximal weakness.
•Example: Myasthenia gravis.
•Diagnosis: antibody testing, edrophonium/pyridostigmine response.
•Treatment: anticholinesterases, immunosuppressants, thymectomy, plasma exchange.
Primary Muscle Disorders (Myopathies)
4.
•Proximal weakness, wasting, waddling gait, positive Gowers' sign.
•Examples: muscular dystrophies (Duchenne, Becker), congenital myopathies,
inflammatory myositis, myotonic dystrophy, metabolic myopathies.
•Tests: CK ( T in dystrophies, myositis), DNA testing, muscle MRI/biopsy.
