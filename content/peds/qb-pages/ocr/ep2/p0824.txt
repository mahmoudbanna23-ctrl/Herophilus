3. Jonathan is an 8-year-old boy with a history of urinary tract infections. He now
presents with acute spasmodic pain in the left side of his abdomen, describing it
as the worst pain he has ever felt. He played football yesterday but did not sustain
any Injury. On examination, he is afebrile, his abdomen is soft with no guarding,
and he is tender over the left loin. Urinalysis reveals 2+ haematuria, but no
protein or leucocytes. What is the most likely cause of his pain?
A. Constipation
B. Glomerulonephritis
C. Renal stone
D. Trauma
E. Wilms tumor
Severe, colicky loin pain with microscopic hematuria and a
history Of LITIs strongly Suggests a renal Stone. The absence Of
fever and normal abdominal exam makes infection and trauma
less likely.
