Follow-up
•Check urine with any febrile illness.
•Consider prophylactic antibiotics in recurrent cases.
•Monitor BP annually if renal scarring/dysplasia.
•Screen urine for proteinuria (CKD risk).
•Renal growth & function monitoring if bilateral disease.
•Surgery (anti-VUR) for progressive scarring with high-grade reflux.
•Urodynamic studies for incomplete emptying; may need clean intermittent
catheterization.
