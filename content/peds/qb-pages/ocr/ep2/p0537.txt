31. Which of the following is the most common cause of pneumonia in infants
and young children?
a. Streptococcus pneumoniae
b. Mycoplasma
c. Viruses
d. Chlamydia
e. Haemophilus influenzae
Viral infections, particularly RSV and parainfluenza, are the
leading cause Of pneumonia in this age group. Bacterial causes
like Streptococcus pneumoniae are more common in Older
children.
