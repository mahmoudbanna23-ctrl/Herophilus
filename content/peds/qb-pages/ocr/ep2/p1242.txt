I O. Angel, a 2-year-old boy, has a left-sided abdominal mass that does not cross
the midline. What is the most likely diagnosis?
A. Neuroblastoma
B. Brain tumour
C. Wilms tumour
D. Acute lymphoblastic leukaemia
E. Hodgkin disease
Wilms tumour (nephroblastoma) is a renal tumour that stays
confined to one side and does not usually cross the midline.
Neuroblastomas often cross the midline.
