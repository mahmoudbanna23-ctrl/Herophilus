Proteinuria
•Normal: Protein-to-creatinine ratio < 20 mg/mmol.
•Types:
• Transient: during fever or after exercise benign, no work-up.
• Persistent: significant; needs quantification with early morning Pr/Cr ratio.
•Causes:
• Orthostatic proteinuria (benign, postural).
• Glomerular disease: nephrotic syndrome, glomerulonephritis, basement membrane
defects.
• Increased GFR pressure, CKD (reduced renal mass), hypertension, tubular proteinuria.
•Orthostatic proteinuria: only in daytime; diagnosed with serial morning samples; excellent
prognosis.
Investigation of Proteinuria
•Normal: Pr/Cr <0.2, dipstick <1+, <IOO mg/day.
•Non-nephrotic: Pr/Cr 0.2—2, dipstick 2+, 100—1000 mg/day.
•Nephrotic: Pr/Cr >2, dipstick >3+, >IOOO mg/day.
