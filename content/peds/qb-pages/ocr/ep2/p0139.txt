22. Which of the following is NOT true about autism spectrum disorder?
a. The child may have associated comorbidities
b. It is usually benign with no long-term consequences
c. It is an impaired social interaction
d. There is no evidence for a link with MMR vaccine
Autism is a lifelong condition with significant impact on social interaction and
communication. It is not benign, and comorbidities like epilepsy and
intellectual disability are common.
