79. What does the term "seizure" mean?
A. A paroxysmal abnormality of motor, sensory or autonomic function due
to transient brain dysfunction
B. An abnormal electric activity of the brain
C. A transient brain dysfunction with more components
D. A brain disorder that predisposes to epilepsy
E. An acute brain injury
A seizure is a sudden, temporary disturbance in brain function
causing abnormal movements, sensations, or behavior.
