Myasthenia Gravis
•Autoimmune blockade ofACh receptors.
•Features: ptosis, ophthalmoplegia, facial weakness, chewing/swallowing difficulty,
proximal weakness.
•Diagnosis: improvement with edrophonium/pyridostigmine; antibody testing.
•Treatment: anticholinesterases, immunosuppressants, thymectomy, plasma exchange
for crises.
