46. Brain death occurs after how many minutes of complete cerebral ischemia?
a. 4—8 min
b. 8—10 min
c. 12—15 min
d. 15—20 min
The brain cannot survive without blood supply beyond 4—8
minutes, leading to irreversible brain death.
