36. Neonatal blood spot screening program for congenital hypothyroidism in
Egypt aims at detecting which abnormality?
A. Low TSH
B. Low T4
C. Low T3
D. High TSH
E. High T3
The test detects elevated TSH, which indicates primary
hypothyroidism, allowing early treatment to prevent
developmental delay.
