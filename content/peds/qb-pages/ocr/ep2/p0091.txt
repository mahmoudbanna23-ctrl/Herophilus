Speech, Language, and Communication
•Language Types:
• Receptive: Understanding
• Expressive: Speaking
•Observation: Receptive language develops earlier than expressive.
•Delays: Usually normal pattern but delayed.
Causes:
• Hearing impairment
• Global developmental delay
• Environmental deprivation / limited social interaction
