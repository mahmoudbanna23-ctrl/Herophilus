35. About typical febrile seizures, all are true EXCEPT:
a. Focal or generalizæd
b. Occur in children 6 months—5 years
c. Last less than 15 minutes
d. No neurological deficit afterward
Typical febrile seizures are generalized, short 1<15 min), occur
in children 6 months—5 years, and have no neurological deficit
afterward. Focal features suggest atypical febrile seizures.
