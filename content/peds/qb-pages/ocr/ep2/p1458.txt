13. Which of the following is the most common causative organism of infective
endocarditis?
A. Streptococcus pyogenes
B. Streptococcus pneumoniae
C. Streptococcus viridans
D. Staphylococcus aureus
E. Listeria monocytogenes
The most common cause of infective endocarditis is a-hemolytic Streptococcus
(Streptococcus viridans).
