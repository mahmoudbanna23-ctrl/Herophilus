Presentation
•Often identified early in at-risk neonates.
•Red flags in infancy:
• Feeding difficulties (poor latching, gagging, slow feeding)
• Poor head control, floppy or stiff limbs
• Asymmetry of hand function
• Delayed motor milestones: rolling, sitting, walking
• Abnormal movements or gait (toe walking, arm flexion)
• Global developmental delay
Examination Findings
•Movement: unusual fidgety or writhing movements, ataxia, dystonia
•Tone: hypotonia or increased limb tone; spasticity is velocity-dependent
•Reflexes: brisk tendon reflexes, upgoing plantar response, persistence of primitive
reflexes, exaggerated Moro
•Other: slowed head growth, abnormal gait, poor trunk control
•MRI/cranial ultrasound may help identify cause but not required for diagno
