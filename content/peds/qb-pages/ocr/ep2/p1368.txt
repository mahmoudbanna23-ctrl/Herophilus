49. A 20-month-old child with a febrile illness developed a generalized tonic-
clonic seizure that lasted 5 minutes. Her systemic examination was unremarkable.
After evaluation, she was diagnosed with febrile convulsions. Her parents are
concerned about the risk of future epilepsy. Which of the following is considered
a risk factor for future epilepsy in this child?
A. Generalized tonic-clonic seizures
B. Seizure lasting up to 10 minutes
C. Age of onset less than two years
D. History of epilepsy in the father
Family history Of epilepsy increases the risk Of future seizures
more than the characteristics Of the febrile seizure itself.
