Upper Airway Obstruction
Causes:
•Common: viral croup, foreign body
•Rare: laryngeal edema, trauma, retropharyngeal abscess, bacterial tracheitis,
epiglottitis
Clinical Features:
•Stridor (high-pitched on inspiration or biphasic)
•Chest recession
•Drooling, inability to swallow severe obstruction
•Pulse oximetry: late feature in IJAO
Management:
•Mild: home observation
•Severe: steroids (dexamethasone/prednisolone), nebulized epinephrine
•Bacterial tracheitis/epiglottitis: hospital admission, oxygen, secure airway, antibioti
possible intubation/tracheostomy
