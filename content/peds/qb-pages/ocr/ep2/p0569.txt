46. What is the most common cause of chronic wet cough in children?
a. Persistent bacterial bronchitis
b. Bacterial bronchitis
c. Viral bronchitis
d. Asthma
A chronic, wet-sounding cough in children is often due to
persistent bacterial bronchitis, especially in the absence of
other red flags. It typically responds well to appropriate
antibiotics.
