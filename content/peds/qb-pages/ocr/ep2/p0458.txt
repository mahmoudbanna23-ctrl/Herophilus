Lower Respiratory Tract Infections (LRTI)
Common LRT's in Children:
•Bronchiolitis, bronchitis, pneumonia
•Half are viral; bronchiolitis affects 1 in 3 infants in the first year
Causes of Acute Respiratory Distress in Infants:
•Bronchiolitis, viral episodic wheeze, pneumonia, heart failure, foreign body, anaphylaxis,
pneumothorax/pleural effusion, metabolic acidosis, severe anemia
