12. A 5-week-old boy presents with projectile vomiting for 48 hours. He is
hungry after vomiting and hasn 't opened bowels in 3 days. A mass is felt in the
left upper quadrant. Blood gas: pH 7.50, pC02 5.5 kPa, HC03 30 mmoVL. What
does this blood gas indicate?
A. Normal findings
B. Metabolic acidosis
C. Metabolic alkalosis
D. Respiratory acidosis
E. Respiratory alkalosis
Vomiting causes loss of gastric acid, leading to raised pH and
bicarbonate (metabolic alkalosis).
