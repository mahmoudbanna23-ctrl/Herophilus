62. Which of the following hematological disorders is associated with
prolongation of partial thromboplastin time (PTT) with normal prothrombin time
(PT)?
A. Hemorrhagic disease of the newborn
B. DIC
C. Chronic ITP
D. Henoch-Schönlein purpura
E. Hemophilia A
Hemophilia A is a factor VIII deficiency causing prolonged PTT,
while PT remains normal because the extrinsic pathway is
unaffected.
