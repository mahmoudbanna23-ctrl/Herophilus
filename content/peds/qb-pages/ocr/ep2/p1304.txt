17. What is the initial management of diabetic ketoacidosis?
A. Insulin
B. Fluids
C. Potassium
D. Bicarbonate
E. Antibiotics
Fluid resuscitation is the first priority in DKA to correct
dehydration. Insulin, potassium, and other measures are
started after stabilizing circulation.
