9. Which clinical sign is characteristic of a disorder of the corticospinal
(pyramidal) tract?
A. Brisk hyperreflexia and extensor plantar reflex
B. Difficulty initiating movements
C. Dystonia or dyskinesia
D. Jerky movements
E. Wide-based gait
Lesions of the corticospinal tract cause upper motor neuron
signs such as spasticity, hyperreflexia, and Babinski sign.
