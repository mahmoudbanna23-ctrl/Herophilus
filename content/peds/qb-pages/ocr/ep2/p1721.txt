63. What will be the next step in a child presenting with anemia and
microcytosis?
A. Reticulocyte count
B. Hb electrophoresis
C. Serum ferritin test
D. Sickling test
E. Serum folic acid
Microcytic anemia is most commonly due to iron deficiency;
serum ferritin is the most specific test to confirm low iron
stores.
