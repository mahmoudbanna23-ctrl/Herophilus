21. A 7-year-old girl presents with edema of the face and legs and abdominal
pain. Urine dipstick shows 4+ protein. A diagnosis ofnephrotic syndrome is
made, and she is started on prednisolone.
What would be the likely finding on biopsy and light microscopy?
A. Focal-segmental glomerulosclerosis
B. Mesangiocapillary glomerulonephritis
C. Crescentic glomerulonephritis
D. Minimal change disease
E. Membranous glomerulonephritis
In children with nephrotic syndrome, the most common
histological finding is minimal change disease, which appears
normal under light microscopy and responds well to steroids.
