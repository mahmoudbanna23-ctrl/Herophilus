23. A 5-year-old boy presents to the outpatient clinic with weakness. His parents
report that he finds it difficult to climb the stairs at home. This has been becoming
progressively worse over the preceding 6—12 months. He is playing on the carpet
with his brother when his mother calls. Which clinical sign does this child suffer
from?
A. Kernig's sign
B. Romberg's sign
C. Babinski's sign
D. Dyskinesia
E. Gower's sign
Gower's sign (using hands to rise from the floor) indicates
proximal muscle weakness, typical Of Duchenne or Becker
muscular dystrophy.
