72. Which of the following is a potential complication of Fallot's tetralogy?
A. Pulmonary hypertension
B. Cerebrovascular accidents
C. Recurrent pneumonia
D. Eisenmenger syndrome
It is important to recognize hypercyanotic spells, as they may lead to
myocardial infarction, cerebrovascular accidents, and even death if left
untreated. They are characterized by a rapid increase in cyanosis, usually
associated with irritability or inconsolable crying because Of severe hypoxia
and breathlessness, followed by pallor due to tissue acidosis. On auscultation,
there is a very short murmur or no murmur during a spell.
