38. Which of the following statements is true in acute bronchiolitis?
a. Wheezing is due to spasm of smooth muscles of bronchioles
b. Inhaled salbutamol is the treatment of choice
c. Chest X-ray is diagnostic
d. Feeding difficulties are common
e. Antibiotics are indicated in severe cases
Feeding problems are frequent due to nasal congestion and
increased work Of breathing. Salbutamol and antibiotics are
generally not effective.
