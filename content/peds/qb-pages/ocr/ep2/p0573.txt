48. A 3-year-old child presents with a sudden, dry, loud, repetitive cough. Chest
examination reveals poor air entry and localized wheezing over the right side.
What is the most likely diagnosis?
a. Tension pneumothorax
b. Acute asthma exacerbation
c. Severe complicated pneumonia
d. Pulmonary TB
e. Inhaled bronchial foreign body_
A sudden onset Of cough and localized wheeze suggests an
aspirated foreign body, especially if air entry is reduced on one
side. This is common in toddlers.
