69. What is the best therapeutic strategy for a 4-year-old girl diagnosed with IT P,
with a platelet count of 30,000/dL and no active bleeding or mucous membrane
purpura?
A. Oral steroids
B. IVIG
C. Anti-D
D. Observation and follow-up
E. Splenectomy
ITP with platelet count >20,000 and no bleeding can be
managed conservatively; treatment is reserved for active
bleeding or very low counts.
