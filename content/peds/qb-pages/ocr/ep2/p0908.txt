44. Which of the following is commonly associated with secondary enuresis?
a. Emotional upset
b. Ectopic ureter
c. Posterior urethral valves
d. Neurogenic bladder
Secondary enuresis (child was previously dry) is usually due to
psychological stress, unlike primary enuresis which may have
anatomical causes.
