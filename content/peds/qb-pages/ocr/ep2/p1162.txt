Complications Of Chronic Liver Failure
1.Spontaneous Bacterial Peritonitis (SBP)
•suspect in fever, abdominal pain, tenderness, or unexplained hepatic/renal deterioration.
'Diagnosis: paracentesis (WCC, differential, culture).
'Treatment: broad-spectrum antibiotics.
2.Nutritional Defects
Fat malabsorption -+ requires medium-chain triglycerides; risk of fat-soluble vitamin
deficiencies (A, D, E, K).
Protein malnutrition due to poor intake & high catabolic rate; protein not restricted
unless encephalopathy.
Anorexia may require NGfeeding or parenteral nutrition.
3.Renal Failure
'Causes: renal tubular acidosis, acute tubular necrosis, hepatorenal syndrome.
