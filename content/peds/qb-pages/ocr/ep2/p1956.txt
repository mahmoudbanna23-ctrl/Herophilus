Asthma is suggested by symptoms persisting between infections. Recurrent
wheeze only during colds is less specific and may reflect viral-induced wheezing.
Guillain-Barré syndrome (GBS) presents with ascending symmetrical weakness
and reduced reflexes following infection. It is an acute demyelinating
polyneuropathy.
Gower's sign using hands to rise from the floor indicates proximal muscle
weakness. It is typical of Duchenne and Becker muscular dystrophies.
Down syndrome with one chromosome 21 attached to 14 is
due to an
unbalanced Robertsonian translocation. It results in extra chromosome 21
material.
