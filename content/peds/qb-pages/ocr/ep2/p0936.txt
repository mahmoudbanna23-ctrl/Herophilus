Classification of Epileptic Seizures
2. Focal seizures (from one hemisphere)
•May retain or lose awareness; may generalize secondarily.
•Frontal lobe: motor phenomena, Jacksonian march, hypermotor.
•Temporal lobe: aura (smell/taste), automatisms (lip-smacking, plucking), déjå vu,
impaired consciousness.
•Parietal lobe: sensory symptoms (tingling, dysaesthesia, distorted body image).
•Occipital lobe: visual phenomena (colored lights, scotoma).
