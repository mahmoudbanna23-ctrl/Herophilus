40. A 17-year-old girl had mild fever, increased frequency of urination, and
abdominal pain a few days ago. She is now afebrile and asymptomatic. What is
the most likely diagnosis?
a. Cystitis
b. Pyelonephritis
c. Glomerulonephritis
d. Nephrolithiasis
The girl had fever and urinary symptoms that resolved, leaving
her asymptomatic consistent with a self-limited lower IJTI.
