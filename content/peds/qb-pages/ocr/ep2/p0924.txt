52. A 3-year-old child develops puffy eyes and lower limb and scrotal edema
following an upper respiratory tract infection. What is the most probable
diagnosis?
a. Steroid-sensitive nephrotic syndrome
b. Acute post-streptococcal glomerulonephritis
c. Congenital nephrotic syndrome
d. Cardiac failure
A 3-year-old with puffy eyes and edema after infection fits
classic presentation Of minimal change nephrotic syndrome.
