Peripheral Motor Disorders (Neuromuscular)
Feature: weakness (progressive or static).
Other signs: hypotonia, delayed milestones, abnormal gait, fatigability, cramps.
Anterior Horn Cell Disorders
1.
•Spinal muscular atrophy, poliomyelitis, acute flaccid myelitis.
•Features: weakness, wasting, fasciculations, reflexes.
Peripheral Neuropathies
2.
•Motor: distal wasting, weakness.
•Sensory: pain/temp/touch, areflexia.
•Signs: pes cavus, glove-and-stocking loss.
•Causes: Guillain-Barré, hereditary neuropathy, Bell palsy.
