Guillain-Barré Syndrome (GBS)
•Acute post-infectious autoimmune polyneuropathy.
•Features: ascending symmetrical weakness, areflexia, cranial nerve palsy, sensory
disturbance, autonomic dysfunction.
•Investigations:
• MRI to rule out cord lesions.
CSF: protein, normal WBC (after week 2).
• Nerve conduction: slowed velocities.
•Treatment: supportive (esp. respiratory), IVIG or plasma exchange. No steroids.
•Prognosis: full recovery (up to 2 years).
