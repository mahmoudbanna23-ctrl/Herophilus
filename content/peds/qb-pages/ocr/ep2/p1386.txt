58. A 7-year-old girl presents with edema of the face and legs, and abdominal
pain. There is no hypertension, and the urine dipstick shows 4+ protein. She is
started on prednisolone. What would be the likely finding on biopsy and light
microscopy?
A. Focal-segmental glomerulosclerosis
B. Mesangiocapillary glomerulonephritis
C. Crescentic glomerulonephritis
D. Minimal change disease
Minimal change disease is the most common cause Of
nephrotic syndrome in young children, showing normal
glomeruli on light microscopy but responding well to steroids.
