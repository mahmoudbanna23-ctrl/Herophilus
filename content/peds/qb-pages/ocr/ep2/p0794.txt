Kidney Functions
1. Filtration of water-soluble wastes without losing large molecules.
2. Reabsorption of small molecules (glucose, amino acids).
3. Concentration/dilution of urine fluid balance.
4. Regulation of BP & acid—base balance.
5. Vitamin D metabolism.
Assessment of Renal Function
•Plasma creatinine: late rise, age-dependent.
•Plasma urea: rises earlier, but nonspecific (also in dehydration, Gl bleed, high protein
diet).
eGFR = 31 x height (cm) / creatinine (gmol/L).
• Low at birth, adult levels by 1—2 yrs.
•Formal GFR (inulin clearance): rarely used, except before chemo/research.
•Urinary protein loss: early marker in CKD.
•Urine osmolality: tests concentrating ability.
