13. Gerald is a 4-year-old boy born in Tanzania who had severe neonatal jaundice
that was untreated. He now shows abnormal movements and postures, with
asymmetrical limb responses to startle. He is hypotonic when asleep. What type
of movement disorder best fits this scenario?
A. Dyskinetic
B. Ataxic
C. Spastic diplegia
D. Spastic hemiplegia
E. Spastic quadriplegia
Untreated neonatal jaundice can cause kernicterus, leading to
dyskinetic cerebral palsy, characterized by involuntary, writhing
movements and fluctuating tone.
