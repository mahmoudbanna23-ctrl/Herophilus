22. Type I diabetes mellitus is not associated with:
a. Obesity
b. Weight loss
c. Enuresis
d. Polydipsia
Type 1 diabetes is typically associated with weight loss, polydipsia, polyuria,
and sometimes enuresis due to osmotic diuresis. Unlike type 2 diabetes, it is
not associated with obesity.
