35. Which of the following is a feature ofa typical febrile seizure?
A. Age usually before 6 months
B. Focal or generalized
C. Lasts more than 15 minutes
D. No post-ictal manifestations
E. Recurs within 24 hours
Typical febrile seizures usually in children 6 months—5 years,
are brief (<15 min), and can be generalized or focal, often with post-
ictal recovery.
