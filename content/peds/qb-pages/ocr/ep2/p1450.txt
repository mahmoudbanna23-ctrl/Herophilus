9. What is the underlying pathophysiologic mechanism of anemia in patients with
G6PD enzyme deficiency?
A. Bone marrow aplasia
B. Consumption coagulopathy
C. Food poisoning
D. Intravascular hemolysis
E. Extravascular hemolysis
Hemolysis due to G6PD deficiency is mainly intravascular,
associated with fever, malaise, abdominal pain, and dark urine
containing hemoglobin and urobilinogen.
