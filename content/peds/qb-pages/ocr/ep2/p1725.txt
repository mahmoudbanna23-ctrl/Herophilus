65. What is the most common cause of cerebral palsy?
A. Prenatal
B. Perinatal brain injury
C. Neonatal infections
D. Accidents
E. Meningitis/encephalitis
Most cases of cerebral palsy result from prenatal brain insults,
such as malformations or intrauterine injury, rather than
perinatal events.
