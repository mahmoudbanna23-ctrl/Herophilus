65. Hemolytic uremic syndrome is characterized by all of the following except?
A. Thrombocytopenia
B. Good prognosis in most cases
C. Thrombocytosis
D. Acute renal failure
HUS is characterized by thrombocytopenia, microangiopathic
hemolytic anemia, and acute renal failure, not elevated
platelets.
