Learning / Intellectual Disabilities
•Definition: Significant reduction in ability to understand, learn, and cope independently;
impacts most life areas.
•Classification: Mild, moderate, or severe; sometimes described by IQ.
•Presentation:
• Moderate/severe: preschool years, global developmental delay
• Mild: may present later
•Assessment: Similar to global developmental delay; vision, hearing, and comorbid
conditions (ASD, ADHD) must be evaluated.
Specific Learning Difficulties
•Definition: Impairment in a particular academic skill.
•Examples:
• Dyslexia: reading difficulties
• Dyscalculia: difficulties with numbers/math
• Dysgraphia: difficulties with writing
