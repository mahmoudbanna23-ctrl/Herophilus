18. A 7-year-old boy has abdominal pain and a rash that started several days ago.
On examination, you notice a palpable purpuric rash over his calves and buttocks,
with swelling of both ankles. Abdominal examination is unremarkable. What is
the most likely laboratory finding?
A. Decreased platelet count
B. Hypochromic microcytic anemia
C. Low C3 complement level
D. Normal clotting parameters
E. Prolonged prothrombin time
Henoch-Schönlein purpura presents with palpable purpura and
normal coagulation. Platelets, PT. and aPTT are usually normal.
