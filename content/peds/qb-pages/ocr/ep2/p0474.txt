Immunodeficiency and Lung Infections
•Secondary causes: Malignancy, corticosteroid/chemotherapy, HIV
•Primary immune deficiency: Rare, but can predispose to severe/recurrent infections.
•Types & Associated Infections:
• IgG deficiency recurrent bacterial infections with encapsulated organisms (e.g.
Streptococcus pneumoniae).
• Cell-mediated immunodeficiency opportunistic infections (Pneumocystis
jirovecii, fungi).
• Neutrophil-killing defects Staphylococcus aureus infections.
