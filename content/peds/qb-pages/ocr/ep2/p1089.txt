Acquired Hypothyroidism
•Most often autoimmune thyroiditis.
•Risk in females, children with Down, Turner, or autoimmune diseases.
•May also co-exist with Addison disease in families.
Symptoms
•Cold intolerance, constipation, learning difficulties, poor concentration, delayed
puberty/amenorrhea.
Signs
•Short stature, weight gain, goiter, dry/thin hair, pale puffy eyes, dry skin, bradycardia,
slow reflexes.
•May cause slipped upper femoral epiphysis.
•Bone age delayed.
Diagnosis
•T TSH, T4/T3, delayed bone age.
Treatment
•Lifelong thyroxine.
