5. A 12-year-old girl experienced signs 3 days before manifesting progressive
weakness and inability to walk. Examination reveals marked symmetrical muscle
weakness without atrophy. Deep tendon reflexes are absent at the ankles and I + at
the knees.What is the most likely diagnosis?
A. Disorder of neuromuscular transmission
B. Muscle disorder
C. Poliomyelitis
D. Guillain-Barré syndrome
E. Disorder of anterior horn cell
Rapidly progressive symmetric weakness with hyporeflexia/areflexia is classic
for GBS. Muscle disorders and poliomyelitis usually have different patterns of
weakness and reflex changes.
