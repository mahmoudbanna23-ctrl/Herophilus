17. A 2-year-old boy has had pallor since the age of 6 months and received a
blood transfusion one month ago. There is a history of a similar illness in an
older sibling. On examination, the child has pallor, jaundice, frontal bossing, and
hepatosplenomegaly.
What is the most likely diagnosis?
a) Iron deficiency anemia
b) Beta thalassemia
c) Peutz-Jeghers syndrome
d) Sickle cell anemia
e) Dyskeratosis congenita
Chronic hemolysis, transfusion dependence, family history, and
skeletal changes like frontal bossing point to beta thalassemia
major.
