36. A 15-month-old baby boy is brought to the clinic with features of spastic
diplegia. While counseling the parents on the diagnosis of cerebral palsy:
Which of the following statements is correct?
A. The insult usually occurs during delivery
B. Cerebral palsy is a disorder of motor and sensory function due to a static brain
injury
C. Reflexes are reduced or absent
D. Botulinum toxin may be useful to treat spasticity
E. MRI scan is diagnostic
Cerebral palsy is a static motor disorder caused by early brain injury. Spasticity can
be treated symptomatically with botulinum toxin; reflexes are usually increased, not
decreased.
