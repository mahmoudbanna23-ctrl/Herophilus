Brain Tumors in Children
General
•Most common solid tumor in children.
•Leading cause of childhood cancer deaths (UK).
•60% infratentorial (posterior fossa).
•Mostly primary, unlike adults.
Types
•Astrocytoma (40%) spectrum from low grade to glioblastoma.
•Medulloblastoma (20%) midline posterior fossa, seeds via CSF (20% spinal mets).
•Ependymoma (8%) usually in 4th ventricle, aggressive.
•Brainstem glioma (6%) very poor prognosis.
•Craniopharyngioma benign, locally invasive, suprasellar.
•Atypical teratoid/rhabdoid tumor rare, aggressive, early childhood.
