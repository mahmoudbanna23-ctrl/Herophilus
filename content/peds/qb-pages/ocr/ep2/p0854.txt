18. John is a 9-year-old boy with a history of multiple urinary tract infections. An
ultrasound shows dysplastic kidneys. At a routine paediatric follow-up, his
mother reports he is not growing as well as his siblings. His blood pressure is
130/90 mmHg.
Which of the following investigations would you perform next?
A. DMSA scan
B. Urinary electrolytes
Hypertension, poor growth, and dysplastic kidneys suggest
chronic kidney disease. Measuring creatinine and electrolytes is
crucial to assess renal function and guide further management.
C. Urine microscopy and culture
D. Ultrasound of the kidneys and urinary tract
