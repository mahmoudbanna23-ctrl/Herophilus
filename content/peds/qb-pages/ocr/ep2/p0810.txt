Acute Glomerulonephritis (AGN)
•Definition: Increased glomerular cellularity GFR.
•Clinical features: Oliguria, edema (periorbital), HTN (may cause seizures), hematuria,
proteinuria.
•Causes:
• Post-infectious (esp. streptococcus)
• Vasculitis (HSP, SLE, polyarteritis nodosa)
• IgA nephropathy, mesangiocapillary GN
• Rare: anti-GBM (Goodpasture's)
•Management: Water/electrolyte balance, diuretics if needed.
•Complications: Rarely rapidly progressive GN biopsy + immunosuppression/plasma
exchange.
