9. Given the findings shown in the opposite figure, which of the following is the
most likely congenital heart disease associated with the shown clinical findings?
A. Pulmonary stenosis
B. Atrial septal defect
C. Ventricular septal defect
D. Mitral stenosis
E. Persistent ductus arteriosus
The lack of an opening click and the presence of
a pansystolic murmur are highly suggestive of
ventricular septal defect. In general, all
pansystolic murmurs are appreciated most easily
below the level of the nipples.
