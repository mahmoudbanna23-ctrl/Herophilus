49. Which laboratory finding is characteristic of bone marrow failure?
A. Reticulocytosis
B. Low reticulocyte count
C. High indirect bilirubin
D. Positive Coombs test
E. Hypochromic microcytic anemia
Bone marrow failure results in low production of red cells, so
the reticulocyte count is low despite anemia.
