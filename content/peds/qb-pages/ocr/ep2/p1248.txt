13. Connor, a 4-year-old boy, presents with leg pain, pallor, petechiae, and
hepatosplenomegaly. Blood results show pancytopenia. What investigation would
confirm the diagnosis?
A. Bone marrow aspirate
B. Blood film
C. Chest X-ray
D. Clotting screen
E. CT scan
Bone marrow aspiration confirms leukaemia by showing blasts.
Blood films may suggest it, but definitive diagnosis requires
marrow.
