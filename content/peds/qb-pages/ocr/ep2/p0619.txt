C. Eisenmenger Syndrome
•Pathogenesis: long-standing L-->R shunt pulmonary vascular remodeling reversal
•Clinical picture: cyanosis, clubbing, exercise tolerance, eventual RHF (4th—5th
decade).
•Management: prevention by early repair; pulmonary vasodilators may delay
transplant.
