34. A 2-week-old baby is brought with vomiting and poor feeding. Examination
shows hypotonia and ambiguous genitalia. Blood tests reveal hyponatraemia,
hyperkalaemia, and hypoglycaemia. What is the most likely diagnosis?
A. Congenital hypothyroidism
B. Inborn error of metabolism
C. Galactosaemia
D. Congenital adrenal hyperplasia
E. Neonatal sepsis
The classic presentation Of congenital adrenal hyperplasia
(CAH) includes salt-wasting, ambiguous genitalia in females,
and electrolyte abnormalities.
