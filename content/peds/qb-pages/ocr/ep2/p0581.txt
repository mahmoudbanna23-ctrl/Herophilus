52. Regarding acute laryngotracheobronchitis (viral croup), which of the
following is WRONG?
a. It is usually caused by a parainfluenza virus
b. Dexamethasone speeds resolution
c. Endotracheal intubation is rarely indicated
d. Nebulized adrenaline produces a sustained decrease in airway obstruction
e. Antibiotics provide no benefit
Nebulized adrenaline offers rapid but temporary relief in croup.
It's not sustained, which is why steroids like dexamethasone
are also used for longer-term effect.
