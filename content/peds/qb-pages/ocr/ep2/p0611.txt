5. Presentation
•Modes:
Antenatal ultrasound diagnosis.
Heart murmur.
Heart failure.
Shock (duct-dependent systemic lesions).
Cyanosis (structural heart disease if persistent).
•Heart Murmurs:
• Innocent murmurs: soft, systolic, asymptomatic, left sternal edge ("5 S's").
Pathological murmurs require echo confirmation.
•Heart Failure:
Neonates (week 1): obstructive lesions (e.g. CoA).
Later weeks: left-to-right shunts (VSD, AVSD, PDA) pulmonary edema, breathlessness.
Untreated: pulmonary hypertension Eisenmenger syndrome (irreversible, cyanotic).
•Cyanosis:
Central (tongue) = true hypoxemia.
Peripheral (hands, feet) = may be benign.
Persistent cyanosis = CHD until proven othen.vise
