48. On a routine CBC-screening, a 1.5-year-old is noted to have a microcytic
hypochromic anemia. A follow-up hemoglobin electrophoresis demonstrates
increased level of hemoglobin A2. The child is MOST likely to have:
a. Lead poisoning
b. Iron deficiency
c. Chronic systemic illness
d. Sickle cell trait
e. Beta thalassemia minor
Increased HbA2 on electrophoresis is the hallmark Of ß-
thalassemia minor. These children present with microcytic
hypochromic anemia that does not improve with iron.
