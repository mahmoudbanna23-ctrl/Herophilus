Cerebral Palsy (CP)
Definition
•CP is a group of permanent disorders of movement and posture causing activity
limitation.
•Results from non-progressive brain abnormalities during development.
•Motor disorders often coexist with cognitive, communication, vision, perception,
sensation, behavioral, seizure, and musculoskeletal problems.
•Although the brain lesion is static, clinical manifestations evolve over time.
•Most common cause of motor impairment in children (2—3.5 per 1000 live births in high-
income countries).
•Brain injury after 2 years classified as acquired brain injury.
