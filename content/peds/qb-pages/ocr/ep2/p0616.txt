Right-to-Left Shunts (Cyanotic Heart Disease)
Presentation
•Cyanosis (02 sat '94%) or collapse, often in first week of life.
•Hyperoxia test:
• Pa02 < 15 kPa cyanotic CHD (if lung disease/PPHN excluded).
• Pa02 kPa not cyanotic CHD.
•Blood gases more reliable than pulse oximetry.
Initial Management of Cyanosed Neonate
•ABC resuscitation, mechanical ventilation if needed.
•Prostaglandin EI infusion (5 ng/kg/min) maintains ductal patency.
•Monitor for side effects: apnea, seizures, flushing, hypotension.
