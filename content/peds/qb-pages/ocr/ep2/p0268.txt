I. Microcytes are seen in blood films of which of the following conditions?
A. Aplastic anemia
B. Liver disease
C. Anemia of chronic disease
D. Down syndrome
E. Hypothyroidism
Microcytes (small red blood cells) are commonly seen in iron
deficiency and anemia of chronic disease due to impaired iron
utilization. Other listed conditions typically show normocytic or
macrocytic anemia.
