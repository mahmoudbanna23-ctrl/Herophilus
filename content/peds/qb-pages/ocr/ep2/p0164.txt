3. Y-Linked Inheritance
•Inheritance: Only males affected; transmitted father all sons.
•Examples: Rare; related to spermatogenesis and male fertility.
4. Mitochondrial (Cytoplasmic) Inheritance
•Inheritance: Only maternal; mitochondria come from the egg.
•Features: Variable tissue involvement; severity depends on proportion of mutated
mitochondria.
•Examples:
• Leber hereditary optic neuropathy
• Mitochondrial myopathies (MERRF, MELAS)
• Kearns-Sayre syndrome (KSS)
