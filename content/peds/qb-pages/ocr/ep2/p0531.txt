last 3 days. His breathing is now interfering with feeding. He has no history of previous
illnesses, and his growth is normal. On chest examination:
Heart rate: 120 beats/min Heart sounds: Normal, no murmurs
Respiratory rate: 40 breaths/min Dullness to percussion
Bronchial breathing and inspiratory crepitations over the front lower right hemithorax
What is the most likely diagnosis?
a. Acute exacerbation of asthma
b. Bronchiolitis
c. Inhaled foreign
d. Right pneumothorax
e. Pneumonia
Signs like dullness to percussion, bronchial breath sounds, and
inspiratory crackles are typical of lobar pneumonia. The child's
history and examination support this.
