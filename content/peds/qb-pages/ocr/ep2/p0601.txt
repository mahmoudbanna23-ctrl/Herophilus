62. Aly is a previously healthy person who has now developed a cough and
difficulty breathing. On examination, his temperature is 390C. He has a
respiratory rate of 55 breaths/min. His breaths are rapid but shallow with mild
substernal recession. There is no wheeze on auscultation but some coarse crackles
at the right base. His oxygen saturation is 91% in air. Select the most probable
diagnosis for his respiratory symptoms:
a. Acute asthma
b. Bronchiolitis
c. Chronic asthma
Acutely ill presentation with fever, localized crackles, and
desaturation Suggests pneumonia. Rapid shallow breathing and
raised respiratory rate further support a lower respiratory tract
infection.
d. Laryngotracheobronchitis (Croup)
e. Pneumonia
