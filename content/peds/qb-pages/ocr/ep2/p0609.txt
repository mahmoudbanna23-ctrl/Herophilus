2. Epidemiology
•Heart disease in children: mostly congenital.
•Incidence: 8/1000 live births have major malformations; 30% need intervention in 1st
year.
•Minor defects: 53/1000 births (e.g. bicuspid aortic valve).
•1 in 10 stillbirths cardiac anomaly.
•9 most common lesions 80% of cases.
• Left-to-right shunts: VSD (30%), PDA (12%), ASD (7%).
• Right-to-left shunts: Tetralogy of Fallot (5%), TGA (5%).
• Outflow obstruction: PS (7%), AS (5%), CoA (5%).
• Common mixing: AVSD (2%).
