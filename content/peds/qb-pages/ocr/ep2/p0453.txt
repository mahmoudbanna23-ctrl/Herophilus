Respiratory Disorders in Children
General Features:
•Very common; main cause of pediatric hospital admissions
•Asthma: most common chronic illness
•Age affects susceptibility & severity
•Risk factors: genetics, prematurity, immune defects, pollution, smoke, low SES
Clinical Presentation:
•Cough (upper vs. lower respiratory tract)
•Increased respiratory rate and effort
•Chest recession (suprasternal, intercostal, subcostal)
•Extra respiratory noises: wheeze (musical), crackles (secretions)
•Reduced oxygen saturation marker of severity
•Impending respiratory failure: cyanosis, grunting, hypercapnia, reduced consciousness
