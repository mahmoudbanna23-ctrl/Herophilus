Post-Streptococcal GN (PSGN)
•Epidemiology: Boys, 2—12 yrs.
Group A strep (throat/skin), 2—3 weeks post-infection.
•Cause:
Hematuria (red/brown urine), proteinuria, edema, HTN, AKI.
•Features:
•Diagnosis: Evidence of recent strep (culture, TASO/anti-DNase B), low C3 (normalizes in
wks).
•Treatment: Supportive (salt restriction, diuretics, antihypertensives), antibiotics if active
strep.
•Prognosis: Excellent (>95% recover fully); microscopic hematuria may persist.
