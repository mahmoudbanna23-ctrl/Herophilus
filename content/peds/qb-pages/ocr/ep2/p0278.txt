6. What is the MOST common inherited pancytopenia syndrome?
A. Dyskeratosis congenita
B. Fanconi anemia
C. Shwachman-Diamond syndrome
D. Diamond-Blackfan anemia
E. Congenital amegakaryocytic thrombocytopenia
Fanconi anemia is the most common inherited cause of
pancytopenia, often presenting with physical anomalies and
progressive bone marrow failure.
