9. Which of the following is an X-Iinked disease?
a. Favism
b. Sickle cell disease
c. Thalassemia
d. Phenylketonuria
G6PD deficiency (favism) is X-Iinked. Sickle cell disease,
thalassemia, and PKIJ are autosomal recessive.
