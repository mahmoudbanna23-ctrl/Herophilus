Diabetes Mellitus in Children
Epidemiology & Types
•95% = Type 1 DM (autoimmune 6-cell destruction).
•Type 2 DM increasing with obesity.
•Other causes: monogenic DM (MODE neonatal DM), drugs (steroids), pancreatic
disease (CF, thalassemia), endocrine (Cushing's), gestational DM.
Pathogenesis of Type 1 DM
•Genetic predisposition + immune activation 6-cell destruction.
•Autoantibodies: GAD, ZnT8, IA-2.
•Associated with autoimmune diseases (thyroid, Addison's, coeliac, RA).
•More common in Down & Turner syndromes.
