Gross & Fine Motor Skills
•Newborn: Flexed limbs, symmetrical posture.
•6 weeks: Smiles responsively.
•3 months: No head lag, follows objects.
•4 months: Reaches for toys, palmar grasp.
•6 months: Sits with round back, transfers toys hand-to-hand.
•7 months: Sits with straight back.
•8—9 months: Stands with support, crawls.
•10 months: Cruises along furniture, pincer grip develops.
•12 months: Walks unsteadily, broad gait, hands apart.
•15 months: Walks steadily.
