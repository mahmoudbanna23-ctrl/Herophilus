1. General Principles
•Child development follows a predictable sequence, but the rate varies between children.
•Milestones mark expected achievements; delays beyond set red flag ages need assessment.
•Development integrates gross motor, fine motor/vision, speech/hearing, and social-
emotional-behavioral domains.
•Progress should always be assessed longitudinally (over time), across all domains.
2. Monitoring & Screening
•Development is assessed at:
• Home (parents via Child Health Record)
• Scheduled checks (health surveillance)
• Any healthcare contact
•Hearing: all newborns screened.
•Vision: acuity and squint screened at school entry.
•Healthy Child Program (UK): integrated system of immunization, screening, and
developmental reviews.
