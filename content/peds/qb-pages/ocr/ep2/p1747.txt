76. Which clinical finding should not be associated with rheumatic fever?
A. Carditis
B. Migratory polyarthritis
C. Chorea
D. Maculopapular rash
E. Subcutaneous nodules
Rheumatic fever does not cause a maculopapular rash; it presents with
migratory arthritis, carditis, chorea, erythema marginatum, and
subcutaneous nodules.
