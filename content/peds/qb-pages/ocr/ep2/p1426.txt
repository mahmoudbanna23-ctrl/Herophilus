78. Which one of the following organisms is most likely to cause a life-
threatening infection in a child who underwent emergency splenectomy?
A. Actinomyces
B. Pseudomonas aeruginosa
C. Staphylococcus aureus
D. Streptococcus pneumoniae
Asplenic children are highly susceptible to encapsulated
bacteria, particularly S. pneumoniae, which can cause
overwhelming sepsis.
