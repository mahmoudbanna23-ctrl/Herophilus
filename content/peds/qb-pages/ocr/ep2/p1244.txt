I l. Kay, a 3-year-old girl, presents with weight loss, pallor, and a large central
abdominal mass. What is the most likely diagnosis?
A. Neuroblastoma
B. Brain tumour
C. Wilms tumour
D. Acute lymphoblastic leukaemia
E. Hodgkin disease
Neuroblastoma usually arises from the adrenal or sympathetic
chain and presents as a large irregular central abdominal mass.
It is associated with systemic features like weight oss.
