24. Common forms of inheritance include autosomal dominant and autosomal
recessive patterns. Which of the following conditions is autosomal dominant?
A. Neurofibromatosis type I
B. Cystic fibrosis
C. Sickle cell disease
D. Haemophilia A
E. Duchenne muscular dystrophy
Neurofibromatosis type 1 is inherited in an autosomal
dominant pattern, meaning only one copy Of the defective
gene is sufficient to cause the condition. In contrast, cystic
fibrosis and sickle cell disease are autosomal recessive, and
