22. A 12-year-old girl experienced diarrhea 3 days before manifesting progressive
weakness and inability to walk. Examination reveals marked peripheral
symmetrical muscle weakness without atrophy. Deep tendon reflexes were absent
in the ankles and 1+ in the knees. What is the most likely diagnosis?
A. Guillain-Barré syndrome
B. Transverse myelitis
C. Myasthenia gravis
D. Muscular dystrophy
E. Poliomyelitis
Symmetrical ascending weakness with decreased reflexes after
a recent infection is classic for GBS. Lack Of muscle atrophy
helps distinguish it from muscular dystrophy.
