Kidney Anomalies
•Renal agenesis: Bilateral Potter sequence (fatal: lung hypoplasia, characteristic
facies, limb deformities).
•Multicystic dysplastic kidney (MCDK): Non-functioning; involutes by 2 yrs; bilateral
Potter sequence.
ARPKD/ADPKD HTN, CKD, extrarenal features.
•Cystic dysplastic kidneys:
•Pelvic/Horseshoe kidney: abnormal migration/fusion, risk infection/obstruction.
Bladder & Ureter Anomalies
•Duplex kidney: abnormal ureter drainage reflux (lower pole) or ectopic
drainage/ureterocele (upper pole).
•Bladder exstrophy / Prune-belly syndrome: absent abdominal wall muscles, enlarged
