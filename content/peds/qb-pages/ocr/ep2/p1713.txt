59. A 7-year-old boy has abdominal pain and a rash that started several days ago.
On examination, you notice a palpable purpuric rash over his calves and
buttocks, with swelling ofboth ankles. Abdominal examination is unremarkable.
What is the most likely laboratory finding?
a. Decreased platelet count
b. Hypochromic microcytic anemia
c. Low C3 complement level
Henoch-Schönlein purpura presents with palpable purpura and
normal coagulation. Platelets, PT, and aPTT are usually normal.
d. Normal clotting parameters
e. Prolonged prothrombin time
