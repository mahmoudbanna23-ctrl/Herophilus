5. Mr. and Mrs. are examined as their baby is diagnosed with Down syndrome.
Chromosome analysis shows three copies of chromosome 21, with one of them
attached to chromosome 14. What is this chromosomal anomaly called?
a. Translocation
b. Mosaicism
c. Nondisjunction
d. Inversion
This is a Robertsonian translocation, a common cause Of familial Down
syndrome, where chromosome 21 attaches to chromosome 14.
