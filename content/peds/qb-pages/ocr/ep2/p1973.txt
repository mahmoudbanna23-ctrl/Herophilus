Pediatric nephrotic syndrome typically presents with normal blood pressure,
normal complement (C3), and absence of hematuria. It rarely causes chronic
renal failure when steroid-sensitive.
The standard treatment for hydrocephalus is ventriculoperitoneal shunt
insertion. This procedure diverts cerebrospinal fluid to the peritoneal cavity,
relieving raised intracranial pressure.
