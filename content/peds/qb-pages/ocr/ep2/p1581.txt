74. A 20-month-old infant presents with inability to walk unsupported. He can
use both his hands well. Physical examination was otherwise normal. Which of
the following is the likely disorder?
A. Hemiplegia
B. Diplegia
C. Quadriplegia
D. Dyskinesia
Hemiplegia: Paralysis Of one side Of the body (right or left limbs)
Diplegia: Paralysis Of the lower part Of the body (lower limbs)
Quadriplegia: Paralysis Of all four limbs (upper and lower)
Dyskinesia: Involuntary, uncontrolled movements, Often more
evident with active movement or stress
