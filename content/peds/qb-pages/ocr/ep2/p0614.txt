7. Common Lesions
Persistent Ductus Arteriosus (PDA)
c.
•Failure of ductus arteriosus to close >1 month.
•Clinical: continuous "machinery" murmur, bounding pulse, wide pulse pressure.
•Investigations: Echo.
•Management: coil or device closure via catheter (around 1 year); surgical ligation if
needed.
