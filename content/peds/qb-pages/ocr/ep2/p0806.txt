Hematuria
•Definition: RBC/HPF on microscopy.
•Types:
Glomerular: brown urine, dysmorphic RBCs, RBC casts, often with proteinuria.
Lower tract: red urine, occurs at start/end of stream, usually no proteinuria.
•Causes:
Non-glomerular: UTI (most common in children), trauma, stones, tumors, sickle cell disease,
bleeding disorders, renal vein thrombosis, hypercalciuria, infections (bacterial, viral, TB,
schistosomiasis).
• Glomerular: post-infectious GN, IgA nephropathy, HSP, vasculitis, Alport syndrome, thin basement
membrane disease.
• Investigations:
• All: urine microscopy & culture, Pr/Cr & Ca/Cr ratios, renal ultrasound, renal function tests,
CBC/coagulation/sickle screen.
If glomerular: ESR, complement, ANA/anti-dsDNA, ASO/anti-DNase B, hepatitis screen, family urine
screen, hearing test (Alport), ± renal biopsy.
Renal biopsy indicated if: persistent significant proteinuria, recurrent macroscopic hematuria, abnor
renal function, persistently low complement.
