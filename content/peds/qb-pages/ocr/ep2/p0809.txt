Congenital Nephrotic Syndrome
Within first 3 months of life (rare).
•Presentation:
•Genetics: Usually recessive; high incidence in Finland; >70% have genetic defect
genetic testing recommended.
•Treatment:
• Severe cases: unilateral nephrectomy dialysis renal transplant when older.
• Milder cases: may not need replacement therapy in childhood.
•Prognosis: Historically high mortality from hypoalbuminemia; now survival improved,
complications mainly from progressive CKD.
