Pediatric Bleeding Disorders
Inherited Coagulation Disorders
1.
•Hemophilia A & B: X-Iinked; FVIII or FIX deficiency; presents with spontaneous
joint/muscle bleeds; managed with factor replacement ± DDAVP (for mild hemophilia A)
•Von Willebrand Disease (vWD): autosomal dominant; mucosal bleeding; treated with
DDAVP or FVIII concentrates
Acquired Coagulation Disorders
2.
•Vitamin K deficiency: leads to decreased factors II, VII, IX, X prolonged PT, bleeding
•Liver disease, DIC ITP also cause bleeding
