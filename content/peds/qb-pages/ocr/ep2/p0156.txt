Incidence of Genetic Disorders
•Overall:
• 2% of live-born babies have a significant congenital anomaly.
• 5% have a genetic disorder.
• Types of Genetic Disorders:
• Chromosomal abnormalities (numerical or structural)
• Single-gene (Mendelian) disorders
• Unusual mechanisms (imprinting, mitochondrial)
• Polygenic / multifactorial disorders (gene-environment interactions, epigenetic
influences)
