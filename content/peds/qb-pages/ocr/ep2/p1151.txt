Neonatal Hepatitis Syndrome
•Prolonged jaundice, liver inflammation, faltering growth.
•Biopsy: giant cell hepatitis.
•Must differentiate from biliary atresia.
Progressive Familial Intrahepatic Cholestasis (PFIC)
•Autosomal recessive, bile salt transport defect.
•Presentation: jaundice, severe pruritus, faltering growth, rickets.
•Diagnosis: gene mutations.
•Treatment: nutrition, fat-soluble vitamins, IBAT inhibitors for pruritus, eventual liver
transplant.
