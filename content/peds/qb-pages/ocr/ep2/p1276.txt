3. A 2-year-old child is brought to the outpatient clinic for developmental delay.
He is just able to stand with support and can pronounce 2 or 3 words. He cannot
grasp a pencil. What is the most important initial diagnostic step?
A. MRI brain
B. CT brain
C. Fundus examination
D. EEG
—Detailedbirth_history
The first Step in evaluating developmental delay is a thorough history,
especially perinatal and birth events. Imaging or EEG may follow based on
history and examination findings.
