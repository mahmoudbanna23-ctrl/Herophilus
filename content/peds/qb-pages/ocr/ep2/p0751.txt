60. A I -year-old infant presents with recurrent respiratory infections and a harsh
pansystolic murmur. Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis?
a. Atrial septal defect (ASD)
b. Ventricular septal defect (VSD)
c. Coarctation of the aorta
d. Patent ductus arteriosus (PDA)
Recurrent respiratory infections with a harsh pansystolic
murmur are classic for VSD.
