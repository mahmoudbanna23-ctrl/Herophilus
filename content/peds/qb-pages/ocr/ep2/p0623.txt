Rheumatic Heart Disease (RHD)
•Long-term valve damage (scarring, fibrosis).
•Mitral valve most affected mitral stenosis/regurgitation.
•Also aortic, tricuspid, rarely pulmonary.
•Repeated ARF symptoms may appear by 2nd decade.
Management
•NSAIDs/naproxen for arthritis.
•Steroids for severe carditis ± heart failure.
•Supportive care for Sydenham chorea.
•Penicillin to eradicate GAS.
•Monitor with serial CRP, echo.
Secondary Prophylaxis
•Prevent recurrence (U severity of RHD).
•Monthly benzathine penicillin injection (preferred).
•Alternative: daily oral penicillin (less effective).
•Continue until 10 yrs post-last ARF or age 221 (whichever longer).
