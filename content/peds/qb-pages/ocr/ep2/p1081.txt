Clinical Features
•Peaks in spring & autumn.
•Classical triad: polyuria, polydipsia, weight loss.
•Late/DKA signs: acetone breath, vomiting, dehydration, Kussmaul breathing, shock,
reduced consciousness.
•Risk of misdiagnosis (pneumonia, appendicitis).
Diagnosis
•Random glucose >11.1 mmol/L (>200 mg/dL) + symptoms.
•Fasting glucose >7 mmol/L (>126 mg/dL).
• HbA1c supportive.
•Glycosuria, ketosis.
•Type 2 suspicion: obese, insulin resistance (acanthosis nigricans, skin tags, PCOS).
