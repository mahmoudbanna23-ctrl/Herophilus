69. Which is an essential aspect in the management of acute lymphoblastic
leukemia (ALL) in children?
A. Iron therapy
B. Folic acid supplementation
C. Anticoagulants
D. Intrathecal chemotherapy
E. Bone marrow radiation
ALL can infiltrate the CNS, so intrathecal chemotherapy is essential to prevent
or treat CNS leukemia. Supportive therapies like iron or folic acid are not
primary treatments, and bone marrow radiation is rarely first-line.
