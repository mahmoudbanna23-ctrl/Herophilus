42. What would be the initial step in the management of diabetic ketoacidosis
(DKA)?
A. Start IV insulin infusion
B. SC rapid-acting insulin
C. IV NaHC03
D. Oxygen therapy
E. IV bolus of normal saline
The first step in DKA management is fluid resuscitation to treat
dehydration and stabilize circulation. Insulin and bicarbonate
come after initial fluid therapy.
