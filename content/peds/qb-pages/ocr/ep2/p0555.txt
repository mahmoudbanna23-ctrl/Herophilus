39. Which of the following is NOT a complication of whooping cough?
a. Pneumonia
b. Intracranial hemorrhage
c. Convulsions
d. Hemolytic anemia
e. Subconjunctival hemorrhage
While pertussis can cause pneumonia, convulsions, and
hemorrhages, hemolytic anemia is not a recognized
complication.
