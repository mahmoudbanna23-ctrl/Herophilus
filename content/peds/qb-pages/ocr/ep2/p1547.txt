57. Shlomo, a 9-day-old Jewish boy, presents with persistent bleeding after
circumcision. PT is mildly prolonged, APT T is >120s. What is the likely
diagnosis?
A. ß-Thalassaemia major
B. Liver disease
C. Haemophilia A
D. von Willebrand disease
E. Immune thrombocytopenic purpura
Severe prolonged APTT with normal platelets and mild PT
prolongation Suggests haemophilia A. vWD typically has mild
APTT prolongation.
