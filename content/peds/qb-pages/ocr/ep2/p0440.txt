85. A 7-year-old boy presents acutely with petechiae on his legs, severe
abdominal pain, bloody feces, hematuria and painful joint swelling. The
hematology laboratory results are normal. Which one of the following is the most
likely diagnosis?
a. Acute lymphocytic leukemia
b. Aplast syndrome
c. Henoch-Schönlein purpura
d. Juvenile rheumatoid arthritis
e. Typhus abdominalis
HSP is a small-vessel vasculitis presenting with purpuric rash,
abdominal pain, hematuria, and joint swelling.
