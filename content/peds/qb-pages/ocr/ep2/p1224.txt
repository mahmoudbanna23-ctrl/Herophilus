I. Concerning the epidemiology of childhood cancer, which of the following
types is most common in the UK?
A. Bone tumour
B. Brain tumour
C. Leukaemia
D. Neuroblastoma
E. Wilms tumour (nephroblastoma)
Leukaemia is the most common childhood cancer, followed by
brain tumours and lymphomas. Bone and renal tumours are
less frequent.
