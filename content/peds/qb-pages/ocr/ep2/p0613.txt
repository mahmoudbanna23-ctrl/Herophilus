7. Common Lesions
Atrial Septal Defect (ASD)
•Types: Secundum (80%), Partial AVSD.
•Symptoms: often asymptomatic, recurrent infections, arrhythmias later.
•Signs: ejection systolic murmur (ULSE), fixed split S2, ± pansystolic murmur in AVSD.
•Diagnosis: Echo (gold standard).
•Management:
• Secundum ASD device closure at 3—5 years.
• Partial AVSD surgical repair.
Ventricular Septal Defect (VSD)
B.
•Most common (30%).
•Small VSD: loud murmur, often close spontaneously.
•Large VSD: heart failure, recurrent infections, pulmonary hypertension.
•Investigations: Echo + Doppler.
•Management:
• Medical (diuretics, ACE inhibitors, calories).
• Surgical closure at 3—6 months if symptomatic or pulmonary hypertension risk.
