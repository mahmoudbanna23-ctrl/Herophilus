Epstein-Barr virus (EBV) infection (infectious mononucleosis) causes fever,
lymphadenopathy, tonsillar exudate, and splenomegaly. It often presents with
mild jaundice in adolescents.
Wheezing that occurs only during viral infections in a previously healthy
child is typical of viral-induced episodic wheeze. Unlike asthma, symptoms do
not occur between infections.
'Innocent murmurs are systolic, vary with posture, have no radiation, and
occur in asymptomatic children. A third heart sound suggests cardiac disease
and needs further evaluation.
• Early hand preference before 18 months and asymmetric use of limbs
suggest hemiplegic cerebral palsy rather than normal development. This
indicates fine motor delay from upper motor neuron involvement.
