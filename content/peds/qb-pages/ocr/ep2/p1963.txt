A family history of epilepsy increases the risk of developing epilepsy after
febrile convulsions. Genetic predisposition is a stronger risk factor than seizure
duration.
• Croup presents with a barking cough and inspiratory stridor in young
children. It is usually mild and caused by viral infection of the upper airway.
Parainfluenza virus is the leading cause of croup. It causes inflammation of
the larynx and trachea, producing stridor and the classic barking cough.
'A chest X-ray is the next best step for a persistent cough unresponsive to
antibiotics. It helps detect pneumonia, collapse, or foreign body aspiration.
Prostaglandin El infusion is vital for duct-dependent congenital heart
diseases in neonates. It maintains ductal patency to ensure systemic or
pulmonary circulation.
