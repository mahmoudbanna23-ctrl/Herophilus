10. Ji, a 12-month-old Japanese girl, presents with a 2-day history of fever and
vomiting. She is well-hydrated and has signs of coryza and pharyngitis. A bag
urine sample was sent. Results: WBCs 100/mm3, RBCs negative, organisms not
seen, red cell casts absent, culture not available.
What is the most likely diagnosis?
A. Balanitis
B. Glomerulonephritis
C. Normal result
A bag-collected urine sample showing high WBCs without
organisms or red cells, especially in a child with viral symptoms,
suggests contamination rather than true IJTI. Bag samples are
unreliable and prone to contamination.
D. Perineal contamination
E. Urinary tract infection (UTI)
