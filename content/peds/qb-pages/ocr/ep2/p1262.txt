20. Which of the following conditions is not associated with an increased risk of
malignancy?
A. Down syndrome
B. Beckwith—Wiedemann syndrome
C. Ataxia telangiectasia
D. Li-Fraumeni syndrome
E. Turner syndrome
Turner syndrome is not linked to increased cancer risk. In contrast, Down syndrome
(T leukaemia), Beckwith—Wiedemann (T Wilms tumour, hepatoblastoma), ataxia
telangiectasia ('P lymphoma/leukaemia), and Li-Fraumeni (T multiple cancers) all
carry higher malignancy risk.
