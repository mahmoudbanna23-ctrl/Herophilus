Guillain-Barré syndrome typically follows a URTI or gastroenteritis by 1—3 weeks.
Weakness is more prominent than sensory symptoms. MRI is preferred for ruling out
cord pathology. CSF shows raised protein with normal WBC (albuminocytologic
