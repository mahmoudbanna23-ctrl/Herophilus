16. Hannah is a 3-month-old infant who has had a cough for over 2 weeks. She
has now developed prolonged bouts of coughing. She has started to vomit at the
end of the bout of coughing. Her temperature is 380C. Her respiratory rate is 25
breaths/min. On auscultation of her chest, there are some scattered crackles. What
is the most likely diagnosis?
A. Bronchiolitis
B. Pneumonia
C. Pertussis (whooping cough)
D. Bronchiolitis
Pertussis typically presents with paroxysms of coughing
followed by post-tussive vomiting, especially in young infants.
The prolonged cough history and classic vomiting pattern are
key diagnostic clues.
