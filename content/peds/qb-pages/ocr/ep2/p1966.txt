Immune thrombocytopenic purpura (ITP) follows viral infections and causes
isolated thrombocytopenia. Children present with bruises and petechiae but
otherwise appear healthy.
'Spastic hemiplegia affects one side of the body, producing increased tone,
exaggerated reflexes, and early hand preference on the unaffected side
. It
usually results from a unilateral brain lesion.
Bone marrow failure leads to reduced red cell production, causing anemia
with a low reticulocyte count. The marrow's inability to produce new cells
differentiates it from hemolytic anemias.
Botulinum toxin injections temporarily reduce muscle spasticity in cerebral
palsy by inhibiting acetylcholine release. This improves mobility and comfo but
does not treat the underlying neurological damage.
