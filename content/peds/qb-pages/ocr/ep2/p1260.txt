19. Which of the following is a good prognostic factor in acute lymphoblastic
leukaemia?
A. White cell count >50 x 109/14 at diagnosis
B. Age > I O years at diagnosis
C. Age year at diagnosis
D. Non-B cell, non-T cell leukaemia
E. Presence of translocations, e.g. Philadelphia chromosome
The "common ALL" (non-B, non-T type) is associated with the
best prognosis. Very high WBC, age extremes, and genetic
translocations are poor prognostic signs.
