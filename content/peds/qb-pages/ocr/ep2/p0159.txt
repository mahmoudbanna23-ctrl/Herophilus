B. Turner Syndrome (45,X)
•Incidence: 1 in 2500 live-born females
• Cytogenetics:
• 50% monosomy X, others have deletions, isochromosomes, structural X defects
•Features:
• Short stature (cardinal), ovarian dysgenesis infertility, delayed puberty
• Webbed neck, wide carrying angle, lymphoedema, congenital heart defects
(coarctation)
• Mostly normal intelligence
•Diagnosis: Prenatal US, postnatal karyotype
•Treatment: Growth hormone therapy, estrogen replacement; supportive care
