54. Which clinical condition is associated with a diastolic cardiac murmur?
A. Anemia
B. Hyperthyroidism with hyperdynamic circulation
C. Mitral stenosis
D. Aortic stenosis
E. Fever
Diastolic murmurs are caused by obstructive lesions like mitral
stenosis; systolic murmurs are from obstruction or
regurgitation.
