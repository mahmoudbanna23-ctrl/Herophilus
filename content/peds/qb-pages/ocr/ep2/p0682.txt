27. A 9 months-old infant presented with sweating, tachypnea, severe tachycardia,
refusal of feeding over the past couple ofhours. Pulses are thread (HR 250 /min).
No murmur is heard. What is the most probable diagnosis?
a. Supraventricular tachycardia
b. Rheumatic carditis
c. VSD
d. Coarctation of aorta
e. ASD
The infant has very fast HR (250/min), sweating, tachypnea,
poor feeding, and no murmur, which strongly suggests SVT
rather than structural heart disease.
