63. A patient with which of the following conditions would benefit most from
splenectomy?
A. Sickle cell anemia
B. Leukemia
C. Thalassemia
D. Hereditary spherocytosis
Splenectomy reduces hemolysis in hereditary spherocytosis,
improving anemia and jaundice. Other conditions like sickle cell
or thalassemia may benefit only in specific cases.
