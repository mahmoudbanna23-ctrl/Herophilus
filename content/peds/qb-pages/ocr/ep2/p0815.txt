Acute Kidney Injury (AKI)
•Definition: Sudden, reversible reduction in renal function; oliguria (<0.5 ml/kg/h)
common.
•Causes:
• Prerenal: hypovolemia (gastroenteritis, sepsis, burns, hemorrhage, nephrotic
syndrome), heart failure.
• Renal: HUS, vasculitis, GN, ATN, interstitial nephritis, pyelonephritis.
• Postrenal: obstruction (posterior urethral valves, stones, catheter block).
•Management: Monitor fluid balance, correct hypovolemia, treat cause, adjust meds,
dialysis if needed.
•Key markers: FENO (low in prerenal), ultrasound for obstruction vs CKD vs acute
nephritis.
