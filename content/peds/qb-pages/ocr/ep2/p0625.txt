Myocarditis & Cardiomyopathy
•Dilated cardiomyopathy: inherited, metabolic, or viral myocarditis.
•Suspect in child with cardiomegaly + heart failure.
•Echo confirms diagnosis.
•Management: diuretics, ACEi, carvedilol. Steroids/lVlG controversial. Some progress to
transplant.
•Hypertrophic/restrictive forms often syndromic (e.g. Pompe, Noonan).
