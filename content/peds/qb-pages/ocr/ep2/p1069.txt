56. The following is RIGHT regarding Guillain-Barre Syndrome:
a. CSF is diagnostic in early cases
b. Dysphagia, aspiration, and respiratory failure may occur
c. Mild cases at presentation could be home treated
d. Residual neurologic damage commonly occurs in children
e. Bilateral asymmetrical ascending paralysis is characteristic
Severe GBS can cause bulbar weakness and respiratory muscle
involvement, requiring ICU Support.
