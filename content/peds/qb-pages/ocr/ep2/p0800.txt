Dipstick & Culture
•Dipstick (23 yrs):
• Leukocyte esterase + nitrite + -5 UTI likely start antibiotics.
• One positive culture confirmation.
• Both negative —Y UTI unlikely.
•Culture diagnostic: 2105 CFIJ/mL of single organism (clean sample) or any growth
(catheter/SPA).
•Common organisms: E. coli (most), Klebsiella, Proteus (boys, stones), Pseudomonas (structural
abnormality), Enterococcus.
Risk Factors
•Structural/antenatal urinary abnormalities (e.g. VUR).
•Incomplete emptying: constipation, infrequent voiding, neuropathic bladder.
Investigations
•First UTI in infants: ultrasound obstruction or structural anomaly.
•Further imaging reserved for: recurrent/atypical UTIs, poor response to antibiotics, non-E
•MCIJG if obstruction suspected.
•DMSA/MAG3 deferred 3 months (to assess scars).
