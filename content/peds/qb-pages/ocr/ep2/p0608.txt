1. Recent Developments in Pediatric Cardiac Disease
•Early detection: of congenital lesions identified antenatally; >70% of severe cases
diagnosed before birth.
•Imaging: Echocardiography is the main diagnostic tool; MRI adds 3D anatomical and
hemodynamic assessment, reducing catheterization needs.
•Management: 60% of defects treated non-invasively (e.g. PDA closure).
•New therapies: Target pulmonary hypertension, delaying the need for transplantation.
•Outcomes: Infant cardiac surgical mortality has dramatically fallen from 20% (1970) to
1.8% (2016).
