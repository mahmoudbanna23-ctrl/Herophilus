33. The most common causative organism of urinary tract infection (UTI) is:
a. E. coli
b. Staphylococcus aureus
c. Klebsiella
d. Enterococcus
E. coli causes about 80—90% of pediatric UTIs. Other organisms
like Klebsiella and Enterococcus are less common.
