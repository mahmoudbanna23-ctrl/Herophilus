42. The parents of a 6-year-old girl report that she frequently stares blankly at her
teachers for a few seconds and her academic performance has declined. What is
the most helpful investigation to reach the diagnosis?
a. Brain MRI
b. Brain CT
c. Fundus examination
d. EEG study
e. EMG study
Brief episodes of blank staring in a child suggest absence
seizures. EEG is the most helpful diagnostic tool to confirm
generalized 3 Hz spike-and-wave discharges characteristic of
this condition.
