14. Anoushka, a I -year-old girl, presents to the Emergency Department with a
respiratory tract infection. She is pink and well-perfused. There is a thrill and a
pansystolic murmur at the lower left sternal edge. In this case, which of the
following would be the most likely findings on echocardiography?
A. Atrial septal defect
B. Ventricular septal defect
C. Coarctation of the aorta
D. Mitral stenosis
E. Mitral regurgitation
A pansystolic murmur with a thrill at the lower left sternal edge
is typical of a VSD. The child may be otherwise well if the defect
is small.
