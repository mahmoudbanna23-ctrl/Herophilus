Central Motor Disorders
1. Corticospinal (Pyramidal) Tract Disorders
•Weakness pattern:
IJL: shoulder adduction, elbow flexion, forearm pronation.
LL: hip adduction + internal rotation + extension, knee extension, ankle plantarflexion.
•Features: hyperreflexia, extensor plantar reflex (+ve Babinski), loss offine finger movement.
•Causes: cerebral palsy, brain injury, stroke, tumour, dysgenesis, hypoxia-ischemia, ADEM, myelitis.
2. Basal Ganglia Disorders
•Movement initiation difficulty.
•Dyskinesias:
• Dystonia (fluctuating tone).
• Chorea (jerky movements).
• Athetosis (writhing).
•Causes: hypoxia, CO poisoning, post-surgery chorea, post-streptococcal chorea, Wilson disease, Huntington disease,
mitochondrial disease.
3. Cerebellar Disorders
•Signs: dysmetria (past-pointing), dysdiadochokinesis (poor alternating movements), ataxic wide-based gait,
nystagmus, scanning dysarthria.
•Causes: posterior fossa lesions (e.g. medulloblastoma), post-viral (varicella), intoxication, genetic (Friedreich at
ataxia telangiectasia).
•Posterior column disease may mimic (worse with eyes closed).
