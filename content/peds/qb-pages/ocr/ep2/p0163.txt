2. X-Linked Inheritance
•Gene location: X chromosome.
. Types:
• X-Iinked recessive (most common):
• Males affected; females usually carriers.
• Sons of carrier mothers 50% affected; daughters 50% carriers.
• Sons of affected males unaffected; daughters all carriers.
• Examples: Hemophilia A & B, Duchenne/Becker muscular dystrophy, G6PD
deficiency.
• X-Iinked dominant (rare):
• Both sexes affected; severity may differ.
• Sons of affected males unaffected; daughters all affected or carriers.
• Examples: Hypophosphatemic rickets, Rett syndrome (lethal in males).
