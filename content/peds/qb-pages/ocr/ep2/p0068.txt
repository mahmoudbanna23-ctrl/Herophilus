25. A 20-month-old girl is not yet walking and drags her right leg when crawling.
She has increased tone and reflexes in the right limbs. What is the most
appropriate investigation?
a. Chromosome karyotype
b. Congenital infection screen
c. Creatine kinase
d. CT or MRI scan of the brain
Asymmetric motor function with hypertonia suggests a central cause like cerebral
palsy. Neuroimaging (CT or MRI) is necessary to assess brain structure and
diagnose possible perinatal injury.
