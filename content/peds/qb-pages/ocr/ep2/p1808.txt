25. Developmental milestones vary between children; however, there are normal
ranges for each domain. Which of the following is not a developmental concern?
A. Not visually fixing at 5 weeks
B. Not sitting unsupported at 7 months
C. Not using single words at 16 months
D. Not walking at 17 months
E. Not copying a line at 24 months
Not using single words at 15 months. Single words, e.g. mama and dada, should
be heard by about 12monthsofage.
