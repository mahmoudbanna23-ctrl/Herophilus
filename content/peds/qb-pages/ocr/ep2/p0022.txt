2. Sophie is a well-bom 8-week-old baby who was bom at term. She has come
for a routine developmental check. Which of the following would you NOT
expect her to be able to do?
A. Auditory brainstem response audiometry
B. Fix and follow a toy
C. Quiet to a loud noise
D. Raise her head when lying prone
E. Reach out and grasp an object
At 8 weeks, babies can fix and follow, respond to noise, and lift their head briefly
when prone. Reaching and grasping is expected later, around 3—4 months.
