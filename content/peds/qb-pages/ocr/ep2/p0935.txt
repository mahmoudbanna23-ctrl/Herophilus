Classification of Epileptic Seizures
1. Generalized seizures (bilateral, synchronous onset)
•Absence: brief stare, eyelid flicker, no recall, provoked by hyperventilation, EEG: 3 Hz
spike-wave.
•Myoclonic: sudden jerks (trunk/limbs).
•Tonic: sustained muscle stiffening.
•Clonic: rhythmic jerks.
•Tonic-clonic: tonic phase clonic jerks, cyanosis, incontinence, post-ictal sleep.
•Atonic: sudden loss of tone falls.
•Infantile spasms (West syndrome): 3—12 months, flexor spasms in clusters,
developmental regression, EEG: hypsarrhythmia.
