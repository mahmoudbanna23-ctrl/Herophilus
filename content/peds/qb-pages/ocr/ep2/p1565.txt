66. The classical clinical presentation of minimal change nephrotic syndrome
does not include which of the following?
A. A child aged between 2—6 years
B. Normal blood pressure
C. Abnormal kidney function tests
D. High serum cholesterol and triglycerides
E. Response to corticosteroid therapy
Minimal change nephrotic syndrome usually preserves kidney function, with
proteinuria, edema, hyperlipidemia, and steroid responsiveness.
