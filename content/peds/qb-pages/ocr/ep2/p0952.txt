Clinical Features
•Infants:
• Rapidly enlarging head circumference
• Separated sutures, bulging fontanelle, engorged scalp veins
• Sun-setting eyes (downward gaze)
•Older children:
• Symptoms/signs Of raised ICP (headache, vomiting, papilledema)
Diagnosis
•Infants: cranial ultrasound (including antenatal, preterm screening)
•Children: MRI (preferred) or CT if urgent
•Serial measurement Of head circumference on growth chart
Treatment
•Relieve raised ICP + prevent neurological damage
1. Ventriculoperitoneal (VP) shunt* mainstay
2. Endoscopic third ventriculostomy (ETV) alternative in selected cases
•Complications Of shunts:
• Blockage, infection (e.g. coagulase-negative staphylococci) require revision
• Over-drainage low-pressure headaches (programmable valves help)
