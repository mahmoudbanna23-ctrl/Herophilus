Management
Multidisciplinary diabetes team (paediatrician, nurse, dietitian, psychologist, social
worker).
Education: insulin use, glucose/ketone monitoring, diet (carbohydrate counting, high-
fiber), sick-day rules, hypoglycemia recognition.
School + family involvement essential.
A) Insulin Therapy
•Aim: mimic physiological secretion.
•Basal—bolus regimen (multiple daily injections): rapid-acting with meals + long-acting
background insulin.
•CSII (pump): continuous rapid-acting insulin with programmable boluses.
•Sites: abdomen, thighs, buttocks; rotate to prevent lipohypertrophy.
•Honeymoon phase temporary low insulin needs.
•Puberty increased needs.
