24. What is the most ideal insulin regimen for an 8-year-old child recently
diagnosed with type I diabetes mellitus, who has 3 regular meals a day?
a. Short-acting insulin 3 times daily
b. Basal-bolus regimen
c. Intermediate-acting insulin every 12 hours
d. Premixed insulin every 12 hours
The basal-bolus regimen best mimics physiological insulin release,
providing long-acting insulin for baseline needs and rapid-acting insulin for
meals. It offers flexibility and optimal glycemic control, especially in
school-aged children.
