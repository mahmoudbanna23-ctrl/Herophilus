37. What is the most likely causative pathogen in infective endocarditis?
A. Streptococcus pneumoniae
B. Streptococcus pyogenes
C. Streptococcus viridans
D. Group A hemolytic streptococcus
E. Group B hemolytic streptococcus
Viridans group streptococci are the most common pathogens
causing subacute infective endocarditis in children, especially
with underlying heart lesions.
