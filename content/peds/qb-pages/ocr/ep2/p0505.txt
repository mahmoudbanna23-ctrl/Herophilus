15. Fred is a normally well 4-year-old boy. He has had a runny nose and fever for
3 days. He has now developed a cough and difficulty breathing. On examination,
his temperature is 390C. He watches you but sits quietly on his mother's lap. He
has a respiratory rate of 55 breaths/min. His breaths are rapid but shallow with
some mild substernal recession. There is no wheeze on auscultation but some
coarse crackles at the right base. His oxygen saturation is 91 % in air. What is the
most likely diagnosis?
A. Bronchiolitis
B. Pneumonia
C. Pertussis (whooping cough)
D. Bronchiolitis
Fever, localized crackles, tachypnea, and oxygen desaturation
point toward pneumonia. The lack Of wheeze and presence Of
coarse crackles at the base further support bacterial
pneumonia.
E. Laryngotracheobronchitis (croup)
