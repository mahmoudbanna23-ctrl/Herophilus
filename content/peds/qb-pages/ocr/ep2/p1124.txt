16. On routine newborn exam, a baby is noted to have bilaterally undescended
testes. Genitalia appears male. What is the most important initial investigation?
A. Karyotype with FISH for SRY
B. Abdominal ultrasound scan
C. Abdominal CT scan
D. 17-Hydroxyprogesterone levels
E. Urea and electrolytes (U&Es)
In a newborn with bilateral undescended testes, the most urgent concern is
salt-wasting congenital adrenal hyperplasia (CAH), which can cause life-
threatening electrolyte imbalances. Therefore, urea and electrolytes (U&ES)
should be checked immediately. Further tests like karyotype, ultrasound,
and 17-hydroxyprogesterone are done after stabilization to confirm the
diagnosis and assess anatomy.
