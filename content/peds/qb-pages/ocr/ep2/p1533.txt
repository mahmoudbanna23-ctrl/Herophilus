50. Which of the following organisms is likely to cause pneumonia in a newborn?
A. Group B streptococcus
B. Escherichia coli
C. Listeria monocytogenes
D. Streptococcus pneumoniae
In newborns, causative organisms of pneumonia are typically
acquired from the maternal genital tract. Group B
streptococcus is the most common, along with Gram-negative
organisms such as E. coli.
