Urinary Tract Infection (UT') in Children
Epidemiology
•Incidence: 3—7% girls, 1—2% boys have 21 UTI before age 6.
•Recurrence: 12—30% within 1 year.
•Sites:
• Pyelonephritis fever, systemic illness, renal scarring risk.
• Cystitis local symptoms, usually no fever.
•Up to 50% have underlying structural abnormality.
