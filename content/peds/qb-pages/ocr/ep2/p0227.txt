28. Which type of inheritance is expected if a woman with a genetic disorder can
have affected offspring of either sex, but an affected father cannot pass on the
disease to his offspring?
A. Pseudogenetic Inheritance
B. Digenic inheritance
C. Mitochondrial inheritance
D. Autosomal recessive inheritance
E. Autosomal dominant inheritance
Mitochondrial DNA is inherited only from the mother. Affected
mothers can transmit the condition to all children, but affected
fathers do not pass it on.
