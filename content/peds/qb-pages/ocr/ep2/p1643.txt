24. A 2-month-old baby has coarse facies, a large fontanelle, hypotonia, and
jaundice. Blood sugar is normal.
Which investigation is most likely to reveal the diagnosis?
A. Growth hormone
B. Karyotype
C. Thyroid function tests
D. Blood group
E. Creatine kinase (CK) level

thyroid function tests confirm the diagnosis.
