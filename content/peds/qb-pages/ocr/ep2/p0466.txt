Acute Asthma in Children
Assessment:
•Document symptom duration, prior treatments, and past attack course.
•Classify severity: Mild, Moderate, Severe, Life-threatening based on:
Breathlessness
Respiratory effort (recession, accessory muscles)
• Auscultation (wheeze, silent chest)
Oxygen saturation (Sp02)
Peak expiratory flow (PEF)
Signs of exhaustion, cyanosis, altered consciousness
Causes of Acute Breathlessness:
•Asthma, infections (pneumonia), foreign body, anaphylaxis, pneumothorax, metabolic acidosis, heart
failure, hyperventilation.
Hospital Admission Criteria:
•Poor response to high-dose inhaled bronchodilators
•Exhaustion
•PEF predicted
•sp02 <92%
