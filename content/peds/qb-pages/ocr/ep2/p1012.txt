28. Which of the following is true about antiepileptic drugs (AEDs)?
a. All AEDs have side effects
b. Using multiple drugs is the preferred first-line approach
c. All cases of epilepsy should automatically receive AEDs
d. AEDs have no long-term adverse effects
All antiepileptic drugs carry potential side effects. Not every
seizure needs lifelong AEDs, and monotherapy is usually
preferred over multiple drugs initially.
