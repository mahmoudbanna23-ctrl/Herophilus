Hypertension in Children
•Definition: BP >95th percentile (adjusted for age, height, sex).
•Causes:
• Renal (CKD, renal artery stenosis, PCKD, tumors)
• Vascular (coarctation)
• Endocrine (CAH, Cushing, hyperthyroidism, pheochromocytoma)
• Essential (increasingly common with obesity/OSA).
•Management: Treat underlying cause if possible; otherwise antihypertensives (CCBs,
ACEi/ARBs).
