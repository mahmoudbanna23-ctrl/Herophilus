2. Basophil stippling is seen in blood films of which of the following conditions?
A. Hereditary spherocytosis
B. Aplastic anemia
C. Liver disease
D. Thalassemia
E. Xerocytosis
Basophilic stippling refers to aggregated ribosomes in red cells
and is characteristic of thalassemia, lead poisoning, and some
anemias. It is not typically seen in hereditary spherocytosis or
aplastic anemia.
