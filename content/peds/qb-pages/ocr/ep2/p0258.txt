Diagnosis:
•CBC: normocytic-normochromic anemia
•Blood film: sickled RBCs
•Hb electrophoresis/HPLC
•Neonatal screening recommended
Management:
•Prophylaxis: Vaccination, daily penicillin
•Acute crises: Analgesia, hydration, antibiotics, oxygen, exchange transfusion if severe
•Chronic: Hydroxyurea (increases HbF), bone marrow transplant for severe cases
Prognosis:
•HbSS: childhood mortality in high-income countries; severe cases may die years
•HbSC: milder, regular eye exams recommended
•Sickle trait: asymptomatic, importance of screening before anesthesia
