49. A 4-month-old infant presents with fever, cough, and reduced feeding.
Respiratory rate is 60/min, with mild recession, wheeze, and crackles throughout.
What is the most likely diagnosis?
a. Croup
b. Virally induced atopic wheeze
c. Cystic fibrosis
d. Bacterial chest infection
e. Bronchiolitis
Bronchiolitis is common in infants under 1 year and presents
with cough, wheezing, and respiratory distress. It is usually
caused by RSV and is managed supportively.
