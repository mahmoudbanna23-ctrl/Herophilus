Headaches
•Very common in older children/adolescents.
•Classification (International Headache Society):
• Primary headaches migraine, tension-type, cluster, other primary headaches.
• Secondary headaches due to raised 'CP, trauma, infection, vascular lesions,
drugs, sinusitis, metabolic causes, etc.
• Cranial neuralgias & facial pain syndromes.
Red flags (suggest secondary causes, esp. space-occupying lesion):
•Worse when lying down, night waking, morning vomiting.
•Change in mood, school performance.
•Visual field defects, CN palsies (esp. VI), abnormal gait, papilledema, growth/puberty
disturbance.
