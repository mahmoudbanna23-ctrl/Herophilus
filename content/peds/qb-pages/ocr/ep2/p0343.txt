37. Hemoglobin production differs through fetal life and extrauterine life, and the
molecule itself changes with age. Which of the following statements regarding
hemoglobin is correct?
A. At birth, HbA is predominant
B. Hb concentration falls after birth until around 7 weeks
C. Hematopoiesis mainly occurs in the liver and spleen at term
D. The lifespan of a normal red blood cell is 10 days
E. Fetal hemoglobin has lower affinity for oxygen
After birth, Hb levels physiologically drop due to reduced
erythropoiesis and transition from fetal to adult hemoglobin,
reaching a nadir around 6—8 weeks. This is known as
physiological anemia Of infancy.
