saliva, and respiratory distress. What is the most appropriate next step in
management?
A. Intravenous steroids
B. Oxygen and nebulized adrenaline
C. Intravenous antibiotics
D. Immediate intubation
E. Nebulized salbutamol

immediate priority before any other interventions.
