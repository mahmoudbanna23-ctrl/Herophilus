54. A 6-year-old boy with a known diagnosis of ventricular septal defect has a 2-
week history of fever of unknown source, fatigue, weight loss, myalgia, and
headaches. On repeated examinations during this time, he is found to have
developed a new heart murmur, petechiae, and mild splenomegaly. Which of the
following investigations are essential to establish a diagnosis?
A. Echocardiography & ECG
B. Complete blood count & CRP
C. Blood culture & Echocardiography.
D. ASOT & CRP
These findings suggest infective endocarditis. Blood cultures
E. Chest X-ray & ECG identify the organism, and echocardiography detects
vegetations on the heart valves.
