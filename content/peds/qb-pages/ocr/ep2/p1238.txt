8. Britney, aged 5 years, presents with progressive headaches and a normal red
reflex. What is the most likely diagnosis?
A. Craniopharyngioma
B. Concomitant squint
C. Optic glioma
D. Posterior fossa tumour
E. Retinoblastoma
Brain tumours, especially in the posterior fossa, cause raised
intracranial pressure leading to headaches and vomiting.
Normal red reflex excludes eye disease.
