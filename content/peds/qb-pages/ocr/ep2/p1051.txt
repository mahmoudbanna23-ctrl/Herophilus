weakness. His parents report that he finds it difficult to climb the stairs at home.
This has been becoming progressively worse over the preceding 6—12 months. He
is playing on the carpet with his brother when his mother calls him over, he has to
roll from his back onto his front in order to stand up. Choose the sign that is being
described.
a. Gowers sign
b. Kernig's sign
c. Brudzinski's sign
d. Babinski's sign
e. Dyskinesia
Gowers sign: Characteristic maneuver where a child with proximal
muscle weakness (commonly Duchenne muscular dystrophy) uses
his hands to "climb up" his Own body from the floor in order to
stand.
