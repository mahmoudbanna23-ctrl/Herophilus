27. A 4-years old male child developed an upper respiratory tract infection that
was followed in a 2-weeks by generalized edema. His blood pressure is normal,
urinalysis reveal 2 to 5 RBC/HPF and 4+ protein. His serum urea is 9 mg/dl
(normal), creatinine 0.6 mg/dl (normal), ASOT 200 (normal) and C3 is normal.
The most likely diagnosis would be:
a. Post streptococcal glomerulonephritis
b. Nephrotic syndrome
c. Membranoproliferative glomerulonephritis
d. Pyelonephritis
e. Hemolytic uremic syndrome
The child has generalized edema, heavy
proteinuria (4+), and normal renal function
with normal complement, which is typical of
nephrotic syndrome. Post-streptococcal GN
usually shows low C3 and hypertension, which
are absent here.
