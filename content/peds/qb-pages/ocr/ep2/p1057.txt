50. Which of the following is a treatment modality in cerebral palsy?
a. Botulinum toxin
b. Resection of the brain area
c. Anticonvulsants only
d. Chemotherapy
Botulinum toxin is used to reduce spasticity in CP. Other
treatments include physiotherapy and supportive care, not
brain resection or chemotherapy.
