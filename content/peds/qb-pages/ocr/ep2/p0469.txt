Cystic Fibrosis (CF)
Genetics & Pathophysiology:
•Autosomal recessive, CFTR gene mutations (chromosome 7)
•Abnormal chloride transport thick secretions in lungs, pancreas, intestines, sweat
glands
•Multi-system disorder: lungs, pancreas, liver, Gl tract
Clinical Features:
•Newborn: Meconium ileus, prolonged jaundice, growth faltering
•Infancy/Young Child: Recurrent chest infections, malabsorption, steatorrhea, rectal
prolapse
•Older Child/Adolescent: Bronchiectasis, nasal polyps, ABPA, diabetes, liver disease,
