9. At a routine consultation, a 12-month-old boy is cruising around furniture and
saying 'mama' and 'papa'. He only uses his left hand. What is the most likely
finding?
A. Gross motor delay
B. Fine motor delay
C. Speech and language delay
D. Play and social delay
E. Global delay
Using one hand preferentially may indicate delayed
development of fine motor skills rather than gross motor or
speech delays.
