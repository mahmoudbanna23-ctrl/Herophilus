30. Iman is a 2-year-old girl. She presents to the Emergency Department with a 2-
day history of fever and vomiting, limping, and "smelly" urine. She has no
significant medical history and is not on any medication. On examination she has
a temperature of 390C and has a heart rate of 126 beats/min. She has generalized
tenderness over her abdomen. What is the best way to collect a urine sample from
her?
a. Bag sample
b. Catheter sample
c. Pad/cotton wool balls in nappy
d. Clean catch
e. Suprapubic aspirate
This involves waiting with a sterile bowl to catch the
urine. It is the least invasive and has less risk of
contamination. The child is well enough to wait before
starting treatment and you want to be sure that you are
actually treating a urinary tract infection. The smell of
urine is an unreliable sign of genuine infection.
