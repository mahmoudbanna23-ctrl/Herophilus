• Side effects:
• Valproate: weight gain, hair loss, liver failure, teratogenic.
•Carbamazepine: rash, ataxia, enzyme inducer.
•Lamotrigine: rash, insomnia.
•Ethosuximide: nausea, vomiting.
•Levetiracetam: irritability.
• Topiramate: weight loss, depression.
• Vigabatrin: visual field restriction (use in infantile spasms).
