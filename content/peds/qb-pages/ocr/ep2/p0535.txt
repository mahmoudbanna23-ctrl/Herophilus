30. A boy, while attending a birthday party, develops sudden onset edema that
started on his lips and eyelids, and then spread to the dorsum of the hands, eyes,
and genitalia.
What is the most likely diagnosis?
a. Nutritional anemia
b. Nephrotic syndrome
c. Hepatic insufficiency
d. Angioedema
e. Heart failure
Angioedema presents with rapid, non-pitting edema of soft
tissues, often triggered by allergens or idiopathically. It may
accompany or precede anaphylaxis.
