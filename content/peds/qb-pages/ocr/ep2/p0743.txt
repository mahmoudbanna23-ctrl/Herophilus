56. An 8-month-old girl is brought to the emergency room because of appearing
ill. She is pale and dyspneic with a respiratory rate of 80 breaths per min. Heart
rate is 195 beats per min, heart sounds are weak, and a gallop is heard. Chest
auscultation reveals fine crepitations. There is cardiomegaly by chest X-ray. She
had low grade fever and rhinorrhea 2 days before. The diagnosis suggested by
these findings is:
a. Acute glomerulonephritis
b. Bronchopneumonia
c. Viral Myocarditis
d. Pericarditis
e. Wide VSD
Viral prodrome followed by cardiomegaly, tachycardia, gallop
rhythm, and heart failure suggests viral myocarditis.
