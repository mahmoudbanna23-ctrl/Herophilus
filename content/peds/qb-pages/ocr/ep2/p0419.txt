75. Splenectomy has NO beneficial therapeutic effect in:
a. Hereditary spherocytosis
b. Leukemia
c. Refractory chronic idiopathic thrombocytopenic purpura
d. B-thalassemia major
Splenectomy helps in hereditary spherocytosis, ITP, and
sometimes thalassemia, but not in leukemia where the
problem is bone marrow, not spleen.
