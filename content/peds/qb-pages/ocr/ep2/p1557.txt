62. What is the best therapeutic strategy for a 4-year-old girl diagnosed with IT P,
platelet count 30,000/gL, no active bleeding?
A. Oral steroids
B. IVIG
C. Anti-D
D. Observation and follow-up
E. Splenectomy
Children with ITP who are not bleeding usually do not require treatment, as
spontaneous recovery is common. Medications like steroids or IVIG are
reserved for active bleeding or very low platelet counts.
