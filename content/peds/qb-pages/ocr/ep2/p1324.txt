27. Which of the following is not a feature of an innocent heart murmur and
requires further investigation?
A. No radiation
B. Varies with posture
C. Systolic
D. Third heart sound
E. No symptoms
Innocent murmurs are usually systolic, vary with posture, have no
radiation, and occur in asymptomatic children. A third heart sound is
abnormal in children and may indicate heart disease, so it requires
further investigation.
