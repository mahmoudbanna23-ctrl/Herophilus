19. A child develops severe bleeding following a tonsillectomy. Laboratory tests
show a prolonged bleeding time and a prolonged partial thromboplastin time
(PTT).
What is the most likely diagnosis?
a) Hemophilia A
b) Hemophilia B
c) Von Willebrand disease
d) Factor I deficiency
This condition causes both platelet function defect (T bleeding
time) and factor VIII deficiency (T PTT), and often presents
with mucosal bleeding.
