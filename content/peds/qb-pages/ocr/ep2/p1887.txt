64. What is the optimal emergency management of a child presenting to the ER
with severe croup?
A. Suction of the pharynx
B. IV epinephrine
C. Oral ampicillin
D. Nebulized epinephrine
E. Immediate intubation
Severe croup with stridor at rest is treated urgently with
nebulized epinephrine; intubation is reserved for impending
airway obstruction.
