72. All of the following are true medical causes of abdominal pain in children,
EXCEPT?
a. Thalassemia
b. Sickle cell anemia
c. Henoch—Schönlein purpura
d. Porphyria
Thalassemia causes chronic anemia but not abdominal pain.
Sickle cell anemia, HSP, and porphyria are well-known causes Of
abdominal pain in children.
