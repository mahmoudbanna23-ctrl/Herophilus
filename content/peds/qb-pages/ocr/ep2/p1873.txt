57. A 2-year-old previously healthy boy is found to be cyanosed while playing
with his toys. When brought to the emergency department, his HR was 164/min,
RR was 70/min, Sa02 was 80% on room air, and he was gasping for air.
What is the most likely diagnosis?
A. Acute bronchiolitis
B. Congenital heart disease
C. Viral croup
D. Bronchopneumonia
E. Foreign body aspiration
Acute cyanosis, gasping, and airway obstruction in a
previously healthy child strongly suggest foreign body
aspiration.
