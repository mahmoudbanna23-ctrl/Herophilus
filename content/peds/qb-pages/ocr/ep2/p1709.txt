57. Which clinical problem is a serious complication of Fallot tetralogy?
A. Eisenmenger syndrome
B. Recurrent pneumonia
C. Cerebrovascular accidents
D. Pulmonary hypertension
E. Heart failure
In Tetralogy of Fallot, chronic low oxygen levels cause polycythemia, which
increases blood viscosity and the risk of clots. These clots can travel to the
brain, causing cerebrovascular accidents (strokes).
