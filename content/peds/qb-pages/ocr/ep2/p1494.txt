31. Which of the following statements best describes the findings in a 2-week-old
infant with pyloric stenosis?
A. Severe bilious vomiting
B. Dehydration with a metabolic acidosis
C. Hypokalaemic hypochloraemic alkalosis
D. Massive abdominal distension
E. Bowel sounds are absent
Pyloric stenosis leads to projectile non-bilious vomiting,
dehydration, and hypokalaemic hypochloraemic metabolic
alkalosis due to loss of gastric acid.
