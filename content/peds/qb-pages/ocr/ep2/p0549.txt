36. In a child whose asthma is not controlled on regular inhaled steroids and
occasional beta-2 agonist, the single next best action would be to:
a. Add long-acting beta-2 agonist
b. Increase the dose of inhaled steroid
c. Check the inhaler technique
d. Add a leukotriene inhibitor
e. Add a short course of oral steroids
Before stepping up asthma treatment, it's essential to ensure
that the child is using their inhaler correctly and consistently.
Poor technique or poor adherence is a common cause Of poor
asthma control and must be addressed before making
medication changes.
