Febrile Seizures (FS)
•Most common childhood convulsive disorder.
•Occur in 3% of children aged 6 mo—6 yrs, often with genetic predisposition.
•Usually resolve without sequelae.
Definition
Seizure with fever (2380C), age 6 mo—5 yrs, no CNS infection, no prior afebrile/neonatal
seizures.
Types
•Simple FS:
• Generalized, <15 min, once in 24 hrs, no post-ictal signs.
• Risk of later epilepsy: (same as general population).
•Complex FS:
• Focal or generalized, 215 min, may recur in 24 hrs.
• May have post-ictal paralysis/disturbed consciousness.
• Higher risk (4—12%) of later epilepsy.
