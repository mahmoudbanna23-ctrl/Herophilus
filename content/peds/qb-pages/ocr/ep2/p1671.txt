38. When counseling her parents, which of the following statements regarding
simple febrile convulsions is true?
A. They are often focal in nature
B. Children frequently go on to develop epilepsy
C. Occur between ages of 6 months and 5 years
D. Should always be investigated with lumbar puncture
E. May occur without fever
Simple febrile seizures are generalized, brief, and age-specific. They rarely lead
to epilepsy and usually do not require extensive investigation.
