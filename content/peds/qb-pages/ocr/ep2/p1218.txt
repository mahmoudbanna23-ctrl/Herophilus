CNS Therapy
•Systemic chemo penetrates CNS poorly.
•Intrathecal chemo prevents CNS relapse.
•Extra doses for patients with CNS disease at diagnosis.
Continuing Therapy
•Modest intensity chemo for up to 3 years.
•Cotrimoxazole prophylaxis prevents Pneumocystis pneumonia.
Treatment of Relapse
•High-dose chemo ± total body irradiation bone marrow transplant.
•CAR T-cell therapy for relapsed/refractory B-cell ALL (selected patients).
