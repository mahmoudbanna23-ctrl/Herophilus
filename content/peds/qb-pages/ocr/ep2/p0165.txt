5. Multifactorial & Polygenic Inheritance
•Definition: Traits or disorders caused by multiple genes ± environmental factors.
•Recurrence risk: Usually low; higher for first-degree relatives.
•Factors increasing risk: Severe form, multiple affected relatives, sex differences.
•Examples:
• Congenital malformations: Neural tube defects, cleft lip/palate, DDH, congenital
heart disease.
• Childhood diseases: Type 1 diabetes, asthma, epilepsy.
• Adult diseases: Type 2 diabetes, coronary artery disease, hypertension,
Alzheimer's, common cancers.
•Environmental/epigenetic influences: Diet, infections, folic acid, early-life exposures
can modify risk.
