50. A 14-month-old infant presents with pallor and anorexia. Laboratory results
reveal: Hg 7 g/dl, hypochromic microcytic RBCs, normal WBCs morphology,
and low serum ferritin. Which of the following statement is CORRECT?
a. He probably has beta-thalassemia major
b. Long-term treatment with packed RBCs transfusion will be required
c. A dietary history is important
d. Bone marrow examination is indicated
e. Osmotic fragility test should be done
Low Hb, microcytic hypochromic anemia, and low ferritin
suggest iron deficiency anemia. The most useful step is taking a
dietary history to guide management.
