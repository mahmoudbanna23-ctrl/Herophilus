Bile Formation & Flow
•Hepatocytes bile canaliculi ducts right & left hepatic ducts common hepatic
duct.
•Common hepatic duct + cystic duct common bile duct duodenum.
•Bile aids fat digestion; some stored in gallbladder, released after meals.
Bilirubin Metabolism
•Senescent RBCs unconjugated bilirubin (lipid soluble, toxic, crosses BBB).
•In liver: conjugated with glucuronate conjugated bilirubin (water soluble).
•Obstructed bile ducts:
• Conjugated bilirubin reflux dark urine, pale stools, pruritus, jaundice.
