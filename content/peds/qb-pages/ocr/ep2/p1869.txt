55. A 5-week-old baby boy is brought into the emergency department with a 48-
hour history of projectile vomiting. The infant is hungry after vomiting and has
not opened his bowels in 3 days. Clinical examination reveals a mass in the right
upper quadrant region. A blood gas is performed.
What finding in this blood gas is expected to be present?
A. Normal findings
B. Metabolic acidosis
C. Metabolic alkalosis
D. Respiratory acidosis
E. Respiratory alkalosis
Projectile, non-bilious vomiting in pyloric stenosis leads to loss
Of gastric acid, causing hypochloremic metabolic alkalosis.
