Phenotypes in Preschool Children:
•Viral episodic wheeze: Triggered only by viral infections, usually resolves by age 5
•Multiple trigger wheeze: Triggered by viruses + other stimuli, may continue into later
childhood
Risk Factors:
•23 episodes/year lasting days
•Interval symptoms, exercise/laughter-induced wheeze
•Family history of asthma/atopy, eczema, food allergy
Investigations:
•Children <5 yrs: Usually clinical; may use skin prick testing
•Children >5 yrs: PEFR, spirometry, bronchodilator reversibility, FeNO (airway
inflammation)
•Chest X-ray only if other conditions suspected
Differential Diagnosis:
•Viral wheeze, multiple trigger wheeze, recurrent anaphylaxis, chronic aspiration,
fibrosis, BPD, bronchiolitis obliterans, tracheobronchomalacia
