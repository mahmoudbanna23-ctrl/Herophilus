Diagnosis & Investigations
1.Clinical exam look for neuropathic bladder signs, abnormal reflexes, perineal
sensation, gait, spinal lesions.
1. Girls dry at night but wet on rising ectopic ureter.
2.Urine dipstick check for infection, glycosuria, proteinuria.
3.Ultrasound bladder emptying, wall thickening, anomalies.
4. Urodynamic studies if instability suspected.
5.X-ray/MRl spine to exclude spinal lesion/tethered cord.
6.For secondary enuresis early morning urine osmolality, renal tract ultrasound,
rarely water deprivation test.
Management
•General measures: star charts, bladder training, pelvic floor exercises.
•Treat constipation.
•Enuresis alarm: for inattentive children (pad + alarm when wet).
•Anticholinergics (oxybutynin): for bladder instability if other measures fail.
•Underlying cause treatment: infection, diabetes, neurological issues.
