69. Ahmed is a 4-week-old infant who presents with persistent bleeding from a
circumcision wound. Laboratory results show normal platelet count, prolonged
prothrombin time (P T), and normal activated partial thromboplastin time (aPTT).
What is the most appropriate management?
a. No action needed
b. factor VIII
c. Vitamin K
d. Platelet transfusion
Prolonged PT with normal platelets and aPTT in a newborn suggests
vitamin K deficiency bleeding. Vitamin K injection corrects it.
