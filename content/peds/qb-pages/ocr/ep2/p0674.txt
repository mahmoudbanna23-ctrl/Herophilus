23. It can be possible to assess whether a murmur is significant by its character
and associated features. Which of the following is not a feature of an innocent
heart murmur?
A. No radiation
B. Varies with posture
C. Systolic
D. Third heart sound
E. No symptoms
A third heart sound suggests ventricular dysfunction and is not
a feature Of an innocent murmur. Innocent murmurs are
typically systolic, soft, and positional.
