26. An I I -month-old Caucasian boy attends his GP as his mother has been
struggling to wean him. He will drink cow's milk at regular intervals and eats
some baby rice but refuses most other solids. On examination, he appears very
pale but otherwise well. There is no hepatosplenomegaly. A full blood count
(FBC) is arranged in view of the pallor, which shows: Hb — 7.8 g/dL, mean
corpuscular volume (MCV) — 69 tL. What is the most likely cause of his anemia?
A. Sickle cell anemia
B. Thalassaemia
C. Iron deficiency anemia
D. Vitamin B12 deficiency
E. Folate deficiency
Excess cow's milk, low solid intake, and microcytic anemia
indicate iron deficiency.
