19. Screening for congenital hypothyroidism in Egypt is:
a. Done by screening for T4 level
b. Done by screening for TSH level
c. Not routinely done in Egypt
d. Done only if symptoms develop
In Egypt, neonatal screening for congenital hypothyroidism is typically
done by measuring TSH levels. Early detection allows for prompt
treatment and prevention of neurodevelopmental delay.
