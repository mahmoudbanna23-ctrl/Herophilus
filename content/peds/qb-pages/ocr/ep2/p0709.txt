40. What is the most common causative agent of infective endocarditis?
a. Streptococcus viridans
b. Staphylococcus aureus
c. Enterococcus
d. Pseudomonas aeruginosa
It is the most common cause of subacute infective endocarditis,
especially on abnormal valves.
