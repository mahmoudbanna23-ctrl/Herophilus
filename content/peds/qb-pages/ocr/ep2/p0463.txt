Pediatric Asthma
Pathophysiology:
•Genetic predisposition + environmental triggers airway inflammation, edema, mucus
production, bronchial hyperresponsiveness reversible airway obstruction.
Triggers:
•Viral infections, allergens, smoke, pollution, chemical irritants, exercise, cold air
Symptoms:
•Wheeze, cough, breathlessness, chest tightness
•Worse at night/early morning or with triggers
Examination:
•May be normal between attacks
•Chronic asthma hyperinflation, polyphonic expiratory wheeze, Harrison sulci
•Atopy signs: eczema, allergic rhinitis
•Wet cough, growth faltering, or clubbing consider cystic fibrosis or chronic infect •
