44. What is the most common type of cerebral palsy associated with hypoxic-
ischemic encephalopathy?
A. Spastic hemiplegia
B. Spastic diplegia
C. Spastic quadriplegia
D. Spastic paraplegia
Severe perinatal hypoxia commonly results in spastic
quadriplegia, affecting all four limbs with increased tone.
