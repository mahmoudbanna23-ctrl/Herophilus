23. An 8-year-old boy is referred with suspected ADHD. He is hyperactive and
struggles to concentrate in school.
Which of the following features suggests a diagnosis other than ADHD?
A. Easily distracted by other children
B. Difficulty waiting for his turn
C. Symptoms only present at school
D. Constantly talking and interrupting others
E. Fidgeting with his hands while sitting
ADHD symptoms must be present in multiple settings;
only symptoms suggest alternative diagnosis.
