50. In Fallot's tetralogy, the following is NOT true:
a. Cyanosis starts at birth in all cases.
b. X ray shows boot-shaped heart
c. There is right ventricular hypertrophy
d. Hematocrit is increased
e. A very short murmur is heard during hyper-cyanotic spells
Not true; cyanosis often appears later when RV outflow
obstruction worsens.
