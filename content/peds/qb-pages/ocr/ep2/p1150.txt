Biliary Atresia
•Incidence: 1 in 15,000 births.
•Progressive fibrosis/obliteration of bile ducts.
•Presentation: prolonged jaundice, pale stools, hepatomegaly later splenomegaly.
•Investigations: conjugated bilirubin & GGT, ultrasound (small/absent gallbladder),
biopsy, confirm with cholangiogram.
•Treatment: Kasai hepatoportoenterostomy (best days; 60% success).
•Prognosis: often progresses to cirrhosis; many need liver transplant.
•Most common pediatric indication for liver transplantation.
