Hepatitis E (HEV)
•RNA virus, fecal—oral, contaminated water, also via blood transfusion or pork.
•Worldwide, more common in low-income countries.
•Clinical: mild, self-limiting.
•Pregnancy: fulminant hepatic failure with high mortality.
•No chronic disease in children.
