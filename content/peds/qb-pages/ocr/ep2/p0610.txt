3. Etiology of Congenital Heart Disease
•Genetic causes: (chromosomal abnormalities, microdeletions, point mutations).
•Polygenic inheritance: doubles recurrence risk in siblings; higher if a parent is affected.
•Teratogens: small proportion linked to environmental/external factors.
•Screening: essential in children with multisystem genetic syndromes.
4. Circulatory Changes at Birth
•Fetal: Right atrial pressure > Left atrial pressure foramen ovale open.
•At birth: Lung expansion pulmonary resistance LA pressure closure of
foramen ovale.
•Ductus arteriosus normally closes within hours—days.
•Duct-dependent lesions: closure leads to collapse, acidosis, and death unless patency
maintained.
