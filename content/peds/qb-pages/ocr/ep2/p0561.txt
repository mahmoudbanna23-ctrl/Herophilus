42. Newborns and young infants are NOT protected through maternal antibodies
against which of the following diseases?
a. Tuberculosis
b. Poliomyelitis
c. Measles
d. Mumps
e. Tetanus
Maternal antibodies (IgG) cross the placenta and provide
passive immunity to the newborn against viral diseases (e.g„
measles, mumps, polio) and bacterial toxoid-induced immunity
(e.g., tetanus). However, tuberculosis (TB) is mediated by cell-
mediated immunity, which is not transferred via maternal
antibodies, leaving newborns unprotected.
