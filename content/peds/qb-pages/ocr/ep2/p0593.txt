58. Which of the following statements about bronchiolitis is true?
a. It is caused by viruses only
b. Antibiotics are recommended in severe cases
c. Chest X-ray is diagnostic
d. It is most commonly caused by a bacterial infection
Bronchiolitis is almost always viral in origin, most commonly
due to RSV. Antibiotics and chest X-rays are not routinely
indicated unless there's a suspicion of bacterial superinfection
or complications.
