Whooping Cough (Pertussis)
Diagnosis:
•Clinical suspicion; lymphocytosis (>15 x 109/L)
•PCR or culture from pernasal swab
•Serology
Treatment:
•Macrolides (effective if given early, reduce infectivity).
•Hospitalize infants with severe cough or cyanotic attacks.
Prophylaxis:
•Macrolides for close contacts
•Vaccination for unimmunized infants
Immunization:
•Reduces severity but does not guarantee protection
•Maternal vaccination recommended during pregnancy
