41. An 8-month-old girl with Greek-Cypriot parents is brought to her GP as her
parents are worried she is not growing well and is very pale. On examination,
there is pallor and mildly icteric sclerae. She has a distended abdomen with
hepatosplenomegaly and mild frontal bossing. She was born on the 25th centile
and her weight has fallen below the 2nd centile. Blood tests show microcytic
hypochromic anemia. What is the likely diagnosis?
A. Sickle cell disease
B. Hereditary spherocytosis
C. Thalassaemia major
D. G6PD deficiency
E. Iron deficiency anemia
Features such as severe microcytic anemia,
hepatosplenomegaly, and failure to thrive in a child from a
high-risk ethnic group are classic for ß-thalassaemia major.
