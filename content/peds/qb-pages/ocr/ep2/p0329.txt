30. Melissa is a 3-year-old girl. She presents to her general practitioner with a 3—4-
week history of lethargy and weight loss. On examination, she is pale and has
widespread bruising. She has no other medical history and is not currently on any
medications. The general practitioner orders a full blood count, which reveals:
Hb: 66 g/L' WBC: 43.2 x 109/L' Platelet count: 50 x 109/L
Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis?
A. Acute lymphoblastic leukaemia
B. Acute myeloid leukaemia
C. Alpha-thalassaemia major
D. Beta-thalassaemia major
E. Iron deficiency anemia
A child with pallor, bruising, and pancytopenia (low Hb, high
WBCs, and low platelets) is highly suggestive of ALL, the most
common childhood cancer
