21. Which of the following statements about brain tumours in childhood is true?
A. They are usually supratentorial
B. Signs ofraised intracranial pressure are rare
C. Astrocytomas carry poor prognosis
D. Medulloblastomas are the most common type
E. Metastasis is common
Most childhood brain tumours are infratentorial, and diagnosis is often
delayed due to insidious onset and signs of raised intracranial pressure.
Astrocytomas are the most common type and generally have a poor
prognosis, while metastasis is rare in CNS tumours.
