32. A 5-month-old baby presents with tachypnea, fever, mild intercostal and
subcostal retractions, dry cough, and wheezing. A chest radiograph shows
hyperinflation and increased interstitial markings.
How would you manage this patient?
a. Oxygen supplementation and fluid support
b. Glucocorticoids
c. Epinephrine
d. Theophylline
e. Penicillin
This is likely bronchiolitis, for which supportive care with
oxygen and hydration is the primary treatment.
Bronchodilators and steroids have limited benefit.
