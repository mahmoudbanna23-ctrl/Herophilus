Types of CP
Spastic CP (upper motor neuron damage)
•Tone: increased, velocity-dependent (dynamic catch), "clasp-knife"
•Reflexes: brisk tendon reflexes, extensor plantar response
•Subtypes:
• Hemiplegia (unilateral): arm > leg, asymmetric movements, often mild (GMFCS I—
• Diplegia (bilateral): legs > arms, abnormal walking, preterm-related
periventricular leukomalacia (GMFCS I—III)
• Quadriplegia (bilateral): all limbs and trunk affected, severe, often with seizures,
intellectual impairment (GMFCS IV-V)
