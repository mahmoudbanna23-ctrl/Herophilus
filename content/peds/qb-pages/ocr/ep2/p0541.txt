33. Aya, a 3-year-old girl, presents with a history of eczema, rhinitis, chronic
nocturnal cough, and intermittent wheezing. Asthma is suspected, and a
bronchodilator is prescribed.
What is the most appropriate method to deliver the bronchodilator?
a. Dry powder inhaler
b. Metered dose inhaler (MDI)
c. Metered dose inhaler with spacer
d. Nebulizer
e. Inhaler with face mask
An MDI with spacer is the preferred method in young children
as it ensures better delivery Of medication to the lungs and is
easy to use.
