78. A 4-year-old girl is referred after her GP detects a heart murmur. On examination,
she has webbed neck, low posterior hairline, a shield-shaped chest, and cubitus valgus.
What is the most likely cause of the murmur?
a. Coarctation of the aorta
b. Mitral regurgitation
c. Patent ductus arteriosus
d. Pulmonary stenosis
The girl shows classic features Of Turner syndrome (webbed neck, low
hairline, shield chest, cubitus valgus). The most common congenital heart
defect in Turner syndrome is coarctation Of the aorta, which explains the
murmur.
