33. Which of the following is the most common cause of febrile convulsions in
children?
A. Bacterial meningitis
B. Human herpesvirus 6 (HHV-6)
C. Influenza
D. Pneumococcal sepsis
E. Otitis media
HHV-6 is the most common cause of febrile convulsions,
especially in children aged 6 months to 5 years.
