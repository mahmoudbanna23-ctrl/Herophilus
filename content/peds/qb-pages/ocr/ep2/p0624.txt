Infective Endocarditis (IE)
•Risk: CHD (esp. VSD, PDA, coarctation, prosthetic valves).
•Organism: most common Strep. viridans (a-hemolytic).
•Clinical signs: fever, anemia, clubbing, splinter hemorrhages, embolic phenomena,
changing murmurs.
•Diagnosis: multiple blood cultures, echocardiography (vegetations), 'T' acute-phase
reactants.
•Treatment: IV penicillin + aminoglycoside for 6 weeks. Prosthetic material may require
surgery.
•Prophylaxis: good dental hygiene, avoid tattoos/piercings. Antibiotic prophylaxis only in
high-risk (prosthetic valves/conduits).
