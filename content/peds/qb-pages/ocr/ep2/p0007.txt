3. Factors Influencing Development
•Nature: genetic potential.
•Nurture: environment, care, stimulation.
•Epigenetics: environment can modify gene expression across generations.
•Optimal growth needs secure, responsive, and loving care plus food, rest, security,
learning opportunities.
4. Developmental Domains
Gross motor : head control sitting crawling walking.
Vision & fine motor : reaching, grasping, transferring objects, drawing.
Hearing, speech & language : cooing words sentences.
Social, emotional & behavioral : smiling, play, feeding self, independence.
•Delay in one domain may affect others (e.g., hearing loss speech/social delay).
