66. Which clinical feature strongly supports the diagnosis of asthma?
A. Daytime cough
B. Spasmodic cough
C. Persistent moist cough
D. The presence of symptoms between coughs
Persistent symptoms, including between episodes Of cough,
strongly support asthma rather than transient coughs from viral
infections.
