Developmental Problems
•Can be anticipated antenatally or identified from birth onwards.
•Cerebral palsy: most common motor impairment.
•Autism spectrum disorder (ASD) and ADHD: usually detected in preschool or early
school age.
•Early detection of hearing or vision impairment is crucial.
•Children often require multidisciplinary support (medical, social, emotional,
educational).
Presentation of Developmental Problems
•Parental concerns: delayed development, behavior, or social skills.
•Routine health checks: surveillance or screening.
•Risk factors: family history, prematurity, dysmorphic features, abnormal neurology.
•Education professionals may detect delays.
•Opportunistic detection during other health visits.
