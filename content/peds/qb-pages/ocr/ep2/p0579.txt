51. A 16-year-old girl presents to the emergency department with a severe asthma
attack. What is the most important treatment to relieve her bronchoconstriction?
a. Propranolol
b. Salbutamol
c. Oxygen
d. Glucocorticosteroids
Salbutamol, a short-acting beta-2 agonist, is the first-line
bronchodilator for immediate relief in asthma exacerbations. It
provides rapid airway relaxation.
