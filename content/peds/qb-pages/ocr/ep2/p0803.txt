Enuresis
Definition
•Daytime enuresis: lack of bladder control during the day in children >3—5 years.
•Nocturnal enuresis: lack of bladder control at night.
•May be primary (never dry) or secondary (previously dry, then relapsed).
Causes
Daytime Enuresis
•Behavioral/developmental ignoring bladder sensation.
•Detrusor instability urgent urge from sudden bladder contractions.
•Bladder neck weakness.
•Neuropathic bladder incomplete emptying, associated with spina bifida.
•UTI (rare as sole cause).
•Constipation bladder outlet compression.
•Ectopic ureter constant dribbling, always wet.
Secondary (onset) Enuresis
•Emotional stress (most common).
•Polyuria diabetes mellitus, CKD, sickle cell, or diabetes insipidus.
