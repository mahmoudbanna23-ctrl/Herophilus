27. Causes of delayed speech include all of the following EXCEPT:
a. Emotional deprivation
b. Craniosynostosis
c. Autism
d. Hearing loss
e. Mental handicap
Craniosynostosis mainly causes abnormal head shape, not speech
delay. Common causes of delayed speech include autism, hearing loss,
emotional deprivation, and intellectual disability.
