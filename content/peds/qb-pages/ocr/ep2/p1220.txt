Clinical Features
•Depend on age (reporting symptoms) + tumor site.
•Raised ICP: headache, vomiting, papilledema (late).
•Focal signs vary:
• Supratentorial: seizures, hemiplegia, focal deficits.
• Midline/suprasellar: visual field loss, bitemporal hemianopia, pituitary failure
(growth failure, Dl, obesity).
• Cerebellar/4th ventricle: ataxia, coordination problems, abnormal eye
movements.
• Brainstem: cranial nerve palsy, pyramidal tract signs, ataxia.
•Spinal tumors: back pain, limb weakness, bladder/bowel dysfunction.
•Persistent back pain in children MRI.
Investigations
•MRI = best for characterization.
•Lumbar puncture for CSF spread (but avoid if ICP unless neurosurgeon clears
