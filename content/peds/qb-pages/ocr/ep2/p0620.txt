7. Rheumatic Fever & Related Acquired Heart Disease
Etiology & Epidemiology
•Autoimmune response to Group A Streptococcus (GAS).
•Affects 5—15 yrs; can progress to rheumatic heart disease (RHD).
•Incidence: N19/100,ooo (LMIC) vs (HIC).
•Risk factors: overcrowding, streptococcal virulence.
•Prevention: 10-day penicillin for GAS pharyngitis.
