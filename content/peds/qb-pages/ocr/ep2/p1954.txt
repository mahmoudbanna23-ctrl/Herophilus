Turner syndrome results from a chromosomal abnormality (monosomy X), not
a single-gene defect. It causes short stature, gonadal dysgenesis, and cardiac
defects.
• E. coli sepsis with cataracts in a neonate indicates galactosemia.
Accumulation of galactose leads to liver failure, sepsis, and lens opacity.
'Sitting without support and crawling appear around 9 months. Inability to
stand independently is normal at this stage but should develop by 12 months.
'During school age, the main developmental progress is in cognition and
abstract thinking. Motor and language milestones are largely established earlier.
'Sudden unilateral wheeze and decreased air entry after choking suggest
foreign body aspiration. The side depends on where the object lodges.
