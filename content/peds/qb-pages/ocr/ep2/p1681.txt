43. What is the underlying pathophysiologic mechanism of anemia in patients
with G6PD enzyme deficiency?
A. Bone marrow aplasia
B. Consumption coagulopathy
C. Food poisoning
D. Intravascular hemolysis
E. Extravascular hemolysis
G6PD deficiency makes red blood cells vulnerable to oxidative
stress, leading to hemolysis within blood vessels.
