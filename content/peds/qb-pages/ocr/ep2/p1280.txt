5. A 6-year-old boy attends the outpatient clinic with a diagnosis of Down
syndrome. He has been pale and lethargic for the last 4 weeks and has some
bruises on his shins. Which of the following conditions is it important to rule out
in view of his underlying diagnosis?
A. Parvovirus infection
B. ITP
C. Hemophilia A
D. Acute leukemia
E. Henoch-Schönlein purpura
Children with Down syndrome have a higher risk of leukemia.
Pallor, bruising, and lethargy are red flags that need urgent
evaluation.
