73. What would be the initial management of a newbom with an antenatal
diagnosis of a urinary tract anomaly?
A. Collect urine for culture immediately after birth
B. Cord blood sample for serum electrolytes
C. Start prophylactic antibiotics
D. Give an appointment after one month for ultrasonographic assessment
Newborns with antenatally detected urinary tract anomalies
may need prophylactic antibiotics to prevent infection until
further evaluation.
