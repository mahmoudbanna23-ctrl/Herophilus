24. An 8-year-old girl presents with a second generalized tonic-clonic seizure. It
required lorazepam to terminate, and a decision was made to start anti-epileptic
treatment. Which of the following is first-line therapy?
A. Phenobarbital
B. Sodium valproate
C. Vigabatrin
D. Ethosuximide
E. Phenytoin
Sodium valproate is first-line for generalized tonic-clonic
seizures in children. It's effective for multiple seizure types but
may have teratogenic risks in females Of childbearing age.
