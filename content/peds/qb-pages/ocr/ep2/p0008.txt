5. Developmental Milestones
•Median age: when 50% achieve a skill (guide to normal).
•Red flag age: when 97.5% should have achieved it (delay if not).
•Example: Walking
• by 12 months
• 90% by 15 months
• Red flag: 18 months
•Children with variants (e.g., bottom-shuffling) may walk later but still be normal.
6. Reflexes
•Primitive reflexes (rooting, grasp, Moro, ATNR) present at birth, fade by 3—5 months.
•Postural reflexes (parachute, propping) appear 4—12 months; essential for
sitting/walking.
•Persistence of primitive reflexes abnormal.
