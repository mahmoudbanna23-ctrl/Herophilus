47. A I -year-old girl has recently been diagnosed and treated for urinary tract
infection. Investigation shows that she has a vesicoureteral reflux. Which of the
following is the IDEAL antibiotic prophylaxis in this child?
a. Monthly 1M penicillin
b. Daily oral cephalexin
c. Daily oral erythromycin
e. Weekly IV gentamicin
For vesicoureteral reflux in children, low-dose daily oral
antibiotics (co-trimoxazole or nitrofurantoin) are used for
prophylaxis.
