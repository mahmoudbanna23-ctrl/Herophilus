15. Which one of the following is the most important cause of jaundice presenting
in the first 24 hours of life?
a. Prematurity
c. Breastfeeding
d. Physiological jaundice
Jaundice within the first 24 hours Of life is always pathological.
Hemolysis due to blood group incompatibility (e.g., ABO or Rh)
is the most common serious cause. Physiological jaundice
typically appears after 24 hours.
