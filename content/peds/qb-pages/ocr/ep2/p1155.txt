Hepatitis A (HAV)
•RNA virus, fecal—oral transmission.
•Incidence with improved socioeconomic conditions.
•Clinical:
• Often asymptomatic.
• Most children: mild illness, recovery in 2—4 weeks.
• Rare: prolonged cholestatic hepatitis (self-limiting), fulminant hepatitis.
• No chronic disease.
•Diagnosis: IgM anti-HAU
•Treatment: supportive only.
•Prevention:
• Vaccination (routine for travelers, chronic liver disease).
• Close contacts: vaccine within 2 weeks.
• HNIG in high-risk.
