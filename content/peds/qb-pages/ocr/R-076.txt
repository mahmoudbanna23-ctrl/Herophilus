17.Treatment  of  duodenalatresia:
→     The best treatment option depends on the type of duodenal atresia as follows:
l.In type 2 duodenal atresia,and with annular pancreas:Diamond-shaped duodeno- duodenostomy.
Il.In type 1 duodenal atresia:Duodenotomy with excision of the web.
Ⅲ.In type 3 duodenal atresia:Side-to-side duodeno-jejunostomy(esp.in wide gap DA or if the the atresia is in the 3rd or 4th parts of duodenum).


18.Types of duodenal atresia:
→     Typel(92%):an obstructing septum formed of mucosa and submucosa.
Typell(1%):a short fibrous cord connects 2 blind ends of duodenum.
TypeⅢ(7%):no connection between the 2 blind ends of duodenum.


19.Types  ofjejuno-ileal  atresia:
→    1.Type   l:atransluminal  septum(no   mesenteric  defect).
2.TypeII:the 2 blind-ending atretic ends are connected bya fibrous cord along the edge of mesentry(no mesenteric defect).
3.Type  Illa:completelyseparated  2blind-ending  atretic  ends  without  a fibrous  cord. (there's a V-shaped mesenteric defect)
4.TypeIIb:Apple  peelor  Christmas  tree.
5.Type IV:Multiple segmental atresias like a string of sausages.
