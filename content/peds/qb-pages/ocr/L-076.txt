5.CHARGE syndrome associated with esophageal atresia:
  Coloboma,Heart  disease,Atresia  choana,Retarded  growth,Genital  hypoplasia  &Ear (deafness).


6.Treatment of esophageal atresia:
→ ▶Division of the fistula,with its closure on the tracheal side followed byend-to-end
anastomosis between the esophageal segments.The standard approach is through right-sided extrapleural  latero-dorsal thoracotomy through the fourth  interspace.    →        In case of
long-gap atresia(as in isolated esophageal atresia),the primary procedure is placement of a gastrostomy tube to allow early enteral feeding.Then,one of those is done:
1.preservation of the patient's own esophagus with delayed primary anastomosis at 2-3 months of life(using Foker's external traction technique).
2.esophageal replacement when the baby is 8-10 kg or 1 year-old using either gastric tube,colon  interposition,jejunal  interposition  or  gastric  pull-up.


7.Most common type of CDH:    Bochdalek  hernia.


8.CDH time of presentation:
→ ▶ At   birth.

9.Important investigation in CDH:
   Echocardiography(for  pulmonary  hypertension).


10.Contraindicated in CDH:
   Oxygen  mask(intstead,intubation  is  performed)


11.Incidence of IHPS:
→      2-3 per  1000 live births.Boys are affected four times more than girls.It is most commonly seen in infants aged 3-6weeks.


12.Treatment of IHPS:
   Ramstedt's  pyloromyotomy.


13.Metabolic abnormality of IHPS:
→    Hypokalemic hypochloremic metabolic alkalosis.
