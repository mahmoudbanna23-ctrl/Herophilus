     5.4.1
     D. Intravenous
     In acutely ill neonates and infants, drugs are
     given intravenously to ensure reliable and
     adequate blood and tissue concentrations. Fever
     without a source in this age group is an
     indication for a septic screen and starting
     intravenous antibiotics. With oral formulations,
     intake cannot be guaranteed and absorption is
     unpredictable as it is affected by gastric
     emptying and acidity, gut motility and the
     effects of milk in the stomach.
