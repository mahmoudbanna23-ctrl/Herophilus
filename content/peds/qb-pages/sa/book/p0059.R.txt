Later medical problems
•       Delayed motor milestones
•       Learning difficulties – severity is variable,
        usually mild to moderate but may be severe
•       Short stature
•       Increased susceptibility to infections

Box 9.3 Clinical findings in males with
fragile X syndrome
    •    Moderate–severe learning difficulty (IQ
         20–80, mean 50)
    •    Macrocephaly
    •    Macro-orchidism – postpubertal
    •    Characteristic facies – long face, large everted
         ears, prominent mandible, and broad
         forehead, most evident in affected adults
    •    Other features – mitral valve prolapse, joint
         laxity, scoliosis, autism, hyperactivity.
