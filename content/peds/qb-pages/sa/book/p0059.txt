9.15.2
M. Turner syndrome
Features include delayed puberty, primary
amenorrhoea, short stature, coarctation of the
aorta and lymphoedema as a neonate. Noonan
syndrome shares many of these features.
9.15.3
K. Patau syndrome (trisomy 13)
Cyril has a cleft lip and palate, a scalp defect,
polydactyly, and a heart defect. These are all
features of Patau syndrome.
