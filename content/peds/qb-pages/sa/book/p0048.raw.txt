8.5.3
Aisha, a 6-year-old girl, is brought by her mother
to the paediatric emergency clinic because of
bruises. The family say that she had a viral upper
respiratory tract infection (URTI) the previous
week for which she saw her general practitioner.
The URTI had completely resolved. She was well
when she went to spend a long weekend with
relatives but returned with multiple bruises. On
examination she is well in herself and is afebrile.
There is no hepatosplenomegaly. The appearance
of her legs is shown in Fig. 8.5, and there is a bruise
around her eye and a few scattered petechiae.
