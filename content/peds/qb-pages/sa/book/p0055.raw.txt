Bruce is a 12-year-old boy who is referred by the
ophthalmology team. He presented with blurred
vision. Ophthalmological examination revealed a
dislocated lens. He is very disappointed, as he
had been doing very well in his basketball team.

He is also doing well academically at school. On
examination you note he is very tall. He has a
high arched palate, stretch marks on his skin and
lax joints. His height is well above the 99th
centile and he has long arms, legs, and fingers.
His mother is also tall (178 cm) but reports that
she is healthy.
