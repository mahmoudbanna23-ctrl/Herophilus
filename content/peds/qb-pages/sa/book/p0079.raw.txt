12.6.1
H. Karyotype
Turner syndrome needs to be excluded as she
has always been short. The elevated blood
pressure may be because of an undetected
coarctation of the aorta which is associated with
the syndrome.

12.6.2
K. Thyroid stimulating hormone (TSH)
Her height has stopped increasing and she has
put on weight. This suggests an endocrine
problem. Her clinical symptoms suggest
hypothyroidism.
12.6.3
B. Coeliac screen
Children with Down syndrome are at increased
risk. He is also at increased risk of
hypothyroidism, but his decreased appetite,
irritability and weight loss are characteristic of
coeliac disease.
