24.2
Zoe is a 14-year-old girl who has always been
quiet and hard working. She is very thin and is
losing weight despite being very interested in
food; she enjoys baking cakes for her classmates
and is always keen to go to the supermarket
with her mother. Zoe herself denies that there
is a problem with her weight or that she has
been vomiting. Her parents are both extremely
worried about her weight loss and poor appetite;
they have not noticed any vomiting. There are
no other symptoms and her examination is
normal except that her height is on the 30th
centile but her weight is below the 0.4th centile.
What is the most likely diagnosis?
Select one answer only.
A.   Anorexia nervosa
B.   Bulimia
C.   Chronic fatigue syndrome
D.   Coeliac disease
E.   Depression
