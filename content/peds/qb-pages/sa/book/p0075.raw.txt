C. Coarctation of the aorta
The absence of femoral pulses should always
suggest that there is coarctation of the aorta. Her
left brachial pulse will also be difficult to palpate.
Heart failure develops when the ductus

arteriosus closes as this is a classic ‘ductdependent’ lesion.
11.9.2
L. Transient tachypnoea of the newborn
The most common cause of respiratory distress
in a term infant. It is due to a delay in the
resorption of lung liquid – an increased risk
following caesarean section, as the babies have
not had to undergo the same physical and
physiological stressors as those who pass
through the birth canal.
