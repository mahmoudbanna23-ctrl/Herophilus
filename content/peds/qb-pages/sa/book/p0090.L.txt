                   J. Migraine
                   Migraine classically is associated with headache
                   with photophobia and vomiting. It may be
                   accompanied by abdominal pain. Meningitis is a
                   differential diagnosis that should be considered
                   but she is afebrile and does not have any neck
                   stiffness. Cyclical vomiting, by definition, occurs
                   recurrently.
                   14.10.1
                   K. Mesenteric adenitis
                   The mesenteric nodes in the abdomen become
                   inflamed following an upper respiratory tract
                   infection. Can be difficult to differentiate from
                   appendicitis and requires frequent clinical
                   re-assessment to check it resolves. The condition
                   is frequently only diagnosed at laparotomy when
                   a non-inflamed appendix is identified (and
                   removed).
