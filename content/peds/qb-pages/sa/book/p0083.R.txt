J. Vitamin K deficiency, causing

There is insufficient vitamin K in breast milk to
reliably prevent the disorder. Most babies are
given prophylactic vitamin K at birth, usually
intramuscularly or else orally, but this requires
parental consent. Formula feeds are
supplemented with vitamin K.
