Answer 25.11.3
I. Molluscum contagiosum
This is caused by a poxvirus. The lesions are
small, skin-coloured, pearly papules with central
umbilication. They may be single but are usually
multiple. Lesions are often widespread but
tend to disappear spontaneously within
a year.

Answer 25.11.4
L. Scabies
Always consider scabies if a rash is very itchy.
It is a highly contagious (and unpleasant)
condition.
Answer 25.11.5
B. Atopic eczema
Atopic eczema affects 20% of children.
Lichenification and erythema are shown. The
skin looks sore and the excoriations suggest itch.
The face often remains affected from undue
reluctance to use topical steroids on the face of a
child of this age.
