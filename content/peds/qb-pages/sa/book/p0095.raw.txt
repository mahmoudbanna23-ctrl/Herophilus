15.11.3
Mustafa, an 18-month-old boy, presents with 3
days of vomiting and diarrhoea. The vomiting
has now settled but he continues to have loose
stools (he had about eight loose stools in the last
24 hours). His mucous membranes are dry, he
has sunken eyes and appears lethargic. His skin
turgor is reduced. His extremities are warm and
capillary refill time is normal.
15.11.4
Josie is 3 years old. She has had a cough and
fever for 6 days. On examination her oxygen

saturation is 92% in air. Her temperature is 39° C
and she has a respiratory rate of 50 breaths/
minute. She has marked indrawing between her
ribs and is using her accessory muscles of
respiration. On percussion there is ‘stony
dullness’ at her left base and on auscultation
decreased air entry at the left base.

cervical lymphadenopathy. He also has a
