28.8
A. Positional talipes equinovarus
Correct. This is a common problem and is caused
by intrauterine compression. The foot is of normal
size, the deformity is mild and can be corrected
to the neutral position with passive manipulation.
B. Talipes calcaneovalgus
Talipes calcaneovalgus is a fixed bony deformity,
in which the foot is everted and dorsiflexed.
C. Talipes equinovarus
Talipes equinovarus is a fixed bony deformity, in
which the foot is inverted and supinated. Passive
movement to a normal, neutral position is not
possible. See Figs. 28.5 and 28.6 in Illustrated
Textbook of Paediatrics for more details.
D. Tarsal coalition
Tarsal coalition is a diagnosis made in adolescent
years.
E. Vertical talus
Vertical talus is in children with rocker bottom feet
who often have other congenital abnormalities.
