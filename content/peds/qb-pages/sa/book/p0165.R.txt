28.3
Zain is a 14-year-old boy who presents to the
Emergency Department. He is a good footballer,
and after training today he complained of pain
in his left knee. He has had the pain for a while
and it gets worse when he exercises. On
examination there is swelling over the left tibial
tuberosity. He is afebrile. There is no night pain
or pain on waking.
What is the most likely diagnosis?
Select one answer only.
A.   Osteomyelitis
B.   Osgood–Schlatter disease
C.   Perthes disease
D.   Septic arthritis
E.   Slipped capital femoral epiphysis
