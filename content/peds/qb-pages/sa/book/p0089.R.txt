14.9.2
D. Diabetic ketoacidosis
Diabetic ketoacidosis classically presents
with a history of weight loss, polydipsia
and polyuria. The fast breathing is due to
the metabolic acidosis. This can sometimes
be mistaken as being a respiratory infection
but she is afebrile. A urine dipstick and blood
glucose and blood gas will confirm the
diagnosis.

14.9.3
K. Pyloric stenosis
This is a typical history. Pyloric stenosis occurs at
2–7 weeks of age; the vomiting is milky and
projectile and occurs after each feed. A mass can
be felt in the abdomen during or after a test
feed. A blood gas will usually show a
hypochloremic metabolic alkalosis because
chloride and acid are lost from the vomitus. An
ultrasound scan of the pylorus is usually
diagnostic and is used in many centres to
confirm the diagnosis made on clinical
