 Hamim (as outlined in Question 7.3) is admitted
 to hospital for a period of observation.
 Eight hours after admission, the nurses note a
 change in his level of consciousness. He is now
 responsive only to painful stimuli; his left pupil is
 dilated although still responsive to light. His
 airway, breathing and circulation are satisfactory.
 A CT scan (Fig. 7.2) shows that there is a
 haemorrhage and a skull fracture. He is stabilized
 in the resuscitation room.
