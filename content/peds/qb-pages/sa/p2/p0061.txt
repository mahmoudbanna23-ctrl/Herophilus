9.1 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

How much does an average baby cry at 6 weeks of age?

 A. About 15 minutes a day
 B. About 2 hours a day, falling to about 1 hour a day by 10 weeks
 C. About 6 hours a day
D. Only when hungry
 E. Crying at this age is always abnormal

Answer: B.

Crying peaks at around 6 weeks and reduces significantly by 3–4 months. Knowing the normal range is
what makes reassurance credible.




9.2 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which medical cause of excessive crying must never be missed?

 A. Teething
 B. Unrecognised trauma such as an occult fracture
 C. Wind
D. Growth spurt
 E. Overstimulation

Answer: B.

Medical causes are a minority but include urinary or ear infection, meningitis, obstructed bowel,
strangulated hernia, testicular torsion, reflux, cow's milk protein allergy and unrecognised trauma. A
full examination and a documented weight are mandatory.
