9.5 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which best distinguishes a night terror from a nightmare?

 A. Night terrors occur in the early hours of the morning
 B. In a night terror the child has eyes open, appears awake but is unresponsive, and has NO
    recollection in the morning
 C. Nightmares cannot be recalled
D. Night terrors respond to sedatives
 E. Nightmares only occur after trauma

Answer: B.

Night terrors occur about 1.5 hours after settling and are a parasomnia — a disorder of arousal.
Sleepwalking shares the same origin, and the priority there is making the environment safe. A regular
sleep routine reduces both.

A 6-year-old soils his pants with loose stool several times a day and has a palpable abdominal faecal
mass. What is the management?

 A. Antidiarrhoeal medication
 B. Disimpaction with an escalating laxative regimen, then maintenance laxatives with toilet
    retraining
 C. Psychological therapy alone
D. Reassurance — this is normal at 6 years
 E. Immediate spinal MRI

Answer: B.

Overflow soiling: liquid stool passes around a faecal mass and the child is unaware of the need to empty.
Soiling after 4 years is always abnormal. Retraining takes weeks, with a regular laxative throughout,
while the distended rectum returns to normal size.
