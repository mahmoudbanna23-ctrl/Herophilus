Which primitive reflex, if it persists beyond the expected age, most suggests an underlying
neurological abnormality?

 A. Moro reflex persisting beyond 6 months
 B. Rooting reflex at 2 months
 C. Palmar grasp at 3 months
D. Stepping reflex at 4 weeks
 E. Asymmetrical tonic neck reflex at 3 months

Answer: A.

Primitive reflexes normally disappear in the first few months as cortical control develops. Persistence
beyond the expected age — for example a Moro after about 6 months — suggests a central motor
disorder such as cerebral palsy.
