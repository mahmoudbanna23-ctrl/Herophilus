2.9 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which is the most appropriate first-line treatment for focal spasticity limiting function in a single
muscle group?

 A. Oral diazepam long term
 B. Botulinum toxin injection with physiotherapy
 C. Immediate tendon lengthening surgery
D. Bed rest
 E. Intrathecal baclofen for all children

Answer: B.

Focal spasticity → botulinum toxin plus physiotherapy and orthoses. Generalised spasticity may need
oral baclofen or intrathecal baclofen; surgery is reserved for fixed contractures and deformity.




2.10 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

A 3-year-old has lost the ability to walk and to speak in sentences over 6 months. What does this
pattern indicate?

 A. Cerebral palsy
 B. Global developmental delay
 C. REGRESSION — investigate for a neurodegenerative or metabolic disorder
D. Normal variation
 E. Isolated language disorder

Answer: C.

Loss of previously acquired skills is never cerebral palsy — the lesion in cerebral palsy is non-
progressive. Regression demands investigation for neurodegenerative, metabolic or epileptic
encephalopathy causes.
