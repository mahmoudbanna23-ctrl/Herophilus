2.3 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

A 10-month-old ex-preterm infant has stiff, scissored legs with brisk reflexes and up-going plantars,
and normal arms. Which type of cerebral palsy is this?

 A. Spastic hemiplegia
 B. Spastic DIPLEGIA
 C. Dyskinetic cerebral palsy
D. Ataxic cerebral palsy
 E. Spastic quadriplegia

Answer: B.

Diplegia = legs much more affected than arms, classically after preterm birth with periventricular
injury, because the leg fibres run closest to the ventricles.




2.4 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which form of cerebral palsy is most associated with severe learning difficulties, seizures and feeding
problems?

 A. Spastic hemiplegia
 B. Spastic diplegia
 C. Spastic QUADRIPLEGIA
D. Mild ataxia
 E. Isolated dyskinesia

Answer: C.

Quadriplegia is the most severe pattern — all four limbs, often with seizures, microcephaly, severe
learning difficulty and swallowing problems with aspiration.
