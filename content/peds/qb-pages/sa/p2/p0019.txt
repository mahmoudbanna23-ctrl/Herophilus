3.11 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

A newborn has a single palmar crease, hypotonia, upslanting palpebral fissures and a flat occiput.
Which investigations should be arranged?

 A. Karyotype (or rapid molecular test) and echocardiography
 B. Karyotype only
 C. MRI brain and EEG
D. Sweat test and chest X-ray
 E. No investigations — the diagnosis is clinical

Answer: A.

Down syndrome is suspected clinically but must be confirmed cytogenetically — which also identifies
translocation cases needing parental testing. Echocardiography is essential because heart disease may be
present without a murmur.




3.12 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which statement about genetic counselling is correct?

 A. It should direct parents to a particular reproductive decision
 B. It provides accurate risk information non-directively so the family can make its own decision
 C. It is only offered after a second affected child
D. Carrier testing of children for adult-onset conditions is routine
 E. A three-generation family tree is rarely useful

Answer: B.

Counselling is non-directive, based on an accurate diagnosis and a detailed three-generation pedigree.
Predictive testing of children for adult-onset conditions is generally deferred so they can decide for
themselves.
