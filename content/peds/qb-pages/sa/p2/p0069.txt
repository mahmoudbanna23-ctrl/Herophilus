10.10 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

From what age are annual blood pressure, microalbuminuria and retinal screening recommended in
childhood diabetes?

 A. At diagnosis
 B. From 12 years of age
 C. From 16 years of age
D. Only in type 2 diabetes
 E. Only if the HbA1c is above target

Answer: B.

Coeliac and thyroid screening are done at diagnosis, with thyroid repeated annually. Growth, weight
and BMI are plotted at every visit, and injection sites are checked for lipohypertrophy.




10.11 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

What defines hypoglycaemia in a child WITHOUT diabetes?

 A. Below 4 mmol/L
 B. Below 2.6 mmol/L
 C. Below 5 mmol/L
D. Below 1.0 mmol/L
 E. Any symptomatic reading

Answer: B.

Infants are at particular risk because of high energy requirements, poor gluconeogenic and glycogen
reserves and rapid brain growth — which is why starvation beyond 4 hours (e.g. preoperatively)
requires an IV glucose-containing infusion.
