8.3 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

A 13-year-old with migraine now has headache on 18 days a month and uses ibuprofen or a triptan four
days a week. What is the diagnosis?

 A. Chronic tension-type headache
 B. Medication overuse headache
 C. Idiopathic intracranial hypertension
D. Cluster headache
 E. Brain tumour

Answer: B.

Rebound headache follows use of acute analgesia or triptans on more than 2 days a week; withdrawal of
the drug resolves it in about 2 weeks.




8.4 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which prophylactic drug for migraine is contraindicated in asthma?

 A. Topiramate
 B. Propranolol
 C. Pizotifen
D. Amitriptyline
 E. Valproate

Answer: B.

Beta blockade can precipitate bronchospasm. Alternatives include topiramate or valproate, pizotifen or
amitriptyline, with attention to sleep, food, fluids and lifestyle.
