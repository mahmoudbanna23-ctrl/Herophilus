1.1 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which of the following is a LIMIT AGE rather than a median age?

 A. Smiling at 6 weeks
 B. Sitting without support by 9 months
 C. Walking at 12 months
D. First words at 12 months
 E. Pincer grip at 10 months

Answer: B.

A median age is when half of children have acquired the skill; a limit age is the age by which it should
have been acquired — usually two standard deviations from the mean. Failure to reach a limit age is
what triggers assessment.




1.2 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

A child born at 28 weeks' gestation is seen at 9 months of chronological age. How should his
development be assessed?

 A. At his chronological age of 9 months
 B. Corrected for prematurity, i.e. at 6 months, until about 2 years of age
 C. Corrected for prematurity indefinitely
D. No correction is ever applied
 E. Corrected only for gross motor skills

Answer: B.

Development is corrected for gestation until about 2 years of age. Failing to correct labels a normal
preterm infant as delayed.
