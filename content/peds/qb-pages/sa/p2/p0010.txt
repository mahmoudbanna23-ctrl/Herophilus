2.5 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Dyskinetic cerebral palsy with chorea and athetosis classically follows damage to which structure?

 A. Cerebellum
 B. Basal ganglia
 C. Anterior horn cells
D. Peripheral nerves
 E. Posterior columns

Answer: B.

Basal ganglia (extrapyramidal) damage — historically from kernicterus, now more often from hypoxic–
ischaemic injury. Intellect is often relatively preserved, which matters enormously for communication
aids.




2.6 · CLINICAL SCENARIO

A 14-month-old is not yet sitting, has definite right hand preference and a fisted left hand. What is the
single most appropriate action?

 A. Reassure and review in 6 months
 B. Refer for developmental assessment — early hand preference suggests a hemiplegia
 C. Arrange a hearing test only
D. Start physiotherapy without assessment
 E. Repeat the newborn examination

Answer: B.

Hand preference before 12 months is abnormal. With failure to sit by the limit age, this needs full
assessment for spastic hemiplegia.
