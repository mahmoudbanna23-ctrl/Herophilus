3.9 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

A boy has learning difficulties, a long face with large ears, macro-orchidism after puberty and a family
history of affected male relatives on the mother's side. What is the most likely diagnosis?

 A. Down syndrome
 B. Fragile X syndrome
 C. Klinefelter syndrome
D. Prader–Willi syndrome
 E. Williams syndrome

Answer: B.

Fragile X is the commonest inherited cause of learning disability, caused by a trinucleotide (CGG)
repeat expansion in FMR1. It shows anticipation, and carrier females may be mildly affected.




3.10 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which condition is an example of a multifactorial (polygenic) trait rather than single-gene inheritance?

 A. Cystic fibrosis
 B. Achondroplasia
 C. Neural tube defects
D. Haemophilia A
 E. Duchenne muscular dystrophy

Answer: C.

Multifactorial traits — neural tube defects, cleft lip and palate, congenital heart disease, pyloric
stenosis, developmental dysplasia of the hip — result from genetic susceptibility plus environmental
factors, and recurrence risks are given empirically, not by Mendelian ratios.
