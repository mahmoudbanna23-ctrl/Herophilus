11.11 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which fat-soluble vitamin deficiency in chronic cholestasis causes bleeding, including intracranial
haemorrhage?

 A. Vitamin A
 B. Vitamin D
 C. Vitamin E
D. Vitamin K
 E. Vitamin C

Answer: D.

The others: A — night blindness; D — rickets and fractures; E — peripheral neuropathy, haemolysis
and ataxia. Cholestatic infants need a medium-chain triglyceride formula, keeping about 40% of fat as
long-chain to prevent essential fatty acid deficiency.




11.12 · CLINICAL SCENARIO

A 14-year-old with abnormal liver function has deteriorating school performance, mood change, a
tremor and dysarthria. Which diagnosis must be excluded?

 A. Wilson disease
 B. Hepatitis A
 C. Gilbert syndrome
D. Biliary atresia
 E. Alagille syndrome

Answer: A.

A rare but TREATABLE cause of liver failure. Neuropsychiatric features appear from the second decade,
with extrapyramidal signs and Kayser–Fleischer rings from corneal copper. Remember too that in
cirrhosis the markers of function are the albumin and prothrombin time, not the transaminases.
