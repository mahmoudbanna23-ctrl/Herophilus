3.5 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

A condition shows male-to-male transmission and affects both sexes equally in every generation. What
is the inheritance pattern?

 A. Autosomal recessive
 B. Autosomal dominant
 C. X-linked recessive
D. X-linked dominant
 E. Mitochondrial

Answer: B.

Father-to-son transmission excludes any X-linked pattern. Autosomal dominant conditions affect both
sexes, appear in every generation, and carry a 50% risk to each offspring.




3.6 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Two unaffected parents have a child with cystic fibrosis. What is the risk that their next child is
affected?

 A. 1 in 2
 B. 1 in 3
 C. 1 in 4
D. 2 in 3
 E. Negligible

Answer: C.

Autosomal recessive: both parents are carriers, so each pregnancy carries a 1 in 4 risk of being affected
and a 2 in 3 chance that an UNAFFECTED sibling is a carrier — a favourite second half to this question.
