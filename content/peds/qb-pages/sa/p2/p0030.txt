5.8 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which is an indication for admission in bronchiolitis?

 A. Any wheeze
 B. Apnoea, saturation below 92%, intake under 70% of normal, or respiratory rate over 70
 C. Cough lasting more than 3 days
D. Temperature 38 °C
 E. Parental anxiety alone

Answer: B.

Management is supportive — humidified oxygen, fluids, monitoring. Bronchodilators, steroids,
antibiotics, mist and hypertonic saline are of no proven benefit.




5.9 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

In a child over 5 years with pneumonia, which organism should be considered in addition to
Streptococcus pneumoniae?

 A. Mycoplasma pneumoniae
 B. Group B streptococcus
 C. Listeria
D. Pseudomonas
 E. RSV

Answer: A.

Over 5 years, think Mycoplasma — a longer prodrome, headache, and sometimes extrapulmonary
features. A macrolide is used. Respiratory rate is the most sensitive sign of pneumonia, and oral
antibiotics are as effective as IV in mild disease.
