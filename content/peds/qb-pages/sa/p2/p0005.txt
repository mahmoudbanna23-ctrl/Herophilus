1.6 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which screening test is used for hearing in the newborn period?

 A. Distraction test
 B. Otoacoustic emissions, with automated auditory brainstem response if abnormal
 C. Pure tone audiometry
D. Speech discrimination testing
 E. Tympanometry

Answer: B.

Universal newborn hearing screening uses otoacoustic emissions, with automated auditory brainstem
response for those who do not pass. Pure tone audiometry needs a cooperative child (about 4 years and
over).




1.7 · CLINICAL SCENARIO

Parents are worried that their 20-month-old says only a few single words. Which additional feature
would most raise concern about a hearing impairment rather than isolated language delay?

 A. He has an older sibling who talks for him
 B. He does not turn to his name and had recurrent otitis media with effusion
 C. He points to what he wants
D. He plays imaginatively
 E. He is bilingual at home

Answer: B.

Every child with delayed speech must have a hearing test. Not turning to sound plus a history of otitis
media with effusion (glue ear) points to a conductive loss. Bilingualism does not cause delay, and
pointing and imaginative play are reassuring for social communication.
