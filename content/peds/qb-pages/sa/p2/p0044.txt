7.1 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which statement about plasma creatinine in children is correct?

 A. The normal range is the same at all ages
 B. The normal range RISES through childhood with increasing muscle mass and height
 C. It rises before urea in acute kidney injury
 D. It is unaffected by muscle mass
 E. It is the earliest marker of chronic kidney disease

Answer: B.

Urea rises before creatinine but is less specific. Urinary protein loss occurs before the GFR falls in
chronic kidney disease. eGFR = 31 × height (cm) ÷ creatinine.




7.2 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

What is the GFR of a term newborn, corrected for surface area?

 A. 80–120 mL/min per 1.73 m²
 B. 20–30 mL/min per 1.73 m², rising to adult values by 1–2 years
 C. Identical to the adult value at birth
 D. Zero for the first week
 E. 50% of adult values at 28 weeks' gestation

Answer: B.

At 28 weeks' gestation the GFR is only 10% of the term infant's, which is why renally excreted drugs
need such care in the preterm.
