1.8 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

An absent red reflex is detected in a 6-week-old. What is the most important diagnosis to exclude?

 A. Refractive error
 B. Congenital cataract and retinoblastoma
 C. Conjunctivitis
D. Blocked nasolacrimal duct
 E. Strabismus

Answer: B.

An absent or white red reflex (leukocoria) requires same-day ophthalmology referral — congenital
cataract needs early surgery for vision to develop, and retinoblastoma is life-threatening.




1.9 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

A squint is noted in a 3-year-old. Which statement is correct?

 A. All squints resolve spontaneously by school age
 B. Untreated squint may cause amblyopia, so referral is required
 C. Squint after 6 weeks of age is normal
D. Squint never indicates intracranial pathology
 E. Patching the squinting eye is the treatment

Answer: B.

A persistent squint causes amblyopia because the brain suppresses one image. Treatment is patching the
GOOD eye to force use of the weaker one, with correction of refractive error. A new squint may also
signal raised intracranial pressure or retinoblastoma.
