8.12 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

A toddler bumps his head, goes very PALE, collapses and has a brief convulsion, then sleeps. What is
the diagnosis?

 A. Breath-holding spell
 B. Reflex asystolic syncope (reflex anoxic seizure)
 C. Epilepsy
D. Long QT syndrome
 E. Febrile seizure

Answer: B.

Triggered by sudden pain, cold food, fright or fever; caused by cardiac asystole from excessive vagal
inhibition. Contrast breath-holding spells: an angry toddler who cries first and turns BLUE.




8.13 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which set of signs indicates a CEREBELLAR rather than pyramidal disorder?

 A. Brisk reflexes and extensor plantars
 B. Past-pointing, dysdiadochokinesis, wide-based gait, nystagmus and scanning dysarthria
 C. Chorea and athetosis
D. Fasciculation and absent reflexes
 E. Waddling gait with Gowers' sign

Answer: B.

Causes in childhood include acute intoxication, post-infectious cerebellitis, medulloblastoma,
Friedreich ataxia and ataxia telangiectasia. Chorea and athetosis point to the basal ganglia (post-
streptococcal chorea, Wilson disease).
