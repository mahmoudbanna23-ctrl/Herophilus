11.5 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which features characterise Alagille syndrome?

 A. Autosomal recessive with bile salt transport defects
 B. Autosomal dominant with triangular facies, butterfly vertebrae and peripheral pulmonary
    stenosis
 C. X-linked with deafness
 D. Trisomy with duodenal atresia
 E. Acquired after parenteral nutrition

Answer: B.

Most survive into adult life, and mortality is most often secondary to the cardiac disease. Option A
describes progressive familial intrahepatic cholestasis, treated with nutrition, fat-soluble vitamins and
ileal bile acid transporter inhibitors for pruritus.




11.6 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which protein phenotype of α1-antitrypsin deficiency is associated with liver disease?

 A. PiMM
 B. PiZZ
 C. PiMS
 D. PiSS only
 E. No phenotype predicts liver disease

Answer: B.

Autosomal recessive, 1 in 2000–4000 in the UK, gene on chromosome 14. Abnormal folding causes
accumulation WITHIN hepatocytes (liver disease), while lack of circulating enzyme causes emphysema
in adults — so smoking, active and passive, must be avoided. About 50% of children do well.
