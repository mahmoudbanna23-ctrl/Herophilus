13.23 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which statement about hepatitis B serology is correct?

 A. Anti-HBs alone indicates natural infection
 B. Anti-HBc indicates true infection and is never produced by vaccination
 C. HBeAg indicates low infectivity
D. HBsAg indicates immunity
 E. IgG anti-HBc indicates acute infection

Answer: B.

s = sick (HBsAg = infected now; anti-HBs = immune). e = easily spread. c = core, meaning real infection —
IgM acute, IgG past or chronic.




13.24 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

A 9-year-old has had headaches for 8 weeks that are worse on lying down and on coughing, with
vomiting on waking, a new convergent squint and an unsteady gait. Fundoscopy is normal. What is the
correct next step?

 A. Reassure — normal fundi exclude raised intracranial pressure
 B. Urgent neuroimaging
 C. Lumbar puncture
D. Start migraine prophylaxis
 E. Refer for refraction

Answer: B.

Every red flag is present, and papilloedema is a LATE sign whose absence excludes nothing. A lumbar
puncture before imaging is dangerous when pressure may be raised.
