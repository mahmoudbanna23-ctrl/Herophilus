6.18 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which statement about infective endocarditis in children is correct?

 A. A normal echocardiogram excludes the diagnosis
 B. Multiple blood cultures must be taken before antibiotics; treatment is 6 weeks of IV penicillin
    plus an aminoglycoside
 C. Routine antibiotic prophylaxis before dental treatment is recommended for all cardiac lesions
D. Staphylococcus epidermidis is the commonest organism
 E. Peripheral stigmata are reliable for diagnosis

Answer: B.

The commonest organism is α-haemolytic streptococcus (Strep. viridans). Echocardiography can confirm
but never exclude, the classical stigmata are unreliable, and prevention is good dental hygiene — routine
antibiotic prophylaxis is no longer recommended for most conditions.
