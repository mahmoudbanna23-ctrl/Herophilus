3.3 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which is the most common serious congenital abnormality associated with Down syndrome?

 A. Congenital heart disease, especially atrioventricular septal defect
 B. Renal agenesis
 C. Cleft lip
D. Neural tube defect
 E. Congenital diaphragmatic hernia

Answer: A.

Around 40% have congenital heart disease, classically an atrioventricular septal defect — which is why
echocardiography is part of the initial work-up. Duodenal atresia, Hirschsprung disease,
hypothyroidism, atlantoaxial instability, leukaemia and hearing/vision problems are the other
associations.




3.4 · CLINICAL SCENARIO

A 14-year-old girl has short stature, no breast development, a webbed neck and widely spaced nipples.
Blood pressure is 145/90 in the right arm. What is the most likely diagnosis and the key associated
lesion to exclude?

 A. Down syndrome — atrioventricular septal defect
 B. Turner syndrome — coarctation of the aorta and bicuspid aortic valve
 C. Noonan syndrome — pulmonary stenosis
D. Marfan syndrome — aortic root dilatation
 E. Klinefelter syndrome — no cardiac lesion

Answer: B.

Turner syndrome (45,X0): short stature, delayed puberty from ovarian dysgenesis, webbed neck, wide
carrying angle, coarctation and bicuspid aortic valve, and renal anomalies. Treatment includes growth
hormone and oestrogen replacement.
