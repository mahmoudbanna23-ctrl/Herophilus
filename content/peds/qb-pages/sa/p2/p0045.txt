7.3 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which investigation is used to detect renal SCARS?

 A. MAG3 scan
 B. DMSA scan
 C. MCUG
D. Plain abdominal X-ray
 E. CT

Answer: B.

DMSA = static cortical scan for damage; MAG3 = dynamic scan for drainage (often with furosemide);
MCUG = reflux and urethral valves. A DMSA must be deferred at least 2–3 months after a UTI or
transient inflammation is misread as scarring.




7.4 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Antenatal ultrasound shows bilateral hydronephrosis in a male fetus. What is the correct postnatal
plan?

 A. Ultrasound at 4–6 weeks
 B. Ultrasound within 48 hours of birth to exclude posterior urethral valves, then MCUG
 C. No imaging unless symptomatic
D. DMSA in the first week
 E. Immediate nephrectomy

Answer: B.

All other antenatally detected anomalies have their scan delayed a few weeks, because the newborn is
relatively oliguric in the first days and early scanning under-estimates dilatation.
