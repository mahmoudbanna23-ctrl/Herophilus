2.7 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which professional group is LEAST likely to be needed in the routine multidisciplinary management
of a child with cerebral palsy?

 A. Physiotherapist
 B. Occupational therapist
 C. Speech and language therapist
D. Dietitian
 E. Interventional radiologist

Answer: E.

Cerebral palsy care is multidisciplinary and lifelong — physiotherapy, occupational therapy, speech and
language therapy (communication AND swallowing), dietetics, orthotics, orthopaedics, paediatric
neurology, education and social care.




2.8 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

A child with spastic cerebral palsy has painful hip subluxation. Which statement is correct?

 A. Hip surveillance is unnecessary if the child walks
 B. Hip surveillance with regular examination and radiographs is part of routine care
 C. Subluxation never causes pain
D. Surgery is contraindicated in cerebral palsy
 E. Scoliosis is unrelated to cerebral palsy

Answer: B.

Hip surveillance is standard because spasticity progressively displaces the hip, and a dislocated hip is
painful and hard to treat late. Scoliosis is another recognised musculoskeletal complication.
