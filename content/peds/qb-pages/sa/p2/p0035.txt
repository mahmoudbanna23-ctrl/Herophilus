6.1 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

What is the commonest congenital cardiac malformation?

 A. Atrial septal defect
 B. Ventricular septal defect
 C. Persistent ductus arteriosus
D. Tetralogy of Fallot
 E. Pulmonary stenosis

Answer: B.

VSD accounts for about 30%, then PDA 12%, ASD 7%, PS 7%, ToF 5%, TGA 5%, AS 5%, coarctation 5%,
AVSD 2% — the nine commonest lesions make up 80% of all congenital heart disease.




6.2 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which of the following is NOT a hallmark of an innocent murmur?

 A. Asymptomatic child
 B. Soft blowing quality
 C. Diastolic component
D. Left sternal edge
 E. Normal heart sounds

Answer: C.

The five Ss: aSymptomatic, Soft, Systolic only, left Sternal edge, normal Sounds — with no thrill and no
radiation. Any diastolic murmur is pathological.
