5.1 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which sign is the most SPECIFIC indicator of respiratory illness in a child?

 A. Tachypnoea
 B. Increased work of breathing
 C. Fever
D. Tachycardia
 E. Vomiting

Answer: B.

Tachypnoea is the most SENSITIVE sign of illness but is not specific — it occurs in acidosis, sepsis and
heart failure. Increased work of breathing is what localises the problem to the chest.




5.2 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Why does obstruction OUTSIDE the thorax cause inspiratory stridor?

 A. Because expiration is passive
 B. Because negative intrapleural pressure on inspiration collapses the extrathoracic airway while
    dilating intrathoracic airways
 C. Because the trachea is rigid
D. Because of increased lung compliance
 E. Because of bronchospasm

Answer: B.

Upper (extrathoracic) obstruction bites on the way IN → stridor. Lower (intrathoracic) obstruction bites
on the way OUT → wheeze.
