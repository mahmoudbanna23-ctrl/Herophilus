5.15 · MANAGEMENT

Which combination defines a LIFE-THREATENING asthma attack?

 A. Wheeze with a peak flow of 60% predicted
 B. Silent chest, poor respiratory effort, exhaustion or confusion, saturation below 92%
 C. Able to speak in sentences with mild recession
D. Cough only at night
 E. Heart rate 110

Answer: B.

Treat with oxygen, nebulised bronchodilators with ipratropium, systemic steroids, IV therapies and
intensive care involvement. Blood gases are often normal until the child is in extremis, so do not be
reassured by them.




5.16 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which investigation confirms cystic fibrosis?

 A. Chest X-ray
 B. Sweat chloride greater than 60 mmol/L, with CFTR mutation analysis
 C. Spirometry with reversibility
D. Exhaled nitric oxide
 E. Faecal calprotectin

Answer: B.

CF is autosomal recessive, gene on chromosome 7, commonest mutation Phe508del. Meconium ileus
occurs in 10–20%; other clues are nasal polyps, rectal prolapse, malabsorption with bulky offensive
stools, and clubbing.
