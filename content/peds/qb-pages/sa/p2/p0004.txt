1.3 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which pattern of hand use is ALWAYS abnormal before 12 months of age?

 A. Palmar grasp
 B. Transferring objects between hands
 C. Definite hand preference
D. Pincer grip
 E. Casting objects

Answer: C.

Hand preference before 12 months is abnormal and suggests a hemiplegia — the "preference" is really
weakness on the other side.




1.4 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which of the following describes GLOBAL developmental delay?

 A. Delay confined to speech and language
 B. Delay in two or more developmental domains
 C. Loss of previously acquired skills
D. Delay in gross motor skills only
 E. Normal development with abnormal behaviour

Answer: B.

Global delay = two or more domains. Loss of skills is REGRESSION (option C), which is far more
serious and always needs investigation for a neurodegenerative or metabolic cause.




1.5 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

At what age would you expect a child to normally build a tower of 6 cubes and join two words
together?

 A. 12 months
 B. 18 months
 C. 2 years
D. 3 years
 E. 4 years

Answer: C.

At 2 years a child typically builds a tower of about 6 cubes, joins two words, runs, and uses a spoon. At 3
years the tower is about 9 cubes and speech is in short sentences.
