10.16 · CLINICAL SCENARIO

A 2-week-old boy is collapsed with vomiting and weight loss. Sodium 122 mmol/L, potassium 7.1
mmol/L, glucose 1.9 mmol/L, with metabolic acidosis. What is the diagnosis and immediate treatment?

 A. Pyloric stenosis — correct the alkalosis then operate
 B. Salt-losing congenital adrenal hyperplasia — IV saline, glucose and hydrocortisone
 C. Gastroenteritis — oral rehydration
D. Duct-dependent coarctation — prostaglandin alone
 E. Urinary tract infection — oral antibiotics

Answer: B.

Over 90% of CAH is 21-hydroxylase deficiency (about 1 in 15 000, autosomal recessive), confirmed by a
markedly raised 17α-hydroxyprogesterone — but treatment must not wait for the result. Pyloric stenosis
gives the opposite picture: hypochloraemic, hypokalaemic ALKALOSIS.
