2.11 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which feature would most suggest autism spectrum disorder rather than isolated language delay in a 3-
year-old?

 A. Uses fewer than 50 words
 B. Does not point to share interest, avoids eye contact and has restricted repetitive interests
 C. Speaks only at home
 D. Has a family history of late talking
 E. Was born preterm

Answer: B.

Autism is defined by impaired social communication and interaction plus restricted, repetitive
behaviours and interests. Failure of joint attention (pointing to share) is one of the earliest and most
useful discriminators.




2.12 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which statement about attention deficit hyperactivity disorder is correct?

 A. Symptoms must be present in only one setting
 B. Symptoms of inattention, hyperactivity and impulsivity must be present in more than one setting
   and impair function
 C. It is diagnosed on a blood test
 D. Medication is always first line in preschool children
 E. It always resolves by adolescence

Answer: B.

ADHD requires pervasive symptoms — home AND school — causing impairment. Management begins
with parent training and educational support; medication is added in older children when symptoms
remain impairing.
