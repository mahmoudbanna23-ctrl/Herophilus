3.7 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which pattern best describes X-linked recessive inheritance?

 A. Affected males, carrier females, no male-to-male transmission, affected boys are related through
   the mother
 B. Affected females only
 C. Male-to-male transmission is typical
 D. Every generation is affected in both sexes equally
 E. Only maternal transmission to all offspring of both sexes

Answer: A.

Classic examples are Duchenne muscular dystrophy, haemophilia A and B, and G6PD deficiency.
Carrier mothers pass the gene to half their sons (affected) and half their daughters (carriers); an affected
father has no affected sons but all carrier daughters.




3.8 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

A family has a disorder transmitted only by affected mothers to all of their children, both boys and
girls. What is the inheritance?

 A. Autosomal dominant
 B. X-linked recessive
 C. MITOCHONDRIAL
 D. Autosomal recessive
 E. Imprinting defect

Answer: C.

Mitochondria are inherited exclusively from the mother. Variable severity between siblings is explained
by heteroplasmy — the proportion of abnormal mitochondria differs between cells and individuals.
