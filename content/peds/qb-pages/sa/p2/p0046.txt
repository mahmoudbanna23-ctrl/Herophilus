7.5 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which statement distinguishes multicystic dysplastic kidney from polycystic kidney disease?

 A. MCDK is a non-functioning kidney with ureteric atresia that usually involutes; PKD affects both
   kidneys with preserved function
 B. MCDK always affects both kidneys
 C. PKD never causes hypertension
D. MCDK requires nephrectomy in all cases
 E. PKD is only diagnosed in adulthood

Answer: A.

Half of multicystic dysplastic kidneys involute by 2 years. In ADPKD (1 in 1000) the childhood problem
is hypertension, with extrarenal cysts, cerebral aneurysms and mitral valve prolapse.




7.6 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Bilateral renal agenesis leads to death in the newborn period principally because of:

 A. Renal failure
 B. Pulmonary hypoplasia
 C. Cardiac malformation
D. Hyperkalaemia
 E. Limb deformity

Answer: B.

Potter sequence: no fetal urine → oligohydramnios → fetal compression (Potter facies, limb deformities)
and, critically, pulmonary hypoplasia — the lungs are what kill.




7.7 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which statement about vesicoureteric reflux is correct?

 A. It is familial in 30–50% of first-degree relatives, and mild reflux often resolves with age
 B. All grades require surgery
 C. It never causes hypertension
D. It cannot occur temporarily after a UTI
 E. Reflux nephropathy does not progress to chronic kidney disease

Answer: A.

Severe reflux risks pyelonephritis, scarring (reflux nephropathy), CKD and up to 10% hypertension —
but operating on MILD reflux has not been shown to reduce renal damage.
