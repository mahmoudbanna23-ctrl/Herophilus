10.1 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

What proportion of children with diabetes have type 1?

 A. About 50%
 B. About 75%
 C. About 95%
D. About 30%
 E. Fewer than 10%

Answer: C.

Type 1 is autoimmune β-cell destruction, with antibodies to GAD, ZnT8 and IA-2. Twin risk is about
70% within 3 years versus 15% in other siblings.




10.2 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which combination is diagnostic of diabetes in a symptomatic child?

 A. Random glucose above 11.1 mmol/L with glycosuria
 B. Fasting glucose of 6 mmol/L
 C. HbA1c of 40 mmol/mol
D. Glycosuria alone
 E. A positive glucose tolerance test is always required

Answer: A.

If there is doubt, use a fasting glucose above 7 mmol/L or a raised HbA1c. A glucose tolerance test is
rarely needed for type 1 but may be required for type 2.
