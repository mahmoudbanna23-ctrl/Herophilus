      How to read an SBA stem    1. Read the last line first — diagnosis, commonest cause, investigation of
     choice, or next step in management have different right answers for the same stem.
     2. Find the discriminating detail — age, one number, one sign.
     3. "Most appropriate next step" is often resuscitate or reassess, not the definitive test.
     4. In "EXCEPT" questions, mark each option true or false in the margin.
