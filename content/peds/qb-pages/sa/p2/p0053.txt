8.1 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which headache feature is a RED FLAG for a space-occupying lesion?

 A. Headache present on waking that improves through the morning
 B. Headache that is worse lying down and wakes the child at night
 C. Bilateral pulsatile headache relieved by sleep
D. Headache with photophobia in a dark quiet room
 E. Band-like constriction headache

Answer: B.

Red flags: worse lying down or with coughing/straining, wakes the child, morning vomiting, and
change in personality or school performance — plus growth failure, visual field defect, squint, torticollis,
ataxia, bradycardia and papilloedema (a late sign).




8.2 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

A child with raised intracranial pressure has a squint and cannot abduct one eye. Which nerve is
affected and what does it tell you?

 A. IIIrd nerve — the lesion is in the midbrain
 B. VIth nerve — a FALSE localising sign
 C. IVth nerve — cerebellar lesion
D. VIIth nerve — pontine lesion
 E. IInd nerve — optic pathway lesion

Answer: B.

The abducent nerve has a long intracranial course and is compressed wherever the lesion is. A VIIth
nerve palsy, in contrast, does suggest a pontine lesion.
