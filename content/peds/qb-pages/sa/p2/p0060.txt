8.16 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which is the commonest cause of OBSTRUCTIVE (non-communicating) hydrocephalus in a preterm
infant?

 A. Intraventricular haemorrhage
 B. Subarachnoid haemorrhage
 C. Pneumococcal meningitis
D. Choroid plexus papilloma
 E. Chiari malformation

Answer: A.

Obstructive causes: aqueduct stenosis, Chiari malformation, posterior fossa tumour, intraventricular
haemorrhage. Communicating causes (failure of reabsorption): subarachnoid haemorrhage and
meningitis. Watch the head circumference crossing centiles; sun-setting eyes is advanced. Treatment is a
ventriculoperitoneal shunt.
