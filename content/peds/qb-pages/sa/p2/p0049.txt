7.12 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

A 6-year-old girl is constantly damp with no dry intervals, day and night, since toilet training. What is
the most likely cause?

 A. Detrusor instability
 B. Ectopic ureter
 C. Urinary tract infection
 D. Constipation
 E. Emotional upset

Answer: B.

Constant dribbling with no dry periods = an ectopic ureter draining below the sphincter. Detrusor
instability causes urgency with dry intervals; secondary enuresis after a period of dryness is most often
emotional, but exclude UTI and diabetes.




7.13 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which finding indicates GLOMERULAR rather than lower urinary tract haematuria?

 A. Red urine at the end of the stream
 B. Brown urine with deformed red cells, red cell casts and proteinuria
 C. Absence of proteinuria
 D. Clots in the urine
 E. Dysuria

Answer: B.

Red cells deform passing through the basement membrane. Confirm haematuria on microscopy (>10
RBCs per high-power field) — rifampicin, beetroot and haemoglobinuria all discolour urine without red
cells.
