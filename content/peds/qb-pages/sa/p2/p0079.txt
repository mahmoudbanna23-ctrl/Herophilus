12.1 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

What is the approximate risk that a child develops cancer before the age of 15 years?

 A. 1 in 50
 B. 1 in 500
 C. 1 in 5000
D. 1 in 50 000
 E. 1 in 5

Answer: B.

About 120–140 new cases per million children under 15 each year. Overall 5-year survival is about 75%,
but over 60% of survivors live with at least one chronic condition related to the disease or its treatment.




12.2 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which tumours are "embryonal" and almost always present in the first 6 years of life?

 A. Neuroblastoma and Wilms tumour
 B. Osteosarcoma and Ewing sarcoma
 C. Hodgkin lymphoma and thyroid carcinoma
D. Astrocytoma and ependymoma
 E. Testicular germ cell tumours

Answer: A.

Childhood cancer types differ completely from adults, where lung, breast, bowel and prostate carcinomas
predominate.
