12.6 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which is a HIGH-RISK prognostic feature in acute lymphoblastic leukaemia?

 A. Age 3 years
 B. White cell count above 50 × 10⁹/L at presentation
 C. Common B-precursor immunophenotype
D. Undetectable minimal residual disease after induction
 E. Hyperdiploidy

Answer: B.

Other high-risk features: age under 1 or over 10 years, MLL rearrangement or t(4;11), hypodiploidy (<44
chromosomes), and detectable minimal residual disease after induction.




12.7 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Why is intrathecal chemotherapy given in acute lymphoblastic leukaemia?

 A. To reduce nausea
 B. Because leukaemic cells in the CNS may survive effective systemic treatment
 C. To treat bone pain
D. To prevent tumour lysis
 E. To shorten maintenance therapy

Answer: B.

The CNS is a sanctuary site. Maintenance chemotherapy continues for up to 3 years from diagnosis, with
co-trimoxazole prophylaxis against Pneumocystis; induction achieves remission in about 95%.




12.8 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

What proportion of childhood brain tumours are infratentorial?

 A. 10%
 B. 30%
 C. 60%
D. 90%
 E. Fewer than 5%

Answer: C.

Almost all are primary rather than metastatic. They are the commonest solid tumour of childhood and
the leading cause of childhood cancer death. Astrocytoma ~40%, medulloblastoma ~20%, ependymoma
~8%, brainstem glioma ~6%, craniopharyngioma ~4%.
