10.6 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which statement about HbA1c is correct?

 A. It reflects control over the previous week
 B. It reflects control over 6–12 weeks and is unreliable in sickle cell trait and thalassaemia
 C. It should be measured once a year
D. The target is below 70 mmol/mol
 E. It is unaffected by red cell lifespan

Answer: B.

Check it at least four times a year; the target is below 48 mmol/mol (6.5%). Time in range above 70%
correlates with an HbA1c in target.




10.7 · MANAGEMENT

A child with type 1 diabetes is unconscious in the emergency department with a glucose of 1.9 mmol/L.
What is given?

 A. Oral glucose gel
 B. 2 mL/kg of intravenous 10% glucose
 C. Subcutaneous insulin
D. 0.9% saline bolus alone
 E. Nothing until a laboratory glucose returns

Answer: B.

Outside hospital or when access is delayed, IM glucagon is used. After recovery, give a complex
carbohydrate to stop the glucose falling again. Frequent hypos cause loss of awareness and must be
avoided.
