5.17 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which best describes the management of cystic fibrosis?

 A. Antibiotics only when symptomatic
 B. Physiotherapy, pancreatic enzymes with fat-soluble vitamins, high-calorie diet, aggressive
    antibiotic therapy and CFTR modulators
 C. Low-fat diet and fluid restriction
D. Inhaled corticosteroids alone
 E. Avoidance of exercise

Answer: B.

Care is multidisciplinary and proactive. CFTR modulators have changed the outlook substantially for
eligible genotypes.




5.18 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

A 7-year-old has a chronic wet cough, recurrent sinusitis, bronchiectasis and dextrocardia on the chest
X-ray. What is the diagnosis?

 A. Cystic fibrosis
 B. Primary ciliary dyskinesia (Kartagener syndrome)
 C. Asthma
D. Immunodeficiency
 E. Inhaled foreign body

Answer: B.

About 50% of children with primary ciliary dyskinesia have Kartagener syndrome — situs inversus +
sinusitis + bronchiectasis. Suspect it with a persistent wet cough and chronic ear and sinus disease.
