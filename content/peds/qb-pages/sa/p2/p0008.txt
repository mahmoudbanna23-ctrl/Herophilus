2.1 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which statement best defines cerebral palsy?

 A. A progressive disorder of movement and posture
 B. A permanent disorder of movement and posture due to a NON-PROGRESSIVE lesion of the
    developing brain
 C. A disorder of the peripheral nerves in infancy
D. A condition always associated with learning disability
 E. A disorder that appears only after the age of 5 years

Answer: B.

The lesion is non-progressive, but the clinical picture changes as the child grows — which is why signs
may become more obvious over the first two years. Progressive deterioration means the diagnosis is
something else.




2.2 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

When does the causal brain injury occur in the majority of children with cerebral palsy?

 A. Antenatally
 B. During labour and delivery
 C. In the first week after birth
D. Between 1 and 2 years of age
 E. After the age of 5 years

Answer: A.

About 80% of cases are ANTENATAL in origin — vascular occlusion, cortical migration disorders,
structural maldevelopment, congenital infection. Only around 10% follow intrapartum hypoxic–
ischaemic injury, which is why cerebral palsy is not synonymous with "birth asphyxia".
