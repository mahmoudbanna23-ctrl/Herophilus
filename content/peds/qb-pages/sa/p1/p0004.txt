1.3 · CALCULATION

A girl's father is 180 cm and her mother 164 cm. What is her mid-parental height?

 A. 158 cm
 B. 165 cm
 C. 172 cm
 D. 179 cm
 E. 186 cm

Answer: B.

Mean = (180+164)/2 = 172 cm; she is a girl, so subtract 7 → 165 cm. The distractors are the unadjusted mean
(172) and adding instead of subtracting (179).




1.4 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

A baby born at 30 weeks' gestation is now 18 weeks old. Her weight plots below the 0.4th centile at
actual age. What should be done before concluding she has faltering growth?

 A. Repeat the measurement on different scales
 B. Plot at corrected age by subtracting 10 weeks
 C. Measure the parents' heights
 D. Check thyroid function
 E. Refer for a skeletal survey

Answer: B.

Correction for gestational age is used until 2 years. Corrected, she is likely between the 2nd and 9th centile
— entirely different clinical picture.
