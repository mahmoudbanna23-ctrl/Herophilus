9.3 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which organisms cause bacterial meningitis in an infant under 3 months?

 A. Meningococcus and pneumococcus
 B. Group B streptococcus, E. coli and Listeria
 C. Haemophilus influenzae type b and Staphylococcus aureus
D. Mycobacterium tuberculosis and Cryptococcus
 E. Enteroviruses only

Answer: B.

Which is why amoxicillin is added to the third-generation cephalosporin under 3 months — to cover
Listeria.




9.4 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

A CSF sample is turbid, with numerous polymorphs, a high protein and a low glucose. What does it
indicate?

 A. Viral meningitis
 B. Bacterial meningitis
 C. Tuberculous meningitis
D. Encephalitis
 E. A normal sample

Answer: B.

TB gives lymphocytes with a very high protein and a very low glucose; viral gives lymphocytes with
normal or slightly raised protein. Two exceptions: lymphocytes can predominate in Lyme meningitis,
and glucose can be low in enteroviral meningitis.
