M9 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

A previously well 9-week-old is found dead in his cot. Which statement is correct?

 A. A postmortem is rarely needed
 B. The parents should not be allowed to see the baby
 C. A paediatrician takes a detailed history and examines the baby, samples are taken, and agencies
   communicate
D. Police involvement implies the family is under suspicion of a crime
 E. SIDS is the commonest cause of death in the first month of life

Answer: C.

SIDS is the commonest cause of death between 1 month and 1 year (peak 2–4 months). The coroner will
usually order a postmortem, agencies must communicate, and the family should be offered the chance to
see and hold the baby, with bereavement support and follow-up.




M10 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which single feature would most change your management of a febrile 2-year-old with a rash?

 A. A temperature of 39.5 °C rather than 38.5 °C
 B. The rash does not blanch under a glass
 C. The rash is itchy
D. An older sibling has a cold
 E. The child has had paracetamol

Answer: B.

A non-blanching rash in a febrile child means meningococcal sepsis until proven otherwise — antibiotics
now. The height of the fever does not predict serious bacterial infection.
