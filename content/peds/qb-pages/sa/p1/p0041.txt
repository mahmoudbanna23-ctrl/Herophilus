7.8 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Bilateral posterior periventricular cysts on cranial ultrasound carry an 80–90% risk of which outcome?

 A. Hemiplegia
 B. Spastic diplegia
 C. Deafness
D. Hydrocephalus
 E. Blindness

Answer: B.

Periventricular leukomalacia → spastic diplegia, often with cognitive impairment. A unilateral
parenchymal haemorrhagic infarct causes hemiplegia. Both PVL and IVH can occur with no abnormal
clinical signs.




7.9 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Bronchopulmonary dysplasia is defined as:

 A. Oxygen requirement at 28 days of age
 B. Oxygen requirement at 36 weeks' post-menstrual age
 C. Ventilation for more than 7 days
D. Any preterm with an abnormal chest X-ray
 E. Persistent wheeze at discharge

Answer: B.

Caused by delayed lung maturation, pressure/volume trauma, oxygen toxicity and infection.
Corticosteroids help weaning but are restricted to short, low-dose courses because of neurodevelopmental
concerns.
