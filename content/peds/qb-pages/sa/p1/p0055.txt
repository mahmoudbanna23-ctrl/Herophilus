9.12 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

An infant has fever, erythema and skin tenderness with epidermal separation on gentle pressure. What
is the diagnosis?

 A. Toxic epidermal necrolysis
 B. Staphylococcal scalded skin syndrome
 C. Scald from non-accidental injury
D. Bullous impetigo
 E. Erythema multiforme

Answer: B.

An exfoliative staphylococcal toxin separates the epidermal layers — a positive Nikolsky sign. Treat
with IV anti-staphylococcal antibiotics; it heals without scarring. Its appearance must not be mistaken for
a scald.




9.13 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

A 15-month-old had 3 days of high fever. The fever has now settled and a macular rash has appeared on
the trunk. He looks well. What is the diagnosis?

 A. Measles
 B. Rubella
 C. Roseola infantum (HHV-6/7)
D. Scarlet fever
 E. Drug rash

Answer: C.

The rash appears as the fever wanes. Most children are infected by 2 years. This sequence is the reason
many toddlers are wrongly labelled antibiotic-allergic.
