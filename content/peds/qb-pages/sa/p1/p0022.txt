3.13 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Why does oral rehydration solution work in diarrhoeal illness?

 A. It stops the diarrhoea by slowing gut transit
 B. Glucose drives active sodium co-transport, which drags water across the mucosa
 C. It is hypertonic and draws water into the circulation
D. It kills the causative organism
 E. It replaces bicarbonate lost in the stool only

Answer: B.

The glucose-coupled sodium transporter remains intact in diarrhoeal illness. ORS does not stop the
diarrhoea — it keeps the child hydrated until the organism is cleared. Fruit juices and carbonated drinks
are unsuitable.




3.14 · CALCULATION

A 24 kg child needs maintenance IV fluids. What is the 24-hour volume?

 A. 1200 ml
 B. 1440 ml
 C. 1580 ml
D. 1800 ml
 E. 2400 ml

Answer: C.

100 ml/kg × 10 = 1000, plus 50 ml/kg × 10 = 500, plus 20 ml/kg × 4 = 80 → 1580 ml/24 h.
