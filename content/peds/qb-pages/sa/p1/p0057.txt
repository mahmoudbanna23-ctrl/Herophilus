9.17 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Subacute sclerosing panencephalitis follows which infection, and after roughly how long?

 A. Mumps, after 1 year
 B. Measles, after an average of 7 years
 C. Rubella, after 6 months
D. Varicella, after 2 years
 E. Parvovirus, after 10 years

Answer: B.

About 1 in 100,000 cases; most had measles before 2 years of age. Caused by a variant virus persisting in
the CNS; progressive and fatal. Acute measles encephalitis is different — 1 in 5000, a few days after the
illness, mortality 15%.




9.18 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

A 3-year-old with atopic eczema develops a rapidly spreading crop of painful vesicles on the
eczematous skin, with fever. What is the treatment?

 A. Increase the topical steroid
 B. Intravenous aciclovir
 C. Oral flucloxacillin alone
D. Emollients and observation
 E. Oral prednisolone

Answer: B.

Eczema herpeticum — it may be complicated by secondary bacterial infection and septicaemia. Its
lookalike, eczema coxsackium, needs no treatment, but you treat for herpes until you are sure.
