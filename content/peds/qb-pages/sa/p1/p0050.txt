9.1 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

A 6-week-old has a temperature of 38.4 °C and no focus of infection. What is the correct management?

 A. Paracetamol and review in 24 hours
 B. Oral antibiotics at home
 C. Full septic screen and immediate broad-spectrum IV antibiotics
 D. Urine dipstick only
 E. Reassure — the height of fever is what matters

Answer: C.

Infants under 3 months present non-specifically and bacterial infection cannot be excluded clinically.
"The younger the child, the lower the threshold for a septic screen and antibiotics."




9.2 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which statement about fever in children is correct?

 A. Fever is defined as a temperature over 37.5 °C
 B. Axillary temperature overestimates body temperature by 0.5 °C
 C. Antipyretics prevent febrile seizures
 D. Parental report of fever is a valid assessment
 E. The height of the fever predicts serious bacterial infection

Answer: D.

Fever is >38.0 °C; axilla underestimates by 0.5 °C; antipyretics do not prevent febrile seizures; and it is the
presence, not the height, of fever that matters.
