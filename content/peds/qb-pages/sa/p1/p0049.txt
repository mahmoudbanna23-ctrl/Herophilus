8.8 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

A child has recurrent episodes of lip and tongue swelling with NO urticaria, unresponsive to
antihistamines and adrenaline. What should be considered?

 A. Peanut allergy
 B. C1 esterase inhibitor deficiency
 C. Idiopathic anaphylaxis
D. Chronic urticaria
 E. Papular urticaria

Answer: B.

Angioedema WITHOUT urticaria is the classic pointer to hereditary angioedema, which does not
respond to adrenaline, steroids or antihistamines and needs specialist review.
