2.14 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

A 9-year-old boy has a BMI on the 99th centile. He is on the 9th height centile and has mild learning
difficulty. Which is the most appropriate assessment?

 A. Reassure — this is simple obesity
 B. Investigate for an endocrine or genetic cause
 C. Start orlistat
D. Refer for bariatric surgery
 E. Advise a very low calorie diet alone

Answer: B.

Simple (exogenous) obesity makes children tall for age. Obesity with short stature, developmental delay
or dysmorphism points to hypothyroidism, Cushing syndrome, GH deficiency or a genetic syndrome
such as Prader–Willi. Drugs and surgery are for a small minority.
