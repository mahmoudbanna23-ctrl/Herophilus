7.6 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

A 26-week infant on day 12, recently established on formula feeds, has abdominal distension, bile-
stained aspirates and blood in the stool. Which X-ray finding is characteristic?

 A. Double bubble
 B. Intramural gas (pneumatosis intestinalis) and portal venous gas
 C. Ground-glass lungs
 D. Absent rectal gas only
 E. Multiple air-fluid levels alone

Answer: B.

Necrotising enterocolitis — mortality about 20%. Breast milk is protective; formula, rapid feed increases
and antibiotics adversely affect the gut microbiome. Stop feeds, give antibiotics and parenteral nutrition;
operate for perforation.




7.7 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Intraventricular haemorrhage in preterm infants arises from which site, and when?

 A. The choroid plexus, in the second week
 B. The germinal matrix, within the first 72 hours
 C. The subarachnoid space, at delivery
 D. The dural sinuses, after a month
 E. The cerebellum, at 2 weeks

Answer: B.

The germinal matrix above the caudate nucleus contains a fragile vascular network. Risk factors: perinatal
asphyxia, severe RDS, pneumothorax. Antenatal steroids reduce both RDS and IVH.
