6.3 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

A 20-week scan shows severe oligohydramnios. Which fetal abnormality is most likely to be
responsible?

 A. Duodenal atresia
 B. Oesophageal atresia
 C. Bilateral renal dysplasia
D. Anencephaly
 E. Exomphalos

Answer: C.

Amniotic fluid is fetal urine. Too little → renal problem, obstructive uropathy or ruptured membranes,
leading to pulmonary hypoplasia and Potter sequence. Atresias cause polyhydramnios because the fetus
cannot swallow.




6.4 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Antenatal corticosteroids are given before anticipated preterm delivery. For optimal effect, the
completed course should be given:

 A. At any time in the preceding month
 B. At least 24 hours before delivery and within 1–7 days of birth
 C. Within 2 hours of delivery
D. Only after the membranes rupture
 E. Only after 34 weeks' gestation

Answer: B.

They accelerate lung maturity and surfactant production and reduce RDS, intraventricular haemorrhage
and neonatal mortality.




6.5 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Fetal anaemia from rhesus isoimmunisation is detected non-invasively by:

 A. Amniocentesis
 B. Doppler velocimetry of the fetal middle cerebral artery
 C. Cordocentesis
D. Maternal haemoglobin measurement
 E. Fetal MRI

Answer: B.

Severely affected fetuses may need intrauterine transfusion via the umbilical vein from about 20 weeks.
