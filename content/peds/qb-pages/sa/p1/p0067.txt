  M1 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

  Which is the correct compression-to-ventilation ratio in newborn resuscitation?

   A. 15 : 2
   B. 30 : 2
   C. 3 : 1
   D. 5 : 1
   E. Continuous compressions

  Answer: C.

  90 compressions to 30 breaths = 120 events per minute. Older children get 15 : 2. The newborn ratio reflects
  the hypoxic cause of arrest.




  M2 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

  At what age does a term infant's weight usually triple?

   A. 4 months
   B. 6 months
   C. 12 months
   D. 2 years
   E. 4 years

  Answer: C.

  Doubles at 4–5 months, triples at 12 months. Height doubles at 4 years and triples at 12 years.
