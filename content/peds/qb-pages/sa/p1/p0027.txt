4.6 · CLINICAL SCENARIO

A 16-year-old took 25 paracetamol tablets 6 hours ago and looks completely well. What is the most
appropriate management?

 A. Discharge with advice
 B. Activated charcoal
 C. Paracetamol level, N-acetylcysteine as indicated, and mental health assessment
 D. Gastric lavage
 E. Urine toxicology screen and observation

Answer: C.

Paracetamol poisoning is silent for the first 24 hours; hepatic failure appears at 3–4 days. Charcoal is
useless after an hour. Deliberate self-harm always needs CAMHS assessment before discharge.




4.7 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

A 2-year-old is drowsy after drinking from a bottle at a party. Pupils are normal, breathing is adequate.
What must be checked first?

 A. Blood alcohol level
 B. Blood glucose
 C. Urine toxicology
 D. Liver function tests
 E. ECG

Answer: B.

Alcohol causes hypoglycaemia in young children, which is what harms the brain. "Don't Ever Forget
Glucose" applies to every drowsy child.
