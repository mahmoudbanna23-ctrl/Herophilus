1.1 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which phase of growth accounts for the greatest proportion of final adult height?

 A. Fetal
 B. Infantile
 C. Childhood
D. Pubertal
 E. Neonatal

Answer: C.

Childhood ≈40%; fetal 30%; infantile and pubertal 15% each. The fetal phase is the fastest but not the
largest contributor — read whether the stem says "fastest" or "greatest proportion".




1.2 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

The main determinant of growth rate during the childhood phase is:

 A. Nutrition
 B. Growth hormone acting through IGF-1
 C. Testosterone and oestradiol
D. Maternal and placental health
 E. Insulin

Answer: B.

Nutrition dominates the infantile phase, sex steroids the pubertal phase, and maternal/placental factors
the fetal phase. GH → IGF-1 at the epiphyses drives childhood growth, provided nutrition and health are
adequate.
