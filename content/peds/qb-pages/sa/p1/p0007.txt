1.10 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which finding best distinguishes gonadotrophin-independent from gonadotrophin-dependent
precocious puberty?

 A. The age of onset
 B. The presence of a growth spurt
 C. Whether the sequence of pubertal development is consonant or dissonant
D. The height of the oestradiol level
 E. The presence of acne

Answer: C.

Consonant (normal order and proportion) = central. Dissonant (out of order, e.g. virilisation before breast
development) = peripheral.




1.11 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

A 15-year-old girl has not started puberty. She is 145 cm tall. LH and FSH are elevated. What is the most
likely diagnosis?

 A. Constitutional delay
 B. Anorexia nervosa
 C. Turner syndrome
D. Kallmann syndrome
 E. Craniopharyngioma

Answer: C.

High gonadotrophins = primary gonadal failure (hypergonadotrophic hypogonadism). In a short girl this
is Turner syndrome until the karyotype says otherwise. All the other options give low gonadotrophins.
