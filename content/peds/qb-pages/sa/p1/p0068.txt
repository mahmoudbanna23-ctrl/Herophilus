M3 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

A child weighing 30 kg needs maintenance fluid. What is the 24-hour volume?

 A. 1500 ml
 B. 1700 ml
 C. 1800 ml
 D. 2000 ml
 E. 3000 ml

Answer: B.

1000 (first 10 kg) + 500 (second 10 kg) + 200 (20 ml/kg × 10) = 1700 ml.




M4 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which statement about the anterior fontanelle is correct?

 A. It closes by 8 weeks
 B. It closes between 12 and 18 months
 C. A tense fontanelle in a crying baby always means raised pressure
 D. Its size is a reliable measure of brain growth
 E. It should be closed at birth

Answer: B.

The posterior fontanelle closes by 8 weeks. A tense fontanelle in a baby who is not crying suggests raised
intracranial pressure and needs cranial ultrasound.




M5 · EXCEPT

All of the following are live vaccines EXCEPT:

 A. MMR
 B. BCG
 C. Rotavirus
 D. Oral polio (Sabin)
 E. Inactivated polio (Salk)

Answer: E.

Live vaccines must not be given to immunocompromised children, those on or in contact with
immunosuppressive therapy, or in pregnancy.
