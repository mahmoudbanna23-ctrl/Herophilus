7.1 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Surfactant is produced by which cells?

 A. Type I pneumocytes
 B. Type II pneumocytes
 C. Alveolar macrophages
D. Clara cells
 E. Bronchial goblet cells

Answer: B.

A mixture of phospholipids and proteins that lowers alveolar surface tension. Deficiency → widespread
alveolar collapse.




7.2 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which chest X-ray appearance is characteristic of respiratory distress syndrome?

 A. Fluid in the horizontal fissure
 B. Diffuse ground-glass appearance with an air bronchogram
 C. Over-inflation with patchy consolidation
D. Bowel loops in the left hemithorax
 E. Normal lung fields with a small heart

Answer: B.

With an indistinct heart border. Option A is TTN, C is meconium aspiration, D is a diaphragmatic hernia
and E fits PPHN or cyanotic heart disease.
