A 16-year-old boy is 196 cm tall with long limbs, arachnodactyly and a high-arched palate. Which
investigation must not be omitted?

 A. Karyotype
 B. Growth hormone suppression test
 C. Echocardiography
D. Thyroid function tests
 E. Bone age

Answer: C.

Marfan syndrome — the complication that kills is aortic root dilatation. Homocystinuria is the main
differential and is excluded biochemically.
