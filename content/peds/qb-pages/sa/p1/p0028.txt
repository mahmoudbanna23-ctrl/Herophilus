5.1 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Cardiopulmonary arrest in children is most often secondary to:

 A. Ischaemic heart disease
 B. Primary arrhythmia
 C. Hypoxia from respiratory failure, or shock
D. Electrolyte disturbance
 E. Congenital heart disease

Answer: C.

This single fact explains why paediatric basic life support begins with 5 rescue breaths and why early
recognition of respiratory and circulatory failure saves lives.




5.2 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Where should the brachial pulse be used to check for a pulse?

 A. In all children
 B. In infants under 1 year
 C. In children over 1 year
D. Only in the newborn
 E. Only when the carotid is impalpable

Answer: B.

Under 1 year: brachial or femoral. Over 1 year: carotid or femoral. Check for a maximum of 10 seconds.
