2.1 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

A term newborn, after the first week of life, requires approximately what daily volume of milk?

 A. 60 ml/kg
 B. 100 ml/kg
 C. 150 ml/kg
 D. 200 ml/kg
 E. 250 ml/kg

Answer: C.

150 ml/kg/day — about 15% of body weight every day, which is why even a small reduction in intake
rapidly affects hydration, nutrition and growth.




2.2 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which hormone is responsible for the milk-ejection ("let-down") reflex?

 A. Prolactin
 B. Oxytocin
 C. Oestrogen
 D. Progesterone
 E. Human placental lactogen

Answer: B.

Prolactin makes the milk (cuboidal alveolar cells); oxytocin contracts the myoepithelial cells to eject it —
and contracts the uterus, reducing postpartum blood loss.
