8.6 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

A child with egg allergy reacts to lightly cooked egg but tolerates cake containing egg. What is the
explanation?

 A. He has outgrown the allergy
 B. Heating to high temperatures degrades the allergenic proteins
 C. The cake contains a different species of egg
 D. Sugar blocks histamine release
 E. He has a non-IgE-mediated allergy

Answer: B.

Heat degradation and food processing reduce allergenicity — the same applies to baked milk. This is
flagged as an MCQ point in your notes.




8.7 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

A 9-year-old has had urticaria almost daily for 4 months. There is no consistent trigger. What is the
most likely situation?

 A. Undiagnosed food allergy — send a broad allergy panel
 B. Chronic urticaria, which is usually non-allergic
 C. Hereditary angioedema
 D. Mastocytosis
 E. Drug allergy

Answer: B.

Allergic urticaria appears within 1 hour, is reproducible on every exposure and resolves within 2–4
hours. Chronic urticaria (>6 weeks) is usually non-allergic; treat with non-sedating antihistamines, at
higher than standard dose if needed.
