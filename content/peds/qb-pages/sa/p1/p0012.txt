2.6 · EXCEPT

All of the following are advantages of breastfeeding for the INFANT EXCEPT:

 A. Reduced risk of necrotising enterocolitis
 B. Reduced risk of sudden infant death syndrome
 C. Reduced risk of maternal breast cancer
D. Reduced incidence of later obesity and diabetes
 E. Higher IQ score by approximately 3 points

Answer: C.

Reduced breast (and probably ovarian) cancer risk is a benefit to the mother. Read whether the question
asks about the infant or the mother.




2.7 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which is an absolute contraindication to breastfeeding?

 A. Breast milk jaundice
 B. Galactosaemia in the infant
 C. Maternal mastitis
D. Prematurity
 E. Maternal hepatitis B when the infant has been immunised

Answer: B.

Galactosaemia and fatty-acid oxidation disorders contraindicate breastfeeding. Breast milk jaundice is
benign — continue feeding and investigate only if it lasts beyond 2 weeks.




2.8 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Why is unmodified cow's milk unsuitable as the main drink before 12 months of age?

 A. It contains too little protein
 B. It has a very low bioavailable iron content and a high renal solute load
 C. It is deficient in carbohydrate and cannot sustain growth
D. It causes lactose intolerance
 E. It has a whey-dominant protein profile

Answer: B.

Cow's milk has 3× the protein and 3× the sodium of breast milk, a casein-dominant 80:20 ratio, and almost
no absorbable iron — hence iron-deficiency anaemia. It may be used in cooking.
