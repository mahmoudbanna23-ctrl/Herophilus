      Q Bank · How to use this bank READ FIRST


       IMPORTANT — what this bank is      These questions are written for you in the Single Best Answer (SBA)
      and Extended Matching Question (EMQ) formats used by Self-Assessment in Paediatrics (Lissauer &
      Carroll, Elsevier 2018), at the same level and covering the same topics — they are not reproductions of
      that book's questions. Each block tells you which chapter of the book to open next, so you can work
      through the original alongside this bank and get double the practice on the same material.



      How to work through it
      1. Use the "Hide answers" button, or "PDF exam mode" to print the bank as a blank paper.
      2. Answer a whole block before revealing anything.
      3. For every question you get wrong, go back to the chapter in the notebook — the ⚠️ MCQ TRAPS box
      for that topic is almost always where the mistake came from.
      4. Do the matching chapter in the Self-Assessment book straight afterwards.
