6.9 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which is the most common congenital infection and the commonest non-genetic cause of sensorineural
hearing loss?

 A. Rubella
 B. Toxoplasmosis
 C. Cytomegalovirus
 D. Syphilis
 E. Parvovirus B19

Answer: C.

Of infected infants, 90% are normal at birth, 5% have features at birth (hepatosplenomegaly, petechiae)
and 5% develop later problems, mainly deafness.




6.10 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

A newborn has chorioretinitis, hydrocephalus and intracranial calcification. The mother ate
undercooked meat during pregnancy. What is the treatment?

 A. Ganciclovir for 6 weeks
 B. Pyrimethamine and sulfadiazine for 1 year
 C. Benzylpenicillin for 10 days
 D. Aciclovir for 3 weeks
 E. No treatment is available

Answer: B.

Congenital toxoplasmosis. About 40% of fetuses are infected during a primary maternal parasitaemia;
most infected infants are asymptomatic but remain at risk of chorioretinitis into adulthood.




6.11 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Why is transient tachypnoea more common after elective caesarean section?

 A. The baby is usually preterm
 B. There is no maternal catecholamine surge and no squeezing of the chest in the birth canal
 C. Surfactant production is reduced by anaesthesia
 D. The cord is clamped too late
 E. Maternal antibodies are not transferred

Answer: B.

Both mechanisms normally clear lung fluid. Chest X-ray classically shows fluid in the horizontal fissure,
and it is self-limiting.
