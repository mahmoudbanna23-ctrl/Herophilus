5.12 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which infant does NOT meet the criteria for a low-risk BRUE?

 A. Age 3 months
 B. Born at 34 weeks' gestation
 C. First such event
D. Event lasting 30 seconds
 E. CPR given by a trained provider

Answer: E.

Low-risk criteria: age >60 days, born ≥32 weeks, first event, lasting <1 minute, no CPR by a trained
provider, and no concerning features. Failing any one makes it higher risk and requires investigation.




5.13 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which advice most reduces the risk of sudden infant death syndrome?

 A. Sleeping the baby prone to prevent aspiration
 B. Sleeping the baby supine, feet to the foot of the cot, in the parents' room
 C. Keeping the room at 24 °C
D. Using a soft pillow and duvet
 E. Bed-sharing on a sofa for closeness

Answer: B.

"Back to sleep" reduced SIDS from 1.9 to 0.3 per 1000 live births in the UK. Peak age 2–4 months; other
advice — smoke-free, head uncovered, room about 18 °C, never sleep with the baby on a sofa or armchair,
and breastfeed.
