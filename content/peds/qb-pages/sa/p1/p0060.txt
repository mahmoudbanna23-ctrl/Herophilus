9.23 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which vaccine must NOT be given to a child known to be HIV-positive?

 A. Inactivated polio
 B. BCG
 C. Hepatitis B
 D. Pneumococcal conjugate
 E. Influenza

Answer: B.

BCG is a live vaccine and risks dissemination. Live vaccines are also contraindicated in other
immunocompromised children, those on or in contact with immunosuppressive therapy, and in
pregnancy. Children with HIV should be vaccinated against influenza, hepatitis A and B, and varicella.




9.24 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

A boy has had two episodes of meningococcal meningitis. Which investigation is most appropriate?

 A. Immunoglobulin levels
 B. Nitroblue tetrazolium test
 C. Complement function testing (CH50 and AP50)
 D. Lymphocyte subsets
 E. Sweat test

Answer: C.

Recurrent neisserial infection = terminal complement component deficiency until proven otherwise.
Compare: abscesses and granulomas → NBT test for chronic granulomatous disease; infections after 6
months with bronchiectasis → antibody defect; severe early infections with thrush and diarrhoea → T-cell
defect.
