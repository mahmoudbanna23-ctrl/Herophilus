3.1 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

A 7-year-old has right iliac fossa pain for 18 hours with anorexia and a temperature of 38.0 °C. The
white cell count and ultrasound are normal. What is the most appropriate next step?

 A. Discharge with safety-netting
 B. Admit for repeated clinical review
 C. Abdominal CT
D. Immediate laparotomy
 E. Barium enema

Answer: B.

Appendicitis is a progressive condition and no test reliably excludes it — normal neutrophils and a
normal ultrasound do not. Serial examination every few hours is the standard answer.




3.2 · EXCEPT

In a child with acute abdominal pain, all of the following must be examined or excluded EXCEPT:

 A. The chest, for lower lobe pneumonia
 B. The urine, for infection
 C. The blood glucose, for diabetic ketoacidosis
D. The testes and hernial orifices
 E. The fundi, for papilloedema

Answer: E.

The other four are the standard "do not miss" list in your notes. Fundoscopy has its place in headache and
raised intracranial pressure, not routinely in the acute abdomen.
