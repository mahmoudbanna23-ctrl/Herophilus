1.12 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

A 6-month-old has occipital flattening on the right, with the right ear displaced anteriorly and a normal
head circumference. No ridge is palpable. Which statement is correct?

 A. Urgent surgical referral is needed
 B. He should now be placed prone to sleep
 C. This is positional plagiocephaly and improves with mobility
D. A skull X-ray will show a fused lambdoid suture
 E. The head circumference will fall across centiles

Answer: C.

Anteriorly displaced ear, all sutures open, normal head circumference. Lambdoid synostosis displaces the
ear posteriorly and gives a palpable ridge. The baby must still sleep supine — SIDS prevention outweighs
the cosmetic issue.




1.13 · MOST COMMON

What is the most common cause of a head circumference above the 98th centile in a developmentally
normal infant?

 A. Hydrocephalus
 B. Familial macrocephaly
 C. Sotos syndrome
D. Chronic subdural haematoma
 E. Mucopolysaccharidosis

Answer: B.

Measure the parents' heads. The concerning pattern is a head circumference crossing centiles upwards,
which needs cranial ultrasound (if the fontanelle is open) or CT/MRI.
