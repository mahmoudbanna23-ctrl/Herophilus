6.1 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

How is the perinatal mortality rate defined?

 A. Deaths in the first 4 weeks per 1000 live births
 B. Stillbirths plus deaths in the first week per 1000 live births and stillbirths
 C. Deaths under 1 year per 1000 live births
 D. Stillbirths per 1000 pregnancies
 E. Deaths in the first 24 hours per 1000 deliveries

Answer: B.

Option A is the neonatal mortality rate. A stillbirth is an infant born with no signs of life after 24 weeks.




6.2 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Which drug should not be prescribed to females of child-bearing age because of a 10% risk of
congenital anomaly and a 30–40% risk of developmental delay?

 A. Carbamazepine
 B. Sodium valproate
 C. Lamotrigine
 D. Levetiracetam
 E. Phenytoin

Answer: B.

Other named teratogens in your notes are retinoids and warfarin. Women on anticonvulsants also need a
higher dose of folic acid.
