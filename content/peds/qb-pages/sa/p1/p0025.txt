4.1 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

What is the most common cause of non-fatal choking in children?

 A. Toys
 B. Food
 C. Coins
D. Button batteries
 E. Balloons

Answer: B.

Food first, toys second. Young children are vulnerable because their airway diameter is small and easily
occluded.




4.2 · MANAGEMENT

An 8-month-old is choking, conscious, with an ineffective cough. What should be done?

 A. Abdominal thrusts
 B. Blind finger sweep
 C. Five back blows then five chest thrusts
D. Immediate chest compressions
 E. Encourage coughing only

Answer: C.

Under 1 year: no abdominal thrusts — the liver and spleen are large and unprotected. Never do a blind
finger sweep; remove only what you can see. If the child becomes unconscious, start CPR.
