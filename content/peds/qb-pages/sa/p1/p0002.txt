 How to read an SBA stem    1. Read the last line first — it tells you whether the question wants a
diagnosis, the most common cause, the investigation of choice, or the next step in management. These
four have different right answers for the same stem.
2. Find the discriminating detail — age, one number, one sign. Exam stems rarely contain filler.
3. Beware "most appropriate next step" — often the answer is resuscitate or reassess, not the definitive
test.
4. In "EXCEPT" questions, mark each option true or false in the margin rather than hunting for the odd
one out.
