8.1 · DEFINITIONS

A 5-year-old has a positive skin-prick test to peanut but eats peanut butter regularly with no symptoms.
What is the correct term?

 A. Peanut allergy
 B. Sensitisation to peanut
 C. Anaphylaxis risk
 D. Atopy
 E. Food intolerance

Answer: B.

Sensitisation is a positive test; allergy is a positive test PLUS a clinical reaction. He must not be told to
avoid peanut — that would risk losing tolerance.




8.2 · SINGLE BEST ANSWER

Why must a child be observed in hospital for several hours after a moderate or severe food allergy
reaction?

 A. Because adrenaline wears off in 30 minutes
 B. Because of the late-phase response 4–6 hours after exposure
 C. To complete a food challenge
 D. To measure the specific IgE
 E. Because antihistamines take 6 hours to work

Answer: B.

Early phase = minutes, mast cell histamine. Late phase = 4–6 hours, cellular inflammation — the reaction
can return.
