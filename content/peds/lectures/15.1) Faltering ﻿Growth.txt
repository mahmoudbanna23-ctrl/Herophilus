                   Red Flags
                Growth faltering


• Dysmorphic features
• Failure to gain weight despite adequate caloric intake
• Recurrent vomiting, diarrhea, or dehydration
• Organomegaly or lymphadenopathy
• Signs suggestive of chronic respiratory disease
• Developmental delay

 Management growth
      faltering



• Dietary and behavioural modification designed to increase
  energy intake and monitoring growth




• Faltering Growth is not a disease, but rather a description of a
   relatively common growth pattern
• Ensure accurate serial measuring and plotting of height and
   weight on appropriate growth chart
• Investigations should be guided by history and examination.
• Simple behaviral advice can improve quantity and quality of
   nutritional intake

   Obesity


 Obesity          is       defined               as        an         excessive
  accumulation of body fat.

 Body mass index (BMI) is the most reliable and
  most commonly used clinical indicator of
  obesity




                                                                                          7
                                                             4/20/2026




       BMI

 BMI is calculated by dividing the weight in Kg
  by the height in meters squared (kg/m2)

 In     children         BMI   should   be   plotted   on
  appropriate charts

 BMI in children is age- and sex-specific




       OBESE
  BMI at > 95th centile




   Over weight
 BMI at 85th - 95th centile




     Healthy Wt
  BMI at 5 - 85th centile

            Drug treatment
 When ?
Drug treatment has a part to play in children
over the age of 12 years who have physical
co-morbidities or severe psychological problems
 What ?
Orlistat is a lipase inhibitor which reduces the
absorption of dietary fat and thus produces
steatorrhoea




          Bariatric surgery
 When ?
Children almost achieved maturity, have very
extreme obesity (BMI > 40 kg/ m2 )
 or have a BMI 35 to 40 kg/m2 with
complications of obesity with complications, e.g.
type 2 diabetes or hypertension and all other
interventions have failed to achieve or maintain
weight loss.
