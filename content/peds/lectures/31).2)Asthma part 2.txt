      The Goals for Successful
      Management of Asthma
➢ Absence of day time and night time symptoms
  (minimal – ideally NO).
➢ Prevent asthma exacerbations in previous 6
  months.
➢ Minimal use ( ≤ 2 / W) of short-acting β2- agonist.
➢ Maintain normal activity levels, including exercise.




                                                     3
➢ Maintain pulmonary function as close to normal
  levels
➢ Avoid adverse effects from asthma medications
  (minimal – ideally NO).
➢ Prevent development of irreversible airflow
  limitation.
➢ Prevent asthma mortality.
➢ Satisfy patient and family expectations.

       Asthma Management has Six
            Interrelated Parts
1. Educate patients to develop a partnership in asthma
   management (health education).
2. Assess and monitor asthma severity with both symptom
   reports and, as much as possible, measurements of
   lung function.
3. Avoid exposure to risk factors.
4. Establish individual medication plans for long-term
   management .
5. Establish individual plans for managing exacerbations.
6. Provide regular follow-up care.

              Reliever Medications

Relievers / Rescue drugs are taken only during acute
exacerbation as they act quickly to relieve
bronchoconstriction and its accompanying acute
symptoms such as wheezing, chest tightness, and
cough.
 ⚫ Rapid-acting inhaled β2-agonists (the most potent
   bronchodilators)
 ⚫ Systemic glucocorticosteroids   (the most potent anti-
   inflammatory)
 ⚫ Inhaled Anticholinergics

           Controller Medications
Controllers / Preventers are medications taken daily on
a long term-basis to achieve and maintain control of
asthma.
 ⚫ Inhaled glucocorticosteroids (gold standard)

 ⚫ Systemic glucocorticosteroids (most potent)

 ⚫ Methylxanthines (SR-slow release)

 ⚫ Long-acting inhaled β2-agonist

 ⚫ Leukotriene receptor antagonists

 ⚫ Anti-IgE monoclonal antibody= Omalizumab

 ⚫ Anti-IL5 monoclonal antibody     Mepolizumab

            Routes of Administration
                   Inhalation
Advantages;
✓ Site specific (high concentration effectively and
  directly delivered to A.W).
✓ Permitting very small dose.
✓ Systemic side effects are reduced to minimal.
✓ Rapid and predictable relief (SABA).
✓ Some drugs (ipratropium ) can only be
  used by inhalation as they are not absorbed orally.
Drawback;
Patient him- or herself due to improper use or rejection
for fear of addiction of their use.
