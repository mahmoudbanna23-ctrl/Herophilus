     Definition: Anemia is defined as a reduction
  of the red cell volume (hematocrit) or
  hemoglobin concentration below the range of
  normal values for age.


• Neonate: Hb <140 g/L
• 1 month - 12 months : Hb <100 g/L
• 1 year - 12 years : Hb < 110 g/L.
