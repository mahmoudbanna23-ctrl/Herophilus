 Definition of Asthma
A chronic inflammatory disease of the airways with the
following clinical features:
▪ Episodic and/or chronic symptoms of airway
  obstruction (cough, dyspnea, chest tightness,
  wheezes)
▪ Bronchial hyper-responsiveness to triggers
▪ Evidence of at least partial reversibility of the airway
  obstruction
▪ Alternative diagnoses are excluded

                Differential Diagnosis


       Although most children who have recurrent episodes of
coughing, wheezing or/and breathlessness have asthma, we
should think about other less common or rarer conditions
that     compromise the airways and mimic B.A, especially
when cases show inadequate response to anti-asthma
treatment or have other features suggesting other diagnoses.

Investigations
Usually not needed in children .
Diagnosis depends on good history and examination.

X ray chest:
Usually normal may show hyperinflation.
Is not necessary except to exclude other causes of
 wheezy chest , if signs of severe infection or
 asymmetry of chest signs ( pneumothorax or
 collapse)

Allergic Testing:
 Skin prick tests to identify triggering allergens

 Pulmonary function tests:
 Difficult to perform in children < 5 years
 PEFR or FEV1 measurement before and after
inhaling a bronchodilator . An improvement
of 20% or more in PEFR and 12% or more in
FEV1 after bronchodilator is diagnostic of
asthma

Blood gases:
Done only in life threatening or refractory cases
