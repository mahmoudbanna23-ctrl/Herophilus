--- slide 11 ---
SVT
 
Regular Narrow Complex Tachycardia
Mechanism: reentrant atrioventricular Tachycardia 
HR is usually above 180 beats/min
 In newborns, usually above 220 beats/min  
 Presents with palpitation, syncope , heart failure, cardiogenic shock 
Treatment for hemodynamically stable patients, IV adenosine is the first line 
For hemodynamically unstable patients, synchronized cardioversion is the first line

Ventricular Tachycardia 
 
Wide  Complex Tachycardia
Presents with palpitation, syncope, heart failure, cardiogenic shock 
For hemodynamically unstable patients, cardioversion is the first line  
Treatment for hemodynamically stable patients, IV amiodarone  is  usually the first line  (but not always)

--- slide 19 ---
Complete heart block 
Bradycardia with complete Atrio-ventricular dissociation 
Presents with palpitation, syncope, heart failure, cardiogenic shock 
Could be Congenital (SLE , CHD)  or Acquired (viral myocarditis, digitalis toxicity) 
For Symptomatic patients, pacemaker implantation is indicated

--- slide 25 ---
Viral myocarditis  
 
Viral-induced myocardial injury 
Ranges from an asymptomatic patient to fulminant carditis with cardiogenic shock 
Treatment of Heart failure and cardiogenic shock are the cornerstone of management 
 Intravenous immunoglobulins and steroids could be used in some patients 

--- slide 26 ---
Dilated 
Cardiomypathy
 
 
Genetic myocardial dysfunction leading to impaired systolic  cardiac function  and dilated left ventricle with global hypokinesia 
Presents with HF  and/or cardiogenic shock 
Treatment of Heart failure and cardiogenic shock are the cornerstone of management

--- slide 29 ---
Kawasaki disease 
 
Immune mediated vasculitis 
Has high tendency for coronary arteritis 
Intravenous immunoglobulins  is the gold standard for treatment 
Steroids and/or other immunomodulators are indicated  in high-risk and resistant patients as adjuvant therapy with IVIG

--- slide 41 ---
MIS-C 
 
Immune mediated multiorgan dysfunction after COVID infection  
Has high tendency for cardiac affection (impaired LV systolic function , MR , pericardial effusion , coronary affection) 
Steroids  , other immunomodulators  and IVIG  help in inhibiting the inflammatory cascade
