Abnormalities leading to Primary
immunodeficiency can be broadly
classified as defects of the:

(1) B-cell.
(2) T-cell.
(3) complement system.
(4) phagocytic system.

Vaccines
• Live attenuated vaccines, e.g. MMR, varicella, OPV, rotavirus, BCG –
  modified organisms
• Inactivated vaccines, e.g. inactivated polio (IPV), whole organisms
  obtained by chemical or heat treatment (pertussis).
• Subunit vaccines, e.g. diphtheria, tetanus toxoid, pneumococcus, Hib
  vaccine, Hepatitis B (HBV) – contain antigens of the organism.
