URINARY TRACT INFECTIONS
BACKGROUND


 Urinary tract infection (UTI) is the presence of
 infection in any part of the urinary tract.


 The 2 broad clinical categories of UTI are
 pyelonephritis (febrile, systemic) and cystitis (usually
 afebrile).
MAGNITUDE OF THE PROBLEM



 UTI is one of most common/serious bacterial
  infection in children.
 50% of patients have a structural abnormality of
  their urinary tract.

 pyelonephritis may damage the growing kidney by
  forming a scar, predisposing to HTN and to
  progressive CKD (if bilateral)

 SUMMARY
 UTI may present as a febrile illness without localization hence high
  index of suspicion required
 Infants, children and young people presenting with unexplained
  fever of 38°C or higher should have a urine sample tested within
  24-48 hours
 May bring to notice presence of VUR/ obstructive anomaly
 Early & aggressive treatment of acute pyelonephritis prevents renal
  damage, HTN, CKD
 Clinical manifestation and urine analysis can suspect UTI, but
  diagnosis can’t be confirmed without the culture
 Asymptomatic bacteriuria in infants and children should not be
  treated with antibiotics
