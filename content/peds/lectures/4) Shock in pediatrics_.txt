--- slide 8 ---
Early sign of shock is 
tachycardia
late sign of shock is 
low blood pressure
It is important to recognize early compensated shock,
 
as it is reversible, in contrast to decompensated shock,
 
which may be irreversible.

--- slide 17 ---
Fluid deficit replacement
Assessment of the volume of fluid needed to restore circulating volume (after resuscitation) is based
 
on either:
Estimation of percentage dehydration based on 
clinical features 
(clinical dehydration,
  
i.e. moderately severe dehydration – 5% deficit; shock, i.e. severe dehydration – 10% deficit)
Change in 
weight
 (where an accurate premorbid weight measurement is available).
Fluid boluses given during resuscitation are deducted from the fluid deficit
