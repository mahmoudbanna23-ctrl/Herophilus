### Adverse Effects:

#### 1) TCAs:
- **Anticholinergic:** peripheral (dry mouth, constipation, blurring of vision, urine retention) &
  central (confusion, memory loss especially in elderly)
- **Antihistaminic:** weight gain, sedation.
- **Antiadrenergic:** postural hypotension, syncope, sexual dysfunction.
- **Quinidine like action:** prolongation of QTc interval, arrhythmias.

#### 2) SSRIs:
Nausea, GIT upset, headache, insomnia, anxiety (at initiation), and sexual dysfunctions. Most of
these side effects **stop with continuation of treatment.**

#### 3) SNRIs:
GIT upset, elevation of blood pressure, insomnia, headache and sexual dysfunctions.

### Other adverse effects & special considerations:

**1) Serotonin syndrome: (uncommon)**
- Almost any medication involved with serotonin has the potential to cause it.
- *Manifestations:* restlessness, fever, myoclonus, insomnia and confusion
- It is serious, uncommon & appears at *high doses or with combinations*
- Proper medical intervention (**within 24 hours**) decreases mortality a lot.

**2) Pregnancy:** Avoidance of prescription is better unless they are highly needed. *Paroxetine is
class D*.

**3) Antidepressant-induced mania:** More with TCAs, SSRIs, SNRIs & NaSSAs.

[NOTE BOX — pale-blue, lightbulb glyph, centred word `Note`]
> NDRI (Bupropion) usually does not induce mania

**4) Suicide:**
- All antidepressants have this risk.
- Those aged *under 25 years* have the highest risk.
- The highest risk occurs within **1st (6) months** of medication use.
- The ratio is **4-7/1000**.

**5) Sexual side-effects:** usually reversible

**6) Discontinuation syndrome**

People with discontinuation syndrome have been on an antidepressant for at **least four weeks and**
have recently stopped taking the medication, either **abruptly or after a fast taper.**

### Common symptoms include:
- **Flu-like symptoms** (nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, headaches, sweating)
- **Sleep disturbances** (insomnia, nightmares, constant sleepiness [sic — the opening bracket is
  never closed on this bullet]
- **Sensory/movement disturbances** (imbalance, tremors, vertigo, dizziness, electric-shock-like
  experiences)
- **Mood disturbances** (dysphoria, anxiety, agitation)**.**
- **Cognitive disturbances** (confusion and hyper arousal).
