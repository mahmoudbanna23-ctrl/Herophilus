 Mental Health Problems Are a Sign of Weakness

 Recall the case:
 The patient's mother said: "You are a weak person." This common misattribution interferes with
 help-seeking, increases shame, and may worsen outcomes. As clinicians, challenging this
 narrative directly is part of our therapeutic role.


✗ MYTH                                                           ✓ FACT
