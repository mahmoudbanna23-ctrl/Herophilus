CLINICAL DIFFERENTIATION

Provoked vs. Unprovoked
Provoked (Acute Symptomatic): Occur in close
temporal relationship with systemic insult (fever,
metabolic shift, trauma). These do not constitute
epilepsy.

Ictal and Post-ictal States
Clinical observation focus: Aura (initial warning),
Ictus (the event itself), and Post-ictal (period of
recovery, confusion, or Todd’s paralysis).

The Role of EEG
While a seizure is a clinical diagnosis, the EEG is vital
for identifying interictal epileptiform discharges

Psychogenic Non-Epileptic Seizures

                           What is PNES?
                           Psychogenic Non-Epileptic Seizures (PNES) are paroxysmal events
                           that superficially resemble epileptic seizures but are not caused
                           by abnormal electrical discharges in the brain.
                               Functional Disorder: Often linked to psychological distress,
                               trauma, or internal conflicts.
                               Involuntary: Patients are not "faking"; the events are a
                               manifestation of the mind-body connection.

Understanding Syncope


Mechanisms of Fainting
Syncope is a transient loss of consciousness (TLOC) due to global
cerebral hypoperfusion. It is characterized by rapid onset, short
duration, and spontaneous complete recovery.
    Neurally Mediated: Vasovagal (emotional stress/pain),
    Situational.
    Orthostatic: Blood pressure drop upon standing.

    Cardiac: Arrhythmias or structural heart disease (Requires
    urgent workup).

KEY CLINICAL TAKEAWAYS
01   History is the cornerstone of epilepsy diagnosis — EEG and MRI SUPPORT, not replace.



02   Epilepsy = ≥2 unprovoked seizures OR 1 unprovoked with high recurrence risk.



03   NEVER give carbamazepine or phenytoin to absence or myoclonic epilepsy patients.



04   Status epilepticus: A-B-C → Diazepam IV → Phenytoin IV → ICU. Time is neurons.



05   Drug-resistant? Refer early — epilepsy surgery offers 70% seizure freedom in TLE.



06   Always think autoimmune encephalitis in young patients with new atypical seizures.
