Definition

• Mood Disorders (MD) are
  psychiatric disorders that
  involve disturbed mood
  parameter that has at one
  end; elation and at the
  other end; depression.


• They are characterized by
  an episodic nature,
  remission and relapsing
  course, and sometimes,
  psychotic features

             Psychotic Features
             • Delusions and Hallucinations may occur
               during the course of depressive and manic
               episodes.
             • Delusions may be mood congruent as
               delusions of guilt or deserved punishment
               in depressive episodes or delusion of
Specifiers     grandeur in manic episodes.
             • Mood incongruent delusions include
               delusions of control or influence.
             Melancholic features
                 These features may occur in a depressive
             episode and include diurnal variation of the
             mood or its non-reactivity, guilt, and middle,
             late insomnia or early morning awakening

             •                These features may occur
                 during the course of depressive episode.
Specifiers       They include mood reactivity, hypersomnia,

                 Catatonic Features
             •                 These features may occur
                 during the course of all episodes. They
                 include excitement, stupor, and negativism.
