  1) Major depressive disorder

• Sodium valproate:
Stomach irritation, weight gain, tremors, menstrual irregularities and
polycystic ovary.
Liver function and CBC should be monitored.
It is Contraindicated in pregnancy & lactation

• Lamotrigine:
Monitoring for Stevens–Johnson syndrome “a very rare but potentially
fatal skin condition” is highly important.

• Carbamazepine:
CBC should be monitored, as it can lower white blood cell count.
Indications: (must NOT be used more than ONE
